# ¶ The BOOK of NEHEMIAH.

## CHAPTER I.

Nehemiah mourneth, fasteth, and prayeth.

THE words of Nehemiah the son of Hachaliah. And it came to pass in the month <sup>a</sup>Chisleu, in the <sup>b</sup>twentieth year, as I was in Shushan the palace,

2 That <sup>c</sup>Hanani, one of my brethren, came, he and *certain* men of Judah; and I asked them concerning the Jews that had escaped, which were left of the captivity, and concerning Jerusalem.

3 And they said unto me, The remnant that are left of the captivity there in the <sup>d</sup>province *are* <sup>e</sup>in great affliction and reproach: the wall of Jerusalem also *is* <sup>f</sup>broken down, and the gates thereof are burnt with fire.

4 And it came to pass, when I heard these words, that <sup>g</sup>I sat down and wept, and mourned *certain* days, and <sup>h</sup>fasted, and <sup>i</sup>prayed before the God of heaven,

5 And said, I beseech thee, <sup>k</sup>O LORD God of heaven, the <sup>l</sup>great and terrible God, that <sup>m</sup>keepeth covenant and mercy for them that love him and observe his commandments;

6 <sup>n</sup>Let thine ear now be attentive, and thine eyes open, that thou mayest hear the prayer of thy servant, which I pray before thee now, day and <sup>o</sup>night, for the children of Israel thy servants, <sup>p</sup>and confess the sins of the children of Israel, which we have sinned <sup>q</sup>against thee: both I and my father's house have sinned.

7 We have dealt very corruptly against thee, and have not kept the commandments, nor the statutes, nor the judgments, which thou commandedst thy servant Moses.

8 <sup>r</sup>Remember, I beseech thee, the word that thou commandedst thy servant Moses, saying, <sup>s</sup>*If* ye transgress, I will scatter you abroad among the nations:

9 But *if* ye <sup>t</sup>turn unto me, and keep my commandments, and do them; though there were of you <sup>u</sup>cast out unto the uttermost part of the heaven, *yet* will I gather them from thence, and will bring them unto the place that I have chosen to set my name there.

10 Now these *are* thy <sup>x</sup>servants, and thy people, whom thou hast redeemed by thy great power, and by thy <sup>y</sup>strong hand.

11 O LORD, I beseech thee, let now thine ear be attentive to the prayer of thy servant, and to the prayer of thy servants, who desire to <sup>z</sup>fear thy name; and <sup>a</sup>prosper, I pray thee, thy servant this day, and grant him mercy in the sight of this man: For I was the king's cup-bearer.

## CHAPTER II.

Artaxerxes sendeth Nehemiah to Jerusalem.

AND it came to pass in the month <sup>a</sup>Nisan, in the twentieth year of <sup>b</sup>Artaxerxes the king, *that* wine *was* before him: and I took up the <sup>c</sup>wine, and gave *it* unto the king. Now I had not been *beforetime* <sup>d</sup>sad in his presence.

2 Wherefore the king said unto me, Why *is* thy countenance sad, seeing thou *art* not sick? this *is* nothing *else* but <sup>e</sup>sorrow of heart. Then I was <sup>f</sup>very sore afraid,

3 And said unto the king, <sup>g</sup>Let the king live for ever: <sup>h</sup>why should not my countenance be sad, when the city, the place of my fathers' sepulchres, *lieth* waste, and the gates thereof are consumed with fire?

4 Then the king said unto me, For what dost thou make request? So I <sup>i</sup>prayed to the God of heaven.

5 And I said unto the king, If it <sup>k</sup>please the king, and if thy servant have found favour in thy sight, that thou wouldest <sup>l</sup>send me unto Judah, unto the city of my fathers' sepulchres, that <sup>m</sup>I may build it.

6 And the <sup>n</sup>king said unto me, (the queen also sitting by him,) For how long shall thy journey be? and when wilt thou return? So it pleased the king to send me; and I <sup>o</sup>set him a time.

7 Moreover, I said unto the king, If it please the king, let <sup>p</sup>letters be given me to the governors beyond the river, that they may convey me over, till I come into Judah;

8 And a letter unto Asaph the keeper of the king's forest, that he may give me timber to make beams for the gates of the palace which <sup>q</sup>*appertained* to the house, and for the wall of the city, and for the house <sup>r</sup>that I shall enter into. And the king granted me, according to the <sup>s</sup>good hand of my God upon me.

9 Then I came to the governors beyond the river, and gave them the king's letters. (Now the king had sent captains of the army and horsemen with me.)

10 When Sanballat the <sup>t</sup>Horonite, and Tobiah the <sup>u</sup>servant, the <sup>x</sup>Ammonite, heard *of it,* it <sup>y</sup>grieved them exceedingly that there was come a man to seek the welfare of the children of Israel.

11 So I came to Jerusalem, and was <sup>z</sup>there three days.

12 And I arose <sup>a</sup>in the night, I and some few men with me; neither told I *any* man what my God had put in my heart to do at Jerusalem: neither *was there any* beast with me, save the beast that I rode upon.
