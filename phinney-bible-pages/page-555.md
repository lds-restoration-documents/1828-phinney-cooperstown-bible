## CHAPTER X.

Israel reproved and threatened for impiety and idolatry.

ISRAEL *is* <sup>a</sup>an empty vine, he bringeth forth fruit <sup>b</sup>unto himself: according to the multitude of his fruit he hath increased the altars; according to the goodness of his land they have made goodly images.

2 Their heart is <sup>c</sup>divided; now shall they be found faulty: he shall break down their altars, he shall spoil their images.

3 For now they shall say, We have <sup>d</sup>no king, because we <sup>e</sup>feared not the LORD; what then should a king do to us?

4 They have spoken <sup>f</sup>words, swearing falsely in making a covenant: thus <sup>g</sup>judgment springeth up as hemlock in the furrows of the field.

5 The inhabitants of Samaria shall fear because of the calves of Beth-aven: for the people thereof shall mourn over it, and the <sup>†</sup>priests thereof *that* rejoiced on it, for the glory thereof, because it is departed from it.

6 It shall be also carried unto Assyria *for* a present to king <sup>h</sup>Jareb: Ephraim shall receive shame, and Israel shall be ashamed of his <sup>i</sup>own counsel.

7 *As for* Samaria, <sup>k</sup>her king is cut off as the foam upon the water.

8 The high places also of Aven, the <sup>l</sup>sin of Israel, shall be destroyed: the thorn and the thistle shall come up on their altars; and they shall say to the mountains, <sup>m</sup>Cover us; and to the hills, Fall on us.

9 O Israel, thou hast sinned <sup>n</sup>from the days of Gibeah; there they stood: the battle in Gibeah against <sup>o</sup>the children of iniquity did not overtake them.

10 *It is* in <sup>p</sup>my desire that I should chastise them; and the people shall be gathered against them, when they shall bind themselves in their two furrows.

11 And Ephraim *is as* a heifer *that is* taught, *and* loveth <sup>q</sup>to tread out *the corn;* but I passed over upon her fair neck: I will make Ephraim <sup>r</sup>to ride; Judah shall plow, *and* Jacob shall break his clods.

12 Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap in mercy; <sup>s</sup>break up your fallow ground: for *it is* <sup>t</sup>time to seek the LORD, till he come and rain righteousness upon you.

13 Ye have <sup>u</sup>plowed wickedness, ye have reaped iniquity: ye have eaten the fruit of lies: because thou didst trust in thy way, in the <sup>x</sup>multitude of thy mighty men.

14 Therefore shall a tumult arise among thy people, and all thy fortresses shall be spoiled, as Shalman spoiled Beth-arbel in the day of battle: the <sup>y</sup>mother was dashed in pieces upon *her* children.

15 So shall Beth-el do unto you because of your great wickedness: in a <sup>z</sup>morning shall the king of Israel utterly be cut off.

## CHAPTER XI.

1 The ingratitude of Israel unto God for his benefits; 5 his judgment....8 God's mercy toward them.

WHEN Israel *was* a child, then <sup>a</sup>I loved him, and called <sup>b</sup>my son out of Egypt.

2 *As* <sup>c</sup>they called them, so they went from them: they sacrificed unto Baalim, and burned incense to graven images.

3 I taught Ephraim also <sup>d</sup>to go, taking them by their <sup>e</sup>arms; but they knew not that I healed them.

4 I drew them with <sup>f</sup>cords of a man, with bands of love; and I was to them as they that take off the yoke on their jaws, and I <sup>g</sup>laid meat unto them.

5 He shall <sup>h</sup>not return into the land of Egypt, but the Assyrian shall be <sup>i</sup>his king, because they refused to return.

6 And the <sup>k</sup>sword shall abide on his cities, and shall consume his branches, and devour *them,* because of their own <sup>l</sup>counsels.

7 And my people are bent to backsliding from me: though <sup>m</sup>they called them to the Most High, none at all would exalt *him.*

8 <sup>n</sup>How shall I give thee up, Ephraim? *how* shall I deliver thee, Israel? *how* shall I make thee as Admah? *how* shall I set thee as Zeboim? <sup>o</sup>My heart is turned within me, my repentings are kindled together.

9 I will <sup>p</sup>not execute the fierceness of mine anger, I will not return to destroy Ephraim: for <sup>q</sup>I *am* God, and not man; the Holy One in the midst of thee; and I will not enter into the city.

10 They shall walk <sup>r</sup>after the LORD; he shall roar like a lion: when he shall roar, then the children shall tremble from the west.

11 They shall <sup>s</sup>tremble as a bird out of Egypt, and as a dove out of the land of Assyria: and I will place them in their houses, saith the LORD.

12 Ephraim compasseth me about <sup>t</sup>with lies, and the house of Israel with deceit: but Judah yet <sup>u</sup>ruleth with God, and is faithful with the saints.

## CHAPTER XII.

1 A reproof of Ephraim, Judah, and Jacob....7 Ephraim's sins and ingratitude provoke God.

EPHRAIM feedeth on <sup>a</sup>wind, and followeth after the east wind: he daily increaseth lies and desolation; and they do make a covenant with the Assyrians, and <sup>b</sup>oil is carried into Egypt.

2 The LORD hath also a controversy with <sup>c</sup>Judah, and will punish Jacob according to his ways; <sup>d</sup>according to his doings will he recompense him.

3 He took his brother <sup>e</sup>by the heel in the womb, and by his strength he had <sup>f</sup>power with God:

4 Yea, he had power over the angel, and prevailed: he wept and made supplication unto him: he found him *in* <sup>g</sup>Beth-el, and there he spake <sup>h</sup>with us;

5 Even the Lord GOD of hosts; The LORD *is* his <sup>i</sup>memorial. <!-- uncertain: "Lord GOD" as printed, first word appears in mixed case -->

6 Therefore turn thou to thy God: <sup>k</sup>keep mercy and judgment, and <sup>l</sup>wait on thy God continually.

7 *He is* a merchant, the <sup>m</sup>balances of deceit *are* in his hand: he loveth to oppress.

8 And Ephraim said, Yet I am become <sup>n</sup>rich, I have found me out substance: *in* all my labours they shall find <sup>o</sup>none iniquity in me that *were* sin.

9 And I, *that am* the LORD thy God <sup>p</sup>from the land of Egypt, will yet make thee to dwell in tabernacles, as in the days of the solemn feasts.

10 <sup>||</sup>I have also spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and used similitudes, by the ministry of the prophets.

11 *Is there* iniquity *in* Gilead? surely they are vanity: <sup>q</sup>they sacrifice bullocks in Gilgal; yea, their altars *are* as heaps in the furrows of the fields.

12 And Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a <sup>r</sup>wife, and for a wife he kept *sheep.*

13 And by <sup>s</sup>a prophet the LORD brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he <sup>t</sup>preserved.

14 Ephraim <sup>u</sup>provoked *him* to anger most bitterly: therefore shall he leave his blood upon him, and his reproach shall his LORD <sup>x</sup>return unto him.
