## CHAPTER XX.

The priest's exhortation to encourage the people to battle.

7 And what man *is there* that <sup>h</sup>hath betrothed a wife, and hath not taken her? let him go and return unto his house, lest he die in battle, and another man take her.

8 And the officers shall <sup>i</sup>speak further unto the people, and they shall say, What man *is there that is* fearful and faint-hearted? let him go and return unto his house, lest his brethren's heart <sup>†</sup>faint as well as his heart.

9 And it shall be, when the officers have made an end of speaking unto the people, that they shall make captains of the armies <sup>†</sup>to lead the people.
