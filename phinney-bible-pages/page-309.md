## CHAPTER XIX.

Jehoshaphat, reproved by Jehu, visiteth his kingdom.

AND Jehoshaphat the king of Judah returned to his house <sup>a</sup>in peace to Jerusalem.

2 And <sup>b</sup>Jehu, the son of Hanani the seer, went out to meet him, and said to king Jehoshaphat, Shouldest thou help the <sup>c</sup>ungodly, and <sup>d</sup>love them that <sup>e</sup>hate the LORD? therefore <sup>f</sup>*is* wrath upon thee from before the LORD.

3 Nevertheless, there are <sup>g</sup>good things found in thee, in that thou hast taken away the <sup>h</sup>groves out of the land, and hast <sup>i</sup>prepared thy heart to seek God.

4 And Jehoshaphat dwelt at Jerusalem: and he <sup>k</sup>went out again through the people, from <sup>l</sup>Beer-sheba to mount Ephraim, and brought them back unto the LORD God of their fathers.

5 And he set judges in the land, throughout all the fenced cities of Judah, city by city,

6 And said to the judges, Take heed what ye do: for ye judge not for man, but for the LORD, who *is* <sup>m</sup>with you in the judgment.

7 Wherefore now, let the <sup>n</sup>fear of the LORD be upon you; take heed and do *it:* for *there is* no <sup>o</sup>iniquity with the LORD our God, nor respect of persons, nor taking of gifts.

8 Moreover, in Jerusalem did Jehoshaphat set of the <sup>p</sup>Levites, and *of* the priests, and of the chief of the fathers of Israel, for the judgment of the LORD, and for controversies, when they returned to Jerusalem.

9 And he charged them, saying, Thus shall ye do in the <sup>q</sup>fear of the LORD, faithfully, and with a perfect heart.

10 And what cause soever shall come to you of your brethren that dwell in their cities, between <sup>r</sup>blood and blood, between <sup>s</sup>law and commandment, statutes and judgments, ye shall even warn them that they trespass not against the LORD, and *so* <sup>t</sup>wrath come <sup>u</sup>upon you, and upon your brethren: this do, and ye shall not trespass.

11 And, behold, Amariah the chief priest *is* over you in all matters of the LORD; and Zebadiah the son of Ishmael, the ruler of the house of Judah, for all the king's <sup>x</sup>matters: also the Levites *shall be* <sup>y</sup>officers before you. Deal courageously, and the LORD shall be with the good.

4 And Judah gathered themselves together to ask *help* of the LORD; even out of <sup>i</sup>all the cities of Judah they came to seek the LORD.

5 And Jehoshaphat stood in the congregation of Judah and Jerusalem, in the house of the LORD, before <sup>k</sup>the new court,

6 And said, O LORD God of our fathers, *art* not thou <sup>l</sup>God in heaven? and <sup>m</sup>rulest *not* thou over all the kingdoms of the heathen? and in thy hand *is there not* power and might, so that <sup>n</sup>none is able to withstand thee?

7 *Art* not thou <sup>o</sup>our God, *who* didst <sup>p</sup>drive out the inhabitants of this land before thy people Israel, and gavest it to the seed of Abraham <sup>q</sup>thy friend for ever?

8 And they dwelt therein, and have built thee <sup>r</sup>a sanctuary therein for thy <sup>s</sup>name, saying,

9 If, *when* <sup>t</sup>evil cometh upon us, *as* the sword, judgment, or pestilence, or famine, we stand before this house, and in thy presence, (for thy name *is* in this house,) and cry unto thee in our affliction, then thou wilt hear and help.

10 And now, behold, the children of Ammon, and Moab, and mount Seir, whom thou <sup>u</sup>wouldest not let Israel invade, when they came out of the land of Egypt, but they turned from them, and destroyed them not;

11 Behold, *I say, how* they <sup>x</sup>reward us, to come to cast us out of thy possession, which thou hast given us to inherit.

12 O our God, wilt thou <sup>y</sup>not judge them? for we have <sup>z</sup>no might against this great company that cometh against us; neither know we what to do: but <sup>a</sup>our eyes *are* upon thee.

13 And all Judah stood <sup>b</sup>before the LORD, with their <sup>c</sup>little ones, their wives, and their children.

14 <sup>d</sup>Then upon Jahaziel, the son of Zechariah, the son of Benaiah, the son of Jeiel, the son of Mattaniah, a Levite, of the sons of Asaph, came the <sup>e</sup>Spirit of the LORD in the midst of the congregation;

15 And he said, Hearken ye, all Judah, and ye inhabitants of Jerusalem, and thou king Jehoshaphat; Thus saith the LORD unto you, <sup>f</sup>Be not afraid nor dismayed by reason of this great multitude; for the battle *is* <sup>g</sup>not yours, but God's.

16 To-morrow go ye down against them: behold, they come up by the cliff of Ziz; and ye shall find them at the end of the brook, before the wilderness of Jeruel.

17 Ye shall <sup>h</sup>not *need* to fight in this *battle;* set yourselves, <sup>i</sup>stand ye *still,* and see the salvation of the LORD with you, O Judah and Jerusalem: fear not, nor be dismayed; to-morrow go out against them; for the LORD *will be* <sup>k</sup>with you.

18 And Jehoshaphat <sup>l</sup>bowed his head, with *his* face to the ground: and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell before the LORD, worshipping the LORD.

19 And the Levites of the children of the <sup>m</sup>Kohathites, and of the children of the Korhites, stood up to praise the LORD God of Israel with a loud voice on high.

20 ¶ And they rose early in the morning, and went forth into the wilderness of <sup>n</sup>Tekoa: and as they went forth, Jehoshaphat stood and said, Hear me, O Judah, and ye inhabitants of Jerusalem; <sup>o</sup>Believe in the LORD your God, so shall you be <sup>p</sup>established; believe his prophets, so shall ye <sup>q</sup>prosper.

21 And when he <sup>r</sup>had consulted with the people, he appointed singers unto the LORD, and that should praise the <sup>s</sup>beauty of holiness as they went out before the army, and to say, <sup>t</sup>Praise the LORD; for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

22 And when they began to sing and to praise, the LORD set <sup>u</sup>ambushments against the children of Ammon, Moab, and mount Seir, which were come against Judah; and they were smitten.
