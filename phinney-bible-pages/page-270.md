10 And king Ahaz went to Damascus <sup>r</sup>to meet Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, and <sup>s</sup>saw an altar that *was* at Damascus: and king Ahaz sent to Urijah the priest the fashion of the <sup>t</sup>altar, and the pattern of it, according to all the workmanship thereof.

11 And <sup>u</sup>Urijah the priest built an altar according to all that king Ahaz had sent from Damascus: so Urijah the priest <sup>x</sup>made *it* against king Ahaz came from Damascus.

12 And when the king was come from Damascus, the king saw the altar: and the king approached to the altar, and <sup>y</sup>offered thereon.

13 And he burnt his burnt-offering, and his <sup>z</sup>meat-offering, and poured his drink-offering, and sprinkled the blood of his peace-offerings upon the altar.

14 And he brought also the <sup>a</sup>brazen altar, which *was* <sup>b</sup>before the LORD, from the fore-front of the house, from between the altar and the house of the LORD, and put it on the north side of the altar.

15 And king Ahaz commanded Urijah the priest, saying, Upon the great altar burn the morning burnt-offering, and the evening meat-offering, and the king's burnt-sacrifice, and his meat-offering, with the burnt-offering of all the people of the land, and their meat-offering, and their drink-offerings; and sprinkle upon it all the blood of the burnt-offering, and all the blood of the sacrifice: and the brazen altar shall be for me to inquire *by*.

16 <sup>c</sup>Thus did Urijah the priest, according to all that king Ahaz commanded.

17 And king Ahaz cut off the <sup>†</sup>borders of the bases, and removed the laver from off them; and took down the sea from off the brazen oxen that *were* under it, and put it upon a pavement of stones:

18 And the <sup>d</sup>covert for the sabbath that they had built in the house, and the king's entry without, turned he from the house of the LORD <sup>e</sup>for the king of Assyria.

19 Now the rest of the acts of Ahaz which he did, *are* they not written in the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Judah?

20 And Ahaz slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in <sup>f</sup>the city of David: and Hezekiah his son reigned in his stead.

## CHAPTER XVII.

Hoshea's wicked reign.

IN the <sup>a</sup>twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, began Hoshea the son of Elah to reign in Samaria over Israel nine years.

2 And he did *that which was* evil in the sight of the LORD, but <sup>b</sup>not as the kings of Israel that were before him.

3 Against him came up <sup>c</sup>Shalmaneser king of Assyria; and Hoshea became his servant, and gave him presents.

4 And the king of Assyria found conspiracy <sup>d</sup>in Hoshea: for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt, and brought no present to the king of Assyria, as *he had done* year by year: therefore the king of Assyria shut him up, and bound him in <sup>†</sup>prison.

5 Then the king of Assyria came up <sup>e</sup>throughout all the land, and went up to Samaria, and besieged it three years.

6 In the <sup>f</sup>ninth year of Hoshea the king of Assyria took Samaria, and <sup>g</sup>carried Israel away into Assyria, and placed them in <sup>h</sup>Halah, and in <sup>i</sup>Habor *by* the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the <sup>k</sup>Medes.

7 For *so* it was, that the children of Israel had sinned against the LORD their God, which had <sup>l</sup>brought them up out of the land of Egypt, from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and had feared <sup>m</sup>other gods,

8 And walked in the <sup>n</sup>statutes of the heathen, whom the LORD cast out from before the children of Israel, and of the kings of Israel, <sup>o</sup>which they had made.

9 And the children of Israel <sup>†</sup>did secretly *those* things that *were* not right against the LORD their God, and they built them high places in all their cities, from the <sup>p</sup>tower of the watchmen to the fenced city.

10 And they set them up <sup>q</sup>images and <sup>r</sup>groves in every high hill, and under every green tree:

11 And there they burnt <sup>s</sup>incense in all the high places, as *did* the heathen whom the LORD carried away <sup>t</sup>before them; and wrought wicked things to provoke the LORD to anger:

12 For they served idols, whereof the LORD had said unto them, Ye shall not do this thing.

13 Yet the LORD testified against Israel, and against Judah, by all <sup>u</sup>the prophets, *and by* all the seers, saying, Turn ye <sup>x</sup>from your evil ways, and keep my commandments *and* my statutes, according to all the law which I commanded your fathers, and which I sent to you by my servants the prophets.

14 Notwithstanding, they would not hear, but <sup>y</sup>hardened their necks, like to the neck of their fathers, that did not believe in the LORD their God.

15 And they rejected his statutes, and his covenant that he made with their fathers, and his testimonies which he testified against them; and they followed vanity, and became <sup>z</sup>vain, and went after the heathen that *were* round about them, *concerning* whom the LORD had charged them, that they should <sup>a</sup>not do like them.

16 And they <sup>b</sup>left all the commandments of the LORD their God, and made them <sup>c</sup>molten images, *even* two <sup>d</sup>calves, and made a grove, and worshipped all the <sup>e</sup>host of heaven, and served Baal.

17 And they caused their sons and their daughters <sup>f</sup>to pass through the fire, and used divination and enchantments, and <sup>g</sup>sold themselves to do evil in the sight of the LORD, to provoke him to anger.

18 Therefore the LORD was very angry with Israel, and removed them out <sup>h</sup>of his sight: there was none left but the tribe of <sup>i</sup>Judah only.

19 Also <sup>k</sup>Judah kept not the commandments of the LORD their God, but walked in the statutes of Israel which they made.

20 And the LORD rejected all the seed of Israel, and afflicted them, and delivered them into the hand of <sup>l</sup>spoilers, until he had cast them out of his sight.

21 For he rent <sup>m</sup>Israel from the house of David; and they made Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, king: and Jeroboam drave Israel from following the LORD, and made them sin a great sin.

22 For the children of Israel walked in all the sins of Jeroboam which he did; they departed not from them;

23 Until the LORD removed Israel out of his sight, as he had said <sup>n</sup>by all his servants the prophets. So was Israel carried away out of their own land to Assyria unto this day.

24 And <sup>o</sup>the king of Assyria brought *men* from <sup>p</sup>Babylon, and from Cuthah, and from <sup>q</sup>Ava, and from <sup>r</sup>Hamath, and from Sepharvaim, and placed *them* in the cities of Samaria, <sup>s</sup>instead of the children of Israel: and they possessed Samaria, and dwelt in the cities thereof.
