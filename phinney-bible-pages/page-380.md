## PSALM LXII.

No trust is to be put in worldly things.

*To the chief Musician, to* <sup>a</sup>*Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.*

TRULY my soul waiteth upon God: from him *cometh* my salvation.

2 He <sup>b</sup>only *is* my rock and my salvation; *he is* my defence: I shall not be <sup>c</sup>greatly moved.

3 How long will ye imagine mischief against <sup>d</sup>a man? ye shall be <sup>e</sup>slain all of you: as a bowing wall *shall ye be, and as* <sup>f</sup>a tottering fence.

4 They <sup>g</sup>only consult to cast *him* down from his <sup>h</sup>excellency; they delight <sup>i</sup>in lies: they bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly. Selah.

5 My soul, wait thou only upon God: for <sup>k</sup>my expectation *is* from him.

6 He only *is* <sup>l</sup>my rock and my salvation; *he is* my defence: I shall not be moved.

7 In God *is* my salvation and <sup>m</sup>my glory: the rock of my strength, *and* my refuge, *is* in God.

8 Trust in him at all times, ye people; <sup>n</sup>pour out your heart before him: God *is* a refuge for us. Selah.

9 Surely men of <sup>o</sup>low degree *are* vanity, *and* men of high degree *are* <sup>p</sup>a lie: to be laid in the balance, they *are* altogether *lighter* than <sup>q</sup>vanity.

10 <sup>r</sup>Trust not in oppression, become not vain in robbery: if riches increase, <sup>s</sup>set not your heart *upon them.*

11 God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this, that <sup>t</sup>power *belongeth* unto God.

12 Also unto thee, O LORD, *belongeth* <sup>u</sup>mercy: for thou renderest to every man according to his work.

## PSALM LXIII.

1 David's thirst for God....4 His manner of blessing God.

*A Psalm of David, when he was in the* <sup>a</sup>*wilderness of Judah.*

O GOD, thou *art* <sup>b</sup>my God; <sup>c</sup>early will I seek thee, my <sup>d</sup>soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee <sup>e</sup>in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is;

2 <sup>f</sup>To see thy <sup>g</sup>power and thy glory, so *as* I have seen thee in the sanctuary.

3 Because thy loving-kindness *is* <sup>h</sup>better than life, <sup>i</sup>my lips shall praise thee.

4 Thus will I bless thee <sup>k</sup>while I live: I will lift up <sup>l</sup>my hands in thy name.

5 My soul shall be satisfied as *with* marrow and <sup>m</sup>fatness; and my mouth shall praise *thee* with joyful lips;

6 When <sup>n</sup>I remember thee upon my bed, *and* meditate on thee in the <sup>o</sup>*night*-watches.

7 Because thou hast been my help: <sup>p</sup>therefore in the shadow of thy wings will I rejoice.

8 My soul followeth hard after thee: <sup>q</sup>thy right hand upholdeth me.

9 But those *that* <sup>r</sup>seek my soul, to destroy *it*, shall go into the lower parts of the earth.

10 They shall <sup>s</sup>fall by the sword; they shall be a portion for foxes.

11 But the king shall rejoice in God; every one that sweareth by him shall glory: but the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped.

## PSALM LXIV.

David prayeth for deliverance, complaining of his enemies.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David.*

HEAR <sup>a</sup>my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from <sup>b</sup>fear of the enemy.

2 Hide me from the <sup>c</sup>secret counsel of the wicked; from the <sup>d</sup>insurrection of the workers of iniquity:

3 Who <sup>e</sup>whet their tongue like a sword, *and* bend *their bows to shoot* their arrows, *even* bitter words;

4 That they may <sup>f</sup>shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and <sup>g</sup>fear not.

5 They <sup>h</sup>encourage themselves *in* an evil matter: they commune of laying <sup>i</sup>snares privily; they say, Who shall <sup>k</sup>see them?

6 They <sup>l</sup>search out iniquities; they accomplish <sup>m</sup>a diligent search: both the inward *thought* of every one *of them*, and the heart, *is* deep.

7 But God shall shoot at them *with* an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded.

8 So they shall make their <sup>n</sup>own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall <sup>o</sup>flee away.

9 And all men shall <sup>p</sup>fear, and shall <sup>q</sup>declare the work of God: for they shall wisely <sup>r</sup>consider of his doing.

10 The righteous shall be glad in the LORD, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.

## PSALM LXV.

The blessedness of God's chosen by reason of benefits.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm and Song of David.*

PRAISE <sup>†</sup>waiteth for thee, O God, <sup>a</sup>in Zion: and unto thee shall the vow be performed.

2 O thou that <sup>b</sup>hearest prayer, unto thee shall <sup>c</sup>all flesh come.

3 <sup>d</sup>Iniquities prevail against me: *as for* our transgressions, thou shalt <sup>e</sup>purge them away.

4 Blessed *is the man whom* thou <sup>f</sup>choosest, and causest <sup>g</sup>to approach *unto thee, that* he may dwell in thy courts: we shall be satisfied with the goodness of thy house, *even* of thy holy temple.

5 <sup>h</sup>*By* terrible things in righteousness wilt thou answer us, O God of our salvation; *who art* the confidence of all the ends of the earth, and of them that <sup>i</sup>are afar off *upon* the sea:

6 Which by his strength setteth fast the <sup>k</sup>mountains; *being* <sup>l</sup>girded with power:

7 Which stilleth the <sup>m</sup>noise of the seas, the noise of their waves, and the tumult of the people.

8 They also that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid of thy <sup>n</sup>tokens: thou makest the outgoings of the morning and evening to rejoice.

9 Thou <sup>o</sup>visitest the earth, and waterest it: thou greatly enrichest it with the river of God, *which* is full <sup>p</sup>of water: thou preparest them corn, when thou hast so provided for it.

10 Thou <sup>q</sup>waterest the ridges thereof abundantly; thou settlest the furrows thereof; thou makest it soft with showers; thou blessest the <sup>†</sup>springing thereof.

11 Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness.

12 They drop *upon* the pastures of the wilderness; and the little hills rejoice on every side.

13 The <sup>r</sup>pastures are clothed with flocks; the valleys also are covered over with corn: they shout for joy, they also sing.

## PSALM LXVI.

David declareth God's especial goodness to himself.

*To the chief Musician. A Song or Psalm.*

MAKE <sup>a</sup>a joyful noise unto God, all ye lands.

2 Sing forth the <sup>b</sup>honour of his name; make his praise glorious.

3 Say unto God, How <sup>c</sup>terrible *art thou in* thy works! through the greatness of thy power shall thine enemies <sup>†</sup>submit themselves unto thee.
