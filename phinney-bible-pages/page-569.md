## CHAPTER II.

The judgment upon the Chaldeans for their sins.

I WILL stand upon my <sup>a</sup>watch, and set me upon the tower, and will watch to see what he will say <sup>||</sup>unto me, and what I shall answer when I am reproved.

2 And the LORD answered me, and said, Write the vision, and make *it* plain upon <sup>b</sup>tables, that he may run that readeth it.

3 For the vision *is* yet for <sup>c</sup>an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely <sup>d</sup>come, it will not tarry.

4 Behold, his soul *which* is <sup>e</sup>lifted up is not upright in him: but the <sup>f</sup>just shall live by his faith.

5 Yea also, because he transgresseth <sup>g</sup>by wine, *he* is a proud man, neither keepeth at home, who <sup>h</sup>enlargeth his desire as hell, and *is* as death, and cannot be satisfied, but gathereth unto him all nations, and heapeth unto him all people:

6 Shall not all these take up <sup>i</sup>a parable against him, and a taunting proverb against him, and say, Wo to him that increaseth *that which is* not his! how long? and to him that ladeth himself <sup>k</sup>with thick clay!

7 Shall they not rise up <sup>l</sup>suddenly that shall bite thee, and awake that shall vex thee, and thou shalt be for booties unto them?

8 Because thou hast spoiled many nations, all the remnant of the people shall spoil thee; because of men's blood, and *for* the <sup>m</sup>violence of the land, of the city, and of all that dwell therein.

9 Wo to him that <sup>n</sup>coveteth an evil covetousness to his house, that he may set his <sup>o</sup>nest on high, that he may be delivered from the power of evil!

10 Thou hast consulted <sup>p</sup>shame to thy house by cutting off many people, and hast sinned *against* thy soul.

11 For the stone shall cry out of the wall, and the beam out of the timber shall answer it.

12 Wo to him that buildeth a town with blood, and establisheth a city by iniquity!

13 Behold, *is it* not of the LORD of hosts that the people shall labour in the <sup>q</sup>very fire, and the people shall weary themselves for very vanity?

14 For the <sup>r</sup>earth shall be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD, as the waters cover the sea.

15 Wo unto him that giveth his neighbour drink, that puttest thy bottle to *him,* and makest *him* <sup>s</sup>drunken also, that thou mayest <sup>t</sup>look on their nakedness!

16 Thou art filled with <sup>u</sup>shame for glory: <sup>x</sup>drink thou also, and let thy foreskin be uncovered: the cup of the LORD's right hand shall be turned unto thee, and shameful spewing *shall be* on thy glory.

17 For the <sup>y</sup>violence of Lebanon shall cover thee, and the spoil of beasts, *which* made them afraid, because of men's blood, and for the violence of the land, of the city, and of all that dwell therein.

18 What profiteth the graven image, that the maker thereof hath graven it; the molten image, and <sup>z</sup>a teacher of lies that the maker of his work trusteth therein, to make <sup>a</sup>dumb idols?

19 Wo unto him that saith to the wood, <sup>b</sup>Awake; to the dumb stone, Arise, it shall teach! Behold, it *is* laid over with gold and silver, and *there is* no breath at all in the midst of it.

20 But the LORD *is* in his holy temple; let all the earth keep <sup>c</sup>silence before him.

## CHAPTER III.

1 Habakkuk's prayer: 3 He trembleth at God's majesty: 7 The stability of his faith.

A PRAYER of Habakkuk the prophet upon <sup>a</sup>Shigionoth.

2 O LORD, I have heard thy speech, *and* was afraid: O LORD, <sup>b</sup>revive thy work in the midst of the years, in the midst of the years make known; <sup>c</sup>in wrath remember mercy.

3 God came from Teman, and the Holy One from mount Paran. Selah. <sup>d</sup>His glory covered the heavens, and the earth was full of his praise.

4 And *his* brightness was as the light; he had <sup>e</sup>horns *coming* out of his hand: and there *was* the hiding of his power.

5 Before him went the <sup>f</sup>pestilence, and burning coals went forth at his feet.

6 He stood, and <sup>g</sup>measured the earth: he beheld, and drove asunder the nations; and the everlasting mountains were scattered, the perpetual hills did bow: his ways *are* everlasting.

7 I saw the tents of Cushan <sup>h</sup>in affliction: *and* the curtains of the land of Midian did tremble.

8 Was the LORD displeased against the rivers? *was* thine anger against the <sup>i</sup>rivers? *was* thy wrath against the sea, that thou didst ride upon thy horses, *and* thy chariots of salvation?

9 Thy bow was made quite naked, *according* to the oaths of the tribes, *even thy* word. Selah. Thou didst <sup>k</sup>cleave the earth with rivers.

10 The mountains saw thee, *and* they trembled: the overflowing of the water passed by: the deep uttered his voice, *and* lifted up his hands on high.

11 The <sup>l</sup>sun *and* moon stood still in their habitation: at the light of thine arrows they went, *and* at the shining of thy glittering spear.

12 Thou didst march through the land in indignation, thou <sup>m</sup>didst thresh the heathen in anger.

13 Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, *even* for salvation with thine <sup>n</sup>anointed; thou woundedst the head out of the house of the wicked, by discovering the foundation unto the neck. Selah.

14 Thou didst <sup>o</sup>strike through with his staves the head of his villages; they came out as a whirlwind to scatter me: their rejoicing *was* as to <sup>p</sup>devour the poor secretly.

15 Thou didst <sup>q</sup>walk through the sea with thy horses, *through* the heap of great waters.
