## CHAPTER VIII.

1 Ezekiel's vision....18 God's wrath for idolatry.

AND it came to pass in the sixth year, in the sixth *month,* in the fifth *day* of the month, *as* <sup>a</sup>I sat in my house, and the elders of Judah sat <sup>b</sup>before me, that the <sup>c</sup>hand of the Lord GOD fell there upon me.

2 Then I beheld, and, lo, a likeness as the appearance of <sup>d</sup>fire: from the appearance of his loins, even downward, fire; and from his loins, even upward, as the appearance of <sup>e</sup>brightness, as the colour of amber.

3 And he put forth the form of a hand, and took me by a lock of my head, and the <sup>f</sup>spirit lifted me up <sup>g</sup>between the earth and the heaven, and brought me in the visions of God to Jerusalem, to the door of the inner gate that looketh toward the north, where *was* the seat of the <sup>h</sup>image of jealousy, which <sup>i</sup>provoketh to jealousy.

4 And, behold, the <sup>k</sup>glory of the God of Israel *was* there, according to the vision that I <sup>l</sup>saw in the plain.

5 Then said he unto me, Son of man, <sup>m</sup>lift up thine eyes now the way toward the north. So I lifted up mine eyes the way toward the north, and behold, northward at the gate of the altar, this image of jealousy in the entry.

6 He said furthermore unto me, Son of man, seest thou what they do? *even* the great abominations that the house of Israel committeth here, that <sup>n</sup>I should go far off from my sanctuary? But turn thee yet again, *and* thou shalt see greater abominations.

7 And he brought me to the <sup>o</sup>door of the court; and when I looked, behold, <sup>p</sup>a hole in the wall.

8 Then said he unto me, Son of man, <sup>q</sup>dig now in the wall: and when I had digged in the wall, behold, a door.

9 And he said unto me, Go in, and behold the wicked abominations that they do here.

10 So I went in and saw; and, behold, every form of creeping things, and <sup>r</sup>abominable beasts, and all the <sup>†</sup>idols of the house of Israel, pourtrayed upon the wall round about.

11 And there stood before them seventy men of the ancients of the house of Israel, and in the midst of them stood Jaazaniah the son <sup>s</sup>of Shaphan, with every man his censer in his hand; and a thick cloud of <sup>t</sup>incense went up.

12 Then said he unto me, Son of man, hast thou seen what the ancients of the house of Israel do in the <sup>u</sup>dark, every man in the chambers of his imagery? for they say, The LORD <sup>x</sup>seeth us not; the LORD hath <sup>y</sup>forsaken the earth.

13 He said also unto me, Turn thee yet again, *and* thou shalt <sup>z</sup>see greater abominations that they do.

14 Then he brought me to the door of the gate of the LORD's house which *was* toward the north; and, behold, there sat <sup>a</sup>women weeping for <sup>b</sup>Tammuz.

15 Then said he unto me, Hast thou seen *this,* O son of man? Turn thee yet again, *and* thou shalt see greater abominations than these.

16 And he brought me into the inner court of the LORD's house, and, behold, at the door of the temple of the LORD, <sup>c</sup>between the porch and the altar, *were* about <sup>d</sup>five and twenty men, with their <sup>e</sup>backs toward the temple of the LORD, and their faces toward the east; and they worshipped the <sup>f</sup>sun toward the east.

17 Then he said unto me, Hast thou seen *this,* O son of man? Is it a light thing to the house of Judah that they commit the abominations which they commit <sup>g</sup>here? for they have <sup>h</sup>filled the land with violence, and have returned to provoke me to anger; and, lo, they put the branch <sup>i</sup>to their nose.

18 Therefore will I also deal <sup>k</sup>in fury: mine eye shall not spare, neither will I have pity; and though they <sup>l</sup>cry in mine ears with a loud voice, *yet* will I not hear them.

## CHAPTER IX.

A vision, whereby is shewed the preservation of some, and the destruction of the rest.

HE cried also <sup>a</sup>in mine ears with a loud voice, saying, Cause them that <sup>b</sup>have charge over the city to draw near, even every man *with* his destroying weapon in his hand.

2 And, behold, six <sup>c</sup>men came from the way of the higher gate, which lieth toward the north, and every man <sup>d</sup>a slaughter-weapon in his hand; and <sup>e</sup>one man <sup>f</sup>among them *was* clothed with linen, with a writer's <sup>g</sup>ink-horn by his side: and they went in, and stood beside the brazen altar.

3 And the <sup>h</sup>glory of the God of Israel was gone up from the cherub, whereupon he was, to the <sup>i</sup>threshold of the house: and he called to the man clothed with linen, which *had* the writer's ink-horn by his side;

4 And the LORD said unto him, Go through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem, and set <sup>k</sup>a mark upon the foreheads of the men that <sup>l</sup>sigh, and that cry, for all the abominations that be done in the midst thereof.

5 And to the others he said in my hearing, Go ye <sup>m</sup>after him through the city, and smite; let not your eye spare, neither have ye pity:

6 Slay utterly old *and* young, both maids, and little children, and women; but <sup>n</sup>come not near any man upon whom *is* the mark; and <sup>o</sup>begin at my sanctuary. Then they began at <sup>p</sup>the ancient men which *were* before the house.

7 And he said unto them, <sup>q</sup>Defile the house, and fill the courts with the slain; go ye forth. And they went forth and slew in the city.

8 And it came to pass while they were slaying them, and I was left, that <sup>r</sup>I fell upon my face, and cried, and said, <sup>s</sup>Ah Lord GOD! wilt thou destroy all the <sup>t</sup>residue of Israel in thy pouring out of thy fury upon Jerusalem?

9 Then said he unto me, The iniquity of the house of Israel and Judah *is* <sup>u</sup>exceeding great, and the land is full of <sup>x</sup>blood, and the city full of perverseness; for they say, The LORD hath forsaken the earth, and the LORD <sup>y</sup>seeth not.

10 And as for me also, mine eye shall not spare, neither will I have pity; *but* I will <sup>z</sup>recompense their way upon their head.

11 And, behold, the man clothed with linen, which *had* the ink-horn by his side, reported the matter, saying, I have <sup>a</sup>done as thou hast commanded me.
