10 And the LORD said unto Moses, Bring Aaron's rod again before the testimony, to be <sup>k</sup>kept for a token against the <sup>†</sup>rebels; and thou shalt quite take away their murmurings from me, that they die not.

11 And Moses did *so*: as the LORD commanded him, so did he.
