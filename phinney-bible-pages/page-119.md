9 And a man *that is* clean shall gather up the <sup>n</sup>ashes of the heifer, and lay *them* up without the camp in a <sup>o</sup>clean place, and it shall be kept for the congregation of the children of Israel, for a water of <sup>p</sup>separation: it *is* a purification <sup>q</sup>for sin.

10 And he that gathereth the ashes of the heifer shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: and it shall be unto the children of Israel, and unto the stranger that sojourneth among them, for a statute for ever.

11 ¶ He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall <sup>r</sup>be unclean seven days.

12 He shall <sup>s</sup>purify himself with it on the third day, and on the seventh day he shall be clean: but if he purify not himself the third day, then the seventh day he shall not be clean.

13 Whosoever toucheth the dead body of any man that is dead, and purifieth not himself, defileth the tabernacle of the LORD; and that soul shall be cut <sup>t</sup>off from Israel: because the water of separation was not sprinkled upon him, he shall be unclean; his uncleanness *is* yet upon him.

14 This *is* the law when a man dieth in a tent; All that come into the tent, and all that *is* in the tent, shall be unclean seven days.

15 And every <sup>u</sup>open vessel, which hath no covering bound upon it, *is* unclean.

16 And whosoever toucheth one that is slain with a <sup>x</sup>sword in the open fields, or a dead body, or a <sup>y</sup>bone of a man, or <sup>z</sup>a grave, shall be unclean seven days.

17 And for an unclean *person* they shall take of the ashes of the burnt heifer of purification for sin, and <sup>†</sup>running water shall be put thereto in a vessel;

18 And a clean person shall take hyssop, and dip *it* in the water, and sprinkle *it* upon the tent, and upon all <sup>a</sup>the vessels, and upon the persons that were there, and upon him that touched a bone, or one slain, or one dead, or a grave:

19 And the clean *person* shall <sup>b</sup>sprinkle upon the unclean on the <sup>c</sup>third day, and on the seventh day; and on the <sup>d</sup>seventh day he shall purify himself, and <sup>e</sup>wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and shall be clean at even.

20 But the man that shall be unclean, and shall not purify himself, that soul shall be cut off from among the congregation, because he hath <sup>f</sup>defiled the sanctuary of the LORD: the water of separation hath not been sprinkled upon him; he *is* unclean.

21 And it shall be a perpetual statute unto them, that he that sprinkleth the water of separation shall <sup>g</sup>wash his clothes; and he that toucheth the water of separation shall be unclean until even.

22 And whatsoever the unclean *person* <sup>h</sup>toucheth shall be unclean; and the soul that toucheth *it* shall be unclean until even.

## CHAPTER XX.

2 The children of Israel murmur for want of water....7 Moses, smiting the rock, bringeth forth water at Meribah.

THEN came the children of Israel, *even* the <sup>a</sup>whole congregation, into the desert of <sup>b</sup>Zin, in the <sup>c</sup>first month: and the people <sup>d</sup>abode in <sup>e</sup>Kadesh; and Miriam <sup>f</sup>died there, and was buried there.

2 And there was <sup>g</sup>no water for the congregation: and they gathered themselves together against Moses and against Aaron.

3 And the people <sup>h</sup>chode with Moses, and spake, saying, <sup>i</sup>Would God that we had died when <sup>k</sup>our brethren died before the LORD!

4 And why have ye brought up the congregation of the LORD into this wilderness, that we and our cattle should die there?

5 And wherefore have ye made us to come up out of Egypt, to bring us in unto this evil place? it *is* no place of seed, or of figs, or of vines, or of pomegranates; neither *is* there any water to drink.

6 And Moses and Aaron went from <sup>l</sup>the presence of the assembly unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and they <sup>m</sup>fell upon their faces; and the glory of the LORD <sup>n</sup>appeared unto them.

7 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

8 Take the <sup>o</sup>rod, and gather thou the assembly together, thou and Aaron thy brother, and <sup>p</sup>speak ye unto the rock before their eyes; and it shall <sup>q</sup>give forth his water, and thou shalt bring forth to them water out of the rock: so thou shalt give the congregation and their beasts drink.

9 And Moses took the rod <sup>r</sup>from before the LORD, as he commanded him.

10 And Moses and Aaron gathered the congregation together before the rock; and he said unto them, <sup>s</sup>Hear now, ye <sup>t</sup>rebels; <sup>u</sup>must we fetch you water out of this rock?

11 And Moses lifted up his hand, and with his rod he smote the rock twice: and the water came out <sup>x</sup>abundantly: and the <sup>y</sup>congregation drank, and their <sup>z</sup>beasts *also*.

12 And the LORD spake unto Moses and Aaron, Because ye <sup>a</sup>believed me not, to <sup>b</sup>sanctify me in the <sup>c</sup>eyes of the children of Israel, therefore ye shall <sup>d</sup>not bring this congregation into the land which I have given them.

13 This *is* the water of <sup>||</sup>Meribah; because the children of Israel strove with the LORD, and he was <sup>e</sup>sanctified in them.

14 ¶ And Moses sent messengers from Kadesh unto the king of <sup>f</sup>Edom, Thus saith thy brother Israel, Thou knowest all the travail that hath <sup>†</sup>befallen us;

15 How our fathers <sup>g</sup>went down into Egypt, and we have dwelt in Egypt a <sup>h</sup>long time; and the Egyptians vexed us and our fathers:

16 And when we cried unto the LORD, he heard our voice, and sent an <sup>i</sup>angel, and hath brought us forth out of Egypt; and, behold, we *are* in Kadesh, a city in the uttermost of thy border:

17 Let us pass, I pray thee, through <sup>k</sup>thy country: we will not pass through the fields, or through the vineyards, neither will we drink *of* the water of the wells: we will go by the king's *high*-way, we will not turn to the right hand nor to the left, until we have passed thy borders.

18 And Edom said unto him, Thou shalt not pass by me, lest I come out against thee with the sword.

19 And the children of Israel said unto him, We will go by the highway, and if I and my cattle drink of thy water, then I will <sup>l</sup>pay for it: I will only, without *doing* any thing *else*, go through on my feet.

20 And he said, Thou shalt not go through. And Edom came out against him with much people, and with a strong hand.

21 Thus Edom refused to give Israel passage through his border: wherefore Israel <sup>m</sup>turned away from him.

22 ¶ And the children of Israel, *even* the whole congregation, journeyed from Kadesh, and came unto <sup>n</sup>mount Hor.
