## CHAPTER XXIII.

1 Of slander and false witness....3, 6 Of justice....4 Of charitableness....10 Of the year of rest....12 Of the Sabbath....13 Of idolatry....14 Of the three feasts....18 Of the blood and the fat of the sacrifice....20 An Angel is promised.

THOU shalt not <sup>||</sup>raise a false report: <sup>a</sup>put not thine hand with the wicked to be an <sup>b</sup>unrighteous witness.

2 ¶ Thou shalt not follow <sup>c</sup>a multitude to *do* evil: neither shalt thou speak in a cause to decline after many to wrest *judgment:*

3 Neither shalt thou <sup>d</sup>countenance a poor man in his cause.

4 ¶ If thou meet thine enemy's ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt surely <sup>e</sup>bring it back to him again.

5 If thou see the ass of him that hateth thee lying under <sup>f</sup>his burden, and wouldest forbear to help him; thou shalt surely help with him.

6 ¶ Thou shalt not wrest the judgment of <sup>g</sup>thy poor in his cause.

7 <sup>h</sup>Keep thee far from a false matter; and the innocent and righteous slay thou not: for I will <sup>i</sup>not justify the wicked.

8 ¶ And thou shalt take <sup>k</sup>no gift; for the gift blindeth <sup>†</sup>the wise, and <sup>l</sup>perverteth the words of the righteous.

9 ¶ Also thou shalt not oppress a stranger: for ye know the heart of a stranger, seeing ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.

10 ¶ And <sup>m</sup>six years thou shalt sow thy land, and shalt gather in the fruits thereof:

11 But the seventh *year* thou shalt let <sup>n</sup>it rest and lie still; that the poor of thy people may eat: and what they leave, the beasts of the field shall eat. In like manner thou shalt deal with thy vineyard, *and* with thy olive-yard.

12 ¶ Six days thou shalt do thy work, and on the seventh day thou shalt rest; that thine ox and thine ass may rest, and the son of thy handmaid and the stranger may be refreshed.

13 ¶ And in all *things* that I have said unto you be <sup>o</sup>circumspect: and make no mention of the <sup>p</sup>names of other gods, neither let it be heard out of thy mouth.

14 ¶ Three times thou shalt keep a feast unto me in the year.

15 Thou shalt keep the feast of unleavened bread: (thou shalt eat unleavened bread seven days, as I commanded thee, in the time appointed of the month Abib; for in it thou camest out from Egypt; and none shall <sup>q</sup>appear before me empty:)

16 And the feast of harvest, the first-fruits of thy labours, which thou hast sown in the field: and the feast of in-gathering, *which is* in the end of the year, when thou hast gathered in thy labours out of the field.

17 Three times in the year <sup>r</sup>all thy males shall appear before the LORD God.

18 ¶ Thou shalt <sup>s</sup>not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leavened bread; neither shall the fat of my <sup>||</sup>sacrifice remain until the morning.

19 The first of the <sup>t</sup>first-fruits of thy land thou shalt bring into the <sup>u</sup>house of the LORD thy God. Thou shalt not <sup>x</sup>seethe a kid in his mother's milk.

20 ¶ Behold, I send <sup>y</sup>an Angel before thee, <sup>z</sup>to keep thee in the way, and to bring thee into the <sup>a</sup>place which I have prepared.

21 Beware of him, and obey his voice, provoke him not; for he will <sup>b</sup>not pardon your transgressions: for my name *is* <sup>c</sup>in him.

22 But if thou shalt indeed <sup>d</sup>obey his voice, and do all that I speak, then I will be <sup>e</sup>an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries.

23 For mine Angel shall go <sup>f</sup>before thee, and bring thee in unto the <sup>g</sup>Amorites, and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites; and I will cut them off.

24 Thou shalt not bow down to their gods, nor serve them, nor do <sup>h</sup>after their works; but thou shalt utterly overthrow them, and quite break down their images.

25 And ye shall serve the LORD your God, and he shall bless <sup>i</sup>thy bread and thy water, and I will take <sup>k</sup>sickness away from the midst of thee.

26 There shall nothing <sup>l</sup>cast their young, nor be barren, in thy land: the number of thy days I will <sup>m</sup>fulfil.

27 I will send <sup>n</sup>my fear before thee, and will destroy all the people to whom thou shalt come; and I will make all thine enemies turn their backs unto thee.

28 And I will send <sup>o</sup>hornets before thee, which shall drive out the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite, from before thee.

29 I will not drive them out from before thee in one year; lest the land become desolate, and the beast of the field multiply against thee.

30 By little and little I will drive them out from before thee, until thou be increased, and inherit the land.
