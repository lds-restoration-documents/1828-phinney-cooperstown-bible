27 And they told him all the words of Joseph, which he had said unto them: and when he saw the waggons which Joseph had sent to carry him, the spirit of Jacob their father revived:

28 And Israel said, <sup>x</sup>*It is* enough: Joseph my son *is* yet alive: I will go and see him before I die.

## CHAPTER XLVI.

1 Jacob is comforted by God at Beer-sheba....5 Thence he with his company goeth into Egypt.

AND Israel took his journey with all that he had, and came to <sup>a</sup>Beer-sheba, and <sup>b</sup>offered sacrifices unto the <sup>c</sup>God of his father Isaac.

2 And God spake unto Israel in the <sup>d</sup>visions of the night, and said, <sup>e</sup>Jacob, Jacob. And he said, Here *am* I.

3 And he said, I *am* <sup>f</sup>God, the God of thy father: fear not to go down into Egypt; for I will there make of thee a great nation:

4 <sup>g</sup>I will go down with thee into Egypt; and I will also surely <sup>h</sup>bring thee up *again:* and Joseph shall put his hand upon thine eyes.

5 ¶ And Jacob rose up from Beer-sheba: and the sons of Israel carried Jacob their father, and their little ones, and their wives, in the waggons which Pharaoh had sent to carry him.

6 And they took their cattle, and their goods, which they had gotten in the land of Canaan, and came into <sup>i</sup>Egypt, Jacob, and all his seed with him:

7 His sons, and his sons' sons with him, his <sup>k</sup>daughters, and his sons' daughters, and all his seed brought he with him into Egypt.

8 ¶ And these *are* the names of the children of Israel which came into Egypt; Jacob and his sons: Reuben, Jacob's first-born.

9 And the sons of Reuben; <sup>l</sup>Hanoch, and Phallu, and Hezron, and Carmi.

10 And the sons of Simeon; <sup>m</sup>Jemuel, and Jamin, and Ohad, and Jachin, and Zohar; and Shaul, the son of <sup>n</sup>a Canaanitish woman.

11 And the sons of Levi; <sup>o</sup>Gershon, <sup>p</sup>Kohath, and Merari.

12 And the sons of Judah; Er, and Onan, and Shelah, and Pharez, and Zarah: but Er and Onan <sup>q</sup>died in the land of Canaan. And the sons of Pharez were <sup>r</sup>Hezron and Hamul.

13 And the sons of <sup>s</sup>Issachar; <sup>t</sup>Tola, and <sup>u</sup>Phuvah, and Job, and Shimron.

14 And the sons of Zebulun; Sered, and Elon, and Jahleel.

15 These *be* the sons of Leah, which she bare unto Jacob in <sup>x</sup>Padan-aram, with his daughter Dinah: all the souls of his sons and his daughters *were* <sup>y</sup>thirty and three.

16 And the sons of Gad; <sup>z</sup>Ziphion, and Haggi, Shuni, and Ezbon, Eri, and Arodi, and Areli.

17 And the sons of Asher; Jimnah, and Ishuah, and Isui, and Beriah, and Serah their sister: And the sons of Beriah; Heber, and Malchiel.

18 These *are* the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to Leah his daughter: and these she bare unto Jacob, *even* sixteen souls.

19 The sons of Rachel, Jacob's <sup>a</sup>wife; Joseph and Benjamin.

20 And unto Joseph, in the land of Egypt, were born Manasseh and Ephraim, which Asenath, the daughter of Poti-pherah <sup>||</sup>priest of On, bare unto him.

21 And the sons of Benjamin *were* <sup>b</sup>Belah, and Becher, and <sup>c</sup>Ashbel, Gera, and Naaman, Ehi, and Rosh, <sup>d</sup>Muppim, and Huppim, and Ard.

22 These *are* the sons of Rachel, which were born to Jacob: all the souls *were* fourteen.

23 And the <sup>e</sup>sons of Dan; Hushim.

24 And the sons of Naphtali; Jahzeel, and Guni, and Jezer, and <sup>f</sup>Shillem.

25 These *are* the sons of Bilhah, which Laban gave unto Rachel his daughter; and she bare these unto Jacob: all the souls *were* seven.

26 All the souls that came with Jacob into Egypt, which came out of his <sup>†</sup>loins, besides Jacob's sons' wives, all the souls *were* threescore and six:

27 And the sons of Joseph, which were born him in Egypt, *were* two souls: All the souls of the house of Jacob, which came into Egypt, *were* <sup>g</sup>threescore and ten.

28 ¶ And he sent Judah before him unto Joseph, to direct his face unto Goshen; and they came into the land of Goshen.
