27 (For all these abominations have the men of the land done, which *were* before you, and the land is defiled;)

29 For whosoever shall commit any of these abominations, even the souls that commit *them* shall be <sup>d</sup>cut off from among their people.

30 Therefore shall ye keep mine <sup>e</sup>ordinance, that *ye* commit not *any one* of these abominable customs, which were committed before you, and that ye defile not yourselves therein: I *am* the LORD your God.

## —CHAPTER XIX.

A repetition of sundry laws.

AND the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2 Speak unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, and say unto them, Ye shall <sup>a</sup>be holy: for I the LORD your God *am* holy.

3 Ye shall fear every man <sup>b</sup>his mother, and his father, and keep <sup>c</sup>my sabbaths: I *am* the LORD your God.

4 <sup>d</sup>Turn ye not unto <sup>e</sup>idols, nor make to yourselves <sup>f</sup>molten gods: I *am* the LORD your God.

5 And if ye offer a sacrifice of <sup>g</sup>peace-offerings unto the LORD, ye shall offer it at your own will.

6 It shall be eaten the same day ye offer it, and on the morrow: and if aught remain until the third day, it shall be <sup>h</sup>burnt in the fire.

7 And if it be eaten at all on the third day, it *is* <sup>i</sup>abominable; it shall not be accepted.

8 Therefore *every one* that eateth it shall bear <sup>k</sup>his iniquity, because he hath profaned the hallowed thing of the LORD; and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.

9 And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt <sup>l</sup>not wholly reap the corners of thy field, neither shalt thou gather the gleanings of thy harvest.

10 And thou shalt not glean thy vineyard, neither <sup>m</sup>shalt thou gather *every* grape of thy vineyard; thou shalt <sup>n</sup>leave them for the poor and stranger: I *am* the LORD your God.

11 Ye shall not steal, neither <sup>o</sup>deal falsely, neither lie one to another.

12 And ye shall not swear by my name <sup>p</sup>falsely, neither shalt thou profane the <sup>q</sup>name of thy God: I *am* the LORD.

13 Thou shalt not <sup>r</sup>defraud thy neighbour, neither rob *him*: the <sup>s</sup>wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning.

14 Thou shalt <sup>t</sup>not curse the deaf, nor <sup>u</sup>put a stumbling-block before the blind, but shalt fear thy God: I *am* the LORD.

15 Ye shall do no <sup>x</sup>unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not <sup>y</sup>respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: *but* in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

16 Thou shalt not go up and down *as* <sup>z</sup>a tale-bearer among thy people; neither shalt thou <sup>a</sup>stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I *am* the LORD.

17 Thou shalt <sup>b</sup>not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, <sup>||</sup>and not suffer sin upon him.

18 Thou shalt <sup>c</sup>not avenge, nor bear any <sup>d</sup>grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I *am* the LORD.
