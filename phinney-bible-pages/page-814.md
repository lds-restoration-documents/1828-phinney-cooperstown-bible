**Header:** *Bountifulness recommended.* | II. CORINTHIANS. | *Paul's spiritual authority.*

## CHAPTER IX.

1 He sheweth why he sent the brethren, 6 and stirreth them up to give bountifully, as a likely means to increase their store, &c.

<sup>a</sup>FOR as touching the ministering to the saints, it is superfluous for me to write to you:

2 <sup>b</sup>For I know the forwardness of your mind, for which I boast of you to them of Macedonia, that Achaia was ready a year ago; and your zeal hath provoked very many.

3 <sup>c</sup>Yet have I sent the brethren, lest our boasting of you should be in vain in this behalf; that, as I said, ye may be ready:

4 <sup>d</sup>Lest haply if they of Macedonia come with me, and find you unprepared, we (that we say not, ye) should be ashamed in this same confident boasting.

5 <sup>e</sup>Therefore I thought it necessary to exhort the brethren, that they would go before unto you, and make up beforehand your <sup>†</sup>bounty, whereof ye had notice before, that the same might be ready, as *a matter of* bounty, and not as *of* covetousness.

6 <sup>f</sup>But this *I say,* He which soweth sparingly shall reap also sparingly; and he which soweth bountifully shall reap also bountifully.

7 <sup>g</sup>Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, *so let him give;* not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.

8 <sup>h</sup>And God *is* able to make all grace abound toward you; that ye, always having all-sufficiency in all *things,* may abound to every good work:

9 <sup>i</sup>(As it is written, He hath dispersed abroad; he hath given to the poor: his righteousness remaineth for ever.

10 <sup>k</sup>Now he that ministereth seed to the sower, both minister bread for *your* food, and multiply your seed sown, and increase the fruits of your righteousness;)

11 <sup>l</sup>Being enriched in every thing to all bountifulness, which causeth through us thanksgiving to God.

12 <sup>m</sup>For the administration of this service not only supplieth the want of the saints, but is abundant also by many thanksgivings unto God;

13 <sup>n</sup>While by the experiment of this ministration they glorify God for your professed subjection unto the gospel of Christ, and for *your* liberal distribution unto them, and unto all *men;*

14 <sup>o</sup>And by their prayer for you, which long after you for the exceeding grace of God in you.

15 Thanks *be* unto God for his unspeakable gift.

## CHAPTER X.

Paul wisheth for no cause to exert his spiritual power and authority, &c.

<sup>a</sup>NOW I Paul myself beseech you by the meekness and gentleness of Christ, who in presence *am* base among you, but being absent am bold toward you:

2 <sup>b</sup>But I beseech *you,* that I may not be bold when I am present with that confidence, wherewith I think to be bold against some, which think of us as if we walked according to the flesh.

3 <sup>c</sup>For though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh:

4 <sup>d</sup>(For the weapons of our warfare *are* not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;)

5 <sup>e</sup>Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ;

6 <sup>f</sup>And having in a readiness to revenge all disobedience, when your obedience is fulfilled.

7 <sup>g</sup>Do ye look on things after the outward appearance? If any man trust to himself that he is Christ's, let him of himself think this again, that, as he *is* Christ's, even so *are* we Christ's.

8 <sup>h</sup>For though I should boast somewhat more of our authority, which the Lord hath given us for edification, and not for your destruction, I should not be ashamed:

9 <sup>i</sup>That I may not seem as if I would terrify you by letters.

10 <sup>k</sup>For *his* letters, say they, *are* weighty and powerful; but *his* bodily presence *is* weak, and *his* speech contemptible.

11 <sup>l</sup>Let such a one think this, that, such as we are in word by letters when we are absent, such *will we be* also indeed when we are present.

12 <sup>m</sup>For we dare not make ourselves of the number, or compare ourselves with some that commend themselves: but they measuring themselves by themselves, and comparing themselves among themselves, are not wise.

13 <sup>n</sup>But we will not boast of things without *our* measure, but according to the measure of the rule which God hath distributed to us, a measure to reach even unto you.

14 <sup>o</sup>For we stretch not ourselves beyond *our measure,* as though we reached not unto you: for we are come as far as to you also in *preaching* the gospel of Christ:

15 <sup>p</sup>Not boasting of things without *our* measure, *that is,* of other men's labours; but having hope, when your faith is increased, that we shall be enlarged by you according to our rule abundantly,

16 To preach the gospel in the *regions* beyond you, *and* not to boast in another man's line of things made ready to our hand.

17 <sup>q</sup>But he that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord.

18 <sup>r</sup>For not he that commendeth himself is approved, but whom the Lord commendeth.

## CHAPTER XI.

1 Paul unwillingly entereth into a commendation of himself with the other apostles; 7 he declineth being chargeable; 23 he sheweth his sufferings for the gospel.

<sup>a</sup>WOULD to God ye could bear with me a little in *my* folly: and indeed bear with me.

2 <sup>b</sup>For I am jealous over you with godly jealousy: for I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present *you as* a chaste virgin to Christ.

3 <sup>c</sup>But I fear, lest by any means, as the serpent beguiled Eve through his subtilty, so your minds should be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ.

4 <sup>d</sup>For if he that cometh preacheth another Jesus, whom we have not preached, or *if* ye receive another spirit, which ye have not received, or another gospel, which ye have not accepted, ye might well bear with *him.*

5 <sup>e</sup>For I suppose I was not a whit behind the very chiefest apostles.

6 <sup>f</sup>But though *I be* rude in speech, yet not in knowledge; but we have been thoroughly made manifest among you in all things.

7 <sup>g</sup>Have I committed an offence in abasing myself that ye might be exalted, because I have preached to you the gospel of God freely?
