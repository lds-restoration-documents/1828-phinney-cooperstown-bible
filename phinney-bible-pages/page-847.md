## CHAPTER V.

1 The elders exhorted to feed the flock of Christ; 5 the younger to obey the elder; 8 and all to be sober, watchful, and steadfast in the faith, resisting the devil, &c.

<sup>a</sup>THE elders which are among you I exhort, who am also an elder and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a partaker of the glory that shall be revealed:

2 <sup>b</sup>Feed the flock of God which is among you, taking the oversight *thereof,* not by constraint, but willingly; not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind;

3 <sup>c</sup>Neither as being lords over *God's* heritage, but being ensamples to the flock.

4 <sup>d</sup>And when the chief Shepherd shall appear, ye shall receive a crown of glory that fadeth not away.

5 <sup>e</sup>Likewise, ye younger, submit yourselves unto the elder: yea, all *of you* be subject one to another, and be clothed with humility: for God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble.

6 <sup>f</sup>Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in due time:

7 <sup>g</sup>Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.

8 <sup>h</sup>Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about seeking whom he may devour:

9 <sup>i</sup>Whom resist, steadfast in the faith, knowing that the same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren that are in the world.

10 <sup>k</sup>But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle *you:*

11 <sup>l</sup>To him *be* glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen.

12 <sup>m</sup>By Silvanus, a faithful brother unto you, as I suppose, I have written briefly, exhorting, and testifying that this is the true grace of God wherein ye stand.

13 <sup>n</sup>The *church that is* at Babylon, elected together with *you,* saluteth you; and *so doth* Marcus my son.

14 <sup>o</sup>Greet ye one another with a kiss of charity. Peace *be* with you all that are in Christ Jesus. Amen.

# ¶ The Second Epistle general of PETER.

## CHAPTER I.

Peter, saluting the Christians, admonisheth them of the gifts and promises of the gospel, &c.

<sup>a</sup>SIMON Peter, a servant and an apostle of Jesus Christ, to them that have obtained like precious faith with us through the righteousness of God and our Saviour Jesus Christ:

2 <sup>b</sup>Grace and peace be multiplied unto you through the knowledge of God, and of Jesus our Lord,

3 <sup>c</sup>According as his divine power hath given unto us all things that *pertain* unto life and godliness, through the knowledge of him that hath called us to glory and virtue:

4 <sup>d</sup>Whereby are given unto us exceeding great and precious promises; that by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.

5 <sup>e</sup>And besides this, giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue; and to virtue knowledge;

6 And to knowledge temperance; and to temperance patience; and to patience godliness,

7 <sup>f</sup>And to godliness brotherly kindness; and to brotherly kindness charity.

8 <sup>g</sup>For if these things be in you, and abound, they make *you that ye shall* neither *be* barren nor unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

9 <sup>h</sup>But he that lacketh these things is blind, and cannot see afar off, and hath forgotten that he was purged from his old sins.

10 <sup>i</sup>Wherefore the rather, brethren, give diligence to make your calling and election sure: for if ye do these things, ye shall never fall:

11 For so an entrance shall be ministered unto you abundantly into the everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
