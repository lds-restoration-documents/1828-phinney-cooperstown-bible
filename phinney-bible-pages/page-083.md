## CHAPTER V.

14 The trespass-offering in sacrilege, 17 and in sins of ignorance.

AND if <sup>a</sup>a soul sin, and hear the voice of <sup>b</sup>swearing, and *is* a witness, whether he hath seen or known *of it;* if he do not utter *it,* then he shall bear his iniquity:

2 Or if a soul touch any <sup>c</sup>unclean thing, whether *it be* a <sup>d</sup>carcase of an unclean beast, or a carcase of unclean cattle, or the carcase of unclean creeping things, and *if* it be hidden from him; he also shall be <sup>e</sup>unclean, and guilty:

3 Or if he touch the uncleanness of man, <sup>f</sup>whatsoever uncleanness *it be* that a man shall be defiled withal, and it be hid from him; when he knoweth *of it,* then he shall be guilty:

4 Or if a soul swear, pronouncing with *his* lips <sup>g</sup>to do evil, or to do good, whatsoever *it be* that a man shall pronounce with an oath, and it be hid from him; when he knoweth *of it,* then he shall be guilty in one of these.

5 And it shall be, when he shall be guilty in one of these *things,* that he shall <sup>h</sup>confess that he hath sinned in that *thing.*

6 And he shall bring his <sup>i</sup>trespass-offering unto the LORD, for his sin which he hath sinned, <sup>k</sup>a female from the flock, a lamb or a kid of the goats, for a sin-offering; and the priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his sin.

7 And if he be <sup>†</sup>not able to bring a lamb, then he shall bring, for his trespass which he hath committed, two turtle-doves, or two <sup>l</sup>young pigeons, unto the LORD; one for a sin-offering, and the other for a burnt-offering.

8 And he shall bring them unto the priest, who shall offer *that* which *is* for the sin-offering <sup>m</sup>first, and wring off his head from his neck, but shall not divide *it* asunder:

9 And he shall sprinkle of the blood of the sin-offering <sup>n</sup>upon the side of the altar; and the rest of the blood shall be wrung out at the bottom of the altar: it *is* a sin-offering.

10 And he shall offer the second *for* a burnt-offering, according to the manner: and the priest shall make <sup>o</sup>an atonement for him, for his sin which he hath sinned, and it shall be forgiven him.

11 But if he be not able to bring two turtle-doves, or two young pigeons, then he that sinned shall bring for his offering the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour, for a sin-offering: he shall put <sup>p</sup>no oil upon it, neither shall he put *any* frankincense thereon; for it *is* a sin-offering.

12 Then shall he bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take his handful of it, *even* <sup>q</sup>a memorial thereof, and burn *it* on the altar, according to the offerings <sup>r</sup>made by fire unto the LORD: it *is* a sin-offering.

13 And the <sup>s</sup>priest shall make an atonement for him, as touching his sin that he hath sinned in one of these, and it shall be forgiven him: and *the remnant* shall be the priest's, as a meat-offering.

14 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

15 If a soul commit a trespass, and sin <sup>t</sup>through ignorance, in the <sup>u</sup>holy things of the LORD; then he shall bring, for <sup>x</sup>his trespass unto the LORD, a ram <sup>y</sup>without blemish out of the flocks, with <sup>z</sup>thy estimation by shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary, for a trespass-offering:

16 And he shall make amends for the harm that he hath done in the <sup>a</sup>holy thing, and shall add the <sup>b</sup>fifth part thereto, and give it unto the priest: and the priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of the trespass-offering, and it shall be forgiven him.

17 And if a soul sin, and commit any of these things which are forbidden to be done by the commandments of the LORD; though he <sup>d</sup>wist *it* not, yet is he guilty, and shall bear his iniquity.

18 And he shall bring a ram without blemish out of the flock, with thy estimation, for a trespass-offering, unto the priest: and the priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his <sup>e</sup>ignorance wherein he erred, and wist *it* not; and it shall be forgiven him.

19 It *is* <sup>f</sup>a trespass-offering: he hath certainly trespassed against the LORD.

## CHAPTER VI.

1 The trespass-offering for sins done wittingly....19 The offering at the consecration of a priest....24 The law of the sin-offering.

AND the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2 If a soul sin, and commit a <sup>a</sup>trespass <sup>b</sup>against the LORD, and <sup>c</sup>lie unto his neighbour in that which was <sup>d</sup>delivered him to keep, or in fellowship, or in a thing taken away <sup>†</sup>by violence, or hath <sup>e</sup>deceived his neighbour;

3 Or have found that which was <sup>f</sup>lost, and lieth concerning it, and sweareth falsely; in any of all these that a man doeth, sinning therein:

4 Then it shall be, because he hath sinned, and is guilty, that he shall <sup>g</sup>restore that which he took violently away, or the thing which he hath deceitfully gotten, or that which was delivered him to keep, or the lost thing which he found,

5 Or all that about which he hath sworn falsely; he shall even restore it in the principal, and shall add the <sup>h</sup>fifth part more thereto, *and* give it unto him to whom it appertaineth, <sup>i</sup>in the day of his trespass-offering.

6 And he shall bring his <sup>k</sup>trespass-offering unto the LORD, a ram without blemish out of the flock, with thy estimation, for a trespass-offering, unto the priest:

7 And the priest shall make <sup>l</sup>an atonement for him before the LORD: and it shall be forgiven him for any thing of all that he hath done in trespassing therein.

8 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

9 Command Aaron and his sons, saying, This *is* <sup>m</sup>the law of the burnt-offering: (it *is* the burnt-offering, because of the burning upon the altar all <sup>n</sup>night unto the morning, and the fire of the altar shall be burning in it.)

10 And the priest shall put on his <sup>o</sup>linen garment, and his linen breeches shall he put upon <sup>p</sup>his flesh, and take up the ashes which the fire hath <sup>q</sup>consumed with the burnt-offering on the altar, and he shall put them beside the altar.

11 And he shall put off his garments, and put <sup>r</sup>on other garments, and carry forth the ashes <sup>s</sup>without the camp, unto a clean place.

12 And the fire upon the altar shall be burning in it; it shall <sup>t</sup>not be put out: and the priest shall burn wood on it every morning, and lay the burnt-offering in order upon it, and he shall burn thereon the fat of the peace-offerings.

13 The fire shall <sup>u</sup>ever be burning upon the altar; it shall never go out.
