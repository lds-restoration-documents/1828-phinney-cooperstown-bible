## CHAPTER XV.

The rejection of Jerusalem foreshewn.

AND the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

2 Son of man, what is the <sup>a</sup>vine-tree more than any tree, or *than* a branch which is among the trees of the forest?

3 Shall wood be taken thereof <sup>b</sup>to do any work? or will *men* take <sup>c</sup>a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon?

4 Behold, it is cast into the fire for fuel; the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the midst of it is burned. Is it meet for *any* work?

5 Behold, when it was whole it was meet for no work: <sup>d</sup>how much less shall it be meet yet for *any* work when the fire hath devoured it, and it is burned?

6 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, <sup>e</sup>As the vine-tree among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so will I give the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

7 And I will <sup>f</sup>set my face against them; they shall go out from <sup>g</sup>*one* fire, and *another* fire shall devour them; and ye shall know that I *am* the LORD, when I set my face against them.

8 And I will make the land <sup>h</sup>desolate, because they have committed a trespass, saith the Lord GOD.
