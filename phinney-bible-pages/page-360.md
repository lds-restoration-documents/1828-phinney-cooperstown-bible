## CHAPTER XXXVIII.

God, by his mighty works, convinceth Job of ignorance.

15 And from the wicked their <sup>r</sup>light is withholden, and the high <sup>s</sup>arm shall be broken.

22 Hast thou entered into the treasures of the snow, or hast thou seen the <sup>z</sup>treasures of the hail,

27 To satisfy the desolate and waste *ground,* and to cause the bud of the tender herb to spring forth?

28 Hath the rain <sup>e</sup>a father? or who hath begotten the drops of dew?

29 Out of whose womb came the ice? and the <sup>f</sup>hoary frost of heaven, who hath gendered it?

31 Canst thou bind the sweet influences of <sup>h</sup>Pleiades, or loose the bands of Orion?

32 Canst thou bring forth Mazzaroth in his season? or canst thou guide Arcturus with his sons?

33 Knowest thou the <sup>i</sup>ordinances of heaven? canst thou set the <sup>k</sup>dominion thereof in the earth?

34 Canst thou lift up thy voice to the <sup>l</sup>clouds, that <sup>m</sup>abundance of waters may cover thee?

35 Canst thou send lightnings, that they may go, and say unto thee, Here we *are?*

36 Who hath put wisdom in the <sup>n</sup>inward parts? or who hath <sup>o</sup>given understanding to the heart?

37 Who can number the clouds in wisdom? or who can stay the bottles of heaven,

38 When the dust groweth into hardness, and the clods cleave fast together?

39 Wilt thou <sup>p</sup>hunt the prey for the lion? or fill the appetite of the young lions,

40 When they couch in *their* dens, *and* abide in the covert to lie in wait?

41 Who provideth for the raven his food? when his <sup>q</sup>young ones cry unto God, they wander for lack of meat.

## CHAPTER XXXIX.

Of the wild goats and hinds, &c.

KNOWEST thou the time when the <sup>a</sup>wild goats of the rock bring forth? *or* canst thou mark when the <sup>b</sup>hinds do calve?

2 Canst thou number the <sup>c</sup>months *that* they fulfil; or knowest thou the time when they bring forth?

3 They bow themselves, they bring forth their young ones, they cast out <sup>d</sup>their sorrows.

4 Their young ones are in good liking, they grow up with corn; they go forth, and <sup>e</sup>return not unto them.

5 Who hath sent out the <sup>f</sup>wild ass free? or who hath loosed <sup>g</sup>the bands of the wild ass?

6 Whose house I have made the <sup>h</sup>wilderness, and the barren land his dwellings.

7 He scorneth the multitude of the city, neither regardeth he the crying of the driver.

8 The range of the mountains *is* his pasture, and he searcheth after every <sup>i</sup>green thing.

9 Will the <sup>k</sup>unicorn be willing to serve thee, or abide by thy crib?

10 Canst thou <sup>l</sup>bind the unicorn with his band in the furrow? or will he harrow the valleys after thee?

11 Wilt thou trust him, because his strength *is* great? or wilt thou <sup>m</sup>leave thy labour to him?

12 Wilt thou believe him, that he will bring home thy seed, and <sup>n</sup>gather *it into* thy barn?

13 *Gavest thou* the goodly wings unto the peacocks? or wings and feathers unto the ostrich?
