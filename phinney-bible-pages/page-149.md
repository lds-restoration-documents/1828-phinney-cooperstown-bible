## CHAPTER XXIII.

9 Uncleanness to be avoided in the host....19 Of usury....21 Of vows....24 Of trespasses.

HE that is wounded in the stones, or hath his privy member cut off, shall <sup>||</sup>not enter into the congregation of the LORD.

2 <sup>a</sup>A bastard shall not enter into the congregation of the LORD; even to his tenth generation shall he not enter into the congregation of the LORD.

3 An <sup>b</sup>Ammonite or Moabite shall not enter into the congregation of the LORD; even to their tenth generation shall they not enter into the congregation of the LORD <sup>c</sup>for ever:

4 Because they <sup>d</sup>met you not with bread and with water in the way, when ye came forth out of Egypt; and because they hired against thee Balaam the son of Beor, of Pethor of Mesopotamia, to <sup>e</sup>curse thee.

5 Nevertheless the LORD thy God would not hearken unto Balaam; but the LORD thy God turned the curse into a blessing unto thee, because the LORD thy God loved thee.

6 Thou shalt <sup>f</sup>not seek their peace nor their prosperity all thy days for ever.

7 Thou shalt not abhor an <sup>g</sup>Edomite; for he *is* thy <sup>h</sup>brother: thou shalt not abhor an Egyptian, because thou wast a stranger in his land.

8 The children that are begotten of them shall enter into the congregation of the LORD in their <sup>i</sup>third generation.

9 ¶ When the host goeth forth against thine enemies, then keep thee from every <sup>k</sup>wicked thing.

10 If there be among you any man that is not clean by reason of uncleanness that chanceth him <sup>l</sup>by night, then shall he go abroad out of the <sup>m</sup>camp, he shall not come within the camp:

11 But it shall be, when evening cometh on, he shall <sup>n</sup>wash *himself* with water: and when the sun is down, he shall come into the camp *again.*

12 Thou shalt have a place also without the camp, whither thou shalt go forth abroad:

13 And thou shalt have a paddle upon thy weapon; and it shall be, when thou wilt ease thyself abroad, thou shalt dig therewith, and shalt turn back and cover that which <sup>o</sup>cometh from thee.

14 For the LORD thy God <sup>p</sup>walketh in the midst of thy camp, to deliver thee, and to give up thine enemies <sup>q</sup>before thee; therefore shall thy camp be holy; that he see no unclean thing in thee, and <sup>r</sup>turn away from thee.

15 ¶ Thou shalt not deliver unto his master the servant which is <sup>s</sup>escaped from his master unto thee:

16 He shall dwell with thee, *even* among you, in that place which he shall choose in one of thy gates, where it liketh him best: thou shalt <sup>t</sup>not oppress him.

17 There shall be no <sup>u</sup>whore of the daughters of Israel, nor a <sup>x</sup>sodomite of the sons of Israel.

18 Thou shalt not bring the <sup>y</sup>hire of a <sup>z</sup>whore, or the price of <sup>a</sup>a dog, into the house of the LORD thy God for any vow: for even <sup>b</sup>both these *are* abomination unto the LORD thy God.

19 ¶ Thou shalt not lend upon usury to thy brother; <sup>c</sup>usury of money, usury of victuals, usury of any thing that is lent upon usury.

20 Unto a stranger <sup>d</sup>thou mayest lend upon usury; but unto thy brother thou shalt not lend upon usury: that the LORD thy God may bless thee in all that thou settest thine hand to in the land whither thou goest to possess it.

21 ¶ When thou shalt <sup>e</sup>vow a vow unto the LORD thy God, thou shalt not slack to <sup>f</sup>pay it: for the LORD thy God will surely require it of thee; and it would be <sup>g</sup>sin in thee.

22 But if thou shalt <sup>h</sup>forbear to vow, it shall be <sup>i</sup>no sin in thee.

23 That which is gone out of thy lips thou shalt <sup>k</sup>keep and perform; *even* a <sup>l</sup>free-will-offering, according as thou hast vowed unto the LORD thy God, which thou hast promised <sup>m</sup>with thy mouth.

24 ¶ When thou <sup>n</sup>comest into thy neighbour's vineyard, then thou mayest eat grapes thy fill at thine own pleasure; but thou shalt not put *any* in thy vessel.

25 When thou comest into the standing corn of thy neighbour, then thou mayest pluck the ears <sup>o</sup>with thine hand; but thou shalt not move a sickle unto thy neighbour's standing corn.

## CHAPTER XXIV.

1 Of divorce....6, 10 Of pledges....7 Of man-stealers....14 The hire is to be given....16 Of justice....19 Of charity.

WHEN a man hath taken a wife, and <sup>a</sup>married her, and it come to pass that she find no favour in his eyes, because he hath found some <sup>†</sup>uncleanness in her; <sup>||</sup>then let him write her a bill of <sup>†</sup>divorcement, and give *it* in her hand, and send her out of his house.

2 And when she is departed out of his house, she may go and be <sup>b</sup>another man's *wife.*

3 And *if* the latter husband hate her, and write her a bill of divorcement, and giveth *it* in her hand, and sendeth her out of his house; or if the latter husband die, which took her *to be* his wife;

4 Her former husband, which sent her away, may <sup>c</sup>not take her again to be his wife, after that she is defiled; for that *is* abomination before the LORD; and thou shalt not <sup>d</sup>cause the land to sin, which the LORD thy God giveth thee *for* an inheritance.

5 ¶ When a man hath taken a new wife, he shall <sup>e</sup>not go out to war, neither shall he be charged with any business: *but* he shall be free at home one year, and shall <sup>f</sup>cheer up his wife which he hath taken.

6 ¶ No man shall take the nether or the upper <sup>g</sup>millstone to pledge: for he taketh *a man's* life to pledge.

7 ¶ If a man be found stealing <sup>h</sup>any of his brethren of the children of Israel, and maketh <sup>i</sup>merchandise of him, or selleth him; then that thief shall die; and thou shalt put evil away from among you.
