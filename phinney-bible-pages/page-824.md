4 <sup>d</sup>Though I might also have confidence in the flesh. If any other man thinketh that he hath whereof he might trust in the flesh, I more:

5 Circumcised the eighth day, of the stock of Israel, *of* the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of the Hebrews; as touching the law, a Pharisee;

6 <sup>e</sup>Concerning zeal, persecuting the church; touching the righteousness which is in the law, blameless.

7 <sup>f</sup>But what things were gain to me, those I counted loss for Christ.

8 <sup>g</sup>Yea doubtless, and I count all things *but* loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord: for whom I have suffered the loss of all things, and do count them *but* dung, that I may win Christ,

9 <sup>h</sup>And be found in him, not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith:

10 <sup>i</sup>That I may know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, being made conformable unto his death;

11 <sup>k</sup>If by any means I might attain unto the resurrection of the dead.

12 <sup>l</sup>Not as though I had already attained, either were already perfect: but I follow after, if that I may apprehend that for which also I am apprehended of Christ Jesus.

13 Brethren, I count not myself to have apprehended: but *this* one thing *I do,* forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before,

14 <sup>m</sup>I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.

15 <sup>n</sup>Let us therefore, as many as be perfect, be thus minded: and if in any thing ye be otherwise minded, God shall reveal even this unto you.

16 <sup>o</sup>Nevertheless, whereto we have already attained, let us walk by the same rule, let us mind the same thing.

17 <sup>p</sup>Brethren, be followers together of me, and mark them which walk so, as ye have us for an ensample.

18 <sup>q</sup>(For many walk, of whom I have told you often, and now tell you even weeping, *that they are* the enemies of the cross of Christ:

19 <sup>r</sup>Whose end *is* destruction, whose God *is their* belly, and *whose* glory *is* in their shame, who mind earthly things.)

20 <sup>s</sup>For our conversation is in heaven; from whence also we look for the Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ:

21 <sup>t</sup>Who shall change our vile body, that it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body, according to the working whereby he is able even to subdue all things unto himself.

## CHAPTER IV.

4 General exhortations; 10 his joy for their liberality toward him, and God's grace in them.

<sup>a</sup>THEREFORE, my brethren dearly beloved and longed for, my joy and crown, so stand fast in the Lord, *my* dearly beloved.

2 <sup>b</sup>I beseech Euodias, and beseech Syntyche, that they be of the same mind in the Lord.

3 <sup>c</sup>And I entreat thee also, true yoke-fellow, help those women which laboured with me in the gospel, with Clement also, and *with* other my fellow-labourers, whose names *are* in the book of life.

4 <sup>d</sup>Rejoice in the Lord always: *and* again I say, Rejoice.

5 <sup>e</sup>Let your moderation be known unto all men: The Lord *is* at hand.

6 <sup>f</sup>Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your request be made known unto God:

7 <sup>g</sup>And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

8 <sup>h</sup>Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things *are* honest, whatsoever things *are* just, whatsoever things *are* pure, whatsoever things *are* lovely, whatsoever things *are* of good report; if *there be* any virtue, and if *there be* any praise, think on these things.

9 <sup>i</sup>Those things, which ye have both learned, and received, and heard, and seen in me, do: and the God of peace shall be with you.

10 <sup>k</sup>But I rejoiced in the Lord greatly, that now at the last your care of me hath flourished again; wherein ye were also careful, but ye lacked opportunity.

11 <sup>l</sup>Not that I speak in respect of want: for I have learned, in whatsoever state I am, *therewith* to be content.

12 <sup>m</sup>I know both how to be abased, and I know how to abound: every where, and in all things, I am instructed, both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to suffer need.

13 <sup>n</sup>I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.

14 <sup>o</sup>Notwithstanding, ye have well done that ye did communicate with my affliction.

15 <sup>p</sup>Now, ye Philippians, know also, that in the beginning of the gospel, when I departed from Macedonia, no church communicated with me as concerning giving and receiving, but ye only.

16 For even in Thessalonica ye sent once and again unto my necessity.

17 <sup>q</sup>Not because I desire a gift; but I desire fruit that may abound to your account.

18 <sup>r</sup>But I have all, and abound: I am full, having received of Epaphroditus the things *which were sent* from you, an odour of a sweet smell, a sacrifice acceptable, well-pleasing to God.

19 <sup>s</sup>But my God shall supply all your need, according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus.

20 Now unto God and our Father *be* glory for ever and ever. Amen.

21 Salute every saint in Christ Jesus. The brethren which are with me greet you.

22 <sup>t</sup>All the saints salute you, chiefly they that are of Cesar's household.

23 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ *be* with you all. Amen.

¶ It was written to the Philippians from Rome, by Epaphroditus.
