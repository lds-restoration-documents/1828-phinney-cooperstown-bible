## PSALM LXXIX.

The Psalmist complaineth of the desolation of Jerusalem.

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

O GOD, the heathen are come into <sup>a</sup>thine inheritance; thy <sup>b</sup>holy temple have they defiled; they have laid Jerusalem on <sup>c</sup>heaps.

2 The dead bodies of thy servants have they given *to be* meat unto the <sup>d</sup>fowls of the heaven, the flesh of thy saints unto the beasts of the earth.

3 Their blood have they shed like water round about Jerusalem; and *there was* none <sup>e</sup>to bury *them.*

4 We are become <sup>f</sup>a reproach to our neighbours, a scorn and derision to them that are round about us.

5 How long, LORD? wilt thou be angry for ever? shall thy <sup>g</sup>jealousy burn like fire?

6 <sup>h</sup>Pour out thy wrath upon the heathen that have <sup>i</sup>not known thee, and upon the kingdoms that <sup>k</sup>have not called upon thy name:

7 For they have devoured Jacob, and laid waste his dwelling-place.

8 O remember not against us <sup>l</sup>former iniquities: let thy tender mercies speedily prevent us: for we are brought <sup>m</sup>very low.

9 Help us, O God of <sup>n</sup>our salvation, for the glory of thy name; and deliver us, and purge away our sins, for <sup>o</sup>thy name's sake.

10 Wherefore should the heathen say, <sup>p</sup>Where *is* their God? let him be known among the heathen in our sight, *by* the revenging of <sup>q</sup>the blood of thy servants *which is* shed.

11 Let the <sup>r</sup>sighing of the prisoner come before thee; according to the greatness of thy power preserve thou those that are <sup>s</sup>appointed to die:

12 And render unto our neighbours seven-fold into their <sup>t</sup>bosom their reproach, wherewith they have reproached thee, O LORD.

13 So we thy people, and sheep of thy pasture, will give thee thanks for ever; we will <sup>†</sup>shew forth thy praise to all generations.
