8 Give <sup>l</sup>thanks unto the LORD, call upon his name, <sup>m</sup>make known his deeds among the people.

9 Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him, talk <sup>n</sup>ye of his wondrous works.

10 Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the LORD.

11 Seek the LORD and <sup>o</sup>his strength, seek his <sup>p</sup>face continually.

12 Remember <sup>q</sup>his marvellous works that he hath done, his wonders, and the <sup>r</sup>judgments of his mouth;

14 He *is* the LORD our God; his judgments *are* in all the <sup>u</sup>earth.

15 Be ye <sup>x</sup>mindful always of his covenant, the word *which* he commanded to a thousand generations;

16 *Even of the* <sup>y</sup>*covenant* which he made with Abraham, and of his oath unto Isaac;

17 And hath <sup>z</sup>confirmed the same to Jacob for a law, *and* to Israel *for* an everlasting covenant,

18 Saying, Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance;

19 When ye were but few, even a few, and <sup>a</sup>strangers in it.

20 And *when* they went from <sup>b</sup>nation to nation, and from *one* kingdom to another people;

21 He suffered <sup>c</sup>no man to do them wrong; yea, he <sup>d</sup>reproved kings for their sakes,

22 *Saying*, Touch not mine <sup>e</sup>anointed, and do my prophets no harm.

23 Sing unto the LORD all the earth; shew forth from day to day his salvation.

24 Declare his glory among the heathen; his marvellous works among all nations.

25 For great *is* the LORD, and greatly to be praised: he also *is* to be feared <sup>f</sup>above all gods.

26 For all the gods of the people *are* idols: but the LORD <sup>g</sup>made the heavens.

27 Glory <sup>h</sup>and honour *are* in his presence; strength and gladness *are* in his place.

28 Give unto the LORD, ye kindreds of the people, give unto the LORD <sup>i</sup>glory and strength.

29 Give unto the LORD the glory *due* unto his name: bring an offering, and come before him; worship the LORD in the beauty of holiness.

30 Fear before him, all the earth: the world also shall be <sup>k</sup>stable, that it be not moved.

31 Let the <sup>l</sup>heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice: and let *men* say among the nations, The LORD <sup>m</sup>reigneth.

32 Let the sea <sup>†</sup>roar, and the fulness thereof; let the fields rejoice, and all that *is* therein.

33 Then shall the trees of the wood sing out at the presence of the LORD, because he cometh to judge the earth.

34 O give <sup>n</sup>thanks unto the LORD; for *he is* good: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

35 And <sup>o</sup>say ye, Save us, O God of our salvation, and <sup>p</sup>gather us together, and deliver us from the heathen, that we may give thanks to thy holy name, *and* glory in thy praise.

36 Blessed <sup>q</sup>*be* the LORD God of Israel for ever and ever. And all the people said, <sup>r</sup>Amen, and praised the LORD.
