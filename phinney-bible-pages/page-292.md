## CHAPTER XXI.

David, tempted by Satan, forceth Joab to number the people.

AND <sup>a</sup>Satan stood up against Israel, and <sup>b</sup>provoked David to number Israel.

2 And David said to Joab, and to the rulers of the people, Go number Israel, from <sup>c</sup>Beer-sheba even to Dan; and bring the number of them to me, that I may know *it.*

3 And Joab answered, the LORD <sup>†</sup>make his people a hundred times so many more as they *be:* but, my lord the king, *are* they not all my lord's servants? why then doth my lord require this thing? why will he be a cause of trespass to Israel?

4 <sup>d</sup>Nevertheless the king's word prevailed against Joab: wherefore Joab departed, and went <sup>e</sup>throughout all Israel, and came to Jerusalem.

5 ¶ And Joab gave the sum of the number of the people unto David. And all *they of* Israel were <sup>f</sup>a thousand thousand and a hundred thousand men that drew sword: and Judah *was* four hundred threescore and ten thousand men that drew sword.

6 But Levi and Benjamin counted he not among them; for the king's word was <sup>g</sup>abominable to Joab.

7 And God was displeased with this thing; therefore he <sup>h</sup>smote Israel.

8 And David said unto God, I have <sup>i</sup>sinned greatly, because I have done this thing: but now, I beseech thee, do away the iniquity of thy servant; for I have done very foolishly. <!-- uncertain: superscript mark after "have" is smudged -->

9 And the LORD spake unto Gad, David's <sup>k</sup>seer, saying,

10 Go and tell David, saying, <sup>l</sup>Thus saith the LORD, I offer thee three *things:* <sup>m</sup>choose thee one of them, that I may do *it* unto thee.

11 So Gad <sup>n</sup>came to David, and said unto him, Thus saith the LORD, Choose thee

12 Either three years' <sup>o</sup>famine; or three months to <sup>p</sup>be destroyed before thy foes, while that the sword of thine enemies overtaketh *thee;* or else three days the sword of the LORD, even the pestilence, in the land, and the angel of the LORD destroying throughout all the coasts of Israel. Now, therefore, advise thyself what word I shall bring again to him that sent me.

13 And David said unto Gad, I am in a great strait: let me fall now into the hand of the LORD; for <sup>||</sup>very great *are* his mercies: but let me <sup>q</sup>not fall into the hand of man.

14 So the LORD sent pestilence upon Israel: and there fell of Israel <sup>r</sup>seventy thousand men.

15 And God sent an angel unto Jerusalem to destroy it: and as he was destroying, the LORD beheld, and he <sup>s</sup>repented him of the evil, and said to the angel that destroyed, It is enough, stay now thine hand. And the angel of the LORD stood by the threshing-floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

16 And David lifted up his eyes, and saw the angel of the LORD stand between the earth and the heaven, having <sup>t</sup>a drawn sword in his hand stretched out over Jerusalem: then David and the elders *of Israel, who were* <sup>u</sup>clothed in sackcloth, <sup>x</sup>fell upon their faces.

17 And David said unto God, *Is it* not I *that* commanded the people to be numbered? even I it is that have sinned and done evil indeed: but *as for* these <sup>y</sup>sheep, what have they done? Let thy hand, I pray thee, O LORD my God, be on me, and on my father's house; but not on thy people, that they should be plagued.

18 Then the <sup>z</sup>angel of the LORD commanded <sup>a</sup>Gad to say to David, that David should go up, and set up an altar unto the LORD in the threshing-floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

19 And David went up at <sup>b</sup>the saying of Gad, which he spake in the name of the LORD.

20 And Ornan turned back, and saw the angel; and his four sons with him <sup>c</sup>hid themselves. Now Ornan was <sup>d</sup>threshing wheat.

21 And as David came to Ornan, Ornan looked, and <sup>e</sup>saw David, and went out of the threshing-floor, and <sup>f</sup>bowed himself to David with *his* face to the ground.

22 Then David said to Ornan, <sup>†</sup>Grant me the place of *this* threshing-floor, that I may build an altar therein unto the LORD; thou shalt grant it me for the full price, that the plague may be stayed from the people.

23 And Ornan said unto David, Take *it* to thee, and let my lord the king do *that which is* good in his eyes: lo, I give *thee* the oxen *also* for burnt-offerings, and the <sup>g</sup>threshing instruments for wood, and the wheat for the meat-offering: I give it all.

24 And king David said to Ornan, Nay; but I will verily <sup>h</sup>buy it for the full price: for I will not take *that* which *is* thine for the LORD, nor offer burnt-offerings without cost.

25 So David gave to Ornan for the place <sup>i</sup>six hundred shekels of gold by weight.

26 And David built there an altar unto the LORD, and offered burnt-offerings and peace-offerings, and called upon the LORD; and he answered him from heaven <sup>k</sup>by fire upon the altar of burnt-offering.

27 And the LORD commanded the angel; and he <sup>l</sup>put up his sword again into the sheath thereof.

28 At that time, when David saw that the LORD had answered him in the threshing-floor of Ornan the Jebusite, then he sacrificed <sup>m</sup>there.

29 For the <sup>n</sup>tabernacle of the LORD, which Moses made in the wilderness, and the <sup>o</sup>altar of the burnt-offering, *were* at that season in the high place at <sup>p</sup>Gibeon:

30 But David could not go before it to inquire of God: for he was <sup>q</sup>afraid, because of the sword of the angel of the LORD.

## CHAPTER XXII.

David instructeth Solomon in God's promises, and his duty in building the temple.

THEN David said, <sup>a</sup>This *is* the house of the LORD God, and this *is* the altar of the burnt-offering for Israel.

2 And David commanded to gather together the <sup>b</sup>strangers that *were* in the land of Israel; and he set masons to hew wrought stones to build the house of God.

3 And David prepared iron in abundance for the nails for the doors of the gates, and for the joinings; and brass in abundance <sup>c</sup>without weight;

4 Also cedar-trees in abundance: for the <sup>d</sup>Zidonians and they <sup>e</sup>of Tyre brought much cedar-wood to David.

5 And David said, <sup>f</sup>Solomon my son *is* <sup>g</sup>young and tender, and the house *that is* to be builded for the LORD *must be* exceeding magnifical, of fame and of glory <sup>h</sup>throughout all countries: I will *therefore* now make preparation for it. So David prepared abundantly before his death.
