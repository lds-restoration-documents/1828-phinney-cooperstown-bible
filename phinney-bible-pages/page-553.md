## CHAPTER V.

1 God's judgments against the priests, the people, and the princes of Israel, for their manifold sins.

HEAR ye this, <sup>a</sup>O priests; and hearken, ye house of Israel; and give ye ear, O house of the king; for judgment *is* toward you, because ye have been <sup>b</sup>a snare on Mizpah, and a net spread upon Tabor.

2 And the revolters are <sup>c</sup>profound to make slaughter, though I *have been* <sup>d</sup>a rebuker of them all.

3 I know Ephraim, and Israel is <sup>e</sup>not hid from me: for now, O Ephraim, thou committest <sup>f</sup>whoredom, *and* Israel is defiled.

4 They will <sup>g</sup>not frame their doings to turn unto their God: for the spirit of whoredoms *is* in the midst of them, and they have not known the LORD.

5 And the pride of Israel doth <sup>h</sup>testify to his face: therefore shall Israel and Ephraim fall in their iniquity; Judah also shall fall with them.

6 They shall go with their flocks and with their herds to seek the LORD; but they shall <sup>i</sup>not find *him:* he hath withdrawn himself from them.

7 They have dealt treacherously against the LORD; for they have begotten <sup>k</sup>strange children: now shall <sup>l</sup>a month devour them with their portions.

8 <sup>m</sup>Blow ye the cornet in Gibeah, *and* the trumpet in Ramah: cry aloud *at* <sup>n</sup>Beth-aven, after thee, O Benjamin.

9 Ephraim shall be desolate in the <sup>o</sup>day of rebuke: among the tribes of Israel have I made known that which shall <sup>p</sup>surely be.

10 The princes of Judah were like them that remove the <sup>q</sup>bound: *therefore* I will pour out my wrath upon them like water.

11 Ephraim *is* oppressed *and* broken in judgment, because he willingly walked after the <sup>r</sup>commandment.

12 Therefore *will* I *be* unto Ephraim as <sup>s</sup>a moth, and to the house of Judah as rottenness.

13 When Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah *saw* his <sup>t</sup>wound, then went Ephraim to the <sup>u</sup>Assyrian, and sent to king Jareb: yet could he not heal you, nor cure you of your wound.

14 For I *will be* unto Ephraim as <sup>x</sup>a lion, and as a young lion to the house of Judah: I, *even* I, will tear and go away; I will take away, and none shall rescue *him.*

15 I will go *and* return to my place till they <sup>y</sup>acknowledge their offence, and seek my face; in their affliction they will <sup>z</sup>seek me early.

## CHAPTER VI.

1 An exhortation to repentance....4 A complaint of their untowardness and iniquity.

COME, and <sup>a</sup>let us return unto the LORD: for he hath torn, and he will heal us: he hath smitten, and he will bind us up.

2 <sup>b</sup>After two days will he revive us; in the third day he will raise us up, and we shall <sup>c</sup>live in his sight.

3 Then shall we know, *if* we <sup>d</sup>follow on to know the LORD: his <sup>e</sup>going forth is prepared as the morning; and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter *and* former rain unto the earth.

4 O Ephraim, <sup>f</sup>what shall I do unto thee? O Judah, what shall I do unto thee? for your goodness *is* as <sup>g</sup>a morning cloud, and as the early dew it goeth away.

5 Therefore have I <sup>h</sup>hewed *them* by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of my mouth: and thy judgments *are as* the light *that* goeth forth.

6 For I desired mercy, and <sup>i</sup>not sacrifice; and the <sup>k</sup>knowledge of God more than burnt-offerings.

7 But they, like men, have <sup>l</sup>transgressed the covenant: <sup>m</sup>there have they dealt treacherously against me.

8 Gilead *is* <sup>n</sup>a city of them that work iniquity, *and is* polluted with blood.

9 And as troops of robbers wait for a man, *so* the <sup>o</sup>company of priests murder in the way by consent: for they commit lewdness.

10 I have seen <sup>p</sup>a horrible thing in the house of Israel: there *is* the whoredom of Ephraim, Israel is defiled.

11 Also, O Judah, he hath set <sup>q</sup>a harvest for thee, when I <sup>r</sup>returned the captivity of my people.

## CHAPTER VII.

1 A reproof of manifold sins....11 God's wrath against the people for their hypocrisy.

WHEN I would have <sup>a</sup>healed Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim was discovered, and the wickedness of Samaria: for they commit falsehood; and the <sup>b</sup>thief cometh in, *and* the troop of robbers spoileth without:

2 And they consider not in their hearts *that* <sup>c</sup>I remember all their wickedness: now their own doings have beset them about; they are <sup>d</sup>before my face.

3 They make the king glad with their wickedness, and the princes with their <sup>e</sup>lies.

4 They *are* all adulterers, <sup>f</sup>as an oven heated by the baker, *who* ceaseth from raising after he hath kneaded the dough until it be <sup>g</sup>leavened.

5 In the <sup>h</sup>day of our king the princes have made *him* sick with <sup>i</sup>bottles of wine: he stretched out his hand with scorners.
