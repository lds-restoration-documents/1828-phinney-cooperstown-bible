**Header:** *Of newness of life.* | CHAPTER VI....VII....VIII. | *The law is holy, just, and good.*

3 <sup>c</sup>Know ye not, that so many of us as were baptized into Jesus Christ were baptized into his death?

4 <sup>d</sup>Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death; that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life.

5 <sup>e</sup>For if we have been planted together in the likeness of his death, we shall be also *in the likeness* of *his* resurrection:

6 <sup>f</sup>Knowing this, that our old man is crucified with *him,* that the body of sin might be destroyed, that henceforth we should not serve sin.

7 <sup>g</sup>For he that is dead is freed from sin.

8 <sup>h</sup>Now if we be dead with Christ, we believe that we shall also live with him:

9 <sup>i</sup>Knowing that Christ, being raised from the dead, dieth no more; death hath no more dominion over him.

10 <sup>k</sup>For in that he died, he died unto sin once: but in that he liveth, he liveth unto God.

11 <sup>l</sup>Likewise reckon ye also yourselves to be dead indeed unto sin, but alive unto God through Jesus Christ our Lord.

12 <sup>m</sup>Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body, that ye should obey it in the lusts thereof.

13 <sup>n</sup>Neither yield ye your members *as* instruments of unrighteousness unto sin: but yield yourselves unto God, as those that are alive from the dead, and your members *as* <sup>†</sup>instruments of righteousness unto God.

14 <sup>o</sup>For sin shall not have dominion over you: for ye are not under the law, but under grace.

15 <sup>p</sup>What then? Shall we sin, because we are not under the law, but under grace? God forbid.

16 <sup>q</sup>Know ye not, that to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are to whom ye obey; whether of sin unto death, or of obedience unto righteousness?

17 <sup>r</sup>But God be thanked, that ye were the servants of sin: but ye have obeyed from the heart that form of doctrine which was delivered you.

18 <sup>s</sup>Being then made free from sin, ye became the servants of righteousness.

19 I speak after the manner of men, because of the infirmity of your flesh: for as ye have yielded your members servants to uncleanness and to iniquity, unto iniquity: even so now yield your members servants to righteousness, unto holiness.

20 <sup>t</sup>For when ye were the servants of sin, ye were free from righteousness.

21 <sup>u</sup>What fruit had ye then in those things whereof ye are now ashamed? for the end of those things *is* death.

22 <sup>x</sup>But now being made free from sin, and become servants to God, ye have your fruit unto holiness; and the end, everlasting life.

23 <sup>y</sup>For the wages of sin *is* death; but the gift of God *is* eternal life, through Jesus Christ our Lord.

## CHAPTER VII.

1 No law hath power over a man longer than he liveth....7 The law is not sin, 12 but holy, just, and good.

<sup>a</sup>KNOW ye not, brethren, (for I speak to them that know the law,) how that the law hath dominion over a man as long as he liveth?

2 <sup>b</sup>For the woman which hath a husband is bound by the law to *her* husband, so long as he liveth; but if the husband be dead, she is loosed from the law of *her* husband.

3 <sup>c</sup>So then, if, while *her* husband liveth, she be married to another man, she shall be called an adulteress: but if her husband be dead, she is free from that law; so that she is no adulteress, though she be married to another man.

4 <sup>d</sup>Wherefore, my brethren, ye also are become dead to the law by the body of Christ; that ye should be married to another, *even* to him who is raised from the dead, that we should bring forth fruit unto God.

5 <sup>e</sup>For when we were in the flesh, the motions of sins, which were by the law, did work in our members, to bring forth fruit unto death.

6 <sup>f</sup>But now we are delivered from the law, that being dead wherein we were held; that we should serve in newness of spirit, and not *in* the oldness of the letter.

7 <sup>g</sup>What shall we say then? *Is* the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law: for I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet.

8 <sup>h</sup>But sin, taking occasion by the commandment, wrought in me all manner of concupiscence. For without the law, sin *was* dead.

9 <sup>i</sup>For I was alive without the law once: but when the commandment came, sin revived, and I died.

10 <sup>k</sup>And the commandment, which *was ordained* to life, I found *to be* unto death.

11 <sup>l</sup>For sin, taking occasion by the commandment, deceived me, and by it slew *me.*

12 <sup>m</sup>Wherefore the law *is* holy; and the commandment holy, and just, and good.

13 <sup>n</sup>Was then that which is good made death unto me? God forbid. But sin, that it might appear sin, working death in me by that which is good; that sin by the commandment might become exceeding sinful.

14 <sup>o</sup>For we know that the law is spiritual; but I am carnal, sold under sin.

15 <sup>p</sup>For that which I do, I <sup>†</sup>allow not: for what I would, that do I not; but what I hate, that do I.

16 If then I do that which I would not, I consent unto the law that *it is* good.

17 Now then it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me.

18 <sup>q</sup>For I know that in me (that is, in my flesh) dwelleth no good thing: for to will is present with me; but *how* to perform that which is good I find not.

19 For the good that I would, I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do.

20 Now if I do that I would not, it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me.

21 I find then a law, that, when I would do good, evil is present with me.

22 <sup>r</sup>For I delight in the law of God after the inward man:

23 <sup>s</sup>But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity to the law of sin which is in my members.

24 O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death?

25 <sup>t</sup>I thank God, through Jesus Christ our Lord. So then with the mind I myself serve the law of God, but with the flesh the law of sin.
