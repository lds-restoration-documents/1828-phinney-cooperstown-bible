14 For Menahem the son of Gadi went up from <sup>u</sup>Tirzah, and came to Samaria, and smote Shallum the son of Jabesh in Samaria, and slew him, and <sup>x</sup>reigned in his stead.

16 Then Menahem smote <sup>y</sup>Tiphsah, and all that *were* therein, and the coast thereof from Tirzah: because they opened not *to him*, therefore he smote *it*; *and* all the <sup>z</sup>women therein that were with child he ripped up.

17 In the nine and thirtieth year of Azariah king of Judah, <sup>a</sup>began Menahem the son of Gadi to reign over Israel, *and reigned* ten years in Samaria.

18 And he did *that which was* <sup>b</sup>evil in the sight of the LORD: he departed not all his days from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel to sin.

19 *And* <sup>c</sup>Pul the king of Assyria came against the land: and Menahem gave Pul <sup>||</sup>a thousand talents of silver, that his hand might be with him, to <sup>d</sup>confirm the kingdom in his hand.

20 And Menahem exacted the money of Israel, *even* of all the mighty men of wealth, of each man fifty shekels of silver, to give to the king of Assyria: so the king of Assyria turned back, and stayed not there in the land.

21 And the rest of the acts of Menahem, and all that he did, *are* they not written in the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Israel?

22 And Menahem <sup>e</sup>slept with his fathers: and Pekahiah his son reigned in his stead.

23 In the fiftieth year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekahiah the son of Menahem began to reign over Israel in Samaria, *and reigned* <sup>f</sup>two years.

24 And he did *that which was* <sup>g</sup>evil in the sight of the LORD; he departed not from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel to sin.

25 But <sup>h</sup>Pekah the son of Remaliah, a captain of his, conspired against him, and smote him in Samaria, in the palace of the king's house, with Argob and Arieh, and with him fifty men of the Gileadites: and he killed him, and reigned in his room.

26 And the <sup>i</sup>rest of the acts of Pekahiah, and all that he did, behold, they *are* written in the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Israel.

27 In the two and fiftieth year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekah the son of Remaliah began to reign over Israel in Samaria, *and reigned* twenty years.

28 And he did *that which was* evil in the sight of the LORD; he departed not from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel to sin.

29 In the days of Pekah king of Israel, came <sup>k</sup>Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, and took <sup>l</sup>Ijon, and Abel-beth-maachah, and <sup>m</sup>Janoah, and <sup>n</sup>Kedesh, and <sup>o</sup>Hazor, and <sup>p</sup>Gilead, and <sup>q</sup>Galilee, all the land of Naphtali, and carried them <sup>r</sup>captive to Assyria.

30 And Hoshea the son of <sup>s</sup>Elah made a conspiracy against Pekah the son of Remaliah, and smote him, and slew him, and reigned in his stead, in the twentieth year of Jotham the son of Uzziah.

31 And the rest of the acts of Pekah, and all that he did, behold, they *are* written in the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Israel.

32 ¶ In the second year of Pekah the son of Remaliah king of Israel, began <sup>t</sup>Jotham the son of Uzziah king of Judah to reign.

33 Five and twenty years old was he when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name *was* Jerusha, the daughter of Zadok.

34 And he did *that which was* <sup>u</sup>right in the sight of the LORD: he did according to all that his father Uzziah had done.

35 Howbeit, the <sup>x</sup>high places were not removed: the people sacrificed and burnt incense still in the high places. He built the <sup>y</sup>higher gate of the house of the LORD.

36 Now the rest of the acts of Jotham, and all that he did, *are* they not written in the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Judah?

37 In those days the <sup>z</sup>LORD <sup>a</sup>began to send against Judah, Rezin the king of Syria, and <sup>b</sup>Pekah the son of Remaliah.

38 And <sup>||</sup>Jotham slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father: and Ahaz his son reigned in his stead.

## CHAPTER XVI.

1 Ahaz's wicked reign....19 Hezekiah succeedeth him.

IN the seventeenth year of Pekah the son of Remaliah, <sup>a</sup>Ahaz the son of Jotham king of Judah began to reign.

2 Twenty years old *was* Ahaz when he began to reign, and reigned <sup>b</sup>sixteen years in Jerusalem, and did not *that which was* right in the sight of the LORD his God, like David his father.

3 But he walked in the <sup>c</sup>way of the kings of Israel; yea, and made <sup>d</sup>his son to pass through the fire, according to the abominations of the heathen, whom the LORD cast out from before the children of Israel.

4 And he sacrificed and burnt incense in the <sup>e</sup>high places, and on the <sup>f</sup>hills, and <sup>g</sup>under every green tree.

5 Then Rezin king of Syria, and Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel, came up to Jerusalem to war: and they besieged Ahaz, but <sup>h</sup>could not overcome *him*.

6 At that time Rezin king of Syria recovered Elath to Syria, and drave the Jews from <sup>i</sup>Elath: and the <sup>k</sup>Syrians came to Elath, and dwelt there unto this day.

7 So Ahaz sent messengers to <sup>l</sup>Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, saying, I *am* <sup>m</sup>thy servant, and thy son: come up and save me out of the hand of the king of Syria, and out of the hand of the king of Israel, which rise up against me.

8 And Ahaz took the <sup>n</sup>silver and gold that was found in the house of the LORD, and in the treasures of the king's house, and sent *it for* <sup>o</sup>a present to the king of Assyria.

9 And the king of Assyria hearkened unto him: for the king of Assyria went up against Damascus, and <sup>p</sup>took it, and carried *the people of* it captive to <sup>q</sup>Kir, and slew Rezin.
