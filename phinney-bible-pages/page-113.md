## CHAPTER XII.

God rebuketh the sedition of Miriam and Aaron.

AND <sup>†</sup>Miriam and Aaron <sup>a</sup>spake against Moses because of the <sup>b</sup>Ethiopian woman whom he had married: for he had married an Ethiopian woman.

2 And they said, Hath the LORD indeed spoken only by Moses? hath he not spoken also <sup>c</sup>by us? And the LORD <sup>d</sup>heard *it*.

3 (Now the man Moses *was* very <sup>e</sup>meek, <sup>f</sup>above all the men which *were* upon the face of the earth.)

4 And the LORD <sup>g</sup>spake suddenly unto Moses, and unto Aaron, and unto Miriam, Come <sup>h</sup>out ye three unto the tabernacle of the congregation. And they three came out.

5 And the LORD <sup>i</sup>came down in the pillar of the <sup>k</sup>cloud, and stood *in* the door of the tabernacle, and called Aaron and Miriam: and they both came forth.

6 And he said, Hear now my words: If there be a <sup>l</sup>prophet among you, *I* the LORD will make myself known unto him in a <sup>m</sup>vision, *and* will speak unto him in a <sup>n</sup>dream.

7 My servant Moses *is* not so, who *is* faithful in all <sup>o</sup>mine house?

8 With him will I speak <sup>p</sup>mouth to mouth, even <sup>q</sup>apparently, and not in dark speeches; and the similitude of the LORD shall he behold: wherefore then were ye not afraid to speak <sup>r</sup>against my servant Moses?

9 And the anger of the LORD was kindled against them; and he departed.

10 And the cloud departed from off the tabernacle; and, behold, Miriam *became* <sup>s</sup>leprous, *white* as snow: and Aaron looked upon Miriam, and behold, *she was* leprous.

11 And Aaron said unto Moses, Alas, my lord, I beseech thee, lay not the <sup>t</sup>sin upon us, wherein we have done <sup>u</sup>foolishly, and wherein we have sinned.

12 Let her not be as one <sup>x</sup>dead, of whom the flesh is half consumed when he cometh out of his mother's womb.

13 And Moses cried unto the LORD, saying, Heal her now, O God, I beseech thee.

14 And the LORD said unto Moses, If her father had but <sup>y</sup>spit in her face, should she not be ashamed <sup>z</sup>seven days? Let her be shut out from the camp seven days, and after that let her be received in *again*.

15 And Miriam was <sup>a</sup>shut out from the camp seven days: and the people <sup>b</sup>journeyed not till Miriam was brought in *again*.

16 And afterward the people removed from Hazeroth, and pitched in the wilderness of Paran.

## CHAPTER XIII.

1 The names of the men who were sent to search the land. 26 Their relation.

AND the <sup>a</sup>LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2 <sup>b</sup>Send thou men, that they may <sup>c</sup>search the land of Canaan, which I give unto the children of Israel: of every tribe of their fathers shall ye send a man, every one a ruler among them.

3 And Moses by the <sup>d</sup>commandment of the LORD sent them from the wilderness of Paran: all those men *were* <sup>e</sup>heads of the children of Israel.

4 And these *were* their names: Of the tribe of Reuben, Shammua the son of Zaccur.

5 Of the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat the son of Hori.

6 Of the tribe of Judah, <sup>||</sup>Caleb the son of Jephunneh.

7 Of the tribe of Issachar, Igal the son of Joseph.

8 Of the tribe of Ephraim, Oshea the son of Nun.

9 Of the tribe of Benjamin, Palti the son of Raphu.

10 Of the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel the son of Sodi.

11 Of the tribe of Joseph, *namely*, of the tribe of Manasseh, Gaddi the son of Susi.

12 Of the tribe of Dan, Ammiel the son of Gemalli.

13 Of the tribe of Asher, <sup>||</sup>Sethur the son of Michal.

14 Of the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi the son of Vophsi.

15 Of the tribe of Gad, Geuel the son of Machi.

16 These *are* the names of the men which Moses sent to spy out the land. And Moses called Oshea the son of Nun, Jehoshua.

17 And Moses sent them to spy out the land of Canaan, and said unto them, Get you up this *way* <sup>f</sup>southward, and go up into the <sup>g</sup>mountain:

18 And see the land, what it *is*; and the people that dwelleth therein, whether they *be* strong or weak, few or many;

19 And what the land *is* that they dwell in, whether it *be* <sup>h</sup>good or bad; and what cities *they be* that they dwell in, whether in tents, or in strong holds;

20 And what the land *is*, whether it *be* <sup>i</sup>fat or lean, whether there be wood therein or not: and be ye of good courage, and bring of the fruit of the land. Now the time *was* the time of the first-ripe grapes.

21 So they went up, and searched the land, from the wilderness of <sup>k</sup>Zin unto <sup>l</sup>Rehob, as men come to <sup>m</sup>Hamath.

22 And they ascended by the south, and came unto <sup>n</sup>Hebron, where <sup>o</sup>Ahiman, Sheshai, and <sup>p</sup>Talmai, <sup>q</sup>the children of Anak, *were*. (Now Hebron was <sup>r</sup>built seven years before <sup>s</sup>Zoan in Egypt.)

23 And they came unto the brook of <sup>||</sup>Eshcol, and cut down from thence a branch with one cluster of grapes, and they bare it between two upon a staff; and *they brought* of the pomegranates, and of the figs.

24 The place was called the brook Eshcol, because of the cluster of grapes which the children of Israel cut down from thence.

25 And they returned from searching of the land after forty <sup>t</sup>days.
