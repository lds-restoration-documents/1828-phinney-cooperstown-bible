4 <sup>d</sup>But what saith the answer of God unto him? I have reserved to myself seven thousand men, who have not bowed the knee to *the image of* Baal.

5 <sup>e</sup>Even so then at this present time also there is a remnant according to the election of grace.

6 <sup>f</sup>And if by grace, then *is it* no more of works: otherwise grace is no more grace. But if *it be* of works, then is it no more grace: otherwise work is no more work.

7 <sup>g</sup>What then? Israel hath not obtained that which he seeketh for; but the election hath obtained it, and the rest were blinded,

8 <sup>h</sup>(According as it is written, God hath given them the spirit of slumber, eyes that they should not see, and ears that they should not hear:) unto this day.

9 <sup>i</sup>And David saith, Let their table be made a snare and a trap, and a stumbling-block, and a recompense unto them:

10 Let their eyes be darkened, that they may not see, and bow down their back always.

11 <sup>k</sup>I say then, Have they stumbled that they should fall? God forbid: but *rather* through their fall salvation *is come* unto the Gentiles, for to provoke them to jealousy.

12 Now if the fall of them *be* the riches of the world, and the diminishing of them the riches of the Gentiles, how much more their fulness?

13 <sup>l</sup>For I speak to you Gentiles, inasmuch as I am the apostle of the Gentiles, I magnify mine office;

14 <sup>m</sup>If by any means I may provoke to emulation *them which are* my flesh, and might save some of them.

15 <sup>n</sup>For if the casting away of them *be* the reconciling of the world, what *shall* the receiving *of them be,* but life from the dead?

16 <sup>o</sup>For if the first-fruit *be* holy, the lump *is* also *holy;* and if the root *be* holy, so *are* the branches.

17 <sup>p</sup>And if some of the branches be broken off, and thou, being a wild olive-tree, wert graffed in among them, and with them partakest of the root and fatness of the olive-tree;

18 <sup>q</sup>Boast not against the branches. But if thou boast, thou bearest not the root, but the root thee.

19 Thou wilt say then, The branches were broken off, that I might be graffed in.

20 <sup>r</sup>Well; because of unbelief they were broken off; and thou standest by faith. Be not high-minded, but fear:

21 For if God spared not the natural branches, *take heed* lest he also spare not thee.

22 <sup>s</sup>Behold therefore the goodness and severity of God: on them which fell, severity; but toward thee, goodness; if thou continue in *his* goodness: otherwise thou also shalt be cut off.

23 <sup>t</sup>And they also, if they abide not still in unbelief, shall be graffed in: for God is able to graff them in again.

24 <sup>u</sup>For if thou wert cut out of the olive-tree which is wild by nature, and wert graffed contrary to nature into a good olive-tree; how much more shall these, which be the natural *branches,* be graffed into their own olive-tree?

25 <sup>x</sup>For I would not, brethren, that ye should be ignorant of this mystery, lest ye should be wise in your own conceits: that blindness in part is happened to Israel, until the fulness of the Gentiles be come in.

26 <sup>y</sup>And so all Israel shall be saved: as it is written, There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob:

27 <sup>z</sup>For this *is* my covenant unto them, when I shall take away their sins.

28 <sup>a</sup>As concerning the gospel, *they are* enemies for your sake: but as touching the election, *they are* beloved for the fathers' sakes.

29 <sup>b</sup>For the gifts and calling of God *are* without repentance.

30 <sup>c</sup>For as ye in times past have not believed God, yet have now obtained mercy through their unbelief;

31 Even so have these also now not believed, that through your mercy they also may obtain mercy.

32 <sup>d</sup>For God hath concluded them all in unbelief, that he might have mercy upon all.

33 <sup>e</sup>O the depth of the riches both of the wisdom and knowledge of God! how unsearchable *are* his judgments, and his ways past finding out!

34 <sup>f</sup>For who hath known the mind of the Lord? or who hath been his counsellor?

35 <sup>g</sup>Or who hath first given to him, and it shall be recompensed unto him again?

36 <sup>h</sup>For of him, and through him, and to him, *are* all things: to whom *be* glory for ever. Amen.

## CHAPTER XII.

1 Holiness and conformity to God's will enjoined....6 Gifts to be used for the common benefit....9 Sundry practical duties recommended....19 Revenge specially forbidden.

<sup>a</sup>I BESEECH you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, *which is* your reasonable service.

2 <sup>b</sup>And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what *is* that good, and acceptable, and perfect will of God.

3 <sup>c</sup>For I say, through the grace given unto me, to every man that is among you, not to think *of himself* more highly than he ought to think; but to think soberly, according as God hath dealt to every man the measure of faith.

4 <sup>d</sup>For as we have many members in one body, and all members have not the same office;

5 <sup>e</sup>So we, *being* many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.

6 <sup>f</sup>Having then gifts differing according to the grace that is given to us, whether prophecy, *let us prophesy* according to the proportion of faith;

7 <sup>g</sup>Or ministry, *let us wait* on *our* ministering; or he that teacheth, on teaching;

8 <sup>h</sup>Or he that exhorteth, on exhortation: he that giveth, *let him do it* with simplicity; he that ruleth, with diligence; he that sheweth mercy, with cheerfulness.

9 <sup>i</sup>*Let* love be without dissimulation. Abhor that which is evil; cleave to that which is good.

10 <sup>k</sup>*Be* kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love; in honour preferring one another;

11 <sup>l</sup>Not slothful in business; fervent in spirit; serving the Lord;

12 <sup>m</sup>Rejoicing in hope; patient in tribulation; continuing instant in prayer;

13 <sup>n</sup>Distributing to the necessity of saints; given to hospitality.

14 <sup>o</sup>Bless them which persecute you: bless, and curse not.

15 <sup>p</sup>Rejoice with them that do rejoice, and weep with them that weep.
