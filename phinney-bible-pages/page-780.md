4 But the multitude of the city was divided: and part held with the Jews, and part with the apostles.

5 <sup>c</sup>And when there was an assault made, both of the Gentiles and also of the Jews, with their rulers, to use *them* despitefully, and to stone them,

6 <sup>d</sup>They were ware of *it,* and fled unto Lystra and Derbe, cities of Lycaonia, and unto the region that lieth round about:

7 And there they preached the gospel.

8 ¶ <sup>e</sup>And there sat a certain man at Lystra, impotent in his feet, being a cripple from his mother's womb, who never had walked:

9 <sup>f</sup>The same heard Paul speak: who steadfastly beholding him, and perceiving that he had faith to be healed,

10 <sup>g</sup>Said with a loud voice, Stand upright on thy feet. And he leaped and walked.

11 <sup>h</sup>And when the people saw what Paul had done, they lifted up their voices, saying in the speech of Lycaonia, The gods are come down to us in the likeness of men.

12 And they called Barnabas, Jupiter; and Paul, Mercurius, because he was the chief speaker.

13 <sup>i</sup>Then the priest of Jupiter, which was before their city, brought oxen and garlands unto the gates, and would have done sacrifice with the people.

14 <sup>k</sup>*Which* when the apostles, Barnabas and Paul, heard *of,* they rent their clothes, and ran in among the people, crying out,

15 <sup>l</sup>And saying, Sirs, why do ye these things? We also are men of like passions with you, and preach unto you, that ye should turn from these vanities, unto the living God, which made heaven and earth, and the sea, and all things that are therein:

16 <sup>m</sup>Who in times past suffered all nations to walk in their own ways.

17 <sup>n</sup>Nevertheless, he left not himself without witness, in that he did good, and gave us rain from heaven, and fruitful seasons, filling our hearts with food and gladness.

18 And with these sayings scarce restrained they the people, that they had not done sacrifice unto them.

19 ¶ <sup>o</sup>And there came thither *certain* Jews from Antioch and Iconium, who persuaded the people, and having stoned Paul, drew *him* out of the city, supposing he had been dead.

20 <sup>p</sup>Howbeit, as the disciples stood round about him, he rose up, and came into the city: and the next day he departed with Barnabas to Derbe.

21 And when they had preached the gospel to that city, and had taught many, they returned again to Lystra, and *to* Iconium, and to Antioch,

22 <sup>q</sup>Confirming the souls of the disciples, *and* exhorting them to continue in the faith, and that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.

23 <sup>r</sup>And when they <sup>†</sup>had ordained them elders in every church, and had prayed with fasting, they commended them to the Lord, on whom they believed.

24 And after they had passed throughout Pisidia, they came to Pamphylia.

25 And when they had preached the word in Perga, they went down into Attalia:

26 <sup>s</sup>And thence sailed to Antioch, from whence they had been recommended to the grace of God for the work which they fulfilled.

27 <sup>t</sup>And when they were come, and had gathered the church together, they rehearsed all that God had done with them, and how he had opened the door of faith unto the Gentiles.

28 And there they abode long time with the disciples.

## CHAPTER XV.

1 Dissension about circumcision: 6 the apostles consult about it: 22 their determination....36 Paul and Barnabas contend, and part.

<sup>a</sup>AND certain men which came down from Judea, taught the brethren, *and said,* Except ye be circumcised after the manner of Moses, ye cannot be saved.

2 <sup>b</sup>When therefore Paul and Barnabas had no small dissension and disputation with them, they determined that Paul and Barnabas, and certain other of them, should go up to Jerusalem, unto the apostles and elders, about this question.

3 And being brought on their way by the church, they passed through Phenice and Samaria, declaring the conversion of the Gentiles: and they caused great joy unto all the brethren.

4 <sup>c</sup>And when they were come to Jerusalem, they were received of the church, and *of* the apostles and elders; and they declared all things that God had done with them.

5 ¶ <sup>d</sup>But there rose up certain of the sect of the Pharisees which believed, saying, That it was needful to circumcise them, and to command *them* to keep the law of Moses.

6 And the apostles and elders came together for to consider of this matter.

7 <sup>e</sup>And when there had been much disputing, Peter rose up, and said unto them, Men *and* brethren, ye know how that a good while ago God made choice among us, that the Gentiles by my mouth should hear the word of the gospel, and believe.

8 <sup>f</sup>And God, which knoweth the hearts, bare them witness, giving them the Holy Ghost, even as *he did* unto us;

9 <sup>g</sup>And put no difference between us and them, purifying their hearts by faith.

10 <sup>h</sup>Now therefore why tempt ye God, to put a yoke upon the neck of the disciples, which neither our fathers nor we were able to bear?

11 <sup>i</sup>But we believe that through the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ we shall be saved, even as they.

12 ¶ <sup>k</sup>Then all the multitude kept silence, and gave audience to Barnabas and Paul, declaring what miracles and wonders God had wrought among the Gentiles by them.

13 ¶ <sup>l</sup>And after they had held their peace, James answered, saying, Men *and* brethren hearken unto me:

14 <sup>m</sup>Simeon hath declared how God at the first did visit the Gentiles, to take out of them a people for his name.

15 And to this agree the words of the prophets; as it is written,

16 <sup>n</sup>After this I will return, and will build again the tabernacle of David, which is fallen down; and I will build again the ruins thereof, and I will set it up:

17 That the residue of men might seek after the Lord, and all the Gentiles, upon whom my name is called, saith the Lord, who doeth all these things.

18 Known unto God are all his works, from the beginning of the world.

19 Wherefore my sentence is, that we trouble not them, which from among the Gentiles are turned to God:
