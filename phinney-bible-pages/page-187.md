## CHAPTER XV.

Samson burneth the Philistines' corn with foxes and firebrands.

BUT it came to pass within a while after, in the time of wheat-harvest, that Samson visited his wife with a kid; and he said, I will <sup>a</sup>go in to my wife into the <sup>b</sup>chamber: but her father would not suffer him to go in.

2 And her father said, I verily thought that thou hadst utterly hated her; therefore <sup>c</sup>I gave her to thy companion: *is* not her younger sister fairer than she? take her, I pray thee, instead of her.

3 And Samson said concerning them, <sup>†</sup>Now shall I be more blameless than the Philistines, though I do them a displeasure.

4 And Samson went and caught <sup>d</sup>three hundred foxes, and took firebrands, and turned <sup>e</sup>tail to tail, and put a firebrand in the midst between two tails.

5 And when he had set the brands on fire, he let *them* go into the standing corn of the Philistines, and burnt up both the shocks and also the standing corn, with the vineyards *and* olives.

6 Then the Philistines said, Who hath done this? And they answered, Samson, the son-in-law of the Timnite, because he had taken his wife, and given her to his companion. And the Philistines came up, <sup>f</sup>and burnt her and her father with fire.

7 And Samson said unto them, Though ye have done this, yet will I be <sup>g</sup>avenged of you, and after that I will cease.

8 And he smote them <sup>h</sup>hip and thigh with a great slaughter; and he went down and dwelt in the top of the rock <sup>i</sup>Etam.

9 Then the Philistines went up, and pitched in Judah, and spread themselves in <sup>||</sup>Lehi.

10 And the men of Judah said, Why are ye come up against us? And they answered, To <sup>k</sup>bind Samson are we come up, to do to him as he hath done to us.

11 Then three thousand men of Judah went to the top of the rock Etam, and said to Samson, Knowest thou not that the Philistines *are* <sup>l</sup>rulers over us? what *is* this *that* thou hast done unto us? And he said unto them, As they did unto me, so have I done unto them.

12 And they said unto him, We are come down to bind thee, that we may <sup>m</sup>deliver thee into the hand of the Philistines. And Samson said unto them, Swear unto me, that ye will not fall upon me yourselves.

13 And they spake unto him, saying, No; but we will bind thee fast, and deliver thee into their hand: but surely we will not kill thee. And they bound him with two new cords, and brought him up from the rock.

14 *And* when he came unto Lehi, the Philistines <sup>n</sup>shouted against him: and the Spirit of the LORD came mightily upon him; and the cords that *were* upon his arms became as flax that was burnt with fire, and his bands <sup>†</sup>loosed from off his hands.

15 And he found a new jaw-bone of an ass, and put forth his hand and took it, and <sup>o</sup>slew a thousand men therewith.

16 And Samson said, With the jaw-bone of an ass, heaps upon heaps, with the jaw of an ass have I slain a thousand men.

17 And it came to pass, when he had made an end of speaking, that he cast away the jaw-bone out of his hand, and called that place <sup>||</sup>Ramath-lehi.

18 And he was sore athirst, and called on the LORD, and said, <sup>p</sup>Thou hast given this great deliverance into the hand of thy servant: and now shall I <sup>q</sup>die for thirst, and fall into the hand of the uncircumcised?

19 But God clave a hollow place that *was* in the jaw, and there came water thereout; and when he had drunk, <sup>r</sup>his spirit came again, and he revived: wherefore he called the name thereof <sup>||</sup>En-hakkore, which *is* in Lehi unto this day.

20 And he <sup>s</sup>judged Israel, in the days of the Philistines, twenty years.

## CHAPTER XVI.

Samson escapeth, and carrieth away the gates of Gaza.

THEN <sup>a</sup>went Samson to Gaza, and saw there <sup>†</sup>a harlot, and went in unto her.

2 *And it was told* the Gazites, saying, Samson is come hither. And they <sup>b</sup>compassed *him* in, and laid wait for him all night in the gate of the city, and were quiet all the night, saying, In the morning, when it is day, <sup>c</sup>we shall kill him.

3 And Samson lay till midnight, and arose at midnight, and took the doors of the <sup>d</sup>gate of the city, and the two posts, and went away with them, bar and all, and put *them* upon his shoulders, and carried them up to the top of a hill that *is* before Hebron.

4 And it came to pass afterward, that he <sup>e</sup>loved a woman in the valley of Sorek, whose name *was* <sup>||</sup>Delilah.

5 And the lords of the Philistines came up unto her, and said unto her, <sup>f</sup>Entice him, and see wherein his <sup>g</sup>great strength *lieth*, and by what *means* we may prevail against him, that we may bind him to afflict him; and we will <sup>h</sup>give thee, every one of us, eleven hundred *pieces* of silver.

6 And Delilah said to Samson, <sup>i</sup>Tell me, I pray thee, wherein thy great strength *lieth*, and wherewith thou mightest be bound to afflict thee.

7 And Samson said unto her, If they bind me with <sup>k</sup>seven green withs that were never dried, then shall I be weak, and be <sup>l</sup>as another man.

8 Then the lords of the Philistines brought up to her seven green withs which had not been dried, and she <sup>m</sup>bound him with them.

9 (Now *there were* men lying in wait, abiding with her in the chamber.) And she said unto him, The Philistines *be* upon thee, Samson. And he brake the withs, as a thread of tow is broken when it <sup>†</sup>toucheth the fire: so his <sup>n</sup>strength was not known.

10 And Delilah, said unto Samson, Behold, thou hast <sup>o</sup>mocked me, and told me lies: now tell me, I pray thee, wherewith thou mightest be bound.

11 And he said unto her, If they bind me fast with new ropes that never were occupied, then shall I be weak, and be as another man.

12 Delilah therefore took <sup>p</sup>new ropes, and bound him therewith, and said unto him, The Philistines *be* upon thee, Samson. (And *there were* liers in wait abiding in the chamber.) And he brake them from off his arms like a thread.
