## PSALM CVIII.

David encourageth himself to praise God.

*A Song or Psalm of David.*

3 I will praise thee, O LORD, <sup>b</sup>among the people: and I will sing praises unto thee among the nations.

4 For thy mercy *is* great <sup>c</sup>above the heavens, and thy truth *reacheth* unto the clouds.

5 Be thou <sup>d</sup>exalted, O God, above the heavens; and thy glory above all the earth:

6 That thy <sup>e</sup>beloved may be delivered, save *with* thy right hand, and answer me.

7 God hath spoken <sup>f</sup>in his holiness, I will rejoice; I will <sup>g</sup>divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of <sup>h</sup>Succoth.

9 Moab *is* my <sup>m</sup>washpot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe; <sup>n</sup>over Philistia will I triumph.

10 <sup>o</sup>Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?
