## PSALM XLI.

1 God's care of the poor....10 David fleeth to God for succour.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David.*

BLESSED *is* he that considereth the <sup>||</sup>poor: the LORD will deliver him in time of trouble.

2 The LORD will <sup>a</sup>preserve him, *and* keep him alive; *and* he shall be blessed <sup>b</sup>upon the earth: and thou wilt not deliver him unto the will of his enemies.

3 The LORD will <sup>c</sup>strengthen him upon the bed of languishing: thou wilt <sup>†</sup>make all his bed in his sickness.

4 I said, O LORD, be merciful unto me; <sup>d</sup>heal my soul; for I have <sup>e</sup>sinned against thee.

5 Mine enemies speak evil of me; When shall he die, and his name perish?

6 And if he come to see *me,* he <sup>f</sup>speaketh vanity: his heart gathereth iniquity to itself; *when* he goeth abroad, he telleth *it.*

7 All that hate me whisper together against me: against me do they devise my hurt.

8 <sup>g</sup>An evil disease, *say they,* cleaveth fast unto him: and *now* that he lieth, he shall rise up no more.

9 Yea, mine own <sup>h</sup>familiar friend in whom I trusted, which did eat of my bread, hath lifted up *his* heel against me.

10 But thou, O LORD, be merciful unto me, and raise me up, that I may requite them.

11 By this I know that thou <sup>i</sup>favourest me, because mine enemy doth not triumph over me.

12 And as for me, thou <sup>k</sup>upholdest me in mine integrity, and settest me before thy face for ever.

13 Blessed *be* the LORD God of Israel from everlasting and to everlasting. Amen, and Amen.

## PSALM XLII.

David's zeal to serve God in the temple.

*To the chief Musician, Maschil, for the sons of* <sup>a</sup>*Korah.*

AS the hart <sup>b</sup>panteth after the water-brooks, so panteth my soul after thee, O God.

2 My soul <sup>c</sup>thirsteth for God, for the living God: when shall I come and appear before God?

3 My <sup>d</sup>tears have been my meat day and night, while they continually say unto me, <sup>e</sup>Where *is* thy God?

4 When I <sup>f</sup>remember these *things,* I pour out my soul in me: for I had gone with the multitude; I went with them to the house of God, with the voice of joy and praise, with a multitude that kept <sup>g</sup>holy-day.

5 Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and *why* art thou disquieted in me? <sup>h</sup>Hope thou in God; for I shall yet praise him *for* the help of his countenance.

6 O my God, my soul is cast down within me: <sup>i</sup>therefore will I remember thee from the land of Jordan, and of the Hermonites, from the hill Mizar.

7 <sup>k</sup>Deep calleth unto deep at the noise of thy <sup>l</sup>water-spouts: all thy waves and thy billows are gone over me.

8 *Yet* the LORD will <sup>m</sup>command his loving-kindness in the day-time, and in the night <sup>n</sup>his song *shall be* with me, *and* my prayer unto the God of my life.

9 I will say unto God my rock, Why hast thou forgotten me? why go I mourning because of the oppression of the enemy?

10 *As* with a sword in my bones, mine enemies reproach me; while they say daily unto me, Where *is* thy God?

11 Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me? Hope thou in God; for I shall yet praise him, *who is* <sup>†</sup>the health of my countenance, and my God.
