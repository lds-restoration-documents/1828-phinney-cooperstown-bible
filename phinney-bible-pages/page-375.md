## PSALM XLIV.

The church complaineth of her present evils.

*To the chief Musician for the sons of Korah.*

WE have heard with our ears, O God, <sup>a</sup>our fathers have told us, *what* work thou didst in their days, in the times of old;

2 *How* thou didst <sup>b</sup>drive out the heathen with thy hand, and plantedst them; *how* thou didst afflict the people and cast them out.

3 For they got not the land in possession <sup>c</sup>by their own sword, neither did their own arm save them; but thy right hand, and thine arm, and the light of thy countenance, because <sup>d</sup>thou hadst a favour unto them.

4 Thou art my King, O God: <sup>e</sup>command deliverances for Jacob.

5 Through thee will we push down our enemies; through thy name will we <sup>f</sup>tread them under that rise up against us.

6 For I will not <sup>g</sup>trust in my bow, neither shall my sword save me.

7 But thou hast saved us from our enemies, and hast put them to shame that hated us.

8 In God <sup>h</sup>we boast all the day long, and praise thy name for ever. Selah.

9 But thou <sup>i</sup>hast cast off, and put us to shame; and goest not forth with our armies.

10 Thou makest us <sup>k</sup>to turn back from the enemy; and they which hate us spoil for themselves.

11 Thou hast given us like sheep *appointed* for meat; and hast scattered us among the heathen.

12 Thou sellest thy people <sup>l</sup>for nought, and dost <sup>m</sup>not increase *thy wealth* by their price.

13 Thou makest us <sup>n</sup>a reproach to our neighbours, a scorn and a derision to them that are round about us.

14 Thou makest us <sup>o</sup>a by-word among the heathen, <sup>p</sup>a shaking of the head among the people.

15 My confusion *is continually* before me, and the shame of my face hath covered me,

16 For the voice of him that reproacheth and blasphemeth; by reason of the enemy and <sup>q</sup>avenger.

17 All this is come upon us; <sup>r</sup>yet have we not forgotten thee, neither have we dealt falsely in thy covenant.

18 <sup>s</sup>Our heart is not turned back, neither have our <sup>t</sup>steps declined from thy way;

19 Though thou hast sore broken us in the place of dragons, and covered us with the shadow of death.

20 If we have <sup>u</sup>forgotten the name of our God, or stretched our hands to a strange god;

21 Shall not God <sup>x</sup>search this out? for he knoweth the secrets of the heart.

22 Yea, <sup>y</sup>for thy sake are we killed all the day long; we are counted as sheep for the slaughter.

23 <sup>z</sup>Awake, why sleepest thou, O LORD? arise, cast *us* not off for ever.

24 Wherefore <sup>a</sup>hidest thou thy face, *and* forgettest our affliction and our oppression?

25 For our soul is bowed down to the <sup>b</sup>dust; our belly cleaveth unto the earth.

26 Arise for our help, and redeem us, for thy mercies' sake.

## PSALM XLV.

The majesty and grace of Christ's kingdom.

*To the chief Musician upon Shoshannim, for the sons of Korah, Maschil.* <sup>a</sup>*A Song of loves.*

MY heart is inditing a good matter: I speak of the things which I have made touching <sup>b</sup>the King; my tongue *is* the pen of a ready writer.

2 Thou art <sup>c</sup>fairer than the children of men; grace is <sup>d</sup>poured into thy lips: therefore God hath <sup>e</sup>blessed thee for ever.

3 <sup>f</sup>Gird thy sword upon *thy* thigh, <sup>g</sup>O *most* Mighty, with thy glory and thy majesty.

4 And in thy majesty <sup>h</sup>ride prosperously, because of <sup>i</sup>truth, and <sup>k</sup>meekness, *and* <sup>l</sup>righteousness; and thy right hand shall teach thee <sup>m</sup>terrible things.

5 Thine arrows *are* sharp in the heart of the <sup>n</sup>King's enemies; *whereby* the people fall under thee.

6 <sup>o</sup>Thy throne, O God, *is* for ever and ever: the sceptre of thy kingdom *is* a right sceptre.

7 Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness: therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness <sup>p</sup>above thy fellows.

8 All thy garments *smell* of myrrh, and aloes, *and* cassia, out of the ivory palaces, whereby they have <sup>q</sup>made thee glad.

9 Kings' daughters *were* among thy honourable women: upon thy right hand did stand the <sup>r</sup>queen in gold of Ophir.

10 Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear; forget also thine own people, and thy <sup>s</sup>father's house;

11 So shall the King greatly desire thy <sup>t</sup>beauty; for he *is* <sup>u</sup>thy LORD, and worship thou him.

12 And the daughter of Tyre *shall be there* with a gift; *even* the <sup>x</sup>rich among the people shall entreat thy favour.

13 The King's daughter *is* <sup>y</sup>all glorious within; her clothing *is* of wrought gold.

14 She shall be brought unto the King in raiment of needle-work: the <sup>z</sup>virgins her companions that follow her shall be brought unto thee.

15 With <sup>a</sup>gladness and rejoicing *shall* they *be* brought: they shall enter into the <sup>b</sup>King's palace.

16 Instead of thy fathers shall be <sup>c</sup>thy children, whom thou mayest make <sup>d</sup>princes in all the earth.

17 I will make <sup>e</sup>thy name to be remembered in all generations; therefore shall the people praise thee for ever and ever.

4 *There is* <sup>g</sup>a river, the streams whereof shall <sup>h</sup>make glad <sup>i</sup>the city of God, the holy *place* of the tabernacles of the Most High.

5 God *is* <sup>k</sup>in the midst of her; she shall <sup>l</sup>not be moved: God shall help her, *and that* right early.

6 The heathen <sup>m</sup>raged, the kingdoms were moved: he uttered his voice, the earth <sup>n</sup>melted.
