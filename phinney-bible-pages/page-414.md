14 The law of the wise *is* <sup>u</sup>a fountain of life, to depart from the <sup>x</sup>snares of death.

19 The <sup>g</sup>desire accomplished is sweet to the soul: but *it is* abomination to fools to <sup>h</sup>depart from evil.

25 The righteous eateth to the satisfying of his soul: but the belly of the wicked shall want.

## CHAPTER XIV.

EVERY wise woman <sup>a</sup>buildeth her house: but the foolish plucketh it down <sup>b</sup>with her hands.

2 He that <sup>c</sup>walketh in his uprightness feareth the LORD: but *he that is* perverse in his ways <sup>d</sup>despiseth him.

3 In the mouth of the foolish *is* <sup>e</sup>a rod of pride: but the lips of the wise shall preserve them.

4 Where no <sup>f</sup>oxen *are,* the crib *is* clean: but <sup>g</sup>much increase *is* by the strength of the ox.

5 <sup>h</sup>A faithful witness will not lie: but <sup>i</sup>a false witness will utter lies.

6 A scorner seeketh wisdom, and <sup>k</sup>*findeth it* not: but knowledge *is* <sup>l</sup>easy unto him that understandeth.

7 Go from the presence of a foolish man, when thou <sup>m</sup>perceivest not *in him* the lips of knowledge.

8 The wisdom of the prudent *is* <sup>n</sup>to understand his way: but the folly of fools *is* <sup>o</sup>deceit.

9 Fools make a mock at sin: but among the righteous *there is* <sup>p</sup>favour.

10 The heart knoweth <sup>q</sup>his own bitterness: and a stranger doth <sup>r</sup>not intermeddle with his joy.

11 <sup>s</sup>The house of the wicked shall be overthrown: but the <sup>t</sup>tabernacle of the upright shall flourish.

12 There is a way which <sup>u</sup>seemeth right unto a man; but the <sup>x</sup>end thereof *are* the ways of death.

13 Even in <sup>y</sup>laughter the heart is sorrowful; and the <sup>z</sup>end of that mirth *is* heaviness.

14 The backslider in heart shall be <sup>a</sup>filled with his own ways; and a good man *shall be satisfied* <sup>b</sup>from himself.

15 The simple <sup>c</sup>believeth every word: but the prudent *man* <sup>d</sup>looketh well to his going.

16 A wise *man* <sup>e</sup>feareth, and departeth from evil: but the fool rageth, and is <sup>f</sup>confident.

17 *He that is* <sup>g</sup>soon angry dealeth foolishly; and a man of <sup>h</sup>wicked devices is hated.

18 The simple <sup>i</sup>inherit folly: but the prudent are <sup>k</sup>crowned with knowledge.

19 The evil <sup>l</sup>bow before the good; and the wicked at the gates of the righteous.

20 The poor is <sup>m</sup>hated even of his own neighbour: but the rich *hath* <sup>n</sup>many friends.

21 He that despiseth his neighbour sinneth: but he that hath mercy on the poor, <sup>o</sup>happy *is* he.

22 Do they not err that devise evil? but mercy and truth *shall be* to them that devise good.

23 In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips *tendeth* only to penury.

24 The crown of the wise *is* their riches: *but* the foolishness of fools *is* folly.

25 A true witness delivereth souls: but a deceitful *witness* speaketh lies.

26 In the fear of the LORD *is* strong confidence; and <sup>p</sup>his children shall have a place of refuge.

27 The fear of the LORD *is* <sup>q</sup>a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death.

28 In the multitude of people *is* the <sup>r</sup>king's honour: but in the want of people *is* the destruction of the prince.

29 *He that is* <sup>s</sup>slow to wrath *is* of great understanding: but *he that is* hasty of spirit exalteth folly.

30 <sup>t</sup>A sound heart *is* the life of the flesh: but envy the <sup>u</sup>rottenness of the bones.

31 He that oppresseth the poor <sup>x</sup>reproacheth his Maker: but he that <sup>y</sup>honoureth him hath mercy on the poor.

32 The wicked <sup>z</sup>is driven away in his wickedness: but the righteous hath <sup>a</sup>hope in his death.

33 Wisdom resteth in the <sup>b</sup>heart of him that hath understanding: but *that which is* in the midst of fools is made known.

34 <sup>c</sup>Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin *is* a reproach to any people.

35 The king's favour *is* toward <sup>d</sup>a wise servant: but his wrath is *against* him that causeth shame.

## CHAPTER XV.

A SOFT answer <sup>a</sup>turneth away wrath: but <sup>b</sup>grievous words stir up anger.

2 The tongue of the wise <sup>c</sup>useth knowledge aright: but the mouth of fools poureth out foolishness.

3 <sup>d</sup>The eyes of the LORD *are* in every place, beholding the evil and the good.

4 <sup>e</sup>A wholesome tongue *is* a tree of life: but perverseness therein *is* <sup>f</sup>a breach in the spirit.

5 A fool despiseth his father's instruction: but he that regardeth reproof <sup>g</sup>is prudent.

6 In the house of the righteous *is* <sup>h</sup>much treasure: but in the revenues of the wicked is <sup>i</sup>trouble.

7 The lips of the wise <sup>k</sup>disperse knowledge: but the heart of the foolish <sup>l</sup>*doeth* not so.

8 The <sup>m</sup>sacrifice of the wicked *is* an abomination to the LORD: but the prayer of the upright *is* his <sup>n</sup>delight.

9 The <sup>o</sup>way of the wicked *is* an abomination unto the LORD: but he loveth him that <sup>p</sup>followeth after righteousness.

10 Correction *is* <sup>q</sup>grievous unto him that forsaketh the way; *and* he that hateth reproof shall die.

11 Hell and destruction *are* before the LORD; how much more then the <sup>r</sup>hearts of the children of men?

12 A scorner <sup>s</sup>loveth not one that reproveth him; neither will he <sup>t</sup>go unto the wise.

13 <sup>u</sup>A merry heart maketh a cheerful countenance: but by sorrow of the heart the <sup>x</sup>spirit is broken.
