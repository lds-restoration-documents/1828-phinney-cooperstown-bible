## PSALM XCVIII.

The Psalmist exhorteth all to praise God.

*A Psalm.*

O SING unto the LORD a new song; for he hath done <sup>a</sup>marvellous things: his <sup>b</sup>right hand and his holy arm hath gotten him the victory.

2 The LORD hath made known <sup>c</sup>his salvation: his righteousness hath he openly shewed in the sight of the heathen.

3 He hath <sup>d</sup>remembered his mercy and his truth toward the house of Israel: <sup>e</sup>all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

4 Make <sup>f</sup>a joyful noise unto the LORD, all the earth; make a loud noise, and rejoice, and sing praise.

5 Sing unto the LORD with the harp; with the harp and the voice of a psalm.

6 With <sup>g</sup>trumpets, and sound of cornet, make a joyful noise before the LORD <sup>h</sup>the King.

7 Let the sea <sup>i</sup>roar, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.

8 Let the floods clap *their* hands: let the hills be joyful together

9 Before the LORD: for he cometh <sup>k</sup>to judge the earth: with righteousness shall he judge the world, and the people with equity.

## PSALM XCIX.

The Prophet setteth forth the kingdom of God in Zion.

THE LORD reigneth; <sup>||</sup>let the people tremble: he sitteth *between* the cherubims; let the earth be moved.

2 The LORD *is* great <sup>a</sup>in Zion, and he *is* high <sup>b</sup>above all people.

3 Let them praise thy great and <sup>c</sup>terrible name; *for it is* holy.

4 The King's strength also loveth judgment; thou dost establish equity: thou executest judgment and righteousness in Jacob.

5 Exalt ye the LORD our God, and worship at his <sup>d</sup>footstool; *for* he *is* <sup>e</sup>holy.

6 Moses and Aaron among his priests, and Samuel among them that <sup>f</sup>call upon his name: they called upon the LORD, and he answered them.

7 He spake unto them in the <sup>g</sup>cloudy pillar; they kept his testimonies, and the ordinance *that* he gave them.

8 Thou answeredst them, O LORD our God: thou wast a God that forgavest them, though thou <sup>h</sup>tookest vengeance of their inventions.

9 <sup>i</sup>Exalt the LORD our God, and worship at his holy hill: for the LORD our God *is* holy.

## PSALM C.

An exhortation to praise God for his power.

<sup>a</sup>*A Psalm of praise.*

MAKE a joyful noise unto the LORD, <sup>b</sup>all ye lands.

2 Serve the LORD with gladness; come before his presence with singing.

3 Know ye that the LORD he *is* God: *it is* he *that* hath <sup>c</sup>made us, and not we ourselves: *we are* his people, and the <sup>d</sup>sheep of his pasture.

4 Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, *and* into his <sup>e</sup>courts with praise: be thankful unto him, *and* bless his name.

5 For the LORD *is* <sup>f</sup>good, his <sup>g</sup>mercy *is* everlasting; and his truth *endureth* to all generations.

## PSALM CI.

David maketh a vow and profession of godliness.

*A Psalm of David.*

I WILL sing <sup>a</sup>of mercy and judgment: unto thee, O LORD, will I sing.

2 I will behave myself <sup>b</sup>wisely in a perfect way. O! when wilt thou come unto me? I will walk within my house with a perfect heart.

3 I will set no wicked thing before mine eyes: I hate the work of them that <sup>c</sup>turn aside, *it* shall not cleave to me.

4 A froward heart shall depart from me; I will <sup>d</sup>not know a wicked *person.*

5 Whoso privily slandereth his neighbour, him will I cut off: him that hath a <sup>e</sup>high look and a proud heart will not I suffer.

6 <sup>f</sup>Mine eyes *shall be* upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me: he that walketh <sup>g</sup>in a perfect way, he shall serve me.

7 He that worketh <sup>h</sup>deceit shall not dwell within my house; he that telleth lies shall not tarry in my sight.

8 I will <sup>†</sup>early destroy all the wicked of the land, that I may cut off all wicked doers from the city of the LORD.

## PSALM CII.

The mercies of God are to be recorded.

*A Prayer* <sup>||</sup>*of the afflicted; when he is* <sup>a</sup>*overwhelmed, and poureth out his complaint before the LORD.*

HEAR my prayer, O LORD, and let my <sup>b</sup>cry come unto thee.

2 <sup>c</sup>Hide not thy face from me in the day *when* I am in trouble; incline thine ear unto me: in the day *when* I call, answer me speedily.

3 For my days are consumed like <sup>d</sup>smoke, and my bones are burned as a <sup>e</sup>hearth.

4 My heart is smitten, and withered like <sup>f</sup>grass; so that I forget to eat my bread.

5 By reason of the voice of my groaning, <sup>g</sup>my bones cleave to my skin.

6 I am like <sup>h</sup>a pelican of the wilderness; I am like an <sup>i</sup>owl of the desert.

7 <sup>k</sup>I watch, and am as a sparrow <sup>l</sup>alone upon the house-top.

8 Mine enemies reproach me all the day; *and* they that are <sup>m</sup>mad against me are <sup>n</sup>sworn against me.

9 For I have eaten ashes like bread, and mingled my drink with weeping;

10 Because of thine indignation and thy wrath: for thou hast <sup>o</sup>lifted me up, and cast me down.
