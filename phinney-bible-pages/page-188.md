## CHAPTER XVII.

Micah hireth a Levite to be his priest.

AND there was a man of mount Ephraim, whose name *was* Micah.

2 And he said unto his mother, The eleven hundred *shekels* of silver that were taken <sup>†</sup>from thee, about which thou cursedst, and spakest of also in mine ears, behold, the silver *is* with me; I took it. And his mother said, <sup>||</sup>Blessed *be thou* of the LORD, my son.

3 And when he had restored the eleven hundred *shekels* of silver to his mother, his mother said, I had wholly <sup>a</sup>dedicated the silver unto the LORD, from my hand, for my son, to make a graven image and a <sup>b</sup>molten image; now, therefore, I will restore it unto thee.

4 Yet he restored the money unto his mother; and his mother took <sup>c</sup>two hundred *shekels* of silver, and gave them to the founder, who made thereof a graven image and a molten image: and they were in the house of Micah.

5 And the man Micah had a house of gods, and made an <sup>d</sup>ephod, and <sup>e</sup>teraphim, and <sup>†</sup>consecrated one of his sons, who became his priest.

6 In those days *there was* <sup>f</sup>no king in Israel, *but* every man did *that which was* <sup>g</sup>right in his own eyes.

7 And there was a young man out of <sup>h</sup>Beth-lehem-judah, of the family of Judah, who *was* a Levite, and he sojourned there.

8 And the man departed out of the city from Beth-lehem-judah, to sojourn <sup>i</sup>where he could find *a place*: and he came to mount Ephraim, to the house of Micah, as he journeyed.

9 And Micah said unto him, Whence comest thou? And he said unto him, I *am* a Levite of Beth-lehem-judah, and I go to sojourn where I may find *a place*.

10 And Micah said unto him, Dwell with me, and be unto me <sup>k</sup>a father and a priest, and I will give thee ten *shekels* of silver by the year, and a suit of apparel, and thy <sup>l</sup>victuals. So the Levite went in.

11 And the Levite was content to dwell with the man; and the young man was unto him as one of his sons.

12 And Micah consecrated the Levite; and the young man became his priest, and was in the house of Micah.

13 Then said Micah, <sup>m</sup>Now know I that the LORD will do me good, seeing I have a Levite to *my* priest.
