## CHAPTER VI.

Sanballat practiseth by craft to terrify Nehemiah.

11 And I said, Should <sup>y</sup>such a man as I flee? and <sup>z</sup>who *is there that being* as I *am,* would go into the temple to save his life? <sup>a</sup>I will not go in.

12 And, lo, <sup>b</sup>I perceived that God had not sent him; but that he <sup>c</sup>pronounced this prophecy against me: for Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.

13 Therefore *was* he hired, that I should be <sup>d</sup>afraid, and do so, and sin, and *that* they might have *matter* for an evil report, that they might reproach me.

14 My God, think thou upon Tobiah and Sanballat according to these their works, and on the <sup>e</sup>prophetess Noadiah, and the rest of the prophets that would have put me in fear.

17 Moreover, in those days, the nobles of Judah <sup>l</sup>sent many letters unto Tobiah, and *the letters* of Tobiah came unto them.

18 For *there were* many in Judah sworn unto him, because he *was* the son-in-law of Shechaniah the son of <sup>m</sup>Arab; and his son Johanan had taken the daughter of <sup>n</sup>Meshullam the son of Berechiah.
