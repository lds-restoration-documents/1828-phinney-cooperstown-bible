## CHAPTER XXII.

A <sup>a</sup>*GOOD* name *is* rather to be chosen than great riches, *and* loving favour rather than silver and gold.

2 The rich and poor <sup>b</sup>meet together: the LORD *is* the <sup>c</sup>maker of them all.

3 A prudent *man* <sup>d</sup>foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple <sup>e</sup>pass on, and are punished.

4 By humility, *and* the fear of the LORD, <sup>f</sup>*are* riches, and honour, and life.

5 <sup>g</sup>Thorns *and* snares *are* in the way of the froward: he that doth keep his soul shall be <sup>h</sup>far from them.

6 <sup>i</sup>Train up a child in the way he should go; and when he is old, he will <sup>k</sup>not depart from it.

7 The rich <sup>l</sup>ruleth over the poor, and the borrower *is* servant to the lender.

8 He that <sup>m</sup>soweth iniquity shall reap vanity; and the rod of his anger shall fail.

9 He that hath <sup>n</sup>a bountiful eye shall be blessed; for he giveth of his bread to the poor.

10 Cast out the <sup>o</sup>scorner, and contention shall go out; yea, strife and reproach shall cease.

11 He that loveth <sup>p</sup>pureness of heart, *for* the grace of his lips the <sup>q</sup>king *shall be* his friend.

12 The eyes of the LORD preserve knowledge; and he <sup>r</sup>overthroweth the words of the transgressor.

13 The slothful *man* saith, *There is* <sup>s</sup>a lion without, I shall be slain in the streets.

14 The mouth of strange women *is* <sup>t</sup>a deep pit: he that is <sup>u</sup>abhorred of the LORD shall fall therein.

15 Foolishness *is* bound in the heart of a child; *but* the rod of correction shall drive it far from him.

16 He that oppresseth the poor to increase his *riches,* *and* he that <sup>x</sup>giveth to the rich, *shall surely come* to want.

17 Bow down thine ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply thine heart unto <sup>y</sup>my knowledge.

18 For *it is* a pleasant thing if thou keep them within thee; they shall withal be fitted in thy lips.

19 That <sup>z</sup>thy trust may be in the LORD, I have made known to thee this day, even to thee.

20 Have not I written to thee excellent things in counsels and knowledge,

21 That I might make thee know the certainty of the <sup>a</sup>words of truth, that thou mightest answer the words of truth to them that send unto thee?

22 Rob not the poor, because he *is* poor; neither <sup>b</sup>oppress the afflicted in the gate;

23 For the LORD will <sup>c</sup>plead their cause, and <sup>d</sup>spoil the soul of those that spoiled them.

24 Make no friendship with an angry man, and with a <sup>e</sup>furious man thou shalt not go;

25 Lest thou <sup>f</sup>learn his ways, and get a snare to thy soul.

26 Be not thou *one* of them that <sup>g</sup>strike hands, *or* of them that are sureties for debts.

27 If thou hast nothing to pay, why should he take away thy <sup>h</sup>bed from under thee?

28 Remove not the ancient <sup>i</sup>landmark which thy fathers have set.

29 Seest thou a man <sup>k</sup>diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean *men.*

## CHAPTER XXIII.

WHEN thou sittest to eat with a ruler, <sup>a</sup>consider diligently what *is* before thee;

2 And put <sup>b</sup>a knife to thy throat, if thou *be* a man given to appetite.

3 Be not desirous of his dainties; for they *are* <sup>c</sup>deceitful meat.

4 Labour not to be <sup>d</sup>rich; cease from thine <sup>e</sup>own wisdom.

5 Wilt thou set thine eyes upon that which <sup>f</sup>is not? for *riches* certainly make themselves wings; they fly away, as an eagle toward heaven.

6 Eat thou not the bread of *him that hath* an <sup>g</sup>evil eye, neither desire thou his dainty meats:

7 For as he thinketh in his heart, so *is* he: Eat and drink, <sup>h</sup>saith he to thee; but his heart *is* not with thee.

8 <sup>i</sup>The morsel *which* thou hast eaten shalt thou vomit up, and lose thy sweet words.

9 Speak not in the ears of a fool; for he will despise the wisdom of thy words.

10 Remove not the old landmark; and enter not into the <sup>k</sup>fields of the fatherless:

11 For their <sup>l</sup>Redeemer *is* mighty; he shall plead their cause with thee.

12 <sup>m</sup>Apply thine heart unto instruction, and thine ears to the words of knowledge.

13 Withhold not <sup>n</sup>correction from the child: for *if* thou beatest him with the rod, he shall not die.

14 Thou shalt beat him with the rod, and shalt deliver his soul <sup>o</sup>from hell.

15 My son, if thine heart be wise, my heart shall <sup>p</sup>rejoice, even mine;

16 Yea, my reins shall rejoice when thy lips speak right things.

17 Let not thine heart <sup>q</sup>envy sinners: but *be thou* in the fear of the LORD all the day long.

18 For surely there is an <sup>r</sup>end; and thine expectation shall not be cut off.

19 Hear thou, my son, and be wise, and <sup>s</sup>guide thine heart in the way.

20 Be not among <sup>t</sup>wine-bibbers; among riotous eaters of flesh:

21 For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty; and drowsiness shall clothe *a man* with <sup>u</sup>rags.

22 Hearken unto thy father that begat thee, and despise not thy mother when she is old.

23 <sup>x</sup>Buy the truth, and <sup>y</sup>sell *it* not; *also* wisdom, and instruction, and understanding.

24 The father of the righteous shall greatly rejoice; and he that begetteth a wise *child* shall have joy of him.

25 Thy father and thy mother shall be glad, and she that bare thee shall rejoice.

26 My son, <sup>z</sup>give me thine heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways.

27 For a whore *is* a deep ditch; and a <sup>a</sup>strange woman *is* a narrow pit.
