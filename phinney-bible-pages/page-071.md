**Header:** *The sacrifice and ceremonies* | CHAPTER XXIX. | *of consecrating the Priest.*

5 And thou shalt take the garments, and <sup>g</sup>put upon Aaron the coat, and the robe of the ephod, and the ephod, and the breastplate, and <sup>h</sup>gird him with the curious girdle of the ephod:

6 And thou shalt put the <sup>i</sup>mitre upon his head, and put the <sup>k</sup>holy crown upon the mitre.

7 Then shalt thou take the anointing oil, and pour *it* upon his head, and <sup>l</sup>anoint him.

8 And thou shalt bring his sons, and put coats upon them.

9 And thou shalt gird them with girdles, Aaron and his sons, and put the bonnets on them: and the priest's office shall be theirs for a perpetual statute: and thou shalt <sup>m</sup>consecrate Aaron and his sons.

10 And thou shalt cause a bullock to be brought before the tabernacle of the congregation: and Aaron and his sons shall <sup>n</sup>put their hands upon the head of the bullock.

11 And thou shalt kill the bullock before the LORD, *by* the <sup>o</sup>door of the tabernacle of the congregation.

12 And thou shalt take of the blood of the bullock, and put *it* upon the horns of the <sup>p</sup>altar with thy <sup>q</sup>finger, and pour all the blood beside the bottom of the altar.

13 And thou shalt take all the fat that covereth the inwards, and the caul *that is* above the liver, and the two kidneys, and the <sup>r</sup>fat that *is* upon them, and burn *them* upon the altar.

14 But the flesh of the bullock, and his skin, and his dung, shalt thou burn with fire <sup>s</sup>without the camp: it *is* a sin-offering.

15 Thou shalt also take one ram; and Aaron and his sons shall put their hands upon the head of the ram.

16 And thou shalt slay the ram, and thou shalt take his blood, and sprinkle *it* round about upon the altar.

17 And thou shalt cut the ram in pieces, and <sup>t</sup>wash the inwards of him, and his legs, and put *them* unto his pieces, and unto his head.

18 And thou shalt <sup>u</sup>burn the whole ram upon the altar: it *is* a burnt-offering unto the LORD: it *is* a <sup>x</sup>sweet savour, an offering made by <sup>y</sup>fire unto the LORD.

19 And thou shalt take the other ram; and Aaron and his sons shall put <sup>z</sup>their hands upon the head of the ram.

20 Then shalt thou kill the ram, and take of his blood, and put *it* upon the <sup>a</sup>tip of the right ear of Aaron, and upon the tip of the right ear of his sons, and upon the thumb of their right <sup>b</sup>hand, and upon the great toe of their right <sup>c</sup>foot, and sprinkle the blood upon the <sup>d</sup>altar round about.

21 And thou shalt take of the blood that *is* upon the altar, and of <sup>e</sup>the anointing oil, and <sup>f</sup>sprinkle *it* upon Aaron, and upon his garments, and upon his sons, and upon the garments of his sons with him: and he shall be hallowed, and his garments, and his sons, and his sons' garments with him.

22 Also thou shalt take of the ram the fat and the rump, and the fat that covereth the inwards, and the caul *above* the liver, and the two kidneys, and the fat that *is* upon them, and the <sup>g</sup>right shoulder; for it *is* a ram of consecration:

23 And one <sup>h</sup>loaf of bread, and one cake of oiled bread, and one wafer out of the basket of the unleavened bread that *is* before the LORD:

24 And thou shalt put all in the hands of Aaron, and in the hands of his sons, and shalt <sup>i</sup>wave them *for* a wave-offering before the LORD.

25 And thou shalt receive them of their hands, and burn *them* upon the altar for a burnt-offering, for <sup>k</sup>a sweet savour before the LORD: it *is* an offering made by fire unto the LORD.

26 And thou shalt take the breast of the ram of Aaron's consecration, and wave it *for* a wave-offering before the LORD: and it shall be <sup>l</sup>thy part.

27 And thou shalt sanctify the breast of the wave-offering, and the shoulder of the <sup>m</sup>heave-offering, which is waved, and which is heaved up, of the ram of the consecration, *even* of *that* which *is* for Aaron, and of *that* which *is* for his sons:

28 And it shall be Aaron's and his sons' by a statute <sup>n</sup>for ever from the children of Israel; for it *is* a heave-offering: and it shall be a heave-offering from the children of Israel of the sacrifice of their peace-offerings, *even* their heave-offering unto the LORD.

29 And the holy garments of Aaron shall be his sons' after him, to be <sup>o</sup>anointed therein, and to be consecrated in them.

30 *And* that son that is priest in his stead shall put them on <sup>p</sup>seven days, when he cometh into the tabernacle of the congregation, to minister in the holy *place.*

31 And thou shalt take the ram of the consecration, and seethe his flesh in the <sup>q</sup>holy place.

32 And <sup>r</sup>Aaron and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram, and the bread that *is* in the basket, *by* the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.

33 And they shall eat those things wherewith the <sup>s</sup>atonement was made to consecrate *and* to sanctify them: but <sup>t</sup>a stranger shall not eat *thereof,* because they *are* holy.

34 And if aught of the flesh of the consecrations, or of the bread, remain unto the morning, then thou shalt burn the <sup>u</sup>remainder with fire: it shall not be eaten, because it *is* holy.

35 And thus shalt thou do unto Aaron, and to his sons, according to all *things* which I have commanded thee: seven days shalt thou consecrate them.

36 And thou shalt offer every day a bullock *for* a sin-offering for <sup>x</sup>atonement: and thou shalt <sup>y</sup>cleanse the altar, when thou hast made an atonement for it, and thou shalt anoint it, to sanctify it.

37 Seven days thou shalt make an atonement for the altar, and sanctify it; and it shall be an altar <sup>z</sup>most holy: whatsoever <sup>a</sup>toucheth the altar shall be holy.

38 ¶ Now this *is that* which thou shalt offer upon the altar; two lambs of the first year, <sup>b</sup>day by day continually.

39 The one lamb thou shalt offer in the morning, and the other lamb thou shalt offer <sup>c</sup>at even:

40 And with the one lamb a tenth-deal of flour mingled with <sup>d</sup>the fourth part of a hin of beaten oil; and the fourth part of a hin of wine *for* a drink-offering.

41 And the other lamb thou shalt offer at even, and shalt do thereto according to the meat-offering of the morning, and according to the drink-offering thereof, for a sweet savour, an offering made by fire unto the LORD.

42 *This shall be* a <sup>e</sup>continual burnt-offering throughout your generations, *at* the <sup>f</sup>door of the tabernacle of the congregation, before the LORD; where I will <sup>g</sup>meet you, to speak there unto thee.

43 And there I will meet with the children of Israel, and *the tabernacle* shall be sanctified <sup>h</sup>by my glory.
