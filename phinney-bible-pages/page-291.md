## CHAPTER XIX.

David sendeth messengers to comfort Hanun the son of Nahash.

NOW <sup>a</sup>it came to pass after this, that <sup>b</sup>Nahash the king of the children of Ammon died, and his <sup>c</sup>son reigned in his stead.

2 And David said, I will shew kindness unto Hanun the son of Nahash, <sup>d</sup>because his father shewed kindness to me. And David sent messengers to comfort him concerning his father. So the servants of David came into the land of the children of Ammon to Hanun, <sup>e</sup>to comfort him.

3 But the princes of the children of Ammon said to Hanun, <sup>†</sup>Thinkest thou that David doth honour thy father, that he hath sent comforters unto thee? are not his servants come unto thee for to search, and to overthrow, and <sup>f</sup>to spy out the land?

4 Wherefore Hanun took David's servants, and <sup>g</sup>shaved them, and cut off their garments in the midst, hard by their <sup>h</sup>buttocks, and sent them away.

5 Then there went *certain,* and told David how the men were served. And he sent to meet them; (for the men were greatly ashamed.) And the king said, Tarry at <sup>i</sup>Jericho until your beards be grown, and *then* return.

6 And when the children of Ammon saw that they had made themselves <sup>†</sup>odious to David, Hanun and the children of Ammon sent a thousand talents of silver to hire them chariots and horsemen out of Mesopotamia, and out of Syria-maachah, and out of Zobah.

7 So they hired <sup>k</sup>thirty and two thousand chariots, and the king of Maachah and his people, who came and pitched before <sup>l</sup>Medeba. And the children of Ammon gathered themselves together from their cities, and came to battle.

8 And when David heard *of it,* he sent <sup>m</sup>Joab, and all the host of the <sup>n</sup>mighty men.

9 And the children of Ammon came out, and put the battle in array before the gate of the <sup>o</sup>city; and <sup>p</sup>the kings that were come *were* by themselves in the field.

10 Now when Joab saw that the <sup>†</sup>battle was set against him before and behind, and chose out of all the choice of Israel, and put *them* in array against the Syrians.

11 And the rest of the people he delivered unto the hand of <sup>q</sup>Abishai his brother, and they set *themselves* in array against the children of Ammon.

12 And he said, If the Syrians be too strong for me, then thou shalt <sup>†</sup>help me: but if the children of Ammon be too strong for thee, then I will help thee.

13 <sup>r</sup>Be of good courage, and <sup>s</sup>let us behave ourselves valiantly for our people, and for the cities of our God; and let the LORD do *that which is* good in his sight.

14 So Joab, and the people that *were* with him, drew nigh before the Syrians unto the battle; and they fled before him.

15 And when the children of Ammon saw that the Syrians were fled, they likewise <sup>t</sup>fled before Abishai his brother, and entered into the city. Then Joab came to Jerusalem.

16 And when the Syrians saw that they were <sup>†</sup>put to the worse before Israel, they sent messengers, and drew forth the Syrians that *were* beyond the river; and <sup>u</sup>Shophach, the captain of the host of Hadarezer, *went* before them.

17 And it was told David; and he gathered all Israel, and <sup>x</sup>passed over Jordan, and came upon them, and set *the battle* in array against them. So when David had put the battle in array against the Syrians, they fought with him.

18 But the Syrians fled before Israel; and David slew of the Syrians seven thousand *men which fought in* chariots, and <sup>y</sup>forty thousand footmen, and killed Shophach the captain of the host.

19 And when the servants of Hadarezer saw that they were put to the worse before Israel, they made peace with David, and became his <sup>z</sup>servants; neither would the Syrians help the children of Ammon <sup>a</sup>any more.

## CHAPTER XX.

Rabbah is besieged by Joab, and spoiled by David.

AND it came to pass, that <sup>†</sup>after the year was expired, at the time that <sup>a</sup>kings go out to *battle,* Joab led forth the power of the army, and wasted the country of the children of Ammon, and came and besieged Rabbah; (but David <sup>b</sup>tarried at Jerusalem.) And Joab smote Rabbah, and destroyed it.

2 And David took the <sup>c</sup>crown of their king from off his head, and found it to weigh a talent of gold, and *there were* precious stones in it; and it was set upon David's head: and he brought also exceeding much spoil out of the city.

3 And he brought out the people that *were* in it, and cut *them* with <sup>d</sup>saws, and with harrows of iron, and with axes: even so dealt David with all the cities of the children of Ammon. And David and all the people returned to Jerusalem.

5 And there was war again with the Philistines; and Elhanan the son of <sup>h</sup>Jair slew Lahmi, the brother of Goliath the Gittite, whose spear's staff *was* like a weaver's beam.
