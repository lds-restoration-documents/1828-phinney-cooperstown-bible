24 And Gideon said unto them, I would desire a request of you, that ye would give me every man the ear-rings of his prey: (for they had golden <sup>c</sup>ear-rings, because they *were* <sup>d</sup>Ishmaelites.)

25 And they answered, We will willingly give *them.* And they spread a garment, and did cast therein every man the ear-rings of his prey.

26 And the weight of the golden ear-rings that he requested was a thousand and seven hundred *shekels* of gold, besides ornaments, and collars, and purple raiment that *was* on the kings of Midian, and besides the chains that *were* about their camels' necks.

27 And Gideon <sup>e</sup>made an <sup>f</sup>ephod thereof, and <sup>g</sup>put it in his city, *even* in Ophrah: and all Israel went thither a <sup>h</sup>whoring after it; which thing became <sup>i</sup>a snare unto Gideon, and to his house.

28 Thus was Midian subdued before the children of Israel, so that they lifted up their heads no more: and the country was in quietness <sup>k</sup>forty years in the days of Gideon.

29 ¶ And Jerubbaal the son of Joash, went and <sup>l</sup>dwelt in his own house.

30 And Gideon had threescore and ten sons <sup>†</sup>of his body begotten: for he had many wives.

31 And his <sup>m</sup>concubine, that *was* in Shechem, she also bare him a son, whose name he called Abimelech.

32 And Gideon, the son of Joash, died in <sup>n</sup>a good old age, and was buried in the sepulchre of Joash his father, in Ophrah of the Abi-ezrites.

33 And it came to pass, <sup>o</sup>as soon as Gideon was dead, that the children of Israel turned again, and went a whoring after Baalim, and made Baal-berith their god.

34 And the children of Israel <sup>p</sup>remembered not the LORD their God, who had delivered them out of the hands of all their enemies on every side:

35 Neither <sup>q</sup>shewed they kindness to the house of Jerubbaal, *namely* Gideon, according to all the goodness which he had shewed unto Israel.

## CHAPTER IX.

Abimelech, by conspiracy with the Shechemites, and murder of his brethren, is made king.

AND Abimelech the son of Jerubbaal, went to <sup>a</sup>Shechem unto his mother's brethren, and <sup>b</sup>communed with them, and with all the family of the house of his mother's father, saying,

2 Speak, I pray you, in the ears of all the men of Shechem, Whether *is* better for you either that all the sons of Jerubbaal, *which are* threescore and ten persons, reign over you, or that one reign over you? remember also that I *am* your bone and your flesh.

3 And his mother's brethren <sup>c</sup>spake of him in the ears of all the men of Shechem all these words: and their hearts inclined to follow Abimelech; for they said, He *is* our brother.

4 And they gave him threescore and ten *pieces* of silver out of the house of Baal-berith; wherewith Abimelech hired <sup>d</sup>vain and light persons, which followed him.

5 And he went unto his father's house at Ophrah, and <sup>e</sup>slew his brethren, the sons of Jerubbaal, *being* threescore and ten persons, upon one stone: notwithstanding, yet Jotham, the youngest son of Jerubbaal, was left: for he hid himself.

6 And all the men of Shechem gathered together, and all the house of <sup>f</sup>Millo, and went and <sup>†</sup>made Abimelech king, by the <sup>||</sup>plain of the pillar that *was* in Shechem.

7 And when they told *it* to Jotham, he went and stood in the top of <sup>g</sup>mount Gerizim, and lifted up his voice, and cried, and said unto them, Hearken unto me, ye men of Shechem, that God may hearken unto you.

8 The <sup>h</sup>trees went forth *on a time* to anoint a king over them: and they said unto the olive-tree, Reign thou over us.

9 But the olive-tree said unto them, Should I leave my fatness, <sup>i</sup>wherewith by me they honour God and man, and <sup>†</sup>go to be promoted over the trees?

10 And the trees said to the fig-tree, Come thou, *and* reign over us.

11 But the fig-tree said unto them, <sup>k</sup>Should I forsake my sweetness, and my good fruit, and go to be promoted over the trees?

12 Then said the trees unto the vine, Come thou, *and* reign over us.

13 And the vine said unto them, Should I leave my wine, <sup>l</sup>which cheereth God and man, and go to be promoted over the trees?

14 Then said all the trees unto the bramble, Come thou, *and* reign over us.

15 And the bramble said unto the trees, If in truth ye anoint me king over you, *then* come *and* put your trust in my <sup>m</sup>shadow; and if not, let <sup>n</sup>fire come out of the bramble, and devour the <sup>o</sup>cedars of Lebanon.

16 Now therefore, if ye have done truly and sincerely in that ye have made Abimelech king, and if ye have dealt well with Jerubbaal and his house, and have done unto him according to the deserving of his hands;

17 (For my father fought for you, and <sup>†</sup>adventured his life far, and delivered you out of the hand of Midian;

18 And ye are risen up against my father's house this day, and have slain his sons, threescore and ten persons, upon one stone, and have made Abimelech, the <sup>p</sup>son of his maid-servant, king over the men of Shechem, because he *is* your brother;)

19 If ye then have dealt truly and sincerely with Jerubbaal and with his house this day, *then* rejoice ye in Abimelech, and let him also <sup>q</sup>rejoice in you;

20 But if not, <sup>r</sup>let fire come out from Abimelech, and devour the men of Shechem, and the house of Millo; and let fire come out from the men of Shechem, and from the house of Millo, and devour Abimelech.

21 And Jotham ran away, and fled, and went to <sup>s</sup>Beer, and dwelt there, for fear of Abimelech his brother.

22 ¶ When Abimelech had reigned three years over Israel,

23 Then <sup>t</sup>God sent an <sup>u</sup>evil spirit between Abimelech and the men of Shechem; and the men of Shechem <sup>x</sup>dealt treacherously with Abimelech:

24 That the <sup>y</sup>cruelty *done* to the threescore and ten sons of Jerubbaal might come, and their blood be laid upon Abimelech their brother, which slew them; and upon the men of Shechem, which aided him in the killing of his brethren.

25 And the men of Shechem set <sup>z</sup>liers in wait for him in the top of the mountains, and they robbed all that came along that way by them: and it was told Abimelech.

26 And Gaal, the son of Ebed, came with his brethren, and went over to Shechem: and the men of Shechem <sup>a</sup>put their confidence in him.
