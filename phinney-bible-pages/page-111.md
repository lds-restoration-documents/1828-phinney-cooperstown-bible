7 And those men said unto him, We *are* defiled by the dead body of a man: wherefore are we kept back, that we may not offer <sup>g</sup>an offering of the LORD in his appointed season among the children of Israel?

8 And Moses said unto them, Stand still, and I will hear <sup>h</sup>what the LORD will command concerning you.

9 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

10 Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If any man of you or of your posterity shall be unclean by reason of a dead body, or *be* in a journey <sup>i</sup>afar off, yet he shall keep the passover unto the LORD.

11 The fourteenth day of the second month at even they shall keep it, *and* <sup>k</sup>eat it with <sup>l</sup>unleavened bread and <sup>m</sup>bitter *herbs*.

12 They shall leave none of it <sup>n</sup>unto the morning, <sup>o</sup>nor break any bone of it: according to all the ordinances of the passover they shall keep it.

13 But the man that *is* clean, and is not in a journey, and <sup>p</sup>forbeareth to keep the passover, even the same soul shall be <sup>q</sup>cut off <sup>r</sup>from his people: because he brought not the offering of the LORD in his appointed season, that man shall bear <sup>s</sup>his sin.

14 And if a stranger shall <sup>t</sup>sojourn among you, and will keep the passover unto the LORD; according to the ordinance of the passover, and according to the <sup>u</sup>manner thereof, so shall he do: ye shall have one ordinance, both for the stranger, and for him that was born in the land.

15 ¶ And on the day that the tabernacle was <sup>x</sup>reared up, the <sup>y</sup>cloud covered the tabernacle, *namely*, the <sup>z</sup>tent of the testimony: and at even there was upon the tabernacle as it were the <sup>a</sup>appearance of fire, until the morning.

16 So it was always: the <sup>b</sup>cloud covered it *by day*, and the appearance of fire by night.

17 And when the cloud was taken up from the tabernacle, then <sup>c</sup>after that the children of Israel journeyed: and in the place where the cloud abode, there the children of Israel pitched their tents.

18 At the commandment of the LORD the children of Israel journeyed, and <sup>d</sup>at the commandment of the LORD they pitched: as long as the cloud abode upon the tabernacle they rested in their tents.

19 And when the cloud <sup>e</sup>tarried long upon the tabernacle many days, then the children of Israel kept the charge of the LORD, and journeyed not.

20 And *so* it was, when the cloud was a few days upon the tabernacle: according to the commandment of the LORD they abode in their tents, and according to the commandment of the LORD they journeyed.

21 And *so* it was, when the cloud abode from even unto the morning, and *that* the cloud was taken up in the morning, then they journeyed; whether *it was* by day or by night that the cloud was taken up, they journeyed.

22 Or *whether it were* two days, or a month, or a year, that the cloud tarried upon the tabernacle, remaining thereon, the children of Israel abode in their tents, and journeyed not: but when it was taken up, they journeyed.

23 <sup>f</sup>At the commandment of the LORD they rested in their tents, and at the commandment of the LORD they journeyed: they kept the charge of the LORD, at the commandment of the LORD by the <sup>g</sup>hand of Moses.

AND the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2 Make thee two <sup>a</sup>trumpets of silver: of a <sup>b</sup>whole piece shalt thou make <sup>c</sup>them, that thou mayest use them for the calling of the assembly, and for the journeying of the camps.

3 And when they shall blow with them, all the assembly shall assemble themselves to thee at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.

4 And if they blow *but* with one *trumpet*, then the princes, *which are* <sup>d</sup>heads of the thousands of Israel, shall gather themselves unto thee.

5 When ye blow an <sup>e</sup>alarm, then the camps that lie on the <sup>f</sup>east parts shall go forward.

6 When ye blow an alarm the second time, then the camps that lie on the <sup>g</sup>south side shall take their journey: they shall blow an alarm for their journeys.

7 But when the congregation is to be gathered together, ye shall blow, but ye shall not sound an alarm.

8 And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow with the trumpets; and they shall be to you for an ordinance for ever throughout your generations.

9 And if ye go to war in your land against the enemy that <sup>h</sup>oppresseth you, then ye shall blow an alarm with <sup>i</sup>the trumpets; and ye shall <sup>k</sup>be remembered before the LORD your God, and ye shall be saved from your enemies.

10 Also in the day of your <sup>l</sup>gladness, and in your <sup>m</sup>solemn days, and in the <sup>n</sup>beginnings of your months, ye shall blow <sup>o</sup>with the trumpets over your <sup>p</sup>burnt-offerings, and over the sacrifices of your <sup>q</sup>peace-offerings; that they may be to you for a memorial before your God: I *am* the LORD your God.

11 ¶ And it came to pass on the twentieth *day* of the <sup>r</sup>second month, in the <sup>s</sup>second year, that the <sup>t</sup>cloud was taken up from off the tabernacle of the testimony.

12 And the children of Israel took their journeys out of the wilderness of <sup>u</sup>Sinai; and the cloud rested in the wilderness of <sup>x</sup>Paran.

13 And they first took their journey according to the <sup>y</sup>commandment of the LORD by the hand of <sup>z</sup>Moses.

14 In the first *place* went the standard of the camp of the children <sup>a</sup>of Judah according to their armies: and over his host *was* Nahshon the son of Amminadab.

15 And over the host of the tribe of the children of Issachar *was* Nethaneel, the son of Zuar.

16 And over the host of the tribe of the children of Zebulun *was* Eliab the son of Helon.

17 And the tabernacle was <sup>b</sup>taken down; and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari set forward, <sup>c</sup>bearing the tabernacle.

18 And the standard of the camp of <sup>d</sup>Reuben set forward according to their armies: and over his host *was* Elizur the son of Shedeur.

19 And over the host of the tribe of the children of Simeon *was* Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.

20 And over the host of the tribe of the children of Gad *was* Eliasaph the son of Deuel.

21 And the Kohathites set forward, bearing the <sup>e</sup>sanctuary: and *the other* did set up the tabernacle against they came.

22 And the standard of the camp of the children of Ephraim set forward according to their armies: and over his host *was* Elishama the son of Ammihud.
