## PSALM CL.

An exhortation to praise God with all kind of instruments.

PRAISE ye the LORD. Praise God in his sanctuary: praise him in the <sup>a</sup>firmament of his power.

2 Praise him for his <sup>b</sup>mighty acts: praise him according to his <sup>c</sup>excellent greatness.

3 Praise him with the sound of the trumpet: praise him with the psaltery and harp.

4 Praise him with the timbrel and dance: praise him with stringed instruments and organs.

5 Praise him upon the loud cymbals: praise him upon the high-sounding cymbals.

6 Let <sup>d</sup>every thing that hath breath praise the LORD. <sup>e</sup>Praise ye the LORD.

# ¶ The PROVERBS.

## CHAPTER I.

1 The use of the proverbs....7 An exhortation to fear God and believe his word.

THE <sup>a</sup>Proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel;

2 To know <sup>b</sup>wisdom and instruction; <sup>c</sup>to perceive the words of understanding;

3 To receive the instruction <sup>d</sup>of wisdom, justice, and judgment, and <sup>e</sup>equity;

4 To give <sup>f</sup>subtilty to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion.

5 A wise *man* will hear, and <sup>g</sup>will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels;

6 To understand a proverb, and the interpretation; the words of the wise, and their <sup>h</sup>dark sayings.

7 The <sup>i</sup>fear of the LORD *is* the beginning of knowledge: *but* fools despise wisdom and instruction.

8 My son, <sup>k</sup>hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother:

9 For they *shall be* <sup>l</sup>an ornament of grace unto thy head, and chains about thy neck.

10 My son, if sinners <sup>m</sup>entice thee, <sup>n</sup>consent thou not.

11 If they <sup>o</sup>say, <sup>p</sup>Come with us, let us lay wait for blood, let us lurk privily for the innocent without cause:

12 Let us <sup>q</sup>swallow them up alive, as the grave; and whole, as those that go down into the pit:

13 We shall find all <sup>r</sup>precious substance, we shall <sup>s</sup>fill our houses with spoil:

14 Cast in thy lot among us; let us all have one purse.

15 My son, <sup>t</sup>walk not thou in the way with them; refrain thy foot from their path:

16 For their <sup>u</sup>feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood:

17 (Surely in vain the net is spread in the sight of any bird:)

18 And they lay wait <sup>x</sup>for their *own* blood; they lurk privily for their *own* lives.

19 So *are* the ways of every one that <sup>y</sup>is greedy of gain; *which* <sup>z</sup>taketh away the life of the owners thereof.

20 <sup>a</sup>Wisdom crieth without; she <sup>b</sup>uttereth her voice in the streets;

21 She crieth in the <sup>c</sup>chief place of concourse, in the openings of the gates: in the city she uttereth her words, *saying,*

22 <sup>d</sup>How long, ye simple ones, will ye love simplicity, and the scorners delight in their scorning, and fools hate knowledge?

23 Turn you at my reproof; behold, I will <sup>†</sup>pour out my Spirit unto you, I will make known my words unto you.

24 Because I have <sup>e</sup>called, and ye refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no man regarded;

25 But ye have <sup>f</sup>set at nought all my counsel, and would none of my reproof;

26 I also will <sup>g</sup>laugh at your calamity; I will mock when your <sup>h</sup>fear cometh.

27 When your fear cometh as desolation, and your <sup>i</sup>destruction cometh as a whirlwind; when distress and anguish cometh upon you;

28 Then shall they call upon me, but I will <sup>k</sup>not answer; they shall seek me early, but they shall not find me:

29 For that they <sup>l</sup>hated knowledge, and did <sup>m</sup>not choose the fear of the LORD:

30 They would <sup>n</sup>none of my counsel; they despised all my reproof:

31 Therefore shall they <sup>o</sup>eat of the fruit of their own way, and be <sup>p</sup>filled with their own devices.

32 For the turning away of the simple shall slay them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them.

33 But whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell safely, and shall be <sup>q</sup>quiet from fear of evil.

## CHAPTER II.

Wisdom promiseth godliness to her children.

MY son, if thou wilt receive my words, and <sup>a</sup>hide my commandments with thee;

2 So that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom, *and* <sup>b</sup>apply thy heart to understanding;

3 Yea, if thou <sup>c</sup>criest after knowledge, *and* liftest up thy voice for understanding;

4 If thou <sup>d</sup>seekest her as silver, and <sup>e</sup>searchest for her as *for* hid treasures;

5 Then shalt thou understand the fear of the LORD, and <sup>f</sup>find the knowledge of God:

6 For the LORD <sup>g</sup>giveth wisdom: out of his mouth *cometh* knowledge and understanding;

7 He layeth up <sup>h</sup>sound wisdom for the righteous: *he is* a buckler to them that walk uprightly.

8 He keepeth the <sup>i</sup>paths of judgment, and <sup>k</sup>preserveth the way of his saints.

9 Then shalt thou understand righteousness, and judgment, and equity; *yea,* every <sup>l</sup>good path.

10 When wisdom entereth into thy heart, and knowledge is <sup>m</sup>pleasant unto thy soul,

11 Discretion shall preserve thee, understanding shall keep thee;

12 To deliver thee from the way of the <sup>n</sup>evil *man,* from the man that speaketh froward things;

13 Who leave the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways <sup>o</sup>of darkness;

14 Who <sup>p</sup>rejoice to do evil, *and* delight in the frowardness of the wicked;

15 Whose ways *are* crooked, and *they* <sup>q</sup>froward in their paths:

16 To deliver thee from the <sup>r</sup>strange woman, *even* from the stranger *which* <sup>s</sup>flattereth with her words;

17 Which forsaketh the guide of her youth, and forgetteth the <sup>t</sup>covenant of her God.
