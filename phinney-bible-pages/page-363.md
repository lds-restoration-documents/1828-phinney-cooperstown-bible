## PSALM III.

The security of God's protection.

*A Psalm of David, when he* <sup>a</sup>*fled from Absalom his son.*

LORD, how are they <sup>b</sup>increased that trouble me? many *are* they that rise up against me.

2 Many *there be* which say of my soul, *There is* <sup>c</sup>no help for him in God. Selah.

3 But thou, O LORD, *art* <sup>d</sup>a shield for me; my glory, and the <sup>e</sup>lifter up of my head.

4 I cried unto the LORD with my voice, and he <sup>f</sup>heard me out of his holy hill. Selah.

5 <sup>g</sup>I laid me down and slept; I awaked: for the LORD sustained me.

6 I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people, that have <sup>h</sup>set *themselves* against me round about.

7 <sup>i</sup>Arise, O LORD; save me, O my God: for thou hast smitten all mine enemies *upon* the <sup>k</sup>cheek-bone; thou hast broken the teeth of the ungodly.

8 <sup>l</sup>Salvation *belongeth* unto the LORD: <sup>m</sup>thy blessing *is* upon thy people. Selah.

## PSALM IV.

1 David prayeth for audience....2 He reproveth and exhorteth his enemies....6 Man's happiness is in God's favour.

*To the chief Musician on Neginoth.*

*A Psalm of David.*

HEAR me when I call, O God of <sup>a</sup>my righteousness: <sup>b</sup>thou hast enlarged me *when I was* in distress; have mercy upon me, and hear my prayer.

2 O ye sons of men, how long *will ye turn* <sup>c</sup>my glory into shame? *how long* will ye love vanity, *and* seek after leasing? Selah.

3 But know that the LORD hath set apart him that is godly <sup>d</sup>for himself: the LORD will hear when I call unto him.

4 <sup>e</sup>Stand in awe, and sin not: <sup>f</sup>commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be <sup>g</sup>still. Selah.

5 Offer the <sup>h</sup>sacrifices of righteousness; and put your <sup>i</sup>trust in the LORD.

6 *There be* many that say, Who will shew us *any* <sup>k</sup>good? LORD, lift thou up the <sup>l</sup>light of thy countenance upon us.

7 Thou hast put <sup>m</sup>gladness in my heart, more than in the time *that* their <sup>n</sup>corn and their wine increased.

8 I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell <sup>o</sup>in safety.

## PSALM V.

1 David prayeth, and professeth his study in prayer....7 David, professing his faith, prayeth unto God to guide him.

*To the chief Musician upon Nehiloth.*

*A Psalm of David.*

GIVE ear to my words, O LORD; consider my meditation.

2 <sup>a</sup>Hearken unto the voice of my cry, my King, and my God: for unto thee will I pray.

3 My voice shalt thou hear in the <sup>b</sup>morning, O LORD; in the morning will I <sup>†</sup>direct *my prayer* unto thee, and will <sup>c</sup>look up.

4 For thou *art* not a God that hath <sup>d</sup>pleasure in wickedness; neither shall evil <sup>e</sup>dwell with thee.

5 The foolish shall <sup>f</sup>not stand in thy sight: thou hatest all workers of iniquity.

6 Thou shalt destroy them that speak <sup>g</sup>leasing: the LORD will abhor <sup>†</sup>the bloody and deceitful man.

7 But as <sup>h</sup>for me, I will come *into* thy house in the multitude of thy mercy; *and* in thy fear will I worship toward thy holy temple.

8 <sup>i</sup>Lead me, O LORD, in thy righteousness, <sup>k</sup>because of mine enemies; make thy way straight before my face.

9 For *there is* <sup>l</sup>no faithfulness in their mouth; their inward part *is* very wickedness; their throat *is* <sup>m</sup>an open sepulchre; they flatter with their tongue.

10 Destroy thou them, O God; let them fall <sup>n</sup>by their own counsels: cast them out in the multitude of their transgressions; for they have rebelled against thee.

11 But let all those that put their trust in thee <sup>o</sup>rejoice: let them ever shout for joy, because thou <sup>p</sup>defendest them: let them also that love thy name be joyful in thee.

12 For thou, LORD, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou <sup>q</sup>compass him as *with* a shield.

## PSALM VI.

David's complaint in his sickness.

*To the chief Musician on Neginoth upon Sheminith.*

*A Psalm of David.*

O LORD, rebuke me not in thine anger, neither chasten me in thy <sup>a</sup>hot displeasure.

2 Have mercy upon me, O LORD; <sup>b</sup>for I *am* weak: O LORD, <sup>c</sup>heal me; for my bones are vexed.

3 My soul is also sore vexed: but thou, O LORD, <sup>d</sup>how long?

4 Return, O LORD, <sup>e</sup>deliver my soul: O save me for thy mercies' sake.

5 For <sup>f</sup>in death *there is* no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks?

6 I am weary with my groaning; <sup>g</sup>all the night make I my bed to swim: I water my couch with my tears.

7 Mine eye is <sup>h</sup>consumed because of grief; it waxeth old because of all mine enemies.

8 <sup>i</sup>Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity: for the LORD <sup>k</sup>hath heard the voice of my weeping.

9 The LORD hath heard my supplication; the LORD will receive my prayer.

10 Let all mine enemies <sup>l</sup>be ashamed and sore vexed: let them <sup>m</sup>return *and* be ashamed suddenly.

## PSALM VII.

David prayeth against the malice of his enemies.

*Shiggaion of David, which he sang unto the LORD, concerning the words of* <sup>a</sup>*Cush the Benjamite.*

O LORD my God, in thee do I put my trust; <sup>b</sup>save me from all them that persecute me, and deliver me;

2 Lest he tear my soul like a lion, rending *it* in pieces, while *there is* <sup>c</sup>none to deliver.

3 O LORD my God, <sup>d</sup>if I have done this; if there be iniquity in my hands;

4 If I have rewarded evil unto him that was <sup>e</sup>at peace with me; (yea, I have <sup>f</sup>delivered him that without cause is mine enemy;)

5 Let the enemy persecute my soul, and take *it;* yea, let him tread down my life upon the earth, and lay mine <sup>g</sup>honour in the dust. Selah.

6 Arise, O LORD, in thine anger; <sup>h</sup>lift up thyself, because of the rage of mine enemies; and awake for me *to* the judgment *that* thou hast <sup>i</sup>commanded.

7 So shall the congregation of the people <sup>k</sup>compass thee about: for their sakes, therefore, return thou <sup>l</sup>on high.

8 The LORD shall judge the people: <sup>m</sup>judge me, O LORD, according to <sup>n</sup>my righteousness, and according to mine integrity *that is* in me.

9 O let the wickedness of the wicked come to an end; but establish the just: for the righteous God <sup>o</sup>trieth the hearts and reins.
