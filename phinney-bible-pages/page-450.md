8 The grass withereth, the flower fadeth; but the <sup>m</sup>word of our God shall stand for ever.

9 O Zion, that bringest <sup>n</sup>good tidings, get thee up into the high mountain; O Jerusalem, that bringest good tidings, lift up thy voice with strength: lift *it* up, be not afraid; say unto the cities of Judah, <sup>o</sup>Behold your God!

21 Have ye not known? have ye not heard? hath it not been <sup>g</sup>told you from the beginning? have ye not understood from the foundations of the earth?

25 To whom then will ye <sup>m</sup>liken me, or shall I be <sup>n</sup>equal? saith the Holy One.

26 Lift up your eyes on high, and behold who hath created these *things,* that bringeth out their host <sup>o</sup>by number: he calleth them all by names, by the greatness of his might, for that *he is* strong in power; not one <sup>p</sup>faileth.

27 <sup>q</sup>Why sayest thou, O Jacob, and speakest, O Israel, My way <sup>r</sup>is hid from the LORD, and my <sup>s</sup>judgment is passed over from my God?

28 Hast thou not known, hast thou not <sup>t</sup>heard, *that* the everlasting God, the LORD, the Creator of the ends of the earth, <sup>u</sup>fainteth not, neither is weary? *there is* <sup>x</sup>no searching of his understanding.

29 He giveth power to the <sup>y</sup>faint; and to *them* *that have* no might he increaseth strength.

30 Even the <sup>z</sup>youths shall faint and be weary, and the young men shall utterly fall:

31 But they that wait upon the LORD shall <sup>a</sup>renew *their* strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run and not be weary, *and* they shall walk and <sup>b</sup>not faint.
