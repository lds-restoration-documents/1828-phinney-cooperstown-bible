12 And he shall <sup>p</sup>set up an ensign for the <sup>q</sup>nations, and shall assemble the outcasts of Israel, and gather together the <sup>r</sup>dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth.

13 The <sup>s</sup>envy also of Ephraim <sup>t</sup>shall depart, and the adversaries of Judah shall be cut off; Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall <sup>u</sup>not vex Ephraim.

14 But they shall fly upon the shoulders of the Philistines toward <sup>x</sup>the west; they shall spoil them of the <sup>y</sup>east together: they shall lay their hand upon Edom and Moab; and the children of Ammon shall <sup>z</sup>obey them.

15 And the LORD shall utterly destroy the <sup>a</sup>tongue of the Egyptian sea; and with his mighty wind shall he shake his hand <sup>b</sup>over the river, and shall smite it in the seven streams, and make *men* go over dry-shod.

16 And there shall be a <sup>c</sup>highway for the remnant of his people, which shall be left, from Assyria; like as it was to Israel in the day that he came up out of the land of <sup>d</sup>Egypt.
