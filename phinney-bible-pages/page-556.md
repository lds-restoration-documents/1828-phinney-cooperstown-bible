## CHAPTER XIII.

1 Ephraim's glory, by reason of idolatry, vanisheth....5 God's anger for their unkindness....9 A promise of God's mercy.

WHEN Ephraim spake <sup>a</sup>trembling, he <sup>b</sup>exalted himself in Israel; but when he offended in Baal, he <sup>c</sup>died.

2 And now they sin <sup>d</sup>more and more, and have made them molten images of their silver, *and* idols according to their own <sup>e</sup>understanding, all of it the work of the craftsmen: they say of them, Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves.

3 Therefore they shall be as the morning cloud, and as the early dew that passeth away; as the <sup>f</sup>chaff *that* is driven with a whirlwind out of the floor, and as the smoke out of the chimney.

4 Yet I *am* the LORD thy God from the land of Egypt, and thou shalt know no god but me: for *there is* <sup>g</sup>no saviour besides me.

5 I did know thee in the wilderness, in the land of great <sup>h</sup>drought.

6 According to their pasture, so were they filled; they were filled, and their heart was exalted; therefore have they <sup>i</sup>forgotten me.

7 Therefore I will be unto them <sup>k</sup>as a lion; as a leopard by the way will I observe *them.*

8 I will meet them as <sup>l</sup>a bear *that is* bereaved *of her whelps,* and will rend the caul of their heart, and there will I devour them like a lion: the wild beast shall tear them.

9 O Israel, thou hast <sup>m</sup>destroyed thyself; but in <sup>n</sup>me *is* thy help.

10 I will be <sup>o</sup>thy king: where *is any other* that may save thee in all thy cities? and thy judges, of whom thou saidst, <sup>p</sup>Give me a king and princes?

11 I gave thee <sup>q</sup>a king in mine anger, and took *him* away in my wrath.

12 The iniquity of Ephraim *is* <sup>r</sup>bound up; his sin *is* hid.

13 The sorrows of a travailing woman shall come upon him: he *is* an <sup>s</sup>unwise son; for he should not stay long in *the place of* the <sup>t</sup>breaking forth of children.

14 I will <sup>u</sup>ransom them from the power of the grave; I will redeem them from death: O death, I will be thy plagues; O grave, I will be thy destruction: <sup>x</sup>repentance shall be hid from mine eyes.

15 Though he be <sup>y</sup>fruitful among *his* brethren, <sup>z</sup>an east wind shall come, the wind of the LORD shall come up from the wilderness, and his spring shall become dry, and his fountain shall be dried up: he shall <sup>a</sup>spoil the treasure of all pleasant vessels.

16 Samaria shall become <sup>b</sup>desolate; for she hath rebelled against her God: they shall fall by the sword; their infants shall be <sup>c</sup>dashed in pieces, and their women with child shall be ripped up.

1 An exhortation to repentance....4 A promise of God's blessing.

O ISRAEL, <sup>a</sup>return unto the LORD thy God; for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity.

2 Take with you <sup>b</sup>words, and turn to the LORD: say unto him, <sup>c</sup>Take away all iniquity, and receive *us* graciously: so will we render the calves of our lips.

3 Asshur shall <sup>d</sup>not save us; we will not ride upon horses; neither will we say any more to the work of our hands, *Ye are* our gods: for in thee the <sup>e</sup>fatherless findeth mercy.

4 I will heal their backsliding, I will love them <sup>f</sup>freely: for mine anger is turned away from him.

5 I will be as the <sup>g</sup>dew unto Israel: he shall <sup>h</sup>grow as the lily, and cast forth his roots as Lebanon.

6 His branches shall spread, and his beauty shall be as the <sup>i</sup>olive-tree, and his <sup>k</sup>smell as Lebanon.

7 They that dwell under his <sup>l</sup>shadow shall return; they shall <sup>m</sup>revive *as* the corn, and grow as the vine: the scent thereof *shall be* as the wine of Lebanon.

8 Ephraim *shall say,* What have I <sup>n</sup>to do any more with idols? I have heard *him,* and observed him: I *am* like a green fir-tree. From me <sup>o</sup>*is* thy fruit found.

9 Who *is* wise, and he shall <sup>p</sup>understand these *things?* prudent, and he shall know them? for the ways of the LORD *are* right, and the just shall walk in them: but the transgressors shall <sup>q</sup>fall therein.

5 <sup>f</sup>Awake, ye drunkards, and weep; and howl, all ye drinkers of wine, because of the new wine; for it is <sup>g</sup>cut off from your mouth.

6 For <sup>h</sup>a nation is come up upon <sup>i</sup>my land, strong, and without number, whose teeth *are* the teeth of a lion, and he hath the <sup>k</sup>cheek-teeth of a great lion.

7 He hath laid my <sup>l</sup>vine waste, and <sup>m</sup>barked my fig-tree; he hath made it clean bare, and cast *it* away; the branches thereof are made white.

8 Lament like a <sup>n</sup>virgin girded with sackcloth for the husband of her youth

10 The field is wasted, <sup>q</sup>the land mourneth; for the corn is wasted: the new wine is dried up, the oil languisheth.

11 <sup>r</sup>Be ye ashamed, O ye husbandmen; howl, O ye vine-dressers, for the wheat and for the barley; because the harvest of the field is perished.

12 The vine is dried up, and the fig-tree languisheth; the pomegranate-tree, the palm-tree also, and the apple-tree, *even* all the trees of the field, are withered: because <sup>s</sup>joy is withered away from the sons of men.
