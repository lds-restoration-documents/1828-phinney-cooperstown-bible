3 Therefore came all the elders of Israel to the king to Hebron; and David made a covenant with them in Hebron before the LORD; and they <sup>c</sup>anointed David king over Israel, according to the word of the LORD by Samuel.

4 And <sup>d</sup>David and all Israel went to Jerusalem, which *is* Jebus; where the Jebusites *were*, the inhabitants of the land.

5 And the inhabitants of Jebus said to David, Thou shalt <sup>e</sup>not come hither. Nevertheless David took the <sup>f</sup>castle of Zion, which *is* the city of David.

6 And David said, Whosoever smiteth the Jebusites first shall be chief and captain. So Joab the <sup>g</sup>son of Zeruiah went <sup>h</sup>first up, and was chief.

7 And David dwelt in the castle; therefore they called <sup>i</sup>it The city of David.

8 And he built the city round about, even from Millo round about: and Joab repaired the rest of the city.

9 So David waxed greater and greater: for the LORD of hosts *was* <sup>k</sup>with him.

10 ¶ These also *are* the chief of the mighty men whom David had, who strengthened themselves with him in his kingdom, *and* with all Israel, to make him king, <sup>l</sup>according to the word of the LORD concerning Israel.

11 And this *is* the number of the mighty men whom David had; Jashobeam a Hachmonite, the chief of the captains: he lifted up his spear against <sup>m</sup>three hundred, slain *by him* at one time.

12 And after him *was* Eleazar the son of Dodo the <sup>n</sup>Ahohite, who *was one* of the <sup>o</sup>three mighties:

13 He was with David at <sup>p</sup>Pas-dammim, and there the Philistines were gathered together to battle, where was a parcel of ground full of barley; and the people <sup>q</sup>fled from before the Philistines.

14 And they <sup>r</sup>set themselves in the midst of *that* parcel, and delivered it, and slew the Philistines; and the LORD <sup>s</sup>saved *them* by a great deliverance.

15 Now three of the thirty captains <sup>t</sup>went down to the rock of David, into the cave of <sup>u</sup>Adullam; and the host of the Philistines encamped in the valley of Rephaim.

16 And David *was* then in the hold, and the Philistines' <sup>x</sup>garrison *was* then at Beth-lehem.

17 And David longed, and said, <sup>||</sup>Oh, that one would give me drink of the water of the well of Beth-lehem, that *is* at the gate!

18 And the three <sup>y</sup>brake through the host of the Philistines, and drew water out of the well of Beth-lehem that *was* by the gate, and took *it*, and brought *it* to David: but David would not drink *of* it, but poured it out to the LORD,

19 And said, My God forbid it me, that I should do this thing: shall I drink the blood of these men that have put their lives in jeopardy? for <sup>†</sup>with *the jeopardy of* their lives they brought it. Therefore he would not drink it. These things did these three mightiest.

20 And Abishai the brother of Joab, he was chief of <sup>z</sup>the three: for lifting up his spear against three hundred, he slew *them*, and <sup>||</sup>had a name among the three.

21 Of the three, he was more honourable than the two, for he was their captain; howbeit he attained not to <sup>a</sup>the *first* three.

22 <sup>b</sup>Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the son of a valiant man of <sup>c</sup>Kabzeel, <sup>†</sup>who had done many acts; he slew <sup>d</sup>two lion-like men of Moab: also he went down and slew a lion in a pit in a snowy day.

23 And he slew an Egyptian, a man of *great* stature, <sup>e</sup>five cubits high; and in the Egyptian's hand *was* a spear like a weaver's beam; and he went down to him with a staff, and plucked the spear out of the Egyptian's hand, and slew him with <sup>f</sup>his own spear.

24 These *things* did Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and had the name among the three <sup>g</sup>mighties.

25 Behold, he was honourable among the thirty, but attained not to the *first* three: and David set him over his guard.
