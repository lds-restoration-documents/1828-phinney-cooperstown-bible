## CHAPTER VI.

1 The relative duties of children; 4 of parents; 5 of servants; 9 of masters....13 He exhorteth to resist spiritual enemies by putting on the Christian armour.

<sup>a</sup>CHILDREN, obey your parents in the Lord: for this is right.

2 <sup>b</sup>Honour thy father and mother, (which is the first commandment with promise,)

3 That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest live long on the earth.

4 <sup>c</sup>And, ye fathers, provoke not your children to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.

5 <sup>d</sup>Servants, be obedient to them that are *your* masters, according to the flesh, with fear and trembling, in singleness of your heart, as unto Christ;

6 <sup>e</sup>Not with eye-service, as men-pleasers; but as the servants of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart;

7 With good will doing service, as to the Lord, and not to men:

8 <sup>f</sup>Knowing that whatsoever good thing any man doeth, the same shall he receive of the Lord, whether *he be* bond or free.

9 <sup>g</sup>And, ye masters, do the same things unto them, forbearing threatening: knowing that your Master also is in heaven; neither is there respect of persons with him.

10 <sup>h</sup>Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might.

11 <sup>i</sup>Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.

12 <sup>k</sup>For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high *places.*

13 <sup>l</sup>Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand.

14 <sup>m</sup>Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness;

15 <sup>n</sup>And your feet shod, with the preparation of the gospel of peace;

16 <sup>o</sup>Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.

17 <sup>p</sup>And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God:

18 <sup>q</sup>Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints;

19 <sup>r</sup>And for me, that utterance may be given unto me, that I may open my mouth boldly, to make known the mystery of the gospel,

20 <sup>s</sup>For which I am an ambassador in bonds; that therein I may speak boldly, as I ought to speak.

21 <sup>t</sup>But that ye also may know my affairs, *and* how I do, Tychicus, a beloved brother and faithful minister in the Lord, shall make known to you all things:

22 <sup>u</sup>Whom I have sent unto you for the same purpose, that ye might know our affairs, and *that* he might comfort your hearts.

23 Peace *be* to the brethren, and love with faith, from God the Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ.

24 Grace *be* with all them that love our Lord Jesus Christ in sincerity. Amen.

¶ Written from Rome unto the Ephesians by Tychicus.

## CHAPTER I.

3 Paul's thankfulness and prayer to God for them: 21 his readiness to suffer: 27 he exhorteth them to walk worthily.

<sup>a</sup>PAUL and Timotheus, the servants of Jesus Christ, to all the saints in Christ Jesus which are at Philippi, with the bishops and deacons:

2 <sup>b</sup>Grace *be* unto you, and peace, from God our Father, and *from* the Lord Jesus Christ.

3 <sup>c</sup>I thank my God upon every remembrance of you,

4 (Always in every prayer of mine for you all making request with joy,)

5 <sup>d</sup>For your fellowship in the gospel, from the first day until now;

6 <sup>e</sup>Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform *it* until the day of Jesus Christ:

7 <sup>f</sup>Even as it is meet for me to think this of you all, because I have you in my heart: inasmuch as both in my bonds, and in the defence and confirmation of the gospel, ye all are partakers of my grace.

8 <sup>g</sup>For God is my record, how greatly I long after you all in the bowels of Jesus Christ.

9 <sup>h</sup>And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in knowledge and *in* all judgment;
