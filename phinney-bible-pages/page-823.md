15 <sup>n</sup>Some indeed preach Christ even of envy and strife, and some also of good will.

16 The one preach Christ of contention, not sincerely, supposing to add affliction to my bonds;

17 <sup>o</sup>But the other of love, knowing that I am set for the defence of the gospel.

18 What then? notwithstanding every way, whether in pretence or in truth, Christ is preached; and I therein do rejoice, yea, and will rejoice.

19 <sup>p</sup>For I know that this shall turn to my salvation through your prayer, and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ,

20 <sup>q</sup>According to my earnest expectation and *my* hope, that in nothing I shall be ashamed, but *that* with all boldness, as always, *so* now also, Christ shall be magnified in my body, whether *it be* by life or by death.

21 <sup>r</sup><sup>||</sup>For to me to live *is* Christ, and to die *is* gain.

22 <sup>s</sup>But if I live in the flesh, this *is* the fruit of my labour: yet what I shall choose I wot not.

23 <sup>t</sup>For I am in a strait betwixt two, having a desire to depart, and to be with Christ; which is far better:

24 Nevertheless to abide in the flesh *is* more needful for you.

25 <sup>u</sup>And having this confidence, I know that I shall abide and continue with you all, for your furtherance and joy of faith;

26 <sup>x</sup>That your rejoicing may be more abundant in Jesus Christ for me, by my coming to you again.

27 <sup>y</sup>Only let your conversation be as it becometh the gospel of Christ; that, whether I come and see you, or else be absent, I may hear of your affairs, that ye stand fast in one spirit, with one mind, striving together for the faith of the gospel;

28 <sup>z</sup>And in nothing terrified by your adversaries: which is to them an evident token of perdition, but to you of salvation, and that of God.

29 <sup>a</sup>For unto you it is given in the behalf of Christ, not only to believe on him, but also to suffer for his sake;

30 <sup>b</sup>Having the same conflict which ye saw in me, *and* now hear *to be* in me.

## CHAPTER II.

1 Love and unity recommended; 3 humility; 12 to be diligent in the way of salvation; 14 and cheerfully to submit to God's will.

<sup>a</sup>IF *there be* therefore any consolation in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any bowels and mercies,

2 <sup>b</sup>Fulfil ye my joy, that ye be like-minded, having the same love, *being* of one accord, of one mind.

3 <sup>c</sup>*Let* nothing *be done* through strife or vain glory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves.

4 <sup>d</sup>Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.

5 <sup>e</sup>Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus:

6 <sup>f</sup>Who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God;

7 <sup>g</sup>But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men:

8 <sup>h</sup>And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.

9 <sup>i</sup>Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name;

10 <sup>k</sup>That <sup>†</sup>at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of *things* in heaven, and *things* in earth, and *things* under the earth;

11 <sup>l</sup>And *that* every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ *is* Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

12 <sup>m</sup>Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling:

13 <sup>n</sup>For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of *his* good pleasure.

14 <sup>o</sup>Do all things without murmurings and disputings;

15 <sup>p</sup>That ye may be blameless and harmless, the sons of God without rebuke, in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation, among whom ye shine as lights in the world;

16 <sup>q</sup>Holding forth the word of life; that I may rejoice in the day of Christ, that I have not run in vain, neither laboured in vain.

17 <sup>r</sup>Yea, and if I be <sup>†</sup>offered upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I joy, and rejoice with you all.

18 <sup>s</sup>For the same cause also do ye joy, and rejoice with me.

19 <sup>t</sup>But I trust in the Lord Jesus to send Timotheus shortly unto you, that I also may be of good comfort, when I know your state.

20 <sup>u</sup>For I have no man like-minded, who will naturally care for your state.

21 <sup>x</sup>For all seek their own, not the things which are Jesus Christ's.

22 <sup>y</sup>But ye know the proof of him, that, as a son with the father, he hath served with me in the gospel.

23 Him therefore I hope to send presently, so soon as I shall see how it will go with me.

24 <sup>z</sup>But I trust in the Lord that I also myself shall come shortly.

25 <sup>a</sup>Yet I supposed it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother, and companion in labour, and fellow-soldier, but your messenger, and he that ministered to my wants.

26 For he longed after you all, and was full of heaviness, because that ye had heard that he had been sick.

27 For indeed he was sick nigh unto death: but God had mercy on him; and not on him only, but on me also, lest I should have sorrow upon sorrow.

28 I sent him therefore the more carefully, that, when ye see him again, ye may rejoice, and that I may be the less sorrowful.

29 <sup>b</sup>Receive him therefore in the Lord with all gladness; and hold such in reputation:

30 <sup>c</sup>Because for the work of Christ he was nigh unto death, not regarding his life, to supply your lack of service toward me.
