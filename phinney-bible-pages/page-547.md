**Header:** *Daniel's vision of the four beasts:* | CHAPTER VIII. | *The interpretation thereof.*

2 Daniel spake and said, I saw in my vision by night, and, behold, the <sup>d</sup>four winds of the heaven strove upon the <sup>e</sup>great sea.

3 And <sup>f</sup>four great beasts <sup>g</sup>came up from the sea, diverse one from another.

4 The first *was* <sup>h</sup>like a lion, and had eagle's wings: I beheld till the wings thereof were plucked, and it was lifted up from the earth, and <sup>i</sup>made stand upon the feet as a man, and a man's heart was given to it.

5 And, behold, another beast, <sup>k</sup>a second, like to <sup>l</sup>a bear, and it raised up itself on one side, and *it had* <sup>m</sup>three ribs in the mouth of it between the teeth of it: and <sup>n</sup>they said thus unto it, Arise, <sup>o</sup>devour much flesh.

6 After this I beheld, and lo, <sup>p</sup>another, like a leopard, which had upon the back of it four wings of a fowl: the beast had also <sup>q</sup>four heads; and dominion was given to it.

7 After this I saw in the night visions, and, behold, <sup>r</sup>a fourth beast, dreadful and terrible, and strong exceedingly; and it had <sup>s</sup>great iron teeth: it devoured and brake in pieces, and stamped the residue with the feet of it: and it *was* diverse from all the beasts that *were* before it; and it had <sup>t</sup>ten horns.

8 I considered the horns, and, behold, there came up among them another <sup>u</sup>little horn, before whom there were three of the first horns plucked up by the roots: and, behold, in this horn *were* eyes like the eyes of man, and <sup>x</sup>a mouth <sup>y</sup>speaking great things.

9 I beheld till the thrones were <sup>||</sup>cast down, and the <sup>z</sup>Ancient of days did sit, whose garment *was* white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool: his throne *was like* the fiery flame, *and* <sup>a</sup>his wheels *as* burning fire.

10 <sup>b</sup>A fiery stream issued and came forth from before him: <sup>c</sup>thousand thousands ministered unto him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him: the judgment was set, and the <sup>d</sup>books were opened.

11 I beheld then, because of the voice of the great words which the horn spake; I beheld, *even* till the beast was slain, and his body destroyed, and given to the <sup>e</sup>burning flame.

12 As concerning the <sup>f</sup>rest of the beasts, they had their <sup>g</sup>dominion taken away: yet their lives were <sup>h</sup>prolonged for a season and time.

13 I saw in the night visions, and, behold, *one* like the <sup>i</sup>Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, <sup>k</sup>and they brought him near before him.

14 And there was given him <sup>l</sup>dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion *is* an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom *that* which shall not be destroyed.

15 I Daniel was grieved in my spirit in the midst of *my* body, and the visions of my head troubled me.

16 I <sup>m</sup>came near unto one of them that stood by, and <sup>n</sup>asked him the truth of all this. So he told me, and <sup>o</sup>made me know the interpretation of the things.

17 These great beasts, which are four, *are* four <sup>p</sup>kings, *which* shall <sup>q</sup>arise out of the earth.

18 But the saints of the Most High shall take the kingdom, and <sup>r</sup>possess the kingdom for ever, even for ever and ever.

19 Then I would know <sup>s</sup>the truth of the fourth beast, which was diverse from all the others, exceeding dreadful, whose teeth *were of* iron, and his nails *of* brass; *which* devoured, brake in pieces, and stamped the residue with his feet;

20 And of the ten horns that *were* in his head, and *of* the other which came up, and before whom three fell; even *of* that horn that had eyes, and a mouth that spake very great things, <sup>t</sup>whose look *was* more stout than his fellows.

21 I beheld, and the same horn <sup>u</sup>made war with the saints, and prevailed against them;

22 Until the Ancient of days came, and <sup>x</sup>judgment was given to the saints of the Most High; and <sup>y</sup>the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom.

23 Thus he said, The fourth beast shall be the <sup>z</sup>fourth kingdom upon earth, which shall be diverse from all kingdoms, and shall <sup>a</sup>devour the whole earth, and shall tread it down, and break it in pieces.

24 And the ten horns out of this kingdom *are* <sup>b</sup>ten kings *that* shall arise: and another shall rise after them; and he shall be diverse from the first, and he shall subdue three kings.

25 And he shall speak *great* words <sup>c</sup>against the Most High, and shall wear out the saints of the Most High, and think to <sup>d</sup>change times and laws: and they shall be given into his hand, <sup>e</sup>until a time and times and the dividing of time.

26 But the judgment shall sit, and they shall <sup>f</sup>take away his dominion, to consume and to destroy *it* unto the end.

27 And the kingdom and dominion, and the greatness of the kingdom under the whole heaven, shall be <sup>g</sup>given to the people of the saints of the Most High, whose kingdom *is* an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions shall serve and obey him.

28 Hitherto *is* the end of the matter. As for me Daniel, my cogitations much troubled me, and my <sup>h</sup>countenance changed in me: but I kept the matter in my <sup>i</sup>heart.

Daniel's vision of the ram and he-goat.

2 And I saw in a vision; (and it came to pass, when I saw, that I <sup>c</sup>*was* at Shushan *in* the palace, which *is* in the province of <sup>d</sup>Elam;) and I saw in a vision, and I was by the river of Ulai.

3 Then I <sup>e</sup>lifted up mine eyes and saw, and, behold, there stood before the river <sup>f</sup>a ram which had <sup>g</sup>*two* horns; and the *two* horns *were* high; but one *was* higher than the other, and the higher came up last.

4 I saw the ram pushing <sup>h</sup>westward, and northward, and southward; so that no <sup>i</sup>beasts might stand before him, neither *was there any* that could deliver out of his hand; but he did <sup>k</sup>according to his will, and became great.

5 And as I was considering, behold, a <sup>l</sup>he-goat came from the west, on the face of the whole earth, and touched <sup>m</sup>not the ground: and the goat *had* <sup>n</sup>a notable horn between his eyes.

6 And he came to the ram that had *two* horns, which I had seen standing before the river, and <sup>o</sup>ran unto him in the fury of his power.
