## CHAPTER VIII.

David subdueth the Philistines and the Moabites.

AND after this it came to pass, that David <sup>a</sup>smote the Philistines, and subdued them: and David <sup>b</sup>took Metheg-ammah out of the hand of the Philistines.

2 And he smote Moab, and <sup>c</sup>measured them with a line, casting them down to the ground; even with <sup>d</sup>two lines measured he to put to death, and with one full line to keep alive: and *so* the Moabites became David's servants, *and* brought gifts.

3 David smote also Hadadezer, the son of Rehob, king of <sup>e</sup>Zobah, as he <sup>f</sup>went to recover his border at the river Euphrates.

4 And David took from him a thousand *chariots* and <sup>g</sup>seven hundred horsemen, and twenty thousand footmen: and David <sup>h</sup>houghed all the chariot *horses*, but reserved of them *for* a hundred chariots.

5 And when the Syrians of Damascus came to succour Hadadezer king of Zobah, David slew of the Syrians <sup>i</sup>two and twenty thousand men.

6 Then David put garrisons in Syria of Damascus: and the Syrians became <sup>k</sup>servants to David, *and* brought <sup>l</sup>gifts. And the LORD preserved David whithersoever he went.

7 And David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.

8 And from <sup>m</sup>Betah, and from Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, king David took exceeding much brass.

9 ¶ When Toi king of <sup>n</sup>Hamath heard that David had smitten all the host of Hadadezer,

10 Then Toi sent Joram his son unto king David, to salute him, and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer, and smitten him: (for Hadadezer <sup>†</sup>had wars with Toi:) and *Joram* brought with him vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and vessels of brass;

11 Which also king David did dedicate unto the LORD, with the silver and gold that he had <sup>o</sup>dedicated of all nations which he subdued;

12 Of Syria, and of Moab, and of the children of Ammon, and of the Philistines, and of Amalek, and of the spoil of Hadadezer, son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

13 And David gat *him* <sup>p</sup>a name when he returned from smiting of the Syrians in the <sup>q</sup>valley of Salt, *being* <sup>r</sup>eighteen thousand *men*.

14 And he put garrisons in Edom; throughout all Edom put he garrisons: and all they of Edom became David's <sup>s</sup>servants. And the LORD <sup>t</sup>preserved David whithersoever he went.

15 And David reigned over all Israel; and David executed judgment and justice unto all his people.

16 And Joab the son of Zeruiah *was* <sup>u</sup>over the host; and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud *was* recorder;

17 And <sup>x</sup>Zadok the son of <sup>y</sup>Ahitub, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, *were* the <sup>z</sup>priests: and Seraiah *was* the scribe;

18 And <sup>a</sup>Benaiah the son of Jehoiadah *was over* both the <sup>b</sup>Cherethites and the Pelethites; and David's sons were <sup>c</sup>chief rulers.

## CHAPTER IX.

David by Ziba sendeth for Mephibosheth.

AND David said, Is there yet any that is left of the house of Saul, that I may shew him kindness <sup>a</sup>for Jonathan's sake?

2 And <sup>†</sup>*there was* of the house of Saul a servant whose name *was* Ziba: and when they had called him unto David, the king said unto him, *Art* thou Ziba? And he said, Thy servant *is he*.

3 And the king said, *Is* there not yet any of the house of Saul, that I may shew the <sup>b</sup>kindness of God unto him? And Ziba said unto the king, Jonathan hath yet a son, *which is* <sup>c</sup>lame on *his* feet.

4 And the king said unto him, Where *is* he? And Ziba said unto the king, Behold, he *is* in the house of Machir the son of Ammiel, in <sup>d</sup>Lo-debar.

5 Then king David sent, and fetched him out of the house of Machir the son of Ammiel, from Lo-debar.

6 Now when <sup>||</sup>Mephibosheth, the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, was come unto David, he fell on his face, and did reverence. And David said, Mephibosheth! And he answered, Behold thy servant.

7 And David said unto him, <sup>e</sup>Fear not: for I will surely shew thee kindness for Jonathan thy <sup>f</sup>father's sake, and will restore thee all the land of Saul thy father; and thou shalt eat <sup>g</sup>bread at my table continually.

8 And he bowed himself, and said, What *is* thy servant, that thou shouldest look upon such <sup>h</sup>a dead dog as I *am?*

9 Then the king called to Ziba, Saul's servant, and said unto him, I have given unto thy <sup>i</sup>master's son all that pertained to Saul, and to all his house.

10 Thou therefore, and thy sons, and thy servants, shall till the land for him, and thou shalt bring in *the fruits*, that thy master's son may have food to eat: but Mephibosheth, thy master's son, shall eat bread always at my table. Now Ziba had fifteen sons, and twenty servants.

11 Then said Ziba unto the king, According to all that my lord the king hath commanded his servant, <sup>k</sup>so shall thy servant do. As for Mephibosheth, *said the king*, he shall eat at my table, as one of the king's sons.

12 And Mephibosheth had a young son, whose name *was* <sup>l</sup>Micah. And <sup>†</sup>all that dwelt in the house of Ziba *were* servants unto Mephibosheth.

13 So Mephibosheth dwelt in Jerusalem: for he did eat continually at the king's table; and was lame on both his feet.
