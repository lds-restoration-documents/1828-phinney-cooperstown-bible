## CHAPTER XXIV.

The restoration of them that were in captivity foretold.

THE LORD <sup>a</sup>shewed me, and, behold, two baskets of figs *were* set before the temple of the LORD, after that Nebuchadrezzar <sup>b</sup>king of Babylon had carried away captive <sup>c</sup>Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, and the princes of Judah, with the carpenters and smiths, from Jerusalem, and had brought them to Babylon.

2 <sup>d</sup>One basket *had* very good figs, *even* like the figs *that are* first ripe; and the other basket *had* very naughty figs, which could not be eaten, they were so bad.

3 Then said the LORD unto me, What seest thou, Jeremiah? And I said, Figs; the good figs, very good; and the evil, very evil, that cannot be eaten, they are so evil.

4 Again the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

5 Thus saith the LORD, the God of Israel, Like these good figs, so will I <sup>†</sup>acknowledge them that are carried away captive of Judah, whom I have sent out of this place into the land of the Chaldeans <sup>e</sup>for *their* good.

6 For <sup>f</sup>I will set mine eyes upon them for <sup>g</sup>good, and I will <sup>h</sup>bring them again to this land: and I will build them, and not pull *them* down; and I will plant them, and not pluck *them* up.

7 And <sup>i</sup>I will give them a <sup>k</sup>heart to know me, that I *am* the LORD; and they shall be my people, and I will be their God: for they shall return unto me with their <sup>l</sup>whole heart.

8 And as the <sup>m</sup>evil figs, which cannot be eaten, they are so evil; surely thus saith the LORD, So will I give Zedekiah the king of Judah, and his princes, and the residue of Jerusalem, that remain in this land, and them that <sup>n</sup>dwell in the land of Egypt;

9 And I will deliver them to <sup>o</sup>be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth for *their* hurt, *to be* a reproach and a proverb, a taunt and a curse, in all places whither I shall drive them.

10 And <sup>p</sup>I will send the sword, the famine, and the pestilence, among them, till they be consumed from off the land that I gave unto them and to their fathers.

## CHAPTER XXV.

Jeremiah foretelleth the seventy years' captivity.

THE word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah, in the <sup>a</sup>fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, that *was* the first year of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon;

2 The which Jeremiah the prophet spake unto <sup>b</sup>all the people of Judah, and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying,

3 From the <sup>c</sup>thirteenth year of Josiah the son of Amon king of Judah, even unto this day, (that *is* the three and twentieth year,) the word of the LORD hath come unto me, and I have spoken unto you, <sup>d</sup>rising early and speaking; but ye have not hearkened.

4 And the LORD hath sent unto you all his <sup>e</sup>servants the prophets, rising early and sending *them;* but ye have <sup>f</sup>not hearkened, nor inclined your ear to hear.

5 They said, <sup>g</sup>Turn ye again now every one from his evil way, and from the evil of your doings, and <sup>h</sup>dwell in the land that the LORD hath given unto you and to your fathers for ever and ever:

6 And go not after <sup>i</sup>other gods to serve them, and to worship them, and <sup>k</sup>provoke me not to anger with the works of your hands; and I will do you no hurt.

7 Yet ye have not hearkened unto me, saith the LORD; that ye might provoke me to anger with the works of your hands <sup>l</sup>to your own hurt.

8 Therefore thus saith the LORD of hosts, Because ye have not heard my words,

9 Behold, I will send and take <sup>m</sup>all the families of the north, saith the LORD, and Nebuchadrezzar the king of Babylon <sup>n</sup>my servant, and will bring them against this land, and <sup>o</sup>against the inhabitants thereof, and against all these nations round about, and will utterly destroy them, and make them an astonishment, and a hissing, and perpetual desolations.

10 Moreover, I will take from them <sup>p</sup>the voice of mirth, and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride, the sound of the <sup>q</sup>millstones, and the light of the candle.

11 And this whole land shall be a desolation, *and* an astonishment; and these nations shall serve the king of Babylon <sup>r</sup>seventy years.

12 And it shall come to pass, when seventy years are <sup>s</sup>accomplished, *that* I will punish the king of Babylon, and that nation, saith the LORD, for their iniquity, and the land of the Chaldeans, and will make it <sup>t</sup>perpetual desolations.

13 And I will bring upon that land all my words which I have pronounced against it, *even* all that is written in this book, which Jeremiah hath prophesied against all the nations.

14 For <sup>u</sup>many nations and great kings shall serve themselves of them also: and I will <sup>x</sup>recompense them according to their deeds, and according to the works of their own hands.

15 For thus saith the Lord GOD of Israel unto me, Take the <sup>y</sup>wine-cup of this fury at my hand, and cause all the nations to whom I send thee, to drink it.

16 And they shall drink, and be moved, and be <sup>z</sup>mad, because of the sword that I will send among them.

17 Then took I the cup at the LORD's hand, and <sup>a</sup>made all the nations to drink, unto whom the LORD had sent me:

18 *To wit,* <sup>b</sup>Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, and the kings thereof, and the princes thereof, to make them a desolation, an astonishment, a hissing, and a curse; <sup>c</sup>(as *it is* this day;)
