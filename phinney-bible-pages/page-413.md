**Header:** *Of sundry moral virtues,* | CHAPTER XII....XIII. | *and their contrary vices.*

11 By the blessing of the upright the city is <sup>o</sup>exalted: but it is overthrown <sup>p</sup>by the mouth of the wicked.

12 He that is void of wisdom despiseth his neighbour: but a man of understanding <sup>q</sup>holdeth his peace.

13 A tale-bearer <sup>r</sup>revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.

14 Where no counsel *is,* the people <sup>s</sup>fall: but in the multitude of counsellors *there is* safety.

15 He that is <sup>t</sup>surety for a stranger shall smart *for it:* and he that hateth suretiship is sure.

16 A gracious woman <sup>u</sup>retaineth honour; and strong *men* retain riches.

17 The merciful man doeth good <sup>x</sup>to his own soul: but *he that is* cruel troubleth his own flesh.

18 The wicked worketh <sup>y</sup>a deceitful work: but to him that soweth righteousness *shall be* <sup>z</sup>a sure reward.

19 As righteousness <sup>a</sup>*tendeth* to life; so he that pursueth evil, *pursueth it* to his own death.

20 They that are of a froward heart *are* <sup>b</sup>abomination to the LORD: but *such as are* upright in *their* way *are* <sup>c</sup>his delight.

21 *Though* <sup>d</sup>hand *join* in hand, the wicked shall not be unpunished: but the <sup>e</sup>seed of the righteous shall be delivered.

22 *As* a jewel of gold in a swine's snout, *so is* a fair woman which is without discretion.

23 The desire of the righteous *is* <sup>f</sup>only good: *but* the expectation of the wicked <sup>g</sup>*is* wrath.

24 There is that <sup>h</sup>scattereth, and yet increaseth; and *there is* that withholdeth more than is meet, but *it tendeth* to poverty.

25 <sup>i</sup>The liberal soul shall be made fat: and he that watereth shall be watered also himself.

26 He that withholdeth corn, the people shall curse him: but blessing *shall be* upon the head of him that selleth *it.*

27 He that diligently seeketh good procureth favour: but he that seeketh mischief, it shall <sup>k</sup>come unto him.

28 He that <sup>l</sup>trusteth in his riches shall fall: but the righteous shall <sup>m</sup>flourish as a branch.

29 He that <sup>n</sup>troubleth his own house shall inherit the wind: and the fool *shall be* <sup>o</sup>servant to the wise of heart.

30 The fruit of the righteous *is* a tree of life; and he that <sup>p</sup>winneth souls *is* wise.

31 Behold, the righteous shall be <sup>q</sup>recompensed in the earth: <sup>r</sup>much more the wicked and the sinner.

## CHAPTER XII.

WHOSO loveth instruction <sup>a</sup>loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof *is* <sup>b</sup>brutish.

2 A good *man* obtaineth favour of the LORD: but a man of <sup>c</sup>wicked devices will he condemn.

3 A man shall not be established <sup>d</sup>by wickedness: but the root of the righteous shall <sup>e</sup>not be moved.

4 <sup>f</sup>A virtuous woman *is* a crown to her husband: but she that maketh ashamed *is* as rottenness in his bones.

5 The thoughts of the righteous *are* <sup>g</sup>right: *but* the counsels of the wicked *are* <sup>h</sup>deceit.

6 The words of the wicked *are* to <sup>i</sup>lie in wait for blood: but the mouth of the upright shall <sup>k</sup>deliver them.

7 The wicked are overthrown, and <sup>l</sup>*are* not: but the house of the righteous <sup>m</sup>shall stand.

8 A man shall be commended <sup>n</sup>according to his wisdom: but he that is of a perverse heart shall be <sup>o</sup>despised.

9 *He that is* despised, and hath <sup>p</sup>a servant, *is* better than he that <sup>q</sup>honoureth himself, and lacketh bread.

10 A righteous *man* regardeth the life of his beast: but the tender mercies of the wicked *are* <sup>r</sup>cruel.

11 He that <sup>s</sup>tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that followeth vain *persons is* void of understanding.

12 The wicked desireth the <sup>t</sup>net of evil *men:* but the root of the righteous <sup>u</sup>yieldeth *fruit.*

13 The wicked is <sup>x</sup>snared by the transgression of *his* lips: but the just shall come out of trouble.

14 A man shall be satisfied with good by the fruit of *his* mouth; and the <sup>y</sup>recompense of a man's hands shall be rendered unto him.

15 The way of a fool *is* right in his <sup>z</sup>own eyes: but he that <sup>a</sup>hearkeneth unto counsel *is* wise.

16 A fool's wrath is <sup>b</sup>presently known: but a prudent *man* <sup>c</sup>covereth shame.

17 *He that* speaketh truth <sup>d</sup>sheweth forth righteousness: but a false witness deceit.

18 There is that speaketh like the <sup>e</sup>piercings of a sword: but the tongue of the wise *is* health.

19 The lip of truth shall be <sup>f</sup>established for ever: but a lying tongue *is* but for <sup>g</sup>a moment.

20 Deceit *is* in the heart of them that imagine evil: but to the counsellors <sup>h</sup>of peace *is* joy.

21 There shall <sup>i</sup>no evil happen to the just: but the wicked shall be <sup>k</sup>filled with mischief.

22 Lying lips *are* abomination to the LORD: but they that <sup>l</sup>deal truly *are* his <sup>m</sup>delight.

23 A prudent man <sup>n</sup>concealeth knowledge: but the heart of fools proclaimeth foolishness.

24 The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be <sup>o</sup>under tribute.

25 Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it <sup>p</sup>stoop: but <sup>q</sup>a good word maketh it glad.

26 The righteous *is* <sup>r</sup>more excellent than his neighbour: but the way of the wicked <sup>s</sup>seduceth them.

27 The slothful *man* <sup>t</sup>roasteth not that which he took in hunting: but the substance of a diligent man <sup>u</sup>*is* precious.

28 In the way of righteousness *is* life; and *in* the pathway *thereof there is* no death.

## CHAPTER XIII.

A WISE son *heareth* his father's instruction: but <sup>a</sup>a scorner heareth not rebuke.

2 A man shall eat good by the fruit of *his* <sup>b</sup>mouth: but the soul of the transgressors *shall* <sup>c</sup>*eat* violence.

3 He that <sup>d</sup>keepeth his mouth keepeth his life: *but* he that <sup>e</sup>openeth wide his lips shall have destruction.

4 The soul of the sluggard <sup>f</sup>desireth, and *hath* nothing: but the soul of the <sup>g</sup>diligent shall be made fat.

5 A righteous *man* <sup>h</sup>hateth lying: but a wicked *man* is <sup>i</sup>loathsome, and cometh to shame.

6 Righteousness <sup>k</sup>keepeth *him that is* upright in the way: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner.

7 There is that maketh himself rich, yet *hath* nothing; *there is* that maketh himself poor, yet *hath* great riches.

8 The <sup>l</sup>ransom of a man's life *are* his riches: but the poor heareth not rebuke.

9 The light of the righteous <sup>m</sup>rejoiceth: but the lamp of the wicked shall <sup>n</sup>be put out.

10 Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well-advised *is* wisdom.

11 <sup>o</sup>Wealth *gotten* <sup>p</sup>by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labour shall <sup>q</sup>increase.
