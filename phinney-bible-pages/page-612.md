18 Then Raguel bade his servants to fill the grave.

19 And he kept the <sup>m</sup>wedding-feast fourteen days.

20 For before the days of the marriage were finished, Raguel had said unto him by an oath, that he should not depart till <sup>n</sup>the fourteen days of the marriage were expired ;

21 And then he should take the half of his goods, and go in safety to his father ; and should have the rest when I and my wife be dead.

## CHAPTER IX.

1 Tobias sendeth the angel unto Gabael for the money....6 The angel bringeth it and Gabael to the wedding.

THEN Tobias called <sup>a</sup>Raphael, and said unto him,

2 Brother Azarias, take with thee a servant, and two camels, and go to <sup>b</sup>Rages of Media, to Gabael, and bring me the money, and bring him to the wedding.

3 For Raguel hath sworn that I shall not depart.

4 But my father counteth the days ; and if I tarry long, he will be very sorry.

5 So Raphael went out, and lodged with <sup>c</sup>Gabael, and gave him the hand-writing : who brought forth bags which were sealed up, and gave them to him.

6 And early in the morning they went forth both together, and came to the wedding : and <sup>||</sup>Tobias blessed his wife.

## CHAPTER X.

1 Tobit and his wife long for their son....7 She will not be comforted by her husband....10 Raguel sendeth Tobias and his wife away with half his goods, and blesseth them.

NOW Tobit his father <sup>a</sup>counted every day : and when the days of the journey were expired, and they came not,

2 Then Tobit said, Are they detained ? or is Gabael dead, and there is no man to give him the money ?

3 Therefore he was very sorry.

4 Then his wife said unto him, My son is dead, seeing he stayeth long ; and she began to bewail him, and said,

5 *Now* <sup>b</sup>*I care for nothing, my son, since I have let thee go,* the light of mine eyes.

6 To whom Tobit said, Hold thy peace, <sup>c</sup>take no care, for he is safe.

7 But she said, Hold thy peace, and deceive me not ; my son is dead. And she went out every day into the way which they went, and did eat no meat on the day time, and ceased not whole nights to bewail her son Tobias, until <sup>d</sup>the fourteen days of the wedding were expired, which Raguel had sworn that he should spend there. Then Tobias said to Raguel, <sup>e</sup>Let me go, for my father and my mother look no more to see me.

8 But his father-in-law said unto him, Tarry with me, and I will send to thy father, and they shall declare unto him how things go with thee.

9 But Tobias said, No ; but let me go to my father.

10 Then Raguel arose, and gave him Sara his wife, and half his goods, servants, and cattle, and money :

11 And <sup>f</sup>he blessed them, and <sup>g</sup>sent them away, saying, The God of heaven give you a prosperous journey, my children.

12 And he said to his daughter, <sup>h</sup>Honour thy father and thy mother-in-law, which are now thy parents, that I may hear good report of thee. And he kissed her. Edna also said to Tobias, The Lord of heaven restore thee, my dear brother, and grant <sup>i</sup>that I may see thy children of my daughter Sara before I die, that I may rejoice before the Lord : behold, I commit my daughter unto thee <sup>||</sup>of special trust : wherefore do not entreat her evil.

## CHAPTER XI.

6 Tobias's mother spieth her son coming....10 His father meeteth him at the door, and recovereth his sight....14 He praiseth God, and welcometh his daughter-in-law.

AFTER these things Tobias <sup>a</sup>went his way, praising God that he had given him a prosperous journey, and blessed Raguel and Edna his wife, and went on his way till they drew near unto <sup>b</sup>Nineve.

2 Then <sup>c</sup>Raphael said to Tobias, thou knowest, brother, how thou didst leave thy father :

3 Let us haste before thy wife, and prepare the house.

4 And take in thy hand the gall of the fish. So they went their way, <sup>d</sup>and the dog went after them.

5 Now Anna sat looking about toward the way for her son.

6 And when she espied him coming, she said to his father, Behold, thy son cometh, and <sup>e</sup>the man that went with him.

7 Then said Raphael, I know, Tobias, that thy father will open his eyes.

8 Therefore anoint thou his eyes with the gall, and being pricked therewith, he shall rub, and <sup>f</sup>the whiteness shall fall away, and he shall see thee.

9 Then Anna ran forth, and <sup>g</sup>fell upon the neck of her son, and said unto him, Seeing I have seen thee, my son, from henceforth <sup>h</sup>I am content to die. And they wept both.

10 Tobit also went forth toward the door, and stumbled ; but his son ran unto him,

11 And took hold of his father ; and he strake of the gall on his father's eyes, saying, Be of good hope my father.

12 And when his eyes began to smart, he rubbed them ;

13 And <sup>i</sup>the whiteness pilled away from the corners of his eyes : and when he saw his son, he fell upon his neck.

14 And he wept, and said, Blessed art thou, O God, and blessed is thy name for ever ; and blessed are all thy holy angels :

15 For thou hast scourged, <sup>k</sup>and hast taken pity *on me :* for behold, I see my son Tobias. And his son went in rejoicing, and told his father the great things that had happened to him in Media.

16 Then Tobit went out to meet his daughter-in-law at the gate of <sup>l</sup>Nineve, rejoicing, and praising God : and they which saw him go marvelled <sup>m</sup>because he had received his sight.

17 But Tobit gave thanks before them, because God had mercy on him. And when he came near to Sara his daughter-in-law, he blessed her, saying, Thou art welcome, daughter : <sup>n</sup>God be blessed, which hath brought thee unto us, and *blessed be* thy father and thy mother. And there was joy among all his brethren which were at Nineve.

18 And Achiacharus, <sup>||</sup>and Nasbas his brother's son, came :

19 And Tobias's wedding was kept <sup>o</sup>seven days with great joy.

THEN Tobit called his son Tobias, and said unto him, My son, <sup>a</sup>see that the man have his wages, which went with thee, and thou must give him more.

2 And Tobias said unto him, O father, it is no harm to me to give him half of those things which I have brought :

3 For he hath brought me again to thee in safety, <sup>b</sup>and made whole my wife, <sup>c</sup>and brought me the money, and likewise healed thee.

4 Then the old man said, It is due unto him.

5 So he called the angel, and he said unto him, <sup>d</sup>Take half of all that ye have brought, and go away in safety.

6 Then he took them both apart, and said unto them, Bless God, praise him, and magnify him, and praise him for the things which he hath done unto you in the sight of all that live. <sup>e</sup>It is good to praise God, and exalt his name, and <sup>||</sup>honourably to shew forth the works of God ; therefore be not slack to praise him.

7 It is good to keep close the secret of a king, but it is honourable to reveal the works of God. Do that which is good, <sup>f</sup>and no evil shall touch you.

8 Prayer is good with fasting, and alms, and righteousness. <sup>g</sup>A little with righteousness is better than much with unrighteousness. It is better to give alms than to lay up gold :
