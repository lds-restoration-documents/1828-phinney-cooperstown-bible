**Header:** *Christ's love to his church.* | ST. JOHN. | *Comforts against tribulation.*

9 As the Father hath loved me, so have I loved you: continue ye in my love.

10 If ye keep my commandments, ye shall abide in my love; even as I have kept my Father's commandments, and abide in his love.

11 <sup>i</sup>These things have I spoken unto you, that my joy might remain in you, and *that* your joy might be full.

12 ¶ <sup>k</sup>This is my commandment, That ye love one another, as I have loved you.

13 <sup>l</sup>Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends.

14 <sup>m</sup>Ye are my friends, if ye do whatsoever I command you.

15 <sup>n</sup>Henceforth I call you not servants; for the servant knoweth not what his lord doeth: but I have called you friends; for all things that I have heard of my Father I have made known unto you.

16 <sup>o</sup>Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, and ordained you, that you should go and bring forth fruit, and *that* your fruit should remain; that whatsoever ye shall ask of the Father in my name, he may give it you.

17 <sup>p</sup>These things I command you, that ye love one another.

18 ¶ <sup>q</sup>If the world hate you, ye know that it hated me before *it hated* you.

19 <sup>r</sup>If ye were of the world, the world would love his own: but because ye are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hateth you.

20 <sup>s</sup>Remember the word that I said unto you, The servant is not greater than his lord. If they have persecuted me, they will also persecute you: if they have kept my saying, they will keep yours also.

21 <sup>t</sup>But all these things will they do unto you for my name's sake, because they know not him that sent me.

22 <sup>u</sup>If I had not come and spoken unto them, they had not had sin: but now they have no cloak for their sin.

23 <sup>x</sup>He that hateth me hateth my Father also.

24 <sup>y</sup>If I had not done among them the works which none other man did, they had not had sin: but now have they both seen and hated both me and my Father.

25 <sup>z</sup>But *this cometh to pass,* that the word might be fulfilled that is written in their law, They hated me without a cause.

26 <sup>a</sup>But when the Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, *even* the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth from the Father, he shall testify of me:

27 <sup>b</sup>And ye also shall bear witness, because ye have been with me from the beginning.

## CHAPTER XVI.

1 Christ warneth his disciples of their sufferings: 29 they profess their faith in him.

<sup>a</sup>THESE things have I spoken unto you, that ye should not be offended.

2 <sup>b</sup>They shall put you out of the synagogues: yea, the time cometh, that whosoever killeth you will think that he doeth God service.

3 <sup>c</sup>And these things will they do unto you, because they have not known the Father, nor me.

4 <sup>d</sup>But these things have I told you, that, when the time shall come, ye may remember that I told you of them. And these things I said not unto you at the beginning, because I was with you.

5 <sup>e</sup>But now I go my way to him that sent me; and none of you asketh me, Whither goest thou?

6 <sup>f</sup>But because I have said these things unto you, sorrow hath filled your heart.

7 <sup>g</sup>Nevertheless I tell you the truth; It is expedient for you that I go away: for if I go not away, the Comforter will not come unto you; but if I depart, I will send him unto you.

8 <sup>h</sup>And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment:

9 Of sin, because they believe not on me;

10 <sup>i</sup>Of righteousness, because I go to my Father, and ye see me no more;

11 <sup>k</sup>Of judgment, because the prince of this world is judged.

12 <sup>l</sup>I have yet many things to say unto you, but ye cannot bear them now.

13 <sup>m</sup>Howbeit when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth: for he shall not speak of himself; but whatsoever he shall hear, *that* shall he speak: and he will shew you things to come.

14 He shall glorify me: for he shall receive of mine, and shall shew *it* unto you.

15 <sup>n</sup>All things that the Father hath are mine: therefore said I, that he shall take of mine, and shall shew *it* unto you.

16 <sup>o</sup>A little while, and ye shall not see me: and again, A little while, and ye shall see me; because I go to the Father.

17 Then said *some* of his disciples among themselves, What is this that he saith unto us, A little while and ye shall not see me: and again, A little while, and ye shall see me: and, Because I go to the Father?

18 They said therefore, What is this that he saith, A little while? We cannot tell what he saith.

19 Now Jesus knew that they were desirous to ask him, and said unto them, Do ye inquire among yourselves of that I said, A little while, and ye shall not see me: and again, A little while, and ye shall see me?

20 <sup>p</sup>Verily, verily, I say unto you, That ye shall weep and lament, but the world shall rejoice: and ye shall be sorrowful, but your sorrow shall be turned into joy.

21 A woman when she is in travail hath sorrow, because her hour is come: but as soon as she is delivered of the child, she remembereth no more the anguish, for joy that a man is born into the world.

22 <sup>q</sup>And ye now therefore have sorrow: but I will see you again, and your heart shall rejoice, and your joy no man taketh from you.

23 <sup>r</sup>And in that day ye shall ask me nothing. Verily, verily, I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall ask the Father in my name, he will give *it* you.

24 <sup>s</sup>Hitherto have ye asked nothing in my name: ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full.

25 <sup>t</sup>These things have I spoken unto you in proverbs: but the time cometh when I shall no more speak unto you in proverbs, but I shall shew you plainly of the Father.

26 <sup>u</sup>At that day ye shall ask in my name: and I say not unto you that I will pray the Father for you:

27 <sup>x</sup>For the Father himself loveth you, because ye have loved me, and have believed that I came out from God.

28 <sup>y</sup>I came forth from the Father, and am come into the world: again, I leave the world, and go to the Father.
