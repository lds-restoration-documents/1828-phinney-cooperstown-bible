## CHAPTER XIX.

The water of separation made of the ashes of a red heifer.

AND the <sup>a</sup>LORD spake unto Moses, and unto Aaron, saying,

2 This *is* the ordinance of the law which the LORD hath commanded, saying, Speak unto the children of Israel, that they bring thee a red heifer <sup>b</sup>without spot, wherein *is* no blemish, *and* upon which <sup>c</sup>never came yoke.

3 And ye shall give her unto <sup>d</sup>Eleazar the priest, that he may bring her forth <sup>e</sup>without the camp, and *one* shall slay her before his face.

4 And Eleazar the priest shall take of her blood with his <sup>f</sup>finger, and <sup>g</sup>sprinkle of her blood directly before the <sup>h</sup>tabernacle of the congregation seven times.

5 And *one* shall burn the heifer in his sight; her skin, and her flesh, and her blood, with her dung, shall he burn:

6 And the priest shall take <sup>i</sup>cedar-wood, and <sup>k</sup>hyssop, and <sup>l</sup>scarlet, and cast *it* into the midst of the burning of the heifer.

7 Then the priest shall <sup>m</sup>wash his clothes, and he shall bathe his flesh in water, and afterward he shall come into the camp, and the priest shall be unclean until the even.

8 And he that burneth her shall wash his clothes in water, and bathe his flesh in water, and shall be unclean until the even.
