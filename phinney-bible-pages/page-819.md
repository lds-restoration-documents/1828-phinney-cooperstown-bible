## CHAPTER VI.

1 He willeth them to reform the faulty with gentleness, and to bear one another's burdens; 6 to be liberal to their teachers, 9 and not to be weary in well-doing.

<sup>a</sup>BRETHREN, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual restore such a one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

2 <sup>b</sup>Bear ye one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.

3 <sup>c</sup>For if a man think himself to be something, when he is nothing, he deceiveth himself.

4 <sup>d</sup>But let every man prove his own work, and then shall he have rejoicing in himself alone, and not in another:

5 <sup>e</sup>For every man shall bear his own burden.

6 <sup>f</sup>Let him that is taught in the word communicate unto him that teacheth in all good things.

7 <sup>g</sup>Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.

8 <sup>h</sup>For he that soweth to his flesh, shall of the flesh reap corruption; but he that soweth to the Spirit, shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting.

9 <sup>i</sup>And let us not be weary in well-doing; for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.

10 <sup>k</sup>As we have therefore opportunity, let us do good unto all *men,* especially unto them who are of the household of faith.

11 Ye see how large a letter I have written unto you with mine own hand.

12 <sup>l</sup>As many as desire to make a fair shew in the flesh, they constrain you to be circumcised, only lest they should suffer persecution for the cross of Christ.

13 <sup>m</sup>For neither they themselves who are circumcised keep the law; but desire to have you circumcised, that they may glory in your flesh.

14 <sup>n</sup>But God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom the world is crucified unto me, and I unto the world.

15 <sup>o</sup>For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision, but a new creature.

16 <sup>p</sup>And as many as walk according to this rule, peace *be* on them, and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.

17 <sup>q</sup>From henceforth let no man trouble me; for I bear in my body the marks of the Lord Jesus.

18 <sup>r</sup>Brethren, the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ *be* with your spirit. Amen.

# ¶ The Epistle of PAUL, the Apostle, to the EPHESIANS.

## CHAPTER I.

1 Paul blesseth God for spiritual blessings....4 Of election, 5 and adoption, 11 which is the proper fountain of man's salvation, &c.

<sup>a</sup>PAUL, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus:

2 <sup>b</sup>Grace *be* to you, and peace, from God our Father, and *from* the Lord Jesus Christ.

3 <sup>c</sup>Blessed *be* the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly *places* in Christ:

4 <sup>d</sup>According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy, and without blame before him in love:

5 <sup>e</sup>Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,

6 <sup>f</sup>To the praise of the glory of his grace, wherein he hath made us accepted in the Beloved:

7 <sup>g</sup>In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace;

8 <sup>h</sup>Wherein he hath abounded toward us in all wisdom and prudence;

9 <sup>i</sup>Having made known unto us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he hath purposed in himself:

10 <sup>k</sup>That in the dispensation of the fulness of times, he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven and which are on earth; *even* in him:

11 <sup>l</sup>In whom also we have obtained an <sup>†</sup>inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will:

12 <sup>m</sup>That we should be to the praise of his glory, who first trusted in Christ.

13 <sup>n</sup>In whom ye also *trusted,* after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also, after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that Holy Spirit of promise,

14 <sup>o</sup>Which is the earnest of our inheritance, until the redemption of the purchased possession, unto the praise of his glory.

15 <sup>p</sup>Wherefore I also, after I heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus, and love unto all the saints,

16 Cease not to give thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers;

17 <sup>q</sup>That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him:

18 <sup>r</sup>The eyes of your understanding being enlightened; that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, and what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,
