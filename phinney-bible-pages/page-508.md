## CHAPTER V.

The judgment of Jerusalem for their rebellion.

3 Thou shalt also take thereof <sup>e</sup>a few in number, and bind them in thy skirts.

4 Then take of them again, and cast them into the midst of the fire, and burn them in the fire; *for* thereof shall <sup>f</sup>a fire come forth into all the house of Israel.

7 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, Because ye <sup>l</sup>multiplied more than the nations that *are* round about you, *and* have not walked in my statutes, neither have kept my judgments, neither have done <sup>m</sup>according to the judgments of the nations that *are* round about you;

8 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold <sup>n</sup>I, even I, *am* against thee, and will execute judgments in the midst of thee, in the sight of the nations.

9 And I will do in thee that which I have not <sup>o</sup>done, and whereunto I will not do any more the like, because of all thine abominations.

10 Therefore the fathers shall <sup>p</sup>eat the sons in the midst of thee, and the sons shall eat their fathers; and I will execute judgments in thee, and the <sup>q</sup>whole remnant of thee will I scatter into all the winds.

11 Wherefore, *as* I live, saith the Lord GOD, Surely, because thou hast <sup>r</sup>defiled my sanctuary with all thy detestable things, and with all thine abominations, therefore will I also diminish *thee;* neither shall <sup>s</sup>mine eye spare, neither will I have any pity.

12 A third part of thee shall die with the <sup>t</sup>pestilence, and with <sup>u</sup>famine shall they be consumed in the midst of thee; and a third part shall fall by the <sup>x</sup>sword round about thee; and I will scatter a third part into all the winds; and I will draw out a sword after them.

13 Thus shall mine <sup>y</sup>anger be accomplished, and I will cause my fury to rest upon them, and I will be <sup>z</sup>comforted: and they shall <sup>a</sup>know that I the LORD have spoken *it* in my zeal, when I have accomplished my fury in them.

14 Moreover, I will make <sup>b</sup>thee waste, and <sup>c</sup>a reproach among the nations that *are* round about thee, in the sight of all that pass by.

15 So it shall be a reproach and a taunt, <sup>d</sup>an instruction and an astonishment, unto the nations that *are* round about thee, when I shall execute judgments in thee, in anger and in fury, and in <sup>e</sup>furious rebukes. I the LORD have spoken *it.*

16 When I shall send upon them the evil <sup>f</sup>arrows of famine, which shall be for *their* destruction, *and* which I will send to destroy you: and I will increase the famine upon you, and will break your <sup>g</sup>staff of bread:

17 So will I send upon you famine, and <sup>h</sup>evil beasts, and they shall bereave thee; and pestilence and blood shall pass through thee; and I will bring the sword upon thee. I the LORD have spoken *it.*

The faithful are exhorted to lament their calamities.
