21 ¶ But I had pity <sup>e</sup>for my holy name, which the house of Israel had profaned among the heathen, whither they went.

22 Therefore say unto the house of Israel, Thus saith the Lord GOD; I do not *this* <sup>f</sup>for your sakes, O house of Israel, but for my holy name's sake, which ye have profaned among the heathen, whither ye went.

23 And I will <sup>g</sup>sanctify my great name, which was profaned among the heathen, which ye have profaned in the midst of them; and the heathen shall know that I *am* the LORD, saith the Lord GOD, when I shall be sanctified in you before their eyes.

24 For I will <sup>h</sup>take you from among the heathen, and gather you out of all countries, and will <sup>i</sup>bring you into your own land.

25 ¶ Then will I <sup>k</sup>sprinkle clean water upon you, and ye shall be clean: from all your <sup>l</sup>filthiness, and from all your idols, will I cleanse you.

26 <sup>m</sup>A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you a heart of flesh.

27 And I will put my Spirit within you, and <sup>n</sup>cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do *them.*

28 And ye shall dwell in the land that I gave to your fathers; and ye shall be <sup>o</sup>my people, and I will be your God.

29 I will also <sup>p</sup>save you from all your uncleannesses: and I will <sup>q</sup>call for the corn, and will increase it, and lay no famine upon you.

30 And I will <sup>r</sup>multiply the fruit of the tree, and the increase of the field, that ye shall receive no more reproach of famine among the heathen.

31 <sup>s</sup>Then shall ye remember your own evil ways, and your doings that *were* not good, and shall <sup>t</sup>loathe yourselves in your own sight for your iniquities, and for your abominations.

32 <sup>u</sup>Not for your sakes do I *this,* saith the Lord GOD, be it known unto you: be ashamed and confounded for your own ways, O house of Israel.

33 Thus saith the Lord GOD; In the day that I shall have cleansed you from all your iniquities, I will also cause *you* to <sup>x</sup>dwell in the cities, and the wastes shall be builded.

34 And the desolate land shall be <sup>y</sup>tilled, whereas it lay desolate in the sight of all that passed by.

35 And <sup>z</sup>they shall say, This land that was desolate is become like the <sup>a</sup>garden of Eden; and the waste, and desolate, and ruined cities, *are become* fenced, *and* are inhabited.

36 Then the heathen, that are left round about you, shall <sup>b</sup>know that I the LORD build the ruined *places, and* plant that that was desolate: I the LORD have <sup>c</sup>spoken *it,* and I will do *it.*

37 Thus saith the Lord GOD, I will yet *for* this be <sup>d</sup>inquired of by the house of Israel to do *it* for them; I will increase them <sup>e</sup>with men like a flock.

38 As the <sup>f</sup>holy flock, as the flock of Jerusalem in her solemn feasts, so shall the waste cities be filled with flocks of men; and they shall know that I *am* the LORD.

## CHAPTER XXXVII.

1 The resurrection of dry bones....21 The promises of Christ's kingdom.

THE <sup>a</sup>hand of the LORD was upon me, and carried me out <sup>b</sup>in the Spirit of the LORD, and set me down in the midst of the valley which *was* <sup>c</sup>full of bones,

2 And caused me to pass by them round about: and, behold, *there were* very many in the open valley; and, lo, *they were* very dry.

3 And he said unto me, Son of man, <sup>d</sup>can these bones live? And I answered, O Lord GOD, <sup>e</sup>thou knowest.

4 Again he said unto me, <sup>f</sup>Prophesy upon these bones, and say unto them, <sup>g</sup>O ye dry bones, hear the word of the LORD.

5 Thus saith the Lord GOD unto these bones, Behold, <sup>h</sup>I will cause breath to enter into you, and ye shall live:

6 And I will lay sinews upon you, and will bring up flesh upon you, and cover you with skin, and put breath in you, and ye shall live; and ye shall know that I *am* the LORD.

7 So I prophesied as I was commanded: and <sup>i</sup>as I prophesied, there was a noise, and, behold, <sup>k</sup>a shaking, and the bones came together, bone to his bone.

8 And when I beheld, lo, the sinews and the flesh came up upon them, and the skin covered them above: but *there was* no breath in them.

9 Then said he unto me, Prophesy unto the <sup>l</sup>wind, prophesy, son of man, and say to the wind, Thus saith the Lord GOD, Come from the <sup>m</sup>four winds, O breath, and breathe upon these slain, that they may live.

10 So I prophesied, as he commanded me, and the breath came into them, and they lived, and <sup>n</sup>stood up upon their feet, <sup>o</sup>an exceeding great army.

11 Then he said unto me, Son of man, these bones are the <sup>p</sup>whole house of Israel: behold they say, Our bones <sup>q</sup>are dried, and our hope is lost; we are <sup>r</sup>cut off for our parts.

12 Therefore prophesy, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, O my people, I will <sup>s</sup>open your graves, and cause you to come up out of your graves, and bring you into the <sup>t</sup>land of Israel.

13 And ye shall know that I *am* the LORD, when I have opened your graves, O my people, and brought you up out of your graves,

14 And shall <sup>u</sup>put my Spirit in you, and ye shall live; and I shall place you in your own land: then shall ye know that I the LORD have spoken *it,* and performed *it,* saith the LORD.

15 ¶ The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying,

16 Moreover, thou son of man, take thee one stick, and write upon it, For <sup>x</sup>Judah, and for the children of Israel his companions: then take another stick, and write upon it, For Joseph, the stick of Ephraim, and *for* all the house of Israel his companions:

17 And <sup>y</sup>join them one to another into one stick; and they shall become <sup>z</sup>one in thy hand.

18 And when the children of <sup>a</sup>thy people shall speak unto thee, saying, Wilt thou not shew us what thou *meanest* by these?

19 Say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, <sup>b</sup>I will take the stick of Joseph, which *is* in the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel his fellows, and will put them with him, *even* with the stick of Judah, and make them one stick, and they shall be one in my hand.

20 And the sticks whereon thou writest shall be in thy hand <sup>c</sup>before their eyes.
