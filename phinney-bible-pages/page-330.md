13 And I went out by night, by the <sup>b</sup>gate of the valley, even before the <sup>c</sup>dragon-well, and to the <sup>d</sup>dung-port, and viewed the walls of Jerusalem which were broken down, and the gates thereof were consumed with fire.

14 Then I went on to the gate of the <sup>e</sup>fountain, and to the <sup>f</sup>king's pool: but *there was* no place for the beast *that was* under me to pass.

15 Then went I up in the night by <sup>g</sup>the brook, and viewed the wall, and turned back, and entered by the gate of the valley, and *so* returned.

16 And the rulers <sup>h</sup>knew not whither I went, or what I did; neither had I as yet told *it* to the Jews, nor to the priests, nor to the nobles, nor to the rulers, nor to the rest that did the work.

17 Then said I unto them, Ye see the distress that we *are* in, how Jerusalem *lieth* waste, and the gates thereof are burnt with fire: come, and let us <sup>i</sup>build up the wall of Jerusalem, that we be no more <sup>k</sup>a reproach.

18 Then I told them of the hand of my God which was <sup>l</sup>good upon me; as also the king's words that he had spoken unto me. And they said, Let us rise up and build. So they <sup>m</sup>strengthened their hands for *this* good *work.*

19 But when Sanballat the Horonite, and Tobiah the servant, the Ammonite, and <sup>n</sup>Geshem the Arabian, heard *it,* they <sup>o</sup>laughed us to scorn, and despised us, and said, What *is* this thing that ye do? will ye <sup>p</sup>rebel against the king?

20 Then answered I them, and said unto them, The God of heaven he will prosper us; therefore we his servants will arise and build: but you have no <sup>q</sup>portion, nor <sup>r</sup>right, nor memorial, in Jerusalem.

## CHAPTER III.

The names and order of them that builded the wall.

THEN <sup>a</sup>Eliashib the high priest rose up, with <sup>b</sup>his brethren the priests, and they builded the <sup>c</sup>sheep-gate; they sanctified it, and set up the doors of it; even unto the tower of Meah they sanctified it, unto the tower of <sup>d</sup>Hananeel.

2 And next unto him builded the men of Jericho. And next to them builded Zaccur the son of Imri.

3 But the <sup>e</sup>fish-gate did the sons of Hassenaah build, who *also* laid the beams thereof, and set up the <sup>f</sup>doors thereof, the locks thereof, and the bars thereof.

4 And next unto them repaired Meremoth the son of <sup>g</sup>Urijah, the son of Koz. And next unto them repaired Meshullam the son of Berechiah, the son of Meshezabeel. And next unto them repaired Zadok the son of Baana.

5 And next unto them the <sup>h</sup>Tekoites repaired; but their <sup>i</sup>nobles put <sup>k</sup>not their necks to the work of their LORD.

6 Moreover, the <sup>l</sup>old gate repaired Jehoiada the son of Paseah, and Meshullam the son of Besodeiah; they laid the beams thereof, and set up the doors thereof, and the locks thereof, and the bars thereof.

7 And next unto them repaired Melatiah the Gibeonite, and Jadon the Meronothite, the men of <sup>m</sup>Gibeon and of <sup>n</sup>Mizpah, unto the <sup>o</sup>throne of the governor on this side the river.

8 Next unto him repaired Uzziel the son of Harhaiah, of the goldsmiths. Next unto him also repaired Hananiah the son of *one of* the apothecaries, and they fortified Jerusalem unto the <sup>p</sup>broad wall.

9 And next unto them repaired Rephaiah the son of Hur, the ruler of the half part of Jerusalem.

10 And next unto them <sup>q</sup>repaired Jedaiah the son of Harumaph, even over against his house. And next unto him repaired <sup>r</sup>Hattush <!-- uncertain: printed as Hat?ush, a blemish over the second t --> the son of Hashabuiah.

11 <sup>s</sup>Malchijah the son of Harim, and Hashub the son of Pahath-moab, repaired the other piece, and the tower of the furnaces.

12 And next unto him repaired Shallum the son of Halohesh, the ruler of the half part of Jerusalem, he and his <sup>t</sup>daughters.

13 The <sup>u</sup>valley-gate repaired Hanun, and the inhabitants of <sup>x</sup>Zanoah; they built it, and set up the doors thereof, the locks thereof, and the bars thereof, and a thousand cubits on the wall unto the <sup>y</sup>dung-gate.

14 But the dung-gate repaired Malchiah the son of <sup>z</sup>Rechab, the ruler of part of <sup>a</sup>Beth-haccerem; he built it, and set up the doors thereof, the locks thereof, and the bars thereof.

15 But the gate of the <sup>b</sup>fountain repaired Shallum the son of Col-hozeh, the ruler of part of Mizpah: he built it, and covered it, and set up the doors thereof, the locks thereof, and the bars thereof, and the wall of the pool of <sup>c</sup>Siloah by the king's garden, and unto the stairs that go down from the <sup>d</sup>city of David.

16 After him repaired Nehemiah the son of Azbuk, the ruler of the half part of <sup>e</sup>Beth-zur, unto *the place* over against the sepulchres of David, and to the <sup>f</sup>pool that was made, and unto the <sup>g</sup>house of the mighty.

17 After him repaired the Levites, <sup>h</sup>Rehum the son of Bani. Next unto him repaired Hashabiah, the ruler of the half part of <sup>i</sup>Keilah, in his part.

18 After him repaired their brethren, Bavai the son of <sup>k</sup>Henadad, the ruler of the half part of Keilah.

19 And next to him repaired <sup>l</sup>Ezer the son of Jeshua, the ruler of Mizpah, another piece over against the going up to the armoury, at the <sup>||</sup>turning *of the wall.*

20 After him Baruch the son of Zabbai <sup>†</sup>earnestly repaired the other piece, from the turning *of the wall* unto the door of the house of Eliashib the high priest.

21 After him repaired <sup>m</sup>Meremoth the son of Urijah, the son of <sup>n</sup>Koz, another piece, from the door of the house of Eliashib even to the end of the house of Eliashib.

22 And after him repaired the priests, the men of the <sup>o</sup>plain.

23 After him repaired Benjamin and Hashub, over against their house. After him repaired Azariah the son of Maaseiah, the son of Ananiah, by his house.

24 After him repaired Binnui the son of Henadad, another piece, from the house of Azariah unto the turning *of the wall,* even unto the corner.

25 Palal the son of Uzai, over against the turning *of the wall,* and the tower which lieth out from the <sup>p</sup>king's high house, that *was* by the court of the prison. After him Pedaiah the son of Parosh.

26 Moreover, the <sup>q</sup>Nethinims dwelt in <sup>||</sup>Ophel, unto *the place* over against the <sup>r</sup>water-gate toward the east, and the tower that lieth out.

27 After them the Tekoites repaired another piece, over against the great tower that lieth out, even unto the wall of Ophel.

28 From above the <sup>s</sup>horse-gate repaired the priests, every one over against his house.

29 After them repaired Zadok the son of Immer, over against his house. After him repaired also Shemaiah the son of Shechaniah, the keeper of the <sup>t</sup>east gate.
