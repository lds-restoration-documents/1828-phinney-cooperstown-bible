## CHAPTER II.

1 The terribleness of God's judgments....12 He exhorteth to repentance, 18 and promiseth a blessing thereon.

BLOW ye the trumpet in Zion, <sup>a</sup>sound an alarm in my holy mountain: let all the inhabitants of the land <sup>b</sup>tremble: for the day of the LORD cometh, for *it is* nigh at <sup>c</sup>hand:

2 A day of darkness, and of gloominess, a day of <sup>d</sup>clouds and of thick darkness, as the morning spread upon the mountains: <sup>e</sup>a great people and a strong; there hath not been ever the like, neither shall be any more after it, *even* to the years of many generations.

3 A fire <sup>f</sup>devoureth before them; and behind them a flame burneth: the land *is* <sup>g</sup>as the garden of Eden before them, and behind them <sup>h</sup>a desolate wilderness; yea, and nothing shall escape them.

4 The appearance of them *is* as the appearance of <sup>i</sup>horses; and as horsemen, so shall they run.

5 Like the noise of chariots on the tops of mountains shall they leap, like the noise of a flame of fire that devoureth the stubble, as a strong people set in battle array.

6 Before their face the people shall be much pained; all faces shall gather <sup>†</sup>blackness.

7 They shall run like mighty men; they shall <sup>k</sup>climb the wall like men of war; and they shall <sup>l</sup>march every one on his ways, and they shall not break their ranks:

8 Neither shall one thrust another; they shall walk every one <sup>m</sup>in his path: and *when* they fall upon the sword, they shall not be wounded.

9 They shall run to and fro in the city; they shall run upon the wall; they shall climb up upon the houses; they shall enter in at the <sup>n</sup>windows like a thief.

10 The earth shall <sup>o</sup>quake before them; the heavens shall tremble: the <sup>p</sup>sun and the moon shall be dark, and the stars shall withdraw their shining;

11 And the LORD shall <sup>q</sup>utter his voice before his army; for his camp *is* very great: for *he is* <sup>r</sup>strong that executeth his word: for the day of the LORD *is* great and very terrible; and <sup>s</sup>who can abide it?

12 Therefore also <sup>t</sup>now, saith the LORD, Turn ye *even* to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning;

13 And <sup>u</sup>rend your heart, and not your garments, and turn unto the LORD your God: for he *is* gracious and merciful, <sup>x</sup>slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repenteth him of the evil.

14 Who knoweth *if* he will return and repent, and <sup>y</sup>leave a blessing behind him, *even* a meat-offering, and a drink-offering, unto the LORD your God?

15 Blow the trumpet in Zion, <sup>z</sup>sanctify a fast, call a solemn assembly:

16 Gather the people, sanctify the congregation, assemble the elders, gather the <sup>a</sup>children, and those that suck the breasts; let the <sup>b</sup>bridegroom go forth of his chamber, and the bride out of her closet:

17 Let the priests, the ministers of the LORD, weep <sup>c</sup>between the porch and the altar, and let them say, Spare thy <sup>d</sup>people, O LORD, and give not thy heritage to reproach, that the heathen should rule over them: wherefore should they say among the people, <sup>e</sup>Where *is* their God?

18 Then will the LORD be <sup>f</sup>jealous for his land, and <sup>g</sup>pity his people.

19 Yea, the LORD will <sup>h</sup>answer and say unto his people, Behold, I will send you <sup>i</sup>corn and wine, and oil, and ye shall be satisfied therewith; and I will no more make you a reproach among the heathen:

20 But I will remove far off from you the <sup>k</sup>northern *army,* and will drive him into a land barren and desolate, with his face toward the east sea; and his hinder part toward the utmost sea: and his stink shall come up, and his ill savour shall come up, because he hath done great things.

21 <sup>l</sup>Fear not, O land; be glad and rejoice: for the LORD will do <sup>m</sup>great things.

22 Be not afraid, <sup>n</sup>ye beasts of the field: for the pastures of the wilderness do spring, for the tree beareth her fruit, the fig-tree and the vine do yield their strength.

23 Be glad then, ye children of Zion, and rejoice in the LORD your God: for he hath given you the former rain moderately, and he will cause to come down for you the rain, the former rain, and the latter rain in the first *month.*

24 And the floors shall be <sup>o</sup>full of wheat, and the fats shall overflow with wine and oil.

25 And I will <sup>p</sup>restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten, the canker-worm, and the caterpillar, and the palmer-worm, my great army, which I sent among you.

26 And ye shall eat in plenty, and be satisfied, and <sup>q</sup>praise the name of the LORD your God, that hath dealt wonderously with you: and my people shall never be <sup>r</sup>ashamed.

27 And ye shall know that I *am* in the <sup>s</sup>midst of Israel, and *that* I *am* the LORD your God, and none else: and my people shall never be ashamed.

28 And it shall come to pass <sup>t</sup>afterward, *that* I will <sup>u</sup>pour out my Spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions:

29 And also upon the <sup>x</sup>servants and upon the handmaids in those days will I pour out my Spirit.

30 And I will shew <sup>y</sup>wonders in the heavens and in the earth, blood, and fire, and pillars of smoke.

31 The <sup>z</sup>sun shall be turned into darkness, <sup>a</sup>and the moon into blood, before the great and the terrible day of the LORD come.

32 And it shall come to pass, *that* whosoever shall <sup>b</sup>call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered: for in mount Zion and in Jerusalem shall be deliverance, as the LORD hath said, and in the <sup>c</sup>remnant whom the LORD shall call.
