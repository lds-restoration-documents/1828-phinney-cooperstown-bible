## PSALM XVII.

David craveth defence of God against his enemies.

*A prayer of David.*

HEAR the right, O LORD, attend unto my cry; give ear unto my prayer, *that goeth* <sup>a</sup>not out of feigned lips.

2 Let my sentence come forth from thy presence; let thine eyes behold the things that are equal.

3 Thou hast proved my heart; thou hast visited me in the night; thou hast tried me, *and* shalt <sup>b</sup>find nothing: I am <sup>c</sup>purposed *that* my mouth shall not transgress.

4 Concerning the works of men, by the word of thy lips I have kept *me from* the paths of the <sup>d</sup>destroyer.

5 <sup>e</sup>Hold up my goings in thy paths, *that* my footsteps slip not.

6 I have called upon thee; for thou <sup>f</sup>wilt hear me, O God: incline thine ear unto me, *and hear* my speech.

7 Shew thy marvellous loving-kindness, O thou that <sup>g</sup>savest by thy right hand them which put their trust *in thee* from those that rise up *against them.*

8 Keep me as the apple of the eye; hide <sup>h</sup>me under the shadow of thy wings,

9 From the wicked that oppress me, *from* my <sup>i</sup>deadly enemies *who* compass me about.

10 They are enclosed in their own <sup>k</sup>fat: with their mouth they speak proudly.

11 They have now <sup>l</sup>compassed us in our steps; they have set their eyes bowing down to the earth;

12 Like as <sup>m</sup>a lion *that* is greedy of his prey, and as it were a young lion lurking in secret places.

13 Arise, O LORD, disappoint him, cast him down: deliver my soul from the wicked, *which is* thy <sup>n</sup>sword:

14 From men *which are* thy hand, O LORD, from men of the world, *which have* their <sup>o</sup>portion in *this* life, and whose belly thou fillest with thy hid treasure: they are full of children, and leave the rest of their *substance* to their babes.

15 As for me, I will behold thy <sup>p</sup>face in righteousness: I shall be satisfied, when I awake, with thy likeness.

## PSALM XVIII.

David praiseth God for his manifold and marvellous blessings.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David, the* <sup>a</sup>*servant of the LORD, who spake unto the LORD the words of this* <sup>b</sup>*song in the day that the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of* <sup>c</sup>*Saul: and he said,*

I WILL <sup>d</sup>love thee, O LORD, my strength.

2 The LORD *is* my <sup>e</sup>rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the <sup>f</sup>horn of my salvation, *and* my high tower.

3 I will call upon the LORD, *who is* <sup>g</sup>*worthy* to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies.

4 The <sup>h</sup>sorrows of death compassed me, and the <sup>i</sup>floods of ungodly men made me afraid.

5 The sorrows of hell compassed me about; the <sup>k</sup>snares of death prevented me.

6 In my distress I called upon the LORD, and <sup>l</sup>cried unto my God: he heard my voice out of his temple, and my cry came before him, *even* into his ears.

7 Then the <sup>m</sup>earth shook and trembled; the foundations also of the hills moved and were shaken, because he was <sup>n</sup>wroth.

8 There went up <sup>o</sup>a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it.

9 <sup>p</sup>He bowed the heavens also, and came down: and darkness *was* under his feet.

10 And he rode upon <sup>q</sup>a cherub, and did fly; yea, he did fly upon the wings of the wind.

11 He made darkness his secret place: his pavilion round about him *were* dark waters *and* thick clouds of the skies.

12 At the brightness *that was* before him his thick clouds passed; hail-stones and coals of fire.

13 The LORD also <sup>r</sup>thundered in the heavens, and the Highest gave his voice; hail-*stones* and coals of fire.

14 Yea, he sent out his <sup>s</sup>arrows, and scattered them; and he shot out lightnings, and <sup>t</sup>discomfited them.

15 Then the channels of waters were seen, and the foundations of the world were discovered at thy rebuke, O LORD, at the <sup>u</sup>blast of the breath of thy nostrils.

16 He sent from above, he took me, he drew me out of <sup>x</sup>many waters.

17 He delivered me from my <sup>y</sup>strong enemy, and from them which hated me: for they were too strong for me.

18 They prevented me in the day of my calamity: but the LORD was my <sup>z</sup>stay.

19 He brought me forth also into <sup>a</sup>a large place: he delivered me, because he delighted in me.

20 The LORD rewarded me <sup>b</sup>according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.

21 For I have kept the ways of the LORD, and have not <sup>c</sup>wickedly departed from my God.

22 For all his judgments <sup>d</sup>*were* before me, and I did not put away his statutes from me.

23 I was also <sup>e</sup>upright before him, and I kept myself from mine iniquity.

24 Therefore hath the LORD recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his eye-sight.

25 With the merciful thou wilt shew thyself merciful; with an upright man thou wilt shew thyself upright;

26 With the pure thou wilt shew thyself pure; and with the <sup>f</sup>froward thou wilt shew thyself froward.

27 For thou wilt save the afflicted people; but wilt bring down <sup>g</sup>high looks.

28 For thou wilt <sup>h</sup>light my candle; the LORD my God will enlighten my darkness.

29 For by thee I have run through a troop; and by my God have I leaped over a wall.

30 *As for* God, his way *is* perfect: the word of the LORD is <sup>i</sup>tried; he *is* a buckler to all those that trust in him.

31 For <sup>k</sup>who *is* God save the LORD? or who *is* a rock save our God?

32 *It is* God that girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way perfect.

33 He maketh my feet like <sup>l</sup>hinds' *feet,* and setteth me upon my high places.

34 He <sup>m</sup>teacheth my hands to war, so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms.

35 Thou hast also <sup>n</sup>given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy right hand hath holden me up, and <sup>o</sup>thy gentleness hath made me great.

36 Thou hast <sup>p</sup>enlarged my steps under me, that my feet did not slip.

37 I have pursued mine enemies, and <sup>q</sup>overtaken them: neither did I turn again till they were consumed.
