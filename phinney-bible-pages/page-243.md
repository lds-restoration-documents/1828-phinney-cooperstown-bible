12 Then spake Solomon, The LORD said <sup>t</sup>that he would dwell in the thick darkness.

13 I have surely built <sup>u</sup>thee a house to dwell in, a settled place for thee to abide in for ever.

14 And the king turned his face about, and <sup>x</sup>blessed all the congregation of Israel: (and all the congregation of Israel stood:)

15 And he said, <sup>y</sup>Blessed *be* the LORD God of Israel, which <sup>z</sup>spake with his mouth unto David my father, and hath with his hand fulfilled *it,* saying,

16 Since the day that I brought forth my people Israel out of Egypt, I chose no city out of all the tribes of Israel to build a house, that <sup>a</sup>my name might be therein: but I chose David to be over my people Israel.

17 And it was <sup>b</sup>in the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of the LORD God of Israel.

18 And the LORD said unto David my father, Whereas it was in thine heart to build a house unto my name, thou <sup>c</sup>didst well that it was in thine heart:

19 Nevertheless <sup>d</sup>thou shalt not build the house; but thy son, that shall come forth out of thy loins, he shall build the house unto my name.

20 And the LORD hath performed his word that he spake; and I am risen up in the room of David my father, and sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD <sup>e</sup>promised, and have built a house for the name of the LORD God of Israel.

21 And I have set there a place for the ark, wherein *is* the <sup>f</sup>covenant of the LORD, which he made with our fathers, when he brought them out of the land of Egypt.

22 And Solomon <sup>g</sup>stood before the altar of the LORD, in the presence of all the congregation of Israel, and spread <sup>h</sup>forth his hands towards heaven:

23 And he said, LORD God of Israel, *there is* no god <sup>i</sup>like thee, in heaven above, or on earth beneath, who keepest covenant and mercy with thy servants that <sup>k</sup>walk before thee with all their heart;

24 Who hast kept with thy servant David my father that thou promisedst him: thou spakest also with thy mouth, and hast fulfilled *it* with thine hand, as *it is* this day.

25 Therefore now, LORD God of Israel, keep with thy servant David my father that thou promisedst him, saying, There shall <sup>l</sup>not fail thee a man in my sight to sit on the throne of Israel; so that thy children take heed to their way, that they walk before me, as thou hast walked before me.

26 And now, O God of Israel, let thy word, I pray thee, be verified, which thou spakest unto thy servant David my father.

27 But will God indeed <sup>m</sup>dwell on the earth? Behold, the <sup>n</sup>heaven, and heaven of heavens, <sup>o</sup>cannot contain thee; how much less this house that I have builded!

28 Yet have thou respect unto the <sup>p</sup>prayer of thy servant, and to his supplication, O LORD my God, to hearken unto the cry and to the prayer which thy servant prayeth before thee to-day:

29 That thine <sup>q</sup>eyes may be open toward this house night and day, *even* toward the place of which thou hast said, <sup>r</sup>My name shall be there; that thou mayest hearken unto the prayer which thy servant shall <sup>s</sup>make towards this place.

30 And hearken thou to the supplication of thy servant, and of <sup>t</sup>thy people Israel, when they shall pray towards this place: and hear thou in heaven thy dwelling-place; and when thou hearest, <sup>u</sup>forgive.

31 If any man <sup>x</sup>trespass against his neighbour, and an oath be laid upon him to cause him to swear, and the oath come <sup>y</sup>before thine altar in this house:

32 Then hear thou in heaven, and do, and judge thy servants, <sup>z</sup>condemning the wicked, to bring <sup>a</sup>his way upon his head; and justifying the righteous, to give him according to his righteousness.

33 When thy people Israel be smitten down before the enemy, because they have <sup>b</sup>sinned against thee, and shall turn again to thee, and confess thy name, and pray, and make supplication unto thee in this house:

34 Then hear thou in heaven, and forgive the sin of thy people Israel, and bring them again unto the land which thou gavest unto their fathers.

35 When heaven is <sup>c</sup>shut up, and there is no rain, because they have sinned against thee; if they pray toward this place, and confess thy name, and turn from their sin, when <sup>d</sup>thou afflictest them:

36 Then hear thou in heaven, and forgive the sin of thy servants, and of thy people Israel, that thou <sup>e</sup>teach them the <sup>f</sup>good way wherein they should walk, and give rain upon thy land which thou hast given to thy people for an inheritance.

37 If there be in the land famine, if there be pestilence, blasting, mildew, locust, *or* if there be caterpillar; if their enemy besiege them in the land of their cities, whatsoever plague, whatsoever sickness, *there be;*

38 What prayer and supplication soever be *made* by <sup>g</sup>any man, *or* by all thy people Israel, which shall know every man the plague of <sup>h</sup>his own heart, and spread forth his hands towards this house:

39 Then hear thou in heaven thy dwelling-place, and forgive, and do, and give to every man according to his ways, whose heart thou knowest; (for thou, *even* thou <sup>i</sup>only, knowest the hearts of all the children of men;)

40 That they may <sup>k</sup>fear thee all the days that they live in the land which thou gavest unto our fathers.

41 Moreover, concerning <sup>l</sup>a stranger that *is* not of thy people Israel, but cometh out of a <sup>m</sup>far country for <sup>n</sup>thy name's sake;

42 (For they shall hear of thy great name, and of thy <sup>o</sup>strong hand, and of thy stretched-out arm;) when he shall come and pray towards this house:

43 Hear thou in heaven thy dwelling-place, and do according to all that the stranger calleth to thee for; that <sup>p</sup>all people of the earth may know thy name, to fear thee, as *do* thy people Israel; and that they may know that this house, which I have builded, is called by thy name.

44 If thy people go out <sup>q</sup>to battle against their enemy, whithersoever thou shalt send them, and shall pray unto the LORD toward the city which thou hast chosen, and *toward* the house that I have built for thy name:
