## CHAPTER I.

David lamenteth Saul and Jonathan with a song.

NOW it came to pass, after the death of Saul, when David was returned from <sup>a</sup>the slaughter of the Amalekites, and David had abode <sup>b</sup>two days in Ziklag;

2 It came even to pass on the third day, that, behold, a man came out of the camp from Saul, with his <sup>c</sup>clothes rent, and <sup>d</sup>earth upon his head: and *so* it was, when he came to David, that he fell to the earth, and did obeisance.

3 And David said unto him, From whence comest thou? And he said unto him, Out of the camp of Israel am I escaped.

4 And David said unto him, How went the matter? I pray thee, tell me. And he answered, That the people are fled from the battle, and many of the people also are fallen and dead; and Saul and Jonathan his son are dead also.

5 And David said unto the young man that told him, <sup>e</sup>How knowest thou that Saul and Jonathan his son be dead?

6 And the young man that told him said, <sup>†</sup>As I happened by chance upon mount <sup>f</sup>Gilboa, behold, Saul leaned upon his spear; and, lo, the chariots and horsemen <sup>†</sup>followed hard after him.

7 And when he looked behind him he saw me, and called unto me: and I answered, here *am* I.

8 And he said unto me, Who *art* thou? And I answered him, I *am* <sup>g</sup>an Amalekite.

9 He said unto me again, Stand, I pray thee, upon me, and slay me; for anguish is come upon me, because my life *is* yet whole in me.

10 So I stood upon him, and <sup>h</sup>slew him, because I was sure that he could not live after that he was fallen: and I took the crown that *was* upon his head, and the bracelet that *was* on his arm, and have brought them hither unto my lord.

11 Then David took hold on his clothes, and <sup>i</sup>rent them; and likewise all the men that *were* with him:

12 And they <sup>k</sup>mourned, and wept, and fasted until even, for Saul, and for Jonathan his son, and for the people of the LORD, and for the house of Israel; because they were fallen by the sword.

13 And David said unto the young man that told him, Whence *art* thou? And he answered, I *am* the son of a stranger, an Amalekite.

14 And David said unto him, How wast thou <sup>l</sup>not afraid to stretch forth thine hand <sup>m</sup>to destroy <sup>n</sup>the LORD's anointed?

15 And David called one of the young men, and said, Go near, *and* fall upon him. And he <sup>o</sup>smote him that he died.

16 And David said unto him, <sup>p</sup>Thy blood *be* upon thy head: for thy mouth hath testified against thee, saying, I have slain the LORD's anointed.

17 And David lamented with <sup>q</sup>this lamentation over Saul and over Jonathan his son:

18 (Also he bade them teach the <sup>r</sup>children of Judah *the use of* <sup>s</sup>the bow: behold, *it is* written in the book of Jasher:)

19 The <sup>t</sup>beauty of Israel is slain upon thy high places: how are the mighty fallen!

20 Tell *it* not <sup>u</sup>in Gath, publish *it* not in the streets of Askelon; lest the daughters of the Philistines rejoice, lest the <sup>x</sup>daughters of the uncircumcised triumph.

21 Ye mountains of Gilboa, *let there be* no dew, neither *let there be* rain upon you, nor <sup>†</sup>fields of offerings: for there the shield of the mighty is vilely cast away, the shield of Saul, *as though he had* not *been* <sup>y</sup>anointed with oil.

22 From the blood of the slain, from the fat of the mighty, the bow of Jonathan turned not back, and the sword of Saul returned not empty.

23 Saul and Jonathan *were* <sup>z</sup>lovely and pleasant in their lives, and in their death they were not divided: they were swifter than eagles, they were stronger than lions.

24 Ye daughters of Israel, <sup>a</sup>weep over Saul, who <sup>b</sup>clothed you in scarlet, with *other* <sup>c</sup>delights; who put on ornaments of gold upon your apparel.

25 How are the <sup>d</sup>mighty fallen in the midst of the battle! O Jonathan, *thou wast* slain in thine high places.

26 I am distressed for thee, my brother Jonathan: very pleasant hast thou been unto me: thy love to me was <sup>e</sup>wonderful, passing the love of women.

27 How are the mighty fallen, and the <sup>f</sup>weapons of war perished!
