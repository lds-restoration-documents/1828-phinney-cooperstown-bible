## CHAPTER I.

2 Moses my servant is dead; now therefore arise, go over this Jordan, thou, and all this people, unto the land which <sup>c</sup>I do give to them, *even* to the children of Israel.

7 Only be thou strong and very courageous, that thou mayest observe to do according to <sup>l</sup>all the law which Moses my servant commanded thee: turn not from it *to* <sup>m</sup>the right hand or *to* the left, that thou mayest <sup>||</sup>prosper whithersoever thou goest.

13 Remember the word which Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you, saying, The LORD your God hath given you rest, and hath given you this land.

16 And they answered Joshua, saying, <sup>x</sup>All that thou commandest us we will do, and whithersoever thou sendest us we will go.
