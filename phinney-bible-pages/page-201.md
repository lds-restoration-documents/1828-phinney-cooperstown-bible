## CHAPTER XI.

Nahash offereth them of Jabesh-gilead a reproachful condition.

THEN Nahash the <sup>a</sup>Ammonite came up, and encamped against Jabesh-gilead: and all the men of <sup>b</sup>Jabesh said unto Nahash, <sup>c</sup>Make a covenant with us, and we will serve thee.

3 And the elders of Jabesh said unto him, Give us seven days' respite, that we may send messengers unto all the coasts of Israel: and then if *there be* no man to save us, we will come out to thee.

4 Then came the messengers to <sup>f</sup>Gibeah of Saul, and told the tidings in the ears of the people: and all the people lifted up their voices, and wept.

5 And, behold, Saul came after the <sup>||</sup>herd out of the field; and Saul said, What *aileth* the people that they weep? And they told him the tidings of the men of Jabesh.

6 And the <sup>g</sup>Spirit of God came upon Saul when he heard those tidings, and his <sup>h</sup>anger was kindled greatly.

7 And he took a yoke of oxen, and <sup>i</sup>hewed them in pieces, and sent *them* throughout all the coasts of Israel by the hands of messengers, saying, Whosoever cometh not forth after Saul and after Samuel, <sup>k</sup>so shall it be done unto his oxen. 9 And they said unto the messengers that came, Thus shall ye say unto the men of Jabesh-gilead, To-morrow, by *that time* the sun be hot, ye shall have help. And the messengers came and shewed *it* to the men of Jabesh; and they were glad.

10 Therefore the men of Jabesh said, To-morrow <sup>m</sup>we will come out unto you, and ye shall do with us all that seemeth good unto you.

11 And it was *so* on the <sup>n</sup>morrow, that Saul put the people in three <sup>†</sup>companies; and they came into the midst of the host in the <sup>o</sup>morning watch, and slew the Ammonites until the heat of the day: and it came to pass, that they which remained were scattered, so that <sup>p</sup>two of them were not left together.

12 And the people said unto Samuel, Who *is* he that said, Shall Saul <sup>q</sup>reign over us? bring the men, that we may put them to death.

13 And Saul said, There shall <sup>r</sup>not a man be put to death this day: for to-day the LORD hath wrought salvation in Israel.

14 Then said Samuel to the people, Come, and let us go to <sup>s</sup>Gilgal, and renew the kingdom there.

15 And all the people went to Gilgal; and there they made Saul <sup>t</sup>king before the LORD in Gilgal: and there they sacrificed sacrifices of peace-offerings before the LORD; and there Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced greatly.
