## CHAPTER VI.

The vanity of riches without use.

THERE is <sup>a</sup>an evil which I have seen under the sun, and it *is* common among men:

2 A man to whom God hath given riches, wealth, and honour, so that he wanteth nothing for his soul of all that he <sup>b</sup>desireth, yet God giveth him <sup>c</sup>not power to eat thereof, but a <sup>d</sup>stranger eateth it: this *is* vanity, and it *is* an evil disease.

3 If a man <sup>e</sup>beget a hundred *children,* and live many years, so that the days of his years be many, and his soul be <sup>f</sup>not filled with good, and also *that* he have <sup>g</sup>no burial; I say, *that* an untimely birth *is* better than he:

4 For he <sup>h</sup>cometh in with vanity, and departeth in <sup>i</sup>darkness, and his name shall be covered with darkness.

5 Moreover, he hath not seen the sun, nor known *any thing:* this hath <sup>k</sup>more rest than the other.

6 Yea, though he live <sup>l</sup>a thousand years twice *told,* yet hath he seen no good: do not all go to one place?

7 All the labour of man *is* for his <sup>m</sup>mouth, and yet the appetite is <sup>n</sup>not filled.

8 For what hath the wise <sup>o</sup>more than the fool? what hath the poor, that knoweth to walk before the living?

9 Better *is* the sight of the eyes than <sup>p</sup>the wandering of the desire: this *is* also vanity and vexation of spirit.

10 That which hath been is named already, and it is known that it *is* man: neither may he <sup>q</sup>contend with him that is mightier than he.

11 Seeing there be many things that <sup>r</sup>increase vanity, what *is* man the better?

12 For <sup>s</sup>who knoweth what *is* good for man in *this* life, all the days of his vain life which he spendeth as a <sup>t</sup>shadow? for who can tell a man what shall be after him under the sun?

## CHAPTER VII.

Remedies against vanity are, a good name, patience, and wisdom.

A <sup>a</sup>*GOOD* name *is* better than precious ointment; and the day of <sup>b</sup>death than the day of one's birth.

2 *It is* better to go to the house of mourning than to go to the <sup>c</sup>house of <sup>d</sup>feasting: for that *is* the end of all men; and the living will lay *it* to his heart.

3 <sup>e</sup>Sorrow *is* better than laughter: for by the sadness of the countenance the heart is made better.

4 The <sup>f</sup>heart of the wise *is* in the <sup>g</sup>house of mourning: but the heart of fools *is* in the house of mirth.

5 *It is* better to hear the <sup>h</sup>rebuke of the wise, than for a man to hear the song of fools.

6 For as the crackling of thorns under a pot, *so is* the <sup>i</sup>laughter of the fool. This also *is* vanity.

7 Surely oppression maketh <sup>k</sup>a wise man mad; and a gift destroyeth the heart.

8 Better *is* the <sup>l</sup>end of a thing than the beginning thereof; *and* the <sup>m</sup>patient in spirit *is* better than the proud in spirit.

9 Be not <sup>n</sup>hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of fools.

10 Say not thou, What is *the cause* that the <sup>o</sup>former days were better than these? for thou dost not inquire wisely concerning this.

11 ¶ Wisdom *is* <sup>p</sup>good with an inheritance; and *by it there is* profit to them that see the sun.

12 For wisdom *is* a <sup>q</sup>defence, *and* <sup>r</sup>money *is* a defence: but the excellency of knowledge *is, that* wisdom giveth life to them that have it.

13 Consider <sup>s</sup>the work of God: for who can make *that* <sup>t</sup>straight which he hath made crooked?

14 In the day of prosperity be joyful, but in the day of adversity <sup>u</sup>consider: God also hath set the one over against the other, to the end that man should find nothing after him.

15 All *things* have I seen in the days of my vanity: there is a just *man* that <sup>x</sup>perisheth in his righteousness, and there is a wicked *man* that <sup>y</sup>prolongeth *his life* in his wickedness.

16 Be not righteous <sup>z</sup>over-much; neither make thyself <sup>a</sup>over-wise: why shouldest thou destroy thyself?

17 Be not <sup>b</sup>over-much wicked, neither be thou foolish: why shouldest thou die <sup>c</sup>before thy time?

18 *It is* good that thou shouldest <sup>d</sup>take hold of this; yea, also from this withdraw not thy hand: for he that feareth God shall <sup>e</sup>come forth of them all.

19 Wisdom <sup>f</sup>strengtheneth the wise more than ten mighty *men* which are in the city.
