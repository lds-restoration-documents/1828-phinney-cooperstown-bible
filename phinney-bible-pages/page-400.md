### ZAIN.

49 <sup>x</sup>Remember the word unto thy servant, upon which thou hast <sup>y</sup>caused me to hope.

50 This *is* my <sup>z</sup>comfort in my affliction: for thy word hath quickened me.

51 <sup>a</sup>The proud have had me greatly in derision; *yet* have I not declined from thy law.

52 I remembered thy judgments of old, O LORD; and have <sup>b</sup>comforted myself.

53 <sup>c</sup>Horror hath taken hold upon me, because of the wicked that forsake thy law.

54 Thy statutes have been my songs in the house of my <sup>d</sup>pilgrimage.

55 I have remembered thy name, O LORD, <sup>e</sup>in the night, and have kept thy law.

56 This I had, <sup>f</sup>because I kept thy precepts.

### CHETH.

57 *Thou art* <sup>g</sup>my portion, O LORD: I have said that I would keep thy words.

58 I entreated <sup>†</sup>thy favour with *my* whole heart: be merciful unto me according to thy word.

59 <sup>h</sup>I thought on my ways, and turned my feet unto thy testimonies.

60 I <sup>i</sup>made haste, and delayed not to keep thy commandments.

61 <sup>k</sup>The bands of the wicked have robbed me: *but* I have not forgotten thy law.

62 <sup>l</sup>At midnight I will rise to give thanks unto thee, because of thy righteous judgments.

63 I *am* a <sup>m</sup>companion of all *them* that fear thee, and of them <sup>n</sup>that keep thy precepts.

64 <sup>o</sup>The earth, O LORD, is full of thy mercy: teach me thy statutes.

### TETH.

65 Thou hast dealt well with thy servant, O LORD, according unto thy word.

66 Teach me good judgment and knowledge: for I have believed thy commandments.

67 Before I was <sup>p</sup>afflicted I went astray: but now have I kept thy word.

68 Thou *art* <sup>q</sup>good, and doest good: teach me thy statutes.

69 The proud have <sup>r</sup>forged a lie against me: *but* I will keep thy precepts with *my* whole heart.

70 Their heart is as <sup>s</sup>fat as grease: *but* I delight in thy law.

71 *It is* good for me that I have been afflicted; that I might learn thy statutes.

72 The law of thy mouth <sup>t</sup>*is* better unto me than thousands of gold and silver.

### JOD.

73 Thy hands have <sup>u</sup>made me, and fashioned me: give me understanding, that I may learn thy commandments.

74 They that fear thee will be glad when they <sup>x</sup>see me; because I have hoped in thy word.

75 I know, O LORD, that thy judgments *are* right, and *that* thou <sup>y</sup>in faithfulness hast afflicted me.

76 Let, I pray thee, thy merciful kindness be for my comfort, according to thy word unto thy servant.

77 Let thy tender mercies come unto me, that I may live: for thy law *is* my <sup>†</sup>delight.

78 Let the proud be <sup>z</sup>ashamed; for they dealt perversely with me without a cause: <sup>a</sup>*but* I will meditate in thy precepts.

79 Let those that fear thee turn unto me, and those that have known thy testimonies.

80 Let my heart be <sup>b</sup>sound in thy statutes, <sup>c</sup>that I be not ashamed.

### CAPH.

81 My soul <sup>d</sup>fainteth for thy salvation; *but* I hope in thy word.

82 Mine eyes <sup>e</sup>fail for thy word, saying, When wilt thou comfort me?

83 For I am become like a bottle in the <sup>f</sup>smoke; *yet* do I not forget thy statutes.

84 How many *are* <sup>g</sup>the days of <sup>h</sup>thy servant? when wilt thou execute judgment on them that persecute me?

85 The proud have <sup>i</sup>digged pits for me, which *are* not after thy law.

86 All thy commandments *are* faithful: they persecute me wrongfully; help thou me.

87 They had almost consumed me <sup>k</sup>upon earth: but I forsook not thy precepts.

88 Quicken me after thy loving-kindness; so shall I keep the testimony of thy mouth.

### LAMED.

89 For ever, O LORD, thy word is <sup>l</sup>settled in heaven.

90 Thy faithfulness *is* unto all generations; thou hast established the earth, and it abideth.

91 They continue this day <sup>m</sup>according to thine ordinances: for all *are* thy servants.

92 Unless thy law *had been* my delights, <sup>n</sup>I should then have perished in mine affliction.

93 I will never forget thy precepts: for with them thou hast quickened me.

94 I *am* thine, <sup>o</sup>save me: for I have sought thy precepts.

95 The wicked have <sup>p</sup>waited for me to destroy me: *but* I will consider thy testimonies.

96 I have seen an end of all <sup>q</sup>perfection; *but* thy commandment <sup>r</sup>*is* exceeding broad.

### MEM.

97 O how love I thy law! it *is* my meditation all the day.

98 Thou, through thy commandments, hast made me wiser <sup>s</sup>than mine enemies: for they *are* ever with me.

99 I have more understanding <sup>t</sup>than all my teachers: for thy testimonies *are* my meditation.

100 I understand more than the <sup>u</sup>ancients; because I keep thy precepts.

101 I have refrained my feet from <sup>x</sup>every evil way, that I may keep thy word.

102 I have not departed from thy judgments: for <sup>y</sup>thou hast taught me.

103 How <sup>z</sup>sweet are thy words unto my taste! *yea, sweeter* than honey to my mouth!

104 Through thy precepts I get understanding: therefore I hate every false way.

### NUN.

105 Thy word *is* <sup>a</sup>a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.

106 I have <sup>b</sup>sworn, and I will perform *it,* that I will keep thy righteous judgments.

107 I am afflicted very much: quicken me, O LORD, according unto thy word.

108 Accept, I beseech thee, <sup>c</sup>the free-will-offerings of my mouth, O LORD, and teach me thy judgments.

109 My soul *is* continually <sup>d</sup>in my hand: yet do I not forget thy law.

110 The wicked have <sup>e</sup>laid a snare for me: yet I erred not from thy precepts.
