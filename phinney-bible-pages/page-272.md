Hezekiah mourning, sendeth to Isaiah to pray for them.

8 So Rab-shakeh returned, and found the king of Assyria warring against Libnah: for he had heard that he was departed from <sup>p</sup>Lachish.

9 And when he heard say of Tirhakah king of Ethiopia, Behold, he is come out to fight <sup>q</sup>against thee; <sup>†</sup>he sent messengers again unto Hezekiah, saying,

11 Behold, thou hast heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all lands, by destroying them utterly; and shalt thou be delivered?
