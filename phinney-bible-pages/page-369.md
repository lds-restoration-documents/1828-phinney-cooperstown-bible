## PSALM XXVII.

David sustaineth his faith by the power of God.

*A Psalm of David.*

THE LORD *is* my <sup>a</sup>light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD *is* the strength of my life; of whom shall I be <sup>b</sup>afraid?

2 When the wicked, *even* mine enemies and my foes, came upon me to eat <sup>c</sup>up my flesh, they stumbled and fell.

3 Though a host should encamp against me, my heart shall not fear; though war should rise against me, in this *will* I *be* <sup>d</sup>confident.

4 One *thing* have I desired of the LORD, that will I <sup>e</sup>seek after; that I may dwell in the <sup>f</sup>house of the LORD all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the LORD, and to <sup>g</sup>inquire in his temple.

5 For in the time of trouble he shall <sup>h</sup>hide me in his pavilion: in the secret of his tabernacle shall he hide me: he <sup>i</sup>shall set me up upon a rock.

6 And now shall my head be lifted up above mine enemies round about me: <sup>k</sup>therefore will I offer in his tabernacle sacrifices of joy; I will sing, yea, I will sing praises unto the LORD.

7 Hear, O LORD, *when* I cry with my voice: have mercy also upon me, and answer me.

8 *When thou saidst,* <sup>l</sup>Seek ye my face; my heart said unto thee, Thy face, LORD, will I seek.

9 Hide not thy face *far* from me; put not thy servant away in anger: thou hast been my help; leave me not, neither forsake me, O God of my salvation.

10 When my father and my mother <sup>m</sup>forsake me, then the LORD <sup>†</sup>will take me up.

11 <sup>n</sup>Teach me thy way, O LORD, and lead me in a plain path, because of mine enemies.

12 Deliver me not over unto the will of mine enemies: for false witnesses are risen up against me, and such as <sup>o</sup>breathe out cruelty.

13 *I had fainted,* unless I had believed to see the goodness of the LORD in the <sup>p</sup>land of the living.

14 <sup>q</sup>Wait on the LORD; be of <sup>r</sup>good courage, and he shall strengthen thy heart: wait, I say, on the LORD.

UNTO thee will I <sup>a</sup>cry, O LORD, my <sup>b</sup>rock; be <sup>c</sup>not silent to me: <sup>d</sup>lest, *if* thou be silent to me, I become like them that go down into the pit.

5 Because they <sup>h</sup>regard not the works of the LORD, nor the operation of his hands, he shall destroy them, and not build them up.

6 Blessed *be* the LORD, because he hath <sup>i</sup>heard the voice of my supplications.

7 The LORD *is* <sup>k</sup>my strength and my shield: my heart trusted in him, and I am helped; therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth, and with my song will I praise him.

8 The LORD *is* their strength, and he *is* the saving strength of his anointed.

9 Save thy people, and bless <sup>l</sup>thine inheritance: <sup>m</sup>feed them also, and <sup>n</sup>lift them up for ever.
