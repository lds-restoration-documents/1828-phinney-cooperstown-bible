10 And the man that stood among the myrtle-trees answered and said, These *are they* whom the LORD hath <sup>n</sup>sent to walk to and fro through the earth.

11 And they answered the angel of the LORD that stood among the myrtle-trees, and said, We have <sup>o</sup>walked to and fro through the earth, and, behold, all the earth <sup>p</sup>sitteth still, and is at rest.

14 So the angel that communed with me said unto me, Cry thou, saying, Thus saith the LORD of hosts, I am <sup>u</sup>jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with a great jealousy.

15 And I am <sup>x</sup>very sore displeased with the heathen *that are* at ease: for I was but a little displeased, and they <sup>y</sup>helped forward the affliction.

16 Therefore thus saith the LORD, <sup>z</sup>I am returned to Jerusalem with mercies: <sup>a</sup>my house shall be built in it, saith the LORD of hosts, and a line shall be stretched forth upon Jerusalem.

17 Cry yet, saying, Thus saith the LORD of hosts, My cities through prosperity shall yet be <sup>b</sup>spread abroad; and the LORD shall yet comfort Zion, and shall yet choose Jerusalem.

18 ¶ Then lifted I up mine eyes, and saw, and, behold, <sup>c</sup>four horns.

19 And I said unto the angel that talked with me, What *be* these? And he answered me, These *are* the horns which have <sup>d</sup>scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.

20 And the LORD shewed me four <sup>e</sup>carpenters.

21 Then said I, What come these to do? And he spake, saying, These *are* the horns which have scattered Judah, so that <sup>f</sup>no man did lift up his head: but these are come to fray them, to cast out the horns of the Gentiles, which lifted up *their* <sup>g</sup>horn over the land of Judah to scatter it.

## CHAPTER II.

An angel sent to measure Jerusalem, and its flourishing state under God's protection foretold.

I LIFTED up mine eyes again, and looked, and, behold, a man with a <sup>a</sup>measuring-line in his hand.

2 Then said I, Whither goest thou? And he said unto me, To measure Jerusalem, to see what *is* the <sup>b</sup>breadth thereof, and what *is* the length thereof.

3 And, behold, the angel that talked with me went forth, and <sup>c</sup>another angel went out to meet him,

4 And said unto him, Run, speak to this young man, saying, Jerusalem shall be inhabited *as* <sup>d</sup>towns without walls for the multitude of men and cattle therein:

5 For I, saith the LORD, will be unto her <sup>e</sup>a wall of fire round about, and will be the glory in the midst of her.

6 ¶ Ho, ho, *come forth,* and <sup>f</sup>flee from the land of the north, saith the LORD: for I have <sup>g</sup>spread you abroad as the four winds of the heaven, saith the LORD.

7 <sup>h</sup>Deliver thyself, O Zion, that dwellest *with* the daughter of Babylon.

8 For thus saith the LORD of hosts, After the <sup>i</sup>glory hath he sent me unto the <sup>k</sup>nations which spoiled you; for he that <sup>l</sup>toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye.

9 For, behold, I will <sup>m</sup>shake my hand upon them, and they shall be <sup>n</sup>a spoil to their servants: and ye shall know that the LORD of hosts hath sent me.

10 <sup>o</sup>Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion: for, lo, I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee, saith the LORD.

11 And <sup>p</sup>many nations shall be joined to the LORD in that day, and shall be my people: and I will dwell in the midst of thee; and thou shalt know that the LORD of hosts hath sent me unto thee.

12 And the LORD shall <sup>q</sup>inherit Judah his portion in the holy land, and shall choose Jerusalem again.

13 <sup>r</sup>Be silent, O all flesh, before the LORD: for he is <sup>s</sup>raised up out of his holy habitation.

## CHAPTER III.

1 Under the type of Joshua, the restoration of the church promised....8 Christ foretold.

AND he shewed me Joshua the high priest standing before the <sup>a</sup>angel of the LORD, and <sup>b</sup>Satan standing at his right hand to resist him.

2 And the LORD said unto Satan, The LORD <sup>c</sup>rebuke thee, O Satan; even the LORD that hath chosen Jerusalem rebuke thee: *is* not this <sup>d</sup>a brand plucked out of the fire?

3 Now Joshua was clothed with <sup>e</sup>filthy garments, and stood before the angel.

4 And he answered and spake unto those that stood before him, saying, Take away the filthy garments from him. And unto him he said, Behold, I have <sup>f</sup>caused thine iniquity to pass from thee, and I will clothe thee with <sup>g</sup>change of raiment.

5 And I said, Let them set <sup>h</sup>a fair mitre upon his head. So they set a fair mitre upon his head, and clothed him with garments. And the angel of the LORD stood by.

6 And the angel of the LORD protested unto Joshua, saying,

7 Thus saith the LORD of hosts, If thou wilt <sup>i</sup>walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge, then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts; and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by.

8 Hear now, O Joshua the high priest, thou and thy fellows that sit before thee: for they *are* men <sup>k</sup>wondered at; for, behold, I will bring forth my servant, The <sup>l</sup>BRANCH.

9 For, behold, the <sup>m</sup>stone that I have laid before Joshua; upon one stone *shall be* <sup>n</sup>seven eyes: behold, I will <sup>o</sup>engrave the graving thereof, saith the LORD of hosts, and I will <sup>p</sup>remove the iniquity of that land in one day.

10 In that day, saith the LORD of hosts, shall ye call every man his neighbour <sup>q</sup>under the vine and under the fig-tree.
