10 And shewedst <sup>e</sup>signs and wonders upon Pharaoh, and on all his servants, and on all the people of his land; for thou knewest that they dealt <sup>f</sup>proudly against them: so didst thou get thee <sup>g</sup>a name, as *it is* this day.

11 And thou didst <sup>h</sup>divide the sea before them, so that they went through the midst of the sea on the dry land; and their persecutors thou threwest into the deeps, <sup>i</sup>as a stone into the mighty waters.

12 Moreover, thou <sup>k</sup>leddest them in the day by a cloudy pillar, and in the night by a pillar of fire, to give them light in the way wherein they should go.

13 Thou camest down also upon <sup>l</sup>mount Sinai, and spakest with them <sup>m</sup>from heaven, and gavest them <sup>n</sup>right judgments and true laws, good statutes and commandments:

14 And madest known unto them thy <sup>o</sup>holy sabbath, and commandedst them precepts, statutes, and laws, by the hand of Moses thy servant:

15 And gavest them <sup>p</sup>bread from heaven for their hunger, and broughtest forth water for them out of the <sup>q</sup>rock for their thirst, and promisedst them that they should <sup>r</sup>go in to possess the land which thou hadst sworn to give them.

16 But they and <sup>s</sup>our fathers dealt <sup>t</sup>proudly, and hardened their necks, and <sup>u</sup>hearkened not to thy commandments,

17 And refused to obey, neither were <sup>x</sup>mindful of the wonders that thou didst among them; but hardened their necks, and in their rebellion appointed <sup>y</sup>a captain to return to their bondage: but thou *art* a God ready to pardon, gracious and merciful, <sup>z</sup>slow to anger, and of great kindness, and <sup>a</sup>forsookest them not.

18 Yea, when they had made them <sup>b</sup>a molten calf, and said, This *is* thy god that brought thee up out of Egypt, and had wrought great provocations;

19 Yet thou, in thy <sup>c</sup>manifold mercies, forsookest them not in the wilderness: the <sup>d</sup>pillar of the cloud departed not from them by day, to lead them in the way; neither the pillar of fire by night, to shew them light, and the way wherein they should go.

20 Thou gavest also thy <sup>e</sup>good Spirit to instruct them, and withheldest not thy <sup>f</sup>manna from their mouth, and gavest them water for their thirst.

21 Yea, forty years didst thou sustain them in the wilderness, *so that* <sup>g</sup>they lacked nothing; their <sup>h</sup>clothes waxed not old, and their feet <sup>i</sup>swelled not.

22 Moreover, thou gavest them kingdoms and nations, and didst divide them into corners: so they possessed the land of Sihon, and the land of the king of <sup>k</sup>Heshbon, and the land of Og king of Bashan.

23 Their children also multipliedst thou as the <sup>l</sup>stars of heaven, and broughtest them into the land, concerning which thou hadst promised to their fathers, that they should go <sup>m</sup>in to possess *it.*

24 So the children went in and possessed the land, and thou <sup>n</sup>subduedst before them the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, and gavest them into their hands, with their kings, and the people of the land, that they might do with them as they would.

25 And they took strong cities, and <sup>o</sup>a fat land, and possessed <sup>p</sup>houses full of all goods, wells digged, vineyards, and olive-yards, and fruit-trees in abundance: so they did eat, and were filled, and became fat, and <sup>q</sup>delighted themselves in thy great goodness.

26 Nevertheless they were disobedient, and rebelled against thee, and cast thy law <sup>r</sup>behind their backs, and <sup>s</sup>slew thy prophets, which testified against them to turn them to thee; and they wrought great provocations.

27 Therefore <sup>t</sup>thou deliveredst them into the hand of their enemies, who vexed them: and in the <sup>u</sup>time of their trouble, when they cried unto thee, thou heardest *them* from heaven; and, according to thy manifold mercies, thou gavest them <sup>x</sup>saviours, who saved them out of the hand of their enemies.

28 But after they had <sup>y</sup>rest, they did evil again before thee: therefore leftest thou them in the hand of their enemies, so that they had the dominion over them: yet when they returned, and cried unto thee, thou <sup>z</sup>heardest *them* from heaven; and many times didst thou deliver them, according to thy mercies;

29 And <sup>a</sup>testifiedst against them, that thou mightest bring them again unto thy law: yet they dealt proudly, and hearkened not unto thy commandments, but sinned against thy judgments, (which if a man <sup>b</sup>do, he shall live in them,) and withdrew the shoulder, and hardened their neck, and would not hear:

30 Yet many years didst thou <sup>c</sup>forbear them, and testifiedst against them by thy Spirit in thy prophets; yet would they <sup>d</sup>not give ear: therefore <sup>e</sup>gavest thou them into the hand of the people of the lands.

31 Nevertheless, for thy <sup>f</sup>great mercies' sake, thou didst <sup>g</sup>not utterly consume them, nor forsake them; for thou *art* a gracious and merciful God.

32 Now therefore, our God, <sup>h</sup>the great, the mighty, and the terrible God, who keepest covenant and mercy, let not all the trouble seem <sup>i</sup>little before thee, that hath come upon us, on our kings, on our princes, and on our priests, and on our prophets, and on our fathers, and on all thy people, since the time of the kings <sup>k</sup>of Assyria unto this day.

33 Howbeit, thou *art* <sup>l</sup>just in all that is brought upon us; for thou hast done <sup>m</sup>right, but we have done wickedly:

34 Neither have <sup>n</sup>our kings, our princes, our priests, nor our fathers, kept thy law, nor hearkened unto thy commandments and thy testimonies, wherewith thou didst testify against them.

35 For they have not served thee in their kingdom, and <sup>o</sup>in thy great goodness that thou gavest them, and in the large and fat land which thou gavest before them, neither turned they from <sup>p</sup>their wicked works.

36 Behold, we *are* <sup>q</sup>servants this day; and *for* the land that thou gavest unto our fathers, <sup>r</sup>to eat the fruit thereof, and the good thereof, behold, we *are* servants in it:

37 And it yieldeth much increase unto the kings whom thou hast set over us because of our sins: also they have dominion over our bodies, and over our cattle, at their pleasure, and we *are* in great distress.

38 And <sup>s</sup>because of all this, we make a <sup>†</sup>sure *covenant,* and write *it:* and our princes, Levites, *and* priests, <sup>†</sup>seal *unto it.*

## CHAPTER X.

The names of them that sealed the covenant.

NOW <sup>a</sup>those that sealed *were,* Nehemiah the Tirshatha, the son of Hachaliah, and Zidkijah,

2 <sup>b</sup>Seraiah, Azariah, Jeremiah,

3 Pashur, Amariah, Malchijah,

4 <sup>c</sup>Hattush, Shebaniah, Malluch,

5 Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah,

6 <sup>d</sup>Daniel, Ginnethon, Baruch,

7 Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin,

8 <sup>e</sup>Maaziah, Bilgai, Shemaiah: these *were* the priests.
