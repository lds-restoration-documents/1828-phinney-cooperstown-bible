## PSALM VIII.

God's glory magnified by his works, and by his love to man.

*A Psalm of David.*

O LORD our Lord, how excellent *is* <sup>a</sup>thy name in <sup>b</sup>all the earth! who hast set thy glory <sup>c</sup>above the heavens.

2 Out of the <sup>d</sup>mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength, because of thine enemies; that thou mightest still the enemy and the <sup>e</sup>avenger.

3 When I <sup>f</sup>consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers; the moon and the stars, which thou hast ordained;

4 What *is* <sup>g</sup>man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of man, that thou visitest him?

5 For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels, and hast <sup>h</sup>crowned him with glory and honour.

6 Thou madest him to have <sup>i</sup>dominion over the works of thy hands: thou hast <sup>k</sup>put all *things* under his feet:

9 O LORD our Lord, how excellent *is* thy name in all the earth!

## PSALM X.

WHY standest thou afar off, O LORD? *why* <sup>a</sup>hidest thou *thyself* in times of trouble?

3 For the wicked <sup>d</sup>boasteth of his heart's desire, and blesseth the covetous, *whom* the LORD abhorreth.

4 The wicked, through the pride of his countenance, will not seek *after God:* God *is* not <sup>e</sup>in all his thoughts.

5 His ways are always grievous; thy judgments *are* far above <sup>f</sup>out of his sight: *as for* all his enemies, he <sup>g</sup>puffeth at them.

6 He hath said in his heart, I shall <sup>h</sup>not be moved: for *I shall* never be in adversity.

7 His mouth is full of cursing and deceit and fraud; <sup>i</sup>under his tongue *is* mischief and vanity.

8 He sitteth in the <sup>k</sup>lurking-places of the villages; in the secret places doth he murder the innocent: his eyes are privily set against the poor.

9 He lieth in wait <sup>l</sup>secretly, as a lion in his den: he lieth in wait to catch the poor: he doth catch the poor, when he draweth him into his net.

10 He <sup>m</sup>croucheth, *and* humbleth himself, that the poor may fall by his strong ones.

11 He hath said in his heart, God hath forgotten: he hideth his face; he will <sup>n</sup>never see *it.*

12 Arise, O LORD; O God, <sup>o</sup>lift up thy hand: forget not the humble.

13 <sup>p</sup>Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God? he hath said in his heart, Thou wilt not require *it.*

14 Thou hast seen *it;* for thou beholdest mischief and spite, to requite *it* with thy hand: the poor <sup>q</sup>committeth himself unto thee; thou art the helper of the <sup>r</sup>fatherless.

15 Break thou the <sup>s</sup>arm of the wicked and the evil *man:* seek out his wickedness *till* thou find none.
