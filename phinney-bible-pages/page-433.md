27 None shall be <sup>p</sup>weary nor stumble among them; none shall slumber nor sleep; neither shall the girdle of their loins be loosed, nor the latchet of their shoes be broken:

28 Whose <sup>q</sup>arrows *are* sharp, and all their bows bent, their horses' hoofs shall be counted like flint, and their wheels like a whirlwind:

29 Their <sup>r</sup>roaring *shall be* like a lion, they shall roar like young lions; yea, they shall roar, and lay hold of the prey, and shall carry *it* away safe, and none shall deliver *it.*

30 And in that day they shall roar against them like the roaring of the <sup>s</sup>sea; and if *one* look unto the land, behold <sup>t</sup>darkness *and* sorrow; and the light is darkened in the <sup>u</sup>heavens thereof.

## CHAPTER VI.

Isaiah's vision of the Lord's glory.

IN the year that king Uzziah <sup>a</sup>died I saw also the LORD <sup>b</sup>sitting upon a throne, high and lifted up, and his train filled the temple.

2 Above it stood the <sup>c</sup>seraphims: each one had six wings; with twain he covered his <sup>d</sup>face, and with twain he covered his feet, and with twain he did fly.

3 And one cried unto another, and said, <sup>e</sup>Holy, holy, holy, *is* the LORD of hosts; the whole <sup>f</sup>earth *is* full of his glory.

4 And the <sup>g</sup>posts of the door moved at the voice of him that cried, and the house was <sup>h</sup>filled with smoke.

5 Then said I, <sup>i</sup>Wo *is* me! for I am undone; because I *am* a man of <sup>k</sup>unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips: for mine eyes have seen the King, the LORD of hosts.

6 Then <sup>l</sup>flew one of the seraphims unto me, having a live coal in his hand, *which* he had taken with the tongs from off the <sup>m</sup>altar;

7 And he laid *it* upon my mouth, and said, Lo, this hath touched thy lips, and thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin purged.

8 Also I heard the <sup>n</sup>voice of the LORD, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go <sup>o</sup>for us? Then said I, Here *am* I; <sup>p</sup>send me.

9 And he said, <sup>q</sup>Go, and tell this people, <sup>r</sup>Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not.

10 <sup>s</sup>Make the heart of this people fat, and make their ears heavy, and shut their eyes; <sup>t</sup>lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and convert, and be healed.

11 Then said I, LORD, how long? And he answered, Until the <sup>u</sup>cities be wasted without inhabitant, and the houses without man, and the land be <sup>x</sup>utterly desolate;

12 And the LORD have removed men far away, and *there be* <sup>y</sup>a great forsaking in the midst of the land.

13 But yet in it *shall be* a <sup>z</sup>tenth, and *it* shall return, and shall be eaten: as a teil-tree, and as an oak, whose substance *is* in them when they cast *their leaves, so* the <sup>a</sup>holy seed *shall be* the substance thereof.
