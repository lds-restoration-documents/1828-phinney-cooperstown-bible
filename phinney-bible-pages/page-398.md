## PSALM CXV.

God is to be praised for his blessings.

NOT unto us, O LORD, <sup>a</sup>not unto us, but unto thy name give glory, for thy mercy, *and* for thy truth's sake.

2 Wherefore should the heathen say, <sup>b</sup>Where *is* now their God?

3 But our God <sup>c</sup>*is* in the heavens; he <sup>d</sup>hath done whatsoever he pleased.

4 Their <sup>e</sup>idols *are* silver and gold, the <sup>f</sup>work of men's hands.

5 They have mouths, but they <sup>g</sup>speak not; eyes have they, but they see not;

6 They have ears, but they hear not; noses have they, but they smell not;

7 They have hands, but they handle not; feet have they, but they <sup>h</sup>walk not; neither speak they through their throat.

8 They that <sup>i</sup>make them are like unto them; *so is* every one that trusteth in them.

9 <sup>k</sup>O Israel, trust thou in the LORD; he *is* their help and their shield.

10 O house of <sup>l</sup>Aaron, trust in the LORD; he *is* their help and their shield.

11 Ye that <sup>m</sup>fear the LORD, trust in the LORD; he *is* their help and their shield.

12 The LORD hath been mindful of us; he will bless *us:* he will <sup>n</sup>bless the house of Israel, he will bless the house of Aaron.

13 He will bless them that fear the LORD, *both* <sup>o</sup>small and great.

14 The LORD shall increase you more and more, you and your children.

15 You *are* <sup>p</sup>blessed of the LORD, which made heaven and earth.

16 The <sup>q</sup>heaven, *even* the heavens, *are* the LORD's: but the earth hath he given to the children of men.

17 <sup>r</sup>The dead praise not the LORD, neither any that go down into silence.

18 But <sup>s</sup>we will bless the LORD from this time forth and for evermore. Praise the LORD.

## PSALM CXVI.

The Psalmist professeth his love and duty to God for his deliverance.

I <sup>a</sup>LOVE the LORD, because he hath heard my voice *and* my supplications.

2 Because he hath inclined his ear unto me, therefore will I call upon *him* <sup>b</sup>as long as I live.

3 The <sup>c</sup>sorrows of death compassed me, and the pains of hell gat hold upon me: I found trouble and sorrow.

4 Then called I upon <sup>d</sup>the name of the LORD: O LORD, <sup>e</sup>I beseech thee, deliver my soul.

5 Gracious *is* the LORD, and <sup>f</sup>righteous; yea, our God *is* merciful.

6 The LORD <sup>g</sup>preserveth the simple: I was brought low, and he helped me.

7 Return unto thy <sup>h</sup>rest, O my soul; for the LORD hath dealt bountifully with thee.

8 For thou hast delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, *and* my feet from <sup>i</sup>falling.

9 <sup>k</sup>I will walk before the LORD in the land of the living.

10 <sup>l</sup>I believed, therefore have I spoken: I was greatly afflicted.

11 I said in <sup>m</sup>my haste, <sup>n</sup>All men *are* liars.

12 What shall I render unto the LORD *for* all his benefits towards me?

13 I will take the <sup>o</sup>cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the LORD.

14 I will pay my vows unto the LORD now in the presence of all his people.

15 Precious in the sight of the LORD *is* the <sup>p</sup>death of his saints.

16 O LORD, truly, I *am* thy servant; I *am* thy servant *and* the son of thy <sup>q</sup>handmaid: thou hast loosed my <sup>r</sup>bonds.

17 I will offer to thee the <sup>s</sup>sacrifice of thanksgiving, and will call upon the name of the LORD.

18 I will pay <sup>t</sup>my vows unto the LORD now in the <sup>u</sup>presence of all his people,

19 In the <sup>x</sup>courts of the LORD's house, in the midst of thee, O Jerusalem. Praise ye the LORD.

## PSALM CXVII.

An exhortation to praise God for his mercy and truth.

O PRAISE the LORD, <sup>a</sup>all ye nations: praise him, all ye people.

2 For his merciful kindness is great toward us: and the <sup>b</sup>truth of the LORD *endureth* for ever. Praise ye the LORD.

## PSALM CXVIII.

The coming of Christ in his kingdom is expressed.

O <sup>a</sup>GIVE thanks unto the LORD; for *he is* good; because his mercy *endureth* for ever.

2 Let <sup>b</sup>Israel now say, that his mercy *endureth* for ever.

3 Let the <sup>c</sup>house of Aaron now say, that his mercy *endureth* for ever.

4 Let them now that <sup>d</sup>fear the LORD say, that his mercy *endureth* for ever.

5 I called upon the LORD <sup>e</sup>in distress: the LORD answered me, *and set me* in <sup>f</sup>a large place.

6 The LORD *is* on my side; I <sup>g</sup>will not fear: what can man do unto me?

7 The LORD taketh my part with them that help me: therefore shall I <sup>h</sup>see *my desire* upon them that hate me.

8 *It is* <sup>i</sup>better to trust in the LORD, than to put confidence <sup>k</sup>in man:

9 *It is* better to trust in the LORD, than to put confidence in princes.

10 <sup>l</sup>All nations compassed me about: but in <sup>m</sup>the name of the LORD will I destroy them.

11 They <sup>n</sup>compassed me about; yea, they compassed me about: but in the name of the LORD I will destroy them.

12 They compassed me about like <sup>o</sup>bees; they are quenched <sup>p</sup>as the fire of thorns: for in the name of the LORD I will destroy them.

13 <sup>q</sup>Thou hast thrust sore at me, that I might fall: but the LORD helped me.

14 The LORD *is* <sup>r</sup>my strength and song, and is become my salvation.
