## CHAPTER XXIII.

1 Joshua's exhortation before his death, 3 by former benefits, 5 by promises, 11 and by threatenings.

AND it came to pass, <sup>†</sup>a long time after that <sup>a</sup>the LORD had given rest unto Israel from all their enemies round about, that Joshua waxed old *and* stricken in age.

2 And Joshua <sup>b</sup>called for all Israel, *and* for their elders, and for their heads, and for their judges, and for their officers, and said unto them, I am old *and* stricken in age:

3 And ye have <sup>c</sup>seen all that <sup>d</sup>the LORD your God hath done unto all these nations because of you: for the LORD your God *is* he that hath fought for you.

4 Behold, I have divided unto you by <sup>e</sup>lot these nations that remain, to be an inheritance for your tribes, from Jordan, with all the nations that I have cut off, even unto the great sea westward.

5 And the LORD your God, <sup>f</sup>he shall expel them from before you, and drive them from out of your sight: and ye shall possess their land, as the LORD your God hath promised unto you.

6 Be ye therefore <sup>g</sup>very courageous, to keep and to do all that is written in the book of the law of Moses, that ye turn not aside therefrom *to* the <sup>h</sup>right hand or *to* the left;

7 That ye <sup>i</sup>come not among these nations, these that remain among you; neither <sup>k</sup>make mention of the name of their gods, nor cause to <sup>l</sup>swear *by them,* neither serve them, nor bow yourselves unto them:

8 But <sup>m</sup>cleave unto the LORD your God, as ye have done unto this day.

9 For the LORD hath driven out from before you great nations and strong: but *as for* you, no man hath been able to stand before you unto this day.

10 <sup>n</sup>One man of you shall chase a thousand: for the LORD your God, he *it is* that fighteth for you, as he hath promised you.

11 Take good heed therefore unto yourselves, that ye love the LORD your God.

12 Else if ye do in any wise <sup>o</sup>go back, and cleave unto the remnant of these nations, *even* these that remain among you, and shall make marriages with them, and go in unto them, and they to you:

13 Know for a certainty that the LORD your God will no more drive out *any of* these nations from before you; but they shall be <sup>†</sup>snares and traps unto you, and scourges in your sides, and thorns in your eyes, until ye perish from off this good land, which the LORD your God hath given you.

14 And, behold, this day I *am* <sup>p</sup>going the way of all the earth; and ye know in all your hearts, and in all your souls, that <sup>q</sup>not one thing hath failed of all the good things which the LORD your God spake concerning you; all are come to pass unto you, *and* not one thing hath failed thereof.

15 Therefore it shall come to pass, *that* as <sup>r</sup>all good things are come upon you, which the LORD your God promised you; so shall the LORD bring upon you <sup>s</sup>all evil things, until he have destroyed you from off this good land which the LORD your God hath given you.

16 When ye have transgressed the covenant of the LORD your God, which he commanded you, and have gone and served other gods, and bowed yourselves to them; then shall the anger of the LORD be kindled against you, and ye shall perish quickly from off the good land which he hath given unto you.

1 Joshua assembleth the tribes at Shechem....14 He reneweth a covenant between them and God.

AND Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to <sup>a</sup>Shechem, and called for the elders of Israel, and for their heads, and for their judges, and for their officers; and they presented themselves before God.

2 And Joshua said unto <sup>b</sup>all the people, Thus saith the LORD God of Israel, Your fathers dwelt on the other side of the <sup>c</sup>flood in old time, *even* <sup>d</sup>Terah the father of Abraham, and the father of Nachor: and they <sup>e</sup>served other gods.

3 And <sup>f</sup>I took your father Abraham from the other side of the flood, and led him throughout all the land of Canaan, and <sup>g</sup>multiplied his seed, and <sup>h</sup>gave him Isaac.

4 And I gave unto Isaac, <sup>i</sup>Jacob and Esau; and <sup>k</sup>I gave unto Esau mount Seir, to possess it: but <sup>l</sup>Jacob and his children went down into Egypt.

5 <sup>m</sup>I sent Moses also and Aaron, and I plagued Egypt, according to that which I did among them: and afterward I brought you out.

6 And I <sup>n</sup>brought your fathers out of Egypt: and you came unto the sea; and the Egyptians pursued after your fathers with chariots and horsemen unto the <sup>o</sup>Red Sea.

7 And when they cried unto the LORD, he <sup>p</sup>put darkness between you and the Egyptians, and brought the sea upon them, and covered them: and your eyes have seen what I have done in Egypt: and ye dwelt in the wilderness <sup>q</sup>a long season.

8 And I brought you into the land of the Amorites, which dwelt on the other side Jordan; and they fought <sup>r</sup>with you: and I gave them into your hand, that ye might possess their land; and I destroyed them from before you.
