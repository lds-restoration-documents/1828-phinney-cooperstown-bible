27 <sup>y</sup>For as many of you as have been baptized into Christ, have put on Christ.

28 <sup>z</sup>There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus.

29 <sup>a</sup>And if ye *be* Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise.

## CHAPTER IV.

1 The Jews were for a while under the law, 4 but Christ came to redeem, and to give both to Jews and Gentiles the adoption of sons....11 Paul's tender regard for them.

<sup>a</sup>NOW I say, *That* the heir, as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all;

2 But is under tutors and governors, until the time appointed of the father.

3 <sup>b</sup>Even so we, when we were children, were in bondage under the elements of the world:

4 <sup>c</sup>But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law,

5 <sup>d</sup>To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons.

6 <sup>e</sup>And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father.

7 <sup>f</sup>Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.

8 <sup>g</sup>Howbeit, then when ye knew not God, ye did service unto them which by nature are no gods.

9 <sup>h</sup>But now, after that ye have known God, or rather are known of God, how turn ye again to the weak and beggarly elements, whereunto ye desire again to be in bondage?

10 <sup>i</sup>Ye observe days, and months, and times, and years.

11 <sup>k</sup>I am afraid of you, lest I have bestowed upon you labour in vain.

12 <sup>l</sup>Brethren, I beseech you, be as I *am;* for I *am* as ye *are:* ye have not injured me at all.

13 <sup>m</sup>Ye know how through infirmity of the flesh I preached the gospel unto you at the first:

14 <sup>n</sup>And my temptation which was in my flesh ye despised not, nor rejected: but received me as an angel of God, *even* as Christ Jesus.

15 Where is then the blessedness ye spake of? for I bear you record, that, if *it had been* possible, ye would have plucked out your own eyes, and have given them to me.

16 <sup>o</sup>Am I therefore become your enemy, because I tell you the truth?

17 <sup>p</sup>They zealously affect you, *but* not well; yea, they would exclude you, that ye might affect them.

18 But *it is* good to be zealously affected always in *a* good *thing,* and not only when I am present with you.

19 <sup>q</sup>My little children, of whom I travail in birth again until Christ be formed in you,

20 I desire to be present with you now, and to change my voice; for I stand in doubt of you.

21 Tell me, ye that desire to be under the law, do ye not hear the law?

22 <sup>r</sup>For it is written, That Abraham had two sons; the one by a bond-maid, the other by a free-woman.

23 <sup>s</sup>But he *who was* of the bond-woman was born after the flesh; but he of the free-woman *was* by promise.

24 <sup>t</sup>Which things are an allegory: for these are the two covenants; the one from the mount Sinai, which gendereth to bondage, which is Agar.

25 For this Agar is mount Sinai in Arabia, and answereth to Jerusalem which now is, and is in bondage with her children.

26 <sup>u</sup>But Jerusalem which is above is free, which is the mother of us all.

27 <sup>x</sup>For it is written, Rejoice, *thou* barren that bearest not; break forth and cry, thou that travailest not; for the desolate hath many more children than she which hath a husband.

28 <sup>y</sup>Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are the children of promise.

29 <sup>z</sup>But as then he that was born after the flesh, persecuted him *that was born* after the Spirit, even so *it is* now.

30 <sup>a</sup>Nevertheless what saith the scripture? Cast out the bond-woman and her son: for the son of the bond-woman shall not be heir with the son of the free-woman.

31 <sup>b</sup>So then, brethren, we are not children of the bond-woman, but of the free.

## CHAPTER V.

1 He moveth them to maintain their Christian liberty, 3 and not to observe circumcision, 13 but rather love....19 The works of the flesh; 22 the fruits of the Spirit.

<sup>a</sup>STAND fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.

2 <sup>b</sup>Behold, I Paul say unto you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing.

3 <sup>c</sup>For I testify again to every man that is circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law.

4 <sup>d</sup>Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law: ye are fallen from grace.

5 <sup>e</sup>For we through the Spirit wait for the hope of righteousness by faith.

6 <sup>f</sup>For in Jesus Christ neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision; but faith which worketh by love.

7 <sup>g</sup>Ye did run well; who did hinder you, that ye should not obey the truth?

8 <sup>h</sup>This persuasion *cometh* not of him that calleth you.

9 <sup>i</sup>A little leaven leaveneth the whole lump.

10 <sup>k</sup>I have confidence in you through the Lord, that ye will be none otherwise minded: but he that troubleth you shall bear *his* judgment, whosoever he be.

11 <sup>l</sup>And I, brethren, if I yet preach circumcision, why do I yet suffer persecution? then is the offence of the cross ceased.

12 <sup>m</sup>I would they were even cut off which trouble you.

13 <sup>n</sup>For, brethren, ye have been called unto liberty; only *use* not liberty for an occasion to the flesh, but by love serve one another.

14 <sup>o</sup>For all the law is fulfilled in one word, *even* in this, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

15 <sup>p</sup>But if ye bite and devour one another, take heed that ye be not consumed one of another.

16 <sup>q</sup>*This* I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.

17 <sup>r</sup>For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other; so that ye cannot do the things that ye would.

18 <sup>s</sup>But if ye be led by the Spirit, ye are not under the law.

19 <sup>t</sup>Now the works of the flesh are manifest;
