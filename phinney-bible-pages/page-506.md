# ¶ The Book of the Prophet EZEKIEL.

## CHAPTER I.

1 The time of Ezekiel's prophecy at Chebar; 4 his vision.

NOW it came to pass in the thirtieth year, in the fourth *month*, in the fifth *day* of the month, as I *was* among the captives by the river of Chebar, *that* the <sup>a</sup>heavens were opened, and I saw <sup>b</sup>visions of God.

2 In the fifth *day* of the month, (which *was* the fifth year of king Jehoiachin's captivity,)

3 The word of the LORD <sup>c</sup>came expressly unto Ezekiel the priest, the son of Buzi, in the land of the <sup>d</sup>Chaldeans, by the river Chebar; and the <sup>e</sup>hand of the LORD was there upon him.

4 And I <sup>f</sup>looked, and, behold, <sup>g</sup>a whirlwind came out of <sup>h</sup>the north, a great cloud, and a fire infolding itself, and a <sup>i</sup>brightness *was* about it, and out of the midst thereof, as the colour of amber, out of the midst of the fire:

5 Also out of the <sup>k</sup>midst thereof *came* the likeness of <sup>l</sup>four living creatures. And this *was* their appearance; they had the likeness of a man.

6 And every one had four faces, and every one had four <sup>m</sup>wings.

7 And their feet *were* <sup>n</sup>straight feet; and the sole of their feet *was* like the sole of a <sup>o</sup>calf's foot; and they sparkled like the colour of <sup>p</sup>burnished brass.

8 And *they had* the <sup>q</sup>hands of a man <sup>r</sup>under their wings, on their four sides; and they four had their faces and their wings.

9 Their wings *were* <sup>s</sup>joined one to another; they <sup>t</sup>turned not when they went; they went every one <sup>u</sup>straight forward.

10 As for the likeness of their faces, they four had the face of <sup>x</sup>a man, and the face of <sup>y</sup>a lion on the right side; and they four had the face of an <sup>z</sup>ox on the left side; they four also had the face of <sup>a</sup>an eagle.

11 Thus *were* their faces: and their wings *were* stretched upward; two *wings* of every one *were* joined one to another, and two covered their bodies.

12 And they went every one straight forward: whither the <sup>b</sup>spirit was to go, they went; *and* they turned not when they went.

13 As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance *was* like <sup>c</sup>burning coals of fire, *and* like the appearance of lamps: <sup>d</sup>it went up and down among the living creatures; and the fire was bright, and out of the fire went forth lightning.

14 And the living creatures <sup>e</sup>ran and <sup>f</sup>returned as the appearance of a flash of lightning.

15 Now, as I beheld the living creatures, behold, <sup>g</sup>one wheel upon the <sup>h</sup>earth by the living creatures, with his four faces.

16 The appearance <sup>i</sup>of the wheels and their work *was* like unto the colour of a beryl; and they four had one likeness: and their appearance and their work *was* as it were a wheel in the middle of a wheel.

17 When they went, they went upon their four sides; *and* they turned not when they went.

18 As for their rings, they were so <sup>k</sup>high, that they were dreadful; and their rings *were* <sup>l</sup>full of eyes round about them four.

19 And when the living creatures went, the wheels went <sup>m</sup>by them; and when the living creatures were lifted up from the earth, the <sup>n</sup>wheels were lifted up.

20 Whithersoever the spirit was to go, they went, thither *was their* spirit to go; and the wheels were lifted up over against them: for the spirit of the living creature <sup>o</sup>*was* in the wheels.

21 When those went, *these* went; and when those stood, *these* stood; and when those were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up over against them: for <sup>p</sup>the spirit of the living creature *was* in the wheels.

22 And the likeness of the <sup>q</sup>firmament upon the heads of the living creature *was* as the colour of <sup>r</sup>the terrible crystal, stretched forth over their heads above.

23 And under the firmament *were* their wings straight, the one toward the other: every one had two which <sup>s</sup>covered on this side, and every one had two, which covered on that side, their bodies.

24 And when they went, I heard the noise of their wings, like the noise of <sup>t</sup>great waters, as the voice of <sup>u</sup>the Almighty, the voice of speech, as the noise of a host: when they stood, they let down their wings.

25 And there was <sup>x</sup>a voice from the firmament that *was* over their heads, when they <sup>y</sup>stood, *and* had let down their wings.

26 And above the firmament that *was* over their heads *was* the likeness of a <sup>z</sup>throne, as the appearance of a sapphire-stone: and upon the likeness of the throne *was* the likeness as the appearance of a man above <sup>a</sup>upon it.

27 And I saw as the colour of amber, as the appearance of <sup>b</sup>fire round about within it; from the appearance of his loins even upward, and from the appearance of his loins even downward, I saw as it were the appearance of fire, and it had <sup>c</sup>brightness round about.

28 As the appearance of the <sup>d</sup>bow that is in the cloud in the day of <sup>e</sup>rain, so *was* the appearance of the brightness round about. This *was* the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD. And when I saw *it,* <sup>f</sup>I fell upon my face, and I <sup>g</sup>heard a voice of one that spake.
