## CHAPTER IV.

The posterity of Jabez, and his prayer.

THE sons of Judah; <sup>a</sup>Pharez, Hezron, and Carmi, and Hur, and Shobal.

2 And Reaiah the son of Shobal begat Jahath; and Jahath begat Ahumai and Lahad. These *are* the families of the <sup>b</sup>Zorathites.

3 And these *were of* the father of <sup>c</sup>Etam; Jezreel, and Ishma, and Idbash: and the name of their sister *was* Hazelelponi:

4 And Penuel the father of <sup>d</sup>Gedor, and Ezer the <sup>e</sup>father of Hushah. These *are* the sons of Hur, the first-born of Ephratah, the father of Beth-lehem.

5 ¶ And <sup>f</sup>Ashur the father of Tekoa had <sup>g</sup>two wives, Helah and Naarah.

6 And Naarah bare him Ahuzam, and Hepher, and Temeni, and Haahashtari. These *were* the sons of Naarah.

7 And the sons of Helah *were*, Zereth, and Jezoar, and Ethnan.

8 And Coz begat Anub, and Zobebah, and the families of Aharhel the son of Harum.

9 ¶ And Jabez was <sup>h</sup>more honourable than his brethren: and his mother called his name Jabez, saying, Because <sup>i</sup>I bare him with sorrow.

10 And Jabez <sup>k</sup>called on the God of Israel, saying, Oh that thou wouldest bless me indeed, and <sup>l</sup>enlarge my coast, and that thine hand might be with me, and that thou wouldest keep *me* from evil, that it may not grieve me! And <sup>m</sup>God granted him that which he requested.

11 ¶ And Chelub the brother of Shuah begat Mehir, which *was* the father of Eshton.

12 And Eshton begat Beth-rapha, and Paseah, and Tehinnah the father of Irnahash. These *are* the men of Rechah.

13 And the sons of <sup>n</sup>Kenaz; Othniel, and Seraiah: and the sons of <sup>o</sup>Othniel; Hathath.

14 And Meonothai begat Ophrah: and Seraiah begat Joab, the father of the valley of <sup>||</sup>Charashim: for they were craftsmen.

15 And the sons of <sup>p</sup>Caleb the son of Jephunneh; Iru, Elah, and Naam: and the sons of Elah, even Kenaz.

16 And the sons of Jehaleleel; <sup>q</sup>Ziph, and Ziphah, Tiria, and Asareel.

17 And the sons of Ezra *were* <sup>r</sup>Jether and Mered, and Epher, and Jalon: and she bare Miriam, and Shammai, and Ishbah the father of Eshtemoa.

19 And the sons of *his* wife Hodiah the sister of Naham, the father of <sup>u</sup>Keilah the Garmite, and Eshtemoa the <sup>x</sup>Maachathite.

20 And the sons of Shimon *were*, Amnon, and Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. And the sons of Ishi *were*, Zoheth, and Ben-zoheth.

21 ¶ The sons of <sup>y</sup>Shelah the son of Judah *were*, Er the father of Lecah, and Laadah the father of <sup>z</sup>Mareshah, and the families of the house of them that wrought fine linen, of the house of Ashbea,

22 And Jokim, and the men of Chozeba, and Joash, and Saraph, who had the dominion in Moab, and Jashubi-lehem. And *these are* ancient things.

23 These *were* the potters, and those that dwelt among <sup>a</sup>plants and hedges: there they dwelt with the king for his work.

24 ¶ The sons of Simeon *were*, <sup>||</sup>Nemuel, and Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, *and* <sup>b</sup>Shaul:

25 Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.

26 And the sons of Mishma; Hamuel his son, Zacchur his son, Shimei his son.

27 And Shimei had <sup>c</sup>sixteen sons and six daughters; but his brethren had not many children, neither did all their family multiply, like to the children of Judah.

28 And they dwelt at <sup>d</sup>Beer-sheba, and Moladah, and Hazar-shual,

29 And at Bilhah, and at Ezem, and at Tolad,
