## CHAPTER XLVIII.

1 Joseph with his sons visiteth his sick father....2 Jacob strengtheneth himself to bless them....3 He repeateth the promise....21 He prophesieth their return to Canaan.

AND it came to pass, after these things, that *one* told Joseph, Behold, thy father *is* sick: and he took with him his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim.

2 And *one* told Jacob, and said, Behold, thy son Joseph cometh unto thee: And Israel strengthened himself, and sat upon the bed.

3 And Jacob said unto Joseph, <sup>a</sup>God Almighty appeared unto me at <sup>b</sup>Luz in the land of Canaan, and blessed me,

4 And said unto me, Behold, <sup>c</sup>I will make thee fruitful, and multiply thee, and I will make of thee a multitude of people; and will give this land to thy seed after thee, *for* <sup>d</sup>an everlasting possession.

5 And now thy two sons, Ephraim and Manasseh, which were born unto thee in the land of Egypt, before I came unto thee into Egypt, <sup>e</sup>*are* mine: <sup>f</sup>as Reuben and Simeon, they shall be mine.

6 And thy issue, which thou begettest after them, shall be thine, *and* shall be called <sup>g</sup>after the name of their brethren in their inheritance.

7 And as for me, when I came from <sup>h</sup>Padan, <sup>i</sup>Rachel died by me in the land of Canaan, in the way, when yet *there was* but a little way to come unto Ephrath: and I buried her there in the way of Ephrath; the same *is* Beth-lehem.

8 ¶ And Israel beheld Joseph's sons, and said, Who *are* these?

9 And Joseph said unto his father, They *are* my sons, <sup>k</sup>whom God hath given me in this *place.* And he said, Bring them, I pray thee, unto me, and I will <sup>l</sup>bless them.

10 (Now the eyes of Israel were <sup>†</sup>dim for age, *so that* he could not see.) And he brought them near unto him; and he kissed them, and embraced them.

11 And Israel said unto Joseph, I had not thought to see thy face; and, lo, God hath shewed me also thy seed.

12 And Joseph brought them out from between his knees, and he bowed himself with his face to the earth.

13 And Joseph took them both, Ephraim in his right hand toward Israel's left hand, and Manasseh in his left hand toward Israel's right hand, and brought *them* near unto him.

14 And Israel stretched out <sup>m</sup>his right hand, and laid *it* upon Ephraim's head, who *was* the younger, and his left hand upon Manasseh's head, <sup>†</sup>guiding his hands wittingly; for Manasseh *was* the first-born.

15 And he blessed <sup>n</sup>Joseph, and said, God, before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac <sup>o</sup>did walk, the God which <sup>p</sup>fed me all my life long unto this day,

16 The <sup>q</sup>angel which redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads; and let my name be named on them, and the name of my fathers Abraham and Isaac; and let them <sup>†</sup>grow into a multitude in the midst of the earth.

17 And when Joseph saw that his father laid his right hand upon the head of Ephraim, it <sup>r</sup>displeased him: and he held up his father's hand, to remove it from Ephraim's head unto Manasseh's head.

18 And Joseph said unto his father, Not so, my father: for this *is* the first-born; put thy right hand upon his head.

19 And his father refused, and said, I know *it* my son, I know *it:* he also shall become a people, and he also shall be great; but truly his younger brother shall be <sup>s</sup>greater than he, and his seed shall become a multitude of nations.

20 And he blessed them that day, saying, <sup>t</sup>In thee shall Israel bless, saying, God make thee as Ephraim and as Manasseh. And he set Ephraim before Manasseh.

21 And Israel said unto Joseph, Behold, I die; but God shall be with you, and bring you again unto the land of your fathers.

22 Moreover, <sup>u</sup>I have given to thee one portion above thy brethren, which I took out of the hand of the Amorite with <sup>x</sup>my sword and with my bow.

## CHAPTER XLIX.

1 Jacob calleth his sons to bless them....3 Their blessings in particular....29 He chargeth them about his burial....33 He dieth.

AND Jacob called unto his sons, and said, <sup>a</sup>Gather yourselves together, that I may <sup>b</sup>tell you *that* which shall befall you in the <sup>c</sup>last days.

2 Gather yourselves together, and hear, ye sons of Jacob; and hearken <sup>d</sup>unto Israel your father.

3 ¶ Reuben, thou *art* my <sup>e</sup>first-born, my might and the beginning of my strength, the excellency of dignity, and the excellency of power:

4 Unstable as water, <sup>†</sup>thou shalt not excel; because thou <sup>f</sup>wentest up to thy father's bed, then defiledst thou *it:* he went up to my couch.

5 ¶ Simeon and Levi *are* <sup>g</sup>brethren; <sup>h</sup>instruments of cruelty *are in* their habitations.

6 O my soul, come not thou into their <sup>i</sup>secret; unto their assembly, mine <sup>k</sup>honour, be not thou united: for in their anger they slew a man, and in their self-will they digged down a wall.

7 Cursed *be* their <sup>l</sup>anger, for *it was* fierce; and their wrath, for it was cruel: I will <sup>m</sup>divide them in Jacob, and <sup>n</sup>scatter them in Israel.

8 ¶ Judah, thou *art he* whom thy brethren shall praise; thy hand *shall be* in the <sup>o</sup>neck of thine enemies: thy father's children shall bow down before thee.

9 Judah *is* <sup>p</sup>a lion's whelp; from the prey, my son, thou art gone up: he stooped down, he <sup>q</sup>couched as a lion, and as an old lion; who shall rouse him up?

10 The sceptre shall not depart from Judah, nor <sup>r</sup>a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come; and unto him *shall* the <sup>s</sup>gathering of the people *be:*

11 Binding his foal unto the <sup>t</sup>vine, and his ass's colt unto the choice vine; he washed his garments in wine, and his clothes in the <sup>u</sup>blood of grapes:

12 His eyes *shall be* red with wine, and his teeth white with milk.

13 ¶ Zebulun shall dwell at the <sup>x</sup>haven of the sea; and he *shall be* for an haven of ships: and his border *shall be* unto Zidon.

14 ¶ Issachar *is* <sup>y</sup>a strong ass, couching down between two burdens:

15 And he saw that rest *was* good, and the land that *it was* pleasant; and bowed his shoulder to bear, and became a servant unto tribute.

16 ¶ Dan shall <sup>z</sup>judge his people, as one of the tribes of Israel.

17 Dan shall be <sup>a</sup>a serpent by the way, an adder in the path, that biteth the horse-heels, so that his rider shall fall backward.

18 I have <sup>b</sup>waited for thy salvation, O LORD!

19 ¶ Gad, <sup>c</sup>a troop shall overcome him: but he shall overcome at the last.

20 ¶ Out of Asher <sup>d</sup>his bread *shall be* fat, and he shall yield royal dainties.
