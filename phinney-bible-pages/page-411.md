## CHAPTER VII.

Solomon persuadeth to a sincere and kind familiarity with wisdom.

MY son, <sup>a</sup>keep my words, and lay up my commandments with thee.

2 Keep my commandments, and <sup>b</sup>live; and my law as the apple of thine eye.

3 <sup>c</sup>Bind them upon thy fingers, write them upon the table of thy heart.

4 Say unto Wisdom, Thou *art* my <sup>d</sup>sister; and call Understanding *thy* kinswoman:

5 That they may <sup>e</sup>keep thee from the strange woman, from the stranger *which* flattereth with her words.

6 For at the window of my house I looked through my casement,

7 And beheld among the <sup>f</sup>simple ones, I discerned among the youths, a young man <sup>g</sup>void of understanding,

8 Passing through the street <sup>h</sup>near her corner; and he <sup>i</sup>went the way to her house;

9 In <sup>k</sup>the twilight, in the evening, in the black and dark night:

10 And, behold, there met him a woman, *with* the <sup>l</sup>attire of a harlot, and <sup>m</sup>subtle of heart.

11 (She *is* <sup>n</sup>loud and stubborn; her feet <sup>o</sup>abide not in her house:

12 Now *is she* without, now in the streets, and <sup>p</sup>lieth in wait at every corner.)

13 So she <sup>q</sup>caught him, and kissed him, *and* <sup>r</sup>with an impudent face said unto him,

14 *I have* <sup>s</sup>peace-offerings with me; this day have I paid my vows:

15 Therefore came I forth to meet thee, diligently to seek thy face; and I have <sup>t</sup>found thee.

16 I have <sup>u</sup>decked my bed with coverings of tapestry, with carved *works,* with fine linen of Egypt.

17 I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.

18 Come, let us take our fill of love until the morning; let us solace ourselves with loves:

19 For the good man *is* not at home, he is gone <sup>x</sup>a long journey:

20 He hath taken a bag of money with him, *and* will come home at the day appointed.

21 With much fair speech she <sup>y</sup>caused him to yield, with the <sup>z</sup>flattering of her lips she forced him.

22 He goeth after her straightway, as <sup>a</sup>an ox goeth to the slaughter, or as a fool to the <sup>b</sup>correction of the stocks;

23 Till <sup>c</sup>a dart strike through his liver, as a bird <sup>d</sup>hasteth to the snare, and knoweth not that it *is* for his life.

24 Hearken unto me now, therefore, O ye children, and attend to the words of my mouth:

25 Let not <sup>e</sup>thy heart decline to her ways, go not astray in her paths:

26 For she hath cast down many wounded; yea, many <sup>f</sup>strong *men* have been slain by her.

27 <sup>g</sup>Her house *is* the way to hell, going down to the <sup>h</sup>chambers of death.

## CHAPTER VIII.

Wisdom is to be desired for the blessedness it bringeth.

DOTH not <sup>a</sup>Wisdom cry? and Understanding <sup>b</sup>put forth her voice?

2 She standeth in the top of high places, <sup>c</sup>by the way in the places of the paths.

3 She <sup>d</sup>crieth at the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors;

4 Unto <sup>e</sup>you, O men, I call; and my voice *is* to the sons of man.

5 O ye simple, understand wisdom; and, <sup>f</sup>ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart.

6 Hear; for I will speak of <sup>g</sup>excellent things; and the opening of my lips *shall be* <sup>h</sup>right things.

7 For my mouth shall speak <sup>i</sup>truth; and wickedness *is* an abomination to my lips.

8 All the words of my mouth *are* in righteousness; *there is* nothing froward or perverse in them.

9 They *are* all <sup>k</sup>plain to him that understandeth, and right to them that find knowledge.

10 <sup>l</sup>Receive my instruction, and <sup>m</sup>not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold.

11 For wisdom *is* <sup>n</sup>better than rubies; and all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it.

12 <sup>o</sup>I Wisdom dwell with Prudence, and find out knowledge of <sup>p</sup>witty inventions.

13 <sup>q</sup>The fear of the LORD *is* to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the <sup>r</sup>froward mouth, do I hate.

14 Counsel *is* mine, and <sup>s</sup>sound wisdom: I *am* understanding; I have strength.

15 <sup>t</sup>By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.

16 By me <sup>u</sup>princes rule, and nobles, *even* all the judges of the earth.

17 I love them that <sup>x</sup>love me; and those that seek me early shall find me.

18 <sup>y</sup>Riches and honour *are* with me; *yea,* durable riches and righteousness.

19 <sup>z</sup>My fruit *is* better than gold, yea, than fine gold; and my revenue than choice silver.

20 <sup>a</sup>I lead in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment;

21 That I may cause those that love me to inherit <sup>†</sup>substance; and I will fill their treasures.

22 The LORD <sup>b</sup>possessed me in the beginning of his way, before his works of old.

23 I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, or ever the earth was.

24 When *there were* no depths, I was brought forth; when *there were* no fountains abounding with water.

25 Before the mountains were settled, before the hills was I brought forth:

26 While as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields, nor the highest part of the dust of the world.

27 When he prepared the heavens, <sup>c</sup>I *was* there; when he set a compass upon the face of the depth;

28 When he established the clouds above; when he strengthened the fountains of the deep;

29 When he gave to the sea <sup>d</sup>his decree, that the waters should not pass his commandment; when he appointed the foundations of the earth:

30 Then I was <sup>e</sup>by him, *as* one brought up *with him;* and I was daily <sup>f</sup>*his* delight, rejoicing always before him,

31 Rejoicing in the habitable part of his earth; and <sup>g</sup>my delights *were* with the sons of men.

32 Now, therefore, hearken unto me, O ye children: for <sup>h</sup>blessed *are they that* keep my ways.

33 Hear instruction, and be wise, and <sup>i</sup>refuse it not.

34 Blessed *is* the man that <sup>k</sup>heareth me, <sup>l</sup>watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.

35 For whoso findeth me findeth life, and shall obtain favour of the LORD.

36 But he that sinneth against me <sup>m</sup>wrongeth his own soul: all they that <sup>n</sup>hate me love death.
