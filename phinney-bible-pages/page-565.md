8 But truly I am <sup>l</sup>full of power by the Spirit of the LORD, and of judgment, and of might, to declare unto Jacob his transgression, and to Israel his sin.

9 Hear this, I pray you, ye heads of the house of Jacob, and princes of the house of Israel, that abhor judgment, and pervert all equity.

10 They build up Zion <sup>m</sup>with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity.

11 The heads thereof judge for reward, and the priests thereof <sup>n</sup>teach for hire, and the prophets thereof divine for money: yet will they <sup>o</sup>lean upon the LORD, and say, *Is* not the LORD among us? none evil can come upon us.

12 Therefore shall Zion for your sake be plowed *as* a field; and Jerusalem shall become <sup>p</sup>heaps; and the mountain of the house as the high places of the forest.

8 And thou, O tower of the flock, the strong hold of the daughter of Zion, unto thee shall it come, even the first dominion; the kingdom shall come to the daughter of Jerusalem.

9 Now why dost thou <sup>o</sup>cry out aloud? *is there* <sup>p</sup>no king in thee? is thy counsellor perished? for <sup>q</sup>pangs have taken thee as a woman in travail.

10 <sup>r</sup>Be in pain, and labour to bring forth, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in travail; for <sup>s</sup>now shalt thou go forth out of the city, and thou shalt <sup>t</sup>dwell in the field; and thou shalt <sup>u</sup>go *even* to Babylon; <sup>x</sup>there shalt thou be delivered; there the LORD shall redeem thee from the hand of thine enemies.

11 Now also <sup>y</sup>many nations are gathered against thee, that say, Let her be defiled, and let our eye look upon Zion.

12 But they know not the <sup>z</sup>thoughts of the LORD, neither understand they his counsel: for he shall <sup>a</sup>gather them as the sheaves into the floor.

13 Arise and <sup>b</sup>thresh, O daughter of Zion; for I will make thy horn iron, and I will make thy hoofs brass; and thou shalt beat in pieces many people: and I will <sup>c</sup>consecrate their gain unto the LORD, and their substance unto the LORD of the whole earth.
