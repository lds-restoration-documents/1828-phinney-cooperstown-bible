## CHAPTER XII.

Job maintaineth himself against his friends.

AND Job answered and said,

2 No doubt but ye *are* <sup>a</sup>the people, and wisdom shall die <sup>b</sup>with you.

3 But I have understanding as well as you; I *am* not <sup>c</sup>inferior to you: yea, who knoweth not such things as these?

4 I am *as* one mocked of his neighbour, who <sup>d</sup>calleth upon God, and he answereth him: the just upright *man is* laughed to scorn.

5 He that is ready <sup>e</sup>to slip with *his* feet *is as* a lamp despised in the thought of him that is at ease.

6 The tabernacles of robbers <sup>f</sup>prosper, and they that provoke God are secure; into whose hand God bringeth *abundantly.*

7 But <sup>g</sup>ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee; and the fowls of the air, and they shall tell thee:

8 Or speak to the earth, and it shall teach thee; and the fishes of the sea shall declare unto thee.

9 Who knoweth not in all these, that the <sup>h</sup>hand of the LORD hath wrought this?

10 In whose <sup>i</sup>hand *is* the soul of every living thing, and the breath of all mankind.

11 Doth not the <sup>k</sup>ear try words; and the mouth taste his meat?

12 With the <sup>l</sup>ancient *is* wisdom; and in length of days understanding.

13 With him *is* <sup>m</sup>wisdom and strength, he hath counsel and understanding.

14 Behold, he breaketh down, and it cannot be <sup>n</sup>built again; he shutteth up a man, and there can be no opening.

15 Behold, he withholdeth the waters, and they dry up; also he <sup>o</sup>sendeth them out, and they overturn the earth.

16 With him *is* <sup>p</sup>strength and wisdom: the deceived and the deceiver *are* his.

17 He leadeth counsellors <sup>q</sup>away spoiled, and maketh the judges fools.

18 He <sup>r</sup>looseth the bond of kings, and girdeth their loins with a girdle.

19 He leadeth princes away spoiled, and <sup>s</sup>overthroweth the mighty.

20 He removeth away <sup>t</sup>the speech of the trusty, and taketh away the understanding of the aged.

21 He poureth <sup>u</sup>contempt upon princes, and weakeneth the strength of the mighty.

22 He discovereth <sup>x</sup>deep things out of darkness, and bringeth out to light the shadow of death.

23 He <sup>y</sup>increaseth the nations, and destroyeth them: he enlargeth the nations, and straiteneth them *again.*

24 He taketh away <sup>z</sup>the heart of the chief of the people of the earth, and causeth them to wander in a wilderness *where there is* no way.

25 They <sup>a</sup>grope in the dark without light, and he maketh them to stagger like *a* drunken *man.*

## CHAPTER XIII.

Job reproveth his friends of partiality.

LO, mine eye hath seen all *this,* mine ear hath heard and understood it.

2 What ye know, *the same* <sup>a</sup>do I know also: I *am* not inferior unto you.

3 Surely I would <sup>b</sup>speak to the Almighty, and I desire to reason with God.

4 But ye *are* forgers of lies, ye *are* all physicians of <sup>c</sup>no value.

5 Oh that you would altogether hold your peace! and it should be <sup>d</sup>your wisdom.

6 <sup>e</sup>Hear now my reasoning, and hearken to the pleadings of my lips.

7 Will you speak wickedly <sup>f</sup>for God? and talk deceitfully for him?

8 Will ye <sup>g</sup>accept his person? will ye contend for God?

9 Is it good that he should search you out? or, as one man <sup>h</sup>mocketh another, do ye *so* mock him?

10 He will surely <sup>i</sup>reprove you, if ye do secretly accept persons.

11 Shall not his excellency make you afraid? and his <sup>k</sup>dread fall upon you?

12 Your remembrances *are* like unto <sup>l</sup>ashes, your bodies to bodies of clay.

13 Hold your peace, let me alone, that I may speak, and <sup>m</sup>let come on me what *will.*

14 Wherefore do I take my flesh in my teeth, and put my life <sup>n</sup>in my hand?

15 Though he slay me, <sup>o</sup>yet will I trust in him: but I will maintain mine own ways before him.

16 He also *shall be* my salvation: for a hypocrite shall <sup>p</sup>not come before him.

17 <sup>q</sup>Hear diligently my speech and my declaration with your ears.

18 Behold now, I have ordered *my* cause; <sup>r</sup>I know that I shall be justified.

19 Who *is* he <sup>s</sup>*that* will plead with me? for now, if I <sup>t</sup>hold my tongue, I shall give up the ghost.

20 Only do not <sup>u</sup>two *things* unto me; then will I not hide myself from thee.

21 Withdraw <sup>x</sup>thy hand far from me; and let not thy dread make me afraid:

22 Then <sup>y</sup>call thou, and I will answer; <sup>z</sup>or let me speak, and answer thou me.

23 <sup>a</sup>How many *are* mine iniquities and sins! make me to know my transgression and my sin.

24 Wherefore <sup>b</sup>hidest thou thy face, and holdest me for thine enemy?

25 Wilt thou <sup>c</sup>break a leaf driven to and fro? and wilt thou pursue the dry stubble?

26 For thou writest bitter things against me, and makest me to possess the iniquities of <sup>d</sup>my youth.
