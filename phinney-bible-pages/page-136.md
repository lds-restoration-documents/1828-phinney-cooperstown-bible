**Header:** *Moses exhorteth the* | DEUTERONOMY. | *people to obedience.*

7 For what nation *is there so* great, who *hath* God *so* <sup>i</sup>nigh unto them, as the LORD our God *is* in all *things that* we call upon him *for?*

8 And what nation *is there so* great, that hath statutes and judgments *so* righteous as all this law, which I set before you this day?

9 Only take heed to thyself, and keep thy soul diligently, lest thou forget the things which thine eyes have seen, and lest they <sup>k</sup>depart from thy heart all the days of thy life; but <sup>l</sup>teach them thy sons, and thy sons' sons;

10 *Specially* <sup>m</sup>the day that thou stoodest before the LORD thy God in Horeb, when the LORD said unto me, Gather me the people together, and I will <sup>n</sup>make them hear my words, that they may learn to fear me all the days that they shall live upon the earth, and *that* they may teach their children.

11 And ye came near, and stood under the mountain; and the mountain <sup>o</sup>burned with fire unto the midst of heaven, with darkness, clouds, <sup>p</sup>and thick darkness.

12 And the LORD spake unto you out of the midst of the fire: ye heard the voice of the words, but saw <sup>q</sup>no similitude; only *ye heard* a voice.

13 And he declared unto you his covenant, which he commanded you to perform, *even* ten commandments; and he wrote them upon two tables of <sup>r</sup>stone.

14 And the LORD commanded me at that time to teach you <sup>s</sup>statutes and judgments, that ye might do them in the land whither ye go over to possess it.

15 Take ye therefore <sup>t</sup>good heed unto yourselves, (for ye saw <sup>u</sup>no manner of similitude on the day *that* the LORD spake unto you in Horeb out of the midst of the fire,)

16 Lest ye corrupt *yourselves,* and make you a graven image, the similitude of any <sup>x</sup>figure, the likeness of male or female;

17 The likeness of any <sup>y</sup>beast that *is* on the earth, the likeness of any winged fowl that flieth in the air;

18 The likeness of any thing that creepeth on the ground, the likeness of any fish that *is* in the waters beneath the earth:

19 And lest thou lift up thine eyes unto heaven, and when thou seest the <sup>z</sup>sun, and the moon, and the stars, *even* all the host of heaven, shouldest be <sup>a</sup>driven to worship them, and serve them, which the LORD thy God <sup>b</sup>hath divided unto all nations under the whole heaven.

20 But the LORD hath taken you, and brought you forth out of <sup>c</sup>the iron furnace, *even* out of Egypt, to be unto him a people of <sup>d</sup>inheritance, as *ye are* this day.

21 Furthermore, the LORD was angry with me <sup>e</sup>for your sakes, and sware that I should not go over Jordan, and that I should not go in unto that good land which the LORD thy God giveth thee *for* an inheritance:

22 But I must die in this land, I must <sup>f</sup>not go over Jordan: but ye shall go over, and possess that good land.

23 Take heed unto yourselves, <sup>g</sup>lest ye forget the covenant of the LORD your God, which he made with you, and make you a graven image, *or* the likeness of any *thing,* which the LORD thy God hath forbidden thee.

24 For the LORD thy God *is* a consuming <sup>h</sup>fire, *even* a <sup>i</sup>jealous God.

25 When thou shalt beget children, and children's children, and shalt have <sup>†</sup>remained long in the land, and shall corrupt *yourselves,* and make a graven image, *or* the likeness of any *thing,* and shall do evil in the sight of the LORD thy God, to provoke him to anger;

26 <sup>k</sup>I call heaven and earth to witness against you this day, that ye shall soon utterly <sup>l</sup>perish from off the land whereunto you go over Jordan to possess it; ye shall not prolong *your* days upon it, but shall utterly be destroyed.

27 And the LORD shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left <sup>†</sup>few in number among the heathen, whither the LORD shall lead you.

28 And there ye shall serve gods, the work of men's hands, wood and stone, which neither <sup>m</sup>see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.

29 But if <sup>n</sup>from thence thou shalt seek the LORD thy God, thou shalt find *him,* if thou seek him with all thy heart and with all thy soul.

30 When thou art in tribulation, and all these things <sup>†</sup>are come upon thee, *even* in <sup>o</sup>the latter days, if thou <sup>p</sup>turn to the LORD thy God, and shalt be obedient unto his voice,

31 (For the LORD thy God *is* a merciful God,) he will not forsake thee, neither destroy thee, nor forget the covenant of thy fathers, which he sware unto them.

32 For <sup>q</sup>ask now of the days that are past, which were before thee, since the day that God created man upon earth, and *ask* from the <sup>r</sup>one side of heaven unto the other, whether there hath been *any such thing* as this <sup>s</sup>great thing *is,* or hath been heard like it?

33 Did *ever* people hear the voice of God speaking out of the midst of the fire, as thou hast heard, <sup>t</sup>and live?

34 Or hath God assayed to go *and* take him a nation from the midst of *another* nation, by <sup>u</sup>temptations, by <sup>x</sup>signs, and by <sup>y</sup>wonders, and by war, and by a mighty hand, and by a <sup>z</sup>stretched-out arm, and by great <sup>a</sup>terrors, according to all that the LORD your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?

35 Unto thee it was shewed, that thou mightest <sup>b</sup>know that the LORD he *is* God; *there is* none else besides him.

36 <sup>c</sup>Out of heaven he made thee to hear his voice, that he might <sup>d</sup>instruct thee: and <sup>e</sup>upon earth he shewed thee his great fire; and thou heardest his words out of the midst of the fire.

37 And because he loved thy fathers, therefore he chose their seed after them, and brought thee out <sup>f</sup>in his sight with his mighty power out of Egypt;

38 To drive out nations from before thee, <sup>g</sup>greater and mightier than thou *art,* to bring thee in, to give thee their land *for* an inheritance, as *it is* this day.

39 <sup>h</sup>Know therefore this day, and consider *it* in thine heart, that the LORD he *is* God in heaven above, and upon the earth beneath: *there is* none else.

40 Thou shalt keep therefore his statutes, and his commandments, which I command thee this day, that it may <sup>i</sup>go well with thee, and with thy children after thee, and that thou mayest prolong *thy* days upon the earth, which the LORD thy God giveth thee, for ever.

41 ¶ Then Moses severed <sup>k</sup>three cities on this side Jordan, toward the sun-rising;

42 That the slayer might flee thither, which should kill his neighbour <sup>l</sup>unawares, and hated him not in times past; and that, fleeing unto one of these cities, he might live:
