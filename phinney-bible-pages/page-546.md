## CHAPTER VI.

1 Daniel's preferment: 10 He is cast into the den of lions: 18 He is miraculously saved.

IT pleased Darius <sup>a</sup>to set over the kingdom a hundred and twenty princes, which should be over the whole kingdom;

2 And over these, three presidents, of whom Daniel *was* <sup>b</sup>first; that the princes might give <sup>c</sup>accounts unto them, and the king should have no damage.

3 Then this Daniel was <sup>d</sup>preferred above the presidents and princes, <sup>e</sup>because an excellent spirit *was* in him; and the king thought to set him over the whole realm.

4 Then <sup>f</sup>the presidents and princes <sup>g</sup>sought to find occasion against Daniel concerning the kingdom; but they could <sup>h</sup>find none occasion nor fault; forasmuch as he *was* faithful, neither was there any <sup>i</sup>error or fault found in him.

5 Then said these men, We <sup>k</sup>shall not find any occasion against this Daniel, except we find *it* against him concerning the <sup>l</sup>law of his God.

6 Then these presidents and princes <sup>m</sup>assembled together <sup>n</sup>to the king, and said thus unto him, King Darius, live for ever.

7 <sup>o</sup>All the presidents of the kingdom, the governors, and the princes, the counsellors, and the captains, have <sup>p</sup>consulted together to establish a royal statute, and to make a firm decree, that whosoever shall ask a petition of any <sup>q</sup>god or man for thirty days, save of thee, O king, he shall be cast into the den of lions.

8 Now, O king, <sup>r</sup>establish the decree, and sign the writing, that it be not changed, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which <sup>s</sup>altereth not.

9 Wherefore king Darius <sup>t</sup>signed the writing and the decree.

10 Now when Daniel <sup>u</sup>knew that the writing was signed, he went into his house; and, his <sup>x</sup>windows being open in his chamber <sup>y</sup>toward Jerusalem, he kneeled upon his <sup>z</sup>knees three times a-day, and prayed, <sup>a</sup>and gave thanks before his God, <sup>b</sup>as he did aforetime.

11 Then these men assembled, and <sup>c</sup>found Daniel praying and making supplication before his God.

12 <sup>d</sup>Then they came near, and spake before the king concerning the king's decree; Hast thou not signed a decree, that every man that shall ask *a petition* of any god or man within thirty days, save of thee, O king, shall be cast into the den of lions? The king answered and said, <sup>e</sup>The thing *is* true, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which altereth not.

13 Then answered they, and said before the king, That Daniel, which *is* of the children of the <sup>f</sup>captivity of Judah, <sup>g</sup>regardeth not thee, O king, nor the decree that thou hast signed, but maketh his petition three times a-day.

14 Then the king, when he heard *these* words, was sore displeased with himself, and <sup>h</sup>set *his* heart on Daniel to deliver him; and he laboured <sup>i</sup>till the going down of the sun to deliver him.

15 Then these men assembled unto the king, and said unto the king, Know, O king, that the <sup>k</sup>law of the Medes and Persians *is,* That no decree nor statute which the king establisheth may be changed.

16 <sup>l</sup>Then the king commanded, and they brought Daniel, and <sup>m</sup>cast *him* into the den of lions. *Now* the king spake and said unto Daniel, <sup>n</sup>Thy God, whom thou servest <sup>o</sup>continually, he will deliver thee.

17 And <sup>p</sup>a stone was brought, and laid upon the mouth of the den; and the king sealed it with his own signet, and with the signet of his lords, that the purpose might not be <sup>q</sup>changed concerning Daniel.

18 Then the king <sup>r</sup>went to his palace, and passed the night <sup>s</sup>fasting: neither were instruments of music brought before him; and his <sup>t</sup>sleep went from him.

19 Then the king arose <sup>u</sup>very early in the morning, and went in haste unto the den of lions.

20 And when he came to the den, he cried with a lamentable voice unto Daniel; *and* the king spake and said to Daniel, O Daniel, <sup>x</sup>servant of the living God, is thy <sup>y</sup>God, whom thou servest continually, able to deliver thee from the lions?

21 Then said Daniel unto the king, O king, live for ever.

22 My God hath sent his <sup>z</sup>angel, and <sup>a</sup>hath shut the lions' mouths, that they have not hurt me: forasmuch as <sup>b</sup>before him innocency was found in me; and also <sup>c</sup>before thee, O king, have I done no hurt.

23 Then was the king <sup>d</sup>exceeding glad for him, and commanded that they should take Daniel up out of the den. So Daniel was taken up out of the den, and <sup>e</sup>no manner of hurt was found upon him, because he <sup>f</sup>believed in his God.

24 And the king commanded, and they brought <sup>g</sup>those men which had accused Daniel, and they <sup>h</sup>cast *them* into the den of lions, them, their <sup>i</sup>children, and their wives; and the lions had the mastery of them, and brake all their bones in pieces or ever they came at the bottom of the den.

25 Then king Darius <sup>k</sup>wrote unto all people, nations, and languages, that dwell in all the earth; <sup>l</sup>Peace be multiplied unto you.

26 I make a decree, That in every dominion of my kingdom men tremble <sup>m</sup>and fear before the God of Daniel; for he *is* the <sup>n</sup>living God, and <sup>o</sup>steadfast for ever, and his kingdom *that* which shall <sup>p</sup>not be destroyed, and his dominion *shall be even* unto the end.

27 He <sup>q</sup>delivereth and rescueth, and he worketh <sup>r</sup>signs and wonders in heaven and in earth, who hath delivered Daniel from the power of the lions.

28 So <sup>s</sup>this Daniel prospered in the reign of Darius, and in the reign of Cyrus the Persian.
