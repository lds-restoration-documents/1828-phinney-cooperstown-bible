5 Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I the LORD thy God *am* a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth *generation* of them that hate me;

6 And shewing mercy unto thousands of them that <sup>h</sup>love me, and keep my commandments.

7 Thou shalt not <sup>i</sup>take the name of the LORD thy God in vain; for the LORD will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain.

8 <sup>k</sup>Remember the sabbath-day, to keep it holy.

9 <sup>l</sup>Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work:

10 But the seventh day *is* the sabbath of the LORD thy God; *in it* thou shalt not do <sup>m</sup>any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy man-servant, nor thy maid-servant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that *is* within thy gates;

11 For *in* <sup>n</sup>six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them *is*, and rested the seventh day: wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath-day, and hallowed it.

12 <sup>o</sup>Honour thy father and thy mother; that thy days may be long upon the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.

13 Thou shalt <sup>p</sup>not kill.

14 Thou shalt not commit <sup>q</sup>adultery.

15 Thou shalt <sup>r</sup>not steal.

16 Thou shalt not <sup>s</sup>bear false witness against thy neighbour.

17 Thou shalt not <sup>t</sup>covet thy neighbour's house, thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's wife, nor his man-servant, nor his maid-servant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that *is* thy neighbour's.

21 And the people stood afar off: and Moses drew near unto the <sup>e</sup>thick darkness where God *was*.

22 And the LORD said unto Moses, Thus thou shalt say unto the children of Israel, Ye have seen that I have talked with you from <sup>f</sup>heaven.

23 Ye shall not make with me <sup>g</sup>gods of silver, neither shall ye make unto you gods of gold.

24 An altar of earth thou shalt make unto me, and shalt sacrifice thereon thy burnt-offerings, and thy peace-offerings, thy sheep, and thine oxen: <sup>h</sup>in all places where I <sup>i</sup>record my name I will come unto thee, and I will <sup>k</sup>bless thee.

25 And if thou wilt make me an altar of <sup>l</sup>stone, thou shalt not build it of hewn stone: for if thou lift up thy tool upon it, thou hast <sup>m</sup>polluted it.

26 Neither shalt thou go up by steps unto mine altar, that thy <sup>n</sup>nakedness be not discovered thereon.
