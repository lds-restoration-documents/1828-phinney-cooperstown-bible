## CHAPTER IX.

A prayer unto God for his wisdom, without which the best man is nothing worth, neither can he tell how to please God.

O GOD of my fathers, and Lord of mercy, <sup>a</sup>who hast made all things with thy word,

2 And ordained man through thy wisdom, that he should <sup>b</sup>have dominion over the creatures which thou hast made,

3 And order the world according to equity and righteousness, and execute judgment with an upright heart:

4 Give <sup>c</sup>me wisdom, that sitteth by thy throne; and reject me not from among thy children:

5 For I <sup>d</sup>thy servant, and son of thy handmaid, am a feeble person, and of a short time, and too young for the understanding of judgment and laws.

6 For though a man be never so perfect among the children of men, yet if thy wisdom be not with him, he shall be nothing regarded.

7 Thou hast chosen me to be a <sup>e</sup>king of thy people, and a judge of thy sons and daughters:

8 Thou hast commanded me to <sup>f</sup>build a temple upon thy holy mount, and an altar in the city wherein thou dwellest, a resemblance of the holy tabernacle, which thou hast prepared from the beginning.

9 And <sup>g</sup>wisdom was with thee: which knoweth thy works, <sup>h</sup>and was present when thou madest the world, and knew what was acceptable in thy sight, and right in thy commandments.

10 O send her out of thy holy heavens, and from the throne of thy glory, that being present she may labour with me, that I may know what is pleasing unto thee.

11 For she knoweth and understandeth all things, and she shall lead me soberly in my doings, and preserve me <sup>||</sup>in her power.

12 So shall my works be acceptable, and then shall I judge thy people righteously, and be worthy to sit in my father's seat.

13 For <sup>i</sup>what man is he that can know the counsel of God? or who can think what the will of the Lord is?

14 For the thoughts of mortal men are <sup>||</sup>miserable, and our devices are but uncertain.

15 For the corruptible body presseth down the soul, and <sup>k</sup>the earthly tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things.

16 And hardly do we guess aright at things that are upon earth, and with labour do we find the things that are <sup>†</sup>before us: <sup>l</sup>but the things that are in heaven who hath searched out?

17 And thy counsel who hath known, except thou give wisdom, and send thy <sup>m</sup>Holy Spirit from above?

18 For so the ways of them which lived on the earth were reformed, and men were taught the things that are pleasing unto thee, and were saved through wisdom.

## CHAPTER X.

What wisdom did for Adam, Noe, Abraham, Lot, and against the five cities, for Jacob, Joseph, Moses, and the Israelites.

SHE preserved <sup>a</sup>the first-formed father of the world, that was created alone, and brought him out of his fall,

2 And <sup>b</sup>gave him power to rule all things.

3 <sup>c</sup>But when the unrighteous went away from her in his anger, he perished also in the fury wherewith he murdered his brother.

4 For whose cause the earth <sup>d</sup>being drowned with the flood, wisdom again preserved it, <sup>e</sup>and directed the course of the righteous in a piece of wood of small value.

5 Moreover, <sup>f</sup>the nations in their wicked conspiracy being confounded, she found out the righteous, and preserved him blameless unto God, and <sup>g</sup>kept him strong <sup>||</sup>against his tender compassion toward his son.

6 When the ungodly perished, <sup>h</sup>she delivered the righteous man, who fled from the fire which fell down upon the <sup>†</sup>five cities. <!-- the margin letter i (Gen. 14, 8) has no visible matching mark in the text -->

7 Of whose wickedness even to this day the waste land that smoketh is a testimony, and plants bearing fruit that never come to ripeness: and a <sup>k</sup>standing pillar of salt *is* a monument of an unbelieving soul.

8 For regarding not wisdom, they gat not only this hurt, that they knew not the things which were good; but also left behind them to the world a memorial of their foolishness: so that in the things wherein they offended they could not so much as be hid.

9 But wisdom delivered from pain those that attended upon her.

10 <sup>l</sup>When the righteous fled from his brother's wrath, she guided him in right paths, shewed him the kingdom of God, and gave him knowledge of holy things, made him rich in his travels, and multiplied *the fruit of* his labours.

11 <sup>m</sup>In the covetousness of such as oppressed him she stood by him, and made him rich.

12 She defended him from his enemies, and kept him safe from those that lay in wait, <sup>n</sup>and in a sore conflict she gave him the victory; that he might know that godliness is stronger than all.

13 <sup>o</sup>When the righteous was sold, she forsook him not, but delivered him from sin: she went down with him into the pit,

14 And left him not in bonds, till she brought him the sceptre of the kingdom, and <sup>||</sup>power against those that oppressed him: as for them that had accused him, she shewed them to be liars, and gave him perpetual glory.

15 She delivered the <sup>||</sup>righteous people and blameless seed <sup>p</sup>from the nation that oppressed them.

16 She entered into the soul of the servant of the Lord, and <sup>q</sup>withstood dreadful kings in wonders and signs;

17 Rendered to the righteous a reward of their labours, guided them in a marvellous way, and was unto them for a covert by day, and a <sup>||</sup>light of stars in the night season;

18 <sup>r</sup>Brought them through the Red Sea, and led them through much water:

19 But <sup>s</sup>she drowned their enemies, and cast them up out of the bottom of the deep.

20 Therefore the righteous spoiled the ungodly, and <sup>t</sup>praised thy holy name, O Lord, and magnified with one accord thy hand that fought for them.

21 For wisdom <sup>u</sup>opened the mouth of the dumb, and made the tongues of them that cannot speak eloquent.
