## PSALM LXIX.

*To the chief Musician upon Shoshannim. A Psalm of David.*

SAVE <sup>a</sup>me, O God; for the <sup>b</sup>waters are come in unto *my* soul.

2 I sink in deep mire, where *there is* no standing: I am come into <sup>c</sup>deep waters, where the floods overflow me.

3 I am <sup>d</sup>weary of my crying; my throat is dried: <sup>e</sup>mine eyes fail while I wait for my God.

4 They that hate me <sup>f</sup>without a cause are more than the hairs of my head: they that would destroy me, *being* mine enemies wrongfully, are mighty: then I restored *that* which I took not away.

5 O God, thou knowest <sup>g</sup>my foolishness; and my sins are not hid from thee.

6 Let not them that wait on thee, O Lord GOD of hosts, be ashamed <sup>h</sup>for my sake; let not those that seek thee be confounded for my sake, O God of Israel.

7 Because <sup>i</sup>for thy sake I have borne reproach: shame hath covered my face.

8 I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and <sup>k</sup>an alien unto my mother's children.

9 For the zeal of thy house hath <sup>l</sup>eaten me up; and the reproaches of them that reproached thee <sup>m</sup>are fallen upon me.

10 When I wept, *and chastened* my soul with fasting, that was <sup>n</sup>to my reproach.

11 I made sackcloth also my garment; and I became a proverb unto them.

12 They that sit in the gate <sup>o</sup>speak against me; and I *was* the <sup>p</sup>song of the drunkards.

13 But <sup>q</sup>as for me, my prayer *is* unto thee, O LORD, <sup>r</sup>*in* an acceptable time: O God, in the multitude of thy mercy hear me: in the <sup>s</sup>truth of thy salvation.

14 Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink: let me be delivered from them that hate me, and out of the deep waters.

15 Let not the water-flood overflow me, neither let the deep swallow me up, and let not the pit <sup>t</sup>shut her mouth upon me.

16 Hear me, O LORD; for <sup>u</sup>thy loving-kindness *is* good: turn unto me according to the multitude of thy tender mercies.

17 And <sup>x</sup>hide not thy face from thy servant; for I am in trouble: hear me speedily.

18 Draw nigh unto my soul, *and* redeem it: deliver me, <sup>y</sup>because of mine enemies.

19 Thou hast <sup>z</sup>known my reproach, and my shame, and my dishonour: mine adversaries *are* all before thee.

20 Reproach hath broken my heart, and I am full of heaviness: and I looked *for some* to take pity, but *there was* none; and for comforters, but <sup>a</sup>I found none.

21 They gave me also <sup>b</sup>gall for my meat; and in my thirst they gave me <sup>c</sup>vinegar to drink.

23 Let their <sup>e</sup>eyes be darkened, that they see not; and make their loins continually to shake.

24 <sup>f</sup>Pour out thine indignation upon them, and let thy wrathful anger take hold of them.

25 Let their <sup>g</sup>habitation be desolate; *and* let <sup>h</sup>none dwell in their tents.

26 For they persecute *him* whom <sup>i</sup>thou hast smitten; and they talk to the grief of those whom thou hast wounded.

27 <sup>k</sup>Add iniquity unto their iniquity; and let them not come into <sup>l</sup>thy righteousness.

28 Let them be blotted out of the <sup>m</sup>book of the living, and <sup>n</sup>not be written with the righteous.

29 But I *am* <sup>o</sup>poor and sorrowful: let thy salvation, O God, set me up on high.

30 I will <sup>p</sup>praise the name of God, with a song, and will magnify him with thanksgiving.

31 *This* also shall please the LORD <sup>q</sup>better than an ox *or* bullock that hath horns and hoofs.

32 The humble shall see *this, and* be glad: and your heart shall live that seek God.

33 For the LORD heareth the poor, and despiseth not <sup>r</sup>his prisoners.

34 Let the <sup>s</sup>heaven and earth praise him, the seas, and every thing that moveth therein:

35 For God will save <sup>t</sup>Sion, and will build the cities of Judah; that they may dwell there, and have it in possession.

36 The <sup>u</sup>seed also of his servants shall inherit it; and they that love his name shall dwell therein.
