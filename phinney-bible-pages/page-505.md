## CHAPTER IV.

1 Zion's pitiful estate bewailed....13 She confesseth her sins.

HOW is the gold become dim! *how* is the most fine gold changed! the stones of the sanctuary are <sup>a</sup>poured out in the top of every street.

2 The precious sons of Zion, comparable to fine gold, how are they esteemed <sup>b</sup>as earthen pitchers, the work of the hands of the potter!

3 Even the sea-monsters draw out the breast, they give suck to their <sup>c</sup>young ones: the daughter of my people *is become* cruel, like the ostriches in the wilderness.

4 The tongue of the sucking child cleaveth to the roof of his mouth for thirst: the young children <sup>d</sup>ask bread, *and* no man breaketh *it* unto them.

5 They that did feed delicately are desolate in the streets: they that were brought up in scarlet embrace <sup>e</sup>dunghills.

6 For the punishment of the iniquity of the daughter of my people is greater <sup>f</sup>than the punishment of the sin of Sodom, that was overthrown as in a moment, and no hands stayed on her.

7 Her <sup>g</sup>Nazarites were purer than snow, they were whiter than milk, they were more ruddy in body than rubies, their polishing *was* of sapphire:

8 Their visage is <sup>h</sup>blacker than a coal; they are not known in the streets: their skin cleaveth to their bones; it is withered, it is become like a stick.

9 *They that be* slain with the sword are better than *they that be* slain with hunger: for these <sup>i</sup>pine away, <sup>k</sup>stricken through for *want of* the fruits of the field.

10 The hands of the <sup>l</sup>pitiful women have <sup>m</sup>sodden their own children: they were their meat in the destruction of the daughter of my people.

11 The LORD hath accomplished his fury; he hath <sup>n</sup>poured out his fierce anger, and hath <sup>o</sup>kindled a fire in Zion, and it hath devoured the foundations thereof.

12 The kings of the earth, and all the inhabitants of the world, would not have believed that the adversary and the enemy should have <sup>p</sup>entered into the gates of Jerusalem.

13 For the <sup>q</sup>sins of her prophets, *and* the iniquities of her priests, that have shed the <sup>r</sup>blood of the just in the midst of her,

14 They have wandered <sup>s</sup>*as* blind *men* in the streets, they have polluted themselves with blood, so that men could not touch their garments.

15 They cried unto them, <sup>t</sup>Depart ye; *it is* unclean; depart, depart, touch not: when they fled away and wandered, <sup>u</sup>they said among the heathen, They shall no more sojourn *there.*

16 The anger of the LORD hath divided them; he will no more regard them: they <sup>x</sup>respected not the persons of the priests, they favoured not the elders.

17 As for us, our eyes as yet <sup>y</sup>failed for our vain help: in our <sup>z</sup>watching we have watched for a nation *that* could not save *us.*

18 They hunt our steps, that <sup>a</sup>we cannot go in our streets: our end is near, our days are fulfilled; for <sup>b</sup>our end is come.

19 Our persecutors are <sup>c</sup>swifter than the eagles of the heaven: they pursued us upon the mountains, they laid wait for us in the wilderness.

20 The <sup>d</sup>breath of our nostrils, the anointed of the LORD, was taken in their <sup>e</sup>pits, of whom we said, Under his shadow we shall live among the heathen.

21 ¶ <sup>f</sup>Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom, that dwellest in the land of Uz: the <sup>g</sup>cup also shall pass through unto thee; thou shalt be drunken, and shalt make thyself naked.

22 The punishment of thine iniquity is <sup>h</sup>accomplished, O daughter of Zion; he will no <sup>i</sup>more carry thee away into captivity: he will <sup>k</sup>visit thine iniquity, O daughter of Edom; he will discover thy sins.

## CHAPTER V.

A pitiful complaint of Zion, in prayer unto God.

REMEMBER, O LORD, what is come upon us: consider, and behold our <sup>a</sup>reproach.

2 Our inheritance is turned <sup>b</sup>to strangers, our houses to aliens.

3 We are <sup>c</sup>orphans and fatherless, our mothers *are* as widows.

4 We have drunken our water for money; our wood <sup>d</sup>is sold unto us.

5 <sup>e</sup>Our necks *are* under persecution: we <sup>f</sup>labour, *and* have no rest.

6 We have given the hand *to* the Egyptians, *and to* the Assyrians, to be <sup>g</sup>satisfied with bread.

7 <sup>h</sup>Our fathers have sinned, <sup>i</sup>and *are* not; and we have <sup>k</sup>borne their iniquities.

8 <sup>l</sup>Servants have ruled over us: *there is* none that doth deliver *us* out of their hand.

9 We gat our bread with *the* <sup>m</sup>peril *of* our lives, because of the sword of the wilderness.

10 <sup>n</sup>Our skin was black like an oven, because of the terrible famine.

11 They <sup>o</sup>ravished the women in Zion, *and* the maids in the cities of Judah.

12 <sup>p</sup>Princes are hanged up by their hand: the faces of elders were not honoured.

13 They took the young men <sup>q</sup>to grind, and the children fell under the wood.

14 The elders have ceased from the <sup>r</sup>gate, the young men from their music.

15 The <sup>s</sup>joy of our heart is ceased; our dance is turned into mourning.

16 The <sup>t</sup>crown is fallen *from* our head: <sup>u</sup>wo unto us that we have sinned!
