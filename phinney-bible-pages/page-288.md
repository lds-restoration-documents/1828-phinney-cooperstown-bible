**Header:** *David's two victories.* | I. CHRONICLES. | *The ark brought from Obed-edom.*

8 And David and all Israel played <sup>k</sup>before God with all *their* might, and with singing, and with harps, and with psalteries, and with timbrels, and with cymbals, and with trumpets.

9 And when they came unto the threshing-floor of Chidon, Uzza <sup>l</sup>put forth his hand to hold the ark; for the oxen stumbled.

10 And the <sup>m</sup>anger of the LORD was kindled against Uzza, and he smote him, <sup>n</sup>because he put his hand to the ark: and there he <sup>o</sup>died before God.

11 And David was displeased, because the LORD had made a breach upon Uzza: wherefore that place is called Perez-uzza to this day.

12 And David was afraid of God that day, saying, How shall I bring the ark of God *home* to me?

13 So David brought not the ark *home* to himself to the city of David, but carried it aside into the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.

14 And the ark of God remained with the family of Obed-edom <sup>p</sup>in his house three months. And the LORD <sup>q</sup>blessed the house of Obed-edom, and all that he had.

## CHAPTER XIV.

1 Hiram's kindness to David....2 His felicity.

NOW <sup>a</sup>Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, and timber of cedars, with masons and carpenters to build him a house.

2 And David perceived that <sup>b</sup>the LORD had confirmed him king over Israel; for his kingdom was <sup>c</sup>lifted up on high, because of his people Israel.

3 ¶ And David took <sup>d</sup>more wives at Jerusalem: and David begat more sons and daughters.

4 Now these *are* the names of *his* children which he had in Jerusalem; <sup>e</sup>Shammua, and Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon,

5 And Ibhar, and <sup>f</sup>Elishua, and <sup>g</sup>Elpalet,

6 And Nogah, and Nepheg, and Japhia,

7 And Elishama, and <sup>h</sup>Beeliada, and Eliphalet.

8 ¶ And when the Philistines heard that <sup>i</sup>David was anointed king over all Israel, <sup>k</sup>all the Philistines went up to seek David: and David heard *of it*, and went out against them.

9 And the Philistines came and spread themselves in the <sup>l</sup>valley of Rephaim.

10 And David <sup>m</sup>inquired of God, saying, Shall I go up against the Philistines? and wilt thou deliver them into my hand? And the LORD said unto him, Go up; for I will deliver them into thy hand.

11 So they came up to Baal-perazim, and David smote them there. Then David said, God hath broken in upon mine enemies by my hand, like the breaking forth of waters: therefore they called the name of that place <sup>||</sup>Baal-perazim.

12 And when they had left their <sup>n</sup>gods there, David gave a commandment, and they were burnt with fire.

13 And the Philistines <sup>o</sup>yet again spread themselves abroad in the valley.

14 Therefore David inquired again of God: and God said unto him, Go not up after them; turn away from them, and <sup>p</sup>come upon them over against the mulberry-trees.

15 And it shall be, when thou shalt hear <sup>q</sup>a sound of going in the <sup>r</sup>tops of the mulberry-trees, *that* then thou shalt go out to battle: for God is gone forth before thee to smite the host of the Philistines.

16 David therefore did as God commanded him: and they smote the host of the Philistines from Gibeon even to Gazer.

17 And the <sup>s</sup>fame of David went out into all lands; and the LORD brought the <sup>t</sup>fear of him upon all nations.

## CHAPTER XV.

1 David ordereth the priests and Levites to bring the ark from Obed-edom....25 He performeth the solemnity thereof with great joy....29 Michal despiseth him.

AND *David* made him houses in the <sup>a</sup>city of David, and prepared a place for the ark of God, and pitched for it a <sup>b</sup>tent.

2 Then David said, None ought to carry the ark of God but the <sup>c</sup>Levites: for them hath the LORD chosen to carry the ark of God, and to minister unto him for ever.

3 And David <sup>d</sup>gathered all Israel together to Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the LORD unto his place which he had prepared for it.

4 And David assembled the <sup>e</sup>children of Aaron, and the Levites:

5 Of the sons of <sup>f</sup>Kohath; Uriel the chief, and his brethren, a hundred and twenty.

6 Of the sons of <sup>g</sup>Merari; Asaiah the chief, and his brethren, two hundred and twenty.

7 Of the sons of <sup>h</sup>Gershom; Joel the chief, and his brethren, a hundred and thirty.

8 Of the sons of <sup>i</sup>Elizaphan; Shemaiah the chief, and his brethren, two hundred.

9 Of the sons of <sup>k</sup>Hebron; Eliel the chief, and his brethren, fourscore.

10 Of the sons of <sup>l</sup>Uzziel; Amminadab the chief, and his brethren, a hundred and twelve.

11 And David called for <sup>m</sup>Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and for the Levites, for Uriel, Asaiah, and Joel, Shemaiah, and Eliel, and Amminadab,

12 And said unto them, Ye *are* the chief of the fathers of the Levites; <sup>n</sup>sanctify yourselves, *both* ye and your brethren, that you may bring up the ark of the LORD God of Israel unto *the place that* I have prepared for it.

13 For because ye <sup>o</sup>*did it* not at the first, the LORD our God made <sup>p</sup>a breach upon us, for that we sought him not <sup>q</sup>after the due order.

14 So the priests and the Levites <sup>r</sup>sanctified themselves to bring up the ark of the LORD God of Israel.

15 And the children of the <sup>s</sup>Levites bare the ark of God upon their shoulders, with the staves thereon, <sup>t</sup>as Moses commanded, according to the word of the LORD.

16 And David spake to the chief of the Levites to appoint their brethren *to be* the <sup>u</sup>singers with instruments of music, psalteries, and harps, and cymbals, sounding, by lifting up the voice with joy.

17 So the Levites appointed <sup>x</sup>Heman the son of Joel; and of his brethren, <sup>y</sup>Asaph the son of Berechiah; and of the sons of Merari their brethren, <sup>z</sup>Ethan the son of Kushaiah;

18 And with them their brethren of the second *degree*, Zechariah, Ben, and Jaaziel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Unni, Eliab, and Benaiah, and Maaseiah, and Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneiah; and Obed-edom and Jeiel the porters.

19 So the singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, *were appointed* to sound with cymbals of brass;

20 And Zechariah, and Aziel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Unni, and Eliab, and Maaseiah, and Benaiah, with psalteries on <sup>||</sup>Alamoth;

21 And Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneiah, and Obed-edom, and Jeiel, and Azaziah, with harps on the <sup>a</sup>Sheminith to excel.
