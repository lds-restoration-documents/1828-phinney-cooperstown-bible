# ¶ The LAMENTATIONS of JEREMIAH.

## CHAPTER I.

1 The miserable estate of Jerusalem by her sin....12 Her complaint, 18 and confession of God's righteous judgments.

HOW doth the <sup>a</sup>city sit solitary *that was* full of people! *how* is she become as a widow! she *that was* great among the nations, *and* <sup>b</sup>princess among the provinces, *how* is she become <sup>c</sup>tributary!

2 She <sup>d</sup>weepeth sore in the night, and her tears *are* on her cheeks; among <sup>e</sup>all her lovers she hath none to comfort *her:* all her friends have dealt treacherously with her; they are become her enemies.

3 Judah <sup>f</sup>is gone into captivity, because of affliction, and because of great servitude; she <sup>g</sup>dwelleth among the heathen, she findeth no rest: all her persecutors overtook her between the <sup>h</sup>straits.

4 <sup>i</sup>The ways of Zion do mourn, because none come to the solemn feasts: all her gates are desolate: <sup>k</sup>her priests sigh, her virgins are afflicted, and she *is* in bitterness.

5 Her adversaries are the <sup>l</sup>chief, her enemies prosper; for the LORD hath afflicted her for the <sup>m</sup>multitude of her transgressions: her children are gone into captivity before the enemy.

6 And from the daughter of Zion <sup>n</sup>all her beauty is departed: her princes are become like harts *that* find <sup>o</sup>no pasture; and they are gone without strength before the pursuer.
