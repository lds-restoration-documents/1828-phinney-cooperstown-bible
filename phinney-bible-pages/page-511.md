11 When they <sup>s</sup>went, they went upon their four sides; they turned not as they went, but to the place whither the <sup>t</sup>head looked they <sup>u</sup>followed it; they turned not as they went.

15 And the cherubims were lifted up. This *is* the living creature that I saw by the river of Chebar.

16 And when the cherubims went, the wheels went by them; and when the cherubims lifted up their wings, to mount up from the earth, the same wheels also turned not from beside them.

17 When they stood, *these* stood; and when they were lifted up, *these* lifted up themselves *also:* for the spirit of the <sup>b</sup>living creature *was* in them.

18 Then the <sup>c</sup>glory of the LORD <sup>d</sup>departed from off the threshold of the house, and stood over the cherubims.

19 And the cherubims lifted up their wings, and mounted up from the earth in my sight: when they went out, the wheels also *were* beside them; and *every one* stood at the door of the <sup>e</sup>east gate of the LORD's house; and the glory of the God of Israel *was* over them above.

20 This *is* the living creature that I saw under the God of Israel, by the river of <sup>f</sup>Chebar; and I knew that they *were* the cherubims.

21 Every one had four faces apiece, and every one four wings; and the likeness of the hands of a man *was* under their wings.

22 And the likeness of their faces *was* the same faces which I saw by the river of Chebar, their appearances and themselves: <sup>g</sup>they went every one straight forward.

God sheweth Ezekiel his purpose in saving a remnant.

5 And the <sup>k</sup>Spirit of the LORD fell upon me, and said unto me, <sup>l</sup>Speak; Thus saith the LORD, Thus have ye said, O house of Israel: for I <sup>m</sup>know the things that come into your mind, *every one of* them.

6 Ye have <sup>n</sup>multiplied your slain in this city, and ye have <sup>o</sup>filled the streets thereof with the slain.

7 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, Your <sup>p</sup>slain, whom ye have laid in the midst of it, they *are* the flesh, and this *city is* the caldron; but <sup>q</sup>I will bring you forth out of the midst of it.

8 Ye have feared the sword; and I will bring a sword upon you, saith the Lord GOD.

9 And <sup>r</sup>I will bring you out of the midst thereof, and deliver you into the hands of <sup>s</sup>strangers, and will execute judgments among you.

10 Ye shall fall by the sword: I will judge you in the <sup>t</sup>border of Israel; and ye shall know that I *am* the LORD.

11 This *city* shall not be your caldron, neither shall ye be the flesh in the midst thereof; *but* I will judge you in the border of Israel:

12 And ye shall <sup>u</sup>know that I *am* the LORD: for ye have not walked in my statutes, neither executed my judgments, but have done <sup>x</sup>after the manners of the heathen that *are* round about you.

13 And it came to pass, when I <sup>y</sup>prophesied, that Pelatiah, the son of Benaiah, <sup>z</sup>died: then fell I <sup>a</sup>down upon my face, and cried with a loud voice, and said, Ah Lord GOD! wilt thou make a full end of the remnant of Israel?

14 <sup>b</sup>Again the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

15 Son of man, thy brethren, *even* thy brethren, the men of thy kindred, and all the house of Israel wholly, *are* they <sup>c</sup>unto whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, <sup>d</sup>Get you far from the LORD; unto <sup>e</sup>us is this land given in possession.
