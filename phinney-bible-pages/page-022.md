14 ¶ And it came to pass, that, when Abram was come into Egypt, the Egyptians <sup>y</sup>beheld the woman that she *was* very fair.

15 The <sup>z</sup>princes also of Pharaoh saw her, and commended her before Pharaoh; and the woman was taken into Pharaoh's <sup>a</sup>house.

16 And he entreated Abram well for her sake: and he had sheep, and oxen, and he-asses, and men-servants, and maid-servants, and she-asses, and camels.

17 And the LORD <sup>b</sup>plagued Pharaoh and his house with great plagues, because of Sarai, Abram's wife.

18 And Pharaoh called Abram, and said, What *is* this *that* thou hast done unto me? why didst thou not tell me that she *was* thy wife?

19 Why saidst thou, She *is* my sister? so I might have taken her to me to wife: now therefore behold thy wife, take *her*, and go thy way.

20 And Pharaoh commanded *his* men concerning him: and they <sup>c</sup>sent him away, and his wife, and all that he had.

## CHAPTER XIII.

1 Abram and Lot return out of Egypt....14 God reneweth the promise to Abram....18 He removeth to Hebron, and there buildeth an altar.

AND Abram <sup>a</sup>went up out of Egypt, he, and his wife, and all that he had, and Lot with him, into the <sup>b</sup>south.

2 And Abram *was* <sup>c</sup>very rich in cattle, in silver, and in gold.

3 And he <sup>d</sup>went on his journeys from the south, even to Beth-el, unto the place where his tent had been at the beginning, between Beth-el, and Hai;

4 Unto the place of the altar, which he had <sup>e</sup>made there at the first: and there Abram called on the name of the LORD.

5 And Lot also, which went with Abram, <sup>f</sup>had flocks, and herds, and tents.

6 And the land was not able to bear them, that they might <sup>g</sup>dwell together: for their substance was great, so that they could not dwell together.

7 And there was a <sup>h</sup>strife between the herdmen of Abram's cattle and the herdmen of Lot's cattle: and the Canaanite and the Perizzite <sup>i</sup>dwelled then in the land.

8 And Abram said unto Lot, <sup>k</sup>Let there be no strife, I pray thee, between me and thee, and between my herdmen and thy herdmen: for we *be* brethren.

9 *Is* not the whole land before thee? Separate thyself, I pray thee, from me: <sup>l</sup>If *thou wilt take* the left hand, then I will go to the right; or if *thou depart* to the right hand, then I will go to the left.

10 And Lot lifted up his eyes, and beheld all the plain of Jordan, that it *was* <sup>m</sup>well watered every where, before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah, *even* as the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt, as thou comest unto Zoar.

11 Then Lot <sup>n</sup>chose him all the plain of Jordan; and Lot journeyed east: and they separated themselves the one from the other.

12 Abram dwelled in the land of Canaan, and Lot dwelled in the cities of the plain, and pitched *his* tent toward Sodom.

13 But the men of Sodom *were* wicked, <sup>o</sup>and sinners before the LORD exceedingly.

14 ¶ And the LORD said unto Abram, after that Lot was separated from him, Lift up now thine eyes, and look from the place where thou art, northward, and southward, and eastward, and westward:

15 For all the land which thou seest, to <sup>p</sup>thee will I give it, and to <sup>q</sup>thy seed <sup>r</sup>for ever.

16 And I will make thy seed as the dust of the earth: so that if a man can number the dust <sup>s</sup>of the earth, *then* shall thy seed also be numbered.

17 Arise, walk through the land, in the <sup>t</sup>length of it, and in the breadth of it; for I will give it unto thee.

18 Then Abram removed *his* tent, and came and dwelt in the plain of Mamre, which *is* in Hebron, and built there <sup>u</sup>an altar unto the LORD.

## CHAPTER XIV.

1 The battle of the kings....12 Lot is taken prisoner....18 Melchisedek blesseth Abram....20 Abram giveth him tithe.

AND it came to pass in the days of Amraphel king of <sup>a</sup>Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of <sup>b</sup>Elam, and Tidal king of nations;

2 *That these* made war with Bera king of Sodom, and with Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, and Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is <sup>c</sup>Zoar.

3 All these were joined together in the vale of Siddim, which is the <sup>d</sup>salt sea.

4 Twelve years they <sup>e</sup>served Chedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth year they <sup>f</sup>rebelled.

5 And in the fourteenth year came Chedorlaomer, and the kings that *were* with him, and smote the <sup>g</sup>Rephaims in <sup>h</sup>Ashteroth-Karnaim, and the <sup>i</sup>Zuzims in Ham, and the <sup>k</sup>Emims in Shaveh-Kiriathaim,

6 And the <sup>l</sup>Horites in their mount Seir, unto <sup>m</sup>El-paran, which *is* by the wilderness.

7 And they returned and came to En-mishpat, which *is* Kadesh, and smote all the country of the <sup>n</sup>Amalekites, and also the Amorites that dwelt in <sup>o</sup>Hazezon-tamar.

8 And there went out the king of Sodom, and the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Admah, and the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, (the same *is* Zoar;) and they joined battle with them in the vale of Siddim;

9 With Chedorlaomer the king of Elam, and with Tidal king of nations, and Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar; four kings with five.

10 And the vale of Siddim *was full of* slime-pits; and the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled, and <sup>p</sup>fell there; and they that remained fled to the mountain.

11 And they took all the <sup>q</sup>goods of Sodom and Gomorrah, and all their <sup>r</sup>victuals, and went their way.

12 And they took <sup>s</sup>Lot, Abram's brother's son, (who dwelt in Sodom,) and his goods, and departed.

13 ¶ And there came one that had escaped, and told Abram the <sup>t</sup>Hebrew; for he dwelt in the plain of Mamre the Amorite, brother of Eshcol, and brother of Aner: and these *were* confederate with Abram.

14 And when Abram heard that his <sup>u</sup>brother was taken captive, he armed his trained *servants*, <sup>w</sup>born in his own house, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued *them* unto Dan.

15 And he <sup>x</sup>divided himself against them, he and his servants, by night, and smote them, and pursued them unto Hobah, which *is* on the left hand of Damascus.

16 And he <sup>y</sup>brought back all the goods, and also brought again his brother Lot, and his goods, and the women also, and the people.

17 ¶ And the king of Sodom went out to <sup>z</sup>meet him, (after his return from the slaughter of Chedorlaomer, and of the kings that *were* with him,) at the valley of Shaveh, which *is* the <sup>a</sup>king's dale.
