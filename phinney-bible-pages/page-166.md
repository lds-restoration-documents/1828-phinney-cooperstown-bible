12 And all the cities of those kings, and all the kings of them, did Joshua take, and smote them with the edge of the sword; *and* he utterly destroyed them, <sup>p</sup>as Moses the servant of the LORD commanded.

13 But *as for* the cities that stood still in their strength, Israel burned none of them, save Hazor only; *that* did Joshua burn.

14 And all <sup>q</sup>the spoil of these cities, and the cattle, the children of Israel took for a prey unto themselves; but every man they smote with the edge of the sword, until they had destroyed them, neither left they any to breathe.

15 <sup>r</sup>As the LORD commanded Moses his servant, so did Moses command Joshua, and so did Joshua; he left nothing undone of all that the LORD commanded Moses.

16 So Joshua took all that land, the hills, and all the south country, and all the land of <sup>s</sup>Goshen, and the valley, and the plain, and the mountain of Israel, and the valley of the same;

17 *Even* from the <sup>||</sup>mount Halak, that goeth up to Seir, unto Baal-gad, in the valley of Lebanon, unto mount Hermon: and all their kings he took, and smote them, and slew them.

18 Joshua made war <sup>||</sup>a long time with all those kings.

19 There was not a city that <sup>t</sup>made peace with the children of Israel, save the Hivites, the inhabitants of Gibeon: all *other* they took in battle.

20 For it was of the LORD to <sup>u</sup>harden their hearts, that they should come against Israel in battle, that he might destroy them utterly, *and* that they might have no favour, but that he might destroy them, as the LORD commanded Moses.

21 And at that time came Joshua, and cut off the Anakims from the mountains, from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, and from all the <sup>x</sup>mountains of Judah, and from all the mountains of Israel: Joshua destroyed them utterly with their cities.

22 There was none of the Anakims left in the land of the children of Israel: only in Gaza, in Gath, and in Ashdod, there remained.

23 So Joshua took the whole land, according to all that the LORD said unto Moses: and Joshua gave it for an inheritance unto Israel, <sup>y</sup>according to their divisions by their tribes. And the land <sup>z</sup>rested from war.

## CHAPTER XII.

The one and thirty kings on the other side Jordan which Joshua smote.

NOW these *are* the kings of the land, which the children of Israel smote, and possessed their land on the other side Jordan, toward the rising of the sun; from the river <sup>a</sup>Arnon unto mount Hermon, and all the plain on the east:

2 <sup>b</sup>Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, *and* ruled from Aroer, which *is* upon the bank of the river Arnon, and from the middle of the river, and from half Gilead, unto the river <sup>c</sup>Jabbok, *which is* the border of the children of Ammon;

3 And from the plain to the sea of <sup>d</sup>Chinneroth on the east, and unto the sea of the plain, *even* the salt sea on the east, the way to <sup>e</sup>Beth-jeshimoth; and from the south, under Ashdoth-pisgah:

4 And the coast of Og king of Bashan, *which was* of the <sup>f</sup>remnant of the <sup>g</sup>giants, that dwelt at Ashtaroth and at Edrei,

5 And reigned in mount Hermon, and in <sup>h</sup>Salcah, and in all Bashan, unto the border of the <sup>i</sup>Geshurites, and the <sup>k</sup>Maachathites, and half Gilead, the border of Sihon king of Heshbon.

6 Them did Moses the servant of the LORD and the children of Israel smite: and Moses the servant of the LORD gave it *for* a possession unto the <sup>l</sup>Reubenites, and Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh.

7 And these *are* the kings of the country which Joshua and the children of Israel smote on this side Jordan on the west, from Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon even unto the <sup>m</sup>mount Halak, that goeth up to <sup>n</sup>Seir; which Joshua gave unto the tribes of Israel *for* a possession, according to their divisions:
