33 The clods of the valley shall be sweet unto him, and every man shall <sup>i</sup>draw after him, as *there are* innumerable before him.

## CHAPTER XXII.

Eliphaz sheweth that man's goodness profiteth not God.

THEN Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,

2 Can a man be <sup>a</sup>profitable unto God, as he that is wise may be profitable unto himself?

3 *Is it* any <sup>b</sup>pleasure to the Almighty that thou art righteous? or *is it* gain *to him* that thou makest thy ways perfect?

4 Will he <sup>c</sup>reprove thee for fear of thee? will he enter with thee into judgment?

5 *Is* not thy wickedness great? and thine iniquities <sup>d</sup>infinite?

6 For thou hast taken <sup>e</sup>a pledge from thy brother for nought, and stripped the naked of their clothing.

7 Thou hast not given water to the weary to drink, and thou hast <sup>f</sup>withholden bread from the hungry.

8 But *as for* the mighty man, <sup>g</sup>he had the earth; and the honourable man dwelt in it.

9 Thou hast sent widows away empty; and the <sup>h</sup>arms of the fatherless have been broken:

10 Therefore snares *are* round about thee, and <sup>i</sup>sudden fear troubleth thee;

11 Or darkness, *that* thou canst not see; and abundance of <sup>k</sup>waters cover thee.

12 *Is* not God in the height of heaven? and, behold, the <sup>l</sup>height of the stars, how high they are!

13 And thou sayest, <sup>m</sup>How doth God know? can he judge through the dark cloud?

14 <sup>n</sup>Thick clouds *are* a covering to him, that he seeth not; and he walketh in the circuit of heaven.

15 Hast thou <sup>o</sup>marked the old way which wicked men have trodden;

16 Which were cut down out of time, whose foundation was <sup>p</sup>overflown with a flood:

17 Which said unto God, Depart from us: and what can the Almighty do <sup>q</sup>for them?

18 Yet he <sup>r</sup>filled their houses with good *things:* but the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

19 The righteous see *it,* and are glad; and the innocent <sup>s</sup>laugh them to scorn.

20 Whereas our <sup>t</sup>substance is not cut down: but the remnant of them the fire consumeth.

21 Acquaint now thyself with him, and be at peace: thereby <sup>u</sup>good shall come unto thee.

22 Receive, I pray thee, the law from his mouth, and lay up his words <sup>x</sup>in thy heart.

23 If thou return to the Almighty, thou shalt be <sup>y</sup>built up, thou shalt put away iniquity far from thy tabernacles.

24 Then shalt thou lay up <sup>z</sup>gold as dust, and the *gold* of Ophir as the stones of the brooks.

25 Yea, the Almighty shall be thy <sup>a</sup>defence, and thou shalt have plenty of silver.

26 For then shalt thou have thy <sup>b</sup>delight in the Almighty, and shalt <sup>c</sup>lift up thy face unto God.

27 Thou shalt make thy prayer unto him, and he shall <sup>d</sup>hear thee, and thou shalt pay thy vows.

28 Thou shalt also decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee; and the <sup>e</sup>light shall shine upon thy ways.

29 When *men* are cast down, then thou shalt say, *There is* lifting up; and he shall save the <sup>f</sup>humble person.

30 <sup>g</sup>He shall deliver the island of the innocent; and it is delivered by the pureness of thy <sup>h</sup>hands.

## CHAPTER XXIII.

God, who is invisible, observeth our ways.

THEN Job answered and said,

2 Even to-day *is* my complaint bitter: my <sup>a</sup>stroke is heavier than my groaning.

3 Oh that I knew where I might <sup>b</sup>find him! *that* I might come *even* to his seat!

4 I would <sup>c</sup>order *my* cause before him, and fill my mouth with arguments.
