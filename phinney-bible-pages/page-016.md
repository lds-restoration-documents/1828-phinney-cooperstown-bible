## CHAPTER II.

1 The first sabbath....4 The manner of the creation....19, 20 The naming of the creatures....21 The making of woman, and institution of marriage.

THUS the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the <sup>a</sup>host of them.

2 And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he <sup>b</sup>rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had made.

3 And God <sup>c</sup>blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work which God <sup>†</sup>created and made.

4 ¶ These *are* the <sup>d</sup>generations of the heavens and of the earth when they were created, in the day that the LORD God made the earth and the heavens.

5 And every plant of the <sup>e</sup>field before it was in the earth, and every herb of the field before it grew: for the LORD God had not <sup>f</sup>caused it to rain upon the earth, and *there was* not a man to till the ground.

6 But there went up a mist from the earth, and watered the whole face of the ground.

7 And the LORD God <sup>g</sup>formed man *of* <sup>†</sup>the dust of the ground, and breathed <sup>h</sup>into his nostrils the breath of <sup>†</sup>life; and man became a living soul.

8 ¶ And the LORD God planted a <sup>i</sup>garden eastward in Eden; and there he put the man whom he had formed.

9 And out of the ground made the LORD God to grow every <sup>k</sup>tree that is pleasant to the sight, and good for food; the tree of life also in the midst of the garden, and the tree of knowledge of good and evil.

10 And a <sup>l</sup>river went out of Eden to water the garden: and from thence it was parted, and became into four heads.

11 The name of the first *is* Pison: that *is* it which compasseth the whole land of Havilah, where *there is* gold;

12 And the gold of that land *is* good: there *is* bdellium and the onyx-stone.

13 And the name of the second river *is* Gihon: the same *is* it that compasseth the whole land of <sup>†</sup>Ethiopia.

14 And the name of the third river *is* Hiddekel: that *is* it which goeth toward the east of Assyria. And the fourth river *is* Euphrates.

15 And the LORD God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden, to <sup>m</sup>dress it, and to keep it.

16 And the LORD God <sup>n</sup>commanded the man, saying, Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat:

17 But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou <sup>o</sup>shalt not eat of it: for in the day that thou eatest thereof, thou shalt <sup>†</sup>surely die.

18 ¶ And the LORD God said, *It is* <sup>p</sup>not good that the man should be alone: I will make him a help <sup>†</sup>meet for him.

19 And out of the ground the LORD God formed every beast of the field, and every fowl of the air, and <sup>q</sup>brought *them* unto Adam, to see what he would call them; and whatsoever Adam called every living creature, that *was* the name thereof.

20 And Adam <sup>†</sup>gave names to all cattle, and to the fowl of the air, and to every beast of the field: but for Adam there was not found a help meet for him.

21 ¶ And the LORD God caused a deep sleep to fall upon Adam, and he slept; and he took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh instead thereof.

22 And the rib, which the LORD God had taken from man, <sup>†</sup>made he a woman, and <sup>r</sup>brought her unto the man.

23 And Adam said, This *is* now bone of my bones, and flesh <sup>s</sup>of my flesh: she shall be called Woman, because she was taken <sup>t</sup>out of man.

24 Therefore shall a man <sup>u</sup>leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.

25 And they were both naked, the man and his wife, and were not <sup>x</sup>ashamed.

## CHAPTER III.

1 The serpent deceiveth Eve....6 Man's shameful fall....16 The punishment of mankind....22 Their casting out of paradise.

NOW the serpent was more <sup>a</sup>subtil than any beast of the field which the LORD God had made: and <sup>b</sup>he said unto the woman, <sup>†</sup>Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden?

2 And the <sup>c</sup>woman said unto the serpent, We may eat of the fruit of the trees of the garden:

3 But of the fruit of the tree which *is* in the midst of the garden, God hath said, Ye shall not eat of it, neither shall ye <sup>d</sup>touch it, lest ye die.

4 And the serpent said unto the woman, Ye shall <sup>e</sup>not surely die:

5 For God doth know, that in the day ye eat thereof, then <sup>f</sup>your eyes shall be opened; and ye shall be as gods, knowing good and evil.

6 And when the woman <sup>g</sup>saw that the tree *was* good for food, and that it *was* <sup>†</sup>pleasant to the eyes, and a tree to be desired to make *one* wise, she took of the fruit thereof, and did eat; and <sup>h</sup>gave also unto her husband with her, and he did eat.

7 And the eyes of them both were <sup>i</sup>opened, and they knew that they *were* naked: and they sewed <sup>k</sup>fig-leaves together, and made themselves <sup>||</sup>aprons.

8 And they heard the <sup>l</sup>voice of the LORD God walking in the garden in the <sup>†</sup>cool of the day: and Adam and his wife <sup>m</sup>hid themselves from the presence of the LORD God amongst the trees of the garden

9 And the LORD God called unto Adam, and said unto him, Where *art* thou?

10 And he said, I heard thy voice in the garden, and I was <sup>n</sup>afraid, because I *was* naked; and I hid myself.

11 And he said, Who told thee that thou *wast* naked? Hast thou eaten of the tree, whereof I commanded thee that thou shouldest not eat?

12 And the man said, <sup>o</sup>The woman, whom thou gavest *to be* with me, she gave me of the tree, and I did eat.

13 And the LORD God said unto the woman, What *is* this *that* thou hast done? And the woman said, <sup>p</sup>The serpent beguiled me, and I did eat.

14 And the LORD God said unto the serpent, Because thou hast done this, thou *art* <sup>q</sup>cursed above all cattle, and above every beast of the field: upon thy belly shalt thou go, and dust shalt thou eat all the days of thy life:

15 And I will put <sup>r</sup>enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed: <sup>†</sup>it shall bruise thy <sup>s</sup>head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.

16 Unto the woman he said, I will greatly multiply thy sorrow and thy conception: <sup>t</sup>in sorrow thou shalt bring forth children; and thy desire *shall be* to thy husband, and he shall <sup>u</sup>rule over thee.

17 And unto Adam he said, Because thou hast hearkened unto the voice of thy wife, and hast eaten of the tree of which I commanded thee, saying, Thou shalt not eat of it: <sup>x</sup>cursed *is* the ground for thy sake; in sorrow shalt thou eat *of* it all the days of thy life:
