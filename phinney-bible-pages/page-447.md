## CHAPTER XXXIV.

The judgments wherewith God revengeth his church.

COME near, ye nations, to hear; and hearken, ye people; let the <sup>a</sup>earth hear, and all that is therein; the world, and all things that come forth of it.

2 For the indignation of the LORD *is* upon <sup>b</sup>all nations, and *his* fury upon all their armies: <sup>c</sup>he hath utterly destroyed them, he hath delivered them to the slaughter.

3 Their slain also shall be <sup>d</sup>cast out, and their <sup>e</sup>stink shall come up out of their carcases, and the mountains shall be melted with their blood.

4 And all the <sup>f</sup>host of heaven shall be dissolved, and the heavens shall be rolled together as a scroll: and all their hosts shall fall down, as the leaf falleth off from the vine, and as a <sup>g</sup>falling *fig* from the fig-tree.

5 For my sword shall be <sup>h</sup>bathed in heaven: behold, it shall come down upon Idumea, and upon the people of my <sup>i</sup>curse, to judgment.

6 The sword of the LORD is filled with blood; it is made fat with fatness, *and* with the <sup>k</sup>blood of lambs and goats, with the fat of the kidneys of rams: for the LORD hath a sacrifice in <sup>l</sup>Bozrah, and a great slaughter in the land of Idumea.

7 And the <sup>m</sup>unicorns shall come down with them, and the bullocks with the bulls; and their land shall be soaked with blood, and their dust made fat with fatness.

8 For *it is* the <sup>n</sup>day of the LORD's vengeance, *and* the year of recompenses for the <sup>o</sup>controversy of Zion.

9 And the streams thereof shall be turned into pitch, and the dust thereof into brimstone, and the land thereof shall become <sup>p</sup>burning pitch.

10 It shall not be quenched night nor day: the smoke thereof shall go up for ever: from generation to generation it shall lie waste; none shall pass through it for ever and ever.

11 But the cormorant and the bittern shall possess it; the owl also and the raven shall dwell in it: and he shall stretch out upon it the <sup>r</sup>line of confusion, and the stones of emptiness.

12 They shall call the <sup>†</sup>nobles thereof to the kingdom, but <sup>s</sup>none *shall be* there, and all her princes shall be nothing.

13 And thorns shall come up in her palaces, <sup>t</sup>nettles and brambles in the fortresses thereof; and it shall be a habitation of dragons, *and* a court for owls.

14 The <sup>u</sup>wild beasts of the desert shall also meet with the wild beasts of the island, and the satyr shall cry to his fellow; the screech-owl also shall rest there, and find for herself a place of rest.

15 There shall the great owl make her nest, and lay, and hatch, and gather under her shadow; there shall the vultures also be gathered, every one with her mate.

16 Seek ye out of the <sup>x</sup>book of the LORD, and read; no one of these shall fail, none shall want her mate: for my mouth it hath commanded, and his <sup>y</sup>Spirit it hath gathered them.

17 And he hath cast the <sup>z</sup>lot for them, and his hand hath <sup>a</sup>divided it unto them by line: they shall possess it for ever, <sup>b</sup>from generation to generation shall they dwell therein.

## CHAPTER XXXV.

The joyful flourishing of Christ's kingdom.

THE wilderness, and the <sup>a</sup>solitary place, shall be glad for them; and the desert shall rejoice, and blossom as the rose.

2 It shall blossom abundantly, and rejoice even with joy and singing; the <sup>b</sup>glory of Lebanon shall be given unto it, the excellency of Carmel and Sharon; they shall see the glory of the LORD, *and* the excellency of our God.

3 <sup>c</sup>Strengthen ye the weak hands, and confirm the feeble knees.

4 Say to them *that are* of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not; behold, your God will come *with* <sup>d</sup>vengeance, *even* God *with* a recompense; he will come and <sup>e</sup>save you.

5 Then the eyes of the blind shall be <sup>f</sup>opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be <sup>g</sup>unstopped:

6 Then shall the lame *man* leap as a hart, and the tongue of the dumb <sup>h</sup>sing: for in the wilderness <sup>i</sup>shall waters break out, and streams in the desert.

7 And the <sup>k</sup>parched ground shall become a pool, and the thirsty land springs of water: in the habitation of dragons, where each lay, *shall be* grass, with reeds and rushes.

8 And a highway shall be there, and a way, and it shall be called, The way of holiness; the <sup>l</sup>unclean shall not pass over it; but it *shall be* for those: the wayfaring men, though <sup>m</sup>fools, shall not err *therein.*

9 No <sup>n</sup>lion shall be there, nor *any* ravenous beast shall go up thereon, it shall not be found there: but the redeemed shall walk *there.*

10 And the <sup>o</sup>ransomed of the LORD shall return, and come to Zion with songs and <sup>p</sup>everlasting joy upon their heads: they shall obtain joy and gladness, and sorrow and sighing shall flee away.

NOW it came to pass in the <sup>a</sup>fourteenth year of king Hezekiah, *that* Sennacherib king of Assyria came up against all the <sup>b</sup>defenced cities of Judah, and took them.

2 And the king of Assyria sent Rabshakeh from <sup>c</sup>Lachish to Jerusalem, unto king Hezekiah, with a <sup>†</sup>great army: and he stood by the conduit of the <sup>d</sup>upper pool, in the highway of the fuller's field.

3 Then came forth unto him Eliakim, Hilkiah's son, which *was* over the house, and Shebna the scribe, and Joah, Asaph's son, the recorder.

4 And Rabshakeh said unto them, Say ye now to Hezekiah, Thus saith the <sup>e</sup>great king, the king of Assyria, <sup>f</sup>What confidence *is* this wherein thou trustest?

5 I say, *sayest thou,* (but *they are but* vain words,) *I have* counsel and strength for war: now, on whom dost thou trust, that thou <sup>g</sup>rebellest against me?

6 Lo, thou trustest in the <sup>h</sup>staff of this broken reed, on Egypt; whereon if a man lean, it will go into his hand, and pierce it: so *is* Pharaoh king of Egypt to all that <sup>i</sup>trust in him.

7 But if thou say to me, We trust in the LORD our God: *is it* not he whose <sup>k</sup>high places and whose altars Hezekiah hath taken away, and said to Judah and to Jerusalem, Ye shall worship before <sup>l</sup>this altar?
