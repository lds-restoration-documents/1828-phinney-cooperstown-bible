**Header:** *Israel's victory over Gog.* | CHAPTER XL. | *Of Ezekiel's vision.*

4 Thou shalt <sup>d</sup>fall upon the mountains of Israel, thou, and all thy bands, and the people that *is* with thee: I will give thee unto the <sup>e</sup>ravenous birds of every sort, and *to* the beasts of the field, to be devoured.

5 Thou shalt fall upon the <sup>†</sup>open field; for I have spoken *it,* saith the Lord GOD.

6 And I will send <sup>f</sup>a fire on Magog, and among them that dwell carelessly in the <sup>g</sup>isles; and they shall know that I *am* the LORD.

7 So will I make my holy name known in the midst of my people Israel; and I will not *let them* <sup>h</sup>pollute my holy name any more; and the heathen shall know that I *am* the LORD, the Holy One in Israel.

8 Behold, <sup>i</sup>it is come, and it is done, saith the Lord GOD; this <sup>k</sup>*is* the day whereof I have spoken.

9 And they that dwell in the cities of Israel shall <sup>l</sup>go forth, and shall set on fire and burn the weapons, both the shields and the bucklers, the bows and the arrows, and the hand-staves and the spears, and they shall <sup>m</sup>burn them with fire seven years;

10 So that they shall take no wood out of the field, neither cut down *any* out of the forests; for they shall burn the weapons with fire: and they shall <sup>n</sup>spoil those that spoiled them, and rob those that robbed them, saith the Lord GOD.

11 And it shall come to pass in that day, *that* I will give unto Gog, a place there of graves in Israel, the valley of the passengers on the <sup>o</sup>east of the sea: and it shall stop the *noses* of the passengers: and there shall they bury Gog, and all his multitude; and they shall call *it,* The valley of <sup>||</sup>Hamon-gog.

12 And seven months shall the house of Israel be burying of them, that they may <sup>p</sup>cleanse the land.

13 Yea, all the people of the land shall bury *them;* and it shall be to them <sup>†</sup>a renown, the day that I shall be <sup>q</sup>glorified, saith the Lord GOD.

14 And they shall <sup>r</sup>sever out men of continual employment, passing through the land, to bury with the passengers those that remain upon the face of the earth, to cleanse it: after the end of seven months shall they search.

15 And the passengers *that* pass through the land, when *any* seeth a man's bone, then shall he set up a sign by it, till the buriers have buried it in the valley of Hamon-gog.

16 And also the name of the city *shall be* Hamonah. Thus shall they cleanse the land.

17 ¶ And, thou son of man, thus saith the Lord GOD, Speak unto every feathered fowl, and to every beast of the field, Assemble yourselves, and come; gather yourselves on every side to my <sup>s</sup>sacrifice that I do sacrifice for you, *even* a great sacrifice upon the mountains of Israel, that ye may <sup>t</sup>eat flesh, and drink blood.

18 Ye shall eat the flesh of the mighty, and drink the blood of the princes of the earth, of rams, of lambs, and of goats, of bullocks, all of them <sup>u</sup>fatlings of Bashan.

19 And ye shall eat fat till ye be full, and drink blood till ye be drunken, of my sacrifice which I have sacrificed for you.

20 Thus ye shall be filled at my table with <sup>x</sup>horses and chariots, <sup>†</sup>with mighty men, and with all men of war, saith the Lord GOD.

21 <sup>y</sup>And I will set my glory among the heathen, and all the heathen shall <sup>z</sup>see my judgment that I have executed, and <sup>a</sup>my hand that I have laid upon them.

22 So the house of Israel shall know that I *am* the LORD their God from that day and forward.

23 And the heathen shall know that the house of Israel went into captivity for their <sup>b</sup>iniquity: because they trespassed against me, <sup>c</sup>therefore hid I my face from them, and gave them into the hand of their enemies; so fell they all by the sword.

24 According to their uncleanness, and according to their transgressions, have I done unto them, and hid my face from them.

25 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, <sup>d</sup>Now will I bring again the captivity of Jacob, and have mercy upon the <sup>e</sup>whole house of Israel, and will be jealous for my holy name;

26 After that they have borne their shame, and all their <sup>f</sup>trespasses, whereby they have trespassed against me, when they dwelt safely in their land, and none made *them* afraid.

27 When I have brought them again from the people, and gathered them out of their enemies' lands, and am <sup>g</sup>sanctified in them in the sight of many nations;

28 Then shall they know that I *am* the LORD their God, which caused them to be led into captivity among the heathen: but I have <sup>h</sup>gathered them unto their own land, and have left none of them any more there.

29 Neither will I hide my face <sup>i</sup>any more from them: for I have <sup>k</sup>poured out my Spirit upon the house of Israel, saith the Lord GOD.

## CHAPTER XL.

The time, manner, and end of Ezekiel's vision.

IN the <sup>a</sup>five and twentieth year of our captivity, in the beginning of the year, in the tenth *day* of the month, in the fourteenth year <sup>b</sup>after that the city was smitten, in the self-same day the <sup>c</sup>hand of the LORD was upon me, and brought me thither.

2 In the visions of God brought he me into the land of Israel, and set me upon <sup>d</sup>a very high mountain, by which *was* as the frame of <sup>e</sup>a city on the south.

3 And he brought me thither, and, behold, *there was* <sup>f</sup>a man whose appearance *was* like the appearance of <sup>g</sup>brass, with <sup>h</sup>a line of flax in his hand, and <sup>i</sup>a measuring-reed; and he stood <sup>k</sup>in the gate.

4 And the man said unto me, Son of man, behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears, and <sup>l</sup>set thy heart upon all that I shall shew thee; for to the intent that I might shew *them* unto thee *art* thou brought hither: declare all that thou seest to the house of Israel. <!-- uncertain: "art" may be roman rather than italic -->

5 And, behold, a wall on the outside of the house round about, and in the man's hand a measuring-reed of six cubits *long,* by the cubit; and a hand-breadth: so he measured <sup>m</sup>the breadth of the building one reed; and the <sup>n</sup>height one reed.

6 Then came he unto the <sup>o</sup>gate which looketh toward the east, and went up the stairs thereof, and measured the <sup>p</sup>threshold of the gate, *which was* one reed broad; and the other threshold *of the gate, which was* one reed broad.

7 And *every* <sup>q</sup>little chamber *was* one reed long, and one reed broad; and between the little chambers *were* five cubits; and the threshold of the gate, by the porch of the gate within, *was* one reed.

8 He measured also the porch of the gate <sup>r</sup>within, one reed.
