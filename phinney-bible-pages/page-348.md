10 Which doeth great things <sup>k</sup>past finding out, yea, and wonders without number.

11 Lo, he goeth <sup>l</sup>by me, and I see *him* not; he passeth on also, but I perceive him not.

12 Behold, he taketh away, <sup>m</sup>who can hinder him? who will say unto him, What doest thou?

13 *If* God will not withdraw his anger, the <sup>n</sup>proud helpers do stoop under him.

14 How much less shall I <sup>o</sup>answer him, *and* choose out my words *to reason* with him?

15 Whom, though I were righteous, *yet* would I <sup>p</sup>not answer, *but* I would make supplication to my Judge.

16 If I <sup>q</sup>had called, and he had answered me: *yet* would I not believe that he had hearkened unto my voice.

17 For he breaketh me with a tempest, and multiplieth my wounds <sup>r</sup>without cause.

18 He will not suffer me to take my breath, but filleth me with <sup>s</sup>bitterness.

19 If *I speak* of strength, lo, *he is* <sup>t</sup>strong: and if of judgment, who shall set me a time *to plead?*

20 If I <sup>u</sup>justify myself, mine own mouth shall condemn me: *if I say,* I *am* perfect, it shall also prove me perverse.

21 *Though* I were perfect, *yet* would I <sup>x</sup>not know my soul: I would despise my life.

22 This *is* one *thing,* therefore I said *it,* He destroyeth the <sup>y</sup>perfect and the wicked.

23 If the scourge <sup>z</sup>slay suddenly, he will laugh at the trial of the innocent.

24 The earth <sup>a</sup>is given into the hand of the wicked: he covereth the faces of the judges thereof; if not, where, *and* who *is* he?

25 Now my days are swifter than a post: they flee away, they <sup>b</sup>see no good.

26 They are passed away as the swift ships; as the <sup>c</sup>eagle *that* hasteth to the prey.

27 If I say, I will forget my <sup>d</sup>complaint, I will leave off my heaviness, and comfort *myself;*

28 I am <sup>e</sup>afraid of all my sorrows, I know that thou wilt not hold me innocent.

29 *If* I be wicked, why then labour I <sup>f</sup>in vain?

30 If I <sup>g</sup>wash myself with snow water, and make my hands never so clean;

31 Yet shalt thou plunge me in the <sup>h</sup>ditch, and mine own clothes shall abhor me.

32 For *he is* not a <sup>i</sup>man, as I *am, that* I should answer him, *and* we should come together in judgment.

33 Neither is there any <sup>k</sup>days-man betwixt us, *that* might lay his hand upon us both.

34 Let him take his rod away from me, and let not his <sup>l</sup>fear terrify me;

35 <sup>m</sup>*Then* would I speak, and not fear him: but <sup>n</sup>*it is* not so with me.

## CHAPTER X.

Job expostulateth with God about his afflictions.

MY soul is <sup>a</sup>weary of my life: I will leave my complaint upon myself; I will speak in the <sup>b</sup>bitterness of my soul.

2 I will say unto God, Do not <sup>c</sup>condemn me; shew me wherefore thou contendest with me.

3 *Is it* good unto thee, that thou shouldest oppress, that thou shouldest despise the <sup>d</sup>work of thy hands, and shine upon the counsel of the wicked?

4 Hast thou <sup>e</sup>eyes of flesh? or seest thou as man seeth?

5 <sup>f</sup>*Are* thy days as the days of man? *are* thy years as man's days,

6 That thou <sup>g</sup>inquirest after mine iniquity, and searchest after my sin?

7 <sup>h</sup>Thou knowest that I am not wicked; and *there is* none that can <sup>i</sup>deliver out of thy hand.

8 Thy hands have made me, and fashioned me together round about; yet thou dost destroy me.

9 Remember, I beseech thee, that thou hast made me <sup>k</sup>as the clay; and wilt thou bring me into dust again?

10 Hast thou not <sup>l</sup>poured me out as milk, and curdled me like cheese?

11 Thou hast <sup>m</sup>clothed me with skin and flesh, and hast fenced me with bones and sinews.

12 Thou hast granted me <sup>n</sup>life and favour, and thy visitation hath preserved my spirit.

13 And these *things* hast thou <sup>o</sup>hid in thy heart: I know that this *is* with thee.

14 If I sin, then thou <sup>p</sup>markest me, and thou wilt not acquit me from mine iniquity.

15 If I be wicked, <sup>q</sup>wo unto me; and *if* I be righteous, *yet* will I not lift up my head. *I am* full of confusion; therefore <sup>r</sup>see thou mine affliction;

16 For it <sup>s</sup>increaseth. Thou huntest me as a fierce lion; and again thou shewest thyself <sup>t</sup>marvellous upon me.

17 Thou renewest thy <sup>u</sup>witnesses against me, and increasest thine indignation upon me; <sup>x</sup>changes and war *are* against me.

18 Wherefore then hast thou <sup>y</sup>brought me forth out of the womb? Oh that I had given up the ghost, and <sup>z</sup>no eye had seen me!

19 I should have been as though I had not been; I should have been carried from the womb to the grave.

20 *Are* not my <sup>a</sup>days few? cease *then, and* let me alone, that I may take comfort a little,

21 Before I go *whence* I shall <sup>b</sup>not return, *even* to the land of darkness and the shadow of death;

22 A land of <sup>c</sup>darkness, as darkness *itself: and* of the shadow of death, without any order, and *where* the light *is* as darkness.

## CHAPTER XI.

Zophar reproveth Job for justifying himself.

THEN answered Zophar the Naamathite, and said,

2 Should not the <sup>a</sup>multitude of words be answered? and should a man full of talk be justified?

3 Should thy lies make men hold their peace? and when thou <sup>b</sup>mockest, shall no man make thee ashamed?

4 For <sup>c</sup>thou hast said, My doctrine *is* pure, and I am clean in thine eyes.

5 But oh that God would speak, and open his lips against thee;

6 And that he would shew thee the <sup>d</sup>secrets of wisdom, that *they are* double to that which is! Know, therefore, that God exacteth of thee <sup>e</sup>*less* than thine iniquity *deserveth.*

7 Canst thou by searching find out God? canst thou find out the Almighty unto <sup>f</sup>perfection?

8 *It is* <sup>g</sup>as high as heaven; what canst thou do? deeper than hell; what canst thou know?

9 The measure thereof *is* longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.

10 If he cut off, and shut up, or gather together, then who can <sup>h</sup>hinder him?
