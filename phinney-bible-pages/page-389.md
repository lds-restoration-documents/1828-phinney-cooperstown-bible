2 Let <sup>d</sup>my prayer come before thee: incline thine ear unto my cry;

3 For my soul is full of troubles, and my life <sup>e</sup>draweth nigh unto the grave.

4 I am counted with them that go down into the pit: I am as a man *that hath* no strength:

5 <sup>f</sup>Free among the dead, like the slain that lie in the grave, whom thou rememberest no more: and they are cut off <sup>g</sup>from thy hand.

6 Thou hast laid me in the lowest pit, in <sup>†</sup>darkness, in the deeps.

7 Thy wrath lieth hard upon me, and thou hast afflicted *me* with <sup>h</sup>all thy waves. Selah.

8 Thou hast put away mine acquaintance <sup>i</sup>far from me; thou hast made me an abomination unto them: *I am* <sup>k</sup>shut up, and I cannot come forth.

9 Mine eye mourneth by reason of affliction: LORD, I have called daily upon thee; I have stretched out my hands unto thee.

10 Wilt thou shew wonders to the dead? shall the <sup>l</sup>dead arise *and* praise thee? Selah.

11 Shall thy <sup>m</sup>loving-kindness be declared in the grave? *or* thy faithfulness <sup>†</sup>in destruction?

12 Shall thy wonders be known in the <sup>n</sup>dark? and thy righteousness in the land of <sup>o</sup>forgetfulness?

13 But unto thee have I cried, O LORD; and in the morning shall my prayer prevent thee.

14 LORD, <sup>p</sup>why castest thou off my soul? *why* hidest thou thy face from me?

15 I *am* afflicted and ready to die from *my* youth up: *while* I suffer thy terrors I am distracted.

16 Thy <sup>†</sup>fierce wrath goeth over me; thy terrors have cut me off.

17 They came round about me daily like water, they <sup>q</sup>compassed me about together.

18 <sup>r</sup>Lover and friend hast thou put far from me, *and* mine acquaintance into darkness.

## PSALM LXXXIX.

The Psalmist praiseth God for his covenant and wonderful power.

*Maschil of* <sup>a</sup>*Ethan the Ezrahite.*

I WILL <sup>b</sup>sing of the mercies of the LORD for ever: with my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness <sup>c</sup>to all generations.

2 For I have said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy faithfulness shalt thou establish in the very <sup>d</sup>heavens.

3 I have made a covenant with my <sup>e</sup>chosen, I have sworn unto <sup>f</sup>David my servant,

4 Thy <sup>g</sup>seed will I establish for ever, and build up thy <sup>h</sup>throne to all generations. Selah.

5 And the <sup>i</sup>heavens shall praise thy wonders, O LORD; thy faithfulness also in the congregation of the saints.

6 For who in the heaven can be compared unto the LORD? *who* among the sons of the mighty can be likened unto the LORD?

7 God is <sup>k</sup>greatly to be feared in the <sup>||</sup>assembly of the saints, and to be had in reverence of all *them that are* <sup>l</sup>about him.

8 O LORD God of hosts, who *is* a strong LORD like unto thee? or to thy faithfulness round about thee?

9 Thou rulest the <sup>m</sup>raging of the sea: when the waves thereof arise, thou stillest them.

10 Thou hast broken <sup>n</sup>Rahab in pieces, as one that is slain: thou hast scattered thine enemies with thy strong arm.

11 The heavens *are* <sup>o</sup>thine, the earth also *is* thine: *as for* the world, and the fulness thereof, thou hast founded them.

12 The <sup>p</sup>north and the south thou hast created them: Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.

13 Thou hast a mighty arm: strong is thy hand, *and* high is thy right hand.

14 Justice and judgment *are* the habitation of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face.

15 Blessed *is* the people that know the <sup>q</sup>joyful sound: they shall walk, O LORD, in the <sup>r</sup>light of thy countenance.

16 In thy name shall they rejoice all the day: and in thy righteousness shall they be exalted.

17 For thou *art* the <sup>s</sup>glory of their strength; and in thy favour <sup>t</sup>our horn shall be exalted.

18 For the LORD *is* our defence; and the Holy One of Israel *is* our King.

19 Then thou spakest in vision to thy Holy One, and saidst, I have laid help upon <sup>u</sup>*one that is* mighty; I have exalted <sup>x</sup>*one* chosen out of the people.

20 I have found David my servant; with my <sup>y</sup>holy oil have I anointed him:

21 With whom <sup>z</sup>my hand shall be established; mine arm also shall strengthen him.

22 The enemy shall <sup>a</sup>not exact upon him; nor the <sup>b</sup>son of wickedness afflict him.

23 And I will <sup>c</sup>beat down his foes before his face, and plague them that hate him.

24 But my faithfulness and my mercy *shall be* with him; and in my name shall his horn be <sup>d</sup>exalted.

25 I will <sup>e</sup>set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.

26 He shall cry unto me, Thou *art* <sup>f</sup>my Father, my God, and the Rock of my salvation.

27 Also I will make him *my* <sup>g</sup>first-born, <sup>h</sup>higher than the kings of the earth.

28 My mercy will I keep for him for evermore, and my <sup>i</sup>covenant shall stand fast with him.

29 <sup>k</sup>His seed also will I make *to endure* <sup>l</sup>for ever, and <sup>m</sup>his throne as the days of heaven.

30 If <sup>n</sup>his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;

31 If they break my statutes, and keep not my commandments;

32 Then will I visit their transgression with the <sup>o</sup>rod, and their iniquity with stripes.

33 Nevertheless my loving-kindness will I <sup>p</sup>not utterly take from him, nor suffer my faithfulness to fail.

34 My covenant will I not break, <sup>q</sup>nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips.

35 Once have I sworn by <sup>r</sup>my holiness, that I will not lie unto David.

36 His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne <sup>s</sup>as the sun before me.

37 It shall be established for ever as the moon, and <sup>t</sup>*as* a faithful witness in heaven. Selah.

38 But thou hast <sup>u</sup>cast off and abhorred, thou hast been wroth with thine anointed.

39 Thou hast made void the covenant of thy servant; thou hast profaned <sup>x</sup>his crown, *by casting it* to the ground.

40 Thou hast broken down all his <sup>y</sup>hedges; thou hastbrought <!-- uncertain: printed without space, "hastbrought" --> his strong holds to ruin.

41 All that pass by the way spoil <sup>z</sup>him: he is a reproach to his neighbours.

42 <sup>a</sup>Thou hast set up the right hand of his adversaries; thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice.
