**Header:** *Of Israel's rebellion,* | PSALMS. | *and God's mercy.*

3 Blessed *are* they that keep judgment, *and* he that <sup>c</sup>doeth righteousness <sup>d</sup>at all times.

4 <sup>e</sup>Remember me, O LORD, with the favour *that thou bearest unto* thy people; O visit me with thy salvation;

5 That I may <sup>f</sup>see the good of thy chosen, that I may rejoice in the gladness of thy nation, that I may <sup>g</sup>glory with thine inheritance.

6 We have <sup>h</sup>sinned with our fathers, we have committed iniquity, we have done wickedly.

7 Our fathers understood not thy wonders in Egypt; they remembered not the multitude of thy mercies; but provoked *him* at the sea, *even* <sup>i</sup>at the Red Sea.

8 Nevertheless, he saved them <sup>k</sup>for his name's sake, that he might make his mighty power to be known.

9 He <sup>l</sup>rebuked the Red Sea also, and it was dried up: so he led them through <sup>m</sup>the depths, as through the wilderness.

10 And he saved them from the hand of him that hated *them,* and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.

11 And the waters <sup>n</sup>covered their enemies; there was not one of them left.

12 Then <sup>o</sup>believed they his words; they <sup>p</sup>sang his praise.

13 They <sup>q</sup>soon forgat his works; they waited not for his counsel;

14 But <sup>r</sup>lusted exceedingly in the wilderness, and <sup>s</sup>tempted God in the desert.

15 And he gave them their request; but sent <sup>t</sup>leanness into their soul.

16 They <sup>u</sup>envied Moses also in the camp; *and* Aaron the saint of the LORD.

17 The <sup>x</sup>earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered the company of Abiram.

18 And a fire was kindled in their company; the flame burnt up <sup>y</sup>the wicked.

19 They made a calf in <sup>z</sup>Horeb, and worshipped the molten image.

20 Thus they changed their <sup>a</sup>glory into the similitude of <sup>b</sup>an ox that eateth grass.

21 They forgat God their saviour, which had done great things in Egypt;

22 Wondrous works in the land of Ham, *and* terrible things by the Red Sea.

23 Therefore <sup>c</sup>he said that he would destroy them, had not Moses his chosen stood before him in the <sup>d</sup>breach, to turn away his wrath, lest he should destroy *them.*

24 Yea, they despised the <sup>e</sup>pleasant land; they <sup>f</sup>believed not his word;

25 But <sup>g</sup>murmured in their tents, *and* hearkened not unto the voice of the LORD;

26 Therefore <sup>h</sup>he lifted up his hand against them, to overthrow them in the wilderness:

27 To overthrow their seed also among the nations, and to <sup>i</sup>scatter them in the lands.

28 They joined themselves also unto <sup>k</sup>Baal-peor, and ate the <sup>l</sup>sacrifices of the <sup>m</sup>dead.

29 Thus they provoked *him* to anger with their inventions; and the plague brake in upon them.

30 Then stood up <sup>n</sup>Phinehas, and executed judgment: and *so* the plague was stayed.

31 And that was counted unto him <sup>o</sup>for righteousness, unto all generations for evermore.

32 They angered *him* also at the waters of strife, so that it went <sup>p</sup>ill with Moses for their sakes:

33 Because they <sup>q</sup>provoked his spirit, so that he spake <sup>r</sup>unadvisedly with his lips.

34 They did <sup>s</sup>not destroy the nations, concerning whom the LORD commanded them:

35 But were <sup>t</sup>mingled among the heathen, and learned their works.

36 And they served their idols; which were <sup>u</sup>a snare unto them.

37 Yea, they sacrificed their sons and their daughters unto devils,

38 And shed innocent blood, *even* the blood of their sons, and of their daughters, whom they sacrificed unto the <sup>x</sup>idols of Canaan: and the land was polluted with blood.

39 Thus were they defiled with their own works, and went <sup>y</sup>a whoring with their own inventions.

40 Therefore was the wrath of the LORD kindled against his people, insomuch that he abhorred his own inheritance.

41 And <sup>z</sup>he gave them into the hand of the heathen; and they that hated them ruled over them.

42 Their enemies also oppressed them, and they were brought into subjection under their hand.

43 <sup>a</sup>Many times did he deliver them; but they provoked *him* with their counsel, and were brought low for their iniquity.

44 Nevertheless, <sup>b</sup>he regarded their affliction, when he heard their cry:

45 And he <sup>c</sup>remembered for them his covenant, and repented according to the multitude of his mercies.

46 He made them also <sup>d</sup>to be pitied of all those that carried them captives.

47 Save us, O LORD our God, and gather us from among the heathen, to give thanks unto thy holy <sup>e</sup>name, *and* to triumph in thy praise.

48 Blessed *be* the LORD God of Israel from everlasting to everlasting: and let all the people say, Amen. Praise ye the LORD.

## PSALM CVII.

The Psalmist exhorteth, in praising God, to observe his providence.

O GIVE thanks unto the LORD, for *he is* <sup>a</sup>good; for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

2 Let the <sup>b</sup>redeemed of the LORD say so, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy;

3 And <sup>c</sup>gathered them out of the lands, from the east, and from the west, from the north, and from the south.

4 They wandered in the <sup>d</sup>wilderness in a solitary way; they found no city to dwell in.

5 <sup>e</sup>Hungry and thirsty, their soul <sup>f</sup>fainted in them.

6 Then they <sup>g</sup>cried unto the LORD in their trouble, *and* he delivered them out of their distresses.

7 And he <sup>h</sup>led them forth by the right way, that they might go to a <sup>i</sup>city of habitation.

8 Oh that *men* would praise the LORD *for* his goodness, and *for* his wonderful works to the children of men!

9 For he <sup>k</sup>satisfieth the longing soul, and filleth the hungry soul with goodness.

10 Such as sit in darkness, and in the <sup>l</sup>shadow of death, *being* bound in <sup>m</sup>affliction and iron;

11 Because they rebelled <sup>n</sup>against the words of God, and <sup>o</sup>contemned the counsel of the Most High;

12 Therefore he brought down their heart with labour; they fell down, and *there was* none to help.

13 Then they <sup>p</sup>cried unto the LORD in their trouble, *and* he saved them out of their distresses.
