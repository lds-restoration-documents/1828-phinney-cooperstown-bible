7 They are all <sup>m</sup>hot as an oven, and have devoured their judges: all their <sup>n</sup>kings are fallen; *there is* none among them that <sup>o</sup>calleth unto me.

8 Ephraim, he hath <sup>p</sup>mixed himself among the people; Ephraim is a cake not turned.

9 Strangers have <sup>q</sup>devoured his strength, and he knoweth *it* not; yea, gray hairs are here and there upon him, yet he <sup>r</sup>knoweth not.

10 And the <sup>s</sup>pride of Israel testifieth to his face; and they do not return to the LORD their God, nor seek him <sup>t</sup>for all this.

11 Ephraim also is like <sup>u</sup>a silly dove without heart: they call <sup>x</sup>to Egypt, they go to Assyria:

12 When they shall go, I will spread my <sup>y</sup>net upon them; I will bring them down as the fowls of the heaven; I will chastise them as their congregation <sup>z</sup>hath heard.

13 Wo unto them! for they have fled from me: destruction unto them! because they have transgressed against me: though I have <sup>a</sup>redeemed them, yet they have <sup>b</sup>spoken lies against me.

14 And they have <sup>c</sup>not cried unto me with their heart, when they howled upon their <sup>d</sup>beds: they assemble themselves for corn and wine, *and* they rebel against me.

15 Though I have <sup>e</sup>bound *and* strengthened their arms, yet do they imagine mischief <sup>f</sup>against me.

16 They return, *but* <sup>g</sup>not to the Most High; they are like a deceitful bow: their princes shall fall by the sword for the <sup>h</sup>rage of their tongue. This *shall be* their derision in the land of Egypt.

## CHAPTER VIII.

Destruction threatened for Israel's impiety, and idolatry.

SET the trumpet to thy mouth: <sup>a</sup>*he shall come* as an eagle against the house of the LORD, because they have transgressed <sup>b</sup>my covenant, and trespassed against my law.

2 Israel shall <sup>c</sup>cry unto me, My God, <sup>d</sup>we know thee.

3 Israel hath cast off *the thing that is* <sup>e</sup>good: the enemy shall pursue him.

4 They have <sup>f</sup>set up kings, but not by me; they have made princes, and I <sup>g</sup>knew *it* not: of their silver and their gold have they <sup>h</sup>made them idols, that they may be cut off.

5 <sup>i</sup>Thy calf, O Samaria, hath cast *thee* off; mine anger is kindled against them; <sup>k</sup>how long *will it be* ere they attain to innocency?

6 For from Israel *was* it also: the workman made it; therefore it *is* <sup>l</sup>not God: but the calf of Samaria shall be broken in pieces.

7 For they have <sup>m</sup>sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind: it hath no stalk; the bud shall yield no meal: if so be it yield, the <sup>n</sup>strangers shall swallow it up.

8 Israel is <sup>o</sup>swallowed up: now shall they be among the Gentiles as a vessel wherein *is* <sup>p</sup>no pleasure.

9 For they are gone up <sup>q</sup>to Assyria, a wild ass alone by himself: Ephraim hath <sup>r</sup>hired lovers.

10 Yea, though they have hired among the nations, now will I <sup>s</sup>gather them, and they shall sorrow a <sup>t</sup>little for the burden of the king of princes.

11 Because Ephraim hath made many altars <sup>u</sup>to sin, altars shall be unto him to sin.

12 I have written <sup>x</sup>to him the great things of my law, *but* they were counted as a strange thing.

13 They sacrifice flesh *for* the sacrifices of mine offerings, and eat *it; but* the LORD <sup>y</sup>accepteth them not: now will he remember their iniquity, and visit their sins: they shall return <sup>z</sup>to Egypt.

14 For Israel hath forgotten his <sup>a</sup>Maker, and buildeth <sup>b</sup>temples; and Judah hath multiplied fenced cities: but I will send a fire upon his cities, and it shall devour the palaces thereof.

## CHAPTER IX.

The distress and captivity of Israel for sin and idolatry.

REJOICE not, O Israel, for joy, <sup>a</sup>as *other* people: for thou hast gone a whoring from thy God; thou hast loved <sup>b</sup>a reward upon every corn-floor.

2 The floor and the wine-press shall not feed them, and the new wine shall fail in her.

3 They shall not dwell in <sup>c</sup>the LORD's land; but Ephraim shall return to Egypt, and they shall eat <sup>d</sup>unclean *things* in Assyria.

4 They shall not offer wine-*offerings* to the LORD, neither shall they be <sup>e</sup>pleasing unto him: their sacrifices *shall be* unto them as the bread of mourners; all that eat thereof shall be polluted: for their bread <sup>f</sup>for their soul shall not come into the house of the LORD.

5 What will ye do in the <sup>g</sup>solemn day, and in the day of the feast of the LORD?

6 For, lo, they are gone because of destruction: Egypt shall gather them up, Memphis shall bury them: the pleasant *places* for their silver, <sup>h</sup>nettles shall possess them: <sup>i</sup>thorns *shall be* in their tabernacles.

7 The days of visitation are come, the days of recompense are come; Israel shall know *it:* the <sup>k</sup>prophet *is* a fool, the <sup>l</sup>spiritual man *is* mad, for the multitude of thine iniquity, and the great hatred.

8 <sup>m</sup>The watchman of Ephraim *was* with my God: *but* the prophet *is* <sup>n</sup>a snare of a fowler in all his ways, *and* hatred in the house of his God.

9 They have deeply corrupted *themselves,* as in the days of <sup>o</sup>Gibeah: *therefore* he will remember their iniquity, he will visit their sins.

10 I found Israel like <sup>p</sup>grapes in the wilderness; I saw your fathers as the first ripe in the fig-tree at her first time: *but* they went to <sup>q</sup>Baal-peor and separated themselves unto *that* shame; and *their* abominations were according as they loved.

11 *As for* Ephraim, <sup>r</sup>their glory shall fly away like a bird, from the birth, and from the womb, and from the conception.

12 Though they bring up their children, yet will I <sup>s</sup>bereave them, *that there shall* not *be* a man *left:* yea, wo also to them when <sup>t</sup>I depart from them!

13 Ephraim, as I saw Tyrus, *is* planted in a pleasant place: but Ephraim shall bring forth his children to the <sup>u</sup>murderer.

14 <sup>x</sup>Give them, O LORD: what wilt thou give? give them <sup>y</sup>a miscarrying womb and dry breasts.

15 All their wickedness *is* in <sup>z</sup>Gilgal; for there I hated them: for the wickedness of their doings I will drive them out of <sup>a</sup>my house, I will love them no more: all their princes *are* revolters.

16 Ephraim is smitten, their <sup>b</sup>root is dried up, they shall bear no fruit; yea, though they bring forth, yet will I slay *even* the beloved *fruit* of their womb.

17 My God will cast them away, because they did <sup>c</sup>not hearken unto him: and they shall be <sup>d</sup>wanderers among the nations.
