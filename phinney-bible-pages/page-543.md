## CHAPTER III.

Nebuchadnezzar setteth up a golden image.

NEBUCHADNEZZAR <sup>a</sup>the king made <sup>b</sup>an image of gold, whose height *was* <sup>c</sup>threescore cubits, *and* the breadth thereof six cubits: he set it up in the <sup>d</sup>plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon.

2 Then Nebuchadnezzar the king sent <sup>e</sup>to gather together the princes, the governors, and the captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces, to come to the <sup>f</sup>dedication of the image which Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.

3 Then the <sup>g</sup>princes, the governors, and captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces, were gathered together unto the dedication of the image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up; and they <sup>h</sup>stood before the image that Nebuchadnezzar had set up.

4 Then a herald <sup>i</sup>cried aloud, To you it is <sup>k</sup>commanded, O people, nations, and languages,

5 *That* at what time ye hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, dulcimer, and <sup>l</sup>all kinds of music, ye <sup>m</sup>fall down and worship the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king hath set up;

6 And whoso falleth not down and worshippeth, shall the <sup>n</sup>same hour be cast into the midst of a <sup>o</sup>burning fiery furnace.

7 Therefore at that time, when all <sup>p</sup>the people heard the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and all kinds of music, all the people, the nations, and the languages, fell down *and* worshipped the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.

8 Wherefore <sup>q</sup>at that time certain Chaldeans came near and <sup>r</sup>accused the Jews.

9 They spake, and said <sup>s</sup>to the king Nebuchadnezzar, O king, <sup>t</sup>live for ever.

10 Thou, O king, hast made a <sup>u</sup>decree, that every man that shall hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and dulcimer, and all kinds of music, shall fall down and worship the golden image:

11 And <sup>x</sup>whoso falleth not down and worshippeth, *that* he should be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace.

12 There are <sup>y</sup>certain Jews, whom thou hast set over the affairs of the province of Babylon, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego: <sup>z</sup>these men, O king, have <sup>a</sup>not regarded thee; they serve not thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up.

13 Then Nebuchadnezzar, in *his* <sup>b</sup>rage and fury, commanded to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego. Then they <sup>c</sup>brought these men before the king.

14 Nebuchadnezzar spake, and said unto them, <sup>d</sup>*Is it* true, O Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, do not ye serve <sup>e</sup>my gods, nor worship the golden image which I have set up?

15 Now, if ye be ready, that at what time ye hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and dulcimer, and all kinds of music, ye fall down and worship the image which I have made, *well:* but if ye worship not, ye shall be cast the same hour into the midst of a burning fiery furnace; and <sup>f</sup>who *is* that God that shall deliver you out of my hands?

16 Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, <sup>g</sup>answered and said to the king, O Nebuchadnezzar, <sup>h</sup>we *are* not careful to answer thee <sup>i</sup>in this matter.

17 If it be so, our God, whom we serve, <sup>k</sup>is able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace; and <sup>l</sup>he will deliver *us* out of thy hand, O king.

18 But if <sup>m</sup>not, be it <sup>n</sup>known unto thee, O king, <sup>o</sup>that we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up.

19 Then was Nebuchadnezzar <sup>p</sup>full of fury, and the form of his visage was changed against Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego: *therefore* he spake, and commanded that they should heat the furnace one <sup>q</sup>seven times more than it was wont to be heated.

20 And he commanded the most mighty men that *were* in his army to <sup>r</sup>bind Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, *and* to cast *them* into the burning fiery furnace.

21 Then these men were bound in their coats, their hosen, and their hats, and their *other* garments, and <sup>s</sup>were cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.

22 Therefore, because the king's commandment was urgent, and the furnace exceeding hot, the flame of the fire <sup>t</sup>slew those men that took up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego.

23 And these three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, <sup>u</sup>fell down bound into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.

24 Then Nebuchadnezzar the king was <sup>x</sup>astonished, and rose up in haste, *and* spake and said unto his counsellors, Did not we cast three men bound into the midst of the fire? They answered and said unto the king, True, O king.

25 He answered and said, Lo, I see four men loose, walking in the midst of the fire, and they have <sup>y</sup>no hurt; and the form of the fourth is like <sup>||</sup>the <sup>z</sup>Son of God.

26 Then Nebuchadnezzar came near to the mouth of the burning fiery furnace, *and* spake, and said, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, <sup>a</sup>ye servants of the most high God, <sup>b</sup>come forth, and come *hither.* Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, came forth of the midst of the fire.
