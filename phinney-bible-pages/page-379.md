## PSALM LVIII.

1 David reproveth wicked judges, 3 describeth the wicked.

*To the chief Musician, Al-taschith, Michtam of David.*

DO ye indeed <sup>a</sup>speak righteousness, O congregation? do ye judge uprightly, O ye sons of men?

2 Yea, in heart you work wickedness; you <sup>b</sup>weigh the violence of your hands <sup>c</sup>in the earth.

3 The wicked are <sup>d</sup>estranged from the womb; they go astray as soon as they be born, speaking lies.

4 Their <sup>e</sup>poison *is* like the poison of a serpent; *they are* like the deaf adder *that* <sup>f</sup>stoppeth her ear;

5 Which will not hearken to the voice of <sup>g</sup>charmers, charming never so wisely.

6 Break their <sup>h</sup>teeth, O God, in their mouth; break out the great teeth of the young lions, O LORD.

7 Let them melt away as waters *which* run continually: *when* he bendeth, *his bow to shoot* his arrows, let them be as <sup>i</sup>cut in pieces.

8 As a snail *which* melteth, let *every one of them* <sup>k</sup>pass away; *like* the <sup>l</sup>untimely birth of a woman, *that* they may not see the sun.

9 Before your pots can feel the thorns, he shall take them away as with <sup>m</sup>a whirlwind, both living, and in *his* wrath.

10 The righteous shall <sup>n</sup>rejoice when he seeth the vengeance: he shall wash his feet in the <sup>o</sup>blood of the wicked.

11 So that a man shall say, Verily *there is* <sup>p</sup>a reward for the righteous: verily he is a God that judgeth in the earth.

## PSALM LIX.

David, praying to be delivered from his enemies, trusteth in God.

*To the chief Musician, Al-taschith, Michtam of David; when Saul sent, and they watched the house* <sup>a</sup>*to kill him.*

DELIVER me from mine enemies, O my God: defend me from them that rise up against me.

2 Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from bloody men.

3 For, lo, they lie <sup>b</sup>in wait for my soul; the mighty are gathered against me; <sup>c</sup>not *for* my transgression, nor *for* my sin, O LORD.

4 They run and prepare themselves without *my* fault: <sup>d</sup>awake to help me, and behold.

5 Thou, therefore, O LORD God of hosts, the God of Israel, awake to <sup>e</sup>visit all the heathen: be not merciful to any <sup>f</sup>wicked transgressors. Selah.

6 They <sup>g</sup>return at evening: they make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city.

7 Behold, they belch out with their mouth: swords *are* in their lips; for <sup>i</sup>who, *say they*, doth hear?

8 But thou, O LORD, shalt <sup>k</sup>laugh at them; thou shalt have all the heathen in derision.

9 *Because of* his <sup>l</sup>strength will I <sup>m</sup>wait upon thee: for God *is* my defence.

10 <sup>n</sup>The God of my mercy shall <sup>o</sup>prevent me: God shall let me see *my desire* upon mine enemies.

11 Slay them not, lest my <sup>p</sup>people forget: scatter them by thy power; and bring them down, O LORD our shield.

12 *For* the sin of their <sup>q</sup>mouth, *and* the words of their lips, let them even be <sup>r</sup>taken in their pride; and for <sup>s</sup>cursing and lying *which* they speak.

13 Consume *them* in wrath, consume *them*, that they *may* not *be;* and let them <sup>t</sup>know that God ruleth in Jacob unto the ends of the earth. Selah.

14 And at evening let them return, *and* let them make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city.

15 Let them <sup>u</sup>wander up and down for meat, and <sup>x</sup>grudge, if they be not satisfied

16 But I will sing of thy power; yea, I will <sup>y</sup>sing aloud of thy mercy in the <sup>z</sup>morning: for thou hast been my defence and refuge in the day of my trouble.

17 Unto thee, O my strength, I will sing: for God *is* my defence, *and* the God of my mercy.

## PSALM LX.

David complaineth to God of former judgment.

*To the chief Musician upon Shushan-eduth, Michtam of David, to teach; when he strove with* <sup>a</sup>*Aram-naharaim and with* <sup>b</sup>*Aram-zobah, when Joab returned and smote of* <sup>c</sup>*Edom in the valley of Salt twelve thousand.*

O God, thou hast cast us off, thou hast <sup>d</sup>scattered us, thou hast been displeased; O turn thyself to us again.

2 Thou hast made <sup>e</sup>the earth to tremble; thou hast broken it: <sup>f</sup>heal the breaches thereof; for it shaketh.

3 Thou hast shewed thy people <sup>g</sup>hard things; thou hast made us to drink the <sup>h</sup>wine of astonishment.

4 Thou hast given <sup>i</sup>a banner to them that fear thee, that it may be displayed because of the truth. Selah.

5 That thy beloved may be delivered, save *with* <sup>k</sup>thy right hand, and hear me.

6 God hath spoken in his holiness; <sup>l</sup>I will rejoice: I will <sup>m</sup>divide <sup>n</sup>Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.

7 Gilead *is* mine, and Manasseh *is* mine; Ephraim also *is* the strength of my head; Judah *is* <sup>o</sup>my law-giver;

8 Moab *is* my <sup>p</sup>wash-pot; over Edom will I cast out <sup>q</sup>my shoe: Philistia, triumph thou because of me.

9 Who will bring me *into* the <sup>r</sup>strong city? who will lead me into Edom?

10 *Wilt* not thou, O God, *which* <sup>s</sup>hadst cast us off? and *thou*, O God, *which* didst not go out with our armies?

11 Give us help from trouble: for <sup>t</sup>vain *is* the help of man.

12 Through God we shall do <sup>u</sup>valiantly: for he *it is that* shall tread down our enemies.

## PSALM LXI.

David fleeth to God upon his former experience.

*To the chief Musician upon Neginah. A Psalm of David.*

HEAR my <sup>a</sup>cry O God; attend unto my prayer.

2 From the end of the <sup>b</sup>earth will I cry unto thee, when my heart is <sup>c</sup>overwhelmed: lead me to the Rock *that* is <sup>d</sup>higher than I.

3 For <sup>e</sup>thou hast been a shelter for me, *and* <sup>f</sup>a strong tower from the enemy.

4 I will abide in thy tabernacle for ever; I will trust in the <sup>g</sup>covert of thy wings. Selah.

5 For thou, O God, hast heard <sup>h</sup>my vows: thou hast given *me* the <sup>i</sup>heritage of those that fear thy name.

6 Thou wilt <sup>k</sup>prolong the king's life; *and* his years as <sup>l</sup>many generations.

7 He shall <sup>m</sup>abide before God for ever: O prepare <sup>n</sup>mercy and truth, *which* may preserve him.

8 So will I sing praise unto thy name for ever, that I may daily perform my vows.
