## CHAPTER XXIV.

The division of the sons of Aaron by lot into four and twenty orders.

NOW *these are* the divisions of the sons of Aaron. <sup>a</sup>The sons of Aaron; Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

2 But <sup>b</sup>Nadab and Abihu died <sup>c</sup>before their father, and had <sup>d</sup>no children: therefore Eleazar and Ithamar executed the priest's office.

3 And David distributed them, both <sup>e</sup>Zadok of the sons of Eleazar, and <sup>f</sup>Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their <sup>g</sup>offices in their service.

4 And there were more chief men found of the sons of Eleazar than of the sons of Ithamar; and *thus* were they divided: among the sons of Eleazar *there were* sixteen chief men of the house of *their* fathers, and eight among the sons of Ithamar, according to the house of their fathers.

5 Thus were they divided <sup>h</sup>by lot, one sort with another: for the governors of the <sup>i</sup>sanctuary, and governors *of the house* of God, were of the sons of Eleazar, and of the sons of Ithamar.

6 And Shemaiah the son of Nethaneel the <sup>k</sup>scribe, *one* of the Levites, wrote them before the king, and the princes, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the <sup>l</sup>son of Abiathar, and *before* the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites: one <sup>†</sup>principal household being taken for Eleazar, and *one* taken for Ithamar.

7 Now the first lot came forth to <sup>m</sup>Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah,

8 The third to <sup>n</sup>Harim, the fourth to Seorim,

9 The fifth to Malchijah, the sixth to Mijamin,

10 The seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to <sup>o</sup>Abijah,

11 The ninth to Jeshua, the tenth to Shecaniah,

12 The eleventh to Eliashib, the twelfth to Jakim,

13 The thirteenth to Huppah, the fourteenth to Jeshebeab,

14 The fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer,

15 The seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Aphses,

16 The nineteenth to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezekel,

17 The one and twentieth to Jachin, the two and twentieth to Gamul,

18 The three and twentieth to Delaiah, the four and twentieth to Maaziah.

19 These *were* the <sup>p</sup>orderings of them in their service, to come into the house of the LORD, <sup>q</sup>according to their manner, under Aaron their father, <sup>r</sup>as the LORD God of Israel had commanded him.

20 ¶ And the rest of the sons of Levi *were these:* Of the sons of Amram; <sup>s</sup>Shubael: of the sons of Shubael; Jehdeiah.

21 Concerning <sup>t</sup>Rehabiah; of the sons of Rehabiah, the first *was* Isshiah.

22 Of the Izharites; Shelomoth: of the sons of Shelomoth; Jahath.

23 And the sons *of* <sup>u</sup>Hebron; Jeriah *the first,* Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.

24 *Of* the sons of Uzziel; Michah; of the sons of Michah; Shamir.

25 The brother of Michah *was* Isshiah: of the sons of Isshiah; Zechariah.

26 ¶ The sons of Merari *were* Mahli and Mushi: the sons of Jaaziah; <sup>x</sup>Beno.

27 The sons of Merari by Jaaziah; Beno, and Shoham, and Zaccur, and Ibri.

28 Of <sup>y</sup>Mahli *came* Eleazar, who had no <sup>z</sup>sons.

29 Concerning Kish; the son of Kish *was* Jerahmeel.

30 The sons also of Mushi; Mahli, and Eder, and Jerimoth. These *were* the sons of the Levites, after the house of their fathers.

31 These likewise <sup>a</sup>cast lots over against their brethren the sons of Aaron, in the presence of David the king, and Zadok and Ahimelech, and the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites, <sup>†</sup>even the principal fathers, over against their younger brethren.

## CHAPTER XXV.

The number and offices of the singers.

MOREOVER, <sup>a</sup>David, and the <sup>b</sup>captains of the host, separated to the service of the sons of <sup>c</sup>Asaph, and of Heman, and of Jeduthun, who should <sup>d</sup>prophesy with harps, with psalteries, and with cymbals; and the number of the <sup>†</sup>workmen, according to their service, was;

2 Of the sons of Asaph; Zaccur, and Joseph, and Nethaniah, and <sup>||</sup>Asarelah, the sons of Asaph, under the hands of Asaph, which <sup>e</sup>prophesied <sup>†</sup>according to the order of the king.

3 Of Jeduthun: the sons of Jeduthun; Gedaliah, and Zeri, and Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah, six, under the hands of their father Jeduthun, who prophesied with a harp, to give thanks and to praise the LORD.

4 Of Heman: the sons of Heman; Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, and Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, and Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, *and* Mahazioth:

5 All these *were* the sons of Heman <sup>f</sup>the king's seer in the <sup>g</sup>words of God, to <sup>h</sup>lift up the horn. And <sup>i</sup>God gave to Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

6 All these *were* under the hands of their father <sup>k</sup>for song *in* the house of the LORD, with cymbals, psalteries, and harps, for the service of the house of God, according to the king's order to Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman.

7 So the number of them with their brethren, that were instructed in the <sup>l</sup>songs of the LORD, *even* all that were cunning, *was* <sup>m</sup>two hundred fourscore and eight.

8 And they cast lots, ward against <sup>n</sup>*ward,* <sup>†</sup>as well the small as the great, the teacher as the scholar.
