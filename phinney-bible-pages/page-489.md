## CHAPTER XXXVI.

Jeremiah causeth Baruch to write his prophecy.

AND it came to pass, in the <sup>a</sup>fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, *that* this word came unto Jeremiah from the LORD, saying,

2 Take thee a roll of a book, and write therein <sup>b</sup>all the words that I have spoken unto thee against Israel, and against Judah, and against all the nations, from the day I spake unto thee, from the days of Josiah, even <sup>||</sup>unto this day.

3 <sup>c</sup>It may be that the house of Judah will hear all the evil which I purpose to do unto them; that they may return every man from his <sup>d</sup>evil way; that I may forgive their iniquity and their sin.

4 Then Jeremiah called Baruch the son of Neriah: and Baruch <sup>e</sup>wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the LORD, which he had spoken unto him, upon a roll of a book.

5 And Jeremiah commanded Baruch, saying, I *am* <sup>f</sup>shut up; I cannot go into the house of the LORD:

6 Therefore go thou, and read in the roll, which thou hast written from my mouth, the words of the LORD, in the ears of the people, in the LORD's house upon the <sup>g</sup>fasting-day: and also thou shalt read them in the ears of all Judah that come out of their cities.

7 It may be they will <sup>h</sup>present their supplication before the LORD, and will <sup>i</sup>return every one from his evil way: for great *is* the anger and the fury that the LORD hath pronounced against this people.

8 And Baruch the son of Neriah did according to all that Jeremiah the prophet commanded him, reading in the book the words of the LORD in the LORD's house.

9 And it came to pass, in the fifth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, in the ninth month, *that* they proclaimed <sup>k</sup>a fast before the LORD to all the people in Jerusalem, and to all the people that came from the cities of Judah unto Jerusalem.

10 Then read Baruch in the book the words of Jeremiah in the house of the LORD, in the <sup>l</sup>chamber of Gemariah the son of Shaphan <sup>m</sup>the scribe, in the <sup>n</sup>higher court, at the entry of the <sup>o</sup>new gate of the LORD's house, in the ears of all the people.

11 When Michaiah the son of Gemariah, the son of Shaphan, had heard out of the book all the words of the LORD,

12 Then he went down into the king's house, into the <sup>p</sup>scribe's chamber, and, lo, all the princes sat there, *even* Elishama the scribe, and Delaiah the son of Shemaiah, and Elnathan the son of Achbor, and Gemariah the son of Shaphan, and Zedekiah the son of Hananiah, and all the princes.

13 Then Michaiah <sup>q</sup>declared unto them all the words that he had heard, when Baruch read the book in the ears of the people.

14 Therefore all the princes sent Jehudi the son of Nethaniah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Cushi, unto Baruch, saying, Take in thy hand the roll wherein thou hast read in the ears of the people, and come. So Baruch the son of Neriah <sup>r</sup>took the roll in his hand, and came unto them.

15 And they said unto him, Sit down now, and read it in our ears. So Baruch read *it* <sup>s</sup>in their ears.

16 Now it came to pass, when they had heard all the words, they were <sup>t</sup>afraid both one and other, and said unto Baruch, We will surely tell the king of all these words.

17 And they asked Baruch, saying, <sup>u</sup>Tell us now, How didst thou write all these words at his mouth?

18 Then Baruch answered them, He pronounced all these words unto me with his mouth, and I <sup>x</sup>wrote *them* with ink in the book.

19 Then said the princes unto Baruch, Go hide thee, <sup>y</sup>thou and Jeremiah, and let no man know where ye be.

20 And they went in to the king into the court, but they <sup>z</sup>laid up the roll in the chamber of Elishama the scribe, and told all the words in the ears of the king.

21 So the king sent Jehudi to fetch the roll; and he took it out of Elishama the scribe's chamber: and Jehudi read it in the ears of the king, and in the ears of all the princes which stood beside the king.

22 Now the king sat in the <sup>a</sup>winter-house, in the ninth month: and *there was a fire* on the hearth burning before him.

23 And it came to pass, *that* when Jehudi had read three or four leaves, he <sup>b</sup>cut it with the pen-knife, and cast *it* into the fire that *was* on the hearth, until all the roll was consumed in the fire that *was* on the hearth.

24 Yet they were not afraid, nor <sup>c</sup>rent their garments, *neither* the king, nor any of his servants that heard all these words.

25 Nevertheless, Elnathan, and Delaiah, and Gemariah, had <sup>d</sup>made intercession to the king that he would not burn the roll: but he would not hear them.
