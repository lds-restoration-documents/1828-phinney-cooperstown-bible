## CHAPTER III.

1 The necessity of God's judgment against Israel; 9 the publication of it, with the causes thereof.

HEAR <sup>a</sup>this word that the LORD hath spoken against you, O children of Israel, against the <sup>b</sup>whole family which I brought up from the land of Egypt, saying,

2 <sup>c</sup>You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore I will <sup>d</sup>punish you for all your iniquities.

3 Can <sup>e</sup>two walk together except they be agreed?

4 Will a lion <sup>f</sup>roar in the forest when he hath no prey? will a young lion cry out of his den if he have taken nothing?

5 Can <sup>g</sup>a bird fall in a snare upon the earth where no gin *is* for him? shall *one* take up a snare from the earth, and have taken nothing at all?

6 Shall a trumpet be blown in the city, and the people <sup>h</sup>not be afraid? shall there be evil in a city, and the LORD hath <sup>i</sup>not done *it?*

7 Surely the Lord GOD will do nothing, but he <sup>k</sup>revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets.

8 The lion hath roared, who will not fear? the Lord GOD hath spoken, <sup>l</sup>who can but prophesy?

9 <sup>m</sup>Publish in the palaces at Ashdod, and in the palaces in the land of Egypt, and say, Assemble yourselves upon the mountains of Samaria, and behold the great tumults in the midst thereof, and the oppressed in the midst thereof.

10 For they <sup>n</sup>know not to do right, saith the LORD, who <sup>o</sup>store up violence and robbery in their palaces.

11 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, <sup>p</sup>An adversary *there shall be* even round about the land; and he shall bring down thy <sup>q</sup>strength from thee, and thy palaces shall be spoiled.

12 Thus saith the LORD, As the shepherd taketh out of the mouth of the lion two legs, or a piece of an ear; <sup>r</sup>so shall the children of Israel be taken out that dwell in Samaria in the corner of a bed, and in Damascus *in* a couch.

13 Hear ye, and testify in the house of Jacob, <sup>s</sup>saith the Lord GOD, the God of hosts,

14 That in the day that I shall visit the transgressions of Israel upon him, I will also visit the <sup>t</sup>altars of Beth-el; and the horns of the altar shall be cut off, and fall to the ground.

15 And I will smite the <sup>u</sup>winter-house with the summer-house; and the houses of ivory shall perish, and the great houses shall have an end, saith the LORD

## CHAPTER IV.

1 Israel reproved for oppression, 4 for idolatry, 6 and for their incorrigibleness.

HEAR this word, ye <sup>a</sup>kine of Bashan, that *are* in the mountain of Samaria, which <sup>b</sup>oppress the poor, which crush the needy, which say to their masters, Bring, and let us drink.

2 The Lord GOD hath sworn by his holiness, that, lo, the days shall come upon you, that he will <sup>c</sup>take you away with hooks, and your posterity with fish-hooks.

3 And ye shall go out at the <sup>d</sup>breaches, every *cow at that which is* before her; and ye shall cast *them* into the palace, saith the LORD.

4 Come to Beth-el and <sup>e</sup>transgress; at Gilgal multiply transgression; and bring your sacrifices every morning, *and* your tithes after <sup>f</sup>three years;

5 And offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving with leaven, and proclaim *and* publish the free-offerings; for <sup>g</sup>this liketh you, O ye children of Israel, saith the Lord GOD.

6 And I also have given you <sup>h</sup>cleanness of teeth in all your cities, and want of bread in all your places; yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the LORD.

7 And also I have withholden the rain from you, when *there were* yet three months to the harvest: and I caused it to rain upon one city, and <sup>i</sup>caused it not to rain upon another city: one piece was rained upon, and the piece whereupon it rained not withered.

8 So two *or* three cities <sup>k</sup>wandered unto one city, to drink water; but they were not satisfied: yet have ye <sup>l</sup>not returned unto me, saith the LORD.

9 I have smitten you with <sup>m</sup>blasting and mildew: when your gardens, and your vineyards, and your fig-trees, and your olive-trees increased, the <sup>n</sup>palmer-worm devoured *them:* yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the LORD.
