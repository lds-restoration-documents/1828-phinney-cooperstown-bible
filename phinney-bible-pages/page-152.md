## CHAPTER XXVIII.

1 The blessings for obedience....15 The curses for disobedience.

AND it shall come to pass, if thou shalt <sup>a</sup>hearken diligently unto the voice of the LORD thy God, to observe *and* to do all his commandments which I command thee this day, that the LORD thy God will <sup>b</sup>set thee on high above all nations of the earth:

2 And all these blessings shall come on thee, and <sup>c</sup>overtake thee, if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of the LORD thy God.

3 <sup>d</sup>Blessed *shalt* thou *be* in the city, and blessed *shalt* thou *be* in the field.

4 Blessed *shall be* the <sup>e</sup>fruit of thy body, and the fruit of thy ground, and the fruit of thy cattle, the increase of thy kine, and the flocks of thy sheep.

5 Blessed *shall be* thy <sup>f</sup>basket and thy store.

6 Blessed *shalt* thou *be* when thou <sup>g</sup>comest in, and blessed *shalt* thou *be* when thou goest out.

7 The LORD shall cause thine enemies that rise up against thee to be <sup>h</sup>smitten before thy face: they shall come out against thee one way, and <sup>i</sup>flee before thee <sup>k</sup>seven ways.

8 The LORD shall <sup>l</sup>command the blessing upon thee in thy <sup>m</sup>storehouses, and in all that thou <sup>n</sup>settest thine hand unto; and he shall bless thee in the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.

9 The LORD shall <sup>o</sup>establish thee a holy people unto himself, as he hath sworn unto thee, if thou shalt keep the commandments of the LORD thy God, and walk in his ways.

10 And all people of the earth shall see that <sup>p</sup>thou art called by the name of the LORD; and they shall be <sup>q</sup>afraid of thee.

11 And the LORD shall make thee plenteous in goods, in the fruit of thy body, and in the fruit of thy cattle, and in the fruit of thy ground, in the land which the LORD sware unto thy fathers to give thee.

12 The LORD shall open unto thee his good <sup>r</sup>treasure, the heaven to give the rain unto thy land in his <sup>s</sup>season, and to bless all the work of thine hand: and thou shalt <sup>t</sup>lend unto many nations, and thou shalt not borrow.

13 And the LORD shall make thee the <sup>u</sup>head, and not the tail: and thou shalt be <sup>x</sup>above only, and thou shalt not be beneath; if that thou hearken unto the commandments of the LORD thy God, which I command thee this day, to observe and to do *them*:

14 And thou shalt not go aside from any of the words which I command thee this day, *to* the right hand or *to* the left, to go after other gods to serve them.

15 But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt <sup>y</sup>not hearken unto the voice of the LORD thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day, that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee.

16 <sup>z</sup>Cursed *shalt* thou *be* in the city, and cursed *shalt* thou *be* in the field.

17 Cursed *shall be* thy basket and thy <sup>a</sup>store.

18 Cursed *shall be* the <sup>b</sup>fruit of thy body, and the fruit of thy land, the increase of thy kine, and the flocks of thy sheep.

19 Cursed *shalt* thou *be* when thou <sup>c</sup>comest in, and cursed *shalt* thou *be* when thou goest out.

20 The LORD shall send upon thee <sup>d</sup>cursing, <sup>e</sup>vexation, and <sup>f</sup>rebuke, in all that thou settest thine hand unto for to do, until thou be destroyed, and until thou perish quickly; because of the wickedness of thy doings, whereby thou hast forsaken me.

21 The LORD shall make the <sup>g</sup>pestilence cleave unto thee, until he have consumed thee from off the land whither thou goest to possess it.

22 The LORD shall smite thee with a <sup>h</sup>consumption, and with a fever, and with an inflammation, and with an extreme burning, and with <sup>||</sup>the sword, and with <sup>i</sup>blasting, and with <sup>k</sup>mildew; and they shall pursue thee until thou perish.

23 And the <sup>l</sup>heaven that *is* over thy head shall be brass, and the earth that *is* under thee *shall be* iron.

24 The LORD shall make the rain of thy land <sup>m</sup>powder and dust: from heaven shall it come down upon thee, until thou be destroyed.

25 The LORD shall cause thee to be smitten before thine enemies: thou shalt go out one way against them, and flee seven ways before them; and shalt be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth.

26 And thy <sup>n</sup>carcase shall be meat unto all fowls of the air, and unto the beasts of the earth, and no man shall fray *them* away.

27 The LORD will smite thee with the <sup>o</sup>botch of Egypt, and with <sup>p</sup>the emerods, and with the scab, and with the itch, whereof thou <sup>q</sup>canst not be healed.

28 The LORD shall smite thee with <sup>r</sup>madness, and <sup>s</sup>blindness, and <sup>t</sup>astonishment of heart:

29 And thou shalt <sup>u</sup>grope at noon-day, as the blind gropeth in darkness, and thou shalt not prosper in thy ways: and thou shalt be only oppressed and spoiled evermore, and no man shall <sup>x</sup>save *thee*.

30 Thou shalt betroth a wife, and another man shall <sup>y</sup>lie with her: thou shalt build a house, and thou shalt not dwell therein: thou shalt plant a vineyard, and shalt not <sup>†</sup>gather the grapes thereof.

31 Thine ox *shall be* slain before thine eyes, and thou shalt not eat thereof: thine ass *shall be* violently taken away from before thy face, and shall not be restored to thee: thy sheep *shall be* given unto thine enemies, and thou shalt have none to rescue *them*.

32 Thy sons and thy daughters *shall be* given unto another people, and thine eyes shall look, and <sup>z</sup>fail *with longing* for them all the day long: and *there shall be* no <sup>a</sup>might in thine hand.

33 The fruit of thy land, and all thy labours, shall a nation which thou knowest not <sup>b</sup>eat up; and thou shalt be only oppressed and crushed always:

34 So that thou shalt be mad for the <sup>c</sup>sight of thine eyes which thou shalt see.

35 The LORD shall smite thee in the knees, and in the legs, with <sup>d</sup>a sore botch that cannot be healed, from the sole of thy foot unto the top of thy head.

36 The LORD shall <sup>e</sup>bring thee, and <sup>f</sup>thy king which thou shalt set over thee, unto a nation which neither thou nor thy fathers have known; and there shalt thou serve <sup>g</sup>other gods, wood and stone.

37 And thou shalt become <sup>h</sup>an astonishment, a proverb, and a by-word, among all nations whither the LORD shall lead thee.

38 Thou shalt carry much seed out into the field, and shalt gather *but* little in; for the <sup>i</sup>locust shall consume it.

39 Thou shalt plant vineyards, and dress *them*, but shalt neither drink *of* the wine, nor gather *the grapes*; for the <sup>k</sup>worms shall eat them.

40 Thou shalt have olive-trees throughout all thy coasts, but thou shalt <sup>l</sup>not anoint *thyself* with the oil; for thine olive shall cast *his fruit*.
