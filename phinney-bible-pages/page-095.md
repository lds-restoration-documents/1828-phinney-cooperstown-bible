19 Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy <sup>e</sup>cattle gender with a diverse kind. Thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed; neither shall a garment mingled of linen and <sup>f</sup>woollen come upon thee.

20 And whosoever lieth <sup>g</sup>carnally with a woman that *is* a bondmaid, betrothed to a husband, and not at all redeemed, nor freedom given her, she shall be scourged: they shall not be put to death, because she was <sup>h</sup>not free.

21 And he shall bring his trespass-offering unto the LORD, unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, *even* a ram for a trespass-offering.

22 And the priest shall make an atonement for him, with the ram of the trespass-offering, before the LORD, for his sin which he hath done: and the sin which he hath done shall be forgiven him.

23 And when ye shall come into the land, and shall have planted all manner of trees for food, then ye shall count the fruit thereof as uncircumcised: three years shall it be as uncircumcised unto you; it shall not be eaten of.

24 But in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be <sup>i</sup>holy, to praise the LORD *withal*.

25 And in the fifth year shall ye eat of the fruit thereof, that it may yield unto you the <sup>k</sup>increase thereof: I *am* the LORD your God.

26 Ye shall not eat *any thing* <sup>l</sup>with the blood; neither shall ye use <sup>m</sup>enchantment, nor <sup>n</sup>observe times.

27 Ye shall not <sup>o</sup>round the corners of your heads, neither shalt thou <sup>p</sup>mar the corners of thy beard.

28 Ye shall not make any <sup>q</sup>cuttings in your flesh for the <sup>r</sup>dead, nor <sup>s</sup>print any marks upon you: I *am* the LORD.

29 Do not prostitute thy daughter, to cause her to be <sup>t</sup>a whore; lest the land fall to whoredom, and the land become full of wickedness.

30 Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and <sup>u</sup>reverence my sanctuary: I *am* the LORD.

31 Regard not them that have <sup>x</sup>familiar spirits, neither seek after <sup>y</sup>wizards, to be defiled by them: I *am* the LORD your God.

32 Thou shalt rise up before the <sup>z</sup>hoary head, and honour the face of the <sup>a</sup>old man, and fear thy God: I *am* the LORD.

33 And if <sup>b</sup>a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not vex him.

34 *But* the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you <sup>c</sup>as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt: I *am* the LORD your God.

35 Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in <sup>d</sup>mete-yard, in weight, or in measure.

36 <sup>e</sup>Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin, shall ye have: I *am* the LORD your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt.

37 Therefore shall ye observe all my statutes, and all my judgments, and do them: I *am* the LORD.

6 And <sup>n</sup>the soul that turneth after such as have familiar spirits, and after wizards, to go a whoring after them, I will even set my face against that soul, and will cut him off from among his people.

7 <sup>o</sup>Sanctify yourselves, therefore, and be ye holy: for I *am* the LORD your God.

8 And ye shall keep my statutes, and do them: I *am* the LORD which <sup>p</sup>sanctify you.

9 For every one that <sup>q</sup>curseth his father or his mother shall be surely put <sup>r</sup>to death: he hath cursed his father or his mother; his <sup>s</sup>blood *shall be* upon him.

10 And the man that committeth adultery with *another* man's wife, *even he* that committeth adultery with his neighbour's wife, the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be <sup>t</sup>put to death.

11 And the man that lieth with his father's wife hath uncovered his father's nakedness: both of them shall be put to death; their <sup>u</sup>blood *shall be* upon them.

12 And if a man <sup>x</sup>lie with his daughter-in-law, both of them shall surely be put to death: they have wrought confusion; their blood *shall be* upon them.

13 If a man also lie with mankind <sup>y</sup>as he lieth with a woman, both of them have committed an abomination: they shall surely be put to death; their blood *shall be* upon them.

14 And if a man take a wife and her mother, it *is* <sup>z</sup>wickedness: they shall be burnt with fire, both he and they; that there be no wickedness among you.

15 And if a man lie with a beast, he shall surely be put to death; and ye shall slay the beast.

16 And if a woman approach unto any beast and lie down thereto, thou shalt kill the woman and the beast; they shall surely be put to death; their blood *shall be* upon them.

17 And if a man shall take his sister, his father's daughter, or his mother's daughter, and see her nakedness, and she see his nakedness, it *is* a <sup>a</sup>wicked thing; and they shall be cut off in the sight of their people: he hath uncovered his sister's nakedness; he shall bear his iniquity.

18 And if a man shall lie with a woman having her <sup>b</sup>sickness, and shall uncover her nakedness, he hath discovered her fountain, and she hath uncovered the <sup>c</sup>fountain of her blood; and both of them shall be <sup>d</sup>cut off from among their people.

19 And thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy mother's sister, nor of thy father's sister; for he uncovereth his near kin: they shall bear their iniquity.

20 And if a man shall lie with his <sup>e</sup>uncle's wife, he hath uncovered his uncle's nakedness: they shall bear their sin; they shall die childless.

21 And if a man shall take his brother's wife, it *is* an <sup>f</sup>unclean thing; he hath uncovered his brother's nakedness: they shall be childless.
