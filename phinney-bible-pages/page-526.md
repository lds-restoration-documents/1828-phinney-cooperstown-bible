19 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, <sup>a</sup>I will give the land of Egypt unto Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon; and he shall take her multitude, and take her spoil, and take her prey; and it shall be the wages for his army.

20 I have given him the land of Egypt *for* his <sup>b</sup>labour wherewith he served against it, because they wrought <sup>c</sup>for me, saith the Lord GOD.

## CHAPTER XXX.

The desolation of Egypt and her helpers.

THE word of the LORD came again unto me, saying,

2 Son of man, prophesy and say, Thus saith the Lord GOD, <sup>a</sup>Howl ye, <sup>b</sup>Wo worth the day!

3 For the day *is* <sup>c</sup>near, even the day of the LORD *is* near, a cloudy day; it shall be the time of the <sup>d</sup>heathen.

4 And the sword shall come upon Egypt, and great pain shall be in Ethiopia, when the slain shall fall in Egypt, and they shall take away <sup>e</sup>her multitude, and her <sup>f</sup>foundations shall be broken down.

5 Ethiopia, and Libya, and Lydia, and all the <sup>g</sup>mingled people, and Chub, and the men of the land that is in league, shall fall with them by the sword.

6 Thus saith the LORD, They also that <sup>h</sup>uphold Egypt shall fall; and the <sup>i</sup>pride of her power shall come down: from the tower of Syene shall they fall in it by the sword, saith the Lord GOD.

7 And they shall be desolate in the midst of the countries *that are* desolate, and her cities shall be in the midst of the cities *that are* wasted.

8 And they shall know that I *am* the LORD, when I have <sup>k</sup>set a fire in Egypt, and *when* all her <sup>l</sup>helpers shall be destroyed.

9 In that day shall messengers go forth from me in ships, to make the <sup>m</sup>careless Ethiopians afraid, <sup>n</sup>and great pain shall come upon them, as in the day of Egypt: for, lo, it <sup>o</sup>cometh.

10 Thus saith the Lord GOD, I will also make the multitude of Egypt to cease by the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon.

11 <sup>p</sup>He and his people with him, the terrible of the nations, shall <sup>q</sup>be brought to destroy the land: and they shall draw their swords against Egypt, and fill the land with the slain.

12 And I will make the rivers <sup>r</sup>dry, and sell the land into the hand of the <sup>s</sup>wicked; and I will make the land waste, and all that is therein, by the hand of strangers: I the LORD have spoken *it.*

13 Thus saith the Lord GOD, I will also destroy the idols, and I will cause *their* <sup>†</sup>images to cease out of Noph; and there shall be no more a prince of the land of Egypt: and I will put a fear in the land of Egypt.

14 And I will make Pathros desolate, and will set fire in Zoan, and will execute judgments in <sup>t</sup>No.

15 And I will <sup>u</sup>pour my fury upon Sin, the strength of Egypt; and I will cut off the multitude of No.

16 And I will set <sup>x</sup>fire in Egypt: Sin shall have great pain, and No shall be rent asunder, and Noph *shall have* distresses daily.

17 The young men of Aven and of Pi-beseth shall fall by the sword: and these *cities* shall go into captivity.

18 At <sup>y</sup>Tehaphnehes also the day shall be darkened, when I shall break there the yokes of Egypt: and the pomp of her strength shall cease in her: as for her, a cloud shall cover her, and her daughters shall go into captivity.

19 <sup>z</sup>Thus will I execute judgments in Egypt; and they <sup>a</sup>shall know that I *am* the LORD.

20 ¶ And it came to pass in <sup>b</sup>the eleventh year, in the first *month,* in the seventh *day* of the month, *that* the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

21 Son of man, I have <sup>c</sup>broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt; and, lo, it shall <sup>d</sup>not be bound up to be healed, to put a roller to bind it, to make it strong to hold the sword.

22 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, I *am* against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and will <sup>e</sup>break his arms, the strong, and that which was broken; and I will cause the sword to fall out of his hand.

23 And I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and will disperse them through the countries.

24 And I will <sup>f</sup>strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and put <sup>g</sup>my sword in his hand: but I will break Pharaoh's arms, and he shall <sup>h</sup>groan before him with the groanings of a deadly-wounded *man.*

25 But I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and the arms of Pharaoh shall fall down; and they shall <sup>i</sup>know that I *am* the LORD, when I shall put my sword into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall stretch it out upon the land of Egypt.

26 And I will <sup>k</sup>scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and disperse them among the countries; and they shall know that I *am* the LORD.

2 Son of man, speak unto Pharaoh king of Egypt, and to his multitude; <sup>a</sup>Whom art thou like in thy greatness?

3 Behold, the Assyrian *was* a cedar in Lebanon with fair branches, and with <sup>b</sup>a shadowing shroud, and of a high stature; and his top was among the thick boughs.

4 The <sup>c</sup>waters made him great, the deep set him up on high with her rivers running round about his plants, and sent out her <sup>d</sup>little rivers unto all the trees of the field.

5 Therefore his height was exalted <sup>e</sup>above all the trees of the field, and his boughs were multiplied, and his branches became long, because of the multitude of waters, when he shot forth.

6 All the <sup>f</sup>fowls of heaven made their nests in his boughs, and under his branches did all the beasts of the field bring forth their young, and under his <sup>g</sup>shadow dwelt all great nations.

7 Thus was he fair in his greatness, in the length of his branches: for his root was by great waters.
