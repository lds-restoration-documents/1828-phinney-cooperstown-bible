## PSALM CXIX.

This Psalm containeth sundry prayers, praises, and professions of obedience.

### ALEPH.

BLESSED *are* the <sup>a</sup>undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the LORD.

2 Blessed *are* they that keep his testimonies, *and that* <sup>b</sup>seek him with the whole heart.

3 They also <sup>c</sup>do no iniquity: they <sup>d</sup>walk in his ways.

4 Thou hast commanded *us* to keep thy precepts diligently.

5 O that my ways were <sup>e</sup>directed to keep thy statutes!

6 Then shall I <sup>f</sup>not be ashamed when I have respect unto <sup>g</sup>all thy commandments.

7 I will praise thee with uprightness of heart, when I shall have learned thy righteous judgments.

8 I will keep thy statutes: O forsake me not <sup>h</sup>utterly.

### BETH.

9 Wherewithal shall <sup>i</sup>a young man cleanse his way? By taking heed *thereto* according to <sup>k</sup>thy word.

10 With my <sup>l</sup>whole heart have I sought thee: O let me not wander from thy commandments.

11 Thy word have <sup>m</sup>I hid in my heart, that I might not sin against thee.

12 Blessed *art* thou, O LORD: <sup>n</sup>teach me thy statutes.

13 With my lips have <sup>o</sup>I declared all the judgments of thy mouth.

14 I have rejoiced in the way of thy testimonies, as *much as* in all riches.

15 <sup>p</sup>I will meditate in thy precepts, and have respect unto thy ways.

16 I will <sup>q</sup>delight myself in thy statutes: I will not forget thy word.

### GIMEL.

17 Deal bountifully with thy servant, *that* I may <sup>r</sup>live, and keep thy word.

18 Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold <sup>s</sup>wondrous things out of thy law.

19 I *am* <sup>t</sup>a stranger in the earth; hide not thy commandments from me.

20 My soul <sup>u</sup>breaketh for the longing *that it hath* unto thy judgments at all times.

21 Thou hast rebuked <sup>x</sup>the proud *that are* cursed, which do err from thy commandments.

22 Remove from me <sup>y</sup>reproach and contempt; for I have kept thy testimonies.

23 Princes also did <sup>z</sup>sit *and* <sup>a</sup>speak against me: *but* thy servant did meditate in thy statutes.

24 Thy testimonies also *are* my delight, *and* my <sup>b</sup>counsellors.

28 My soul melteth for heaviness: strengthen thou me according unto thy word.

29 Remove from me the <sup>g</sup>way of lying; and grant me thy law graciously.

30 I have chosen the <sup>h</sup>way of truth: thy judgments have I laid <sup>||</sup>*before me.*

31 I have stuck unto thy testimonies: O LORD, put me not to shame.

32 I will run the way of thy commandments, when thou <sup>i</sup>shalt enlarge my heart.
