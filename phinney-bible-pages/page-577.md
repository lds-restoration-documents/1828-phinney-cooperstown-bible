3 *There is* a voice of the howling of the <sup>d</sup>shepherds; for their <sup>e</sup>glory is spoiled: a voice of the roaring of young lions; for the pride of Jordan is spoiled.

4 Thus saith the LORD my God, <sup>f</sup>Feed the flock of the slaughter,

5 Whose <sup>g</sup>possessors slay them, and hold themselves <sup>h</sup>not guilty; and they that <sup>i</sup>sell them say, <sup>k</sup>Blessed *be* the LORD; for I am rich: and their own shepherds pity them not.

6 For I will no more <sup>l</sup>pity the inhabitants of the land, saith the LORD; but, lo, I will deliver the men every one into his neighbour's hand, and into the hand of his <sup>m</sup>king; and they shall smite the land, and out of their hand I will not deliver *them.*

7 And I will feed the flock of slaughter, *even* you, O <sup>n</sup>poor of the flock. And I took unto me two staves; the one I called <sup>o</sup>Beauty, and the other I called <sup>p</sup>Bands: and I fed the flock.

8 <sup>q</sup>Three shepherds also I cut off in one month; and my soul loathed them, and their soul also abhorred me.

9 Then <sup>r</sup>said I, I will not feed you: that that dieth, <sup>s</sup>let it die; and that that is to be cut off, let it be cut off; and let the rest eat every one the flesh of another.

10 And I took my staff, *even* Beauty, and <sup>t</sup>cut it asunder, that I might break my covenant which I had made with all the people.

11 And it was broken in that day: and so the <sup>u</sup>poor of the flock that waited upon me knew that it *was* the word of the LORD.

12 And I said unto them, If ye think good, give *me* my price; and if not, forbear. So they weighed for my price <sup>x</sup>thirty *pieces* of silver.

13 And the LORD said unto me, Cast it unto the <sup>y</sup>potter: a <sup>z</sup>goodly price that I was prized at of them. And I took the thirty *pieces* of silver, and cast them to the potter in the house of the LORD.

14 Then I cut asunder mine other staff, *even* Bands, that I might <sup>a</sup>break the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.

15 And the LORD said unto me, Take unto thee yet the instruments of a <sup>b</sup>foolish shepherd:

16 For, lo, I will raise up <sup>c</sup>a shepherd in the land, *which* shall not visit those that be cut off, neither shall seek <sup>d</sup>the young one, nor heal that that is broken, nor feed that that standeth still; but he shall eat the flesh of the fat, and tear their claws in pieces.

17 Wo to the <sup>e</sup>idle shepherd that leaveth the flock! the sword *shall be* upon his arm, and upon his right eye: his <sup>f</sup>arm shall be clean dried up, and his right eye shall be utterly darkened.

## CHAPTER XII.

6 The victorious restoring of Judah....10 The repentance of Jerusalem.

THE burden of the word of the LORD for Israel, saith the LORD, which <sup>a</sup>stretcheth forth the heavens, and layeth the foundation of the earth, and formeth the spirit of man within him.

2 Behold, I will make <sup>b</sup>Jerusalem a <sup>c</sup>cup of trembling unto all the people round about, when they shall be in the siege both against Judah *and* against Jerusalem.

3 And in that day will I make Jerusalem <sup>d</sup>a burdensome stone for all people: all that <sup>e</sup>burden themselves with it shall be cut in pieces, though all the people of the earth be gathered together against it.

4 In that day, saith the LORD, I will <sup>f</sup>smite every horse with astonishment, and his rider with madness; and I will <sup>g</sup>open mine eyes upon the house of Judah, and will smite every horse of the people with blindness.

5 And the <sup>h</sup>governors of Judah shall say in their heart, The inhabitants of Jerusalem *shall be* my strength in the LORD of hosts their God.

6 In that day will I make the governors of Judah like <sup>i</sup>a hearth of fire among the wood, and like a torch of fire in a sheaf; and they shall <sup>k</sup>devour all the people round about, on the right hand and on the left: and Jerusalem shall be inhabited again in her own place, *even* in Jerusalem.

7 The LORD also shall <sup>l</sup>save the tents of Judah first, that the <sup>m</sup>glory of the house of David, and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem do not magnify *themselves* against Judah.

8 In that day shall the LORD <sup>n</sup>defend the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and he that is <sup>o</sup>feeble among them at that day shall be as David; and the house of David *shall be* <sup>p</sup>as God, as the angel of the LORD before them.

9 And it shall come to pass in that day, *that* I will seek to destroy <sup>q</sup>all the nations that come against Jerusalem.

10 And I will pour upon the house of David, and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the <sup>r</sup>spirit of grace and of supplications; and they shall <sup>s</sup>look upon me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for him, as one mourneth for *his* only *son,* and shall be in bitterness for him, as one that is in bitterness for *his* first-born.

11 In that day shall there be a great mourning in Jerusalem, as the mourning of <sup>t</sup>Hadadrimmon in the valley of <sup>u</sup>Megiddon.

12 And the <sup>x</sup>land shall mourn, every family apart; the family of the house of David apart, and their wives <sup>y</sup>apart; the family of the house of <sup>z</sup>Nathan apart, and their wives apart;

13 The family of the house of Levi apart, and their wives apart; the family of <sup>a</sup>Shimei apart, and their wives apart;

14 All the families that remain, every family apart, and their wives apart.

## CHAPTER XIII.

1 The fountain of purgation for Jerusalem, 2 from idolatry and false prophecy....7 The death of Christ, &c.

IN that day there shall <sup>a</sup>be a fountain opened to the house of David, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, for <sup>b</sup>sin and for uncleanness.

2 And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the LORD of hosts, *that* I will cut off the <sup>c</sup>names of the idols out of the land, and they shall no more be remembered; and also I will cause the <sup>d</sup>prophets and the unclean spirit to pass out of the land.

3 And it shall come to pass, *that* when any shall yet prophesy, then his <sup>e</sup>father and his mother that begat him shall say unto him, Thou shalt not live; for thou speakest <sup>f</sup>lies in the name of the LORD: and his father and his mother that begat him shall thrust him through when he prophesieth.

4 And it shall come to pass in that day, *that* the prophets shall be <sup>g</sup>ashamed every one of his vision, when he hath prophesied; neither shall they wear <sup>h</sup>a rough garment to deceive:

5 But he shall say, I *am* no <sup>i</sup>prophet, I *am* a husbandman; for man taught me to keep cattle from my youth.
