5 And he put down the <sup>n</sup>idolatrous priests, whom the kings of Judah had ordained to burn incense in the high places in the cities of Judah, and in the places round about Jerusalem; them also that burnt incense unto Baal, to the sun, and to the moon, and to the planets, and to all the <sup>o</sup>host of heaven.

6 And he brought out the <sup>p</sup>groves from the house of the LORD, without Jerusalem, unto the brook Kidron, and burnt it at the brook Kidron, and stamped *it* small to powder, and cast the powder thereof upon the <sup>q</sup>graves of the children of the people.

7 And he brake down the houses of the <sup>r</sup>Sodomites, that *were* by the house of the LORD, where the women wove <sup>||</sup>hangings for the grove.

8 And he brought all the priests out of the cities of Judah, and defiled the <sup>†</sup>high places where the priests had burnt incense, from <sup>s</sup>Geba to Beer-sheba, and brake down the high places of the gates that *were* in the entering in of the gate of Joshua the governor of the city, which *were* on a man's left hand at the gate of the city.

9 Nevertheless the priests of the high places <sup>t</sup>came not up to the altar of the LORD in Jerusalem, but they <sup>u</sup>did eat of the unleavened bread among their brethren.

10 And he defiled <sup>x</sup>Topheth, which *is* in the valley of the children <sup>y</sup>of Hinnom, that no man might make his son or his daughter to pass through the fire to <sup>z</sup>Molech.

11 And he took away the <sup>a</sup>horses that the kings of Judah had given to the sun, at the entering in of the house of the LORD, by the chamber of Nathan-melech the chamberlain, which *was* in the suburbs, and burnt the chariots of the sun with fire.

12 And the altars that *were* on the <sup>b</sup>top of the upper chamber of Ahaz, which the kings of Judah had made, and the altars which <sup>c</sup>Manasseh had made in the two courts of the house of the LORD, did the king beat down, and brake *them* down from thence, and cast the dust of them into the brook Kidron.
