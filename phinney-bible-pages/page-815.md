**Header:** *Paul glorieth in afflictions* | CHAPTER XI....XII. | *His wonderful revelations.*

8 <sup>h</sup>I robbed other churches, taking wages *of them,* to do you service.

9 And when I was present with you, and wanted, I was chargeable to no man: for that which was lacking to me, the brethren which came from Macedonia supplied: and in all *things* I have kept myself from being burdensome unto you, and so will I keep *myself.*

10 <sup>i</sup>As the truth of Christ is in me, no man shall stop me of this boasting in the regions of Achaia.

11 <sup>k</sup>Wherefore? because I love you not? God knoweth.

12 <sup>l</sup>But what I do, that I will do, that I may cut off occasion from them which desire occasion; that wherein they glory, they may be found even as we.

13 <sup>m</sup>For such *are* false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ.

14 And no marvel; for Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light.

15 <sup>n</sup>Therefore *it is* no great thing if his ministers also be transformed as the ministers of righteousness; whose end shall be according to their works.

16 <sup>o</sup>I say again, Let no man think me a fool; if otherwise, yet as a fool receive me, that I may boast myself a little.

17 <sup>p</sup>That which I speak, I speak *it* not after the Lord, but as it were foolishly, in this confidence of boasting.

18 <sup>q</sup>Seeing that many glory after the flesh, I will glory also.

19 <sup>r</sup>For ye suffer fools gladly, seeing ye *yourselves* are wise.

20 <sup>s</sup>For ye suffer, if a man bring you into bondage, if a man devour *you,* if a man take *of you,* if a man exalt himself, if a man smite you on the face.

21 <sup>t</sup>I speak as concerning reproach, as though we had been weak. Howbeit whereinsoever any is bold, (I speak foolishly,) I am bold also.

22 <sup>u</sup>Are they Hebrews? so *am* I. Are they Israelites? so *am* I. Are they the seed of Abraham? so *am* I.

23 <sup>x</sup>Are they ministers of Christ? (I speak as a fool) I *am* more; in labours more abundant, in stripes above measure, in prisons more frequent, in deaths oft.

24 <sup>y</sup>Of the Jews five times received I forty *stripes* save one.

25 <sup>z</sup>Thrice was I beaten with rods, once was I stoned, thrice I suffered shipwreck, a night and a day I have been in the deep;

26 *In* journeyings often, *in* perils of waters, *in* perils of robbers, *in* perils by *mine own* countrymen, *in* perils by the heathen, *in* perils in the city, *in* perils in the wilderness, *in* perils in the sea, *in* perils among false brethren;

27 <sup>a</sup>In weariness and painfulness, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.

28 <sup>b</sup>Besides those things that are without, that which cometh upon me daily, the care of all the churches.

29 <sup>c</sup>Who is weak, and I am not weak? who is offended, and I burn not?

30 <sup>d</sup>If I must needs glory, I will glory of the things which concern mine infirmities.

31 <sup>e</sup>The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, which is blessed for evermore, knoweth that I lie not.

32 <sup>f</sup>In Damascus the governor under Aretas the king kept the city of the Damascenes with a garrison, desirous to apprehend me:

33 And through a window in a basket was I let down by the wall, and escaped his hands.

## CHAPTER XII.

1 He chooseth to commend his apostleship, not by revelations, but by his infirmities, blaming them for forcing his boasting.

<sup>a</sup>IT is not expedient for me doubtless to glory. I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord.

2 <sup>b</sup>I knew a man in Christ above fourteen years ago, (whether in the body I cannot tell; or whether out of the body, I cannot tell: God knoweth;) such a one caught up to the third heaven.

3 And I knew such a man, (whether in the body, or out of the body, I cannot tell: God knoweth;)

4 How that he was caught up into paradise, and heard unspeakable words, which it is not lawful for a man to utter.

5 <sup>c</sup>Of such a one will I glory: yet of myself I will not glory, but in mine infirmities.

6 <sup>d</sup>For though I would desire to glory, I shall not be a fool; for I will say the truth: but *now* I forbear, lest any man should think of me above that which he seeth me *to be,* or *that* he heareth of me.

7 <sup>e</sup>And lest I should be exalted above measure through the abundance of the revelations, there was given to me a thorn in the flesh, the messenger of Satan, to buffet me, lest I should be exalted above measure.

8 <sup>f</sup>For this thing I besought the Lord thrice, that it might depart from me.

9 <sup>g</sup>And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in mine infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.

10 <sup>h</sup>Therefore I take pleasure in infirmities, in reproaches, in necessities, in persecutions, in distresses for Christ's sake: for when I am weak, then am I strong.

11 <sup>i</sup>I am become a fool in glorying; ye have compelled me: for I ought to have been commended of you: for in nothing am I behind the very chiefest apostles, though I be nothing.

12 <sup>k</sup>Truly the signs of an apostle were wrought among you in all patience, in signs, and wonders, and mighty deeds.

13 <sup>l</sup>For what is it wherein ye were inferior to other churches, except *it be* that I myself was not burdensome to you? forgive me this wrong.

14 <sup>m</sup>Behold, the third time I am ready to come to you; and I will not be burdensome to you: for I seek not yours, but you: for the children ought not to lay up for the parents, but the parents for the children.

15 <sup>n</sup>And I will very gladly spend and be spent for you; though the more abundantly I love you, the less I be loved.

16 But be it so, I did not burden you; nevertheless, being crafty, I caught you with guile.

17 <sup>o</sup>Did I make a gain of you by any of them whom I sent unto you?

18 <sup>p</sup>I desired Titus, and with *him* I sent a brother: did Titus make a gain of you? walked we not in the same spirit? *walked we* not in the same steps?

19 <sup>q</sup>Again, think ye that we excuse ourselves unto you? we speak before God in Christ: but *we do* all things, dearly beloved, for your edifying.

20 <sup>r</sup>For I fear, lest, when I come, I shall not find you such as I would, and *that* I shall be found unto you such as ye would not: lest *there be* debates, envyings, wraths, strifes, backbitings, whisperings, swellings, tumults:
