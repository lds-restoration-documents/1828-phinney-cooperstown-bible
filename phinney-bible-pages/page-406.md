## PSALM CXLII.

David sheweth that all his comfort was in prayer unto God.

*Maschil of David; a prayer when he was in the* <sup>a</sup>*cave.*

I <sup>b</sup>CRIED unto the LORD with my voice: with my voice unto the LORD did I make my supplication.

2 <sup>c</sup>I poured out my complaint before him: I shewed before him my trouble.

3 When my spirit was overwhelmed within me, then <sup>d</sup>thou knewest my path: in the way wherein I walked have they privily laid a snare for me.

4 I looked on *my* right hand, and beheld, but *there was* no man that would know me: refuge <sup>e</sup>failed me; no man cared for my soul.

5 I cried unto thee, O LORD: I said, Thou *art* my refuge, *and* <sup>f</sup>my portion in the land of the living.

6 Attend unto my cry; for I am <sup>g</sup>||brought very low: deliver me from my persecutors: for they are stronger than I.

7 Bring my soul out of prison, that I may praise thy name: the righteous shall <sup>h</sup>compass me about; for thou shalt deal <sup>i</sup>bountifully with me.

## PSALM CXLIII.

David strengtheneth his faith by meditation and prayer.

*A Psalm of David.*

HEAR my prayer, O LORD; <sup>a</sup>give ear to my supplications: in thy faithfulness answer me, *and* in thy righteousness.

2 And <sup>b</sup>enter not into judgment with thy servant: for in thy sight shall no man living be <sup>c</sup>justified.

3 For the enemy hath persecuted my soul; he hath smitten my <sup>||</sup>life down to the ground: he hath made me to dwell in darkness, as those that have been long dead.

4 Therefore is my spirit overwhelmed within me: my heart within me is <sup>d</sup>desolate.

5 I remember the days <sup>e</sup>of old; I meditate on all thy works: I muse on <sup>f</sup>the work of thy hands.

6 I stretch forth my hands unto thee: my soul *thirsteth* after thee, as <sup>g</sup>a thirsty land. Selah.

7 Hear me speedily, O LORD; my spirit faileth: hide not thy face from me, <sup>h</sup>lest I be like unto them that go down into the pit.

8 Cause me to hear thy loving-kindness in the <sup>i</sup>morning; for in thee do I trust: <sup>k</sup>cause me to know the way wherein I should walk; for I lift up my soul unto thee.

9 Deliver me, O LORD, from mine enemies: I fly unto thee to hide me.

10 Teach me to do thy will; for thou *art* my God: thy <sup>l</sup>Spirit *is* good; lead me into the <sup>m</sup>land of uprightness.

11 <sup>n</sup>Quicken me, O LORD, for thy name's sake: for thy righteousness' sake bring my soul out of trouble.

12 And of thy mercy cut off mine enemies, and destroy all them that afflict my soul: <sup>o</sup>for I *am* thy servant.

## PSALM CXLIV.

David blesseth God for his mercy both to him and to man.

*A Psalm of David.*

BLESSED *be* the LORD <sup>a</sup>my strength, which teacheth my hands <sup>b</sup>to war, *and* my fingers to fight:

2 My goodness, and my fortress; my high tower, and my deliverer; my shield, and *he* in whom I trust; who subdueth my people under me.

3 LORD, what *is* man, that thou takest <sup>c</sup>knowledge of him? *or* the son of man, that thou makest account of him? <!-- stray superscript mark after "him?" -->

4 Man is like to <sup>d</sup>vanity: his days *are* as a shadow that passeth away.

5 Bow thy heavens, O LORD, and <sup>e</sup>come down: touch the mountains, and they shall smoke.

6 <sup>f</sup>Cast forth lightning, and scatter them: shoot out thine arrows, and <sup>†</sup>destroy them.

7 Send thy hand from above; rid me, and deliver me out of <sup>g</sup>great waters, from the hand of strange children;

8 Whose mouth <sup>h</sup>speaketh vanity; and their right hand *is* a right hand of falsehood.

9 I will sing <sup>i</sup>a new song unto thee, O God: upon a psaltery, *and* an instrument of ten strings, will I sing praises unto thee.

10 *It is he* that giveth <sup>k</sup>salvation unto kings: who delivereth David his servant from the hurtful sword.

11 Rid me, and deliver me from the hand of strange children, whose mouth speaketh vanity, and their <sup>l</sup>right hand *is* a right hand of falsehood:

12 That our sons *may be* as <sup>m</sup>plants grown up in their youth; *that* our daughters *may be* as corner-stones, polished *after* the similitude of a palace:

13 *That* our garners *may be* full, affording all manner of store; *that* our sheep may bring forth thousands and ten thousands in our streets:

14 *That* our oxen *may be* <sup>n</sup>strong to labour; *that there be* no breaking in, nor going out; that *there be* no complaining in our streets.

15 Happy *is that* people that is in such a case: *yea,* <sup>o</sup>happy *is that* people, whose God *is* the LORD.

## PSALM CXLV.

David praiseth God for his providence and mercy.

*David's Psalm* <sup>a</sup>*of praise.*

I WILL extol thee, my God, O King; and I will bless thy name <sup>b</sup>for ever and ever.

2 Every day will I bless thee, and I will praise thy name for ever and ever.

3 Great *is* the LORD, and <sup>c</sup>greatly to be praised; and his greatness *is* <sup>d</sup>unsearchable.

4 <sup>e</sup>One generation shall praise thy works to another, and shall declare thy mighty acts.

5 I will speak of the glorious honour of thy majesty, and of thy <sup>f</sup>wondrous works.

6 And *men* shall speak of the might of thy terrible acts: and I will <sup>g</sup>declare thy greatness.

7 They shall <sup>†</sup>abundantly utter the memory of thy great goodness, and shall sing of thy righteousness.

8 The LORD <sup>h</sup>*is* gracious, and full of compassion; slow to anger, and of great mercy.

9 The LORD *is* <sup>i</sup>good to all; and his tender mercies *are* over all his works.

10 <sup>k</sup>All thy works shall praise thee, O LORD; and thy saints shall <sup>l</sup>bless thee.

11 They shall speak of the glory of <sup>m</sup>thy kingdom, and talk of thy <sup>n</sup>power;

12 To make known to the sons of men his mighty acts, and the <sup>o</sup>glorious majesty of his kingdom.

13 Thy kingdom *is* <sup>p</sup>an everlasting kingdom, and thy dominion *endureth* throughout all generations.

14 The LORD upholdeth all that fall, and <sup>q</sup>raiseth up all *those that be* bowed down.

15 The eyes of all wait upon thee; and thou givest them their meat <sup>r</sup>in due season.

16 Thou openest thy hand, and satisfiest the <sup>s</sup>desire of every living thing.

17 The LORD *is* righteous <sup>t</sup>in all his ways, and holy in all his works.
