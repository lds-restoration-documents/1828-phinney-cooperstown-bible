## CHAPTER XXIV.

By a boiling pot, is shewed Jerusalem's destruction.

3 And utter a parable unto the rebellious house, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD, Set on <sup>c</sup>a pot, set *it* on, and also pour water into it:

4 <sup>d</sup>Gather the pieces thereof into it, *even* every <sup>e</sup>good piece, the thigh, and the shoulder; fill *it* with the choice bones.

8 That it might cause fury to come up <sup>m</sup>to take vengeance; I have set her blood upon the top of a rock, that it should not be covered.

9 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, Wo to the bloody city! I will even make the pile for fire great.

10 Heap on wood, kindle the fire, consume the flesh, and spice <sup>n</sup>it well, and let the bones be burned.

11 Then set it empty upon the coals thereof, that the brass of it may be hot, and may burn, and *that* the <sup>o</sup>filthiness of it may be molten in it, *that* the scum of it may be consumed.

God's vengeance on the nations on account of the Jews.

3 And say unto the Ammonites, Hear the word of the Lord GOD; Thus saith the Lord GOD, Because thou saidst, <sup>c</sup>Aha, against my sanctuary, when it was profaned; and against the land of Israel, when it was desolate; and against the house of Judah, when they went into captivity:

4 Behold, therefore, I will deliver thee to the <sup>d</sup>men of the east for a possession, and they shall <sup>e</sup>set their palaces in thee, and make their dwellings in thee: they shall eat thy fruit, and they <sup>f</sup>shall drink thy milk.
