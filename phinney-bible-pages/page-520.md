9 In thee are men that <sup>l</sup>carry tales to shed blood; and in thee they <sup>m</sup>eat upon the mountains; in the midst of thee they commit <sup>n</sup>lewdness:

11 And one hath <sup>p</sup>committed abomination with his neighbour's wife; and another hath lewdly defiled his daughter-in-law; and another in thee hath humbled his sister, his father's daughter.

12 In thee have they <sup>q</sup>taken gifts to shed blood; thou hast taken usury and increase, and thou hast greedily gained of thy neighbours by extortion, and hast <sup>r</sup>forgotten me, saith the Lord GOD.

13 Behold, therefore, I have <sup>s</sup>smitten my hand at thy dishonest gain which thou hast made, and at thy <sup>t</sup>blood which hath been in the midst of thee.

15 And I will <sup>y</sup>scatter thee among the heathen, and disperse thee in the countries, and will consume thy filthiness out of thee.

16 And thou shalt <sup>z</sup>take thine inheritance in thyself in the sight of the heathen, and thou shalt <sup>a</sup>know that I *am* the LORD.

17 ¶ And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

19 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, Because ye are all become dross, behold, therefore, I will <sup>d</sup>gather you into the midst of Jerusalem.

20 *As* they gather silver, and brass, and iron, and lead, and tin, into the midst of the furnace, to blow the fire upon it, <sup>e</sup>to melt *it;* so will I gather *you* in mine anger and in my fury, and I will leave *you there,* and melt you.

21 Yea, I will gather you, and <sup>f</sup>blow upon you in the fire of my wrath, and ye shall be melted in the midst thereof.

22 As silver is melted in the midst of the furnace, so shall ye be melted in the midst thereof; and ye shall know that I the LORD have <sup>g</sup>poured out my fury upon you.

23 ¶ And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

24 Son of man, say unto her, Thou *art* the land that is <sup>h</sup>not cleansed, nor <sup>i</sup>rained upon in the <sup>k</sup>day of indignation.

26 Her priests have <sup>n</sup>violated my law, and have profaned my holy things: they have put no difference <sup>o</sup>between the holy and profane, neither have they shewed *difference* between the unclean and the clean, and have hid their eyes from my sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.

27 Her princes in the midst thereof *are* like <sup>p</sup>wolves ravening the prey, to shed blood, *and* to destroy souls, to get dishonest gain.

28 And her prophets have <sup>q</sup>daubed them with untempered *mortar,* seeing vanity, and divining lies unto them, saying, <sup>r</sup>Thus saith the Lord GOD, when the LORD hath not spoken.

29 The people of the land have used <sup>s</sup>oppression, and <sup>t</sup>exercised robbery, and have vexed the poor and needy; yea, they have oppressed the stranger wrongfully.

30 And I <sup>u</sup>sought for a man among them that should make up the hedge, and stand in the <sup>x</sup>gap before me for the land, that I should not destroy it: but I found none.

31 Therefore have I poured out mine indignation upon them; I have consumed them with the fire of my wrath: their own way have I recompensed upon their heads, saith the Lord GOD.
