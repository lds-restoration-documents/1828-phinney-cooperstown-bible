5 ¶ Then <sup>h</sup>Adonijah the son of Haggith <sup>i</sup>exalted himself, saying, <sup>k</sup>I will be king: and he prepared him chariots and horsemen, and fifty men to run before him.

6 And his father had not displeased him at any time in saying, Why hast thou done so? and he <sup>l</sup>also *was a* very goodly *man;* and *his mother* bare him after Absalom.

7 And he <sup>m</sup>conferred with Joab the son of Zeruiah, and with <sup>n</sup>Abiathar the priest: and they, following Adonijah, helped *him.*

8 But Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and Nathan the prophet, and Shimei, and Rei, and the <sup>o</sup>mighty men which *belonged* to David, were not with Adonijah.

9 And Adonijah <sup>p</sup>slew sheep and oxen and fat cattle by the stone of Zoheleth, which *is* by En-rogel, and called all his brethren the king's sons, and all the men of Judah the king's servants:

10 But Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah, and the mighty men, and Solomon his brother, he called not.

11 Wherefore Nathan <sup>q</sup>spake unto Bath-sheba the mother of Solomon, saying, Hast thou not heard that Adonijah the son of Haggith doth reign, and David our lord knoweth *it* not?

12 Now therefore come, <sup>†</sup>let me, I pray thee, give thee counsel, that thou mayest <sup>†</sup>save thine own life, and the life of thy son Solomon.

13 Go and get thee in unto king David, and say unto him, Didst not thou, my lord, O king, <sup>r</sup>swear unto thine handmaid, saying, Assuredly Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall <sup>s</sup>sit upon my throne? why then doth Adonijah reign?

14 Behold, while thou yet talkest there with the king, I also will come in after thee, and <sup>†</sup>confirm thy words.

15 And Bath-sheba went in unto the king into the chamber: and the king was very old; and Abishag the Shunammite ministered unto the king.

16 And Bath-sheba bowed, and did obeisance unto the king. And the king said, What wouldest thou?

17 And she said unto him, My lord, thou <sup>t</sup>swarest by the LORD <sup>u</sup>thy God unto thine handmaid, *saying,* Assuredly <sup>x</sup>Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne:

18 And now, behold, Adonijah reigneth; and now, my lord the king, thou knowest *it* not:

19 And he hath slain oxen and fat cattle and sheep in abundance, and hath called all the sons of the king, and Abiathar the priest, and Joab the captain of the host: but Solomon thy servant hath he not called.

20 And thou, my lord, O king, the eyes of all Israel *are* upon thee, that thou shouldest tell them who shall sit on the throne of my lord the king after him.

21 Otherwise it shall come to pass, when my lord the king shall <sup>†</sup>sleep with <sup>y</sup>his fathers, that I and my son Solomon shall be counted <sup>†</sup>offenders.

22 And, lo, while she yet talked with the king, Nathan the prophet also came in.

23 And they told the king, saying, Behold, Nathan the prophet. And when he was come in before the king, he bowed himself before the king with his face to the ground.

24 And Nathan said, My lord, O king, hast thou said, Adonijah shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne?

25 For he is gone down this day, and hath slain oxen and fat cattle and sheep in abundance, and hath called all the king's sons, and the captains of the host, and Abiathar the priest: and, behold, they eat and drink before him, and say, <sup>†</sup>God save king Adonijah.

26 But me, *even* me thy servant, and Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and thy servant Solomon, hath he not called.

27 Is this thing done by my lord the king, and thou <sup>z</sup>hast not shewed *it* unto thy servant, who should sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?

28 Then king David answered and said, Call me Bath-sheba. And she came into the king's presence, and stood before the king.

29 And the king sware, and said, <sup>†</sup>*As* the LORD liveth, that hath redeemed my soul <sup>a</sup>out of all distress,

30 Even as I sware unto thee by the LORD God of Israel, saying, Assuredly Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne in my stead; even so will I certainly do this day.

31 Then Bath-sheba bowed with *her* face to the earth, and did reverence to the king, and said, Let my lord king David live <sup>b</sup>for ever.

32 And king David said, Call me Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada. And they came before the king.

33 The king also said unto them, Take with you the servants of your lord, and cause Solomon my son to ride upon <sup>c</sup>mine own mule, and bring him down to <sup>d</sup>Gihon:

34 And let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet <sup>e</sup>anoint him there king over Israel: and <sup>f</sup>blow ye with the trumpet, and say, <sup>†</sup>God save king Solomon.

35 Then ye shall come up after him, that he may come and sit upon my throne; for he shall be king in my stead: and I have <sup>g</sup>appointed him to be ruler over Israel and over <sup>h</sup>Judah.

36 And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada answered the king, and said, <sup>i</sup>Amen: the LORD God of my lord the king <sup>k</sup>say so *too.*

37 As the LORD hath been with my lord the king, even so be he with Solomon, and make his throne <sup>l</sup>greater than the throne of my lord king David.

38 So Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites, and the Pelethites, went down, and caused Solomon to ride upon king David's mule, and brought him to Gihon.

39 And Zadok the priest took a horn of oil <sup>m</sup>out of the tabernacle, and <sup>||</sup>anointed Solomon. And they blew the trumpet; and all the people said, God save king Solomon.

40 And all the people came up after him; and the people piped with pipes, and <sup>n</sup>rejoiced with great joy, so that the earth <sup>o</sup>rent with the sound of them.

41 And Adonijah, and all the guests that *were* with him, heard *it,* as they had made <sup>p</sup>an end of eating. And when Joab heard the sound of the trumpet, <sup>q</sup>he said, Wherefore *is this* noise of the city being in an uproar?
