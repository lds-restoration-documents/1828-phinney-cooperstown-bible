# ¶ The WISDOM of SOLOMON.

## CHAPTER I.

2 To whom God sheweth himself, and wisdom herself.....6 An evil speaker cannot lie hid.....12 We procure our own destruction: for God created not death.

LOVE <sup>a</sup>righteousness, ye that be judges of the earth: think of the Lord with a good (heart,) and in simplicity of heart seek him.

2 For he will be found of them <sup>b</sup>that tempt him not; and sheweth himself unto such as do not <sup>c</sup>distrust him.

3 For froward thoughts separate from God: and his power, when it is tried, <sup>||</sup>reproveth the unwise.

4 For into a malicious soul wisdom shall not enter; nor dwell in the body that is subject unto sin.

5 <sup>d</sup>For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and <sup>||</sup>will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in.

6 For wisdom is a <sup>e</sup>loving spirit; and will not acquit a blasphemer of his <sup>||</sup>words; for God is witness of his reins, and a true beholder of his heart, and a hearer of his tongue.

7 For the Spirit of the Lord filleth the world: and that which <sup>||</sup>containeth all things, hath knowledge of the voice.

8 Therefore he that speaketh unrighteous things <sup>f</sup>cannot be hid: neither shall vengeance, when it punisheth, pass by him.

9 For inquisition shall be made into the counsels of the ungodly: and the sound of his words shall come unto the Lord for the <sup>||</sup>manifestation of his wicked deeds. <!-- a small mark follows "ungodly" in the scan; not clearly a reference letter -->

10 For the ear of jealousy heareth all things: and the noise of murmurings is not hid.

11 Therefore beware of <sup>g</sup>murmuring, which is unprofitable; and refrain your tongue from backbiting: for there is no word so secret, that shall go for nought: and the mouth that <sup>||</sup>belieth, slayeth the soul.

12 Seek not death in the error of your life: and pull not upon yourselves <sup>h</sup>destruction with the works of your hands.

13 For God made not death: <sup>i</sup>neither hath he pleasure in the destruction of the living.

14 For <sup>k</sup>he created all things, that they might have their being: and the generations of the world were healthful: and there is no poison of destruction in them, nor the kingdom of death upon the earth:

15 (For righteousness is immortal:)

16 <sup>l</sup>But ungodly men with their works and words called *it* to them: for when they thought to have it their friend, they consumed to nought, and made a covenant with it, because they are worthy to take part with it.

## CHAPTER II.

1 The wicked think this life short, and of no other after this.....6 Therefore they will take their pleasure in this, and conspire against the just.....21 What that is which doth blind them.

FOR the *ungodly* said, reasoning with themselves, but not aright, <sup>a</sup>Our life is short and tedious, <sup>b</sup>and in the death of a man there is no remedy: neither was there any man known to have returned from the grave.

2 For we are born at all adventure: and we shall be hereafter as though we had never been: for the breath in our nostrils is as smoke, and a little spark in the moving of our heart:

3 Which being extinguished, <sup>c</sup>our body shall be turned into ashes, and our spirit shall vanish as the <sup>||</sup>soft air,

4 And our name shall be forgotten in time, and no man shall have our works in remembrance, and our life shall pass away as the trace of a cloud, and shall be dispersed as the mist that is driven away with the beams of the sun, and <sup>||</sup>overcome with the heat thereof.

5 <sup>d</sup>For our time is a very shadow that passeth away; and after our end there is no returning: for <sup>||</sup>it is fast sealed, so that no man cometh again.

6 Come on therefore, let us enjoy the good things <sup>e</sup>that are present: and let us <sup>||</sup>speedily use the creatures like as in youth.

7 Let us fill ourselves with costly wine and ointments: and let no flower of the spring pass by us:

8 Let us crown ourselves with rose-buds, <sup>f</sup>before they be withered.

9 Let none of us go without his part of our <sup>||</sup>voluptuousness: let us leave tokens of our joyfulness in every place: <sup>g</sup>for this is our portion, and our lot is this.

10 <sup>h</sup>Let us oppress the poor righteous man, let us not spare the widow, nor reverence the ancient gray hairs of the aged.

11 Let our strength be the law of justice: for that which is feeble is found to be nothing worth.

12 Therefore let us lie in wait for the righteous; because he is not for our turn, and he is clean contrary to our doings: he upbraideth us with our offending the law, and objecteth to our infamy the transgressions of our education. <!-- uncertain: superscript mark for "i Ps. 10, 8." appears only as a dot before the verse number -->

13 <sup>k</sup>He professeth to have the knowledge of God: and he calleth himself the child of the Lord.

14 He was made to <sup>l</sup>reprove our thoughts.

15 <sup>m</sup>He is grievous unto us even to behold: for his life is not like other men's, his ways are of another fashion.

16 We are esteemed of him as <sup>||</sup>counterfeits: he abstaineth from our ways as from filthiness: he pronounceth <sup>n</sup>the end of the just to be blessed, and maketh his boast that God is his father.

17 Let us see if his words be true: and let us prove what shall happen in the end of him.

18 For if the just man be the <sup>o</sup>son of God, he will help him, and deliver him from the hand of his enemies.

19 Let us <sup>p</sup>examine him with despitefulness and torture, that we may know his meekness, and prove his patience.

20 Let us condemn him with a shameful death: for by his own saying he shall be respected.

21 Such things they did imagine, <sup>q</sup>and were deceived: for their own wickedness hath blinded them.

22 As for the mysteries of God, <sup>r</sup>they knew them not: neither hoped they for the wages of righteousness, nor <sup>†</sup>discerned a reward for blameless souls.

23 For God created man to be immortal, and made him to be an <sup>s</sup>image of his own eternity.

24 <sup>t</sup>Nevertheless, through envy of the devil came death into the world: and they that do hold of his side do find it.

## CHAPTER III.

1 The godly are happy in their death, and in their troubles: the wicked are not, nor their children.....13 But they that are pure, are happy, though they have no children: for the adulterer and his seed shall perish.

BUT <sup>a</sup>the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and there shall no torment touch them.

2 <sup>b</sup>In the sight of the unwise they seemed to die: and their departure is taken for misery,

3 And their going from us to the utter destruction: but they are in peace.

4 For though they be punished in the sight of men, yet is their <sup>c</sup>hope full of immortality.

5 And having been a little chastised, they shall be greatly <sup>||</sup>rewarded: for God <sup>d</sup>proved them, and found them <sup>||</sup>worthy for himself.

6 <sup>e</sup>As gold in the furnace hath he tried them, and received them as a burnt-offering.
