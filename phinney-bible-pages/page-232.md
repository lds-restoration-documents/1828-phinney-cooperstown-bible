13 When he was removed out of the highway, all the people went on after Joab, to pursue after Sheba the son of Bichri.

14 And he went through all the tribes of Israel unto Abel, and to <sup>s</sup>Beth-maachah, and all the <sup>t</sup>Berites; and they were gathered together, and went also after him.

18 Then she spake, saying, They were wont to speak in old time, saying, They shall surely <sup>x</sup>ask *counsel* at Abel: and so they ended *the matter.*

## CHAPTER XXI.

The three years' famine for the Gibeonites cease.

THEN there was <sup>a</sup>a famine in the days of David <sup>b</sup>three years, year after year; and David <sup>c</sup>inquired of the LORD. And the LORD answered, *It is* for Saul, and for *his* <sup>d</sup>bloody house, because he slew the Gibeonites.

2 And the king called the Gibeonites, and said unto them; (now the Gibeonites *were* not of the children of Israel, but of the <sup>e</sup>remnant of the Amorites; and the children of Israel had sworn unto them; and Saul sought to slay them <sup>f</sup>in his zeal to the children of Israel and Judah;)

3 Wherefore David said unto the Gibeonites, What shall I do for you? and wherewith shall I make the atonement, that <sup>g</sup>ye may bless the inheritance of the LORD?

4 And the Gibeonites said unto him, We will have <sup>h</sup>no silver nor gold of Saul, nor of his house; neither for us shalt thou kill any man in Israel. And he said, What you shall say, *that* will I do for you.

5 And they answered the king, The man that consumed us, and that <sup>i</sup>devised against us *that* we should be destroyed from remaining in any of the coasts of Israel,

6 Let <sup>k</sup>seven men of his sons be delivered unto us, and we will hang them up <sup>l</sup>unto the LORD in Gibeah of Saul, *whom* the LORD did choose. And the king said, I will give *them.*

7 But the king spared Mephibosheth, the son of Jonathan the son of Saul, because of <sup>m</sup>the LORD's oath that *was* between them, between David and Jonathan the son of Saul.

8 But the king took the two sons of <sup>n</sup>Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, whom she bare unto Saul, Armoni and Mephibosheth; and the five sons of Michal the daughter of Saul, whom she brought up for Adriel the son of Barzillai the <sup>o</sup>Meholathite;

9 And he delivered them into the hands of the Gibeonites, and they hanged them in the <sup>p</sup>hill <sup>q</sup>before the LORD: and they fell *all* seven together, and were put to death in the days of harvest, in the first *days,* in the beginning of <sup>r</sup>barley-harvest.

10 And Rizpah the daughter of Aiah took sackcloth, and spread it for her upon the rock, from the beginning of harvest <sup>s</sup>until water dropped upon them out of heaven, and suffered neither the birds of the air to rest on them by day, nor the beasts of the field by night.

11 And it was told David what Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, the concubine of Saul, had done.

12 And David went and took the bones of Saul, and the bones of Jonathan his son, from the men of <sup>t</sup>Jabesh-gilead, which had stolen them from the street of Beth-shan, where the Philistines had hanged them, when the Philistines had slain Saul in Gilboa:
