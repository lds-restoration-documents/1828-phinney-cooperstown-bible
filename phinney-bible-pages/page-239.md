## CHAPTER IV.

1 Solomon's princes....7 His twelve officers for provision.

SO king Solomon was king over <sup>a</sup>all Israel.

2 And these *were* the <sup>b</sup>princes which he had; Azariah the son of <sup>||</sup>Zadok the priest;

3 Elihoreph and Ahiah, the sons of Shisha, <sup>c</sup>scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud, the recorder.

4 And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada *was* over the host; and Zadok and <sup>||</sup>Abiathar *were* the priests;

5 And Azariah the son of Nathan *was* over the officers; and Zabud the son of Nathan *was* principal officer, *and* the king's <sup>d</sup>friend;

6 And Ahishar *was* over the household; and Adoniram the son of Abda *was* over the <sup>e</sup>tribute.

7 And Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel, which provided victuals for the king and his household: each man his month in a year made provision.

8 And these *are* their names: The son of Hur, in <sup>f</sup>mount Ephraim.

9 The son of Dekar, in <sup>g</sup>Makaz, and in Shaalbim, and Beth-shemesh, and Elon-beth-hanan.

10 The son of Hesed, in <sup>h</sup>Aruboth; to him *pertained* Sochoh, and all the land of <sup>i</sup>Hepher.

11 The son of Abinadab, in all the region of <sup>k</sup>Dor; which had Taphath the daughter of Solomon to wife.

12 Baana, the son of Ahilud; *to him pertained* <sup>l</sup>Taanach and Megiddo, and all Beth-shean, which *is* by <sup>m</sup>Zartanah beneath <sup>n</sup>Jezreel, from <sup>o</sup>Beth-shean to <sup>p</sup>Abel-meholah, *even* unto *the place that is* beyond <sup>q</sup>Jokneam.

13 The son of Geber, in <sup>r</sup>Ramoth-gilead; to him *pertained* the <sup>s</sup>towns of Jair the son of Manasseh, which *are* in Gilead: to him *also pertained* the <sup>t</sup>region of Argob, which *is* in Bashan, threescore great cities, with walls and brazen bars.

14 Ahinadab the son of Iddo *had* <sup>u</sup>Mahanaim.

15 Ahimaaz *was* in Naphtali; he also took Basmath the daughter of Solomon to wife.

16 Baanah the son of Hushai *was* in Asher and in Aloth.

17 Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah in Issachar.

18 Shimei the son of Elah in Benjamin.

19 Geber the son of Uri *was* in the country of Gilead, *in* the country of <sup>x</sup>Sihon king of the Amorites, and of Og king of Bashan; and *he was* the only officer which *was* in the land.

20 ¶ Judah and Israel *were* many, as the <sup>y</sup>sand which *is* by the sea in multitude, <sup>z</sup>eating and drinking, and making merry.

21 And Solomon reigned <sup>a</sup>over all kingdoms from the river unto the land of the Philistines, and unto the border of Egypt: they brought <sup>b</sup>presents, and served Solomon all the days of his life.

22 And Solomon's <sup>†</sup>provision for one day was <sup>||</sup>thirty measures of fine flour, and <sup>||</sup>threescore measures of meal,

23 Ten fat oxen, and twenty oxen out of the pastures, and a hundred sheep, besides harts, and roebucks, and fallow deer, and fatted fowl.

24 For he had dominion over all *the region* on this side the river, from <sup>c</sup>Tiphsah even to <sup>d</sup>Azzah, <sup>e</sup>over all the kings on this side the river: and he had peace on all sides round about him.

25 And Judah and Israel <sup>f</sup>dwelt safely, every man <sup>g</sup>under his vine and under his fig-tree, from Dan even to Beer-sheba, all the days of Solomon.

26 And Solomon had forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen.

27 And those officers provided victual for king Solomon, and for all that came unto king Solomon's table, every man in his month: they lacked nothing.

28 Barley also and straw for the horses and <sup>h</sup>dromedaries brought they unto the place where *the officers* were, every man according to his charge.

29 And <sup>i</sup>God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and <sup>†</sup>largeness of heart, even as the sand that *is* on the sea-shore.

30 And Solomon's wisdom excelled the wisdom of all the children of the <sup>k</sup>east country, and all the wisdom of <sup>l</sup>Egypt.

31 For he was <sup>m</sup>wiser than all men; than <sup>n</sup>Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and <sup>o</sup>Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol: and his fame was in all nations round about.

32 And he spake three thousand <sup>p</sup>proverbs: and his <sup>q</sup>songs were a thousand and five.

33 And he spake of trees, from the cedar-tree that *is* in Lebanon even unto the hyssop that springeth out of the wall: he spake also of beasts, and of fowl, and of creeping things, and of fishes.

34 And there came of <sup>r</sup>all people to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all kings of the earth, which had heard of his wisdom.
