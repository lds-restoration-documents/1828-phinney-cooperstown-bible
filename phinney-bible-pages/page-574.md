## CHAPTER IV.

By the golden candlestick is foreshewed the good success of Zerubbabel's foundation.

AND the angel that talked with me came again and <sup>a</sup>waked me, as a man that is wakened out of his sleep,

2 And said unto me, What seest thou? And I said, I have looked, and behold a <sup>b</sup>candlestick all *of* gold, with <sup>c</sup>a bowl upon the top of it, and his seven <sup>d</sup>lamps thereon, and seven <sup>e</sup>pipes to the seven lamps which *are* upon the <sup>f</sup>top thereof;

3 And <sup>g</sup>two olive-trees by it, one upon the right *side* of the bowl, and the other upon the left *side* thereof.

4 So I answered and spake to the angel that talked with me, saying, <sup>h</sup>What *are* these, my lord?

5 Then the angel that talked with me answered and said unto me, Knowest thou not what these *be?* And I said, <sup>i</sup>No, my lord.

6 Then he answered and spake unto me, saying, This *is* the word of the LORD unto Zerubbabel, saying, <sup>k</sup>Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit, saith the LORD of hosts.

7 Who *art* thou, <sup>l</sup>O great mountain? before Zerubbabel *thou shalt become* a plain: and he shall bring forth the head-stone *thereof* <sup>m</sup>*with* shoutings, *crying,* Grace, grace, unto it.

8 Moreover, the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

9 The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation of this house, his hands shall also <sup>n</sup>finish it; and thou shalt know that the LORD of hosts hath sent me unto you.

10 For who hath despised the <sup>o</sup>day of small things? for they shall rejoice, and shall see the plummet in the hand of Zerubbabel *with* those seven; they *are* the <sup>p</sup>eyes of the LORD, which run to and fro through the whole earth.

11 ¶ Then answered I, and said unto him, What *are* these two <sup>q</sup>olive-trees upon the right *side* of the candlestick, and upon the left *side* thereof?

12 And I answered again, and said unto him, What *be these* two olive-branches, which, through the two golden <sup>r</sup>pipes, empty the golden *oil* out of themselves?

13 And he answered me and said, Knowest thou not what these *be?* And I said, No, my lord.

14 Then said he, These *are* the two <sup>†</sup>anointed ones, that stand by the LORD of the <sup>s</sup>whole earth.

## CHAPTER V.

1 By the flying roll, is shewed the curse of thieves and of false swearers....5 Babylon's ruin.

THEN I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold <sup>a</sup>a flying roll.

2 And he said unto me, What seest thou? And I answered, I see a flying roll; the <sup>b</sup>length thereof *is* twenty cubits, and the <sup>c</sup>breadth thereof ten cubits.

3 Then said he unto me, This *is* the <sup>d</sup>curse that goeth forth over the face of the whole earth: for every one that stealeth shall be cut off *as* on this side, according to it; and every one that sweareth shall be cut off *as* on that side, according to it.

4 I will bring it forth, saith the LORD of hosts, and it shall <sup>e</sup>enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him that sweareth falsely by my name: and it shall remain in the midst of his house, and shall consume it, with the timber thereof, and the stones thereof.

5 ¶ Then the angel that talked with me went forth, and said unto me, <sup>f</sup>Lift up now thine eyes, and see what *is* this that goeth forth.

6 And I said, What *is* it? And he said, This *is* <sup>g</sup>an ephah that goeth forth. He said moreover, This *is* their resemblance through all the earth.

7 And, behold, there was lifted up a <sup>h</sup>talent of lead: and this *is* <sup>i</sup>a woman that sitteth in the midst of the ephah.

8 And he said, This *is* <sup>k</sup>wickedness. And he cast it into the midst of the ephah; and he cast the weight of lead upon <sup>l</sup>the mouth thereof.

9 Then lifted I up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came out <sup>m</sup>two women, and the wind *was* in their wings; (for they had <sup>n</sup>wings like the wings of a stork;) and they lifted up the ephah between the earth and the heaven.

10 Then said I to the angel that talked with me, Whither do these bear the ephah?

11 And he said unto me, To build it a house in the land of <sup>o</sup>Shinar; and it shall be established, and set <sup>p</sup>there upon her own base.

## CHAPTER VI.

1 The vision of the four chariots....9 The temple and kingdom of Christ the Branch shewed.

AND I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came four <sup>a</sup>chariots out from between two mountains; and the mountains *were* mountains of <sup>b</sup>brass.

2 In the first chariot *were* <sup>c</sup>red horses, and in the second chariot <sup>d</sup>black horses,

3 And in the third chariot <sup>e</sup>white horses, and in the fourth chariot grisled and bay horses.

4 Then I answered and said unto the angel that talked with me, What *are* these, my lord?

5 And the angel answered and said unto me, These *are* the four <sup>f</sup>spirits of the heavens, which go forth from <sup>g</sup>standing before the LORD of all the earth.

6 The black horses which *are* therein go forth into the <sup>h</sup>north country; and the <sup>i</sup>white go forth after them; and the grisled go forth toward the <sup>k</sup>south country.

7 And the <sup>||</sup>bay went forth, and <sup>l</sup>sought to go, that they might walk to and fro through the earth: and <sup>m</sup>he said, Get ye hence, walk to and fro through the earth. So they walked to and fro through the earth.

8 Then cried he upon me, and spake unto me, saying, Behold, these that go toward the north country have <sup>n</sup>quieted my spirit in the north country.

9 ¶ And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

10 <sup>o</sup>Take of *them of* the captivity, *even* of Heldai, of Tobijah, and of Jedaiah, which are come from Babylon, and come thou <sup>p</sup>the same day, and go into the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah;

11 Then take silver and gold, and make <sup>q</sup>crowns, and set *them* upon the head of Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest;

12 And speak unto him, saying, Thus speaketh the LORD of hosts, saying, Behold the <sup>r</sup>man, whose name *is* The <sup>s</sup>BRANCH; and he shall grow up out of his place, and he shall <sup>t</sup>build the temple of the LORD:

13 Even he shall build the temple of the LORD; and he shall bear the <sup>u</sup>glory, and shall <sup>x</sup>sit and rule upon his throne; and he shall be a priest upon his throne: and the counsel of peace shall be between them both.
