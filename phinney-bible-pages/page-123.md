9 He <sup>t</sup>couched, he lay down as a lion, and as a great lion: <sup>u</sup>who shall stir him up? Blessed *is* he that blesseth thee, and cursed *is* he that curseth thee.

10 And Balak's anger was kindled against Balaam, and he <sup>x</sup>smote his hands together: and Balak said unto Balaam, I called thee to curse mine enemies, and, behold, thou hast altogether blessed *them* these three times.

11 Therefore now <sup>y</sup>flee thou to thy place: I thought to promote thee unto great honour; but, lo, the LORD hath <sup>z</sup>kept thee back from honour.

12 And Balaam said unto Balak, Spake I not also to thy messengers which thou sentest unto me, saying,

13 If Balak would give me his house full of silver and gold, I cannot go beyond the commandment of the LORD, to do *either* good or bad of <sup>a</sup>mine own mind; *but* what the LORD saith, that will I speak?

14 And now, behold, I go unto my people: come *therefore, and* I will advertise <sup>b</sup>thee what this people shall do to thy people in the <sup>c</sup>latter days.

15 And he took up his parable, and said, Balaam the son of Beor hath said, and the man whose eyes are open hath said;

16 He hath said, which heard the words of God, and knew the knowledge of the Most High, *which* saw the vision of the Almighty, falling *into a trance*, but having his eyes open:

17 I shall <sup>d</sup>see him, but not now; I shall behold him, but not <sup>e</sup>nigh: there shall come <sup>f</sup>a Star out of Jacob, and a <sup>g</sup>Sceptre shall rise out of Israel, and shall smite the <sup>h</sup>corners of Moab, and <sup>†</sup>destroy all the children of Sheth.

18 And <sup>i</sup>Edom shall be a possession, Seir also shall be a possession for his enemies; and Israel shall do <sup>k</sup>valiantly.

19 Out of Jacob shall come <sup>l</sup>he that shall have dominion, and shall destroy him that remaineth of the city.

20 And when he looked on Amalek, he took up his parable, and said, Amalek *was* the <sup>m</sup>first of the nations; but his latter end *shall be*, that he perish for ever.

21 And he looked on the <sup>n</sup>Kenites, and took up his parable, and said, Strong is thy dwelling-place, and thou puttest thy <sup>o</sup>nest in a rock.

22 Nevertheless the <sup>†</sup>Kenite shall be wasted <sup>||</sup>until Asshur shall carry thee away captive.

23 And he took up his parable, and said, Alas, who shall live when God doeth this!

24 And ships *shall come* from the coast of Chittim, and shall afflict Asshur, and shall afflict Eber, and he also shall perish for ever.

25 And Balaam rose up, and went and returned to his place: and Balak also went his way.

## CHAPTER XXV.

6 Phinehas killeth Zimri and Cozbi....10 God therefore giveth him an everlasting priesthood.

AND Israel abode in <sup>a</sup>Shittim, and the <sup>b</sup>people began to commit <sup>c</sup>whoredom with the <sup>d</sup>daughters of Moab.

2 And they <sup>e</sup>called the people unto the sacrifices of their gods: and the people <sup>f</sup>did eat, and bowed down to their gods.

3 And Israel <sup>g</sup>joined himself unto <sup>||</sup>Baal-peor: and the <sup>h</sup>anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel.

4 And the LORD said unto Moses, Take all the heads of the people, and hang them up before the LORD against the sun, that the fierce anger of the LORD may be turned away from Israel.

5 And Moses said unto the judges of Israel, Slay ye every one his men that were joined unto Baal-peor.

6 ¶ And, behold, one of the children of Israel came, and brought unto his brethren a Midianitish woman, in the sight of Moses, and in the sight of all the congregation of the children of Israel, who *were* <sup>i</sup>weeping before the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.

7 And when Phinehas the son of Eleazer, the son of Aaron the priest, saw *it*, he rose up from among the congregation, and took a javelin in his hand;

8 And he went after the man of Israel into the tent, and thrust both of them through, the man of Israel, and the woman through her belly. So the <sup>k</sup>plague was stayed from the children of Israel.

9 And those that died in the plague were <sup>l</sup>twenty and four thousand.

10 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

11 Phinehas the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, hath turned my wrath away from the children of Israel, (while he was <sup>m</sup>zealous for my sake among them,) that I consumed not the children of Israel in my <sup>n</sup>jealousy.

12 Wherefore, say, Behold, I give unto him my <sup>o</sup>covenant of peace:

13 And he shall have it, and his seed after him *even* the covenant of an <sup>p</sup>everlasting priesthood; because he <sup>q</sup>was zealous for his God, and made an <sup>r</sup>atonement for the children of Israel.

14 Now the name of the Israelite that was slain, *even* that was slain with the Midianitish woman, *was* Zimri the son of Salu, a prince of a chief house among the Simeonites.

15 And the name of the Midianitish woman that was slain *was* Cozbi, the daughter of Zur; he *was* head over a people, *and* of a chief house in Midian.

16 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

17 <sup>s</sup>Vex the Midianites, and smite them:

18 For they vex you with their <sup>t</sup>wiles, wherewith they have beguiled you, in the matter of Peor, and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of a prince of Midian, their sister, which was slain in the day of the plague, for Peor's sake.

## CHAPTER XXVI.

The sum of all Israel is taken in the plains of Moab.

AND it came to pass, <sup>a</sup>after the plague, that the LORD spake unto Moses, and unto Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, saying,

2 Take the <sup>b</sup>sum of all the congregation of the <sup>||</sup>children of Israel, from twenty years old and upward, throughout their father's house, all that are able to go to war in Israel.

3 And Moses and Eleazar the priest spake with them in the <sup>c</sup>plains of Moab, by Jordan *near* Jericho, saying,

4 *Take the sum of the people*, from twenty years old and upward; as the LORD commanded Moses, and the children of Israel, which went forth out of the land of Egypt.

5 Reuben, the <sup>d</sup>eldest son of Israel: the children of Reuben; <sup>e</sup>Hanoch, *of whom cometh* the family of the <sup>f</sup>Hanochites: of Pallu, the family of the Palluites:

6 Of Hezron, the family of the Hezronites: of Carmi, the family of the Carmites.

7 These *are* the <sup>g</sup>families of the Reubenites: and they that were numbered of them were <sup>h</sup>forty and three thousand and seven hundred and thirty.
