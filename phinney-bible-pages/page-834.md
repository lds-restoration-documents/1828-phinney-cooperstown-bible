2 <sup>b</sup>Preach the word; be instant in season, out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort, with all long-suffering and doctrine.

3 <sup>c</sup>For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine; but after their own lusts shall they heap to themselves teachers, having itching ears;

4 <sup>d</sup>And they shall turn away *their* ears from the truth, and shall be turned unto fables.

5 <sup>e</sup>But watch thou in all things, endure afflictions, do the work of an evangelist, make full proof of thy ministry.

6 <sup>f</sup>For I am now ready to be offered, and the time of my departure is at hand.

7 <sup>g</sup>I have fought a good fight, I have finished *my* course, I have kept the faith:

8 <sup>h</sup>Henceforth there is laid up for me a crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, shall give me at that day: and not to me only, but unto all them also that love his appearing.

9 <sup>i</sup>Do thy diligence to come shortly unto me:

10 <sup>k</sup>For Demas hath forsaken me, having loved this present world, and is departed unto Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus unto Dalmatia.

11 <sup>l</sup>Only Luke is with me. Take Mark, and bring him with thee; for he is profitable to me for the ministry.

12 <sup>m</sup>And Tychicus have I sent to Ephesus.

13 The cloak that I left at Troas with Carpus, when thou comest, bring *with thee,* and the books, *but* especially the parchments.

14 <sup>n</sup>Alexander the coppersmith did me much evil: the Lord reward him according to his works:

15 Of whom be thou ware also; for he hath greatly withstood our words.

16 <sup>o</sup>At my first answer no man stood with me, but all men forsook me: *I pray God* that it may not be laid to their charge.

17 <sup>p</sup>Notwithstanding the Lord stood with me, and strengthened me: that by me the preaching might be fully known, and *that* all the Gentiles might hear: and I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion.

18 <sup>q</sup>And the Lord shall deliver me from every evil work, and will preserve *me* unto his heavenly kingdom; to whom *be* glory for ever and ever. Amen.

19 <sup>r</sup>Salute Prisca and Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus.

20 <sup>s</sup>Erastus abode at Corinth: but Trophimus have I left at Miletum sick.

21 <sup>t</sup>Do thy diligence to come before winter. Eubulus greeteth thee, and Pudens, and Linus, and Claudia, and all the brethren.

22 <sup>u</sup>The Lord Jesus Christ *be* with thy spirit. Grace *be* with you. Amen.

# ¶ The Epistle of PAUL, to TITUS.

## CHAPTER I.

5 Why Titus was left at Crete....10 Of evil-doers.

<sup>a</sup>PAUL, a servant of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of God's elect, and the acknowledging of the truth which is after godliness;

2 <sup>b</sup>In hope of eternal life, which God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began;

3 <sup>c</sup>But hath in due time manifested his word through preaching, which is committed unto me, according to the commandment of God our Saviour;

4 <sup>d</sup>To Titus, *mine* own son after the common faith: Grace, mercy, *and* peace, from God the Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ our Saviour.

5 <sup>e</sup>For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee:

6 <sup>f</sup>If any be blameless, the husband of one wife, having faithful children, not accused of riot, or unruly.

7 <sup>g</sup>For a bishop must be blameless, as the steward of God; not self-willed, not soon angry, not given to wine, no striker, not given to filthy lucre;

8 <sup>h</sup>But a lover of hospitality, a lover of good men, sober, just, holy, temperate:

9 <sup>i</sup>Holding fast the faithful word, as he hath been taught, that he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.

10 <sup>k</sup>For there are many unruly and vain talkers and deceivers, especially they of the circumcision:

11 <sup>l</sup>Whose mouths must be stopped; who subvert whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for filthy lucre's sake.

12 One of themselves, *even* a prophet of their own, said, The Cretians *are* always liars, evil beasts, slow bellies.

13 <sup>m</sup>This witness is true. Wherefore rebuke them sharply; that they may be sound in the faith:

14 <sup>n</sup>Not giving heed to Jewish fables, and commandments of men that turn from the truth.

15 <sup>o</sup>Unto the pure all things *are* pure; but unto them that are defiled and unbelieving *is* nothing pure; but even their mind and conscience is defiled.

16 <sup>p</sup>They profess that they know God; but in works they deny *him,* being abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work reprobate.

## CHAPTER II.

1 Directions given unto Titus both for his doctrine and life. 9 Of the duty of servants, &c.

<sup>a</sup>BUT speak thou the things which become sound doctrine:

2 That the aged men be sober, grave, temperate, sound in faith, in charity, in patience;

3 <sup>b</sup>The aged women likewise, that *they be* in behaviour as becometh holiness, not false accusers, not given to much wine, teachers of good things;

4 That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children,

5 <sup>c</sup>*To be* discreet, chaste, keepers at home, good, obedient to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed.

6 Young men likewise exhort to be sober-minded.

7 <sup>d</sup>In all things shewing thyself a pattern of good works; in doctrine *shewing* uncorruptness, gravity, sincerity,

8 <sup>e</sup>Sound speech that cannot be condemned; that he that is of the contrary part may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say of you.

9 <sup>f</sup>*Exhort* servants to be obedient unto their own masters, *and* to please *them* well in all *things;* not answering again;
