## CHAPTER XLIII.

Jeremiah prophesieth the conquest of Egypt by the Babylonians.

AND it came to pass, *that* when Jeremiah had made an end of speaking unto all the people all the words of the LORD their God, for which the LORD their God had sent him to them, *even* all these words,

2 Then spake Azariah the son of Hoshaiah, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the <sup>a</sup>proud men, saying unto Jeremiah, Thou speakest <sup>b</sup>falsely: the LORD our God hath not sent thee to say, Go not into Egypt to sojourn there:

3 But <sup>c</sup>Baruch the son of Neriah setteth thee on against us, for to deliver us into the hand of the Chaldeans, that <sup>d</sup>they might put us to death, and carry us away captives into Babylon.

4 So Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces, and all the people, <sup>e</sup>obeyed not the voice of the LORD, to <sup>f</sup>dwell in the land of Judah;

5 But <sup>g</sup>Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces, took all the remnant of Judah, that were returned from all nations whither they had been driven, to dwell in the land of Judah;

6 *Even* men, and women, and children, and the <sup>h</sup>king's daughters, and every person that Nebuzar-adan the captain of the guard had left with Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, and <sup>i</sup>Jeremiah the prophet, and Baruch the son of Neriah.

7 So they came into the land of Egypt: for they obeyed not the voice of the LORD. Thus came they *even* to <sup>k</sup>Tahpanhes.

8 <sup>l</sup>Then came the word of the LORD unto Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,

9 Take great stones in thy hand, and hide them in the clay in the brick-kiln, which *is* <sup>m</sup>at the entry of Pharaoh's house in Tahpanhes, in the sight of the men of Judah;

10 And say unto them, Thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, Behold, I will send and take Nebuchadrezzar the king of <sup>n</sup>Babylon my servant, and will <sup>o</sup>set his throne upon these stones that I have hid; and he shall spread his royal pavilion over them.

11 And when he cometh, he shall <sup>p</sup>smite the land of Egypt, *and deliver* <sup>q</sup>such *as are* for death, to death; and such *as are* for captivity, to captivity; and such *as are* for the sword, to the sword.

12 And I will kindle a fire in the <sup>r</sup>houses of the gods of Egypt; and he shall burn them, and carry them away captives; and he shall array himself with the land of Egypt, as a shepherd putteth on his garment; and he shall go forth from thence <sup>s</sup>in peace.

13 He shall break also the images of Beth-shemesh, that *is* in the land of Egypt; and the houses of the gods of the Egyptians shall he burn with fire.

## CHAPTER XLIV.

Jeremiah prophesieth their destruction who commit idolatry.

THE word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Jews which dwell in the land of Egypt, which dwell at <sup>a</sup>Migdol, and at <sup>b</sup>Tahpanhes, and at <sup>c</sup>Noph, and in the country of <sup>d</sup>Pathros, saying,

2 Thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, Ye have seen all the evil that I have brought upon Jerusalem, and upon all the cities of Judah; and, behold, this day they *are* <sup>e</sup>a desolation, and no man dwelleth therein;

3 Because of their wickedness which they have committed, <sup>f</sup>to provoke me to anger, in that they went to <sup>g</sup>burn incense, *and* to serve other gods, whom they knew not, *neither* they, ye, nor your fathers.

4 Howbeit I sent unto you all my servants the prophets, <sup>h</sup>rising early and sending *them,* saying, Oh, do not this abominable thing that I hate.

5 But they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear to turn from their wickedness, to burn no incense unto other gods.

6 Wherefore <sup>i</sup>my fury and mine anger was poured forth, and was kindled in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem; and they are wasted *and* desolate, <sup>k</sup>as at this day.

7 Therefore now thus saith the LORD, the God of hosts, the God of Israel, <sup>l</sup>Wherefore commit ye *this* great evil <sup>m</sup>against your souls, to cut off from you man and woman, child and suckling, out of Judah, to leave you none to remain;

8 In that ye provoke me unto wrath with the <sup>n</sup>works of your hands, burning incense unto other gods in the land of Egypt, whither ye be gone to dwell, that ye might cut yourselves off, and that ye might be <sup>o</sup>a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth?

9 Have ye forgotten the wickedness of your fathers, and the wickedness of the <sup>p</sup>kings of Judah, and the wickedness of their wives, and your own wickedness, and the wickedness of your wives, which they have committed in the land of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem?

10 They are not <sup>†</sup>humbled *even* unto this day, neither have they feared, <sup>q</sup>nor walked in my law, nor in my statutes, that I set before you, and before your fathers.

11 ¶ Therefore thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, Behold, I will <sup>r</sup>set my face against you for evil, and to cut off all Judah.

12 And I will take the remnant of Judah, that have <sup>s</sup>set their faces to go into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, and they shall all be consumed, *and* fall in the land of Egypt; they shall *even* be consumed by the sword *and* by the famine: they shall die, from the least even unto the greatest, by the sword and by the famine; and they shall be <sup>t</sup>an execration, *and* an astonishment, and a curse, and a reproach.

13 For I will <sup>†</sup>punish them that dwell in the land of Egypt, as I have punished Jerusalem, by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence:

14 So that none of the remnant of Judah, which are gone into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, <sup>†</sup>shall escape or remain, that they should return into the land of Judah, to the which they have a desire to return to dwell there: for none shall return but such as shall escape.
