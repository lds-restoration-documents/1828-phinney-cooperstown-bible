**Header:** *God only to be trusted.* | PSALMS. | *All creatures exhorted to praise God*

18 The LORD *is* nigh unto all them that call upon him, to all that call upon him <sup>u</sup>in truth.

19 He will fulfil <sup>x</sup>the desire of them that fear him: he also will hear their cry, and will save them.

20 The LORD preserveth all them that <sup>y</sup>love him: but all the wicked will he destroy.

21 My mouth shall speak the praise of the LORD: and let <sup>z</sup>all flesh bless his holy name for ever and ever.

## PSALM CXLVI.

The Psalmist voweth perpetual praises to God.

PRAISE ye the LORD. Praise the LORD, <sup>a</sup>O my soul.

2 While I live will I praise the LORD: I will sing praises unto my God <sup>b</sup>while I have any being.

3 <sup>c</sup>Put not your trust in princes, *nor* in the son of man, in whom *there is* no help.

4 His <sup>d</sup>breath goeth forth, he returneth to his <sup>e</sup>earth; in that very day his thoughts perish.

5 <sup>f</sup>Happy *is he* that *hath* the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope *is* in the LORD his God;

6 Which <sup>g</sup>made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that therein *is;* which keepeth truth for ever;

7 Which executeth judgment for the oppressed; which giveth food to the hungry. The LORD looseth the <sup>h</sup>prisoners.

8 The LORD <sup>i</sup>openeth *the eyes of* the blind: the LORD raiseth *them that are* <sup>k</sup>bowed down: the LORD loveth the righteous.

9 The LORD preserveth the strangers, he relieveth the fatherless and widow: but the way of the wicked he <sup>l</sup>turneth upside down.

10 The LORD shall <sup>m</sup>reign for ever, *even* thy God, O Zion, unto all generations. Praise ye the LORD.

## PSALM CXLVII.

The Prophet exhorteth to praise God for his care of the church.

PRAISE ye the LORD: for *it is* <sup>a</sup>good to sing praises unto our God; for *it is* pleasant; *and* praise <sup>b</sup>is comely.

2 The LORD doth <sup>c</sup>build up Jerusalem: he <sup>d</sup>gathereth together the outcasts of Israel.

3 He healeth the <sup>e</sup>broken in heart, and <sup>f</sup>bindeth up their wounds.

4 <sup>g</sup>He telleth the number of the stars; he calleth them all by *their* names.

5 Great *is* our LORD, and of great power: his understanding *is* <sup>h</sup>infinite.

6 The LORD <sup>i</sup>lifteth up the meek: he casteth the wicked <sup>k</sup>down to the ground.

7 Sing unto the LORD with thanksgiving; <sup>†</sup>sing praise upon the harp unto our God;

8 Who covereth the heaven with <sup>l</sup>clouds, who prepareth <sup>m</sup>rain for the earth, who maketh grass to grow upon the mountains.

9 He giveth to the beast his <sup>n</sup>food, *and* to the <sup>o</sup>young ravens which cry.

10 He delighteth not in the strength of the horse; he taketh not pleasure in the legs of a man.

11 The LORD taketh pleasure in them that fear him, in those that hope in his mercy.

12 Praise the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise thy God, O Zion.

13 For he hath strengthened the bars of thy gates; he hath blessed <sup>p</sup>thy children within thee.

14 He maketh <sup>q</sup>peace *in* thy borders, *and* filleth thee with the finest of the wheat.

15 He sendeth forth his commandment *upon* earth: his word runneth very swiftly.

16 He giveth <sup>r</sup>snow like wool: he scattereth the hoar-frost like ashes.

17 He casteth forth his ice like morsels: who can stand <sup>s</sup>before his cold?

18 He sendeth out his word, and melteth them: he causeth his wind to blow, *and* the waters flow.

19 He sheweth <sup>t</sup>his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel.

20 He hath <sup>u</sup>not dealt so with any nation: and *as for his* judgments, they have not known them. Praise ye the LORD.

## PSALM CXLVIII.

The Psalmist exhorteth to praise God.

PRAISE ye the LORD. Praise ye the LORD from the <sup>a</sup>heavens: praise him in the heights.

2 Praise ye him, all his <sup>b</sup>angels: praise ye him, all his <sup>c</sup>hosts.

3 Praise ye him, <sup>d</sup>sun and moon: praise him, all ye <sup>e</sup>stars of light.

4 Praise him, ye <sup>f</sup>heavens of heavens, and ye waters that *be* <sup>g</sup>above the heavens.

5 Let them praise the name of the LORD: for he <sup>h</sup>commanded, and they were created.

6 He hath also <sup>i</sup>established them for ever and ever: he hath made a decree which shall not pass.

7 Praise the LORD from the earth, ye <sup>k</sup>dragons, and all deeps.

8 Fire and hail; snow and vapour; <sup>l</sup>stormy wind fulfilling his word:

9 Mountains, and all hills; <sup>m</sup>fruitful trees, and all cedars:

10 <sup>n</sup>Beasts, and all cattle: creeping things, and flying fowl:

11 Kings of the earth, and all people; princes and all judges of the earth:

12 Both young men and maidens; old men and children:

13 Let them praise the name of the LORD: for his name alone is excellent; his glory *is* above the earth and heaven.

14 He also exalteth the <sup>o</sup>horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; *even* of the children of Israel, <sup>p</sup>a people near unto him. Praise ye the LORD.

## PSALM CXLIX.

The Prophet exhorteth to praise God for his love to the church.

PRAISE ye the LORD. Sing unto the LORD a new song, *and* his praise in the <sup>a</sup>congregation of saints.

2 Let Israel rejoice in him that made him: let the children of Zion be joyful in <sup>b</sup>their King.

3 Let them praise his name in the dance: let them sing praises unto him with the timbrel and harp.

4 For the LORD taketh <sup>c</sup>pleasure in his people: he will <sup>d</sup>beautify the meek with salvation.

5 Let the saints be joyful in glory: let them sing aloud upon their beds.

6 *Let* the <sup>e</sup>high *praises* of God *be* in their mouth, and <sup>f</sup>a two-edged sword in their hand;

7 To execute vengeance upon <sup>g</sup>the heathen, *and* punishments upon the people;

8 To bind their <sup>h</sup>kings with chains, and their nobles with fetters of iron;

9 To execute upon them the judgment <sup>i</sup>written: this <sup>k</sup>honour have all his saints. Praise ye the LORD.
