## CHAPTER XXV.

The prophet praiseth God for his judgments and salvation.

O LORD, thou *art* my God; I will <sup>a</sup>exalt thee, I will praise thy name: for thou hast done <sup>b</sup>wonderful *things; thy* counsels of old *are* faithfulness *and* truth.

2 For thou hast made of a <sup>c</sup>city a <sup>d</sup>heap; *of* a defenced city a ruin: a palace of strangers to be no city; it shall never be built.

3 Therefore shall the strong people <sup>e</sup>glorify thee, the city of the <sup>f</sup>terrible nations shall fear thee.

4 For thou hast been a strength to the poor, a strength to the needy in his distress, a <sup>g</sup>refuge from the storm, a shadow from the heat, when the blast of the terrible ones *is* as a storm *against* the wall.

5 Thou shalt bring down the noise of strangers as the heat in a dry place; *even* the heat with the shadow of a cloud: the branch of the <sup>h</sup>terrible ones shall be brought low.

6 And in this <sup>i</sup>mountain shall the LORD of hosts make unto <sup>k</sup>all people a feast of fat things, a feast of wines on the lees; of fat things full of marrow, of wines on the lees well refined.

7 And he will destroy in this mountain the face of the <sup>l</sup>covering cast over all people, and the vail that is spread over all nations.

8 He will <sup>m</sup>swallow up death in victory; and the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from off all faces; and the <sup>n</sup>rebuke of his people shall he take away from off all the earth: for the LORD hath spoken *it.*

9 And it shall be said in that day, Lo, this *is* <sup>o</sup>our God; we have waited for him, and he will save us: this *is* the LORD; we have <sup>p</sup>waited for him, we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation.

10 For in this mountain shall the hand of the LORD rest, and Moab shall be trodden down <sup>q</sup>under him, even as straw is trodden down for the dunghill.

11 And he shall <sup>r</sup>spread forth his hands in the midst of them, as he that swimmeth spreadeth forth *his hands* to swim, and he shall bring down their <sup>s</sup>pride together with the spoils of their hands.

12 And the fortress of the high fort of thy <sup>t</sup>walls shall he bring down, lay low, *and* bring to the ground, *even* to the dust.

## CHAPTER XXVI.

A song inciting to confidence in God for his judgments.

IN <sup>a</sup>that day shall this song be sung in the land of Judah; We have a <sup>b</sup>strong city: salvation will *God appoint for* <sup>c</sup>walls and bulwarks.

2 Open ye the gates, that the <sup>d</sup>righteous nation which <sup>e</sup>keepeth the truth may enter in.

3 Thou wilt keep *him* in <sup>f</sup>perfect peace, *whose* mind *is* <sup>g</sup>stayed *on thee;* because he trusteth in thee.

4 <sup>h</sup>Trust ye in the LORD for ever: for in the LORD JEHOVAH *is* everlasting strength.

5 For he bringeth down them that dwell on high; the lofty city he layeth it low: he layeth it low, *even* to the ground; he bringeth it *even* to the dust.

6 The foot shall tread it down, *even* the <sup>i</sup>feet of the poor, *and* the steps of the needy.

7 The way of the just *is* <sup>k</sup>uprightness: thou, most upright, dost <sup>l</sup>weigh the path of the just.

8 Yea, in <sup>m</sup>the way of thy judgments, O LORD, have we waited for thee; the desire of *our* soul *is* to <sup>n</sup>thy name, and to the remembrance of thee.

9 With my soul have I desired thee in <sup>o</sup>the night; yea, with my spirit within me will I seek thee early: for when thy judgments *are* in the earth, the inhabitants of the world will <sup>p</sup>learn righteousness.

10 Let <sup>q</sup>favour be shewed to the wicked, *yet* will he not learn righteousness: in the land of uprightness will he deal <sup>r</sup>unjustly, and will not behold the majesty of the LORD.

11 LORD, *when* thy hand is lifted up, they will <sup>s</sup>not see: *but* they <sup>t</sup>shall see, and be ashamed for *their* envy at the people; yea, the fire of thine enemies shall devour them.

12 LORD, thou wilt <sup>u</sup>ordain peace for us: for thou also hast wrought all our works in us.

13 O LORD our God, *other* <sup>x</sup>lords besides thee have had dominion over us; *but* by thee <sup>y</sup>only will we make mention of thy name.

14 *They are* dead, they shall <sup>z</sup>not live; *they are* deceased, they shall not rise: therefore hast thou visited and destroyed them, and made all their <sup>a</sup>memory to perish.

15 Thou hast increased the nation, O LORD, thou hast increased the nation: thou art glorified; thou hadst removed *it* far *unto* all the <sup>b</sup>ends of the earth.

16 LORD, in <sup>c</sup>trouble have they visited thee; they poured out a prayer *when* thy chastening *was* upon them.

17 Like as a woman with child, *that* draweth near the time of her delivery, is in pain, *and* crieth out in her pangs; <sup>d</sup>so have we been in thy sight, O LORD.

18 We have been with child, we have been in pain, we have as it were brought forth <sup>e</sup>wind; we have not <sup>f</sup>wrought any deliverance in the earth, neither have the inhabitants of the world fallen.

19 Thy <sup>g</sup>dead *men* shall live, *together with* my dead body shall they arise. Awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust: for thy dew *is as* the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast out the dead.
