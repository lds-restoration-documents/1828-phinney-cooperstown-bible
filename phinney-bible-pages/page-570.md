# ZEPHANIAH.

## CHAPTER I.

1 When Zephaniah prophesied....2 God's severe judgment against Judah for divers sins.

THE <sup>a</sup>word of the LORD which came unto Zephaniah the son of Cushi, the son of Gedaliah, the son of Amariah, the son of Hizkiah, in the days of Josiah the son of Amon king of Judah.

2 I will utterly <sup>b</sup>consume all *things* from off the land, saith the LORD.

3 I will consume <sup>c</sup>man and beast; I will consume the fowls of the heaven, and the fishes of the sea; and the <sup>d</sup>stumbling-blocks with the wicked; and I will cut off man from off the land, saith the LORD.

4 I will also stretch out my hand upon Judah, and upon all the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and I will cut off the remnant of <sup>e</sup>Baal from this place, *and* the name of the Chemarims with the priests;

5 And them that worship the host of heaven upon the house-tops; and them that worship *and* that <sup>f</sup>swear by the LORD, and that swear by Malcham;

6 And them that are <sup>g</sup>turned back from the LORD; and *those* that have <sup>h</sup>not sought the LORD, nor inquired for him.

7 <sup>i</sup>Hold thy peace at the presence of the Lord GOD; for the day of the LORD *is* at hand: for the LORD hath prepared <sup>k</sup>a sacrifice, he hath bid his guests.

8 And it shall come to pass in the day of the LORD's sacrifice, that I will punish the princes, and the <sup>l</sup>king's children, and all *such as are* clothed with strange apparel.

9 In the same day also will I punish all those that leap on the threshold, <!-- uncertain: a small mark (possibly a reference symbol) appears before "the threshold" --> which <sup>m</sup>fill their masters' houses with violence and deceit.

10 And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the LORD, *that there shall be* the <sup>n</sup>noise of a cry from the fish-gate, and a howling from the second, and a great crashing from the hills.

11 <sup>o</sup>Howl, ye inhabitants of Maktesh, for all the merchant people are cut down; all they that bear silver are cut off.

12 And it shall come to pass at that time, *that* I will <sup>p</sup>search Jerusalem with candles, and punish the men *that are* <sup>q</sup>settled on their lees; that say in their heart, The LORD will not <sup>r</sup>do good, neither will he do evil.

13 Therefore their goods <sup>s</sup>shall become a booty, and their houses a desolation: they shall also build houses, but not inhabit *them;* and they shall plant vineyards, but not drink the wine thereof.

14 The <sup>t</sup>great day of the LORD *is* near, *it is* near, and hasteth greatly, *even* the voice of the day of the LORD: the mighty man shall cry there bitterly.

15 <sup>u</sup>That day *is* a day of wrath, a day of trouble and distress, a day of wasteness and desolation, a day of darkness and gloominess, a day of clouds and thick darkness,

16 A day of the <sup>x</sup>trumpet and alarm against the fenced cities, and against the high towers.

17 And I will bring distress upon men, that they shall walk like <sup>y</sup>blind men, because they have sinned against the LORD: and their blood shall be <sup>z</sup>poured out as dust, and their flesh as the dung.

18 Neither their silver nor their gold shall <sup>a</sup>be able to deliver them in the day of the LORD's wrath; but the whole land shall be devoured by the fire of his jealousy: for he shall make even a <sup>b</sup>speedy riddance of all them that dwell in the land.

## CHAPTER II.

4 The judgment of the Philistines, 8 of Moab and Ammon, 12 of Ethiopia, 13 and Assyria.

GATHER yourselves together, yea, gather together, O nation <sup>a</sup>not desired;

2 Before the <sup>b</sup>decree bring forth, *before* the day pass as the chaff, before the <sup>c</sup>fierce anger of the LORD come upon you, before the day of the LORD's anger come upon you.

3 Seek ye the LORD, all ye <sup>d</sup>meek of the earth, which have wrought his judgment; seek righteousness, seek meekness: it may be ye shall be <sup>e</sup>hid in the day of the LORD's anger.

4 For Gaza shall be forsaken, and Ashkelon a desolation: they shall drive out Ashdod at the <sup>f</sup>noon-day, and Ekron shall be rooted up.

5 Wo unto the inhabitants of the sea-coasts, the nation of the <sup>g</sup>Cherethites! the word of the LORD *is* against you; <sup>h</sup>O Canaan, the land of the <sup>i</sup>Philistines, I will even destroy thee, that there shall be no inhabitant.

6 And the sea-coast shall be dwellings *and* cottages for shepherds, and folds for flocks.

7 And the coast shall be for the <sup>k</sup>remnant of the house of Judah; they shall feed thereupon: in the houses of <sup>l</sup>Ashkelon shall they lie down in the evening; for the LORD their God shall visit them, and <sup>m</sup>turn away their captivity.

8 ¶ I have heard the <sup>n</sup>reproach of Moab, and the revilings of the children of Ammon, whereby they have reproached my people, and magnified *themselves* against their border.

9 Therefore, *as* I live, saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, Surely <sup>o</sup>Moab shall be as Sodom, and the children of Ammon as Gomorrah, *even* the breeding of nettles, and salt-pits, and a perpetual desolation: the residue of my people shall <sup>p</sup>spoil them, and the remnant of my people shall possess them.

10 This shall they have for their <sup>q</sup>pride, because they have reproached and magnified *themselves* against the people of the LORD of hosts.

11 The LORD *will be* terrible unto them: for he will <sup>r</sup>famish all the gods of the earth; and *men* shall worship him, every one from his <sup>s</sup>place, *even* all the isles of the heathen.
