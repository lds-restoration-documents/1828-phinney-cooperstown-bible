## PSALM CXXXIX.

David praiseth God for his all-seeing providence.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David.*

O LORD, thou hast <sup>a</sup>searched me, and known *me.*

## PSALM CXLI.

*A Psalm of David.*
