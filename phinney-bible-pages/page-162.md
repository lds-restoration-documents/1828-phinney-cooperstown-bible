4 So there went up thither of the people about three thousand men: and they fled <sup>f</sup>before the men of Ai.

5 And the men of Ai smote of them about thirty and six men: for they chased them *from* before the gate *even* unto Shebarim, and smote them in the going down; wherefore the hearts of the people <sup>g</sup>melted, and became as water.

6 And Joshua <sup>h</sup>rent his clothes, and fell to the earth upon his face <sup>i</sup>before the ark of the LORD until the even-tide, he and the <sup>k</sup>elders of Israel, and <sup>l</sup>put dust upon their heads.

7 And Joshua said, Alas! O LORD God, <sup>m</sup>wherefore hast thou at all brought this people over Jordan, <sup>n</sup>to deliver us into the hand of the Amorites, to destroy us? Would to God we had been content, and dwelt on the other side Jordan!

8 <sup>†</sup>O LORD, <sup>o</sup>what shall I say, <sup>†</sup>when Israel turneth their backs before their enemies!

9 For the Canaanites, and all the inhabitants of the land shall hear *of it*, and shall environ us around, and <sup>p</sup>cut off our name from the earth: and <sup>q</sup>what wilt thou do unto thy great name?

10 And the LORD said unto Joshua, <sup>r</sup>Get thee up; wherefore <sup>s</sup>liest thou thus upon thy face?

11 <sup>t</sup>Israel hath sinned, and they have also transgressed <sup>u</sup>my covenant which I commanded them: for they have even taken of the accursed thing, and have also <sup>x</sup>stolen, and dissembled also, and they have <sup>y</sup>put *it* even among their own stuff.

12 Therefore the children of Israel <sup>z</sup>could not stand before their enemies, *but* turned *their* backs before their enemies, because they were accursed: neither will I be with you any more, except ye destroy the accursed from among you.

13 Up, <sup>a</sup>sanctify the people, and say, Sanctify yourselves against to-morrow: for thus saith the LORD God of Israel, *There is* an accursed thing in the midst of thee, O Israel: thou canst not stand before thine enemies, until ye take away the accursed thing from among you.

14 In the morning therefore ye shall be brought according to your tribes: and it shall be, *that* the tribe which the LORD <sup>b</sup>taketh shall come according to the families *thereof*; and the family which the LORD shall take shall come by households; and the household which the LORD shall take shall come man by man.

15 And it shall be, *that* he that is <sup>c</sup>taken with the accursed thing shall be <sup>d</sup>burnt with fire, he and all that he hath: because he hath transgressed the covenant of the LORD, and because he hath wrought folly <sup>e</sup>in Israel.

16 So Joshua <sup>f</sup>rose up early in the morning, and brought Israel by their tribes; and the tribe of Judah was taken:

17 And he brought the family of <sup>g</sup>Judah; and he took the family of the Zarhites: and he brought the family of the Zarhites man by man; and Zabdi was taken:

18 And he brought his household man by man; and Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was taken.

19 And Joshua said unto Achan, <sup>h</sup>My son, give, I pray thee, <sup>i</sup>glory to the LORD God of Israel, and <sup>k</sup>make confession unto him; and tell me now what thou hast done; hide *it* not from me.

20 And Achan answered Joshua, and said, Indeed I have sinned against the LORD God of Israel, and thus and thus have I done:

21 When I <sup>l</sup>saw among the spoils a goodly <sup>†</sup>Babylonish garment, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a wedge of gold of fifty shekels weight, then I <sup>m</sup>coveted them, and took them; and, behold, they *are* <sup>n</sup>hid in the earth in the midst of my tent, and the silver under it.

22 So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran unto the tent; and, behold, *it was* hid in his <sup>o</sup>tent, and the silver under it.

23 And they took them out of the midst of the tent, and brought them unto Joshua, and unto all the children of Israel, and <sup>†</sup>laid them out before the LORD.

24 And Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan the son of Zerah, and the silver, and the garment, and the <sup>†</sup>wedge of gold, and his <sup>p</sup>sons, and his daughters, and his oxen, and his asses, and his sheep, and his tent, and <sup>q</sup>all that he had: and they brought them unto the valley of Achor.

25 And Joshua said, Why hast thou <sup>r</sup>troubled us? the LORD shall trouble thee this day. And all Israel stoned him with stones, and burned them with fire after they had stoned them with stones.

26 And they raised over him a great <sup>s</sup>heap of stones unto this day. <sup>t</sup>So the LORD turned from the fierceness of his anger. Wherefore the name of that place was called, The valley of <sup>||</sup>Achor, unto this day.

## CHAPTER VIII.

1 God encourageth Joshua....3 The stratagem whereby Ai was taken....30 Joshua buildeth an altar....34 propoundeth blessings and cursings.

AND the LORD said unto Joshua, <sup>a</sup>Fear not, neither be thou dismayed: <sup>b</sup>take all the people of war with thee, and arise, go up to Ai: see, <sup>c</sup>I have given into thy hand the king of Ai, and his people, and his city, and his land.

2 And thou shalt do to Ai and her king as thou didst unto <sup>d</sup>Jericho and her king: only the <sup>e</sup>spoil thereof, and the cattle thereof, shall ye take for a prey unto yourselves: lay thee an ambush for the city <sup>f</sup>behind it.

3 So Joshua arose, and all the people of war, to go up against Ai: and Joshua chose out <sup>g</sup>thirty thousand mighty men of valour, and sent them away <sup>h</sup>by night.

4 And he commanded them, saying, Behold, ye shall lie <sup>||</sup>in wait against the city, *even* behind the city: go not very far from the city, but be ye all ready:

5 And I, and all the people that *are* with me, will approach unto the city: and it shall come to pass, when they come out against us, as at the first, that we will <sup>i</sup>flee before them,

6 (For they will come out after us,) till we have <sup>†</sup>drawn them from the city: for they <sup>k</sup>will say, They flee before us, as at the first: therefore we will flee before them.

7 Then ye shall rise up from the ambush, and seize upon the city: for the LORD your God will deliver it into your hand.

8 And it shall be when ye have taken the city, *that* ye shall set the city on fire: according to the commandment of the LORD shall ye do. See, <sup>l</sup>I have commanded you.

9 Joshua therefore sent them forth: and they went to lie in ambush, and abode between Beth-el and Ai, on the west side of Ai: but Joshua lodged that night among the people.

10 And Joshua rose up <sup>m</sup>early in the morning, and numbered the people, and went up, he and the elders of Israel, before the people to Ai.
