1 The law of the trespass-offering, 11 and of the peace-offerings....22 The fat, 26 and the blood, are forbidden....28 The priest's portion in the peace-offerings.

LIKEWISE this *is* the law of <sup>a</sup>the trespass-offering: it *is* <sup>b</sup>most holy.

2 In the <sup>c</sup>place where they kill the burnt-offering shall they kill the trespass-offering: and the blood thereof shall he <sup>d</sup>sprinkle round about upon the altar.

3 And he shall offer of it all the fat thereof; the rump, and the fat that covereth the <sup>e</sup>inwards,

4 And the two kidneys, and the fat that *is* <sup>f</sup>on them, which *is* by the flanks, and the caul *that is* above the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he take away:

5 And the priest shall burn them upon the altar *for* an offering made by fire unto the LORD: it *is* a trespass-offering.

6 Every male among the priests shall eat thereof: it shall be eaten in the holy place: it *is* most holy.

7 As the <sup>†</sup>sin-offering *is,* so *is* the trespass-offering: *there is* one law for them: the priest that maketh atonement therewith shall have *it.*

8 And the priest that offereth any man's burnt-offering, *even* the priest shall have to himself the <sup>g</sup>skin of the burnt-offering which he hath offered.

9 And all the meat-offering that is baken in the oven, and all that is dressed in the <sup>h</sup>frying-pan, and in the pan, <sup>i</sup>shall be the priest's that offereth it.

10 And every meat-offering, mingled <sup>k</sup>with oil, and dry, shall all the sons of Aaron have, one *as much* as another.

11 ¶ And this *is* the law of the sacrifice of <sup>l</sup>peace-offerings, which he shall offer unto the LORD.

12 If he offer it for a thanksgiving, then he shall offer with the <sup>m</sup>sacrifice of thanksgiving unleavened cakes mingled with oil, and <sup>n</sup>unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and cakes mingled with oil, of fine flour, <sup>o</sup>fried.

13 Besides the cakes, he shall offer *for* his offering <sup>p</sup>leavened bread with the sacrifice of thanksgiving of his peace-offerings.

14 And of it he shall offer one out of the whole oblation *for* a <sup>q</sup>heave-offering unto the LORD, *and* it shall be the priest's that sprinkleth the blood of the peace-offerings.

15 And the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace-offerings for thanksgiving shall <sup>r</sup>be eaten the same day that it is offered; he shall not leave any of it until the morning.

16 But if the sacrifice of his offering *be* a <sup>s</sup>vow, or a <sup>t</sup>voluntary offering, it shall be eaten the same day that he offereth his sacrifice: and on the morrow also the remainder of it shall be eaten:

17 But the remainder of the flesh of the sacrifice on the <sup>u</sup>third day shall be burnt with fire.

18 And if *any* of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace-offerings be eaten at all on the third day, it shall <sup>x</sup>not be accepted, neither shall it be imputed unto him that offereth it: it shall be an <sup>y</sup>abomination, and the soul that eateth of it shall <sup>z</sup>bear his iniquity.

19 And the flesh that <sup>a</sup>toucheth any unclean *thing* shall not be eaten; it shall be burnt with fire: and as for the flesh, all that be clean shall eat thereof.

20 But the soul that eateth *of* the flesh of the sacrifice of peace-offerings that *pertain* unto the LORD, having his uncleanness upon him, even that soul shall be cut off from his people.

21 Moreover, the soul that shall touch any unclean *thing, as* the uncleanness of <sup>b</sup>man, or *any* unclean beast, or any abominable unclean *thing,* and eat of the flesh of the sacrifice of <sup>c</sup>peace-offerings which *pertain* unto the LORD, even that soul shall be cut off from his people.
