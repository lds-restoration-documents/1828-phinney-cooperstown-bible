## CHAPTER II.

Isaiah prophesieth the coming of Christ's kingdom.

THE word that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem.

8 Their land also is full of <sup>q</sup>idols; they worship the <sup>r</sup>work of their own hands, that which their own fingers have made.

9 And the mean man boweth down, and the great man humbleth himself: therefore <sup>s</sup>forgive them not.

10 Enter into the <sup>t</sup>rock, and hide thee in the dust, for <sup>u</sup>fear of the LORD, and for the glory of his majesty.

11 The lofty looks of man shall be humbled, and the haughtiness of men shall be <sup>x</sup>bowed down; and the LORD alone shall be exalted in that day.

12 For the day of the LORD of hosts *shall be* upon every *one that is* <sup>y</sup>proud and lofty, and upon every *one that is* lifted up, and he shall be brought low;

13 And upon all the cedars of Lebanon, *that are* high and lifted up, and upon all the oaks of Bashan,

14 And upon all the <sup>z</sup>high mountains, and upon all the hills *that are* lifted up,

15 And upon every high tower, and upon every fenced wall,

16 And upon all the <sup>a</sup>ships of Tarshish, and upon all pleasant <sup>b</sup>pictures.

17 And the <sup>c</sup>loftiness of man shall be bowed down, and the haughtiness of men shall be made low; and the LORD <sup>d</sup>alone shall be exalted in that day.

18 And the <sup>e</sup>idols he shall utterly abolish.

19 And they shall go into the <sup>f</sup>holes of the rocks, and into the caves of the earth, for fear of the LORD, and for the glory of his majesty, when he ariseth <sup>g</sup>to shake terribly the earth.
