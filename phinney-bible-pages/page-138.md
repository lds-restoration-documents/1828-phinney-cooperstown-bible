3 Hear therefore, O Israel, and observe to do *it,* that it may be well with thee, and that ye may increase mightily, as the LORD God of thy fathers hath promised thee, in the land that floweth with <sup>d</sup>milk and honey.

4 <sup>e</sup>Hear, O Israel: the LORD our God *is* <sup>f</sup>one LORD;

5 And thou shalt <sup>g</sup>love the LORD thy God with all thine <sup>h</sup>heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy <sup>i</sup>might.

7 And thou shalt <sup>†</sup>teach them diligently unto thy children, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up.

8 And thou shalt bind them for a sign upon thine hand, and they shall be as <sup>l</sup>frontlets between thine eyes.

9 And thou shalt write them upon the <sup>m</sup>posts of thy house, and on thy gates.

10 And it shall be, when the LORD thy God shall have brought thee into the land which he sware unto thy fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give thee great and goodly <sup>n</sup>cities, which thou buildedst not,

11 And houses <sup>o</sup>full of all good *things,* which thou filledst not, and wells digged, which thou diggedst not, vineyards and olive-trees, which thou plantedst not; when thou shalt have eaten, and be full;

12 *Then* beware lest thou <sup>p</sup>forget the LORD, which brought thee forth out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.

13 Thou shalt fear the LORD thy God, and <sup>q</sup>serve him, and shalt <sup>r</sup>swear by his name.

14 Ye shall not <sup>s</sup>go after other gods, of the gods of the people which *are* round about you;

15 (For the LORD thy God *is* <sup>t</sup>a jealous God among you,) lest the anger of the LORD thy God be kindled against thee, and destroy thee from off the face of the earth.

16 Ye shall <sup>u</sup>not tempt the LORD your God, as ye tempted *him* in <sup>x</sup>Massah.

17 You shall <sup>y</sup>diligently keep the commandments of the LORD your God, and his testimonies, and his statutes, which he hath commanded thee.

18 And thou shalt do *that which is* <sup>z</sup>right and good in the sight of the LORD; that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest go in and possess the good land which the LORD sware unto thy fathers;

19 To <sup>a</sup>cast out all thine enemies from before thee, as the LORD hath spoken.

20 *And* when thy son asketh thee in <sup>†</sup>time to come, saying, What *mean* the testimonies, and the statutes, and the judgments, which the LORD our God hath <sup>b</sup>commanded you?

21 Then thou shalt say unto thy son, We were <sup>c</sup>Pharaoh's bondmen in Egypt; and the LORD brought us out of Egypt with <sup>d</sup>a mighty hand:

22 And the LORD shewed signs and wonders, great and <sup>†</sup>sore, upon Egypt, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his household, <sup>e</sup>before our eyes:

23 And he <sup>f</sup>brought us out from thence, that he might bring us in, to give us the land which he sware unto our fathers.

24 And the LORD commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear the LORD our God, <sup>g</sup>for our good always, that he might preserve us <sup>h</sup>alive, as *it is* at this day.

25 And it shall be <sup>i</sup>our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments before the LORD our God, as he hath commanded us.

## CHAPTER VII.

1 All communion with the nations is forbidden.

WHEN the LORD thy God shall bring thee into the land whither thou goest to possess it, and hath <sup>a</sup>cast out many nations before thee, the Hittites, and the Girgashites, and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, <sup>b</sup>seven nations greater and mightier than thou:

2 And when the LORD thy God shall deliver them <sup>c</sup>before thee; thou shalt smite them, *and* utterly destroy them: thou shalt <sup>d</sup>make no covenant with them, nor shew mercy unto them:

3 Neither shalt thou make <sup>e</sup>marriages with them; thy daughter thou shalt not give unto his son, nor his daughter shalt thou take unto thy son.

4 For they will turn away thy son from following me, that they may serve <sup>f</sup>other gods: so will the anger of the LORD be <sup>g</sup>kindled against you, and destroy thee suddenly.

5 But thus shall ye deal with them: ye shall destroy their altars, and break down their <sup>†</sup>images, and cut down their <sup>h</sup>groves, and burn their graven images with fire.

6 For thou *art* a <sup>i</sup>holy people unto the LORD thy God: the LORD thy God hath chosen thee to be <sup>k</sup>a special people unto himself, above all people that *are* upon the face of the earth.

7 The LORD did not set his love upon you, nor choose you, because ye were more in number than any people; for ye *were* the <sup>l</sup>fewest of all people;

8 But because the LORD loved <sup>m</sup>you, and because he would keep the <sup>n</sup>oath which he had sworn unto your fathers, hath the LORD brought you out with a mighty hand, and redeemed you out of the house of <sup>o</sup>bondmen, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.

9 Know therefore that the LORD thy God, he *is* God, the <sup>p</sup>faithful God, which keepeth covenant and mercy with them that <sup>q</sup>love him and keep his commandments, to a thousand generations;

10 And repayeth them that hate him to their face, to destroy them: he will not be slack to him that hateth him, he will <sup>r</sup>repay him to his <sup>s</sup>face.

11 Thou shalt therefore keep the commandments, and the statutes, and the judgments, which I command thee this day, to do them.

12 Wherefore it shall come to pass, <sup>†</sup>if ye hearken to these judgments, and <sup>t</sup>keep and do them, that the LORD thy God shall keep unto thee the covenant and the <sup>u</sup>mercy which he <sup>x</sup>sware unto thy fathers:
