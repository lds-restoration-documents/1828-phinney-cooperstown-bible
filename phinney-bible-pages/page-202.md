## CHAPTER XIII.

1 Saul's selected band....3 He calleth the Hebrews to Gilgal against the Philistines.

SAUL <sup>†</sup>reigned one year; and when he had reigned <sup>a</sup>two years over Israel,

2 Saul chose him <sup>b</sup>three thousand *men* of Israel; *whereof* two thousand were with Saul in Michmash and in mount Beth-el, and a thousand were with Jonathan in <sup>c</sup>Gibeah of Benjamin: and the rest of the people he sent every man to his tent.

3 And Jonathan smote <sup>d</sup>the garrison of the Philistines that *was* in <sup>e</sup>Geba; and the Philistines heard *of it.* And Saul blew the trumpet throughout all the land, saying, Let the Hebrews hear.

4 And all Israel heard say, <sup>f</sup>*that* Saul had smitten a garrison of the Philistines, and *that* Israel also was had in abomination with the Philistines. And the people were called together after Saul to Gilgal.

5 And the Philistines gathered themselves together to fight with Israel, thirty thousand chariots, and six thousand horsemen, and people as the sand which *is* on the <sup>g</sup>sea-shore in multitude: and they came up and pitched in Michmash, eastward from <sup>h</sup>Beth-aven.

6 When the men of Israel saw that they were in a <sup>i</sup>strait, (for the people were distressed,) then the people did hide themselves in caves, and in thickets, and in rocks, and in high places, and in pits.

7 And *some of* the Hebrews went over Jordan to the land of Gad and Gilead. As for Saul, he *was* yet in Gilgal; and all the people <sup>†</sup>followed him trembling.

8 And he tarried <sup>k</sup>seven days, according to the set time that Samuel *had appointed;* but Samuel came not to Gilgal; and the people were scattered from him.

9 And Saul <sup>l</sup>said, Bring hither a burnt-offering to me, and peace-offerings. And he <sup>m</sup>offered the burnt-offering.

10 And it came to pass, that, <sup>n</sup>as soon as he had made an end of offering the burnt-offering, behold, Samuel came; and Saul went out to meet him, that he might salute him.

11 And Samuel said, What hast thou done? And Saul said, Because I saw that the people were scattered from me, and *that* thou camest not <sup>o</sup>within the days appointed, and *that* the Philistines gathered themselves together at Michmash;

12 Therefore, <sup>p</sup>said I, the Philistines will come down now upon me to Gilgal, and I have not made supplication unto the LORD: <sup>q</sup>I forced myself therefore, and offered a burnt-offering.

13 And Samuel said to Saul, <sup>r</sup>Thou hast done <sup>s</sup>foolishly: thou hast not kept the commandment of the LORD thy God, which he commanded thee; for now would the LORD have established thy kingdom upon Israel for ever.

14 But now thy kingdom shall not continue: the LORD hath <sup>t</sup>sought him a man after his own heart, and the LORD hath commanded him *to be* captain over his people, because thou hast not kept *that* which the LORD commanded thee.
