## CHAPTER LI.

The judgment of God against Babylon in revenge of Israel.

THUS saith the LORD, Behold, I will raise up against Babylon, and against them that dwell in the midst of them that rise up <sup>a</sup>against me, <sup>b</sup>a destroying wind;

2 And will send unto Babylon <sup>c</sup>fanners, that shall fan her, and shall <sup>d</sup>empty her land: for in the day of trouble they shall be against her round about.

3 Against *him that* bendeth let the archer bend his bow, and against *him that* lifteth himself up in his brigandine: and <sup>e</sup>spare ye not her young men; destroy ye utterly all her host.

4 Thus the slain shall fall in the land of the Chaldeans, and *they that are* thrust through in her streets.

5 For Israel *hath* not *been* <sup>||</sup>forsaken, nor Judah of his God, of the LORD of hosts; though their land was <sup>f</sup>filled with sin against the Holy One of Israel.

6 <sup>g</sup>Flee out of the midst of Babylon, and deliver every man <sup>h</sup>his soul; be not cut off in her iniquity: for this *is* the <sup>i</sup>time of the LORD's vengeance; he will render unto her a recompense.

7 Babylon *hath been* <sup>k</sup>a golden cup in the LORD's hand, that <sup>l</sup>made all the earth drunken: the nations have drunken of her wine; therefore the nations are mad.

8 Babylon is <sup>m</sup>suddenly fallen and destroyed: <sup>n</sup>howl for her; take balm for her pain, if so be she may be healed.

9 <sup>o</sup>We would have healed Babylon, but she <sup>p</sup>is not healed: forsake her, and <sup>q</sup>let us go every one into his own country; for her judgment reacheth unto heaven, and is lifted up *even* to the skies.

10 The LORD hath <sup>r</sup>brought forth our righteousness: come, and let us <sup>s</sup>declare in Zion the work of the LORD our God.

11 Make bright the arrows, gather the shields: the LORD hath <sup>t</sup>raised up the spirit of the kings of the Medes: for his device *is* against Babylon, to destroy it; because it *is* the <sup>u</sup>vengeance of the LORD, the vengeance of his temple.

12 Set up the <sup>x</sup>standard upon the walls of Babylon, make the watch strong; set up the watchmen, prepare the ambushes: for the LORD hath both devised and done that which he spake against the inhabitants of Babylon.

13 O thou that dwellest upon many <sup>y</sup>waters, abundant in treasures, thine end is come, *and* the <sup>z</sup>measure of thy covetousness.

14 The LORD of hosts hath sworn by himself, *saying,* Surely I will fill thee with men as with <sup>a</sup>caterpillars; and they shall <sup>b</sup>lift up a shout against thee.

15 He hath <sup>c</sup>made the earth by his power, he hath established the world by his wisdom, and hath stretched out the heaven by his understanding.

16 When he uttereth *his* voice, *there is* a multitude of waters in the heavens; and he causeth the <sup>d</sup>vapours to ascend from the ends of the earth: he maketh lightnings with rain, and bringeth forth the wind out of his treasures.

17 Every man is <sup>e</sup>brutish by *his* knowledge; every founder is confounded by the graven image: for his molten image *is* falsehood, and *there is* no breath in them.

18 They *are* vanity, the work of errors: in the time of their <sup>f</sup>visitation they shall perish.

19 <sup>g</sup>The Portion of Jacob *is* <sup>h</sup>not like them; for he *is* the former of all things; and *Israel is* the rod of his inheritance; The LORD of hosts *is* his name.

20 Thou *art* my <sup>i</sup>battle-axe *and* weapons of war: for with thee will I break in pieces the nations; and with thee will I destroy kingdoms;

21 And with thee will I break in pieces the horse and his rider; and with thee will I break in pieces the <sup>k</sup>chariot and his rider;

22 With thee also will I break in pieces man and woman; and with thee will I break in pieces <sup>l</sup>old and young; and with thee will I break in pieces the young man and the maid;

23 I will also break in pieces with thee <sup>m</sup>the shepherd and his flock; and with thee will I break in pieces the husbandman and his yoke of oxen; and with thee will I break in pieces captains and rulers.

24 And I will render unto Babylon, and to all the inhabitants of Chaldea, all their evil that they have done <sup>n</sup>in Zion in your sight, saith the LORD.

25 Behold, I *am* against thee, O destroying <sup>o</sup>mountain, saith the LORD, which <sup>p</sup>destroyest all the earth; and I will stretch out my hand upon thee, and roll thee down from the <sup>q</sup>rocks, and will make thee <sup>r</sup>a burnt mountain.

26 And they shall not take of thee a <sup>s</sup>stone for a corner, nor a stone for foundations; but thou shalt be desolate for ever, saith the LORD.

27 Set ye up a standard in the land, blow the trumpet among the nations, <sup>t</sup>prepare the nations against her, call together against her the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni, and Ashchenaz; appoint a captain against her; cause the horses to come up as the rough caterpillars.

28 Prepare against her the nations, with the kings of the Medes, the captains thereof, and all the rulers thereof, and all the land of his dominion.

29 And the <sup>u</sup>land shall tremble and sorrow: for <sup>x</sup>every purpose of the LORD shall be performed against Babylon, to make the land of Babylon a desolation without an inhabitant.

30 The mighty men of Babylon have forborne to fight, they have remained in *their* <sup>y</sup>holds: their <sup>z</sup>might hath failed; they became as women: they have burned her dwelling-places; her <sup>a</sup>bars are broken.

31 <sup>b</sup>One post shall run to meet another, and one messenger to meet another, to shew the king of Babylon that his city is taken at *one* end,

32 And that the passages are stopped, and the <sup>c</sup>reeds they have burnt with fire, and the <sup>d</sup>men of war are affrighted.

33 For thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, The daughter of Babylon *is* like <sup>e</sup>a threshing-floor, *it is* time <sup>f</sup>to thresh her: yet a little while, and the time of her <sup>g</sup>harvest shall come.
