## CHAPTER XVIII.

Under the type of a potter is shewed God's absolute power

THE word which came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying,

2 <sup>a</sup>Arise, and go down to <sup>b</sup>the potter's house, and <sup>c</sup>there I will cause thee to hear my words.

3 Then <sup>d</sup>I went down to the potter's house, and, behold, he wrought a work on the wheels.

4 And the vessel that <sup>e</sup>he made of clay was marred in the hand of the potter; so he made it again another vessel, <sup>f</sup>as seemed good to the potter to make *it.*

5 Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

6 O house of Israel, cannot I do with you <sup>g</sup>as this potter? saith the LORD. Behold, as the clay *is* in the potter's hand, <sup>h</sup>so *are* ye in my hand, O house of Israel.

7 *At what* instant I shall speak concerning a nation, and concerning a kingdom, <sup>i</sup>to pluck up, and to pull down, and to destroy *it:*

8 If that nation, against whom I have pronounced, <sup>k</sup>turn from their evil, I will <sup>l</sup>repent of the evil that I thought to do unto them.

9 And *at what* instant I shall speak concerning a nation, and concerning a kingdom, to build, and to plant *it;*

10 If it do evil in my sight, that it obey not my voice, then I will repent of the <sup>m</sup>good wherewith I said I would benefit them.

11 Now, therefore, go to, speak to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying, Thus saith the LORD, Behold, <sup>n</sup>I frame evil against you, and devise a device against you: <sup>o</sup>return ye now every one from his evil way, and make your ways and your doings good.

12 And they said, There is <sup>p</sup>no hope; but <sup>q</sup>we will walk after our own devices, and we will every one do the imagination of his evil heart.

13 Therefore thus saith the LORD, <sup>r</sup>Ask ye now among the heathen, who hath heard such things? the <sup>s</sup>virgin of Israel hath done a very <sup>t</sup>horrible thing.

14 Will *a man* leave the snow of Lebanon *which cometh* from the rock of the field? *or* shall the cold flowing waters that come from another place be forsaken?

15 Because my people hath forgotten me, they have burnt incense to vanity, and they have <sup>u</sup>caused them to stumble in their ways *from* the <sup>x</sup>ancient paths, to walk in paths *in* a way not cast up;

16 To make their land desolate, *and* a perpetual <sup>y</sup>hissing: every one that passeth thereby shall be astonished, and wag his head.

17 I will <sup>z</sup>scatter them as with an <sup>a</sup>east wind before the enemy: I will shew them the back, and not the face, in the day of their calamity.

18 <sup>b</sup>Then said they, Come, and let us <sup>c</sup>devise devices against Jeremiah; for the <sup>d</sup>law shall not perish from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet: come, and let us smite him with the <sup>e</sup>tongue, and let us not give heed to any of his words.

19 Give heed to me, O LORD, and hearken to the voice of them that contend with me.

20 Shall <sup>f</sup>evil be recompensed for good? for they have digged a pit for my soul. Remember that I stood before thee to speak good for them, *and* <sup>g</sup>to turn away thy wrath from them.

21 Therefore <sup>h</sup>deliver up their children to the famine, and pour out their *blood* by the force of the sword; and let their wives be bereaved of their children, and *be* widows; and let their men be put to death; *let* their young men *be* slain by the sword in battle.

22 Let a cry be heard from their houses, when thou shalt bring <sup>i</sup>a troop suddenly upon them; for they have <sup>k</sup>digged a pit to take me, and hid snares for my feet.

23 Yet, LORD, thou knowest all their counsel against me to slay *me:* <sup>l</sup>forgive not their iniquity, neither <sup>m</sup>blot out their sin from thy sight; but let them be overthrown before thee: deal *thus* with them in the time of thine anger.
