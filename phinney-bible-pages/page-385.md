## PSALM LXXVII.

The Psalmist sheweth what fierce combat he had with diffidence.

*To the chief Musician, to Jeduthun. A Psalm of Asaph.*

I <sup>a</sup>CRIED unto God with my voice, *even* unto God with my voice; and he gave <sup>b</sup>ear unto me.

2 In the day of my trouble I <sup>c</sup>sought the LORD: my sore ran in the night, and <sup>d</sup>ceased not: my soul refused to be comforted.

3 I remembered God, and <sup>e</sup>was troubled: I complained, and my spirit was <sup>f</sup>overwhelmed. Selah.

4 Thou <sup>g</sup>holdest mine eyes waking: I am so troubled that I cannot speak.

5 I have considered the <sup>h</sup>days of old, the years of ancient times.

6 I call to remembrance my song in the night: I <sup>i</sup>commune with mine own heart, and my spirit made diligent search.

7 Will the LORD cast off for ever? and will he be favourable no more?

8 Is his mercy clean gone <sup>k</sup>for ever? doth *his* promise fail for evermore?

9 Hath God <sup>l</sup>forgotten to be gracious? hath he in anger shut up his tender mercies? Selah.

10 And I said, This *is* my <sup>m</sup>infirmity: *but I will remember* the years of the right hand of the Most High.

11 I will remember the works of the LORD; surely I will remember thy wonders <sup>n</sup>of old.

12 I will meditate also of all thy work, and talk of thy doings.

13 Thy way, O God, *is* in <sup>o</sup>the sanctuary: who *is* so great a God as *our* God!

14 Thou *art* the God that <sup>p</sup>doest wonders: thou hast declared thy strength among the people.

15 Thou hast with *thine* <sup>q</sup>arm redeemed thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

16 The <sup>r</sup>waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee: they were afraid; the depths also were troubled.

17 The <sup>s</sup>clouds poured out water; the skies sent out a sound: thine <sup>t</sup>arrows also went abroad.

18 The voice of thy thunder *was* in the heaven: the lightnings lightened the world: the earth trembled and shook.

19 Thy way *is* <sup>u</sup>in the sea, and thy path in the great waters, and thy footsteps are <sup>x</sup>not known.

20 Thou <sup>y</sup>leddest thy people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

## PSALM LXXVIII.

An exhortation both to learn and to preach the law of God.

*Maschil of Asaph.*

GIVE ear, O <sup>a</sup>my people, *to* my law: <sup>b</sup>incline your ears to the words of my mouth.

2 I will <sup>c</sup>open my mouth in <sup>d</sup>a parable; I will utter dark sayings of old;

3 Which we have <sup>e</sup>heard and known, and our fathers have told us.

4 We will not hide *them* from their children, shewing to the generation to come the <sup>f</sup>praises of the LORD, and his strength, and his wonderful works that he hath done.

5 For he established <sup>g</sup>a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, that they should make them known <sup>h</sup>to their children;

6 That the generation to come might know *them, even* the children *which* should be born, *who* should arise and <sup>i</sup>declare *them* to their children:

7 That they might set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God; but keep his commandments:

8 And might not be <sup>k</sup>as their fathers, <sup>l</sup>a stubborn and rebellious generation; a generation *that* set not their heart aright, and whose spirit was not steadfast with God.

9 The <sup>m</sup>children of Ephraim, *being* armed, *and* carrying bows, turned back in the day of battle.

10 They kept not the covenant of God, and <sup>n</sup>refused to walk in his law;

11 And <sup>o</sup>forgat his works, and his wonders that he had shewed them.

12 Marvellous things did he in the sight of their fathers in the land of Egypt, *in* the field of <sup>p</sup>Zoan.

13 He <sup>q</sup>divided the sea, and caused them to pass through; and he made the waters to stand as a heap.

14 In the day-time also he <sup>r</sup>led them with a cloud, and all the night with a light of fire.

15 He clave the <sup>s</sup>rocks in the wilderness, and gave *them* drink as *out of* the great depths.

16 He brought streams also out of the rock, and caused waters <sup>t</sup>to run down like rivers.

17 And they sinned <sup>u</sup>yet more against him, by <sup>x</sup>provoking the Most High in the wilderness.

18 And they tempted God in their heart, by asking meat for their <sup>†</sup>lust.

19 Yea, they <sup>y</sup>spake against God: they said, Can God furnish a table in the wilderness?

20 Behold, he <sup>z</sup>smote the rock, that the waters gushed out, and the streams overflowed; can he <sup>a</sup>give bread also? can he provide flesh for his people?

21 Therefore the LORD <sup>b</sup>heard *this*, and was <sup>c</sup>wroth: so a fire was kindled against Jacob, and anger also came up against Israel;

22 Because they <sup>d</sup>believed not in God, and <sup>e</sup>trusted not in his salvation;

23 Though he had commanded the clouds from above, and opened the <sup>f</sup>doors of heaven,

24 And had rained down <sup>g</sup>manna upon them to eat, and had given them of the corn of heaven.

25 Man did eat <sup>†</sup>angel's food: he sent them <sup>h</sup>meat to the full.

26 He caused an east wind <sup>i</sup>to blow in the heaven; and by his power he brought in the south wind.

27 He rained <sup>k</sup>flesh also upon them as dust, and feathered fowls like as the sand of the sea;

28 And he let *it* fall in the midst of their camp, round about their habitations.

29 So they did eat, and were well filled: for he gave them their own desire;

30 They were not estranged from their <sup>l</sup>lust: but while their meat *was* yet in their mouths,

31 The wrath of God came upon them, and slew the fattest of them, and smote down the chosen *men* of Israel.

32 For all this they <sup>m</sup>sinned still, and believed not for his wondrous works.

33 Therefore their days did he consume <sup>n</sup>in vanity, and their years in trouble.
