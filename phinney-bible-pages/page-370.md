## PSALM XXX.

David praiseth God for his deliverance.

*A Psalm and Song at the* <sup>a</sup>*dedication of the house of David.*

I WILL <sup>b</sup>extol thee, O LORD: for thou hast lifted me up, and hast not made my foes <sup>c</sup>to rejoice over me.

2 O LORD my God, I cried unto thee, and thou hast <sup>d</sup>healed me.

3 O LORD, thou hast <sup>e</sup>brought up my soul from the grave: thou hast kept me alive, that I should not go down to the pit.

4 Sing unto the LORD, O ye saints of his, and give thanks <sup>f</sup>at the remembrance of his holiness.

5 For his anger *endureth but* <sup>g</sup>a moment; in his <sup>h</sup>favour *is* life: weeping may endure for <sup>i</sup>a night, but joy *cometh* in the morning.

6 And in my <sup>k</sup>prosperity I said, I shall never be moved.

7 LORD, by thy favour thou hast made my <sup>l</sup>mountain to stand strong: thou <sup>m</sup>didst hide thy face, *and* I was troubled.

8 I cried to thee, O LORD; and unto the LORD I made <sup>n</sup>supplication.

9 What profit *is there* in my blood, when I go down to the pit? Shall the <sup>o</sup>dust praise thee? shall it declare thy truth?

10 Hear, O LORD, and have mercy upon me: LORD, be thou my helper.

11 Thou hast turned for me my mourning into <sup>p</sup>dancing: thou hast put off my sackcloth, and girded me with gladness;

12 To the end that *my* glory may sing praise to thee, and not be silent. O LORD my God, I will give thanks unto thee for ever.

## PSALM XXXI.

David, shewing his confidence in God, craveth his help.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David.*

IN thee, O LORD, do I put <sup>a</sup>my trust; let me never be ashamed: deliver me in <sup>b</sup>thy righteousness.

2 Bow down thine ear to me; deliver me speedily: be thou my <sup>c</sup>strong rock, for a house of defence to save me.

3 For thou *art* my rock and my fortress: therefore, for thy name's sake, <sup>d</sup>lead me and guide me.

4 Pull me out of the net that they have laid <sup>e</sup>privily for me; for thou *art* my strength.

5 Into thy hand <sup>f</sup>I commit my spirit: thou hast redeemed me, O LORD God of truth.

6 I have hated them that regard <sup>g</sup>lying vanities: but I trust in the LORD.

7 I will be glad and rejoice in thy mercy: for thou hast considered my trouble; thou hast <sup>h</sup>known my soul in adversities;

8 And hast not <sup>i</sup>shut me up into the hand of the enemy: thou hast set my feet in <sup>k</sup>a large room.

9 Have mercy upon me, O LORD, for I am in trouble; mine eye is <sup>l</sup>consumed with grief, *yea,* my soul and my belly.

10 For my life is spent with grief, and my years with sighing: my strength <sup>m</sup>faileth because of mine iniquity, and my <sup>n</sup>bones are consumed.

11 I was <sup>o</sup>a reproach among all mine enemies, but especially among <sup>p</sup>my neighbours, and a fear to mine acquaintance: they that did see me without <sup>q</sup>fled from me.

12 I am forgotten as a dead man out <sup>r</sup>of mind: I am like a broken vessel.

13 For I have heard the <sup>s</sup>slander of many: fear *was* on every side: while they took counsel together against me, they devised to take away my life.

14 But I trusted in thee, O LORD: I said, Thou *art* <sup>t</sup>my God.

15 <sup>u</sup>My times *are* in thy hand: deliver me from the hand of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me.

16 <sup>x</sup>Make thy face to shine upon thy servant: save me for thy mercies' sake.

17 Let me not be ashamed, O LORD; for I have called upon thee: let the wicked be ashamed, *and* let them <sup>y</sup>be silent in the grave.

18 Let the <sup>z</sup>lying lips be put to silence; which speak grievous things proudly and contemptuously against the righteous.

19 <sup>a</sup>*Oh* how great *is* thy goodness, which thou hast <sup>b</sup>laid up for them that fear thee; *which* thou hast wrought for them that trust in thee before the sons of men!

20 Thou shalt hide them in the <sup>c</sup>secret of thy presence from the pride of man; thou shalt keep them secretly in a pavilion from the strife of tongues.

21 Blessed *be* the LORD; for he hath shewed me his marvellous kindness in a strong city.

22 For I said <sup>d</sup>in my haste, I am <sup>e</sup>cut off from before thine eyes: nevertheless thou heardest the voice of my supplications, when I cried unto thee.

23 O love the LORD, all ye his saints: *for* the LORD preserveth the faithful, and plentifully rewardeth the <sup>f</sup>proud doer.

24 <sup>g</sup>Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart, all ye that hope in the LORD.

## PSALM XXXII.

Confession of sins giveth ease to the conscience.

*A Psalm of David,* <sup>||</sup>*Maschil.*

BLESSED *is he whose* transgression *is* forgiven, *whose sin is* <sup>a</sup>covered.

2 Blessed *is* the man unto whom the LORD <sup>b</sup>imputeth not iniquity, and in whose spirit *there is* <sup>c</sup>no guile.

3 When I <sup>d</sup>kept silence, my bones waxed old: through my roaring all the day long.

4 (For day and night thy hand was heavy upon me) <sup>e</sup>my moisture is turned into the drought of summer. Selah.

5 I acknowledged my sin unto thee, and mine iniquity have I not hid. I said, I will <sup>f</sup>confess my transgressions unto the LORD; and thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin. Selah.

6 For this shall every one that is godly pray unto thee <sup>g</sup>in a time when thou mayest be found: surely in the floods of great waters they shall <sup>h</sup>not come nigh unto him.

7 Thou *art* <sup>i</sup>my hiding-place; thou shalt preserve me from trouble; thou shalt compass me about with <sup>k</sup>songs of deliverance. Selah.
