5 <sup>e</sup>To whom we gave place by subjection, no, not for an hour; that the truth of the gospel might continue with you.

6 <sup>f</sup>But of those who <sup>†</sup>seemed to be somewhat, whatsoever they were, it maketh no matter to me: God accepteth no man's person: for they who seemed *to be somewhat,* in conference added nothing to me:

7 <sup>g</sup>But contrariwise, when they saw that the gospel of the uncircumcision was committed unto me, as *the gospel* of the circumcision *was* unto Peter;

8 <sup>h</sup>(For he that wrought effectually in Peter to the apostleship of the circumcision, the same was mighty in me toward the Gentiles;)

9 <sup>i</sup>And when James, Cephas, and John, who seemed to be pillars, perceived the grace that was given unto me, they gave to me and Barnabas the right hands of fellowship; that we *should go* unto the heathen, and they unto the circumcision.

10 Only *they would* that we should remember the poor; the same which I also was forward to do.

11 <sup>k</sup>But when Peter was come to Antioch, I withstood him to the face, because he was to be blamed.

12 <sup>l</sup>For before that certain came from James, he did eat with the Gentiles: but when they were come, he withdrew and separated himself, fearing them which were of the circumcision.

13 And the other Jews dissembled likewise with him; insomuch that Barnabas also was carried away with their dissimulation.

14 <sup>m</sup>But when I saw that they <sup>||</sup>walked not uprightly, according to the truth of the gospel, I said unto Peter before *them* all, If thou, being a Jew, livest after the manner of Gentiles, and not as do the Jews, why compellest thou the Gentiles to live as do the Jews?

15 <sup>n</sup>We *who are* Jews by nature, and not sinners of the Gentiles,

16 <sup>o</sup>Knowing that a man is not justified by the works of the law, but by the faith of Jesus Christ, even we have believed in Jesus Christ, that we might be justified by the faith of Christ, and not by the works of the law: for by the works of the law shall no flesh be justified.

17 <sup>p</sup>But if, while we seek to be justified by Christ, we ourselves also are found sinners, *is* therefore Christ the minister of sin? God forbid.

18 For if I build again the things which I <sup>†</sup>destroyed, I make myself a transgressor.

19 <sup>q</sup>For I through the law am dead to the law, that I might live unto God.

20 <sup>r</sup>I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me: and the life which I now live in the flesh, I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me.

21 <sup>s</sup>I do not frustrate the grace of God: for if righteousness *come* by the law, then Christ is dead in vain.

## CHAPTER III.

1 He asketh what moved them to depend on the law, having received the Spirit through faith....6 Abraham being justified by faith, so they who are of faith inherit his blessing.

<sup>a</sup>O FOOLISH Galatians, who hath bewitched you, that ye should not obey the truth, before whose eyes Jesus Christ hath been evidently set forth, crucified among you?

2 <sup>b</sup>This only would I learn of you, Received ye the Spirit by the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?

3 <sup>c</sup>Are ye so foolish? having begun in the Spirit, are ye now made perfect by the flesh?

4 <sup>d</sup>Have ye suffered so many things in vain? if *it be* yet in vain.

5 <sup>e</sup>He therefore that ministereth to you the Spirit, and worketh miracles among you, *doeth he it* by the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?

6 <sup>f</sup>Even as Abraham believed God, and it was accounted to him for righteousness.

7 <sup>g</sup>Know ye therefore, that they which are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham.

8 <sup>h</sup>And the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham, *saying,* In thee shall all nations be blessed.

9 So then they which be of faith are blessed with faithful Abraham.

10 <sup>i</sup>For as many as are of the works of the law, are under the curse: for it is written, Cursed *is* every one that continueth not in all things which are written in the book of the law to do them.

11 <sup>k</sup>But that no man is justified by the law in the sight of God, *it is* evident: for, The just shall live by faith.

12 <sup>l</sup>And the law is not of faith: but, The man that doeth them shall live in them.

13 <sup>m</sup>Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed *is* every one that hangeth on a tree:

14 <sup>n</sup>That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.

15 <sup>o</sup>Brethren, I speak after the manner of men; Though *it be* but a man's covenant: yet *if it be* confirmed, no man disannulleth, or addeth thereto.

16 <sup>p</sup>Now to Abraham and his seed were the promises made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ.

17 <sup>q</sup>And this I say, *That* the covenant, that was confirmed before of God in Christ, the law, which was four hundred and thirty years after, cannot disannul, that it should make the promise of none effect.

18 <sup>r</sup>For if the inheritance *be* of the law, *it is* no more of promise: but God gave *it* to Abraham by promise.

19 <sup>s</sup>Wherefore then *serveth* the law? It was added because of transgressions, till the seed should come to whom the promise was made; *and it was* ordained by angels in the hand of a mediator.

20 Now a mediator is not *a mediator* of one; but God is one.

21 *Is* the law then against the promises of God? God forbid: for if there had been a law given which could have given life, verily righteousness should have been by the law.

22 <sup>t</sup>But the scripture hath concluded all under sin, that the promise by faith of Jesus Christ might be given to them that believe.

23 But before faith came, we were kept under the law, shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be revealed.

24 <sup>u</sup>Wherefore the law was our schoolmaster <sup>†</sup>*to bring us* unto Christ, that we might be justified by faith.

25 But after that faith is come, we are no longer under a schoolmaster.

26 <sup>x</sup>For ye are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus.
