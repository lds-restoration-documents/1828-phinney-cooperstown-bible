8 <sup>h</sup>But meat commendeth us not to God: for neither, if we eat, are we the better; neither, if we eat not, are we the worse.

9 <sup>i</sup>But take heed lest by any means this liberty of yours become a stumbling-block to them that are weak.

10 <sup>k</sup>For if any man see thee, which hast knowledge, sit at meat in the idol's temple, shall not the conscience of him which is weak be emboldened to eat those things which are offered to idols;

## CHAPTER IX.

1 Paul asserteth his apostolical character, 3 and right to a maintenance: 24 he striveth to obtain an incorruptible crown.

<sup>a</sup>AM I not an apostle? am I not free? have I not seen Jesus Christ our Lord? are not ye my work in the Lord?

2 <sup>b</sup>If I be not an apostle unto others, yet doubtless I am to you: for the seal of mine apostleship are ye in the Lord.

3 Mine answer to them that do examine me is this;

4 <sup>c</sup>Have we not power to eat and to drink?

5 <sup>d</sup>Have we not power to lead about a sister, a wife, as well as other apostles, and *as* the brethren of the Lord, and Cephas?

6 <sup>e</sup>Or I only and Barnabas, have not we power to forbear working?

7 <sup>f</sup>Who goeth a warfare at any time at his own charges? who planteth a vineyard, and eateth not of the fruit thereof? or who feedeth a flock, and eateth not of the milk of the flock?

8 Say I these things as a man? or saith not the law the same also?

9 <sup>g</sup>For it is written in the law of Moses, Thou shalt not muzzle the mouth of the ox that treadeth out the corn. Doth God take care for oxen?

10 <sup>h</sup>Or saith he *it* altogether for our sakes? For our sakes, no doubt, *this* is written: that he that ploweth should plow in hope; and that he that thresheth in hope should be partaker of his hope.

11 <sup>i</sup>If we have sown unto you spiritual things, *is it* a great thing if we shall reap your carnal things?

12 <sup>k</sup>If others be partakers of *this* power over you, *are* not we rather? Nevertheless we have not used this power; but suffer all things, lest we should hinder the gospel of Christ.

13 <sup>l</sup>Do ye not know, that they which minister about holy things live *of the things* of the temple? and they which wait at the altar are partakers with the altar?

14 <sup>m</sup>Even so hath the Lord ordained, that they which preach the gospel should live of the gospel.

15 <sup>n</sup>But I have used none of these things: neither have I written these things, that it should be so done unto me: for *it were* better for me to die than that any man should make my glorying void.

16 <sup>o</sup>For though I preach the gospel, I have nothing to glory of: for necessity is laid upon me; yea, wo is unto me if I preach not the gospel!

17 <sup>p</sup>For if I do this thing willingly, I have a reward: but if against my will, a dispensation *of the gospel* is committed unto me.

18 <sup>q</sup>What is my reward then? *Verily* that, when I preach the gospel, I may make the gospel of Christ without charge; that I abuse not my power in the gospel.

19 <sup>r</sup>For though I be free from all *men,* yet have I made myself servant unto all, that I might gain the more.

20 <sup>s</sup>And unto the Jews I became as a Jew, that I might gain the Jews; to them that are under the law, as under the law, that I might gain them that are under the law;

21 <sup>t</sup>To them that are without law, as without law, (being not without law to God, but under the law to Christ,) that I might gain them that are without law.

22 <sup>u</sup>To the weak became I as weak, that I might gain the weak: I am made all things to all *men,* that I might by all means save some.

23 And this I do for the gospel's sake, that I may be partaker thereof with *you.*

24 <sup>x</sup>Know ye not that they which run in a race run all, but one receiveth the prize? So run, that ye may obtain.

25 <sup>y</sup>And every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things. Now they *do it* to obtain a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible.

26 <sup>z</sup>I therefore so run, not as uncertainly; so fight I, not as one that beateth the air:

27 <sup>a</sup>But I keep under my body, and bring *it* into subjection; lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a cast-away.

## CHAPTER X.

1 The Jews had sacraments typical of ours....6 Christians must not lust after evil things: 7 they must avoid idolatry; 13 God will in affliction make a way for them to escape, &c.

<sup>a</sup>MOREOVER, brethren, I would not that ye should be ignorant, how that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea;

2 And were all baptized unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea;

3 <sup>b</sup>And did all eat the same spiritual meat;

4 <sup>c</sup>And did all drink the same spiritual drink; for they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was Christ.

5 <sup>d</sup>But with many of them God was not well pleased; for they were overthrown in the wilderness.

6 <sup>e</sup>Now these things were our examples, to the intent we should not lust after evil things, as they also lusted.

7 <sup>f</sup>Neither be ye idolaters, as *were* some of them; as it is written, The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play.

8 <sup>g</sup>Neither let us commit fornication, as some of them committed, and fell in one day, three and twenty thousand.

9 <sup>h</sup>Neither let us tempt Christ, as some of them also tempted, and were destroyed of serpents.

10 <sup>i</sup>Neither murmur ye, as some of them also murmured, and were destroyed of the destroyer.

11 <sup>k</sup>Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples: and they are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come.

12 <sup>l</sup>Wherefore let him that thinketh he standeth take heed lest he fall.
