The nations are exhorted to entertain the kingdom of Christ.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm for the sons of Korah.*

O CLAP your hands, all ye people; <sup>a</sup>shout unto God with the voice of triumph:

2 For the LORD most high *is* <sup>b</sup>terrible; *he is* <sup>c</sup>a great King over all the earth.

3 He shall <sup>d</sup>subdue the people under us, and the nations under our feet.

4 He shall choose <sup>e</sup>our inheritance for us, the <sup>f</sup>excellency of Jacob, whom he loved. Selah.

5 God is <sup>g</sup>gone up with a shout, the LORD with the sound of a trumpet.

## PSALM XLVIII.

The ornaments and privileges of the church.

*A Song and Psalm for the sons of Korah.*

GREAT *is* <sup>a</sup>the LORD, and greatly to be praised in the <sup>b</sup>city of our God, *in* the mountain of his holiness.

2 Beautiful for situation, the <sup>c</sup>joy of the whole earth, *is* mount Zion; *on* the sides of the north, the city of the great King.

3 God is <sup>d</sup>known in her <sup>e</sup>palaces for a refuge.

4 For, lo, the <sup>f</sup>kings were assembled, they passed by together.

5 They <sup>g</sup>saw *it, and* so they marvelled; they <sup>h</sup>were troubled, *and* hasted away.

6 <sup>i</sup>Fear took hold upon them there, *and* pain, as of a woman in travail.

7 Thou breakest the <sup>k</sup>ships of Tarshish with an east wind.

8 As we have <sup>l</sup>heard, <sup>m</sup>so have we seen in the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God: God will establish it for ever. Selah.

9 We have <sup>n</sup>thought of thy loving-kindness, O God, in the midst of thy temple.

10 According to thy <sup>o</sup>name, O God, so *is* thy praise unto the ends of the earth: thy right hand is <sup>p</sup>full of righteousness.

11 Let <sup>q</sup>mount Zion rejoice, let the daughters of Judah be glad, <sup>r</sup>because of thy judgments.

12 Walk <sup>s</sup>about Zion, and go round about her: tell the <sup>t</sup>towers thereof.

13 Mark ye well her bulwarks, <sup>u</sup>consider her palaces; that ye may tell *it* to the generation following.

14 For this God *is* our God for ever and ever; he will be our guide *even* unto death.

## PSALM XLIX.

Worldly prosperity is not to be admired.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm for the sons of Korah.*

HEAR this, all *ye* people; <sup>a</sup>give ear, all *ye* inhabitants of the world:

2 Both low and high, rich and poor together.

3 My mouth shall speak of wisdom; and the meditation of my heart *shall be* of <sup>†</sup>understanding.

4 I will <sup>b</sup>incline mine ear to a parable; I will open my <sup>c</sup>dark saying upon the harp.

5 Wherefore should <sup>d</sup>I fear in the <sup>e</sup>days of evil, *when* the iniquity of my heels shall compass me about?

6 They that <sup>f</sup>trust in their wealth, and <sup>g</sup>boast themselves in the multitude of their riches;

7 None *of them* can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to God <sup>h</sup>a ransom for him;

8 (For the redemption of their soul *is* precious, and it ceaseth for ever;)

9 That he should still <sup>i</sup>live for ever, *and* not see corruption.

10 For he seeth *that* <sup>k</sup>wise men die, likewise the fool and the <sup>l</sup>brutish person perish, and leave their wealth <sup>m</sup>to others.

11 Their inward thought *is, that* their houses *shall continue* for ever, *and* their dwelling-places to all generations: they call *their* lands <sup>n</sup>after their own names.

12 Nevertheless, man *being* <sup>o</sup>in honour abideth not: he is like the beasts *that* <sup>p</sup>perish.

13 This their way *is* their <sup>||</sup>folly: yet their posterity <sup>q</sup>approve their sayings. Selah.

14 <sup>r</sup>Like sheep they are laid in the grave; death shall feed on them; and the upright shall have <sup>s</sup>dominion over them in the morning; and their beauty shall consume in the grave from their dwelling.

15 But God will redeem my soul from the <sup>t</sup>power of the grave: for he shall <sup>u</sup>receive me. Selah.

16 Be not thou afraid when one is made rich, when the glory of his house is increased:

17 For, when he dieth, he shall <sup>x</sup>carry nothing away; his glory shall not descend after him;

18 Though, while he lived, <sup>y</sup>he blessed his soul: (and *men* will praise thee when thou doest well to thyself:)

19 He shall go to the generation of his fathers; they shall <sup>z</sup>never see light.

20 Man *that is* in honour, and understandeth not, is like the beasts *that* perish.

## PSALM L.

The majesty of God in the church.

*A Psalm of* <sup>a</sup>*Asaph.*

THE <sup>b</sup>mighty God, *even* the LORD, hath spoken, and called the earth from the rising of the sun unto the going down thereof.

2 <sup>c</sup>Out of Zion, the <sup>d</sup>perfection of beauty, God hath shined.

3 Our God <sup>e</sup>shall come, and shall not <sup>f</sup>keep silence: a <sup>g</sup>fire shall devour before him, and it shall be very <sup>h</sup>tempestuous round about him.

4 He shall call to the heavens from above, and to the earth, (that he may <sup>i</sup>judge his people.)

5 <sup>k</sup>Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made <sup>l</sup>a covenant with me by sacrifice.

6 And the heavens shall declare his righteousness: for God *is* <sup>m</sup>judge himself. Selah.

7 Hear, O my people, and I will speak; O Israel, and I will testify against thee: I *am* God, *even* thy God.

8 I will not reprove thee for thy <sup>n</sup>sacrifices, or thy burnt-offerings, *to have been* continually before me.

9 I will take no bullock out of thy house, *nor* he-goats out of thy folds:

10 For every beast of the forest *is* <sup>o</sup>mine, *and* the cattle upon a thousand hills.

11 I know all the fowls of the mountains; and the wild beasts of the field *are* mine.
