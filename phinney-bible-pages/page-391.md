14 They shall still <sup>r</sup>bring forth fruit in old age; they shall be fat and flourishing;

15 To shew that the LORD *is* upright: *he is* my rock, and *there is* <sup>s</sup>no unrighteousness in him.

## PSALM XCIII.

The majesty, power, and holiness of Christ's kingdom.

THE LORD <sup>a</sup>reigneth; he is clothed with majesty; the LORD is clothed with strength, *wherewith* he hath <sup>b</sup>girded himself: the world also is established, that it cannot be moved.

2 Thy throne *is* established <sup>c</sup>of old: thou *art* from everlasting.

3 The <sup>d</sup>floods have lifted up, O LORD, the floods have lifted up their voice; the floods lift up their waves.

4 The LORD <sup>e</sup>on high *is* mightier than the noise of many waters, *yea, than* the <sup>f</sup>mighty waves of the sea.

5 Thy testimonies are very sure: <sup>g</sup>holiness becometh thy house, O LORD, for ever.

## PSALM XCIV.

The Prophet sheweth the blessedness of affliction.

O LORD God, to whom <sup>a</sup>vengeance belongeth; O God, to whom vengeance belongeth, shew thyself.

2 Lift up thyself, thou <sup>b</sup>judge of the earth: render a reward to the proud.

3 LORD, how long shall the <sup>c</sup>wicked, how long shall the wicked triumph?

4 *How long* shall they utter *and* speak <sup>d</sup>hard things? *and* all the workers of iniquity boast themselves?

5 They <sup>e</sup>break in pieces thy people, O LORD, and afflict thy heritage:

6 They <sup>f</sup>slay the widow and the stranger, and murder the fatherless.

7 Yet <sup>g</sup>they say, The LORD shall <sup>h</sup>not see, neither shall the God of Jacob regard *it.*

8 Understand, ye <sup>i</sup>brutish among the people; and, *ye* fools, when will ye be wise?

9 He that planted <sup>k</sup>the ear, shall he not hear? he that formed the eye, shall he not see?

10 He that chastiseth the heathen, shall not he correct? he that teacheth <sup>l</sup>man knowledge, *shall not he know?*

11 The LORD knoweth <sup>m</sup>the thoughts of man, that they *are* vanity.

12 Blessed *is* the man whom thou <sup>n</sup>chastenest, O LORD, and <sup>o</sup>teachest him out of thy law;

13 That thou mayest give him rest from the <sup>p</sup>days of adversity, until the pit be digged for the wicked.

14 For the LORD will <sup>q</sup>not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance:

15 But <sup>r</sup>judgment shall return unto righteousness; and all the upright in heart shall follow it.

16 <sup>s</sup>Who will rise up for me against the evil-doers? *or* who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity?

17 <sup>t</sup>Unless the LORD *had been* my help, my soul had almost dwelt in silence.

18 When I said, <sup>u</sup>My foot slippeth; thy mercy, O LORD, held me up.

19 In the <sup>x</sup>multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul.

20 Shall the <sup>y</sup>throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frameth mischief <sup>z</sup>by a law?

21 They <sup>a</sup>gather themselves together against the soul of the righteous, and condemn the innocent blood.

22 But the LORD is <sup>b</sup>my defence; and my God *is* the rock of my refuge.

23 And he shall bring upon them <sup>c</sup>their own iniquity, and shall cut them off in their own wickedness; *yea,* the LORD our God shall cut them off.

## PSALM XCV.

An exhortation to praise God for his goodness.

O COME, <sup>a</sup>let us sing unto the LORD; let us make a joyful noise to the <sup>b</sup>rock of our salvation.

2 Let us <sup>c</sup>come before his presence with thanksgiving, and make a joyful noise unto him with psalms.

3 For the LORD *is* a <sup>d</sup>great God, and a great King <sup>e</sup>above all gods.

4 In his hand *are* the deep places of the earth; the <sup>f</sup>strength of the hills *is* his also.

5 The <sup>g</sup>sea *is* his, and he made it; and his hands formed the dry *land.*

6 O come, let us worship and bow down: let us kneel before the LORD <sup>h</sup>our Maker.

7 For he *is* our God; and we *are* the people of his pasture, and the <sup>i</sup>sheep of his hand. <sup>k</sup>To-day, if ye will hear his voice,

8 <sup>l</sup>Harden not your heart, as in the <sup>m</sup>provocation, *and as in* the day of <sup>n</sup>temptation in the wilderness:

9 When your fathers <sup>o</sup>tempted me, proved me, and saw <sup>p</sup>my work.

10 Forty years long was I grieved with *this* generation, and said, It *is* a people that do <sup>q</sup>err in their heart, and they have not known my ways:

11 Unto whom I sware in my wrath, that they should <sup>r</sup>not enter into my rest.

## PSALM XCVI.

An exhortation to praise God for his general judgment.

O SING unto the LORD <sup>a</sup>a new song; sing unto the LORD all the earth.

2 Sing unto the LORD, bless <sup>b</sup>his name: shew forth <sup>c</sup>his salvation from day to day.

3 Declare his glory <sup>d</sup>among the heathen, his wonders among all people.

4 For the LORD *is* great, <sup>e</sup>and greatly to be praised: he *is* to be feared <sup>f</sup>above all gods.

5 For all the gods of the nations *are* <sup>†</sup>idols: but the LORD made the heavens.

6 <sup>g</sup>Honour and majesty *are* before him; strength and beauty *are* in his sanctuary.

7 <sup>h</sup>Give unto the LORD, O ye kindreds of the people, give unto the LORD glory and strength;

8 Give unto the LORD the glory *due unto* his name: bring <sup>i</sup>an offering, and come into his courts.

9 O worship the LORD in the <sup>k</sup>beauty of holiness: fear before him all the earth.

10 Say <sup>l</sup>among the heathen, *that* the LORD <sup>m</sup>reigneth: the world also shall be established that it shall not be moved: he shall judge the people <sup>n</sup>righteously.

11 Let the <sup>o</sup>heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof.

12 Let the field be joyful, and all that *is* therein: then shall all the trees of the wood rejoice

13 Before the LORD: for he <sup>p</sup>cometh, for he cometh to judge the earth: he shall judge the world with righteousness, and the people with his truth.

## PSALM XCVII.

The church rejoiceth at God's judgments upon idolaters.

THE LORD <sup>a</sup>reigneth, let the earth rejoice; let the multitude of <sup>b</sup>isles be glad *thereof.*

2 <sup>c</sup>Clouds and darkness *are* round about him: righteousness and judgment *are* the habitation of his throne.
