# ¶ The FIRST Book of the CHRONICLES.

## CHAPTER I.

1 Adam's line to Noah....5 The sons of Japheth.

ADAM, <sup>a</sup>Sheth, <sup>b</sup>Enosh,

2 <sup>c</sup>Kenan, Mahalaleel, Jered,

3 <sup>d</sup>Henoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

4 <sup>e</sup>Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth,

5 ¶ The <sup>f</sup>sons of Japheth; Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

6 And the sons of Gomer; Ashchenaz, and Riphath, and <sup>g</sup>Togarmah.

7 And the sons of Javan; <sup>h</sup>Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

8 ¶ The sons of Ham; <sup>i</sup>Cush, and Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

9 And the sons of Cush; Seba, and Havilah, and Sabta, and Raamah, and Sabtecha. And the sons of Raamah; Sheba, and Dedan.

10 And Cush begat <sup>k</sup>Nimrod; he began to be <sup>l</sup>mighty upon the earth.

11 And Mizraim begat <sup>m</sup>Ludim, and Anamim, and Lehabim, and Naphtuhim,

12 And Pathrusim, and Caslushim, (of whom came the <sup>n</sup>Philistines,) and <sup>o</sup>Caphthorim

13 And Canaan begat <sup>p</sup>Zidon his first-born, and Heth,

14 The <sup>q</sup>Jebusite also, and the Amorite, and the <sup>r</sup>Girgashite,

15 And the <sup>s</sup>Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

16 And the <sup>t</sup>Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite.

17 ¶ The sons of Shem; <sup>u</sup>Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram, and Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Meshech.

18 And Arphaxad begat Shelah, and Shelah begat <sup>x</sup>Eber.

19 And unto Eber were born two sons: the name of the one *was* <sup>y</sup>Peleg, (because in his days the earth was divided,) and his brother's name *was* Joktan.

20 And <sup>z</sup>Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

21 Hadoram also, and Uzal, and Diklah,

22 And Ebal, and Abimael, and Sheba,

23 And <sup>a</sup>Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab. All these *were* the sons of Joktan.

24 ¶ <sup>b</sup>Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah,

25 <sup>c</sup>Eber, Peleg, Reu,

26 Serug, <sup>d</sup>Nahor, Terah,

27 Abram, the same <sup>e</sup>*is* Abraham.

28 The sons of Abraham; <sup>f</sup>Isaac, and <sup>g</sup>Ishmael.

29 ¶ These *are* their generations: The <sup>h</sup>first-born of Ishmael, Nebaioth; then Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam,

30 Mishma, and Dumah, Massa, <sup>i</sup>Hadad, and Tema,

31 Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These are the sons of Ishmael.

32 ¶ Now the sons of Keturah, Abraham's <sup>k</sup>concubine: <sup>l</sup>she bare Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah. And the sons of Jokshan; Sheba, and Dedan.

33 And the sons of Midian: <sup>m</sup>Ephah, and Epher, and Henoch, and Abida, and Eldaah. All these *are* the sons of Keturah.

34 ¶ And Abraham begat <sup>n</sup>Isaac. The sons of Isaac; Esau, and Israel

35 The sons of <sup>o</sup>Esau; Eliphaz, Reuel, and Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah.

36 The sons of <sup>p</sup>Eliphaz; Teman, and Omar, Zephi, and Gatam, Kenaz, and Timna, and Amalek.

37 The sons of Reuel; Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and <sup>q</sup>Mizzah.

38 And the sons of <sup>r</sup>Seir; Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and <sup>s</sup>Anah, and Dishon, and Ezar, and Dishan.

39 And the sons of Lotan; Hori, and Homam: and Timna *was* Lotan's sister.

40 The sons of Shobal; Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. And the sons of Zibeon; Aiah, and Anah.

41 The sons of Anah; <sup>t</sup>Dishon. And the sons of Dishon; Amram, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran.

42 The sons of Ezer; Bilhan, and Zavan, *and* Jakan. The sons of Dishan; <sup>u</sup>Uz, and Aran.

43 ¶ Now these *are* the kings that reigned in the land of Edom, before *any* <sup>x</sup>king reigned over the children of Israel; <sup>y</sup>Bela the son of Beor: and the name of his city *was* Dinhabah.

44 And when Bela was dead, Jobab the son of Zerah of <sup>z</sup>Bozrah reigned in his stead.

45 And when Jobab was dead, Husham of the land of the <sup>a</sup>Temanites reigned in his stead.

46 And when Husham was dead, <sup>b</sup>Hadad the son of Bedad, (which smote Midian in the <sup>c</sup>field of Moab,) reigned in his stead: and the name of his city *was* Avith.

47 And when Hadad was dead, Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his stead.

48 And when <sup>d</sup>Samlah was dead, Shaul of Rehoboth by the <sup>e</sup>river reigned in his stead.

49 And when Shaul was dead, Baal-hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his stead.

50 And when Baal-hanan was dead, Hadad reigned in his stead: and the name of his city *was* Pai; and his wife's name *was* Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezahab.

51 Hadad died also. And the <sup>||</sup>dukes of Edom *were;* duke Timnah, duke <sup>||</sup>Aliah, duke Jetheth,

52 Duke Aholibamah, duke Elah, duke Pinon,

53 Duke Kenaz, duke Teman, duke Mibzar,

54 Duke Magdiel, duke Iram. These *are* the dukes of Edom.

## CHAPTER II.

1 The sons of Israel....2 The posterity of Judah.

THESE *are* the sons of <sup>a</sup>Israel; <sup>b</sup>Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun,

2 Dan, Joseph, and Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

3 ¶ The sons of <sup>c</sup>Judah; Er, and Onan, and Shelah; *which* three were born unto him of the daughter of Shua the Canaanitess. And Er, the first-born of Judah, was evil in the sight of the LORD; and he slew him.

4 And <sup>d</sup>Tamar his daughter-in-law bare him Pharez and Zerah. All the sons of Judah *were* five.

5 The sons of <sup>e</sup>Pharez; Hezron, and Hamul.

6 And the sons of Zerah; <sup>||</sup>Zimri, and <sup>f</sup>Ethan, and Heman, and Calcol, and Dara: five of them in all.

7 And the sons of Carmi; Achar the troubler of Israel, who transgressed in the thing <sup>g</sup>accursed.

8 And the sons of Ethan; Azariah.

9 The sons also of <sup>h</sup>Hezron, that were born unto him; Jerahmeel, and Ram, and Chelubai.
