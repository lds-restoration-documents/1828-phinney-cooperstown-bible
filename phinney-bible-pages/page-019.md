9 There went <sup>g</sup>in two and two unto Noah into the ark, the male and the female, as God had commanded Noah.

10 And <sup>h</sup>it came to pass, after seven days, that the waters of the flood were upon the earth.

11 ¶ In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month, the same day were all the fountains of the <sup>i</sup>great deep broken up, and the <sup>k</sup>windows of heaven were opened.

12 And the rain was upon the earth forty days and forty nights.

13 In the self-same day entered Noah, and Shem, and Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife, and the three wives of his sons with them, into the ark;

14 They, and every beast after his kind, and all the cattle after their kind, and every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth after his kind, and every fowl after his kind, every bird of every sort.

15 And they went in unto Noah into the ark <sup>l</sup>two and two of all flesh, wherein *is* the breath of life.

16 And they that went in, went in male and female of all flesh, as God had commanded him: and the <sup>m</sup>LORD shut him in.

17 And the flood was forty days upon the earth: and the waters increased, and bare up the ark, and it was lift up above the earth.

18 And the waters <sup>n</sup>prevailed, and were increased greatly upon the earth; and the ark <sup>o</sup>went upon the face of the waters.

19 And the waters prevailed exceedingly upon the earth; and all the <sup>p</sup>high hills that *were* under the whole heaven, were covered.

20 Fifteen cubits upward did the waters prevail; and the <sup>q</sup>mountains were covered.

21 And all flesh died that moved upon the earth, both of fowl, and of cattle, and of beast, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth, and <sup>r</sup>every man:

22 All in whose nostrils *was* the breath of life, of all that *was* in the dry *land*, died.

23 And every living substance was destroyed which was upon the face of the ground, both man, and cattle, and the creeping things, and the fowl of the heaven; and they were destroyed from the earth: and <sup>s</sup>Noah only remained *alive*, and they that *were* with him in the ark.

24 And the waters prevailed upon the earth a <sup>t</sup>hundred and fifty days.

## CHAPTER VIII.

1 The waters assuage....4 The ark resteth on Ararat....18 Noah goeth forth of the ark....20 He buildeth an altar, and offereth sacrifice.

AND God <sup>a</sup>remembered Noah, and every living thing, and <sup>b</sup>all the cattle that *was* with him in the ark: and God made a wind to pass over the earth, and the waters assuaged:

2 The fountains also of the deep, and the windows of heaven were stopped, and the rain from heaven was <sup>c</sup>restrained;

3 And the waters returned from off the earth <sup>†</sup>continually: and <sup>d</sup>after the end of the hundred and fifty days the waters were abated.

4 ¶ And the ark rested in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat.

5 And the waters <sup>†</sup>decreased continually, until the tenth month: in the tenth *month*, on the first *day* of the month, were the tops of the mountains seen.

6 ¶ And it came to pass, at the end of forty days, that Noah <sup>e</sup>opened the window of the ark which he had made:

7 And he sent forth a <sup>f</sup>raven, which went forth <sup>†</sup>to and fro, until the waters were dried up from off the earth.

8 Also he sent forth <sup>g</sup>a dove from him, to see if the waters were abated from off the face of the ground:

9 But the dove <sup>h</sup>found no rest for the sole of her foot, and she returned unto him into the ark: for the waters *were* on the face of the whole earth. Then he put forth his hand, and took her, and pulled her in unto him into the ark.

10 And he <sup>i</sup>stayed yet other seven days, and again he sent forth the dove out of the ark:

11 And the dove came in to him in the evening, and, lo, in her mouth *was* an <sup>k</sup>olive-leaf pluckt off. So Noah knew that the waters were abated from off the earth.

12 And he stayed <sup>l</sup>yet other seven days, and sent forth the dove, which returned not again unto him any more.

13 ¶ And it came to pass, in the six hundredth and first year, in the first *month*, the first *day* of the month, the waters were dried up from off the earth: and Noah removed the covering of the ark, and looked, and, behold, the face of the ground was dry.

14 And in the second month, on the <sup>m</sup>seven and twentieth day of the month, was the earth dried.

15 ¶ And God spake unto Noah, saying,

16 <sup>n</sup>Go forth of the ark, thou and thy wife, and thy sons, and thy sons' wives with thee.

17 Bring forth with thee every living thing that *is* with thee, of all flesh, *both* of fowl, and of cattle, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth; that they may breed abundantly in the earth, and be fruitful, and multiply upon the earth.

18 And Noah <sup>o</sup>went forth, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons' wives with him:

19 Every beast, every creeping thing, and every fowl, *and* whatsoever creepeth upon the earth, after their <sup>†</sup>kinds, went forth out of the ark.

20 ¶ And Noah builded <sup>p</sup>an altar unto the LORD, and took of every clean beast, and of every clean fowl, and offered burnt-offerings on the altar.

21 And the LORD smelled <sup>†</sup>a sweet savour: and the LORD said in his heart, I will not again curse the ground any more for man's sake; for the imagination of man's heart *is* evil from his youth: neither will I again smite any more every thing living, as I have <sup>q</sup>done.

22 <sup>†</sup>While the earth remaineth, seed-time and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night, shall not cease.

## CHAPTER IX.

1 God blesseth Noah....4 Blood and murder are forbidden. 8 God's covenant, 13 signified by the rainbow....18 Noah replenisheth the world....21 Is drunken, and mocked of his son....25 Curseth Canaan, 29 and dieth.

AND God <sup>a</sup>blessed Noah and his sons, and said unto them, Be <sup>b</sup>fruitful and multiply, and replenish the earth.

2 And <sup>c</sup>the fear of you, and the dread of you, shall be upon every beast of the earth, and upon every fowl of the air, and upon all that moveth *upon* the earth, and upon all the fishes of the sea; into your hand are they delivered.

3 Every moving thing that <sup>d</sup>liveth shall <sup>e</sup>be meat for you; even <sup>f</sup>as the green herb have I given you all things.
