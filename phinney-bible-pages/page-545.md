## CHAPTER V.

1 Belshazzar's impious feast....5 A hand-writing on the wall.

BELSHAZZAR the king made <sup>a</sup>a great feast to a thousand of his lords, and drank wine before the thousand.

2 Belshazzar, while he <sup>b</sup>tasted the wine, commanded to bring the golden and silver vessels which his father Nebuchadnezzar had <sup>c</sup>taken out of the temple which *was* in Jerusalem; that the king and his princes, his wives and his concubines, might <sup>d</sup>drink therein.

3 Then <sup>e</sup>they brought the golden vessels that were taken out of the temple of the house of God which *was* at Jerusalem; and <sup>f</sup>the king and his princes, his wives and his concubines, drank in them.

4 They drank wine and <sup>g</sup>praised the gods of gold, and of silver, of brass, of iron, of wood, and of stone.

5 In the <sup>h</sup>same hour came forth <sup>i</sup>fingers of a man's hand, and wrote over against the candlestick upon the <sup>k</sup>plaster of the wall of the king's palace; and the king saw the part of the hand that wrote.

6 Then the king's <sup>l</sup>countenance was changed, and his thoughts troubled him, so that the joints of his loins were loosed, and his <sup>m</sup>knees smote one against another.

7 The king cried aloud <sup>n</sup>to bring in the astrologers, the Chaldeans, and the soothsayers. *And* the king spake, and said to the wise *men* of Babylon, Whosoever shall read this writing, and shew me the interpretation thereof, shall be clothed with scarlet, and *have* a chain of gold about his neck, and shall be the <sup>o</sup>third ruler in the kingdom.

8 Then came in all the king's wise *men:* but they could not <sup>p</sup>read the writing, nor make known to the king the interpretation thereof.

9 Then was king Belshazzar <sup>q</sup>greatly troubled, and his countenance was changed in him, and his <sup>r</sup>lords were astonished.

10 *Now* the queen, by reason of the words of the king and his lords, came into the banquet-house; *and* the queen spake and said, O king, live for ever: <sup>s</sup>let not thy thoughts trouble thee, nor let thy countenance be changed:

11 There is <sup>t</sup>a man in thy kingdom, in whom *is* the spirit of the holy gods; and, in the days of thy father, <sup>u</sup>light, and understanding, and wisdom, <sup>x</sup>like the wisdom of the gods, was found in him; whom the king Nebuchadnezzar thy father, the king, *I say,* thy father, made <sup>y</sup>master of the magicians, astrologers, Chaldeans, *and* soothsayers;

12 Forasmuch as <sup>z</sup>an excellent spirit, and knowledge, and understanding, interpreting of dreams, and shewing of hard sentences, and <sup>a</sup>dissolving of doubts, were found in the same Daniel, whom the king named Belteshazzar: now let Daniel be called, and he will shew the interpretation.

13 Then was Daniel brought in before the king. *And* the king spake and said unto Daniel, <sup>b</sup>*Art* thou that Daniel, which *art* of the children of the captivity of Judah, whom the king my father brought out of Jewry?

14 I have even <sup>c</sup>heard of thee, that the <sup>d</sup>spirit of the gods *is* in thee, and *that* light, and understanding, and excellent wisdom, is found in thee.

15 And now the wise *men,* the astrologers, have been brought in before me, <sup>e</sup>that they should read this writing, and make known unto me the interpretation thereof: but they could not <sup>f</sup>shew the interpretation of the thing:

16 And I have heard of thee that thou canst make interpretations and dissolve doubts: now, if thou canst read the writing, and make known to me the interpretation thereof, thou shalt be <sup>g</sup>clothed with scarlet, and *have* a chain of gold about thy neck, and shalt be the third ruler in the kingdom.

17 Then Daniel answered and said before the king, Let thy <sup>h</sup>gifts be to thyself, and give thy rewards to another; <sup>i</sup>yet I will read the writing unto the king, and make known to him the interpretation.

18 <sup>k</sup>O thou king, the most high God <sup>l</sup>gave Nebuchadnezzar thy father a kingdom, and majesty, and glory, and honour:

19 And, for the majesty that he gave him, all people, nations, and languages, <sup>m</sup>trembled and feared before him: whom he would he slew, and whom he would he <sup>n</sup>kept alive, and whom he would he set up, and whom he would he put down.

20 But when his <sup>o</sup>heart was lifted up, and his mind hardened in pride, he was <sup>p</sup>deposed from his kingly throne, and they took his glory from him:

21 And he was <sup>q</sup>driven from the sons of men; and his heart was made like the beasts, and his dwelling *was* with the wild asses: they fed him with grass like oxen, and his body was wet with the dew of heaven; till he knew that the most high God ruled in the kingdom of men, and *that* he appointeth over it whomsoever he will.

22 And thou his son, O Belshazzar, hast <sup>r</sup>not humbled thy heart, though thou <sup>s</sup>knewest all this;

23 But hast <sup>t</sup>lifted up thyself against the LORD of heaven; and they have <sup>u</sup>brought the vessels of his house before thee, and thou and thy lords, thy wives and thy concubines, have drunk wine in them; and thou hast praised the gods of silver and gold, of brass, iron, wood, and stone, which <sup>x</sup>see not, nor hear, nor know: and the God in whose hand thy breath *is,* and whose *are* <sup>y</sup>all thy ways, hast thou <sup>z</sup>not glorified.

24 <sup>a</sup>Then was the part of the hand sent from him; and this writing was written.
