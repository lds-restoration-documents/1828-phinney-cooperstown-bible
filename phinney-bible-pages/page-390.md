The Prophet exhorteth to praise God for his great works.

*A Psalm or Song for the* <sup>a</sup>*sabbath-day.*

IT is <sup>b</sup>a good *thing* to give thanks unto the LORD, and to sing praises unto thy name, O Most High:

2 To shew forth thy loving-kindness in the <sup>c</sup>morning, and thy faithfulness <sup>d</sup>every night,

3 Upon an instrument of ten strings, and upon the psaltery; upon the harp with a solemn sound.

5 O LORD, how great are thy works! *and* thy thoughts are very <sup>f</sup>deep.

10 But my horn shalt thou exalt like *the* <sup>l</sup>horn of an unicorn: I shall be anointed with <sup>m</sup>fresh oil.

11 <sup>n</sup>Mine eye also shall see *my desire* on mine enemies; *and* mine ears shall hear *my desire* of the wicked that rise up against me.

12 The righteous shall flourish like the <sup>o</sup>palm-tree; he shall grow like a cedar in Lebanon.
