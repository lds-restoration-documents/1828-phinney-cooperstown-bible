21 And he said unto him, See, I have <sup>k</sup>accepted thee concerning this thing also, that I will not overthrow this city, for the which thou hast spoken.

22 Haste thee, escape thither; for <sup>l</sup>I cannot do any thing till thou be come thither: therefore the name of the city was called Zoar.

23 The sun was <sup>m</sup>risen upon the earth when Lot entered into Zoar.

24 Then the LORD rained upon <sup>n</sup>Sodom, and upon Gomorrah, brimstone and fire from the LORD out of heaven;

25 And he overthrew those cities, and all the plain, and all the inhabitants of the cities, and <sup>o</sup>that which grew upon the ground.

26 But his wife <sup>p</sup>looked back from behind him, and she became <sup>q</sup>a pillar of salt.

27 ¶ And Abraham gat up early in the morning to the place where <sup>r</sup>he stood before the LORD:

28 And he looked toward Sodom and Gomorrah, and toward all the land of the plain, and beheld, and, lo, the <sup>s</sup>smoke of the country went up as the smoke of a furnace.

29 And it came to pass, when God destroyed the cities of the plain, that God <sup>t</sup>remembered Abraham, and sent Lot out of the midst of the overthrow, when he overthrew the cities in the which Lot dwelt.

30 ¶ And Lot went up out of Zoar, and dwelt <sup>u</sup>in the mountain, and his two daughters with him; for he feared to dwell in Zoar: and he dwelt in a cave, he and his two daughters.

31 And the first-born said unto the younger, Our father *is* old, and *there is* <sup>x</sup>not a man in the earth <sup>y</sup>to come in unto us after the manner of all the earth:

32 <sup>z</sup>Come, let us make our father drink wine, and we will lie with him, that we may preserve seed of our father.

33 And they made their father <sup>a</sup>drink wine that night: and the first-born went in, and lay with her father; and he perceived not when she lay down, nor when she arose.

34 And it came to pass on the morrow, that the first-born said unto the younger, Behold, I lay yesternight with my father: let us make him drink wine this night also; and go thou in, *and* lie with him, that we may preserve seed of our father.

35 And they made their father drink wine that night also: and the younger arose, and lay with him; and he perceived not when she lay down, nor when she arose.

36 Thus were both the daughters of Lot with child <sup>b</sup>by their father.

37 And the first-born bare a son, and called his name <sup>||</sup>Moab: the same *is* the father of the <sup>c</sup>Moabites unto this day.

38 And the younger, she also bare a son, and called his name <sup>||</sup>Ben-ammi: the same *is* the father of the children of Ammon unto this day.

## CHAPTER XX.

1 Abraham sojourneth at Gerar....2 He denieth his wife, and loseth her.

AND Abraham journeyed <sup>a</sup>from thence towards the south country, and dwelled between <sup>b</sup>Kadesh and Shur, and sojourned in Gerar.

2 And Abraham said of Sarah his wife, <sup>c</sup>She *is* my sister: and Abimelech king of Gerar sent and took Sarah.

3 But <sup>d</sup>God came to Abimelech in a dream by night, and said to him, Behold, thou *art but* <sup>e</sup>a dead man, for the woman which thou hast taken; for she *is* a man's wife.

4 But Abimelech had <sup>f</sup>not come near her: and he said, LORD, wilt thou slay also <sup>g</sup>a righteous nation?

5 Said he not unto me, She *is* my sister? and she, even she herself, said, He *is* my brother: <sup>h</sup>In the integrity of my heart, and innocency of my hands, have I done this.

6 And God said unto him in a dream, <sup>i</sup>Yea, I know that thou didst this in the integrity of thy heart; for I also <sup>k</sup>withheld thee from sinning <sup>l</sup>against me: therefore <sup>†</sup>suffered I thee not to touch her.

7 Now, therefore, restore the man *his* wife; for he *is* <sup>m</sup>a prophet, and he <sup>n</sup>shall pray for thee, and thou shalt live: and if thou <sup>o</sup>restore *her* not, know thou that thou shalt surely die, <sup>p</sup>thou, and all that *are* thine.

8 Therefore Abimelech <sup>q</sup>rose early in the morning, and called all his servants, and told all these things in their ears: and the men were sore afraid.

9 Then Abimelech called Abraham, and said unto him, What hast thou done unto us? and what have I offended thee, that thou hast <sup>r</sup>brought on me and on my kingdom a <sup>s</sup>great sin? thou hast done deeds unto me that ought not to be done.

10 And Abimelech said unto Abraham, What sawest thou, that thou hast done this thing?

11 And Abraham said, Because I thought, Surely the <sup>t</sup>fear of God *is* not in this place; and they will slay me for my wife's sake.

12 And yet indeed *she is* <sup>u</sup>my sister; she *is* the daughter of my father, but not the daughter of my mother; and she became my wife.

13 And it came to pass, when God caused me to <sup>x</sup>wander from my father's house, that I said unto her, This *is* thy kindness which thou shalt shew unto me; at every place whither we shall come, say of me, He *is* my brother.

14 And Abimelech took sheep, and oxen, and men-servants, and women-servants, and gave *them* unto Abraham, and restored him Sarah his wife.

15 And Abimelech said, Behold, my land *is* before thee: dwell where it pleaseth thee.

16 And unto Sarah he said, Behold, I have given <sup>y</sup>thy brother a thousand *pieces* of silver; behold, he *is* to thee a covering of the eyes unto all that *are* with thee, and with all *other*: <sup>z</sup>thus she was reproved.

17 So Abraham prayed unto God: and God <sup>a</sup>healed Abimelech, and his wife, and his maid-servants; and they bare *children*.

18 For the LORD had <sup>b</sup>fast closed up all the wombs of the house of Abimelech, because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.
