## CHAPTER XVII.

1 God reneweth the covenant....10 Circumcision is instituted. 16 Isaac is promised....23 Abraham and Ishmael are circumcised.

AND when Abram was ninety years old and nine, the LORD <sup>a</sup>appeared to Abram, and said unto him, I *am* the <sup>b</sup>Almighty God: <sup>c</sup>walk before me, and be thou perfect.

2 And I will make my covenant <sup>d</sup>between me and thee; and will multiply thee exceedingly.

3 And Abram <sup>e</sup>fell on his face: and God talked with him, saying,

4 As for me, behold, my covenant *is* with thee, and thou shalt be <sup>f</sup>a father of many nations.

5 Neither shall thy name any more be called Abram; but thy name shall be called Abraham: for a <sup>g</sup>father of many nations have I made thee.

6 And I will make thee exceeding fruitful, and I will make nations of thee, and <sup>h</sup>kings shall come out of thee.

7 And I will establish my covenant between me and thee, and <sup>i</sup>thy seed after thee, in their generations, for an <sup>k</sup>everlasting covenant, to be <sup>l</sup>a God unto thee, and to thy seed after thee.

8 And I will give unto thee, and to thy seed after thee, the land wherein thou art a stranger, all the land of Canaan, for <sup>m</sup>an everlasting possession; and I will be their God.

9 ¶ And God said unto Abraham, Thou shalt keep my covenant therefore, thou, and thy seed after thee, in their generations.

10 This *is* <sup>n</sup>my covenant, which ye shall keep, between me and you, and thy seed after thee; Every <sup>o</sup>man-child among you shall be <sup>p</sup>circumcised.

11 And ye shall circumcise the flesh of your foreskin; and it shall be a token of the covenant betwixt me and you.

12 And he that is <sup>†</sup>eight days old shall be circumcised among you, every man-child in your generations; he that is born in the house, or bought with money of any stranger, which *is* not of thy seed.

13 He that is born in thy house, and he that is bought with thy money, must needs be circumcised: and my covenant shall be in <sup>q</sup>your flesh for an everlasting covenant.

14 And the uncircumcised man-child, whose flesh of his foreskin is not circumcised, that soul shall be <sup>r</sup>cut off from his people; he hath <sup>s</sup>broken my covenant.

15 ¶ And God said unto Abraham, As for Sarai thy wife, thou shalt not call her name Sarai, but Sarah *shall* her name *be*.

16 And I will <sup>t</sup>bless her, and give thee a son also of her; yea, I will bless her, and she shall be *a mother* of <sup>u</sup>nations; kings of people shall be of her.

17 Then Abraham fell upon his face, and <sup>x</sup>laughed, and said in his heart, Shall *a child* be born unto him that is a hundred years old? and shall Sarah, that is ninety years old, bear?

18 And Abraham said unto God, Oh, that Ishmael might live <sup>y</sup>before thee!

19 And God said, <sup>z</sup>Sarah thy wife shall bear thee a son indeed; and thou shalt call his name <sup>a</sup>Isaac; and I will establish my covenant with him for an everlasting covenant, *and* with his seed after him.

20 And as for Ishmael, I have heard thee: Behold, I have blessed him, and will make him fruitful, and will multiply him exceedingly: <sup>b</sup>twelve princes shall he beget; and I will make him a great nation.

21 But <sup>c</sup>my covenant will I establish with Isaac, whom Sarah shall bear unto thee at this set time in the next year.

22 And he left off talking with him, and God <sup>d</sup>went up from Abraham.

23 ¶ And Abraham <sup>e</sup>took Ishmael his son, and all that were born in his house, and all that were bought with his money, every male among the men of Abraham's house, and <sup>f</sup>circumcised the flesh of their foreskin in the <sup>g</sup>self-same day, as God had said unto him.

24 And Abraham *was* <sup>h</sup>ninety years old and nine, when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.

25 And Ishmael his son *was* thirteen years old, when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.

26 In the self-same day was Abraham circumcised, and Ishmael his son;

27 And all the men of his house, born in the house, and bought with money of the stranger, were circumcised <sup>i</sup>with him.

## CHAPTER XVIII.

1 Abraham entertaineth three angels....17 The destruction of Sodom is revealed to Abraham.

AND the LORD <sup>a</sup>appeared unto him in the plains of Mamre: and he sat in the tent-door in the heat of the day:

2 And he lift up his eyes, and looked, and, lo, <sup>b</sup>three men stood by him: and when he saw *them*, he <sup>c</sup>ran to meet them from the tent-door, and <sup>d</sup>bowed himself toward the ground,

3 And said, My Lord, if now I have found <sup>e</sup>favour in thy sight, pass not away, I pray thee, from thy servant:

4 Let a little water, I pray you, be fetched, and <sup>f</sup>wash your feet, and rest yourselves under the tree:

5 And I will fetch a morsel of <sup>g</sup>bread, and <sup>h</sup>comfort ye your hearts; after that you shall pass on: for <sup>i</sup>therefore are ye come to your servant. And they said, So do as thou hast said.

6 And Abraham hastened into the tent unto Sarah, and said, Make ready quickly <sup>k</sup>three measures of <sup>l</sup>fine meal, knead *it*, and make cakes upon the hearth.

7 And Abraham ran unto the herd, and fetched a calf <sup>m</sup>tender and good, and gave *it* unto a young man; and he hasted to dress it.

8 And he took butter and milk, and the calf which he had dressed, and set *it* before them; and he <sup>n</sup>stood by them under the tree, and they did <sup>o</sup>eat.

9 ¶ And they said unto him, Where *is* Sarah thy wife? And he said, Behold, in the <sup>p</sup>tent.

10 And he said, I will certainly <sup>q</sup>return unto thee according to the time of life; and, lo, Sarah thy wife shall have a son. And Sarah heard *it* in the tent-door, which *was* behind him.

11 Now Abraham and Sarah *were* old, *and* <sup>r</sup>well stricken in age; *and* it ceased to be with Sarah after the <sup>s</sup>manner of women.

12 Therefore Sarah <sup>t</sup>laughed within herself, saying, After I am waxed old shall I have pleasure, <sup>u</sup>my lord being old also?

13 And the LORD said unto Abraham, <sup>x</sup>Wherefore did Sarah laugh, saying, Shall I of a surety bear a child which am old?

14 Is any thing <sup>y</sup>too hard for the LORD? At the time appointed <sup>z</sup>I will return unto thee according to the time of life, and Sarah shall have a son.

15 Then Sarah <sup>a</sup>denied, saying, I laughed not; for she was afraid. And he said, Nay; but thou didst laugh.
