## PSALM XXXVI.

The grievous state of the wicked.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David, the* <sup>a</sup>*servant of the LORD.*

THE transgression of the wicked <sup>b</sup>saith within my heart, *that there is* <sup>c</sup>no fear of God before his eyes.

2 For he <sup>d</sup>flattereth himself in his own eyes, until his iniquity be found to be hateful.

3 The words of his mouth *are* iniquity and <sup>e</sup>deceit: he hath left off to <sup>f</sup>be wise, *and* to do good.

4 <sup>g</sup>He deviseth mischief upon his bed; he setteth himself in a way *that is* <sup>h</sup>not good; he abhorreth not evil.

5 Thy mercy, O LORD, *is* in the heavens, and thy faithfulness *reacheth* unto the clouds.

6 Thy righteousness *is* like the great mountains; thy judgments *are* a <sup>i</sup>great deep: O LORD, thou <sup>k</sup>preservest man and beast.

7 How <sup>l</sup>excellent *is* thy loving-kindness, O God! therefore <sup>m</sup>the children of men put their trust under the shadow of thy wings.

8 They shall be <sup>n</sup>abundantly satisfied with the fatness of thy house; and thou shalt make them drink of the river of thy pleasures.

9 For with thee *is* the <sup>o</sup>fountain of life: in thy <sup>p</sup>light shall we see light.

10 O continue thy loving-kindness unto them that know thee: and thy righteousness to the <sup>q</sup>upright in heart.

11 Let not the <sup>r</sup>foot of pride come against me, and let not the hand of the wicked remove me.

12 There are the <sup>s</sup>workers of iniquity fallen: they are cast down, and shall not be able to rise.

## PSALM XXXVII.

David persuadeth to patience and confidence in God.

*A Psalm of David.*

FRET not thyself <sup>a</sup>because of evil-doers, neither be thou envious against the workers of iniquity:

2 For they shall soon be cut down like the <sup>b</sup>grass, and wither as the green herb.

3 Trust in the LORD, and <sup>c</sup>do good: *so* shalt thou dwell in the land, and verily thou shalt be fed.

4 <sup>d</sup>Delight thyself also in the LORD; and he shall give thee the desires of thy heart.

5 <sup>†</sup>Commit thy way unto the LORD; trust also in him, and he shall bring *it* to pass:

6 And he shall bring forth thy righteousness as the <sup>e</sup>light, and thy judgment as the noon-day.

7 <sup>†</sup>Rest in the LORD, and wait patiently for him: fret not thyself because of him who prospereth in his way, because of the man who bringeth wicked devices to pass.

8 Cease from <sup>f</sup>anger, and forsake wrath; fret not thyself in any wise to do evil.

9 For evil-doers shall be <sup>g</sup>cut off: but those that wait upon the LORD, they shall <sup>h</sup>inherit the earth.

10 For <sup>i</sup>yet a little while, and the wicked *shall* not *be:* yea, thou shalt diligently consider his place, and it *shall* <sup>k</sup>not *be.*

11 But the <sup>l</sup>meek shall inherit the earth; and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace.

12 The wicked plotteth against the just, and <sup>m</sup>gnasheth upon him with his teeth.

13 The LORD shall <sup>n</sup>laugh at him; for he seeth that <sup>o</sup>his day is coming.

14 The wicked have drawn out the sword, and have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy, *and* to slay such as be of upright conversation.

15 Their sword shall enter into their <sup>p</sup>own heart, and their bows shall be broken.

16 <sup>q</sup>A little that a righteous man hath *is* better than the riches of many wicked.

17 For the <sup>r</sup>arms of the wicked shall be broken: but the LORD upholdeth the righteous.

18 The LORD knoweth the days of the upright; and their inheritance shall be <sup>s</sup>for ever.

19 They shall not be ashamed in the <sup>t</sup>evil time; and in the days of famine they shall be satisfied.

20 But the wicked shall perish, and the enemies of the LORD *shall be* as the <sup>u</sup>fat of lambs: they shall consume, into smoke shall they consume away.

21 The wicked <sup>x</sup>borroweth and payeth not again: but the righteous <sup>y</sup>sheweth mercy, and giveth.

22 For *such as be* <sup>z</sup>blessed of him shall inherit the earth; and *they that be* cursed of him shall be cut off.
