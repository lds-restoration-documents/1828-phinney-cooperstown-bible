3 For <sup>d</sup>strangers are risen up against me, and oppressors seek after my soul: they have <sup>e</sup>not set God before them. Selah.

4 Behold, God *is* my <sup>f</sup>helper: the LORD *is* with them that uphold my soul.

5 He shall <sup>g</sup>reward evil unto mine enemies: cut them off in thy <sup>h</sup>truth.

6 I will <sup>i</sup>freely sacrifice unto thee; I will praise thy name, O LORD, for *it is* good.

7 For he hath delivered me out of all trouble; and <sup>k</sup>mine eye hath seen *his desire* upon mine enemies.

## PSALM LV.

David in his prayer complaineth of his fearful case.

*To the chief Musician on Neginoth, Maschil. A Psalm of David.*

GIVE ear to my prayer, O God; and <sup>a</sup>hide not thyself from my supplication.

2 Attend unto me, and hear me: <sup>b</sup>I mourn in my complaint, and make <sup>c</sup>a noise;

3 Because of the <sup>d</sup>voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked: for they <sup>e</sup>cast iniquity upon me, and in wrath they <sup>f</sup>hate me.

4 My heart is sore pained within me; and the <sup>g</sup>terrors of death are fallen upon me.

5 Fearfulness and trembling are come upon me, and horror hath <sup>h</sup>overwhelmed me.

6 And I said, Oh that I had wings like a dove! *for then* would I <sup>i</sup>flee away, and be at rest.

7 Lo *then* would I wander far off, *and* remain in the <sup>k</sup>wilderness. Selah.

8 I would <sup>l</sup>hasten my escape from the windy storm *and* tempest.

9 Destroy, O LORD, *and* <sup>m</sup>divide their tongues: for I have seen violence and strife in the city.

10 Day and night they go about it upon the walls thereof; mischief also and sorrow *are* in the <sup>n</sup>midst of it.

11 <sup>o</sup>Wickedness *is* in the midst thereof; deceit and guile depart not from her streets.

12 For *it was* <sup>p</sup>not an enemy *that* reproached me: then could I have borne *it:* neither *was it* he that hated me *that* did magnify *himself* against me; then I would have hid myself from him;

13 But *it was* thou, a man mine <sup>q</sup>equal, <sup>r</sup>my guide, and mine <sup>s</sup>acquaintance.

14 We took sweet counsel together, *and* walked unto the house of God <sup>t</sup>in company.

15 Let death seize upon them, *and* let them go down <sup>u</sup>quick into hell: for wickedness *is* in their dwellings, *and* among them.

16 As for me, I will <sup>x</sup>call upon God, and the LORD shall save me.

17 <sup>y</sup>Evening and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud; and he shall hear my voice.

18 He hath delivered my soul in peace from <sup>z</sup>the battle *that was* against me: for there were many with me.

19 God shall hear and afflict them, even he that abideth of old. Selah. Because they have <sup>a</sup>no changes, therefore they fear not God.

20 He hath <sup>b</sup>put forth his hands against such as be at <sup>c</sup>peace with him; he hath broken his covenant.

21 *The words* of his mouth were <sup>d</sup>smoother than butter, but war *was* in his heart: his words were softer than oil, yet *were* they <sup>e</sup>drawn swords.

22 Cast thy <sup>f</sup>burden upon the LORD, and he shall sustain thee: he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.

23 But thou, O God, shalt bring them down into the pit of destruction: bloody and deceitful men shall <sup>g</sup>not live out half their days; but I will trust in thee.

## PSALM LVI.

David professeth his confidence in God's word.

*To the chief Musician upon Jonath-elem-rechokim, Michtam of David, when the Philistines* <sup>a</sup>*took him in Gath.*

BE merciful unto me, O God; for man would swallow me up: he fighting daily oppresseth me.

2 Mine enemies would daily swallow *me* up: for *they be* <sup>b</sup>many that fight against me, <sup>c</sup>O thou Most High.

3 What time I <sup>d</sup>am afraid I will trust in thee.

4 In God I will praise <sup>e</sup>his word; in God I have put my trust: I will not fear what <sup>f</sup>flesh can do unto me.

5 Every day they <sup>g</sup>wrest my words: all their thoughts *are* against me for evil.

6 They gather themselves together, they hide themselves, they mark my steps, when they wait for my soul.

7 Shall they escape <sup>h</sup>by iniquity? in *thine* anger cast down the people, O God.

8 Thou tellest <sup>i</sup>my wanderings: put thou my tears into thy bottle; *are they* not in <sup>k</sup>thy book?

9 When I cry *unto thee,* <sup>l</sup>then shall mine enemies turn back: this I know; for God *is* for me.

10 In God I will praise *his* word; in the LORD will I praise *his* word.

11 In God have I put <sup>m</sup>my trust: I will not be afraid what man can do unto me.

12 <sup>n</sup>Thy vows *are* upon me, O God: I will render praises unto thee.

13 For <sup>o</sup>thou hast delivered my soul from death; *wilt* not *thou deliver* my feet from falling, that I may <sup>p</sup>walk before God in the <sup>q</sup>light of the living?

## PSALM LVII.

David encourageth himself to praise God.

*To the chief Musician, Al-taschith, Michtam of David, when he fled from Saul in the* <sup>a</sup>*cave.*

BE merciful unto me, O God, be merciful unto me: for my soul trusteth in thee: yea, in the <sup>b</sup>shadow of thy wings will I make my refuge, <sup>c</sup>until *these* calamities be overpast.

2 I will cry unto God most high; unto God that <sup>d</sup>performeth *all things* for me.

3 He shall send <sup>e</sup>from heaven, and save me *from* the reproach of him that would swallow me up. Selah. God shall send forth <sup>f</sup>his mercy and his truth.

4 My soul *is* among <sup>g</sup>lions; *and* I lie *even among* them that are set on fire, *even* the sons of men, whose teeth *are* <sup>h</sup>spears and arrows, and their tongue a sharp sword.

5 Be thou <sup>i</sup>exalted, O God, above the heavens; *let* thy glory *be* above all the earth.

6 They have prepared a net for my steps; my soul is bowed down: they have digged a pit before me, into the midst whereof they <sup>k</sup>are fallen *themselves.* Selah.

7 My heart <sup>l</sup>is fixed, O God, my heart is fixed; I will sing and give praise.

8 <sup>m</sup>Awake up, my glory; awake psaltery and harp; I *myself* will awake early.

9 I will praise thee, O LORD, among the people; I will sing unto <sup>n</sup>thee among the nations:

10 For thy <sup>o</sup>mercy *is* great unto the heavens, and thy truth unto the clouds.
