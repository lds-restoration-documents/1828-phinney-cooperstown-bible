2 Before Ephraim, and Benjamin, and Manasseh, <sup>d</sup>stir up thy strength, and come *and* save us.

3 Turn us again, O God, and cause thy <sup>e</sup>face to shine; and we shall be saved.

4 O LORD God of hosts, how long wilt thou be angry against the prayer of thy people?

5 Thou feedest them with the <sup>f</sup>bread of tears; and givest them tears to drink in great measure.

6 Thou makest us <sup>g</sup>a strife unto our neighbours; and our enemies <sup>h</sup>laugh among themselves.

7 Turn us again, O God of hosts, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.

8 Thou hast brought <sup>i</sup>a vine out of Egypt; thou hast <sup>k</sup>cast out the heathen, and planted it.

9 Thou <sup>l</sup>preparedst *room* before it, and didst cause it to take deep root, and it filled the land.

10 The hills were covered with the shadow of it, and the boughs thereof *were like* the goodly cedars.

11 She sent out her boughs unto the <sup>m</sup>sea, and her branches unto the river.

12 Why hast thou *then* <sup>n</sup>broken down her hedges, so that all they which pass by the way do pluck her?

13 The <sup>o</sup>boar out of the wood doth waste it, and the <sup>p</sup>wild beast of the field doth devour it.

14 Return, we beseech thee, O God of hosts, <sup>q</sup>look down from heaven, and behold, and visit this vine;

15 And the vineyard which thy right hand hath planted, and the branch *that* thou madest <sup>r</sup>strong for thyself.

16 *It is* burnt with fire; *it is* cut down: they perish at the <sup>s</sup>rebuke of thy countenance.

17 Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand, upon the son <sup>t</sup>of man *whom* thou madest strong for thyself.

18 So will not we go back from thee: quicken us, and we will call upon thy name.

19 Turn us again, O LORD God of hosts; cause thy face to shine, and we shall be saved.

## PSALM LXXXI.

An exhortation to a solemn praising of God.

*To the chief Musician upon Gittith. A Psalm of Asaph.*

SING <sup>a</sup>aloud unto God <sup>b</sup>our strength: make a joyful noise unto the God of Jacob.

2 Take a psalm, and bring hither the timbrel, the pleasant harp with the psaltery.

3 Blow up the <sup>c</sup>trumpet in the <sup>d</sup>new moon, in the time <sup>e</sup>appointed, on our solemn <sup>f</sup>feast-day.

4 For this *was* <sup>g</sup>a statute for Israel, *and* a law of the God of Jacob.

5 This he ordained in Joseph *for* a testimony, when he went out through the land of Egypt; *where* I heard a language *that* I <sup>h</sup>understood not.

6 I removed his <sup>i</sup>shoulder from the burden: his hands were delivered from the <sup>k</sup>pots.

7 Thou calledst in trouble, and I delivered thee; I answered thee in the <sup>l</sup>secret place of thunder; I proved thee at the waters of <sup>m</sup>Meribah. Selah.

8 Hear, O my people, and I will <sup>n</sup>testify unto thee: O Israel, <sup>o</sup>if thou wilt hearken unto me;

9 There shall no <sup>p</sup>strange god be in thee; neither shalt thou worship any strange god.

10 I *am* the LORD thy God, which brought thee out of the land of Egypt: <sup>q</sup>open thy mouth wide, and I will fill it.

11 But my people would not hearken to my voice: and Israel would <sup>r</sup>none of me.

12 So <sup>s</sup>I gave them up unto their own hearts' lust: *and* they walked in their <sup>t</sup>own counsels.

13 <sup>u</sup>Oh that my people had hearkened unto me, *and* Israel had walked in my ways!

14 I should soon have <sup>x</sup>subdued their enemies, and turned my hand against their adversaries.

15 The <sup>y</sup>haters of the LORD should have submitted themselves unto him: but their time should have endured for ever.

16 He should have fed them also with the finest of the wheat: and with honey out of the <sup>z</sup>rock should I have satisfied thee.

## PSALM LXXXII.

The Psalmist, having exhorted the judges, prayeth to God.

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

GOD standeth in the congregation of the <sup>a</sup>mighty: he judgeth among the <sup>b</sup>gods.

2 How long will ye judge unjustly, and accept the <sup>c</sup>persons of the wicked? Selah.

3 Defend the poor and fatherless: <sup>d</sup>do justice to the afflicted and needy.

4 <sup>e</sup>Deliver the poor and needy: rid *them* out of the hand of the wicked.

5 They <sup>f</sup>know not, neither will they understand; they <sup>g</sup>walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth are <sup>h</sup>out of course.

6 I have said, Ye *are* <sup>i</sup>gods; and all of you *are* children of the Most High:

7 But ye shall die <sup>k</sup>like men, and fall like one of the princes.

8 Arise, O God, <sup>l</sup>judge the earth: for <sup>m</sup>thou shalt inherit all nations.

## PSALM LXXXIII.

A prayer against them that oppress the church.

*A Song or Psalm of Asaph.*

KEEP not thou <sup>a</sup>silence, O God: hold not thy peace, and be not still, O God.

2 For, lo, <sup>b</sup>thine enemies make a tumult; and they <sup>c</sup>that hate thee have <sup>d</sup>lifted up the head.

3 They have taken crafty counsel against thy people, and consulted against thy <sup>e</sup>hidden ones.

4 They have said, <sup>f</sup>Come, and let us cut them off from *being* <sup>g</sup>a nation; that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.

5 For they have <sup>h</sup>consulted together with one consent; they are confederate against thee:

6 The tabernacles of <sup>i</sup>Edom, and the <sup>k</sup>Ishmaelites; of Moab, and the <sup>l</sup>Hagarenes;

7 <sup>m</sup>Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek; the Philistines, with the inhabitants of Tyre;

8 <sup>n</sup>Assur also is joined with them: they have <sup>o</sup>holpen the children of Lot. Selah.

9 Do unto them as *unto* the <sup>p</sup>Midianites; as *to* <sup>q</sup>Sisera, as *to* Jabin, at the brook of Kison;

10 *Which* perished at En-dor: they became *as* dung for the earth.

11 Make their nobles like Oreb and like Zeeb; yea, all their princes as <sup>r</sup>Zebah and as Zalmunna:

12 Who said, Let us take to ourselves the <sup>s</sup>houses of God in possession.

13 O my God, make them like a wheel; as the <sup>t</sup>stubble before the wind.

14 As the <sup>u</sup>fire burneth a wood, and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire,

15 So persecute them with thy <sup>x</sup>tempest, and make them afraid with thy storm.

16 Fill their faces with <sup>y</sup>shame; that <sup>z</sup>they may seek thy name, O LORD.
