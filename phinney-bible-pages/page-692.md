## CHAPTER VI.

1 Of alms, 5 prayer, 14 forgiveness....19 Our treasure....24 Of God and mammon.

TAKE heed that ye do not your alms before men, to be seen of them: otherwise ye have no reward of your Father which is in heaven.

2 <sup>a</sup>Therefore when thou doest *thine* alms, do not sound a trumpet before thee, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, that they may have glory of men. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward.

3 But when thou doest alms, let not thy left hand know what thy right hand doeth:

4 <sup>b</sup>That thine alms may be in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, himself shall reward thee openly.

5 ¶ <sup>c</sup>And when thou prayest, thou shalt not be as the hypocrites *are:* for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the streets, that they may be seen of men. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward.

6 <sup>d</sup>But thou, when thou prayest, enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.

7 <sup>e</sup>But when ye pray, use not vain repetitions, as the heathen *do:* for they think that they shall be heard for their much speaking.

8 Be not ye therefore like unto them: for your Father knoweth what things ye have need of, before ye ask him.

9 <sup>f</sup>After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father, which art in heaven, hallowed be thy name:

10 <sup>g</sup>Thy kingdom come: thy will be done, in earth as *it is* in heaven:

11 <sup>h</sup>Give us this day our daily bread:

12 <sup>i</sup>And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors:

13 <sup>k</sup>And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: for thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.

14 ¶ <sup>l</sup>For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you:

15 <sup>m</sup>But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.

16 ¶ <sup>n</sup>Moreover, when ye fast, be not, as the hypocrites, of a sad countenance: for they disfigure their faces, that they may appear unto men to fast. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward.

17 But thou, when thou fasteth, anoint thy head, and wash thy face;

18 That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.

19 ¶ <sup>o</sup>Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth, where moth and rust doth corrupt, and where thieves break through and steal:

20 <sup>p</sup>But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt, and where thieves do not break through nor steal:

21 <sup>q</sup>For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.

22 ¶ <sup>r</sup>The light of the body is the eye: If therefore thine eye be single, thy whole body shall be full of light:

23 But if thine eye be evil, thy whole body shall be full of darkness. If therefore the light that is in thee be darkness, how great *is* that darkness!

24 ¶ <sup>s</sup>No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and Mammon.

25 <sup>t</sup>Therefore I say unto you, Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink; nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on. Is not the life more than meat, and the body than raiment?

26 <sup>u</sup>Behold the fowls of the air: for they sow not, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feedeth them. Are ye not much better than they?

27 <sup>x</sup>Which of you by taking thought can add one cubit unto his stature?

28 <sup>y</sup>And why take ye thought for raiment? Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow; they toil not, neither do they spin:

29 And yet I say unto you, That even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.

30 <sup>z</sup>Wherefore, if God so clothe the grass of the field, which to-day is, and to-morrow is cast into the oven, *shall he* not much more *clothe* you, O ye of little faith?

31 Therefore take no thought, saying, What shall we eat? or, What shall we drink? or, Wherewithal shall we be clothed?

32 <sup>a</sup>(For after all these things do the Gentiles seek:) for your heavenly Father knoweth that ye have need of all these things.

33 <sup>b</sup>But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.

34 <sup>c</sup>Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day *is* the evil thereof.

## CHAPTER VII.

1 Christ, ending his sermon on the mount, reproveth rash judgment, 6 forbiddeth to cast holy things to dogs, 7 exhorteth to prayer, 13 to enter in at the strait gate, &c.

<sup>a</sup>JUDGE not, that ye be not judged.

2 <sup>a</sup>For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.

3 <sup>b</sup>And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother's eye, but considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye?

4 <sup>c</sup>Or how wilt thou say to thy brother, Let me pull out the mote out of thine eye: and, behold, a beam *is* in thine own eye?

5 Thou hypocrite, first cast out the beam out of thine own eye; and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brother's eye.
