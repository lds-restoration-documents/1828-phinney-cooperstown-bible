## CHAPTER III.

An exhortation to obedience, faith, and mortification.

MY son, forget not my law; but let <sup>a</sup>thy heart keep my commandments:

2 For length of days, and long life, and <sup>b</sup>peace, shall they add to thee.

3 Let not <sup>c</sup>mercy and truth forsake thee; bind them about thy neck; <sup>d</sup>write them upon the table of thy heart:

4 So shalt thou find <sup>e</sup>favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man.

5 <sup>f</sup>Trust in the LORD with all thy heart; <sup>g</sup>and lean not unto thine own understanding.

6 <sup>h</sup>In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall <sup>i</sup>direct thy paths.

7 <sup>k</sup>Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear the LORD, and <sup>l</sup>depart from evil.

8 It shall be health to thy navel, and marrow to thy bones.

9 Honour the LORD with thy substance, and with the <sup>m</sup>first-fruits of all thine increase:

10 So shall thy barns be filled with <sup>n</sup>plenty, and thy presses shall burst out with new wine.

11 My son, despise not <sup>o</sup>the chastening of the LORD; neither be weary of his correction:

12 For whom the LORD <sup>p</sup>loveth he correcteth, even <sup>q</sup>as a father the son *in whom* he delighteth.

13 <sup>r</sup>Happy *is* the man *that* findeth wisdom, and the man *that* getteth understanding:

14 For the merchandise of it *is* <sup>s</sup>better than the merchandise of silver, and the gain thereof than fine gold.

15 She *is* more precious than rubies: and <sup>t</sup>all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her.

16 Length of days *is* in her right hand; *and* in her left hand <sup>u</sup>riches and honour.

17 Her ways *are* ways of <sup>x</sup>pleasantness, and all her paths *are* <sup>y</sup>peace.

18 She *is* <sup>z</sup>a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her; and happy *is every one* that retaineth her.

19 The LORD <sup>a</sup>by wisdom hath founded the earth; by understanding hath he <sup>b</sup>established the heavens.

20 By his knowledge the <sup>c</sup>depths are broken up, and the clouds drop down the dew.

21 My son, let not them <sup>d</sup>depart from thine eyes: keep sound wisdom and discretion.

22 So shall they be <sup>e</sup>life unto thy soul, and grace to thy neck.

23 Then shalt thou walk in thy way <sup>f</sup>safely, and <sup>g</sup>thy foot shall not stumble.

24 When thou <sup>h</sup>liest down thou shalt not be afraid; yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy <sup>i</sup>sleep shall be sweet.

25 Be not afraid of <sup>k</sup>sudden fear, neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it cometh.

26 For the LORD shall be thy <sup>l</sup>confidence, and shall keep thy foot from being <sup>m</sup>taken.

27 Withhold <sup>n</sup>not good from them to whom it is <sup>o</sup>due, when it is in the power of thy hand to do *it.*

28 Say not unto thy neighbour, <sup>p</sup>Go, and come again, and to-morrow I will give, when thou hast it by thee.

29 <sup>q</sup>Devise not evil against thy neighbour, seeing he dwelleth securely by thee.

30 <sup>r</sup>Strive not with a man without cause, if he have done thee no harm.

31 <sup>s</sup>Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways.

32 For the froward *is* abomination to the LORD: but <sup>t</sup>his secret *is* with the righteous.

33 The <sup>u</sup>curse of the LORD *is* in the house of the wicked: but <sup>x</sup>he blesseth the habitation of the just.

34 Surely he <sup>y</sup>scorneth the scorners: but he giveth grace unto the lowly.

35 <sup>z</sup>The wise shall inherit glory; but <sup>a</sup>shame shall be the promotion of fools.

## CHAPTER IV.

Solomon sheweth what instruction he had of his parents.

HEAR, ye <sup>a</sup>children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding.

2 For I give you <sup>b</sup>good doctrine, forsake you not my law.

3 For I was my father's son, <sup>c</sup>tender and only *beloved* in the sight of my mother.

4 <sup>d</sup>He taught me also, and said unto me, Let thy heart retain my words: keep my commandments, and live.

5 <sup>e</sup>Get wisdom, get understanding; forget *it* not: neither decline from the words of my mouth.

6 Forsake her not, and she shall preserve thee: <sup>f</sup>love her, and she shall keep thee.

7 Wisdom *is* the <sup>g</sup>principal thing: *therefore* get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.

8 Exalt her, and she shall <sup>h</sup>promote thee: she shall <sup>i</sup>bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her.

9 She shall give to thy head <sup>k</sup>an ornament of grace; a crown of glory shall she deliver to thee.

10 Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings; and the <sup>l</sup>years of thy life shall be many.

11 I have taught thee in the way of wisdom; I have led thee in <sup>m</sup>right paths.

12 When thou <sup>n</sup>goest, thy steps shall not be straitened; and when thou runnest, thou shalt not stumble.

13 Take <sup>o</sup>fast hold of instruction; let *her* not go: keep her; for she *is* thy <sup>p</sup>life.

14 Enter not into the path of the wicked, and go not in the <sup>q</sup>way of evil *men.*

15 Avoid it, pass not by it, <sup>†</sup>turn from it and pass away.

16 For they <sup>r</sup>sleep not, except they have done mischief; and their sleep is taken away, unless they cause *some* to fall.

17 For they <sup>s</sup>eat the bread of wickedness, and drink the wine of violence.

18 But the path of the just *is* as the <sup>t</sup>shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day.

19 The way of the wicked *is* <sup>u</sup>as darkness; they <sup>x</sup>know not at what they stumble.

20 My son, <sup>y</sup>attend to my words; incline thine ear unto my sayings:

21 Let them not depart from thine eyes; keep them in the midst of thy heart:

22 For they *are* life unto those that find them, and <sup>†</sup>health to all their flesh.
