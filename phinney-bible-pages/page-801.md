# ¶ The First Epistle of PAUL, the Apostle, to the CORINTHIANS.

## CHAPTER I.

1 Paul, after salutation and thanksgiving, 10 exhorteth to unity, 11 and reproveth their dissensions....26 God, to take away boasting, rejecteth the wisdom of the wise.

<sup>a</sup>PAUL, called *to be* an apostle of Jesus Christ through the will of God, and Sosthenes *our* brother,

2 <sup>b</sup>Unto the church of God which is at Corinth, to them that are sanctified in Christ Jesus, called *to be* saints, with all that in every place call upon the name of Jesus Christ our Lord, both theirs and ours:

3 <sup>c</sup>Grace *be* unto you, and peace, from God our Father, and *from* the Lord Jesus Christ.

4 <sup>d</sup>I thank my God always on your behalf, for the grace of God which is given you by Jesus Christ;

5 <sup>e</sup>That in every thing ye are enriched by him, in all utterance, and *in* all knowledge;

6 <sup>f</sup>Even as the testimony of Christ was confirmed in you:

7 <sup>g</sup>So that ye come behind in no gift; waiting for the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ:

8 <sup>h</sup>Who shall also confirm you unto the end, *that ye may be* blameless in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.

9 <sup>i</sup>God *is* faithful, by whom ye were called unto the fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.

10 <sup>k</sup>Now I beseech you, brethren, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all speak the same thing, and *that* there be no divisions among you; but *that* ye be perfectly joined together in the same mind and in the same judgment.

11 <sup>l</sup>For it hath been declared unto me of you, my brethren, by them *which are of the house* of Chloe, that there are contentions among you.

12 <sup>m</sup>Now this I say, that every one of you saith, I am of Paul; and I of Apollos; and I of Cephas; and I of Christ.

13 <sup>n</sup>Is Christ divided? was Paul crucified for you? or were ye baptized in the name of Paul?

14 <sup>o</sup>I thank God that I baptized none of you but Crispus and Gaius;

15 Lest any should say that I had baptized in mine own name.

16 <sup>p</sup>And I baptized also the household of Stephanas: besides, I know not whether I baptized any other.

17 <sup>q</sup>For Christ sent me not to baptize, but to preach the gospel: not with wisdom of words, lest the cross of Christ should be made of none effect.

18 <sup>r</sup>For the preaching of the cross is to them that perish foolishness; but unto us which are saved it is the power of God.

19 <sup>s</sup>For it is written, I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and will bring to nothing the understanding of the prudent.

20 <sup>t</sup>Where *is* the wise? where *is* the scribe? where *is* the disputer of this world? hath not God made foolish the wisdom of this world?

21 <sup>u</sup>For after that, in the wisdom of God, the world by wisdom knew not God, it pleased God by the foolishness of preaching to save them that believe.

22 <sup>x</sup>For the Jews require a sign, and the Greeks seek after wisdom:

23 <sup>y</sup>But we preach Christ crucified, unto the Jews a stumbling-block, and unto the Greeks foolishness;

24 <sup>z</sup>But unto them which are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God.

25 Because the foolishness of God is wiser than men; and the weakness of God is stronger than men.

26 <sup>a</sup>For ye see your calling, brethren, how that not many wise men after the flesh, not many mighty, not many noble, *are called:*

27 <sup>b</sup>But God hath chosen the foolish things of the world, to confound the wise; and God hath chosen the weak things of the world, to confound the things which are mighty;

28 <sup>c</sup>And base things of the world, and things which are despised, hath God chosen, *yea,* and things which are not, to bring to nought things that are;

29 <sup>d</sup>That no flesh should glory in his presence.

30 <sup>e</sup>But of him are ye in Christ Jesus, who of God is made unto us wisdom, and righteousness, and sanctification, and redemption:

31 <sup>f</sup>That, according as it is written, He that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord.

## CHAPTER II.

1 How Paul preached the gospel; 6 which is God's wise counsel for men's salvation.

<sup>a</sup>AND I, brethren, when I came to you, came not with excellency of speech or of wisdom, declaring unto you the testimony of God:

2 <sup>b</sup>For I determined not to know any thing among you, save Jesus Christ, and him crucified.

3 <sup>c</sup>And I was with you in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling.

4 <sup>d</sup>And my speech and my preaching *was* not with enticing words of man's wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power:
