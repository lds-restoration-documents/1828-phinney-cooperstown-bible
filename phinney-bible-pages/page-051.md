# ¶ The SECOND Book of Moses, called EXODUS.

## CHAPTER I.

22 Pharaoh commandeth the male children to be cast into the river.

NOW these *are* the <sup>a</sup>names of the children of Israel, which came into Egypt; every man and his household came with Jacob.

2 <sup>b</sup>Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah,

3 <sup>c</sup>Issachar, Zebulun, and <sup>d</sup>Benjamin,

4 Dan, and Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

5 And all the souls that came out of the loins of Jacob were <sup>e</sup>seventy souls: <sup>f</sup>for Joseph was in Egypt *already.*

6 And Joseph died, and all <sup>g</sup>his brethren, and all that generation.

7 And the children of Israel were fruitful, and <sup>h</sup>increased abundantly, and multiplied, and waxed exceeding mighty; and the land was filled with them.

8 ¶ Now there arose up a <sup>i</sup>new king over Egypt, which <sup>k</sup>knew not Joseph.

9 And he said unto his people, Behold, the people of the children of Israel *are* <sup>l</sup>more and mightier than we:

10 <sup>m</sup>Come on, let us <sup>n</sup>deal wisely with them; lest they multiply, and it come to pass, that, when there falleth out any war, they join also unto our enemies, and fight against us, and *so* get them up out of the land.

11 Therefore they did set over them task-masters, to afflict them with their <sup>o</sup>burdens. And they built for Pharaoh treasure-cities, Pithom and Raamses.

12 <sup>†</sup>But the more they afflicted them the more they multiplied and grew. And they were <sup>p</sup>grieved because of the children of Israel.

13 And the Egyptians made the children of Israel to serve with <sup>q</sup>rigour:

14 And they made their lives bitter with hard bondage, in mortar, and in brick, and in all manner of service in the field: all their service, wherein they made them serve, *was* with rigour.

15 ¶ And the king of Egypt spake to the Hebrew midwives; (of which the name of one *was* Shiphrah, and the name of the other Puah;)

16 And he said, When ye do the office of a midwife to the Hebrew women, and see *them* upon the stools, if it *be* a son, then ye shall <sup>r</sup>kill him: but if it *be* a daughter, then she shall live.

17 But the midwives <sup>s</sup>feared God, and did not <sup>t</sup>as the king of Egypt commanded them, but saved the men-children alive.

18 And the king of Egypt called for the midwives, and said unto them, <sup>u</sup>Why have ye done this thing, and have saved the men-children alive?

19 And the midwives said unto Pharaoh, Because the Hebrew women *are* not as the Egyptian women; for they *are* lively, and are delivered <sup>x</sup>ere the midwives come in unto them.

20 Therefore God dealt <sup>y</sup>well with the midwives: and the people multiplied, and waxed very mighty.

21 And it came to pass, because the midwives feared God, that he <sup>z</sup>made them houses.

22 And Pharaoh <sup>a</sup>charged all his people, saying, Every son that is born ye shall cast into the river, and every daughter ye shall save alive.

## CHAPTER II.

2 Moses is born, 3 and in an ark cast into the flags....11 He slayeth an Egyptian....21 He marrieth Zipporah.

AND there went <sup>a</sup>a man of the house of Levi, and <sup>b</sup>took *to wife* a daughter of Levi.

2 And the woman conceived and bare a <sup>c</sup>son: and when she saw him that he *was* <sup>d</sup>*a* goodly *child,* she <sup>e</sup>hid him three months.

3 And when she could <sup>f</sup>no longer hide him, she took for him an ark of bulrushes, and daubed it with slime and with pitch, and put the child therein; and she laid *it* in the flags by the river's brink.

4 And <sup>g</sup>his sister stood afar off, to wit what would be done to him.

5 And the <sup>h</sup>daughter of Pharaoh came down to wash *herself* at the river; and her maidens walked along by the river's side: and when she saw the ark among the flags, she sent her maid to fetch it.

6 And when she had opened *it,* she saw the child: and, behold, the babe wept. And she had <sup>i</sup>compassion on him, and said, This *is one* of the <sup>k</sup>Hebrews' children.

7 Then said his sister to Pharaoh's daughter, Shall I go and call to thee a nurse of the Hebrew women, that she may nurse the child for thee?

8 And Pharaoh's daughter said to her, Go. And the maid went and called the <sup>l</sup>child's mother.

9 And Pharaoh's daughter said unto her, Take this child away, and nurse it for me, and I will give *thee* thy wages. And the woman took the child, and nursed it.

10 And the child grew, and she brought him unto Pharaoh's daughter, and he became <sup>m</sup>her son. And she called his name <sup>||</sup>Moses: and she said, Because I drew him out of the water.

11 ¶ And it came to pass in those days, when Moses was <sup>n</sup>grown, that he went out unto his brethren, and looked on their burdens: and he espied an Egyptian smiting a Hebrew, one of his brethren.

12 And he looked this way and that way, and, when he saw that *there was* no man, he <sup>o</sup>slew the Egyptian, and hid him in the sand.

13 And when he went out <sup>p</sup>the second day, behold, two men of the Hebrews strove together: and he said <sup>q</sup>to him that did the wrong, Wherefore smitest thou thy fellow?

14 And he said, Who made thee <sup>r</sup>a prince and a judge over us? intendest thou to kill me, as thou killedst the Egyptian? And Moses <sup>s</sup>feared, and said, Surely this thing is known.

15 Now when Pharaoh heard this thing, he sought to slay Moses. But Moses <sup>t</sup>fled from the face of Pharaoh, and dwelt in the land of <sup>u</sup>Midian: And he sat down by a <sup>x</sup>well.

16 Now the <sup>||</sup>priest of Midian had seven daughters: and they came and drew *water,* and filled the troughs to water their father's flock.

17 And the shepherds came and drove them away: but Moses stood up and <sup>y</sup>helped them, and watered their flock.

18 And when they came to <sup>z</sup>Reuel their father, he said, How *is it that* you are come so soon to-day?

19 And they said, An Egyptian delivered us out of the hand of the shepherds, and also drew *water* enough for us, and watered the flock.

20 And he said unto his <sup>a</sup>daughters, And where *is* he? why *is* it *that* ye have left the man? call him, that he may eat <sup>b</sup>bread.

21 And Moses was content to dwell with the man: and he <sup>c</sup>gave Moses <sup>d</sup>Zipporah his daughter.
