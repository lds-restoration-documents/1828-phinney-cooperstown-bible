## CHAPTER XII.

Jeremiah complaineth of the prosperity of the wicked.

RIGHTEOUS <sup>a</sup>*art* thou, O LORD, when I plead with thee; yet let me talk with thee of *thy* judgments: Wherefore doth the way of the wicked <sup>b</sup>prosper? *wherefore* are all they happy that deal very treacherously?

2 Thou hast planted them; yea, they have taken root: they grow; yea, they bring forth fruit: thou *art* <sup>c</sup>near in their mouth, and far from their reins.

3 But thou, O LORD, <sup>d</sup>knowest me; thou hast seen me, and tried my heart <sup>e</sup>toward thee; pull them out like sheep for the slaughter, and prepare them for the <sup>f</sup>day of slaughter.

4 How long shall the <sup>g</sup>land mourn, and the herbs of every field wither, for the <sup>h</sup>wickedness of them that dwell therein? the beasts are consumed, and the birds; because they said, He shall <sup>i</sup>not see our last end.

5 If thou hast run with the footmen, and they have wearied thee, then how canst thou <sup>k</sup>contend with horses? and *if* in the land of peace, *wherein* thou trustedst, *they wearied thee,* then how wilt thou do in the <sup>l</sup>swelling of Jordan?

6 For even thy <sup>m</sup>brethren, and the house of thy father, even they have dealt treacherously with thee; yea, they have <sup>n</sup>called a multitude after thee: believe them not, though they speak <sup>o</sup>fair words unto thee.

7 I have forsaken <sup>p</sup>my house, I have left my heritage; I have given the dearly-beloved of my soul into the hand of her enemies.

8 My heritage is unto me as <sup>q</sup>a lion in the forest; it crieth out against me: therefore have I hated it.

9 My heritage *is* unto me *as* <sup>r</sup>a speckled bird; the birds round about *are* against her; come ye, assemble all the beasts of the field, <sup>s</sup>come to devour.

10 Many <sup>t</sup>pastors have destroyed my vineyard, they have trodden my <sup>u</sup>portion under foot, they have made my pleasant portion a desolate wilderness.

11 <sup>x</sup>They have made it desolate, *and being* desolate it mourneth unto me; the whole land is made desolate, because no man <sup>y</sup>layeth *it* to heart.

12 The <sup>z</sup>spoilers are come upon all high places through the wilderness: for the sword of the LORD shall devour from the *one* end of the land even to the *other* end of the land: <sup>a</sup>no flesh shall have peace.

13 They have <sup>b</sup>sown wheat, but shall reap thorns; they have put themselves to pain, *but* shall not profit; and they shall be <sup>c</sup>ashamed of your revenues, because of the fierce anger of the LORD.

14 Thus saith the LORD against all mine <sup>d</sup>evil neighbours, that <sup>e</sup>touch the inheritance which I have caused my people Israel to inherit: Behold, I will <sup>f</sup>pluck them out of their land, and pluck out the house of Judah from among them.

15 And it shall come to pass, after that I have plucked them out I will return, and have compassion on them, and will <sup>g</sup>bring them again, every man to his heritage, and every man to his land.

16 And it shall come to pass, if they will diligently learn the ways of my people, to swear by my name, The LORD liveth; (as they <sup>h</sup>taught my people to swear by Baal;) then shall they <sup>i</sup>be built in the midst of my people.

17 But if they will <sup>k</sup>not obey, I will utterly pluck up and destroy that nation, saith the LORD.

## CHAPTER XIII.

By the type of a linen girdle, God prefigureth his people's destruction.

THUS saith the LORD unto me, Go and get thee a <sup>a</sup>linen girdle, and put it upon thy loins, and <sup>b</sup>put it not in water.

2 So I got a girdle, <sup>c</sup>according to the word of the LORD, and put *it* on my loins.

3 And the word of the LORD came unto me the second time, saying,

4 Take the girdle that thou hast got, which *is* upon thy loins, and arise, go to <sup>d</sup>Euphrates, and hide it there in a hole of the rock.

5 <sup>e</sup>So I went, and hid it by Euphrates, as the LORD commanded me.

6 And it came to pass after many days, that the LORD said unto me, Arise, go to Euphrates, and take the girdle from thence, which I commanded thee to hide there.
