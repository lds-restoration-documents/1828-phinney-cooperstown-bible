## CHAPTER II.

1 The idolatry of the people....6 God's judgments against them: 14 His promises of reconciliation with them.

SAY <sup>a</sup>ye unto your brethren, Ammi; and to your sisters, Ruhamah.

2 <sup>b</sup>Plead with your mother, plead; for she *is* not <sup>c</sup>my wife, neither *am* I her husband: let her, therefore, put away her whoredoms out of her sight, and her adulteries from between her breasts;

3 Lest I <sup>d</sup>strip her naked, and set her as in the day that she was born, and make her as a wilderness, and set her like a dry land, and slay her with <sup>e</sup>thirst.

4 And I will not have mercy upon her children; for they *be* the children of <sup>f</sup>whoredoms.

5 For their <sup>g</sup>mother hath played the harlot; she that conceived them hath done shamefully: for she said, <sup>h</sup>I will go after my lovers, that give *me* my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, mine oil and my drink.

6 Therefore, behold, I will <sup>i</sup>hedge up thy way with thorns, and make a wall, that she shall not find her paths.

7 And she shall <sup>k</sup>follow after her lovers, but she shall <sup>l</sup>not overtake them; and she shall seek them, but shall not find *them:* then shall she say, I will go and return to <sup>m</sup>my first husband; for then *was it* <sup>n</sup>better with me than now.

8 For she did <sup>o</sup>not know that I gave her corn, and wine, and oil, and multiplied her silver and gold, *which* they <sup>p</sup>prepared for Baal.

9 Therefore will I <sup>q</sup>return, and <sup>r</sup>take away my corn in the time thereof, and my wine in the season thereof, and will recover my wool and my flax *given* to cover her nakedness.

10 And now will I <sup>s</sup>discover her lewdness in the sight of her lovers, and none shall deliver her out of my hand.

11 I will also cause all her mirth to cease, her <sup>t</sup>feast-days, her new moons, and her sabbaths, and all her solemn feasts.

12 And I will destroy her vines, and her <sup>u</sup>fig-trees, whereof she hath said, These *are* my <sup>x</sup>rewards, that my lovers have given me: and I will make them <sup>y</sup>a forest, and the beasts of the field shall eat them.

13 And I will visit upon her the days of <sup>z</sup>Baalim, wherein she burnt incense to them, and she decked herself with her ear-rings and her jewels, and she went after her lovers, and forgat me, saith the LORD.

14 <sup>a</sup>Therefore, behold, I will allure her, and bring her into the wilderness, and speak comfortably unto her.

15 And I will give her her vineyards from thence, and the <sup>b</sup>valley of Achor for a door of hope; and she shall sing there, as in the days of her <sup>c</sup>youth, and as in the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt.

16 And it shall be at that day, saith the LORD, *that* thou shalt call me <sup>d</sup>Ishi, and shalt call me no more <sup>e</sup>Baali.

17 For I will take away <sup>f</sup>the names of Baalim out of her mouth, and they shall no more be remembered by their name.

18 And in that day will I make a covenant for them with the <sup>g</sup>beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven, and *with* the creeping things of the ground: and I will break the <sup>h</sup>bow, and the sword, and the battle out of the earth, and will make them to lie down <sup>i</sup>safely.

19 And I will betroth thee unto me for ever; yea, I will <sup>k</sup>betroth thee unto me in <sup>l</sup>righteousness, and in judgment, and in loving-kindness, and in mercies:

20 I will even betroth thee unto me in <sup>m</sup>faithfulness; and thou <sup>n</sup>shalt know the LORD.

21 And it shall come to pass in that day, I will hear, saith the LORD, I will <sup>o</sup>hear the heavens; and they shall hear the earth;

22 And the earth shall hear the corn, and the wine, and the oil; and they shall <sup>p</sup>hear Jezreel.

23 And I will <sup>q</sup>sow her unto me in the earth; and I will have mercy upon her that <sup>r</sup>had not obtained mercy; and I will say to *them which were* not my people, Thou *art* my people; and they shall say, *Thou art* my God.

## CHAPTER III.

By the expiation of an adulteress, is shewed the desolation of Israel before their restoration.

THEN said the LORD unto me, <sup>a</sup>Go yet, love a woman beloved of *her* <sup>b</sup>friend, yet an adulteress, according to the love of the LORD toward the children of Israel, who look to other gods, and <sup>c</sup>love flagons of wine.

2 So I <sup>d</sup>bought her to me for <sup>e</sup>fifteen *pieces* of silver, and *for* a homer of barley, and a half homer of barley:

3 And I said unto her, Thou shalt <sup>f</sup>abide for me many days: thou shalt not play the harlot, and thou shalt not be for *another* man; so *will* <sup>g</sup>I also *be* for thee.

4 For the children of Israel shall abide <sup>h</sup>many days without a king, and without a prince, and without a sacrifice, and without an image, and without an ephod, and *without* teraphim.

5 Afterward shall the children of Israel return, and seek the LORD their God, and <sup>i</sup>David their king; and shall <sup>k</sup>fear the LORD and his goodness in the <sup>l</sup>latter days.

## CHAPTER IV.

1 God's judgments against the sins of the people, &c....15 Judah exhorted to take warning by Israel's calamity.

HEAR the word of the LORD, ye children of Israel: for the LORD hath <sup>a</sup>a controversy with the inhabitants of the land, because *there is* no truth, nor mercy, nor <sup>b</sup>knowledge of God, in the land.

2 By swearing, and lying, and killing, and stealing, and committing adultery, they <sup>c</sup>break out, and blood toucheth blood.

3 Therefore shall the land <sup>d</sup>mourn, and every one that dwelleth therein shall languish, with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven; yea, the fishes of the sea also shall be <sup>e</sup>taken away.

4 Yet let no man <sup>f</sup>strive, nor reprove another: for thy people *are* as they that strive <sup>g</sup>with the priest.

5 Therefore shalt thou fall in the <sup>h</sup>day, and the prophet also shall fall with thee in the <sup>i</sup>night, and I will destroy thy mother.

6 ¶ My people are destroyed for <sup>k</sup>lack of knowledge: because thou hast <sup>l</sup>rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be <sup>m</sup>no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also <sup>n</sup>forget thy children.

7 As they were <sup>o</sup>increased, so they sinned against me; *therefore* will I change their <sup>p</sup>glory into shame.

8 They eat up the sin of my people, and they set their heart on their iniquity.
