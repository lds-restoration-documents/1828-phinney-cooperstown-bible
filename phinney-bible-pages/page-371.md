## PSALM XXXIII.

God is to be praised for his goodness.

REJOICE in the LORD, O ye righteous; *for* praise is <sup>a</sup>comely <sup>b</sup>for the upright.

2 Praise the LORD with <sup>c</sup>harp: sing unto him with the psaltery, *and* an instrument of ten strings.

3 Sing unto him <sup>d</sup>a new song; play <sup>e</sup>skilfully with a loud noise:

4 For the word of the LORD *is* <sup>f</sup>right; and all his works *are done* in truth.

5 <sup>g</sup>He loveth righteousness and judgment: <sup>h</sup>the earth is full of the goodness of the LORD.

6 By the word of the LORD were the heavens made: and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth.

7 <sup>i</sup>He gathereth the waters of the sea together as a heap: he layeth up the depth <sup>k</sup>in store-houses.

8 Let all the earth <sup>l</sup>fear the LORD; let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him:

9 For he <sup>m</sup>spake, and it was *done;* he commanded, and it stood fast.

10 The LORD bringeth the counsel of the heathen <sup>n</sup>to nought: he maketh the devices of the people of none effect.

11 The counsel of the LORD standeth for ever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations.

12 Blessed *is* the nation <sup>o</sup>whose God *is* the LORD; *and* the people *whom* he hath <sup>p</sup>chosen for his own inheritance.

13 The LORD <sup>q</sup>looketh from heaven; he beholdeth all the sons of men.

14 From the place of his habitation he looketh upon all the inhabitants of the earth.

15 He fashioneth their hearts alike; he considereth all their works.

16 There is no king saved by the <sup>r</sup>multitude of a host: a mighty man is not delivered by much strength.

17 A horse *is* a vain thing for safety: neither shall he deliver *any* by <sup>s</sup>his great strength.

18 Behold, <sup>t</sup>the eye of the LORD *is* upon them that fear him, upon them that hope in his mercy;

19 To deliver their soul from death, and to keep them alive <sup>u</sup>in famine.

20 Our soul waiteth for the LORD; he *is* <sup>x</sup>our help and our shield.

21 For our heart shall rejoice in him; <sup>y</sup>because we have trusted in his holy name.

22 Let thy mercy, O LORD, be upon us, according as we hope in thee.

David praiseth God, and exhorteth others thereto by his experience.

*A Psalm of David, when he changed his behaviour before Abimelech; who* <sup>a</sup>*drove him away, and he departed.*

I WILL bless the LORD <sup>b</sup>at all times: his praise *shall* continually *be* in my mouth.

2 My soul shall make her <sup>c</sup>boast in the LORD: <sup>d</sup>the humble shall hear *thereof,* and be glad.

3 O magnify the LORD <sup>e</sup>with me, and let us exalt his name together.

4 I sought the LORD, and he heard me, and delivered me from all my <sup>f</sup>fears.

5 They looked unto him, and were lightened; and their faces were not ashamed.

6 This <sup>g</sup>poor man cried, and the LORD heard *him,* and saved him out of all his troubles.

7 The angel of the LORD <sup>h</sup>encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.

8 O <sup>i</sup>taste and see that the LORD *is* good: blessed *is* the man *that* <sup>k</sup>trusteth in him.

9 O <sup>l</sup>fear the LORD, ye his saints: for *there is* <sup>m</sup>no want to them that fear him.

10 The young lions do lack and <sup>n</sup>suffer hunger: but they that seek the LORD shall not want <sup>o</sup>any good *thing.*

11 Come, ye children, hearken unto me; I will <sup>p</sup>teach you the fear of the LORD.

12 What man *is he that* desireth life, *and* loveth *many* days, that he may <sup>q</sup>see good?

13 <sup>r</sup>Keep thy tongue from evil, and thy lips from speaking <sup>s</sup>guile.

14 Depart from evil, and do good; <sup>t</sup>seek peace, and pursue it.

15 <sup>u</sup>The eyes of the LORD *are* upon the righteous, and his ears *are open* unto their <sup>x</sup>cry.

16 <sup>y</sup>The face of the LORD *is* against them that do evil, to <sup>z</sup>cut off the remembrance of them from the earth.

17 *The righteous* <sup>a</sup>cry, and the LORD heareth, and delivereth them out of all their troubles.

18 The LORD *is* nigh unto them that are of a <sup>b</sup>broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

19 <sup>c</sup>Many *are* the afflictions of the righteous: but the LORD <sup>d</sup>delivereth him out of them all.

20 He keepeth <sup>e</sup>all his bones: not one of them is broken.

21 <sup>f</sup>Evil shall slay the wicked; and they that hate the righteous shall be desolate.

22 The LORD <sup>g</sup>redeemeth the soul of his servants; and none of them that trust in him shall be desolate.
