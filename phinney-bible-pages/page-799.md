16 <sup>q</sup>*Be* of the same mind one toward another. Mind not high things, but condescend to men of low estate. Be not wise in your own conceits.

17 <sup>r</sup>Recompense to no man evil for evil. Provide things honest in the sight of all men.

18 <sup>s</sup>If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.

19 <sup>t</sup>Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves; but *rather* give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance *is* mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.

20 <sup>u</sup>Therefore if thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink: for in so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire on his head.

21 <sup>x</sup>Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.

## CHAPTER XIII.

1 Of subjection to magistrates....8 Love is a debt we always owe, and virtually containeth the whole law....11 Against gluttony, &c.

<sup>a</sup>LET every soul be subject unto the higher powers. For there is no power but of God: the powers that be are ordained of God.

2 <sup>b</sup>Whosoever therefore resisteth the power, resisteth the ordinance of God: and they that resist shall receive to themselves damnation.

3 <sup>c</sup>For rulers are not a terror to good works, but to the evil. Wilt thou then not be afraid of the power? do that which is good, and thou shalt have praise of the same:

4 <sup>d</sup>For he is the minister of God to thee for good. But if thou do that which is evil, be afraid; for he beareth not the sword in vain: for he is the minister of God, a revenger to *execute* wrath upon him that doeth evil.

5 <sup>e</sup>Wherefore *ye* must needs be subject, not only for wrath, but also for conscience' sake.

6 For, for this cause pay ye tribute also: for they are God's ministers, attending continually upon this very thing.

7 <sup>f</sup>Render therefore to all their dues; tribute to whom tribute *is due;* custom to whom custom; fear to whom fear; honour to whom honour.

8 <sup>g</sup>Owe no man any thing, but to love one another: for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.

9 <sup>h</sup>For this, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Thou shalt not covet; and if *there* be any other commandment, it is briefly comprehended in this saying, namely, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

10 <sup>i</sup>Love worketh no ill to his neighbour: therefore love *is* the fulfilling of the law.

11 <sup>k</sup>And that, knowing the time, that now *it is* high time to awake out of sleep: for now *is* our salvation nearer than when we believed.

12 <sup>l</sup>The night is far spent, the day is at hand: let us therefore cast off the works of darkness, and let us put on the armour of light.

13 <sup>m</sup>Let us walk honestly, as in the day; not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying.

14 <sup>n</sup>But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to *fulfil* the lusts *thereof.*

## CHAPTER XIV.

How to treat a weak brother, and not to censure one another for things indifferent, &c.

<sup>a</sup>HIM that is weak in the faith receive ye, *but* not to doubtful disputations.

2 <sup>b</sup>For one believeth that he may eat all things: another, who is weak, eateth herbs.

3 <sup>c</sup>Let not him that eateth despise him that eateth not; and let not him which eateth not judge him that eateth: for God hath received him.

4 <sup>d</sup>Who art thou that judgest another man's servant? to his own master he standeth or falleth. Yea, he shall be holden up: for God is able to make him stand.

5 <sup>e</sup>One man esteemeth one day above another: another esteemeth every day *alike.* Let every man be fully persuaded in his own mind.

6 <sup>f</sup>He that regardeth the day, regardeth *it* unto the Lord; and he that regardeth not the day, to the Lord he doth not regard *it.* He that eateth, eateth to the Lord; for he giveth God thanks: and he that eateth not, to the Lord he eateth not; and giveth God thanks.

7 <sup>g</sup>For none of us liveth to himself, and no man dieth to himself.

8 For whether we live, we live unto the Lord; and whether we die, we die unto the Lord: whether we live therefore, or die, we are the Lord's.

9 <sup>h</sup>For to this end Christ both died, and rose, and revived, that he might be Lord both of the dead and living.

10 <sup>i</sup>But why dost thou judge thy brother? or why dost thou set at nought thy brother? for we shall all stand before the judgment-seat of Christ.

11 <sup>k</sup>For it is written, *As* I live, saith the Lord, every knee shall bow to me, and every tongue shall confess to God.

12 <sup>l</sup>So then every one of us shall give account of himself to God.

13 <sup>m</sup>Let us not therefore judge one another any more: but judge this rather, that no man put a stumbling-block or an occasion to fall in *his* brother's way.

14 <sup>n</sup>I know, and am persuaded by the Lord Jesus, that *there is* nothing unclean of itself: but to him that esteemeth any thing to be unclean, to him *it is* unclean.

15 <sup>o</sup>But if thy brother be grieved with *thy* meat, now walkest thou not charitably. Destroy not him with thy meat, for whom Christ died.

16 <sup>p</sup>Let not then your good be evil spoken of:

17 <sup>q</sup>For the kingdom of God is not meat and drink; but righteousness and peace, and joy in the Holy Ghost.

18 For he that in these things serveth Christ, *is* acceptable to God, and approved of men.

19 <sup>r</sup>Let us therefore follow after the things which make for peace, and things wherewith one may edify another.

20 <sup>s</sup>For meat destroy not the work of God. All things indeed *are* pure; but *it is* evil for that man who eateth with offence.

21 <sup>t</sup>*It is* good neither to eat flesh, nor to drink wine, nor *any thing* whereby thy brother stumbleth, or is offended, or is made weak.

22 <sup>u</sup>Hast thou faith? have *it* to thyself before God. Happy *is* he that condemneth not himself in that thing which he alloweth.

23 <sup>x</sup>And he that doubteth is damned if he eat, because *he eateth* not of faith: for whatsoever *is* not of faith is sin.

## CHAPTER XV.

1 The strong ought to bear with the weak....2 We may not please ourselves, 7 but receive one another, as Christ also received us, &c.

<sup>a</sup>WE then that are strong ought to bear the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.
