## PSALM CXXXV.

An exhortation to praise God for his mercy and for his power.

PRAISE ye the LORD. Praise ye the <sup>a</sup>name of the LORD; praise *him,* O ye servants of the LORD.

2 Ye that stand in the house of the LORD, in the <sup>b</sup>courts of the house of our God,

3 Praise the LORD; for the LORD <sup>c</sup>*is* good: sing praises unto his name; for *it is* <sup>d</sup>pleasant.

4 For the LORD hath chosen Jacob unto himself, *and* Israel for his <sup>e</sup>peculiar treasure.

5 For I know that the LORD *is* great, and *that* our LORD *is* <sup>f</sup>above all gods.

6 <sup>g</sup>Whatsoever the LORD pleased, *that* did he in heaven, and in earth, in the seas, and all deep places.

7 He causeth the <sup>h</sup>vapours to ascend from the ends of the earth: he maketh lightnings for the rain: he bringeth the wind out of his <sup>i</sup>treasuries.

8 Who smote the first-born of Egypt, <sup>k</sup>both of man and beast.

9 *Who* sent tokens and wonders into the midst of thee, O Egypt, <sup>l</sup>upon Pharaoh, and upon all his servants.

10 Who smote <sup>m</sup>great nations, and slew mighty kings;

11 Sihon king of the Amorites, and Og king of Bashan, and <sup>n</sup>all the kingdoms of Canaan:

12 And gave their land *for* <sup>o</sup>a heritage, a heritage unto Israel his people.

13 Thy name, O LORD, *endureth* for ever; *and* thy <sup>p</sup>memorial, O LORD, throughout all generations.

14 For the LORD will <sup>q</sup>judge his people, and he will repent himself concerning his servants.

15 The <sup>r</sup>idols of the heathen *are* silver and gold, the <sup>s</sup>work of men's hands.

16 They have mouths, but they speak not; eyes have they, but they <sup>t</sup>see not;

17 They have ears, but they hear not; neither is there *any* breath in their mouths.

18 They that make them are like unto them: *so is* every one that <sup>u</sup>trusteth in them.

19 Bless the LORD, O house of Israel: bless the LORD, O house of <sup>x</sup>Aaron:

20 Bless the LORD, O house of <sup>y</sup>Levi: ye that fear the LORD, bless the LORD.

21 Blessed be the LORD out of Zion, which dwelleth at Jerusalem. <sup>†</sup>Praise ye the LORD.

An exhortation to give thanks to God for particular mercies.

3 O give thanks to the <sup>c</sup>LORD of lords: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

4 To him who <sup>d</sup>alone doeth great wonders: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

5 To him that <sup>e</sup>by wisdom made the heavens: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

6 To him that <sup>f</sup>stretched out the earth above the waters: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

7 To him that <sup>g</sup>made great lights: for his mercy *endureth* for ever:

10 To him that <sup>i</sup>smote Egypt in their first-born: for his mercy *endureth* for ever:

15 But <sup>†</sup>overthrew Pharaoh and his host in the Red Sea: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

16 To him which <sup>n</sup>led his people through the wilderness: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

17 To him which smote <sup>o</sup>great kings: for his mercy *endureth* for ever:

18 And slew <sup>p</sup>famous kings: for his mercy *endureth* for ever:

19 <sup>q</sup>Sihon king of the Amorites: for his mercy *endureth* for ever:

20 And <sup>r</sup>Og the king of Bashan: for his mercy *endureth* for ever:

21 And <sup>s</sup>gave their land for a heritage: for his mercy *endureth* for ever:

22 *Even* a heritage unto Israel <sup>t</sup>his servant: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

23 Who remembered us in our <sup>u</sup>low estate: for his mercy *endureth* for ever:

24 And hath <sup>x</sup>redeemed us from our enemies: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

25 Who giveth <sup>y</sup>food to all flesh: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

26 O give thanks unto the God of heaven: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

## PSALM CXXXVII.

The constancy of the Jews in captivity.

BY the rivers of <sup>a</sup>Babylon there we sat down; yea, we wept, when we <sup>b</sup>remembered Zion.

2 We hanged our <sup>c</sup>harps upon the willows in the midst thereof.

3 For there they that carried us away captive required of us a song; and they that <sup>d</sup>wasted us *required of us* mirth, *saying,* Sing us *one* of the songs of Zion.

4 How shall we sing the LORD's <sup>e</sup>song in a strange land?

5 If I forget thee, O Jerusalem, let my right hand forget *her cunning.*

6 If I do not remember thee, let my tongue cleave to the <sup>f</sup>roof of my mouth; if I prefer not Jerusalem above <sup>g</sup>my chief joy.

7 Remember, O LORD, the children of <sup>h</sup>Edom in the <sup>i</sup>day of Jerusalem; who said, Rase *it,* rase *it, even* to the foundation thereof.

8 O daughter of Babylon, who art to be destroyed; happy *shall he be* that <sup>k</sup>rewardeth thee as thou hast served us.

9 Happy *shall he be* that taketh and <sup>l</sup>dasheth thy little ones against the stones.
