## CHAPTER XIII.

1 Jesus washeth his disciples' feet, 14 and exhorteth them to humility and charity.

<sup>a</sup>NOW before the feast of the passover, when Jesus knew that his hour was come that he should depart out of this world unto the Father, having loved his own which were in the world, he loved them unto the end.

2 <sup>b</sup>And supper being ended, (the devil having <sup>†</sup>now put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, Simon's *son,* to betray him,)

3 <sup>c</sup>Jesus knowing that the Father had given all things into his hands, and that he was come from God, and went to God;

4 <sup>d</sup>He riseth from supper, and laid aside his garments; and took a towel, and girded himself:

5 After that, he poureth water into a bason, and began to wash his disciples' feet, and to wipe *them* with the towel wherewith he was girded.

6 <sup>e</sup>Then cometh he to Simon Peter: and Peter saith unto him, Lord, dost thou wash my feet?

7 <sup>f</sup>Jesus answered and said unto him, What I do thou knowest not now; but thou shalt know hereafter.

8 <sup>g</sup>Peter saith unto him, Thou shalt never wash my feet. Jesus answered him, If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with me.

9 Simon Peter saith unto him, Lord, not my feet only, but also *my* hands and *my* head.

10 <sup>h</sup>Jesus saith to him, He that is washed needeth not save to wash *his* feet, but is clean every whit: and ye are clean, but not all.

11 <sup>i</sup>For he knew who should betray him: therefore said he, Ye are not all clean.

12 So after he had washed their feet, and had taken his garments, and was set down again, he said unto them, Know ye what I have done to you?

13 <sup>k</sup>Ye call me Master, and Lord: and ye say well; for *so* I am.

14 <sup>l</sup>If I then, *your* Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another's feet.

15 For I have given you an example, that ye should do as I have done to you.

16 <sup>m</sup>Verily, verily, I say unto you, The servant is not greater than his lord; neither he that is sent greater than he that sent him.

17 <sup>n</sup>If ye know these things, happy are ye if ye do them.

18 ¶ <sup>o</sup>I speak not of you all; I know whom I have chosen: but that the scripture may be fulfilled, He that eateth bread with me hath lifted up his heel against me.

19 Now I tell you before it come, that when it is come to pass, ye may believe that I am *he.*

20 <sup>p</sup>Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that receiveth whomsoever I send, receiveth me; and he that receiveth me receiveth him that sent me.

21 <sup>q</sup>When Jesus had thus said, he was troubled in spirit, and testified, and said, Verily, verily, I say unto you, That one of you shall betray me.

22 Then the disciples looked one on another, doubting of whom he spake.

23 <sup>r</sup>Now there was leaning on Jesus' bosom one of his disciples, whom Jesus loved.

24 Simon Peter therefore beckoned to him, that he should ask who it should be of whom he spake.

25 He then, lying on Jesus' breast, saith unto him, Lord, who is it?

26 Jesus<sup>||</sup>answered, He it is to whom I shall give a sop when I have dipped *it.* And when he had dipped the sop, he gave *it* to Judas Iscariot, *the son* of Simon.

27 <sup>s</sup>And after the sop, Satan entered into him. Then said Jesus unto him, That thou doest, do quickly.

28 Now no man at the table knew for what intent he spake this unto him.

29 <sup>t</sup>For some *of them* thought, because Judas had the bag, that Jesus had said unto him, Buy *those things* that we have need of against the feast; or, that he should give something to the poor.

30 He then, having received the sop, went immediately out: and it was night.
