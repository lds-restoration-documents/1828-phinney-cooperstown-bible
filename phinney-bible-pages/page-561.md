## CHAPTER VII.

The judgments of grasshoppers, and of fire, are diverted by the prayer of Amos, &c.

THUS hath the Lord GOD shewed unto me; and, behold, he formed <sup>a</sup>grasshoppers in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth; and, lo, *it was* the latter growth after the king's mowings.

2 And it came to pass, *that* when they had made <sup>b</sup>an end of eating the grass of the land, then I said, <sup>c</sup>O Lord GOD, forgive, I beseech thee; by whom shall Jacob arise? for he *is* <sup>d</sup>small.

3 The LORD <sup>e</sup>repented for this: It shall not be, saith the LORD.

4 ¶ Thus hath the Lord GOD shewed unto me; and, behold, the Lord GOD called to contend by fire, and it devoured the <sup>f</sup>great deep, and did eat up a part.

5 Then said I, O Lord GOD, cease, I beseech thee; <sup>g</sup>by whom shall Jacob arise? for he *is* small.

6 The LORD repented for this: This also shall not be, saith the Lord GOD.

7 ¶ Thus he shewed me; and, behold, the LORD stood upon a wall *made* by a plumb-line, <sup>h</sup>with a plumb-line in his hand.

8 And the LORD said unto me, Amos, what seest thou? And I said, A plumb-line. Then said the LORD, Behold, I will set a plumb-line in the midst of my people Israel: I will <sup>i</sup>not again pass by them any more:

9 And the high places of Isaac shall be desolate, and the sanctuaries of Israel shall be laid waste; and I will rise against the <sup>k</sup>house of Jeroboam with the sword.

10 ¶ Then Amaziah, the <sup>l</sup>priest of Beth-el, sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, Amos hath <sup>m</sup>conspired against thee in the midst of the house of Israel; the land is not able <sup>n</sup>to bear all his words.

11 For thus Amos saith, Jeroboam shall <sup>o</sup>die by the sword, and Israel shall surely be led away captive out of their own land.

12 Also Amaziah said unto Amos, O thou seer, go, <sup>p</sup>flee thee away into the land of Judah, and there eat bread, and prophesy there:

13 But prophesy not again any more at Beth-el: for it *is* the <sup>q</sup>king's chapel, and it *is* the king's court.

14 Then answered Amos, and said to Amaziah, I *was* <sup>r</sup>no prophet, neither *was* I a prophet's son: but I *was* <sup>s</sup>a herdman, and a gatherer of <sup>t</sup>sycamore-fruit:

15 And the LORD took me <sup>u</sup>as I followed the flock, and the LORD said unto me, <sup>x</sup>Go, prophesy unto my people Israel.

16 Now, therefore, hear <sup>y</sup>thou the word of the LORD: Thou sayest, <sup>z</sup>Prophesy not against Israel, and drop not *thy word* against the house of Isaac.

17 Therefore thus saith the LORD, <sup>a</sup>Thy wife shall be a harlot in the city, and thy sons and thy daughters shall fall by the sword, and thy land shall be divided by line; and <sup>b</sup>thou shalt die in a polluted land: and Israel shall surely go into captivity forth of his land.

## CHAPTER VIII.

1 By a basket of summer-fruit is shewed Israel's end....4 Oppression of the poor reproved.

THUS hath the Lord GOD shewed unto me: and, behold, <sup>a</sup>a basket of summer-fruit.

2 And he said, Amos, what seest thou? And I said, A basket <sup>b</sup>of summer-fruit. Then said the LORD unto me, <sup>c</sup>The end is come upon my people of Israel; I will not again pass by them any more.

3 And the <sup>d</sup>songs of the temple shall be howlings in that day, saith the Lord GOD: *there shall be* <sup>e</sup>many dead bodies in every place; they shall cast *them* forth with silence.

4 ¶ Hear this, O ye that <sup>f</sup>swallow up the needy, even to make the poor of the land to fail,

5 Saying, When will the <sup>g</sup>new moon be gone, that we may sell corn? and the sabbath, that we may set forth wheat, making the ephah small, and the shekel great, and falsifying the balances by deceit?

6 That we may <sup>h</sup>buy the poor for silver, and the needy for a pair of shoes; *yea,* and sell the refuse of the wheat?

7 The LORD hath sworn by the excellency of Jacob, Surely I will never forget any of their works.

8 Shall not the <sup>i</sup>land tremble for this, and every one mourn that dwelleth therein? and it shall rise up wholly as <sup>k</sup>a flood; and it shall be cast out and drowned, as *by* the flood of Egypt.

9 And it shall come to pass <sup>l</sup>in that day, saith the Lord GOD, that I will cause the sun to go down <sup>m</sup>at noon, and I will darken the earth in the clear day:

10 And I will turn your feasts <sup>n</sup>into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation; and I will bring up sackcloth upon all loins, and baldness upon every head; and I will make it as the mourning of an only *son,* and the end thereof as a <sup>o</sup>bitter day.

11 Behold, the days come, saith the Lord GOD, that I will send a famine in the land; not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, <sup>p</sup>but of hearing the words of the LORD:

12 And they shall <sup>q</sup>wander from sea to sea, and from the north even to the east; they shall run to and fro to seek the word of the LORD, and shall <sup>r</sup>not find *it.*

13 In that day shall the <sup>s</sup>fair virgins and young men faint for thirst.

14 They that swear by the sin of Samaria, and say, Thy god, <sup>t</sup>O Dan, liveth; and, The manner of Beer-sheba liveth; even they <sup>u</sup>shall fall, and never rise up again.
