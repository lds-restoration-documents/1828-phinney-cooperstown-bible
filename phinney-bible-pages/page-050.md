21 ¶ Naphtali *is* <sup>e</sup>a hind let loose: he giveth goodly words.

22 ¶ Joseph *is* <sup>f</sup>a fruitful bough, *even* a fruitful bough by a well, *whose* branches run over the wall.

23 <sup>g</sup>The archers have sorely grieved him, and shot *at him,* and hated him:

24 But his bow abode in strength, and the arms of his hands were made strong by the <sup>h</sup>hands of the mighty *God* of Jacob; (from thence *is* the Shepherd, the stone of Israel:)

25 *Even* by the God of thy father, who shall help thee; and by the Almighty, who shall bless thee with <sup>i</sup>blessings of heaven above, blessings of the deep that lieth under, blessings of the <sup>k</sup>breasts and of the womb:

26 The blessings of thy father have prevailed <sup>l</sup>above the blessings of my progenitors, unto the <sup>m</sup>utmost bound of the <sup>n</sup>everlasting hills: they shall be on the head of Joseph, and on the crown of the head of him that was <sup>o</sup>separate from his brethren.

27 ¶ Benjamin shall <sup>p</sup>ravin *as* a wolf; in the morning he shall devour the prey, and <sup>q</sup>at night he shall divide the spoil.

28 All these *are* the <sup>r</sup>twelve tribes of Israel: and this *is it* that their father spake unto them, and blessed them: every one according to his blessing he blessed them.

29 And he charged them, and said unto them, I am to be <sup>s</sup>gathered unto my people: bury me with my fathers in the cave that *is* in the field of <sup>t</sup>Ephron the Hittite;

30 In the cave that *is* in the field of Machpelah, which *is* before Mamre, in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought with the field of Ephron the Hittite for a possession of a burying-place.

31 There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife; and there I buried Leah.

32 The purchase of the field, and of the cave that *is* therein, *was* from the children of Heth.

33 And when Jacob had made an end of commanding his sons, he gathered up his <sup>u</sup>feet into the bed, and yielded up the ghost, and was <sup>x</sup>gathered unto his people.

## CHAPTER L.

1 The mourning for Jacob....4 Joseph getteth leave of Pharaoh to go to bury him....24 He prophesieth unto his brethren of their return....25 He taketh an oath of them for his bones....26 He dieth.

AND Joseph fell upon his <sup>a</sup>father's face, and <sup>b</sup>wept upon him, and <sup>c</sup>kissed him.

2 And Joseph commanded his servants the physicians to embalm his father: and the physicians <sup>d</sup>embalmed Israel.

3 And forty days were fulfilled for him; (for so are fulfilled the days of those which are embalmed;) and the Egyptians mourned for him <sup>e</sup>threescore and ten days.

4 And when the days of his mourning were past, Joseph <sup>f</sup>spake unto the house of Pharaoh, saying, If now I have found grace in your eyes, speak, I pray you, in the ears of Pharaoh, saying,

5 My father made me swear, saying, Lo, <sup>g</sup>I die: in my grave which I have <sup>h</sup>digged for me in the land of Canaan, there shalt thou <sup>i</sup>bury me. Now, therefore, let me go up, I pray thee, and bury my father, and I will come again.

6 And Pharaoh said, Go up and bury thy father, according <sup>k</sup>as he made thee swear.

7 And Joseph went up to bury his father: and with him went up all the servants of Pharaoh, the elders of his house, and all the elders of the land of Egypt,

8 And all the house of Joseph, and his brethren, and his father's house: only their little ones, and their flocks, and their herds, they left in the land of Goshen.

9 And there went up with him both chariots and horsemen; and it was <sup>l</sup>a very great company.

10 And they came to the threshing-floor of <sup>||</sup>Atad, which *is* <sup>m</sup>beyond Jordan; and there they mourned with a great and very sore lamentation: and he made a mourning for his father <sup>n</sup>seven days.

11 And when the inhabitants of the land, the <sup>o</sup>Canaanites, saw the mourning in the floor of Atad, they said, This *is* a grievous mourning to the Egyptians: wherefore the name of it was called <sup>||</sup>Abel-mizraim, which *is* beyond Jordan.

12 And his sons did unto him <sup>p</sup>according as he commanded them:

13 For his sons carried him into the land of Canaan, and buried him in the cave of <sup>q</sup>the field of Machpelah, which Abraham bought with the field, for a possession of a burying-place, of Ephron the Hittite, before Mamre.

14 And Joseph returned into Egypt, he, and his brethren, and all that went up with him to bury his father, after he had buried his father.

15 And when Joseph's brethren saw that their father was dead, they said, Joseph will <sup>r</sup>peradventure hate us, and will <sup>s</sup>certainly requite us all the evil which we did unto him.

16 And they <sup>†</sup>sent a messenger unto Joseph, saying, Thy father did command before he died, saying,

17 So shall ye say unto Joseph, <sup>t</sup>Forgive, I pray thee now, the trespass of <sup>u</sup>thy brethren, and their sin; for they did unto thee evil: and now, we pray thee, forgive the trespass of the servants of <sup>x</sup>the God of thy father. And Joseph wept when they spake unto him.

18 And his brethren also went and <sup>y</sup>fell down before his face; and they said, Behold, we *be* thy servants.

19 And Joseph said unto them, <sup>z</sup>Fear not; for *am* I in <sup>a</sup>the place of God?

20 But as for you, ye thought evil against me; <sup>b</sup>*but* God meant it unto good, to bring to pass, as *it is* this day, to save much people alive.

21 Now, therefore, fear ye not: <sup>c</sup>I will nourish you, and your little ones. And he comforted them, and spake <sup>†</sup>kindly unto them.

22 ¶ And Joseph dwelt in Egypt, he and his father's house: and Joseph lived a hundred and ten years:

23 And Joseph saw Ephraim's children of the <sup>d</sup>third *generation:* the children also of Machir, the son of Manasseh, were brought up upon Joseph's knees.

24 And Joseph said unto his brethren, I die: and God will surely visit you, and bring you out of this land unto the land which he sware to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.

25 And Joseph took an oath of the children of Israel, saying, God will surely visit you, and ye shall carry up <sup>e</sup>my bones from hence.

26 So Joseph died, *being* a hundred and ten years old: and they embalmed him, and he was put in a coffin in Egypt.
