## CHAPTER XXXV.

The judgment of mount Seir for their hatred of Israel.

MOREOVER, the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

2 Son of man, <sup>a</sup>set thy face against <sup>b</sup>mount Seir, and <sup>c</sup>prophesy against it,

3 And say unto it, Thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, O mount Seir, I *am* <sup>d</sup>against thee, and I will stretch out my hand against thee, and I will make thee most desolate.

4 I will lay thy cities waste, and thou shalt be desolate; and thou shalt <sup>e</sup>know that I *am* the LORD.

5 Because thou hast had <sup>f</sup>a perpetual hatred, and hast <sup>g</sup>shed *the blood of* the children of Israel by the force of the sword in the time of their calamity, in the time *that their* iniquity *had* an end:

6 Therefore, *as* I live, saith the Lord GOD, I will <sup>h</sup>prepare thee unto blood, and blood shall pursue thee: sith thou hast <sup>i</sup>not hated blood, even blood shall pursue thee.

7 Thus will I make mount Seir most desolate, and cut off from it him that <sup>k</sup>passeth out, and him that returneth.

8 And I will <sup>l</sup>fill his mountains with his slain *men:* in thy hills, and in thy valleys, and in all thy rivers, shall they fall that are slain with the sword.

9 I will make thee <sup>m</sup>perpetual desolations, and thy cities shall <sup>n</sup>not return; and ye shall know that I *am* the LORD.

10 Because thou hast said, <sup>o</sup>These two nations, and these two countries, shall be mine, and we will possess it; whereas <sup>p</sup>the LORD was there:

11 Therefore, *as* I live, saith the Lord GOD, I will even do <sup>q</sup>according to thine anger, and according to thine envy, which thou hast used out of thy hatred against them; and I will make myself known <sup>r</sup>among them, when I have judged thee.

12 And thou shalt know that I *am* the LORD, *and that* I have <sup>s</sup>heard all thy blasphemies which thou hast spoken against the mountains of Israel, saying, They are laid desolate, they are given us to consume.

13 Thus with your mouth ye have <sup>t</sup>boasted against me, and have multiplied your words against me: I have heard *them.*

14 Thus saith the Lord GOD, When the whole earth <sup>u</sup>rejoiceth, I will make thee desolate.

15 <sup>x</sup>As thou didst rejoice at the inheritance of the house of Israel, because it was desolate, so will I do unto thee: thou shalt be desolate, O mount Seir, and all Idumea, *even* all of it; and they shall know that I *am* the LORD.

## CHAPTER XXXVI.

1 The land of Israel is comforted, &c....25 The blessings of Christ's kingdom.

ALSO thou son of man, prophesy unto the <sup>a</sup>mountains of Israel, and say, Ye mountains of Israel, hear the word of the LORD:

2 Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because the enemy hath said against you, Aha, even the <sup>b</sup>ancient high places <sup>c</sup>are ours in possession:

3 Therefore prophesy and say, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because they have made *you* desolate, and <sup>d</sup>swallowed you up on every side, that ye might be a possession unto the residue of the heathen, and ye are taken up in the <sup>e</sup>lips of talkers, and *are* an infamy of the people:

4 Therefore, ye mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD; Thus saith the Lord GOD to the mountains, and to the hills, to the rivers, and to the valleys, to the desolate wastes, and to the cities that are forsaken, which became a prey and derision to the residue of the heathen that *are* round about;

5 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; Surely in the <sup>f</sup>fire of my jealousy have I spoken against the residue of the heathen, and against all Idumea, which have appointed <sup>g</sup>my land into their possession with the joy of all *their* heart, with despiteful minds, to cast it out for a prey.

6 Prophesy therefore concerning the land of Israel, and say unto the mountains, and to the hills, to the rivers, and to the valleys, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I have spoken in my jealousy and in my fury, because ye have borne the <sup>h</sup>shame of the heathen:

7 Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; I have lifted up my hand, Surely the heathen that *are* about you, they shall <sup>i</sup>bear their shame.

8 ¶ But ye, O mountains of Israel, ye shall <sup>k</sup>shoot forth your branches, and yield your fruit to my people of Israel; for they are at hand to come.

9 For behold, I *am* for you, and I will turn unto you, and ye shall <sup>l</sup>be tilled and sown:

10 And I will <sup>m</sup>multiply men upon you, <sup>n</sup>all the house of Israel, *even* all of it: and the cities shall be inhabited, and the wastes shall be builded:

11 And I will multiply upon you man and beast; and they shall increase and bring fruit: and I will <sup>o</sup>settle you after your old estates, and will <sup>p</sup>do better *unto you* than at your beginnings: and ye shall know that I *am* the LORD.

12 Yea, I will cause <sup>q</sup>men to walk upon you, *even* my people Israel; and they shall possess thee, and thou shalt be their inheritance, and thou shalt <sup>r</sup>no more henceforth bereave them *of men.*

13 Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because they say unto you, Thou *land* <sup>s</sup>devourest up men, and hast bereaved thy nations;

14 Therefore thou shalt devour men no more, neither <sup>t</sup>bereave thy nations any more, saith the Lord GOD.

15 Neither will I cause *men* to hear in thee the shame of the heathen any more, neither shalt thou bear <sup>u</sup>the reproach of the people any more, neither shalt thou cause thy nations to fall any more, saith the Lord GOD.

16 ¶ Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

17 Son of man, when the house of Israel dwelt in their own land, they <sup>x</sup>defiled it by their own way and by their doings: their way was before me <sup>y</sup>as the uncleanness of a removed woman.

18 Wherefore I <sup>z</sup>poured my fury upon them for the <sup>a</sup>blood that they had shed upon the land, and for their <sup>†</sup>idols *wherewith* they had polluted it:

19 And <sup>b</sup>I scattered them among the heathen, and they were dispersed through the countries: according to their way and according to their doings I judged them.

20 And when they entered unto the heathen, whither they went, they profaned <sup>c</sup>my holy name, when they said to them, <sup>d</sup>These *are* the people of the LORD, and are gone forth out of his land.
