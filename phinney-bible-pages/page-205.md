14 And Samuel said, What *meaneth* then this bleating of the sheep in mine ears, and the lowing of the oxen which I hear?

15 And Saul said, They have brought them from the Amalekites: for the <sup>c</sup>people spared the best of the sheep and of the oxen, to <sup>d</sup>sacrifice unto the LORD thy God; and the rest we have utterly destroyed.

16 Then Samuel said unto Saul, Stay, and I will tell thee what the LORD hath said to me this night. And he said unto him, Say on.

17 And Samuel said, When thou *wast* <sup>e</sup>little in thine own sight, *wast* thou not *made* the head of the tribes of Israel, and the LORD anointed thee king over Israel?

18 And the LORD sent thee on a journey, and said, Go, and utterly destroy the <sup>f</sup>sinners the Amalekites, and fight against them until they be consumed.

19 Wherefore then didst thou not obey the voice of the LORD, but didst <sup>g</sup>fly upon the spoil, and didst evil in the sight of the LORD?

20 And Saul said unto Samuel, Yea, I have obeyed the voice of the LORD, and have gone the way which the LORD sent me, and have brought <sup>h</sup>Agag the king of Amalek, and have utterly destroyed the Amalekites.

21 But the <sup>i</sup>people took of the spoil, sheep and oxen, the chief of the things which should have been utterly destroyed, to sacrifice unto the LORD thy God in Gilgal.

22 And Samuel said, Hath the LORD *as great* <sup>k</sup>delight in burnt-offerings and sacrifices as in obeying the voice of the LORD? Behold, <sup>l</sup>to obey *is* better than sacrifice, *and* to hearken, than the fat of rams.

23 For <sup>m</sup>rebellion *is as* <sup>n</sup>the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness *is as* iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the LORD, he hath also rejected thee from *being* king.

24 And Saul said unto Samuel, I have sinned: for I have transgressed the commandment of the LORD, and thy words; because <sup>o</sup>I feared the people, and obeyed their voice.

25 Now therefore, I pray thee, pardon my sin, and turn again with me, that I may worship the LORD.

26 And Samuel said unto Saul, <sup>p</sup>I will not return with thee: for <sup>q</sup>thou hast rejected the word of the LORD, and the LORD hath rejected thee from being king over Israel.

27 And as Samuel turned about to go away, he laid hold upon the skirt of his mantle, and it rent.

28 And Samuel said unto him, The LORD hath <sup>r</sup>rent the kingdom of Israel from thee this day, and hath given it to a neighbour of thine, *that is* better than thou.

29 And also the <sup>||</sup>Strength of Israel will not <sup>s</sup>lie, nor repent: for he *is* not <sup>t</sup>a man, that he should repent.

30 Then he said, I have sinned; *yet* <sup>u</sup>honour me now, I pray thee, before the elders of my people, and before Israel, and turn again with me, that I may worship the LORD thy God.

31 So Samuel turned again after Saul: and Saul worshipped the LORD.

32 Then said Samuel, Bring you hither to me Agag the king of the Amalekites: and Agag came unto him delicately. And Agag said, Surely the <sup>x</sup>bitterness of death is past.

33 And Samuel said, <sup>y</sup>As thy sword hath made women childless, so shall thy mother be childless among women. And <sup>z</sup>Samuel hewed Agag in pieces before the LORD in Gilgal.

34 ¶ Then Samuel went to Ramah; and Saul went up to his house to Gibeah of Saul.

35 And Samuel came no more <sup>a</sup>to see Saul until the day of his death: nevertheless Samuel mourned for Saul: and the LORD repented that he had made Saul king over Israel.

## CHAPTER XVI.

Samuel anointeth David.

AND the LORD said unto Samuel, How long wilt thou <sup>a</sup>mourn for Saul, seeing I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? fill thine <sup>b</sup>horn with oil, and go; I will send thee to Jesse the Beth-lehemite: for I have <sup>c</sup>provided me a king among his sons.

2 And Samuel said, <sup>d</sup>How can I go? if Saul hear *it,* he will kill me. And the LORD said, Take a heifer with thee, and say, I am come to <sup>e</sup>sacrifice to the LORD.

3 And call Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will shew thee what thou shalt do: and thou shalt anoint unto me *him* whom I name unto thee.

4 And Samuel did that which the LORD spake, and came to Beth-lehem: and the elders of the town <sup>f</sup>trembled at his coming, and said, <sup>†</sup>Comest thou peaceably?

5 And he said, Peaceably: I am come to sacrifice unto the LORD; <sup>g</sup>sanctify yourselves, and come with me to the sacrifice. And he sanctified Jesse and his sons, and called them to the sacrifice.

6 And it came to pass, when they were come, that he looked on Eliab, and <sup>h</sup>said, Surely the <sup>i</sup>LORD'S anointed *is* before him.

7 But the LORD <sup>k</sup>said unto Samuel, Look not on his <sup>l</sup>countenance, or on the height of his stature; because I have <sup>m</sup>refused him: for *the LORD seeth* not as man seeth; for man looketh on the <sup>n</sup>outward appearance, but the LORD looketh on the <sup>o</sup>heart.

8 Then Jesse called Abinadab, and made him pass before Samuel. And he said, Neither hath the LORD chosen this.

9 Then Jesse made <sup>p</sup>Shammah to pass by. And he said, Neither hath the LORD chosen this.

10 Again, Jesse made <sup>q</sup>seven of his sons to pass before Samuel. And Samuel said unto Jesse, The LORD hath not chosen these.

11 And Samuel said unto Jesse, Are here all *thy* children? And he said, There remaineth yet the youngest, and, behold, he keepeth the sheep. And Samuel said unto Jesse, <sup>r</sup>Send and fetch him: for we will not sit down till he come hither.

12 And he sent and brought him in. Now he *was* <sup>s</sup>ruddy, *and* withal of a beautiful countenance, and goodly to look to. And the LORD said, Arise, <sup>t</sup>anoint him: for this *is* he.

13 <sup>u</sup>Then Samuel took the horn of oil, and anointed him in the midst of his brethren: and the <sup>x</sup>Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day forward. So Samuel rose up, and went to Ramah.

14 ¶ But the Spirit of the LORD <sup>y</sup>departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the LORD troubled him.

15 And Saul's servants said unto him, Behold now, an <sup>z</sup>evil spirit <sup>a</sup>from God troubleth thee.

16 Let our lord now command thy servants *which are* <sup>b</sup>before thee, to seek out a man *who is* a cunning player on a harp: and it shall come to pass, when the evil spirit from God is upon thee, that he shall play with his hand, and thou shalt be <sup>c</sup>well.

17 And Saul said unto his servants, Provide me now a man that can play well, and bring *him* to me.
