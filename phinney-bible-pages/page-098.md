23 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

24 Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, In the <sup>m</sup>seventh month, in the first *day* of the month, shall ye have a sabbath, a memorial of <sup>n</sup>blowing of trumpets, a holy convocation.

25 Ye shall do no servile work *therein*; but ye shall offer an offering made by fire unto the LORD.

26 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

27 Also on the tenth *day* of this seventh month *there shall be* a day of <sup>o</sup>atonement: it shall be a holy convocation unto you; and ye shall <sup>p</sup>afflict your souls, and offer an <sup>q</sup>offering made by fire unto the LORD.

28 And ye shall do no work in that same day; for it *is* a day of atonement, to make an atonement for you before the LORD your God.

29 For whatsoever soul *it be* that shall not be afflicted in that same day, he shall be <sup>r</sup>cut off from among his people.

30 And whatsoever soul *it be* that doeth any work in that same day, the same soul will I destroy from among his people.

31 Ye shall do no manner of work: *it shall be* a statute for ever throughout your generations, in all your dwellings.

32 It *shall be* unto you a sabbath of rest, and ye shall afflict your souls: in the ninth *day* of the month at even, from even unto even, shall ye celebrate your sabbath.

33 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

34 Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, The fifteenth day of this seventh month *shall be* the feast <sup>s</sup>of tabernacles *for* <sup>t</sup>seven days unto the LORD.

35 On the first day *shall be* <sup>u</sup>a holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work *therein*.

36 Seven days ye shall offer <sup>x</sup>an offering made by fire unto the LORD: on the <sup>y</sup>eighth day shall be a holy convocation unto you, and ye shall offer an offering made by fire unto the LORD: it *is* <sup>†</sup>a solemn assembly; *and* ye shall do no servile work *therein*.

37 <sup>z</sup>These *are* the feasts of the LORD, which ye shall proclaim *to be* holy convocations, to offer an offering made by fire unto the LORD, a burnt-offering, and a meat-offering, <sup>a</sup>a sacrifice, and drink-offerings, every thing upon his day:

38 Besides the sabbaths of the LORD, and besides your <sup>b</sup>gifts, and besides all your <sup>c</sup>vows, and besides all your free-will-offerings, which ye give unto the LORD.

39 Also in the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when ye have gathered in the <sup>d</sup>fruit of the land, ye shall keep a feast unto the LORD seven days: on the first day *shall be* a sabbath, and on the eighth day *shall be* a sabbath.

40 And ye shall take you on the first day the <sup>e</sup>boughs of <sup>f</sup>goodly trees, <sup>g</sup>branches of palm-trees, and the <sup>h</sup>boughs of thick trees, <sup>i</sup>and willows of the brook; and <sup>k</sup>ye shall rejoice before the LORD your God seven days.

41 And ye shall keep it a feast unto the LORD seven days in the year: *it shall be* a statute for ever in your generations; ye shall celebrate it in the seventh month.

42 Ye shall <sup>l</sup>dwell in booths seven days: all that are Israelites born shall dwell in booths;

43 That your generations <sup>m</sup>may know that I made the children of Israel to dwell in booths, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt: I *am* the LORD your God.

44 And Moses declared unto the children of Israel the feasts of the LORD.

## CHAPTER XXIV.

13 The law of blasphemy....17 Of murder.

AND the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2 Command the children of Israel, that they <sup>a</sup>bring unto thee pure oil-olive, beaten, for the light, to cause <sup>b</sup>the lamps to burn <sup>c</sup>continually.

3 <sup>d</sup>Without the vail of the <sup>e</sup>testimony, in the tabernacle of the congregation, shall <sup>f</sup>Aaron order it from the evening unto the morning, before the LORD continually: *it shall be* a statute for ever in your generations.

4 He shall order the lamps upon the <sup>g</sup>pure candlestick before the LORD continually.

5 And thou shalt take fine flour, and bake <sup>h</sup>twelve cakes thereof: two tenth deals shall be in one cake.

6 And thou shalt set them in two rows, six on a row, upon the <sup>i</sup>pure table before the LORD.

7 And thou shalt put pure frankincense upon *each* row, that it may be on the bread for <sup>k</sup>a memorial, *even* an offering <sup>l</sup>made by fire unto the LORD.

8 <sup>m</sup>Every sabbath he shall set it in order before the LORD continually, *being taken* <sup>n</sup>from the children of Israel by an <sup>o</sup>everlasting covenant.

9 And it shall be <sup>p</sup>Aaron's and his sons'; and they shall eat it in the holy place: for it *is* most holy unto him of the offerings of the LORD made by fire, by a perpetual statute.

10 ¶ And the son of an Israelitish woman, whose father *was* an Egyptian, went out among the children of Israel; and this son of the Israelitish *woman* and a man of Israel strove together in the camp;

11 And the Israelitish woman's son <sup>q</sup>blasphemed the name <sup>r</sup>*of the LORD*, and cursed. And they <sup>s</sup>brought him unto Moses; (and his mother's name *was* Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan;)

12 And they put him in ward, <sup>†</sup>that the mind of the LORD might be shewed them.

13 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

14 Bring forth him that hath cursed <sup>t</sup>without the camp; and let all that heard *him* <sup>u</sup>lay their hands upon his head, and let all the congregation stone him.

15 And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, Whosoever curseth his God shall <sup>x</sup>bear his sin.

16 And he that blasphemeth the <sup>y</sup>name of the LORD, he shall surely be put to death, *and* all the congregation shall certainly stone him; as well the stranger, as he that is born in the land, when he blasphemeth the name *of the LORD*, shall be put to death.

17 ¶ And he that <sup>†</sup>killeth any man shall surely be put to death.

18 And he that killeth a beast shall make it good; beast for beast.

19 And if a man cause a blemish in his neighbour; as he hath done, <sup>z</sup>so shall it be done to him;

20 <sup>a</sup>Breach for breach, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he hath caused a blemish in a man, so shall it be done to him *again*.

21 And he that killeth a beast, he shall restore it: and he that killeth a man, he shall be put to death.

22 Ye shall have one manner of law, as well for the stranger as for one of your own country: for I *am* the LORD your God.
