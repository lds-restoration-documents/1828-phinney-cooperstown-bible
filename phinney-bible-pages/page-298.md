# ¶ The SECOND Book of the CHRONICLES.

## CHAPTER I.

Solomon's choice of wisdom is blessed by God.

AND Solomon the son of David <sup>a</sup>was strengthened in his kingdom, and the LORD his God *was* <sup>b</sup>with him, <sup>c</sup>and magnified him exceedingly.

2 Then Solomon <sup>d</sup>spake unto all Israel, to the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and to the judges, and to every governor in all Israel, the chief of the fathers.

3 So Solomon, and all the congregation with him, went to the <sup>e</sup>high place that *was* at Gibeon; for there was the <sup>f</sup>tabernacle of the congregation of God, which Moses, the servant of the LORD, had made in the wilderness.

4 But the ark of God had David brought up from <sup>g</sup>Kirjath-jearim to *the place which* David had prepared for it: for he had pitched a tent for it at Jerusalem.

5 Moreover, the <sup>h</sup>brazen altar that Bezaleel, the son of Uri, the son of Hur, had made, he put before the tabernacle of the LORD; and Solomon and the congregation <sup>i</sup>sought unto it.

6 And Solomon went up thither to the brazen altar <sup>k</sup>before the LORD, which *was* at the tabernacle of the congregation, and offered a thousand burnt-offerings upon it.

7 In that <sup>l</sup>night did God appear unto Solomon, and said unto him, Ask what I shall give thee.

8 And Solomon said unto God, Thou hast shewed great mercy unto David my father, and hast made me to reign <sup>m</sup>in his stead.

9 Now, O LORD God, let thy promise unto David my father be established: for thou hast made me king over a people like the dust of the earth in <sup>n</sup>multitude.

10 <sup>o</sup>Give me now wisdom and knowledge, that I may <sup>p</sup>go out and come in before this people: for who can <sup>q</sup>judge this thy people *that is so* great?

11 And God said to Solomon, Because this was <sup>r</sup>in thy heart, and thou hast not asked riches, wealth, or honour, nor the life of thine enemies, neither yet hast asked long life; but hast asked wisdom and knowledge for thyself, that thou mayest judge my people, over whom I have made thee king:

12 <sup>s</sup>Wisdom and knowledge *is* granted unto thee; and I will give thee riches, and wealth, and honour, such as <sup>t</sup>none of the kings have had that *have been* before thee, neither shall there any after thee have the like.

13 ¶ Then Solomon came *from his journey* to the high place that *was* at Gibeon to Jerusalem, from before the tabernacle of the congregation, and <sup>u</sup>reigned over Israel.

14 And Solomon <sup>x</sup>gathered chariots and horsemen, and he had a thousand and four hundred chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen, which he placed in the chariot-cities, and with the king at Jerusalem.

15 And the <sup>y</sup>king made silver and gold at Jerusalem *as plenteous* as stones, and cedar-trees made he as the sycamore-trees that *are* in the vale for abundance.

16 And Solomon had <sup>z</sup>horses brought out of Egypt, and linen yarn; the king's merchants received the linen yarn at a price.

17 And they fetched up, and brought forth out of Egypt, a chariot for six hundred *shekels* of silver, and a horse for a hundred and fifty: and so brought they out *horses* for all the kings of the Hittites, and for the kings of Syria, <sup>†</sup>by their means.
