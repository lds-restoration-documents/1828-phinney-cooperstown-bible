20 For *there is* not a just man upon earth, that <sup>g</sup>doeth good, and sinneth not.

21 Also take no heed unto all words that are spoken, lest thou hear thy servant <sup>h</sup>curse thee:

22 For oftentimes also thine own heart <sup>i</sup>knoweth that <sup>k</sup>thou thyself likewise hast cursed others.

23 All this have I <sup>l</sup>proved by wisdom: I said I will be <sup>m</sup>wise; but it *was* far from me.

24 That which is <sup>n</sup>far off, and exceeding <sup>o</sup>deep, who can find it out?

25 I applied my heart to know, and to search, and to seek out wisdom, and the reason *of things,* and to know the wickedness of folly, even of foolishness *and* madness:

26 And <sup>p</sup>I find more bitter than death the woman whose heart <sup>q</sup>*is* snares and nets, *and* her hands *as* bands: whoso <sup>r</sup>pleaseth God shall escape from her; but the sinner shall be taken by her.

27 Behold, this have I found, (saith the Preacher,) *counting* one by one, to find out the account;

28 Which yet my soul seeketh, but I find not: <sup>s</sup>one man among a thousand have I found; but <sup>t</sup>a woman among all those have I not found.

29 Lo, this only have I found, that God hath made man <sup>u</sup>upright; but they have sought out many <sup>x</sup>inventions.

## CHAPTER VIII.

It is better with the godly in adversity, than with the wicked in prosperity.

WHO *is* as the <sup>a</sup>wise *man?* and who knoweth the interpretation of a thing? A man's wisdom maketh <sup>b</sup>his face to shine, and the boldness of his face shall be changed.

2 I *counsel thee* to keep the king's commandment, and *that* in regard of the <sup>c</sup>oath of God.

3 <sup>d</sup>Be not hasty to go out of his sight: stand not in an evil thing; for he doeth <sup>e</sup>whatsoever pleaseth him.

4 Where the word of a king *is, there is* power; and who may say unto him, <sup>f</sup>What doest thou?

5 Whoso <sup>g</sup>keepeth the commandment shall feel no evil thing: and a wise man's heart discerneth both <sup>h</sup>time and judgment.

6 Because to every purpose there is time and judgment; therefore the <sup>i</sup>misery of man *is* great upon him.

7 For he <sup>k</sup>knoweth not that which shall be: for who can tell him when it shall be?

8 *There is* no man that hath power over the spirit, to retain the spirit; neither *hath he* power in the <sup>l</sup>day of death; and *there is* no discharge in *that* war; neither shall wickedness deliver those that are given to it.

9 All this have I seen, and applied my heart unto every work that is done under the sun: *there is* a time wherein one man ruleth over another to his <sup>m</sup>own hurt.

10 And so I saw the wicked <sup>n</sup>buried, who had come and gone from the <sup>o</sup>place of the holy, and they were <sup>p</sup>forgotten in the city where they had so done. This *is* also vanity.

11 Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore <sup>q</sup>the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil.

12 Though a sinner do evil a hundred <sup>r</sup>times, and his *days* be prolonged, yet surely I know that it shall be <sup>s</sup>well with them that fear God, which fear before him:

13 But it shall <sup>t</sup>not be well with the wicked, neither shall he prolong *his* days, *which are* as a shadow; because he feareth not before God.

14 There is a vanity which is done upon the earth; that there be just *men,* unto whom it happeneth according to the <sup>u</sup>work of the wicked: again, there be wicked *men,* to whom it happeneth according to the <sup>x</sup>work of the righteous. I <!-- uncertain: printed as 1 --> said, that this also *is* vanity.

15 Then I commended mirth, because a man hath no better thing under the sun, than <sup>y</sup>to eat, and to drink, and to be merry; for that shall abide with him of his labour the days of his life, which God giveth him under the sun.

16 When I applied mine heart to know wisdom, and to see the business that is done upon the earth: (for also *there is that* neither <sup>z</sup>day nor night seeth sleep with his eyes:)

17 Then I beheld <sup>a</sup>all the work of God, that a man cannot find out the work that is done under the sun; because though a man labour to seek *it* out, yet he shall <sup>b</sup>not find *it;* yea, further, though a wise *man* think to know *it,* yet shall he not be able to find *it.*

## CHAPTER IX.

Like things happen to good and bad.

FOR all this I <!-- uncertain: printed as 1 --> considered in my heart, even <sup>a</sup>to declare all this, that the righteous, and the wise and their works, *are* in the hand of God: no man <sup>b</sup>knoweth either love or hatred *by* all *that is* before them.

2 All *things* come alike to all: *there is* <sup>c</sup>one event to the righteous and to the wicked; to the good, and to the clean, and to the unclean; to him that sacrificeth, and to him that sacrificeth not: as *is* the good, so *is* the sinner; *and* he that sweareth, as *he* that feareth an oath.

3 This *is* an evil among all *things* that are done under the sun, that *there is* one event unto all: yea also, the heart of the sons of men is full of evil, and <sup>†</sup>madness *is* in their heart while they live, and after that *they go* to the dead.

4 For to him that is joined to all the living there is hope: for <sup>d</sup>a living dog is better than a dead lion.

5 For the living <sup>e</sup>know that they shall die: but the dead <sup>f</sup>know not any thing, neither have they any more a <sup>g</sup>reward; for the memory of them is forgotten.

6 Also their love and their hatred, and their envy, is now perished; neither have they any more <sup>h</sup>a portion for ever in any *thing* that is done under the sun.

7 <sup>i</sup>Go thy way, <sup>k</sup>eat thy bread with joy, and drink thy wine with a merry heart; for God now accepteth thy works.

8 Let thy <sup>l</sup>garments be always white; and let thy head lack no ointment.

9 Live joyfully with the wife whom thou lovest all the days of the <sup>m</sup>life of thy vanity, which he hath given thee under the sun, all the days of thy vanity: for that *is* thy portion in *this* life, and in thy labour which thou takest under the sun.

10 Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do *it* with <sup>n</sup>thy might; for *there is* no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave whither thou goest.

11 I returned, and saw under the sun, that the <sup>o</sup>race *is* not to the swift, nor the <sup>p</sup>battle to the strong, neither yet bread to the wise, nor yet riches to men of understanding, nor <sup>q</sup>yet favour to men of skill; but time and chance happeneth to them all.
