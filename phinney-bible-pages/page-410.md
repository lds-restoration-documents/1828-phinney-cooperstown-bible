## CHAPTER V.

Solomon exhorteth to the study of wisdom.

MY son, attend unto my wisdom, *and* <sup>a</sup>bow thine ear to my understanding;

2 That thou mayest regard discretion, and *that* <sup>b</sup>thy lips may keep knowledge.

3 For the lips of a strange woman <sup>c</sup>drop *as* a honey-comb, and her mouth *is* smoother than oil:

4 But her end is <sup>d</sup>bitter as wormwood, sharp as a two-edged sword.

5 Her feet go down <sup>e</sup>to death, her steps take hold on hell.

6 Lest thou shouldest <sup>f</sup>ponder the path of life, her ways are moveable, *that* thou canst <sup>g</sup>not know *them.*

7 <sup>h</sup>Hear me now, therefore, O ye children, and depart not from the words of my mouth.

8 <sup>i</sup>Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the door of her house;

9 Lest thou give thine <sup>k</sup>honour unto others, and thy years unto the <sup>l</sup>cruel:

10 Lest <sup>m</sup>strangers be filled with thy wealth, and thy labours *be* in the house of a stranger;

11 And thou mourn at the last, when thy <sup>n</sup>flesh and thy body are consumed,

12 And say, <sup>o</sup>How have I hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof;

13 And have not obeyed the voice of my teachers, nor inclined mine ear to them that instructed me!

14 I was almost in all evil in the midst of the <sup>p</sup>congregation and assembly.

15 ¶ <sup>q</sup>Drink waters out of thine own cistern, and running waters out of thine own well.

16 Let thy fountains be dispersed abroad, *and* rivers of waters in the streets.

17 Let them be <sup>r</sup>only thine own, and not strangers' with thee.

18 Let thy fountain be <sup>s</sup>blessed; and rejoice with the <sup>t</sup>wife of thy youth.

19 *Let her be as* the loving hind and pleasant <sup>u</sup>roe; let her <sup>x</sup>breasts <sup>y</sup>satisfy thee at all times, and be thou ravished always with her love.

20 And why wilt thou, my son, be ravished with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a stranger?

21 For the ways of man *are* before <sup>z</sup>the eyes of the LORD, and he pondereth all his goings.

22 His own iniquities shall <sup>a</sup>take the wicked himself, and he shall be holden with the cords of his sins.

23 He shall die <sup>b</sup>without instruction; and in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray.

## CHAPTER VI.

Against suretiship, idleness, and mischievousness.

MY son, if thou be <sup>a</sup>surety for thy friend, *if* thou hast <sup>b</sup>stricken thy hand with a stranger,

2 Thou art snared with the words of thy mouth, thou art taken with the words of thy mouth.

3 Do this now, my son, and deliver thyself, when thou art come into the hand of thy friend; go, humble thyself, and make sure thy friend.

4 <sup>c</sup>Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids.

5 Deliver thyself as a roe from the hand *of the hunter,* and as a bird from the hand of the fowler.

6 ¶ <sup>d</sup>Go to the ant, thou sluggard; <sup>e</sup>consider her ways, and be wise:

7 Which having no guide, overseer, or ruler,

8 <sup>f</sup>Provideth her meat in the summer, *and* gathereth her food in the harvest.

9 How long wilt thou <sup>g</sup>sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou <sup>h</sup>arise out of thy sleep?

10 *Yet* a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep;

11 So shall thy <sup>i</sup>poverty come as one that travelleth, and thy want as <sup>k</sup>an armed man.

12 ¶ <sup>l</sup>A naughty person, a wicked man, walketh with <sup>m</sup>a froward mouth.

13 He <sup>n</sup>winketh with his eyes, he <sup>o</sup>speaketh with his feet, he <sup>p</sup>teacheth with his fingers;

14 Frowardness *is* in his heart, he <sup>q</sup>deviseth mischief continually; he soweth discord.

15 Therefore shall his calamity come suddenly; suddenly shall he be broken <sup>r</sup>without remedy.

16 ¶ These six *things* doth the LORD hate; yea, seven *are* an abomination unto him:

17 <sup>s</sup>A proud look, <sup>t</sup>a lying tongue, and <sup>u</sup>hands that shed innocent blood,

18 A heart that deviseth <sup>x</sup>wicked imaginations, <sup>y</sup>feet that be swift in running to mischief,

19 A <sup>z</sup>false witness *that* speaketh lies, and him that <sup>a</sup>soweth discord among brethren.

20 ¶ My son, <sup>b</sup>keep thy father's commandment, and forsake not the law of thy mother:

21 <sup>c</sup>Bind them continually upon thy heart, *and* tie them about thy neck.

22 When thou <sup>d</sup>goest, it shall lead thee; when thou sleepest, it shall keep thee; and *when* thou awakest, it shall talk with thee.

23 For the commandment *is* <sup>e</sup>a lamp, and the law *is* light; and <sup>f</sup>reproofs of instruction *are* the way of life:

24 To keep thee <sup>g</sup>from the evil woman, from the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman.

25 <sup>h</sup>Lust not after her beauty in thy heart; neither let her take thee with her eyelids;

26 For by means of a whorish woman *a man is brought* to <sup>i</sup>a piece of bread; and the <sup>k</sup>adulteress will <sup>l</sup>hunt for the precious life.

27 Can a man take fire in <sup>m</sup>his bosom, and his clothes not be burned?

28 Can one go upon hot coals, and his feet not be burnt?

29 So he that goeth in to his neighbour's wife: whosoever <sup>n</sup>toucheth her shall not be <sup>o</sup>innocent.

30 *Men* do not despise a thief, if he steal to <sup>p</sup>satisfy his soul when he is hungry:

31 But *if* he be found, he shall restore <sup>q</sup>seven-fold; he shall give all the substance of his house.

32 *But* whoso committeth adultery with a woman lacketh <sup>r</sup>understanding: he *that* doeth it destroyeth his own soul.

33 <sup>s</sup>A wound and dishonour shall he get, and his reproach shall not be wiped away.

34 For jealousy *is* the rage of a man; therefore he will not spare in the <sup>t</sup>day of vengeance.
