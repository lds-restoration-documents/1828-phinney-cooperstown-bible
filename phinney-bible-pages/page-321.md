## CHAPTER XXXVI.

Jehoahaz succeeding, is deposed by Pharaoh.

THEN the people of the land took <sup>a</sup>Jehoahaz the son of Josiah, and made him <sup>b</sup>king in his father's stead in Jerusalem.

2 Jehoahaz *was* twenty and three years old when he began to reign; and he reigned three months in Jerusalem.

3 And the king of Egypt <sup>c</sup>put him down at Jerusalem, and condemned the land in a hundred talents of silver, and a talent of gold.

4 And the king of Egypt made Eliakim his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem, and turned his name to Jehoiakim. And Necho took Jehoahaz his brother, and carried him <sup>d</sup>to Egypt.

5 ¶ Jehoiakim *was* <sup>e</sup>twenty and five years old when he began to reign; and he reigned <sup>f</sup>eleven years in Jerusalem: and he did *that which was* evil in the sight of the LORD his God.

6 Against him came up <sup>g</sup>Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and bound him in fetters, to carry him <sup>h</sup>to Babylon.

7 Nebuchadnezzar also carried of the <sup>i</sup>vessels of the house of the LORD to Babylon, and put them in his temple at Babylon.

8 Now the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and his <sup>k</sup>abominations which he did, and that which was found in him, behold, they *are* written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah: and <sup>l</sup>Jehoiachin his son reigned in his stead.

9 ¶ Jehoiachin *was* <sup>m</sup>eight years old when he began to reign; and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem: and he did *that which was* evil in the sight of the LORD.

10 And when the year was expired, king Nebuchadnezzar sent and brought him to Babylon, with the goodly vessels of the house of the LORD, and made <sup>||</sup>Zedekiah his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem.

11 ¶ Zedekiah *was* one and twenty years old when he began to reign; and reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.

12 And he did *that which was* evil in the sight of the LORD his God, *and* <sup>n</sup>humbled not himself before Jeremiah the prophet, *speaking* from the <sup>o</sup>mouth of the LORD.

13 And he also <sup>p</sup>rebelled against king Nebuchadnezzar, who had made him <sup>q</sup>swear by God: but he <sup>r</sup>stiffened his neck, and hardened his heart from turning unto the LORD God of Israel.

14 Moreover, all the chief of the priests, and the people, transgressed very much, after all the abominations of the heathen, and polluted the house of the LORD which he had hallowed in Jerusalem.

15 And the <sup>s</sup>LORD God of their fathers sent to them by his <sup>t</sup>messengers, <sup>u</sup>rising up betimes, and sending; because he had <sup>x</sup>compassion on his people, and on <sup>y</sup>his dwelling-place:

16 But they <sup>z</sup>mocked the messengers of God, and <sup>a</sup>despised his words, and <sup>b</sup>misused his prophets, until the wrath of the LORD arose against his people, till *there was* <sup>†</sup>no remedy.

17 Therefore he brought upon them the king of the Chaldees, who slew their young men with the sword in the house of their sanctuary, and had <sup>c</sup>no compassion upon <sup>d</sup>young man or maiden, old man, or him that stooped for age; he gave *them* all into his hand.

18 And all the vessels of the house of God, great and small, and the treasures of the house of the LORD, and the treasures of the king and of his princes, all *these* he brought to Babylon.
