5 <sup>e</sup>That your faith should not stand in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.

6 <sup>f</sup>Howbeit we speak wisdom among them that are perfect: yet not the wisdom of this world, nor of the princes of this world that come to nought:

7 <sup>g</sup>But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, *even* the hidden *wisdom,* which God ordained before the world unto our glory:

8 <sup>h</sup>Which none of the princes of this world knew: for had they known *it,* they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

9 <sup>i</sup>But, as it is written, Eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man, the things which God hath prepared for them that love him.

10 <sup>k</sup>But God hath revealed *them* unto us by his Spirit: for the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God.

11 <sup>l</sup>For what man knoweth the things of a man, save the spirit of man which is in him? even so the things of God knoweth no man, but the Spirit of God.

12 <sup>m</sup>Now we have received, not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit which is of God; that we might know the things that are freely given to us of God.

13 <sup>n</sup>Which things also we speak, not in the words which man's wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.

14 <sup>o</sup>But the natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God: for they are foolishness unto him: neither can he know *them,* because they are spiritually discerned.

15 <sup>p</sup>But he that is spiritual judgeth all things, yet he himself is judged of no man.

16 <sup>q</sup>For who hath known the mind of the Lord, that he may instruct him? But we have the mind of Christ.

## CHAPTER III.

1 How Paul dealt with weak believers....5 The most eminent preachers only instruments, &c.

<sup>a</sup>AND I, brethren, could not speak unto you, as unto spiritual, but as unto carnal, *even* as unto babes in Christ.

2 <sup>b</sup>I have fed you with milk, and not with meat: for hitherto ye were not able *to bear it,* neither yet now are ye able.

3 <sup>c</sup>For ye are yet carnal: for whereas *there is* among you envying, and strife, and divisions, are ye not carnal, and walk as men?

4 <sup>d</sup>For while one saith, I am of Paul; and another, I *am* of Apollos; are ye not carnal?

5 <sup>e</sup>Who then is Paul, and who *is* Apollos, but ministers by whom ye believed, even as the Lord gave to every man?

6 <sup>f</sup>I have planted, Apollos watered; but God gave the increase.

7 <sup>g</sup>So then neither is he that planteth any thing, neither he that watereth; but God that giveth the increase.

8 <sup>h</sup>Now he that planteth and he that watereth are one: and every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labour. <!-- uncertain: "according" partly smudged in scan -->

9 <sup>i</sup>For we are labourers together with God: ye are God's husbandry, *ye are* God's building.

10 <sup>k</sup>According to the grace of God which is given unto me, as a wise master-builder, I have laid the foundation, and another buildeth thereon. But let every man take heed how he buildeth thereupon.

11 <sup>l</sup>For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.

12 <sup>m</sup>Now if any man build upon this foundation, gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, stubble;

13 <sup>n</sup>Every man's work shall be made manifest: for the day shall declare it, because it shall be revealed by fire; and the fire shall try every man's work, of what sort it is.

14 <sup>o</sup>If any man's work abide which he hath built thereupon, he shall receive a reward.

15 <sup>p</sup>If any man's work shall be burned, he shall suffer loss: but he himself shall be saved; yet so as by fire.

16 <sup>q</sup>Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and *that* the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?

17 If any man defile the temple of God, him shall God destroy; for the temple of God is holy, which *temple* ye are.

18 <sup>r</sup>Let no man deceive himself. If any man among you seemeth to be wise in this world, let him become a fool, that he may be wise.

19 <sup>s</sup>For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God: for it is written, He taketh the wise in their own craftiness.

20 <sup>t</sup>And again, The Lord knoweth the thoughts of the wise, that they are vain.

21 <sup>u</sup>Therefore let no man glory in men: for all things are yours;

22 <sup>x</sup>Whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things to come; all are yours:

23 <sup>y</sup>And ye are Christ's; and Christ *is* God's.

## CHAPTER IV.

1 How to account of Christ's ministers....8 To a self-sufficient vanity Paul opposeth his own afflicted state, &c.

<sup>a</sup>LET a man so account of us, as of the ministers of Christ, and stewards of the mysteries of God.

2 <sup>b</sup>Moreover, it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.

3 <sup>c</sup>But with me it is a very small thing that I should be judged of you, or of man's judgment; yea, I judge not mine own self:

4 <sup>d</sup>For I know nothing by myself; yet am I not hereby justified: but he that judgeth me is the Lord.

5 <sup>e</sup>Therefore judge nothing before the time, until the Lord come, who both will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts: and then shall every man have praise of God.

6 <sup>f</sup>And these things, brethren, I have in a figure transferred to myself and *to* Apollos for your sakes; that ye might learn in us not to think *of men* above that which is written, that no one of you be puffed up for one against another.

7 <sup>g</sup>For who maketh thee to differ *from another?* and what hast thou that thou didst not receive? Now if thou didst receive *it,* why dost thou glory as if thou hadst not received *it?*

8 <sup>h</sup>Now ye are full, now ye are rich, ye have reigned as kings without us: and I would to God ye did reign; that we also might reign with you.

9 <sup>i</sup>For I think that God hath set forth us the apostles last, as it were appointed to death: for we are made a spectacle unto the world, and to angels, and to men.

10 <sup>k</sup>We *are* fools for Christ's sake, but ye *are* wise in Christ; we *are* weak, but ye *are* strong; ye *are* honourable, but we *are* despised.
