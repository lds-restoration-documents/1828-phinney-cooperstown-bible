4 Who hath <sup>d</sup>ascended up into heaven, or descended? who hath gathered the <sup>e</sup>wind in his fists? who hath bound the waters in a garment? who hath established all the ends of the earth? <sup>f</sup>what *is* his name, and what *is* his son's name, if thou canst tell?

5 <sup>g</sup>Every word of God *is* pure; he *is* a shield unto them that put their trust in him.

6 Add thou not unto his words, lest he <sup>h</sup>reprove thee, and thou be found a liar.

7 Two *things* have I required of thee; <sup>i</sup>deny me *them* not before I die:

8 Remove far from me vanity and lies; give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with <sup>k</sup>food convenient for me;

9 Lest I be full, and <sup>l</sup>deny *thee,* and say, Who *is* the LORD? or lest I be <sup>m</sup>poor, and steal, and take the name of my God *in vain.*

10 <sup>n</sup>Accuse not a servant unto his master, lest he curse thee, and thou be found guilty.

11 *There is* a generation *that* curseth their father, and doth not bless their mother.

12 *There is* a generation *that are* pure in their <sup>o</sup>own eyes, and *yet* is not washed from their <sup>p</sup>filthiness.

13 *There is* a generation, O how <sup>q</sup>lofty are their eyes! and their eye-lids are lifted up.

14 *There is* a generation whose teeth *are as* swords, and their jaw-teeth *as* knives, to <sup>r</sup>devour the poor from off the earth, and the needy from *among* men.

15 The horse-leech hath two daughters, *crying,* <sup>s</sup>Give, give. There are three *things that* are never satisfied, *yea,* four *things* say not, *It is* enough:

16 The grave; and the <sup>t</sup>barren womb; the earth *that* is not filled with water; and the fire, *that* saith not, *It is* enough.

17 The eye *that* <sup>u</sup>mocketh at *his* father, and despiseth to obey *his* mother, the ravens of the valley shall pick it out, and the young eagles shall eat it.

18 There be three *things which are* too <sup>x</sup>wonderful for me, yea, four which I know not:

19 The way of an eagle in the air, the way of a serpent upon a rock, the way of a ship in the midst of the sea, and the way of a man with a maid.

20 Such *is* the way of an <sup>y</sup>adulterous woman; she eateth, and <sup>z</sup>wipeth her mouth, and saith, I have <sup>a</sup>done no wickedness.

21 For three *things* the earth is disquieted, and for four *which* it cannot bear:

22 For a servant when he reigneth, and a fool when he is <sup>b</sup>filled with meat;

23 For an odious *woman* when she is married, and a handmaid that is heir to her mistress.

24 There be four *things which are* <sup>c</sup>little upon the earth, but they *are* exceeding wise:

25 The ants *are* a people not strong, yet they prepare their meat in the summer;

26 The conies *are but* a feeble folk, yet make they their houses in the <sup>d</sup>rocks;

27 The locusts have no king, yet go they forth all of them by bands;

28 The spider taketh hold with her hands, and is in kings' palaces.

29 There be three *things* which go well, yea, four are comely in going:

30 <sup>†</sup>A lion, *which is* <sup>e</sup>strongest among beasts, and turneth not away for any,

31 A greyhound; a he-goat also; and a king, against whom *there is* no rising up.

32 If thou hast done foolishly in lifting up thyself, or if thou hast <sup>f</sup>thought evil, *lay* thy hand upon thy mouth.

33 Surely the churning of milk bringeth forth butter, and the wringing of the nose bringeth forth blood; so the <sup>g</sup>forcing of wrath bringeth forth strife.

## CHAPTER XXXI.

Lemuel's lesson of chastity and temperance.

THE words of king Lemuel, the prophecy that <sup>a</sup>his mother taught him.

2 What, my son? and what, the son of my <sup>b</sup>womb? and what, the son of <sup>c</sup>my vows?

3 Give not thy <sup>d</sup>strength unto women, nor thy ways to that which <sup>e</sup>destroyeth kings.

4 *It is* not for kings, O Lemuel, *it is* not for kings to drink wine, nor for princes strong drink;

5 Lest they drink, and <sup>f</sup>forget the law, and pervert the judgment of any of the afflicted.

6 Give strong drink unto him that is ready to perish, and wine unto those that be of heavy hearts.

7 Let him drink, and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more.

8 Open thy mouth for the <sup>g</sup>dumb in the cause of all such as are <sup>h</sup>appointed to destruction.

9 Open thy mouth, <sup>i</sup>judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy.

10 Who can find <sup>k</sup>a virtuous woman? for her price *is* far above rubies.

11 The heart of her <sup>l</sup>husband doth safely <sup>m</sup>trust in her, so that he shall have no need of spoil.

12 She will do him <sup>n</sup>good, and not evil, all the days of her life.

13 She seeketh wool and flax, <sup>o</sup>and worketh <sup>p</sup>willingly with her hands.

14 She is like the <sup>q</sup>merchants' ships, she bringeth her <sup>r</sup>food from afar.

15 She riseth also while it is yet <sup>s</sup>night, and giveth meat to her household, and a <sup>t</sup>portion to her maidens.

16 She considereth a <sup>u</sup>field, and buyeth it; with the fruit of her hands she planteth a vineyard.

17 She girdeth her loins with strength, and <sup>x</sup>strengtheneth her arms.

18 She perceiveth that her merchandise *is* good: her <sup>y</sup>candle goeth not out by night.

19 She layeth her hands to the <sup>z</sup>spindle, and her hands hold the distaff.

20 She <sup>a</sup>stretcheth out her hand to the poor; yea, she reacheth forth her hands to the needy.

21 She is not afraid of the snow for her household; for all her household *are* clothed with scarlet.

22 She maketh herself <sup>b</sup>coverings of tapestry; her clothing *is* silk and purple.

23 Her husband <sup>c</sup>is known in the gates, when he sitteth among the elders of the land.

24 She maketh fine linen, and selleth *it;* and delivereth <sup>d</sup>girdles unto the merchant.

25 <sup>e</sup>Strength and honour *are* her clothing; and she shall rejoice in time to come.

26 She openeth her mouth <sup>f</sup>with wisdom; and in her tongue *is* the <sup>g</sup>law of kindness.

27 She <sup>h</sup>looketh well to the ways of her household, and eateth not the bread of idleness.

28 Her <sup>i</sup>children rise up, and call her blessed; her husband *also,* and he praiseth her.

29 Many daughters have done virtuously, but thou <sup>k</sup>excellest them all.

30 Favour *is* <sup>l</sup>deceitful, and beauty *is* vain: *but* a woman *that* feareth the LORD, she shall be praised.

31 <sup>m</sup>Give her of the fruit of her hands; and let <sup>n</sup>her own works praise her in the gates.
