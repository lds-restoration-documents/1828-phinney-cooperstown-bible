Under the parable of a vineyard, God excuseth his severe judgment.

NOW will I sing to my <sup>a</sup>well-beloved a song of my beloved touching his <sup>b</sup>vineyard. My well-beloved hath a vineyard in a very fruitful hill:

2 And he fenced it, and gathered out the stones thereof, and planted it with the choicest vine, and built a tower in the midst of it, and also made a wine-press therein: and he <sup>c</sup>looked that it should bring forth grapes, and it brought forth <sup>d</sup>wild grapes.

3 And now, O inhabitants of Jerusalem, and men of Judah, <sup>e</sup>judge, I pray you, betwixt me and my vineyard.

4 What could have been done more to my vineyard <sup>f</sup>that I have not done in it? wherefore, when I looked that it should bring forth grapes, brought it forth wild grapes?

5 And now, go to; I will tell you what I will do to my vineyard: I will <sup>g</sup>take away the hedge thereof, and it shall be eaten up; *and* break down the wall thereof, and it shall be trodden down:

6 And I will lay it <sup>h</sup>waste: it shall not be <sup>i</sup>pruned nor digged; but there shall come up briars and thorns: I will also command the clouds that they rain no rain upon it.

7 For the vineyard of the LORD of hosts *is* the house of Israel, and the men of Judah his pleasant plant: and he looked for judgment, but behold oppression; for righteousness, but behold <sup>k</sup>a cry.

8 Wo unto them that <sup>l</sup>join house to house, *that* lay field to field, till *there be* <sup>m</sup>no place, that they may be placed alone in the midst of the earth!

9 In mine ears, *said* the LORD of hosts, Of a truth, many <sup>n</sup>houses shall be desolate, *even* great and fair, without inhabitant.
