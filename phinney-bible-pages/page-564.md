## CHAPTER IV.

Jonah, repining at God's mercy, is reproved by the type of a gourd.

BUT it <sup>a</sup>displeased Jonah exceedingly, and he was very angry.

2 And he <sup>b</sup>prayed unto the LORD, and said, I pray thee, O LORD, *was* not this my saying when I was yet in my country? <sup>c</sup>Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish: for I knew that thou *art* a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil.

3 Therefore now, O LORD, <sup>d</sup>take, I beseech, thee, my life from me: for *it is* <sup>e</sup>better for me to die than to live.

4 Then said the LORD, <sup>f</sup>Doest thou well to be angry?

5 So Jonah <sup>g</sup>went out of the city, and sat on the east side of the city, and there <sup>h</sup>made him a booth, and sat under it in the shadow, till he might see what would become of the city.

6 And the Lord GOD <sup>i</sup>prepared a gourd, and made *it* to come up over Jonah, that it might be a shadow over his head, to deliver him from his <sup>k</sup>grief. So Jonah was <sup>l</sup>exceeding glad of the gourd.

7 But God prepared a worm, when the morning rose the next day, and it <sup>m</sup>smote the gourd that it withered.

8 And it came to pass, when the <sup>n</sup>sun did arise, that God prepared a vehement east wind; and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, that he fainted, and <sup>o</sup>wished in himself to die, and said, *It is* better for me to die than to live.

9 And God said to Jonah, <sup>p</sup>Doest thou well to be angry for the gourd? And he said, <sup>q</sup>I do well to be angry, *even* unto death.

10 Then said the LORD, Thou hast <sup>r</sup>had pity on the gourd, for the which thou hast not laboured, neither madest it grow; which came up in a night, and perished in a night:

11 And should not I <sup>s</sup>spare Nineveh, that <sup>t</sup>great city, wherein are more than sixscore thousand persons that cannot discern between their <sup>u</sup>right hand and their left hand; and *also* much cattle?

# ¶ MICAH.

## CHAPTER I.

1 Micah sheweth the wrath of God against Jacob for idolatry: 10 He exhorteth to mourning.

THE word of the LORD that came to <sup>a</sup>Micah the <sup>b</sup>Morasthite, in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, *and* Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.

2 ¶ Hear, <sup>c</sup>all ye people; hearken, O earth, and all that therein is: and let the Lord GOD <sup>d</sup>be witness against you, the LORD from his holy temple.

3 For, behold, the LORD <sup>e</sup>cometh forth out of his place, and will come down, and <sup>f</sup>tread upon the high places of the earth.

4 And the <sup>g</sup>mountains shall be molten under him, and the valleys shall be cleft, as wax before the fire, *and* as the waters *that are* poured down a steep place.

5 For the transgression of Jacob *is* all this, and for the sins of the house of Israel. <sup>h</sup>What *is* the transgression of Jacob? *is it* not <sup>i</sup>Samaria? and what *are* the high places of Judah? *are they* not <sup>k</sup>Jerusalem?

6 Therefore I will make Samaria as <sup>l</sup>a heap of the field, *and* as plantings of a vineyard: and I will pour down the stones thereof into the valley, and I will <sup>m</sup>discover the foundations thereof.

7 And all the <sup>n</sup>graven images thereof shall be beaten to pieces, and all the hires thereof shall be burnt with the fire, and all the idols thereof will I lay desolate; for she <sup>o</sup>gathered *it* of the hire of a harlot, and they shall return to the hire of a harlot.

8 Therefore I will <sup>p</sup>wail and howl, I will go stripped and naked: I will make a wailing like the dragons, and mourning as the owls.

9 For her wound *is* incurable; for <sup>q</sup>it is come unto Judah: he is come unto the gate of my people, *even* to Jerusalem.

10 <sup>r</sup>Declare ye *it* not at Gath, weep ye not at all: in the house of Aphrah <sup>s</sup>roll thyself in the dust.

11 <sup>t</sup>Pass ye away, thou inhabitant of Saphir, having <sup>u</sup>thy shame naked; the inhabitant of Zaanan came not forth in the mourning of Beth-ezel; he shall receive of you his standing.

12 For the inhabitant of Maroth waited carefully for good; but evil came down <sup>x</sup>from the LORD unto the gate of Jerusalem.

13 O thou inhabitant of <sup>y</sup>Lachish, bind the chariot to the swift beast: she *is* the <sup>z</sup>beginning of the sin to the daughter of *Zion;* for the transgressions of Israel were found in thee.

14 Therefore shalt thou <sup>a</sup>give presents to Moreshethgath: the houses of Achzib *shall be* <sup>b</sup>a lie to the kings of Israel.

15 Yet will I bring <sup>c</sup>an heir unto thee, O inhabitant of Mareshah: he shall come unto Adullam the glory of Israel.

16 <sup>d</sup>Make thee bald, and poll thee for thy <sup>e</sup>delicate children; enlarge thy baldness as the eagle; for they are gone into captivity from thee.

## CHAPTER II.

1 Against oppression....7 A reproof of injustice and idolatry. 12 A promise of restoring Jacob.

WO to them that <sup>a</sup>devise iniquity, and work evil upon their beds! when the morning is light they practise it, because it is in the <sup>b</sup>power of their hand.

2 And they <sup>c</sup>covet fields, and take *them* by violence; and houses, and take *them* away: so they oppress a man and his house, even a man and his heritage.

3 Therefore thus saith the LORD, Behold, against this family do I <sup>d</sup>devise an evil, from which ye shall <sup>e</sup>not remove your necks; neither shall ye go haughtily: for this time *is* <sup>f</sup>evil.

4 <sup>g</sup>In that day shall *one* take up a parable against you, and lament with a doleful lamentation, *and* say, We be utterly spoiled: he hath <sup>h</sup>changed the portion of my people: how hath he removed *it* from me! turning away he hath divided our fields.

5 Therefore thou shalt have none that shall cast a cord by lot in the congregation of the LORD.

6 <sup>i</sup>Prophesy ye not, *say they to them that* prophesy: they shall <sup>k</sup>not prophesy to them *that* they shall not take shame.

7 O *thou that art* <sup>l</sup>named The house of Jacob, is the Spirit of the LORD <sup>m</sup>straitened? *are* these his doings? do not my words do <sup>n</sup>good to him that walketh uprightly?
