## CHAPTER XV.

The utter rejection and manifold judgments of the Jews.

THEN said the LORD unto me, Though <sup>a</sup>Moses and Samuel stood before me, *yet* my mind *could* not *be* toward this people; cast *them* out of my sight, and let them go forth.

2 And it shall come to pass, if they say unto thee, Whither shall we go forth? then thou shalt tell them, Thus saith the LORD, Such as *are* <sup>b</sup>for death, to death; and such as *are* for the sword, to the sword; and such as *are* for the famine, to the famine; and such as *are* for the captivity, to the captivity.

3 And I will <sup>c</sup>appoint over them four kinds, saith the LORD; the sword to slay, and the dogs <sup>d</sup>to tear, and the fowls of the heaven, and the beasts of the earth, to devour and destroy.

6 Thou hast <sup>h</sup>forsaken me, saith the LORD, thou art gone backward; therefore will I stretch out my hand against thee, and destroy thee; I am <sup>i</sup>weary with repenting.
