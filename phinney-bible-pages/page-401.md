**Header:** *David's grief and hope:* | PSALMS. | *He delighteth in God's law*

111 Thy testimonies have I taken as a <sup>f</sup>heritage for ever: for they *are* the rejoicing of my heart.

112 I have inclined my heart to perform thy statutes always, *even unto* the end.

### SAMECH.

113 I hate <sup>||</sup>*vain* thoughts: but thy law do I love.

114 Thou *art* my <sup>g</sup>hiding-place and my shield: I hope in thy word.

115 <sup>h</sup>Depart from me, ye evil-doers: for I will keep the commandments of my God.

116 Uphold me according unto thy word, that I may live: and let me not be <sup>i</sup>ashamed of my hope.

117 Hold thou me up, and I shall be safe: and I will have respect unto thy statutes continually.

118 Thou <sup>k</sup>hast trodden down all them that err from thy statutes: for their deceit *is* falsehood.

119 Thou puttest away all the wicked of the earth *like* dross: therefore I love thy testimonies.

120 My flesh <sup>l</sup>trembleth for fear of thee; and I am afraid of thy judgments.

### AIN.

121 I have done judgment and justice: <sup>m</sup>leave me not to mine oppressors.

122 Be <sup>n</sup>surety for thy servant for good: let not the proud oppress me.

123 Mine eyes fail for thy salvation, and for the word of thy righteousness.

124 Deal with thy servant <sup>o</sup>according unto thy mercy, and teach me thy statutes.

125 I *am* <sup>p</sup>thy servant, give me understanding, that I may know thy testimonies.

126 <sup>q</sup>*It is* time for *thee,* LORD, <sup>r</sup>to work; *for* they have made void thy law.

127 Therefore I love thy commandments <sup>s</sup>above gold, yea, above fine gold.

128 Therefore I esteem all *thy* precepts *concerning* all *things to be* right; *and* I hate every false way.

### PE.

129 Thy testimonies *are* wonderful: therefore doth my soul keep them.

130 The entrance of thy words giveth <sup>t</sup>light; it giveth understanding unto the simple.

131 I opened my mouth, and <sup>u</sup>panted: for I longed for thy commandments.

132 Look thou upon me, and be merciful unto me, <sup>x</sup>as thou usest to do unto those that love thy name.

133 <sup>y</sup>Order my steps in thy word: and let not any iniquity have <sup>z</sup>dominion over me.

134 Deliver me from the oppression of man: <sup>a</sup>so will I keep thy precepts.

135 Make thy face <sup>b</sup>to shine upon thy servant; and teach me thy statutes.

136 Rivers of waters run down mine eyes, because they keep not thy law.

### TSADDI.

137 <sup>c</sup>Righteous *art* thou, O LORD, and upright *are* thy judgments.

138 Thy testimonies *that* thou hast <sup>d</sup>commanded *are* righteous and very faithful.

139 <sup>e</sup>My zeal hath consumed me: because mine enemies have forgotten thy words.

140 Thy word *is* very <sup>f</sup>pure: therefore thy servant <sup>g</sup>loveth it.

141 I *am* small and despised; *yet* do not I forget thy precepts.

142 Thy righteousness *is* an everlasting righteousness, and thy law *is* the <sup>h</sup>truth.

143 Trouble and anguish have taken hold on me; *yet* thy commandments *are* my delights.

144 The righteousness of thy testimonies *is* everlasting: give me understanding, and I shall <sup>i</sup>live.

### KOPH.

145 I cried with *my* whole heart; hear me, O LORD: I will keep thy statutes.

146 I cried unto thee; save me, and I shall keep thy testimonies.

147 <sup>k</sup>I prevented the dawning of the morning, and cried: I hoped in thy word.

148 Mine eyes prevent the <sup>l</sup>*night*-watches, that I might meditate in thy word.

149 Hear my voice, according unto thy loving-kindness: O LORD, <sup>m</sup>quicken me according to thy judgment.

150 They <sup>n</sup>draw nigh that follow after mischief: they are far from thy law.

151 Thou *art* <sup>o</sup>near, O LORD; and all thy commandments *are* truth.

152 Concerning thy testimonies, I have known of old that thou hast founded them <sup>p</sup>for ever.

### RESH.

153 <sup>q</sup>Consider mine affliction, and deliver me: for I do not forget thy law.

154 <sup>r</sup>Plead my cause, and deliver me: quicken me according to thy word.

155 Salvation *is* <sup>s</sup>far from the wicked: for they <sup>t</sup>seek not thy statutes.

156 Great *are* thy tender mercies, O LORD; quicken me according to thy judgments.

157 Many *are* my persecutors and mine enemies; <sup>u</sup>*yet* do I not decline from thy testimonies.

158 I beheld the transgressors, and was grieved; because they kept not thy word.

159 Consider how I love thy precepts: quicken me, O LORD, according to thy loving-kindness.

160 Thy <sup>†</sup>word *is* true *from* the beginning: and every one of thy righteous judgments *endureth* for ever.

### SCHIN.

161 <sup>x</sup>Princes have persecuted me without a cause: but my heart standeth in <sup>y</sup>awe of thy word.

162 I rejoice at thy word, as one that findeth great spoil.

163 I hate and abhor lying; <sup>z</sup>*but* thy law do I love.

164 <sup>a</sup>Seven times a day do I praise thee: because of thy righteous judgments.

165 <sup>b</sup>Great peace have they which love thy law: and nothing shall offend them.

166 LORD, I have hoped for thy salvation, and <sup>c</sup>done thy commandments.

167 My soul hath kept thy testimonies; and <sup>d</sup>I love them exceedingly.

168 I have kept thy precepts and thy testimonies: <sup>e</sup>for all my ways *are* before thee.

### TAU.

169 Let my <sup>f</sup>cry come near before thee, O LORD: give me understanding according to thy word.

170 Let my supplication come before thee: deliver me according to thy word.

171 <sup>g</sup>My lips shall utter praise, when thou hast taught me thy statutes.

172 My tongue shall speak of thy word: for all thy commandments *are* righteousness.

173 Let thy hand help me: for I have <sup>h</sup>chosen thy precepts.

174 I have longed for thy salvation, O LORD; and thy law *is* my <sup>†</sup>delight.
