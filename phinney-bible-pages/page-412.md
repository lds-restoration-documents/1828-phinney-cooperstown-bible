## CHAPTER IX.

The discipline and doctrine of wisdom.

WISDOM hath builded <sup>a</sup>her house, she hath <sup>b</sup>hewn out her seven <sup>c</sup>pillars:

2 She hath killed her <sup>d</sup>beasts; she hath mingled her wine; she hath also furnished her table:

3 She hath <sup>e</sup>sent forth her <sup>f</sup>maidens: she crieth upon the highest places of the city,

4 Whoso *is* <sup>g</sup>simple, let him turn in hither: *as for* him that wanteth understanding, she saith to him,

5 <sup>h</sup>Come, eat of my <sup>i</sup>bread, and drink of the wine *which* I have mingled.

6 <sup>k</sup>Forsake the foolish, and live; and go in the way of understanding.

7 He that reproveth <sup>l</sup>a scorner getteth to himself shame; and he that rebuketh a wicked *man getteth* himself a blot.

8 <sup>m</sup>Reprove not a scorner, lest he hate thee: rebuke <sup>n</sup>a wise man, and he will love thee.

9 Give *instruction* to a wise *man,* and he will be yet wiser; teach a just *man,* and he will <sup>o</sup>increase in learning.

10 <sup>p</sup>The fear of the LORD *is* the beginning of wisdom; and the knowledge of the holy *is* understanding.

11 For <sup>q</sup>by me thy days shall be multiplied, and the years of thy life shall be increased.

12 If thou be wise, thou shalt be wise <sup>r</sup>for thyself: but *if* thou scornest, thou alone shalt bear *it.*

13 ¶ A foolish woman *is* clamorous; *she is* simple, and <sup>s</sup>knoweth nothing.

14 For she <sup>t</sup>sitteth at the door of her house, on a seat in the high places of the city,

15 To call passengers who <sup>u</sup>go right on their ways;

16 Whoso *is* simple, let him turn in hither: and *as for* him that wanteth understanding, she saith to him,

17 <sup>x</sup>Stolen waters are sweet, and bread *eaten* in secret is pleasant.

18 But he knoweth not that the <sup>y</sup>dead *are* there; *and that* her guests *are* in the depths of hell.

## CHAPTER X.

From this chapter to the five and twentieth, are sundry observations of moral virtues, and their contrary vices.

THE Proverbs of Solomon. A wise son maketh a glad father: but a foolish son is the <sup>a</sup>heaviness of his mother.

2 Treasures of wickedness <sup>b</sup>profit nothing: but <sup>c</sup>righteousness delivereth from death.

3 The LORD will <sup>d</sup>not suffer the soul of the righteous to famish: but he casteth away the substance of the wicked.

4 He becometh poor that dealeth *with* a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich.

5 He that gathereth in summer *is* a wise son: *but* he that sleepeth in harvest *is* a son that causeth shame.

6 <sup>e</sup>Blessings *are* upon the head of the just: but violence <sup>f</sup>covereth the mouth of the wicked.

7 <sup>g</sup>The memory of the just *is* blessed: but the <sup>h</sup>name of the wicked shall rot.

8 The wise in heart will receive commandments: but <sup>i</sup>a prating fool shall fall.

9 He that walketh uprightly walketh <sup>k</sup>surely: but he that perverteth his ways shall be <sup>l</sup>known.

10 He that winketh with the eye causeth sorrow; but a prating fool shall fall.

11 The mouth of a righteous *man is* a <sup>m</sup>well of life: but violence covereth the mouth of the wicked.

12 Hatred stirreth up strifes: but <sup>n</sup>love covereth all sins.

13 In the lips of him that hath understanding <sup>o</sup>wisdom is found: but a <sup>p</sup>rod *is* for the back of him that is void of understanding.

14 Wise *men* <sup>q</sup>lay up knowledge: but the mouth of the foolish *is* near destruction.

15 The rich man's wealth *is* <sup>r</sup>his strong city: the destruction of the poor *is* their poverty.

16 The labour of the righteous <sup>s</sup>*tendeth* to life; the <sup>t</sup>fruit of the wicked to sin.

17 He *is in* the <sup>u</sup>way of life that <sup>x</sup>keepeth instruction: but he that refuseth reproof erreth.

18 He that hideth hatred *with* lying lips, and he that uttereth <sup>y</sup>a slander, *is* a fool.

19 In the <sup>z</sup>multitude of words there wanteth not sin: but he that refraineth his lips *is* wise.

20 The tongue of the just *is as* <sup>a</sup>choice silver: <sup>b</sup>the heart of the wicked *is* little worth.

21 The lips of the righteous <sup>c</sup>feed many: but fools die for want of wisdom.

22 The blessing of the LORD, <sup>d</sup>it maketh rich; and he addeth <sup>e</sup>no sorrow with it.

23 *It is* <sup>f</sup>as sport to a fool to do mischief: but a man of understanding hath wisdom.

24 The fear of the wicked, it shall <sup>g</sup>come upon him: but the desire of the righteous shall be <sup>h</sup>granted.

25 As the whirlwind passeth, so *is* the wicked <sup>i</sup>no *more:* but the righteous *is* <sup>k</sup>an everlasting foundation.

26 As vinegar to the teeth, and as smoke to the eyes, so *is* the <sup>l</sup>sluggard to them that send him.

27 The fear of the LORD prolongeth days: but the years of the wicked shall be <sup>m</sup>shortened.

28 <sup>n</sup>The hope of the righteous *shall be* gladness: but the <sup>o</sup>expectation of the wicked shall perish.

29 The way of the LORD *is* strength to the upright: but destruction *shall be* to the workers of iniquity.

30 The righteous shall <sup>p</sup>never be removed: but the wicked shall not inhabit the earth.

31 The mouth of the just bringeth forth wisdom: but the froward tongue shall be cut out.

32 The lips of the righteous know what is <sup>q</sup>acceptable: but the mouth of the wicked *speaketh* frowardness.

## CHAPTER XI.

<sup>a</sup>A FALSE balance *is* abomination to the LORD: but a just weight *is* his delight.

2 *When* pride cometh, then cometh <sup>b</sup>shame: but with the <sup>c</sup>lowly *is* wisdom.

3 The integrity of the upright shall <sup>d</sup>guide them: but the perverseness of transgressors shall <sup>e</sup>destroy them.

4 Riches <sup>f</sup>profit not in the day of wrath: but <sup>g</sup>righteousness delivereth from death.

5 The righteousness of the perfect shall direct his way; but the wicked shall fall by his own wickedness.

6 The righteousness of the upright shall deliver them; but transgressors <sup>h</sup>shall be taken in *their own* naughtiness.

7 When a wicked man dieth, *his* expectation shall perish; and the hope of unjust *men* perisheth.

8 The righteous is delivered out of trouble, and the wicked <sup>i</sup>cometh in his stead.

9 A hypocrite with *his* <sup>k</sup>mouth destroyeth his neighbour: but through <sup>l</sup>knowledge shall the just be delivered.

10 When it goeth well with the righteous, the city <sup>m</sup>rejoiceth: and when the wicked perish, *there is* <sup>n</sup>shouting.
