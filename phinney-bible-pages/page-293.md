6 Then he called for Solomon his son, and charged him to build <sup>i</sup>a house for the LORD God of Israel.

7 And David said to Solomon, My son, as for me, it was in <sup>k</sup>my mind to build a house unto the name of the LORD <sup>l</sup>my God.

8 But the <sup>m</sup>word of the LORD came to me, saying, <sup>n</sup>Thou hast shed blood abundantly, and hast made <sup>o</sup>great wars: thou shalt not build a house unto my name, because thou hast shed much blood upon the earth in my sight.

9 Behold, a son shall be born to thee, who shall be a man of rest; and I will give him rest from all his enemies round about: for his name shall be Solomon, and I will give peace and quietness unto Israel in his days.

10 <sup>p</sup>He shall build a house for my name, and he shall be my son, and I *will be* his father; and I will <sup>q</sup>establish the throne of his kingdom over Israel for ever.

11 Now, my son, <sup>r</sup>the LORD be with thee; and prosper thou, and build the house of the LORD thy God, as he hath said of thee.

12 Only the LORD give thee <sup>s</sup>wisdom and understanding, and give thee charge concerning Israel, that thou mayest keep the law of the LORD thy God.

13 <sup>t</sup>Then shalt thou prosper, if thou takest heed to fulfil the statutes and judgments which the LORD charged Moses with concerning Israel: be strong, and of good courage; dread not, nor be dismayed.

14 Now, behold, in my <sup>u</sup>trouble I have prepared for the house of the LORD <sup>||</sup>a hundred thousand talents of gold, and a thousand thousand talents of silver; and of brass and iron without weight; for it is in abundance: timber also and stone have I prepared; and thou mayest add thereto.

15 Moreover, *there are* workmen with thee in abundance, hewers and workers of stone and timber, and all manner of cunning men for every manner of work.

16 Of the gold, the silver, and the brass, and the iron, *there is* no number. Arise *therefore,* and be doing, and the LORD be with thee.

17 David also commanded all the princes of Israel to help Solomon his son, *saying,*

18 *Is* not the LORD your God with you? and hath he *not* given you rest on every side? for he hath given the inhabitants of the land into my hand; and the land is subdued before the LORD, and before his people.

19 Now <sup>x</sup>set your heart and your soul to seek the LORD your God: arise, therefore, and <sup>||</sup>build ye the sanctuary of the LORD God, <sup>y</sup>to bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD, and the holy vessels of God, into the house that is to be built to the <sup>z</sup>name of the LORD.

## CHAPTER XXIII.

David in his old age maketh Solomon king.

SO when David was <sup>a</sup>old and full of days, he <sup>b</sup>made Solomon his son king over Israel.

2 And he <sup>c</sup>gathered together all the princes of Israel, with the priests and the Levites.

3 Now the Levites were numbered from the age of <sup>d</sup>thirty years and upward; and their number by their polls, man by man, was thirty and eight thousand.

4 Of which, twenty and four thousand *were* to set forward the work of the house of the LORD; and six thousand *were* <sup>e</sup>officers and judges.

5 Moreover, four thousand *were* porters: and four thousand <sup>f</sup>praised the LORD with the instruments which I made (*said David*) to praise *therewith.*

6 And David <sup>g</sup>divided them into <sup>†</sup>courses among the sons of Levi, *namely,* <sup>h</sup>Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

7 ¶ Of the <sup>i</sup>Gershonites *were* <sup>||</sup>Laadan and Shimei.

8 The <sup>k</sup>sons of Laadan; the chief *was* Jehiel, and Zetham, and Joel, three.

9 The sons of Shimei; Shelomith, and Haziel, and Haran, three. These *were* the chief of the fathers of Laadan.

10 And the sons of Shimei *were,* Jahath, Zina, and Jeush, and Beriah. These four *were* the sons of Shimei.

11 And Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second; but Jeush and Beriah <sup>†</sup>had not many sons; therefore they were in one reckoning, according to *their* father's house.

12 ¶ The sons of <sup>l</sup>Kohath; Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, four.

13 The sons of <sup>m</sup>Amram; Aaron and Moses; and <sup>n</sup>Aaron was separated, that he should sanctify <sup>†</sup>the most holy things, he and his sons for ever, to burn incense before the LORD, to minister unto him, and <sup>o</sup>to bless in his name for ever.

14 Now *concerning* Moses the <sup>p</sup>man of God, <sup>q</sup>his sons were named of the tribe of Levi.

15 The sons of Moses *were* Gershom and Eliezer.

16 Of the <sup>r</sup>sons of Gershom, Shebuel *was* the chief.

17 And the sons of <sup>s</sup>Eliezer *were,* Rehabiah the chief. And Eliezer had none other sons; but the sons of Rehabiah <sup>†</sup>were very many.

18 Of the sons of <sup>t</sup>Izhar; <sup>u</sup>Shelomith the chief.

19 Of the sons of Hebron; Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.

20 Of the sons of Uzziel; Micah the first, and Jesiah the second.

21 ¶ <sup>x</sup>The sons of Merari; Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli; Eleazar and Kish.

22 And Eleazar died, and had no sons, but <sup>y</sup>daughters; and their brethren the sons of Kish <sup>z</sup>took them.

23 The sons of Mushi; Mahli, and Eder, and Jerimoth, three.

24 ¶ These *were* the sons of <sup>a</sup>Levi, after the house of their fathers; *even* the chief of the fathers, as they were counted by number of names by their polls, that did the <sup>b</sup>work for the service of the house of the LORD, from the age of <sup>c</sup>twenty years and upward.

25 For David said, The LORD God of Israel hath given <sup>d</sup>rest unto his people, that they may dwell <sup>e</sup>in Jerusalem for ever:

26 And also unto the Levites; they shall <sup>f</sup>no *more* carry the tabernacle, nor any vessels of it for the service thereof.

27 For by the last words of David, the Levites *were* numbered from twenty years old and above.

28 Because <sup>†</sup>their office *was* to wait on the sons of Aaron, for the <sup>g</sup>service of the house of the LORD, in the <sup>h</sup>courts, and in the chambers, and in the purifying of all holy things, and the work of the service of the house of God;
