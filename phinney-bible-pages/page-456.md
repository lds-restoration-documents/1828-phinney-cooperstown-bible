13 Sing, <sup>a</sup>O heavens; and be joyful, O earth; and break forth into singing, O mountains; for the LORD hath comforted his people, and will have mercy upon his afflicted.

14 But <sup>b</sup>Zion said, The LORD hath forsaken me, and my LORD hath forgotten me.

15 Can <sup>c</sup>a woman forget her <sup>d</sup>sucking child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb? yea, they may forget, yet will <sup>f</sup>I not forget thee.

16 Behold, I have <sup>g</sup>graven thee upon the palms of *my* hands; thy <sup>h</sup>walls *are* continually before me.

17 Thy <sup>||</sup>children shall make haste; thy destroyers, and they that made thee waste, shall <sup>i</sup>go forth of thee.

18 <sup>k</sup>Lift up thine eyes round about, and behold: all these gather themselves together, *and* come to thee. *As* I live, saith the LORD, thou shalt surely <sup>l</sup>clothe thee with them all as with an ornament, and bind them *on thee* as a bride *doeth.*

19 For thy <sup>m</sup>waste and thy desolate places, and the land of thy destruction, shall even now be <sup>n</sup>too narrow by reason of the inhabitants, and they that swallowed thee up shall be <sup>o</sup>far away.

20 The children which thou shalt <sup>p</sup>have, after thou hast <sup>q</sup>lost the other, shall say again in thine ears, The place *is* too strait for me: give place to me that I may dwell.

21 Then shalt thou say in thy heart, Who hath <sup>r</sup>begotten me these, seeing I have lost my children, and am desolate, a captive, and removing to and fro? and who hath brought up these? Behold, I was left alone; these, where *had* they *been?*

22 Thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, I will lift up my hand to the <sup>s</sup>Gentiles, and set up my standard to the people: and they shall bring thy sons in *their* arms, and thy daughters shall be carried upon *their* shoulders.

23 And kings shall be thy <sup>t</sup>nursing-fathers, and their queens thy nursing-mothers: they shall bow down to thee with *their* face toward the earth, and <sup>u</sup>lick up the dust of thy feet; and thou shalt know that I *am* the LORD: for they shall not be ashamed that wait for me.

24 Shall the prey be taken from the mighty, or the lawful captive delivered?

25 But thus saith the LORD, Even the <sup>x</sup>captives of the mighty shall be taken away, and the prey of the terrible shall be delivered: for I will contend with him that contendeth with thee, and I will save thy children.

26 And I will feed them that oppress thee with their <sup>y</sup>own flesh; and they shall be drunken with their own blood, as with sweet wine: and all flesh shall <sup>z</sup>know that I the LORD *am* thy Saviour, and thy Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.

## CHAPTER L.

Christ sheweth that the dereliction of the Jews is not to be imputed to him.

THUS saith the LORD, Where *is* the <sup>a</sup>bill of your mother's divorcement, whom I have put away? or which of my <sup>b</sup>creditors *is it* to whom I have sold you? Behold, for your iniquities have ye sold yourselves, and for your transgressions is <sup>c</sup>your mother put away.

2 Wherefore, when <sup>d</sup>I came, *was there* <sup>e</sup>no man? when I called, *was there* none to answer? Is my hand <sup>f</sup>shortened at all, that it cannot redeem? or have I no power to deliver? behold, at my rebuke I dry up the sea: I make the rivers a wilderness: their fish stinketh, because *there is* no water, and dieth for thirst.

3 I clothe the heavens with blackness, and I make sackcloth their covering.

4 The Lord GOD hath given me the <sup>g</sup>tongue of the learned, that I should know how to speak <sup>h</sup>a word in season to *him that is* weary: he wakeneth morning by morning; he <sup>i</sup>wakeneth mine ear to hear as the learned.

5 The Lord GOD hath <sup>k</sup>opened mine ear, and I was not <sup>l</sup>rebellious, neither turned away back.

6 I gave my back to the smiters, and my <sup>m</sup>cheeks to them that plucked off the hair: I hid not my face from shame and spitting.

7 For the Lord GOD will <sup>n</sup>help me; therefore shall I not be confounded: therefore have I set my face like a flint, and I know that I shall <sup>o</sup>not be ashamed.

8 *He is* <sup>p</sup>near that justifieth me: who will contend with me? let us <sup>q</sup>stand together: who *is* mine adversary? let him come near to me.

9 Behold, the Lord GOD will help me; who *is* he *that* shall condemn me? lo, they all shall <sup>r</sup>wax old as a garment; the moth shall eat them up.

10 Who *is* among you that <sup>s</sup>feareth the LORD, that <sup>t</sup>obeyeth the voice of his servant, that walketh <sup>u</sup>*in* darkness, and hath no light? let <sup>x</sup>him trust in the name of the LORD, and <sup>y</sup>stay upon his God.

11 Behold, all ye that <sup>z</sup>kindle a fire, that compass *yourselves* about with sparks; walk in the light of your fire, and in the sparks *that* ye have kindled. This shall ye have of my hand; ye shall lie down <sup>a</sup>in sorrow.

## CHAPTER LI.

An exhortation, after the pattern of Abraham, to trust in Christ.

HEARKEN to me, ye that follow after <sup>a</sup>righteousness, ye that seek the LORD: look unto the rock *whence* ye are hewn, and to the <sup>b</sup>hole of the pit, *whence* ye are digged.

2 Look unto <sup>c</sup>Abraham your father, and unto Sarah *that* bare you: for I <sup>d</sup>called him alone, and <sup>e</sup>blessed him, and increased him.

3 For the LORD shall <sup>f</sup>comfort Zion: he will comfort all her <sup>g</sup>waste places, and he will make her wilderness like Eden, and her desert like the garden of the LORD; joy and gladness shall be found therein, thanksgiving, and the voice of melody.

4 Hearken unto me, my people, and give ear unto me, O my nation: for <sup>h</sup>a law shall proceed from me, and I will make my judgment to rest for <sup>i</sup>a light of the people.

5 <sup>k</sup>My righteousness *is* near; my salvation is gone forth, and mine arms shall judge the people: the isles shall wait upon me, and on mine <sup>l</sup>arm shall they trust.

6 Lift up your eyes to the <sup>m</sup>heavens, and look upon the earth beneath; for the heavens shall vanish away like smoke, and the earth shall wax old like a garment, and they that dwell therein shall die in like manner: but <sup>n</sup>my salvation shall be for ever, and my righteousness shall not be abolished.

7 Hearken unto me, ye that know righteousness, the people in whose <sup>o</sup>heart *is* my law; fear ye not the <sup>p</sup>reproach of men, neither be ye afraid of their revilings.

8 For the <sup>q</sup>moth shall eat them up like a garment, and the <sup>r</sup>worm shall eat them like wool: but my righteousness shall be for ever, and my salvation from generation to generation.
