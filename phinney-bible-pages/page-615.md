2 <sup>b</sup>Therefore they were exceedingly afraid of him, and were <sup>c</sup>troubled for Jerusalem, and for the temple of the Lord their God:

3 For they were newly returned from the captivity, and all the people <sup>||</sup>of Judea were lately gathered together: and the vessels, and the altar, and the house, were sanctified <sup>d</sup>after the profanation.

4 Therefore they sent into all the coasts of Samaria and the villages, and to Bethoron, and Belmen, and Jericho, and to Choba and Esora, and to the valley of Salem:

5 And possessed themselves beforehand of all the tops of the high mountains, and fortified the villages that were in them, and laid up victuals for the provision of war: for their fields were of late reaped.

6 Also Joacim the high priest, which was in those days in Jerusalem, wrote to them that dwelt in Bethulia, and Betomestham, which is over against <sup>||</sup>Esdraelon toward the <sup>||</sup>open country, near to Dothaim,

7 Charging them to keep the passages of the hill-country: for by them there was an entrance into Judea, and it was easy to stop them that would come up, because the passage was strait, <sup>||</sup>for two men at the most.

8 And the children of Israel did as Joacim the high priest had commanded them, with the <sup>||</sup><sup>e</sup>ancients of all the people of Israel, which dwelt at Jerusalem.

9 Then every man of Israel <sup>f</sup>cried to God with great fervency, and with great vehemency did they humble their souls:

10 Both they, <sup>g</sup>and their wives, and their children, <sup>h</sup>and their cattle, and every stranger and hireling, and their servants bought with money, put sackcloth upon their loins.

11 Thus every man and woman, and the little children, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, fell before the temple, and cast ashes upon their heads, and spread out their sackcloth before the face of the Lord: also they put sackcloth about the altar,

12 And cried to the God of Israel all <sup>i</sup>with one consent earnestly, that he would not give their children for a prey, and their wives for a spoil, and the cities of their inheritance to destruction, and the sanctuary to profanation and reproach, and for the nations to rejoice at.

13 <sup>k</sup>So God heard their prayers, and looked upon their afflictions: for the people fasted many days in all Judea and Jerusalem before the sanctuary of the Lord Almighty.

14 And Joacim the high priest, <sup>l</sup>and all the priests that stood before the Lord, and they which ministered unto the Lord, had their loins girt with sackcloth, and offered the daily burnt-offerings, with the vows and free gifts of the people,

15 And had ashes on their <sup>m</sup>mitres, and cried unto the Lord with all their power, that he would look upon all the house of Israel graciously.

## CHAPTER V.

1 Achior telleth Holofernes what the Jews are, and what their God had done for them. 21 He adviseth not to meddle with them.....22 All that heard him were offended at him.

THEN was it declared to Holofernes, the chief captain of the army of Assur, that <sup>a</sup>the children of Israel had prepared for war, and had <sup>b</sup>shut up the passages of the hill-country, and had fortified <sup>†</sup>all the tops of the high-hills, and had laid impediments in the champaign countries:

2 Wherewith <sup>c</sup>he was very angry, and called all the princes of Moab, and the captains of Ammon, and all the governors of the sea-coast,

3 And he said unto them, Tell me now, ye sons of Chanaan, who this people is that dwelleth <sup>d</sup>in the hill-country, and what are the cities that they inhabit, and what is the multitude of their army, and wherein is their power and strength, and what king is set over them, or captain of their army;

4 And why have they determined not to come and meet me, <sup>e</sup>more than all the inhabitants of the west?

5 <sup>f</sup>Then said Achior, <sup>g</sup>the captain of all the sons of Ammon, Let my lord now hear a word from the mouth of thy servant, and I will declare unto thee the truth concerning this people, which dwelleth near thee, and inhabiteth the hill-countries: and there shall no lie come out of the mouth of thy servant.

6 This people are descended of the Chaldeans:

7 <sup>h</sup>And they sojourned heretofore in Mesopotamia, because they would not follow <sup>i</sup>the gods of their fathers, which were in the land of Chaldea.

8 For they <sup>||</sup>left the way of their ancestors, and worshipped the God of heaven, the God whom they knew: so they cast them out from the face of their gods, and they fled into Mesopotamia, and sojourned there many days.

9 Then <sup>k</sup>their God commanded them to depart from the place where they sojourned, and to go into the land of Chanaan: where they dwelt, and were increased with gold and silver, and with very much cattle.

10 But when a famine covered all the land of Chanaan, <sup>l</sup>they went down into Egypt, and sojourned there, while they were nourished, <sup>m</sup>and became there a great multitude, so that one could not number their nation.

11 Therefore the king of Egypt rose up against them, <sup>n</sup>and dealt subtilly with them, and brought them low with labouring in <sup>o</sup>brick, and made them slaves.

12 Then they cried unto their God, and he smote all the land of Egypt with incurable plagues: so the <sup>p</sup>Egyptians cast them out of their sight.

13 And <sup>q</sup>God dried the Red Sea before them,

14 And <sup>r</sup>brought them <sup>†</sup>to mount Sina, and Cades-Barne, and cast forth all that dwelt in the wilderness.

15 So they dwelt in the land of the Amorites, and they destroyed by their strength all them of Esebon, and passing over Jordan they possessed all the hill-country.

16 <sup>s</sup>And they cast forth before them the Chanaanite, the Pherezite, the Jebusite, and the Sychemite, and all the Gergesites, and they dwelt in that country many days.

17 And whilst they sinned not before their God, they prospered, because the God <sup>t</sup>that hateth iniquity was with them.

18 <sup>u</sup>But when they departed from the way which he appointed them, they were destroyed in many battles very sore, <sup>x</sup>and were lead captives into a land that was not theirs, and the temple of their God was cast to the ground, and their cities were taken by the enemies.

19 But <sup>y</sup>now they are returned to their God, and are come up from the places where they were scattered, and have possessed Jerusalem, where their sanctuary is, and <sup>||</sup>are seated in <sup>z</sup>the hill-country; for it was desolate.

20 Now therefore, my lord and governor, <sup>a</sup>if there be any error in this people, and they sin against their God, let us consider that this shall be their ruin, and let us go up, and we shall overcome them.

21 But if there be no iniquity in their nation, let my lord now pass by, lest their Lord defend them, and <sup>b</sup>their God be for them, and we become a reproach before all the world.

22 And when Achior had finished these sayings, all the people standing round about the tent murmured, and the chief men of Holofernes, and all that dwelt by the sea-side, and in Moab, spake that he should kill him.

23 For, *say they,* we will not be afraid of the face of the children of Israel: for lo, it is <sup>c</sup>a people that have no strength nor power <sup>†</sup>for a strong battle.

24 Now therefore, lord Holofernes, we will go up, and they shall be a prey to be devoured of all thine army.

AND when the <sup>a</sup>tumult of men that were about the council was ceased, Holofernes the chief captain of the army of Assur said unto Achior and all the Moabites before all the company of other nations,

2 And who art thou, Achior, and the hirelings of Ephraim, that thou hast prophesied among us as to-day, and hast said, that we should not make war with the people of Israel, because their God will defend them? <sup>b</sup>and who is God but Nabuchodonosor?

3 He will send his power, and will destroy them from the face of the earth, and their God shall not deliver them: but we his servants <sup>c</sup>will destroy them as one man; for they are not able to sustain the power of our horses.

4 For with them we will tread them under foot, and <sup>d</sup>their mountains shall be drunken with their blood, and their fields shall be filled with their dead bodies, and their footsteps shall not be able to stand before us, for they shall utterly perish, saith king Nabuchodonosor, lord of all the earth: for he said, None of my words shall be in vain.

5 And thou, Achior, <sup>e</sup>a hireling of Ammon, which hast spoken these words in the day of thine iniquity, shalt see my face no more from this day, until I take vengeance of this nation <sup>f</sup>that came out of Egypt.

6 And then shall the sword of mine army, and the multitude of them that serve me, pass through thy sides, and thou shalt fall among their slain, when I return.

7 Now therefore <sup>g</sup>my servants shall bring thee back into the hill-country, and shall set thee in one of the cities <sup>h</sup>of the passages:
