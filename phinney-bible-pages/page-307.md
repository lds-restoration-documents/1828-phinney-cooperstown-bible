## CHAPTER XVI.

Asa diverteth Baasha from building of Ramah.

IN the <sup>a</sup>six and thirtieth year of the reign of Asa, Baasha king of Israel came up against Judah, and <sup>b</sup>built Ramah, to the intent that he might let none <sup>c</sup>go out or come in to Asa king of Judah.

3 *There is* a league between me and thee, as *there was* between my father and thy father: behold, I have sent thee silver and gold; go, break thy league with Baasha king of Israel, that he may depart from me.

4 And Ben-hadad hearkened unto king Asa, and sent the captains of his armies against the cities of Israel; and they smote <sup>e</sup>Ijon, and Dan, and Abel-maim, and all the <sup>f</sup>store-cities of Naphtali.

9 For the <sup>l</sup>eyes of the LORD run to and fro throughout the whole earth, to shew himself <sup>m</sup>strong in the behalf of *them* whose heart *is* perfect towards him. Herein <sup>n</sup>thou hast done foolishly; therefore from henceforth thou shalt have wars.

13 And <sup>||</sup>Asa slept with his fathers, and died in the one and fortieth year of his reign.
