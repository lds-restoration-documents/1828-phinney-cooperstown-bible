17 And they did so: for Aaron stretched out his hand with his rod, and smote the dust of the earth, and it became <sup>p</sup>lice in man and in beast; all the dust of the land became lice throughout all the land of Egypt.

19 Then the magicians said unto Pharaoh, This *is* the <sup>r</sup>finger of God: and Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he hearkened not unto them; as the LORD had said.

21 Else, if thou wilt not let my people go, behold, I will send swarms *of flies* upon thee, and upon thy servants, and upon thy people, and into thy houses; and the houses of the Egyptians shall be full of <sup>||</sup>swarms *of flies*, and also the ground whereon they *are*.
