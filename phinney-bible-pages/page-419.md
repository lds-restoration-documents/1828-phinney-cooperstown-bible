## CHAPTER XXIV.

BE not thou <sup>a</sup>envious against evil men, neither desire to be with them:

2 For their heart <sup>b</sup>studieth destruction, and their lips <sup>c</sup>talk of mischief.

3 Through wisdom is a house <sup>d</sup>builded, and by understanding it is established;

4 And by knowledge shall the chambers be filled with all precious and pleasant riches.

5 A wise man *is* <sup>e</sup>strong; yea, a man of knowledge increaseth strength.

6 For by wise counsel thou shalt make thy <sup>f</sup>war; and in multitude of counsellors *there is* safety.

7 Wisdom *is* <sup>g</sup>too high for a fool; he openeth not his mouth in the gate.

8 He that <sup>h</sup>deviseth to do evil shall be called a mischievous person.

9 The <sup>i</sup>thought of foolishness *is* sin; and the scorner *is* an abomination to men.

10 *If* thou <sup>k</sup>faint in the day <sup>l</sup>of adversity, thy strength *is* small.

11 If thou forbear to deliver *them that are* drawn unto death, and *those that are* ready to be slain;

12 If thou sayest, Behold, we knew it not; doth not he that <sup>m</sup>pondereth the heart consider *it?* and he that keepeth thy soul, doth *not* he know *it?* and shall *not* he <sup>n</sup>render to *every* man according to his works?

13 My son, <sup>o</sup>eat thou honey, because *it is* good; and the honey-comb, *which is* sweet to thy taste:

14 So *shall* the <sup>p</sup>knowledge of wisdom *be* unto thy soul: when thou hast found *it,* then there shall be a reward, and thy expectation shall not be cut off.

15 Lay not wait, O wicked *man,* <sup>q</sup>against the dwelling of the righteous; spoil not his resting-place:

16 For a just *man* falleth seven times, and <sup>r</sup>riseth up again: but the wicked shall <sup>s</sup>fall into mischief.

17 <sup>t</sup>Rejoice not when thine enemy falleth; and let not thy heart be glad when he stumbleth;

18 Lest the LORD see *it,* and it <sup>u</sup>displease him, and he turn away his wrath from him.

19 <sup>x</sup>Fret not thyself because of evil *men,* neither be thou envious at the wicked:

20 For there shall be <sup>y</sup>no reward to the evil *man;* the candle of the wicked shall be put out.

21 My son, <sup>z</sup>fear thou the LORD and the king; *and* meddle not with them that are given <sup>a</sup>to change:

22 For their calamity shall rise suddenly; and who knoweth the <sup>b</sup>ruin of them both?

23 These *things* also *belong* to the wise. *It is* not good to have <sup>c</sup>respect of persons in judgment.

24 He that saith unto the <sup>d</sup>wicked, Thou *art* righteous; him shall the people curse, nations shall abhor him:

25 But to them that <sup>e</sup>rebuke *him* shall be delight, and a good blessing shall come upon them.

26 *Every* man shall kiss *his* lips that giveth a <sup>f</sup>right answer.

27 <sup>g</sup>Prepare thy work without, and make it fit for thyself in the field; and afterwards build thine house.

28 Be not a witness against thy neighbour <sup>h</sup>without cause; and deceive *not* with thy lips.

29 Say not, I will <sup>i</sup>do so to him as he hath done to me; I will render to the man according to his work.

30 I went by the field of the slothful, and by the vineyard of the man void of understanding;

31 And lo, it was all grown over with <sup>k</sup>thorns, *and* nettles had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken down.

32 Then I saw, *and* considered *it* well; I looked upon *it, and* received instruction.

33 <sup>l</sup>*Yet* a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:

34 So shall thy poverty come *as* one that travelleth; and thy want as an armed man.

## CHAPTER XXV.

Observations about the kings, and about avoiding quarrels.

THESE *are* also proverbs of Solomon, which the <sup>a</sup>men of Hezekiah, king of Judah, copied out.

2 *It is* the glory of God to <sup>b</sup>conceal a thing: but the honour of kings *is* to <sup>c</sup>search out a matter.

3 The heaven for height, and the earth for depth, and the heart of kings *is* <sup>d</sup>unsearchable.

4 Take away the <sup>e</sup>dross from the silver, and there shall come forth a vessel for the finer.

5 <sup>f</sup>Take away the wicked *from* before the king, and his throne shall be established in righteousness.

6 <sup>g</sup>Put not forth thyself in the presence of the king, and stand not in the place of great *men:*

7 For better *it is* that it be said unto thee, Come up hither, than that thou shouldest be put lower in the <sup>h</sup>presence of the prince whom thine eyes have seen.

8 Go not forth hastily to strive, lest *thou know not* what to do in the end thereof, when thy neighbour hath put thee <sup>i</sup>to shame.

9 <sup>k</sup>Debate thy cause with thy neighbour *himself,* and discover not a secret to another;

10 Lest he that heareth *it* put thee to shame, and thine infamy turn not away.

11 A word <sup>l</sup>fitly spoken *is like* apples of gold in pictures of silver.

12 *As* an ear-ring of gold, and an ornament of fine gold, *so is* a wise reprover upon an <sup>m</sup>obedient ear.

13 As the cold of snow in the time of harvest, *so is* a faithful messenger to them that send him; for he <sup>n</sup>refresheth the soul of his masters.

14 Whoso boasteth himself of <sup>o</sup>a false gift *is like* clouds and wind without rain.

15 By long forbearing is a prince persuaded, and <sup>p</sup>a soft tongue breaketh the bone.

16 Hast thou found honey? eat so much as is <sup>q</sup>sufficient for thee, lest thou be filled therewith, and vomit it.

17 Withdraw thy foot from thy neighbour's house; lest he be <sup>r</sup>weary of thee, and *so* hate thee.

18 A man that beareth false witness against his neighbour *is* a maul, and a <sup>s</sup>sword, and a sharp arrow.
