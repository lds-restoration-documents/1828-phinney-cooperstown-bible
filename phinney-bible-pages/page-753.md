33 <sup>d</sup>He that hath received his testimony hath set to his seal that God is true.

34 <sup>e</sup>For he whom God hath sent speaketh the words of God: for God giveth not the Spirit by measure *unto him.*

35 <sup>f</sup>The Father loveth the Son, and hath given all things into his hand.

36 <sup>g</sup>He that believeth on the Son hath everlasting life: and he that believeth not the Son shall not see life; but the wrath of God abideth on him.

## CHAPTER IV.

1 Christ talketh with a woman of Samaria, and revealeth himself unto her; 27 his disciples marvel....31 Christ's zeal for God's glory.

<sup>a</sup>WHEN therefore the Lord knew how the Pharisees had heard that Jesus made and baptized more disciples than John,

2 (Though Jesus himself baptized not, but his disciples,)

3 He left Judea, and departed again into Galilee.

4 And he must needs go through Samaria.

5 <sup>b</sup>Then cometh he to a city of Samaria, which is called Sychar, near to the parcel of ground that Jacob gave to his son Joseph.

6 Now Jacob's well was there. Jesus therefore, being wearied with *his* journey, sat thus on the well; *and* it was about the sixth hour.

7 There cometh a woman of Samaria to draw water. Jesus saith unto her, Give me to drink.

8 (For his disciples were gone away unto the city to buy meat.)

9 <sup>c</sup>Then saith the woman of Samaria unto him, How is it that thou, being a Jew, askest drink of me, which am a woman of Samaria? (for the Jews have no dealings with the Samaritans.)

10 <sup>d</sup>Jesus answered and said unto her, If thou knewest the gift of God, and who it is that saith to thee, Give me to drink; thou wouldest have asked of him, and he would have given thee living water.

11 <sup>e</sup>The woman saith unto him, Sir, thou hast nothing to draw with, and the well is deep: from whence then hast thou that living water?

12 Art thou greater than our father Jacob, which gave us the well, and drank thereof himself, and his children, and his cattle?

13 <sup>f</sup>Jesus answered and said unto her, Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again:

14 <sup>g</sup>But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him, shall never thirst: but the water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life.

15 The woman saith unto him, Sir, give me this water, that I thirst not, neither come hither to draw.

16 Jesus saith unto her, Go, call thy husband, and come hither.

17 The woman answered and said, I have no husband. Jesus said unto her, Thou hast well said, I have no husband:

18 For thou hast had five husbands; and he whom thou now hast is not thy husband: in that saidst thou truly.

19 <sup>h</sup>The woman saith unto him, Sir, I perceive that thou art a prophet.

20 <sup>i</sup>Our fathers worshipped in this mountain; and ye say, that in Jerusalem is the place where men ought to worship.

21 Jesus saith unto her, Woman, believe me, the hour cometh, when ye shall neither in this mountain, nor yet at Jerusalem, worship the Father.

22 <sup>k</sup>Ye worship ye know not what: we know what we worship; for salvation is of the Jews.

23 But the hour cometh, and now is, when the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and in truth: for the Father seeketh such to worship him.

24 <sup>l</sup>God *is* a spirit: and they that worship him must worship *him* in spirit and in truth.

25 <sup>m</sup>The woman saith unto him, I know that Messias cometh, which is called Christ: when he is come, he will tell us all things.

26 <sup>n</sup>Jesus saith unto her, I that speak unto thee am *he.*

27 ¶ <sup>o</sup>And upon this came his disciples, and marvelled that he talked with the woman: yet no man said, What seekest thou? or, Why talkest thou with her?

28 <sup>p</sup>The woman then left her water-pot, and went her way into the city, and saith to the men,

29 <sup>q</sup>Come, see a man which told me all things that ever I did: Is not this the Christ?

30 Then they went out of the city, and came unto him.

31 ¶ In the mean while his disciples prayed him, saying, Master, eat.

32 <sup>r</sup>But he said unto them, I have meat to eat that ye know not of.

33 Therefore said the disciples one to another, Hath any man brought him *aught* to eat?

34 <sup>s</sup>Jesus saith unto them, My meat is to do the will of him that sent me, and to finish his work.

35 <sup>t</sup>Say not ye, There are yet <sup>||</sup>four months, and *then* cometh harvest? behold, I say unto you, Lift up your eyes, and look on the fields; for they are white already to harvest.

36 <sup>u</sup>And he that reapeth receiveth wages, and gathereth fruit unto life eternal; that both he that soweth and he that reapeth may rejoice together.

37 And herein is that saying true, One soweth, and another reapeth.

38 I sent you to reap that whereon ye bestowed no labour: other men laboured, and ye are entered into their labours.

39 ¶ <sup>x</sup>And many of the Samaritans of that city believed on him for the saying of the woman, which testified, He told me all that ever I did.

40 So when the Samaritans were come unto him, they besought him that he would tarry with them: and he abode there two days.

41 And many more believed because of his own word;

42 <sup>y</sup>And said unto the woman, Now we believe, not because of thy saying; for we have heard *him* ourselves, and know that this is indeed the Christ, the Saviour of the world.

43 ¶ Now after two days he departed thence, and went into Galilee.

44 <sup>z</sup>For Jesus himself testified, that a prophet hath no honour in his own country.

45 <sup>a</sup>Then, when he was come into Galilee, the Galileans received him, having seen all the things that he did at Jerusalem at the feast: for they also went unto the feast.

46 <sup>b</sup>So Jesus came again into Cana of Galilee, where he made the water wine. And there was a certain<sup>†</sup>nobleman, whose son was sick at Capernaum.
