## CHAPTER XI.

Solomon's acts, reign, and death.

BUT king Solomon <sup>a</sup>loved <sup>b</sup>many strange women, (together with the daughter of Pharaoh,) women of the Moabites, Ammonites, Edomites, Zidonians, *and* Hittites;

2 Of the nations *concerning* which the LORD <sup>c</sup>said unto the children of Israel, Ye shall not go in to them, neither shall they come in unto you: *for* surely they will <sup>d</sup>turn away your heart after their gods. Solomon clave unto these in love.

3 And he had seven hundred wives, princesses, and three hundred concubines: and his wives <sup>e</sup>turned away his heart.

4 For it came to pass, when Solomon was <sup>||</sup>old, *that* his wives turned away his heart after other gods: and his heart was not perfect with the LORD his God, as *was* the heart of David his father.

5 For Solomon went after Ashtoreth the goddess of the Zidonians, and after Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites.

6 And Solomon did evil <sup>f</sup>in the sight of the LORD, and went not fully after the LORD, as *did* David his father.

7 Then did Solomon build a high place for Chemosh, the abomination of Moab, in the hill that *is* before Jerusalem; and for Molech, the abomination of the children of Ammon.

8 And likewise did he for all his strange wives which burnt incense, and sacrificed unto their gods.

9 And the LORD was angry with Solomon, because <sup>g</sup>his heart was turned from the LORD God of Israel, which had appeared unto him <sup>h</sup>twice,

10 And had <sup>i</sup>commanded him concerning this thing, that he should not go after other gods: but he kept not that which the LORD commanded.

11 Wherefore the LORD said unto Solomon, Forasmuch as this is done of thee, and thou hast not kept my covenant and my statutes, which I have commanded thee, I will surely rend the kingdom from thee, and will give it to thy servant.

12 Notwithstanding, in thy days I will not do it, <sup>k</sup>for David thy father's sake: *but* I will rend it out of the hand of thy son.

13 Howbeit I will not rend away all the kingdom; *but* will give <sup>l</sup>one tribe to thy son for David my servant's sake, and for Jerusalem's sake, which I have chosen.

14 ¶ And the <sup>m</sup>LORD stirred up an adversary unto Solomon, Hadad the Edomite: he *was* of the king's seed in Edom.

15 For it came to pass, when David was in <sup>n</sup>Edom, and Joab, the captain of the host, was gone up to bury the slain, after he had smitten <sup>o</sup>every male in Edom,

16 (For six months did Joab remain there with all Israel, until he had cut off every male in Edom,)

17 That Hadad fled, he, and certain Edomites of his father's servants with him, to go into Egypt; Hadad *being* yet a little <sup>p</sup>child.

18 And they arose out of <sup>q</sup>Midian, and came to Paran: and they took men with them out of Paran, and they came to Egypt, unto Pharaoh king of Egypt; which gave him a house, and appointed him <sup>†</sup>victuals, and gave him land.

19 And Hadad found <sup>†</sup>great favour in the sight of Pharaoh, so that he gave him to wife the sister of his own wife, the sister of Tahpenes the <sup>||</sup>queen.

20 And the sister of Tahpenes bare him Genubath his son, whom Tahpenes <sup>r</sup>weaned in Pharaoh's house: and Genubath was in Pharaoh's household, among the sons of Pharaoh.

21 And when Hadad heard in Egypt that David slept with his fathers, and that Joab, the captain of the host, was dead, Hadad said to Pharaoh, Let me depart, that I may go to mine own country.

22 Then Pharaoh said unto him, But what hast thou lacked with me, that, behold, thou seekest to go to thine own country? And he answered, Nothing: howbeit, <sup>s</sup>let me go in any wise.

23 ¶ And God stirred him up *another* adversary, Rezon the son of Eliadah, which <sup>t</sup>fled from his lord Hadadezer, king of <sup>u</sup>Zobah.

24 And he gathered men unto him, and became captain over a band, when David <sup>x</sup>slew them *of Zobah:* and they went to Damascus, and dwelt therein, and reigned in Damascus.

25 And he was an adversary to Israel <sup>y</sup>all the days of Solomon, besides the mischief that Hadad *did:* and he abhorred Israel, and reigned over Syria.

26 ¶ And <sup>z</sup>Jeroboam the son of Nebat, an Ephrathite of <sup>a</sup>Zereda, Solomon's servant, (whose mother's name *was* Zeruah, a widow woman,) even he <sup>b</sup>lifted up *his* hand against the king.

27 And this *was* the cause that he lifted up *his* hand against the king: Solomon built <sup>c</sup>Millo, *and* repaired the breaches of the city of David his father.

28 And the man Jeroboam *was* a mighty man of valour: and Solomon seeing the young man that he was industrious, he made him ruler over all the <sup>†</sup>charge of the house of Joseph.

29 And it came to pass at that time, when Jeroboam went out of Jerusalem, that the <sup>d</sup>prophet Ahijah the <sup>e</sup>Shilonite found him in the way; and he had clad himself with a new garment: and they two *were* alone in the field:

30 And Ahijah caught the new garment that *was* on him, and <sup>f</sup>rent it *in* twelve pieces.

31 And he said to Jeroboam, Take thee ten pieces; for thus saith the LORD, the God of Israel, Behold, I will rend the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon, and will give ten tribes to thee:

32 But he shall have one tribe for my servant David's sake, and for Jerusalem's sake, the city which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel:

33 Because that they have forsaken me, and have worshipped Ashtoreth the goddess of the Zidonians, Chemosh the god of the Moabites, and Milcom the god of the children of Ammon: and have not walked in <sup>g</sup>my ways, to do *that which is* <sup>h</sup>right in mine eyes, and *to keep* my statutes and my judgments, as *did* David his father.
