16 <sup>q</sup>Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of a holy-day, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath-*days;*

17 <sup>r</sup>Which are a shadow of things to come; but the body *is* of Christ.

18 <sup>s</sup>Let no man beguile you of your reward, in a voluntary humility and worshipping of angels, intruding into those things which he hath not seen, vainly puffed up by his fleshly mind;

19 <sup>t</sup>And not holding the head, from which all the body by joints and bands having nourishment ministered, and knit together, increaseth with the increase of God.

20 <sup>u</sup>Wherefore, if ye be dead with Christ from the rudiments of the world, why, as though living in the world, are ye subject to ordinances,

21 (Touch not; taste not; handle not;

22 <sup>x</sup>Which all are to perish with the using,) after the commandments and doctrines of men?

23 <sup>y</sup>Which things have indeed a shew of wisdom in will-worship, and humility, and neglecting of the body; not in any honour to the satisfying of the flesh.

## CHAPTER III.

1 He exhorteth to be heavenly minded; 5 to mortify corrupt lusts, and all malice; 12 to put on kindness, charity, &c. 18 Sundry other relative duties.

<sup>a</sup>IF ye then be risen with Christ, seek those things which are above, where Christ sitteth on the right hand of God.

2 <sup>b</sup>Set your affection on things above, not on things on the earth.

3 <sup>c</sup>For ye are dead, and your life is hid with Christ in God.

4 <sup>d</sup>When Christ, *who is* our life, shall appear, then shall ye also appear with him in glory.

5 <sup>e</sup>Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth; fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is idolatry:

6 <sup>f</sup>For which things' sake the wrath of God cometh on the children of disobedience:

7 <sup>g</sup>In the which ye also walked sometime, when ye lived in them.

8 <sup>h</sup>But now ye also put off all these; anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication out of your mouth.

9 <sup>i</sup>Lie not one to another, seeing that ye have put off the old man with his deeds;

10 <sup>k</sup>And have put on the new *man,* which is renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him:

11 <sup>l</sup>Where there is neither Greek nor Jew, circumcision nor uncircumcision, Barbarian, Scythian, bond *nor* free: but Christ *is* all, and in all.

12 <sup>m</sup>Put on therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, bowels of mercies, kindness, humbleness of mind, meekness, long-suffering;

13 <sup>n</sup>Forbearing one another, and forgiving one another, if any man have a quarrel against any: even as Christ forgave you, so also *do* ye.

14 <sup>o</sup>And above all these things *put on* charity, which is the bond of perfectness.

15 <sup>p</sup>And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to the which also ye are called in one body; and be ye thankful.

16 <sup>q</sup>Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly in all wisdom; teaching and admonishing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord.

17 <sup>r</sup>And whatsoever ye do in word or deed, *do* all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God and the Father by him.

18 <sup>s</sup>Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as it is fit in the Lord.

19 <sup>t</sup>Husbands, love *your* wives, and be not bitter against them.

20 <sup>u</sup>Children, obey *your* parents in all things: for this is well-pleasing unto the Lord.

21 <sup>x</sup>Fathers, provoke not your children *to anger,* lest they be discouraged.

22 <sup>y</sup>Servants, obey in all things *your* masters according to the flesh; not with eye-service, as men-pleasers; but in singleness of heart, fearing God:

23 And whatsoever ye do, do *it* heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men;

24 <sup>z</sup>Knowing, that of the Lord ye shall receive the reward of the inheritance: for ye serve the Lord Christ.

25 <sup>a</sup>But he that doeth wrong shall receive for the wrong which he hath done: and there is no respect of persons.

## CHAPTER IV.

1 He exhorteth to be fervent in prayer, 5 to walk wisely, 6 and with well-ordered speech; 7 he commendeth Tychicus and Onesimus....10 Sundry salutations.

<sup>a</sup>MASTERS, give unto *your* servants that which is just and equal; knowing that ye also have a Master in heaven.

2 <sup>b</sup>Continue in prayer, and watch in the same with thanksgiving;

3 <sup>c</sup>Withal, praying also for us, that God would open unto us a door of utterance, to speak the mystery of Christ, for which I am also in bonds:

4 <sup>d</sup>That I may make *it* manifest, as I ought to speak.

5 <sup>e</sup>Walk in wisdom toward them that are without, redeeming the time.

6 <sup>f</sup>Let your speech *be* always with grace, seasoned with salt, that ye may know how ye ought to answer every man.

7 <sup>g</sup>All my state shall Tychicus declare unto you, *who is* a beloved brother, and a faithful minister and fellow-servant in the Lord;

8 Whom I have sent unto you for the same purpose, that he might know your estate, and comfort your hearts;

9 <sup>h</sup>With Onesimus, a faithful and beloved brother, who is *one* of you. They shall make known unto you all things which *are done* here.

10 <sup>i</sup>Aristarchus my fellow-prisoner saluteth you, and Marcus, sister's son to Barnabas, (touching whom ye received commandments; if he come unto you, receive him;)

11 And Jesus, which is called Justus; who are of the circumcision. These only *are my* fellow-workers unto the kingdom of God, which have been a comfort unto me.

12 <sup>k</sup>Epaphras, who is *one* of you, a servant of Christ, saluteth you, always labouring fervently for you in prayers, that ye may stand perfect and complete in all the will of God.

13 For I bear him record, that he hath a great zeal for you, and them *that are* in Laodicea, and them in Hierapolis.

14 <sup>l</sup>Luke, the beloved physician, and Demas, greet you.
