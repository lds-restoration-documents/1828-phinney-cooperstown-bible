An exhortation to bless God for his mercy.

12 As far as the east is from the west, *so* far hath he <sup>m</sup>removed our transgressions from us.

13 Like as <sup>n</sup>a father <sup>o</sup>pitieth *his* children, *so* the LORD pitieth them that fear him.

14 For he knoweth our frame; he remembereth that we *are* <sup>p</sup>dust.

15 *As for* man, his days *are* as <sup>q</sup>grass; as a flower of the field, so he flourisheth:

18 To such as <sup>u</sup>keep his covenant, and to those that remember his commandments to do them.

19 The LORD hath prepared his <sup>x</sup>throne in the heavens; and his kingdom ruleth over all.

20 Bless the LORD, ye his angels, that <sup>y</sup>excel in strength, that <sup>z</sup>do his commandments, hearkening unto the voice of his word.

21 Bless ye the LORD, all *ye* his <sup>a</sup>hosts; *ye* <sup>b</sup>ministers of his that do his pleasure.

22 Bless the LORD, <sup>c</sup>all his works, in all places of his dominion: bless the LORD, O <sup>d</sup>my soul.

11 They give drink to <sup>o</sup>every beast of the field: the wild asses <sup>p</sup>quench their thirst.
