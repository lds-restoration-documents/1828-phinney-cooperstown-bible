## CHAPTER XVII.

1 Nathan promiseth David a blessing....16 David's prayer.

NOW it came to pass, as David sat in his <sup>a</sup>house, that David said to Nathan <sup>b</sup>the prophet, Lo, <sup>c</sup>I dwell in a house of cedars, but the <sup>d</sup>ark of the covenant of the LORD *remaineth* under curtains.

2 Then Nathan said unto David, Do <sup>e</sup>all that *is* in thy heart; for <sup>f</sup>God *is* with thee.

3 And it <sup>g</sup>came to pass the same night, that the word of God came to Nathan, saying,

4 Go and tell David my servant, Thus saith the LORD, Thou <sup>h</sup>shalt not build me a house to dwell in:

5 For I have not dwelt in a house since the day that I brought up Israel unto this day; but have gone <sup>i</sup>from tent to tent, and from *one* tabernacle *to another.*

6 Wheresoever I have walked with all Israel, spake I a word to any of the <sup>k</sup>judges of Israel, whom I commanded to feed my people, saying, Why have ye not built me a house of cedars?

7 Now therefore thus shalt thou say unto my servant David, Thus saith the <sup>l</sup>LORD of hosts, I took thee from the sheepcote, *even* from <sup>m</sup>following the sheep, that thou shouldest be ruler over my people Israel;

8 And <sup>n</sup>I have been with thee whithersoever thou hast walked, and have <sup>o</sup>cut off all thine enemies from before thee, and have made thee a name like the name of the great men that *are* in the earth.

9 Also I will ordain a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, and they shall dwell in their place, and shall be moved no more; neither shall the children of wickedness waste them <sup>p</sup>any more, <sup>q</sup>as at the beginning,

10 And since the time that I commanded judges *to be* over my people Israel. Moreover, I will <sup>r</sup>subdue all thine enemies. Furthermore I tell thee, that the <sup>s</sup>LORD will build thee a house.

11 And it shall come to pass, when thy days be expired, that thou must <sup>t</sup>go *to be* with thy fathers, that I will raise up thy seed <sup>u</sup>after thee, which shall be of thy sons; and I will stablish <sup>x</sup>his kingdom.

12 He <sup>y</sup>shall build me a house, and I will stablish his throne <sup>z</sup>for ever.

13 I will be his <sup>a</sup>father, and he shall be my son; and I will not take my mercy away from him, as I took *it* from *him* that was before thee:

14 But I will settle him in <sup>b</sup>my house and in my kingdom for ever; and his throne shall be established for <sup>c</sup>evermore.

15 According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak unto David.

16 And David the king came and sat before the LORD, and said, <sup>d</sup>Who *am* I, O LORD God, and what *is* my house, that thou hast brought me hitherto?

17 And *yet* this was a small thing in thine eyes, O God; for thou hast *also* spoken of thy servant's house for a great while to come, and hast <sup>e</sup>regarded me according to the estate of a man of high degree, O LORD God.

18 What can David *speak* more to thee for the honour of thy servant? for <sup>f</sup>thou knowest thy servant.

19 O LORD, for thy <sup>g</sup>servant's sake, and <sup>h</sup>according to thine own heart, hast thou done all this greatness, in making known all *these* great things.

20 O LORD, *there is* <sup>i</sup>none like thee, neither *is there any* God besides thee, according to all that we have <sup>k</sup>heard with our ears.

21 And what one nation in the earth <sup>l</sup>*is* like thy people Israel, whom God <sup>m</sup>went to redeem *to be* his own people, to make thee a name of greatness and terribleness, by driving out nations from before thy people, whom thou hast redeemed out of Egypt?

22 For thy people Israel didst thou make <sup>n</sup>thine own people for ever; and thou, LORD, becamest their God.

23 Therefore now, LORD, <sup>o</sup>let the thing that thou hast spoken concerning thy servant, and concerning his house, be established for ever, and do as thou hast said.

24 Let it even be established, that thy name may be magnified for ever, saying, The LORD of hosts *is* the God of Israel, *even* a God to Israel: and *let* the house of David thy servant *be* established before thee.

25 For thou, <sup>p</sup>O my God, <sup>†</sup>hast told thy servant that thou wilt build him a house; therefore thy servant hath found *in his heart* to pray before thee.

26 And now, LORD, thou art God, and hast <sup>q</sup>promised this goodness unto thy servant;

27 Now, therefore, let it please thee to bless the house of thy servant, that it may be before thee for ever: for thou blessest, O LORD, and *it shall be* blessed <sup>r</sup>for ever.
