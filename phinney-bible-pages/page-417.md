## CHAPTER XX.

## CHAPTER XXI.

THE king's <sup>a</sup>heart *is* in the <sup>b</sup>hand of the LORD, *as* the rivers of water; he turneth it whithersoever he will.

2 Every way of a man *is* <sup>c</sup>right in his own eyes: but the LORD pondereth the hearts.

3 To do justice and judgment *is* more acceptable to the LORD than <sup>d</sup>sacrifice.

4 A high look, and a proud heart, *and* the <sup>||</sup>plowing of the wicked, *is* sin.

5 The thoughts of the diligent *tend* only to plenteousness; but of every one *that is* hasty, only to want.

6 The getting of treasures by <sup>e</sup>a lying tongue *is* a vanity tossed to and fro of them that seek death.

7 The robbery of the wicked shall <sup>f</sup>destroy them; because they refuse to do judgment.

8 The way of man *is* froward and strange: but *as for* the <sup>g</sup>pure, his work *is* right.

9 *It is* better to dwell in a corner of the house-top, than with a brawling woman in a wide house.

10 The soul of the wicked <sup>h</sup>desireth evil: his neighbour findeth <sup>i</sup>no favour in his eyes.

11 When the scorner is punished, the simple is <sup>k</sup>made wise; and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge.

12 The righteous *man* <sup>l</sup>wisely considereth the house of the wicked: *but God* overthroweth the wicked for *their* wickedness.

13 Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall <sup>m</sup>cry himself, but shall not be heard.

14 A gift in secret pacifieth anger, and a reward in the bosom strong wrath.

15 *It is* <sup>n</sup>joy to the just to do judgment: but <sup>o</sup>destruction *shall be* to the workers of iniquity.

16 The man that <sup>p</sup>wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead.

17 He that loveth <sup>q</sup>pleasure *shall be* a poor man; he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.

18 The wicked *shall be* <sup>r</sup>a ransom for the righteous, and the transgressor for the upright.

19 *It is* better to dwell in the wilderness, than with a <sup>s</sup>contentious and an angry woman.

20 *There is* <sup>t</sup>treasure to be desired, and oil in the dwelling of the wise: but a foolish man spendeth it up.

21 He that <sup>u</sup>followeth after righteousness and mercy, findeth life, righteousness, and honour.

22 A wise *man* <sup>x</sup>scaleth the city of the mighty, and casteth down the strength of the confidence thereof.
