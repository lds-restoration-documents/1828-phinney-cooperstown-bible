## PSALM LXXXIV.

The Prophet longeth for the communion of the sanctuary.

*To the chief Musician upon Gittith. A Psalm for the sons of Korah.*

HOW <sup>a</sup>amiable *are* thy tabernacles, O LORD of hosts!

2 My soul <sup>b</sup>longeth, yea, even fainteth, for the courts of the LORD; my heart and my flesh crieth out for the living God.

3 Yea, the sparrow hath found a house, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young, *even* thine altars, O LORD of hosts, my King, and my God.

4 <sup>c</sup>Blessed *are* they that dwell in thy house: they will be <sup>d</sup>still praising thee. Selah.

5 Blessed *is* the man whose strength *is* in thee; in whose heart *are* the <sup>e</sup>ways *of them,*

6 *Who* passing through the valley of <sup>||</sup>Baca, make it <sup>f</sup>a well: the <sup>g</sup>rain also filleth the pools.

7 They go from <sup>h</sup>strength to strength; *every one of them* in Zion <sup>i</sup>appeareth before God.

8 O LORD God of hosts, hear my prayer: give ear, O God of Jacob. Selah.

9 Behold, O God, our shield, and look upon the face of <sup>k</sup>thine anointed.

10 For a day in thy courts *is* better than a thousand. I had rather be a door-keeper in the house of my God, than to dwell in the tents of wickedness.

11 For the LORD God *is* <sup>l</sup>a sun and shield; the LORD will give grace and glory: no good *thing* will he withhold from them that walk uprightly.

12 O LORD of hosts, <sup>m</sup>blessed *is* the man that trusteth in thee.

## PSALM LXXXV.

The Psalmist prayeth for the continuance of former mercies.

*To the chief Musician. A Psalm for the sons of Korah.*

LORD, thou hast been favourable unto thy land: thou hast brought back the <sup>a</sup>captivity of Jacob.

2 Thou hast forgiven the iniquity of thy people; thou hast <sup>b</sup>covered all their sin. Selah.

3 Thou hast taken away all thy wrath: thou hast <sup>c</sup>turned *thyself* from the fierceness of thine anger.

4 Turn us, O God of our salvation, and cause thine anger toward us to cease.

5 Wilt thou be angry with us <sup>d</sup>for ever? wilt thou draw out thine anger to all generations?

6 Wilt thou not <sup>e</sup>revive us again, that thy people may rejoice in thee?

7 Shew us <sup>f</sup>thy mercy, O LORD, and grant us thy salvation.

8 I will <sup>g</sup>hear what God the LORD will <sup>h</sup>speak: for he will speak peace unto his people, and to his saints: but let them not <sup>i</sup>turn again to folly.

9 Surely his salvation <sup>k</sup>*is* nigh them that fear him; that <sup>l</sup>glory may dwell in our land.

10 Mercy and truth are <sup>m</sup>met together; righteousness and peace have <sup>n</sup>kissed *each other.*

11 Truth shall spring <sup>o</sup>out of the earth; and righteousness shall <sup>p</sup>look down from heaven.

12 Yea, the LORD shall give *that which is* good: and our <sup>q</sup>land shall yield her increase.

13 Righteousness shall go <sup>r</sup>before him, and shall set *us* in the <sup>s</sup>way of his steps.

## PSALM LXXXVI.

David strengtheneth his prayer by the conscience of his religion.

*A Prayer of David.*

BOW down thine ear, O LORD, hear me; <sup>a</sup>for I *am* poor and needy.

2 Preserve my soul, for I *am* <sup>b</sup>holy: O thou my God, save thy servant that <sup>c</sup>trusteth in thee.

3 Be merciful unto me, O LORD: for I cry unto thee <sup>d</sup>daily.

4 Rejoice the soul of thy servant: for unto thee, O LORD, do <sup>e</sup>I lift up my soul.

5 For thou, LORD, *art* good, and <sup>f</sup>ready to forgive; and <sup>g</sup>plenteous in mercy unto all them that call upon thee.

6 Give ear, O LORD, unto my prayer; and attend to the <sup>h</sup>voice of my supplications.

7 In the <sup>i</sup>day of my trouble I will call upon thee: for thou wilt answer me.

8 Among the gods *there is* <sup>k</sup>none like unto thee, O LORD; neither *are there any works* like unto <sup>l</sup>thy works.

9 <sup>m</sup>All nations whom thou hast made shall come and worship before thee, O LORD; and shall glorify thy name.

10 For thou *art* great, and doest wondrous things: thou *art* <sup>n</sup>God alone.

11 <sup>o</sup>Teach me thy way, O LORD; I will walk in thy truth: <sup>p</sup>unite my heart to fear thy name.

12 I will praise thee, O LORD my God, with all my heart; and I will <sup>q</sup>glorify thy name for evermore.

13 For great *is* thy mercy toward me; and thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest hell.

14 O God, <sup>r</sup>the proud are risen against me, and the assemblies of violent *men* have sought after my soul, and have not set thee before them.

15 But thou, O LORD, *art* a God full of compassion, and gracious; <sup>†</sup>long-suffering, and plenteous in mercy and truth.

16 O turn unto me, and have mercy upon me: give thy strength unto thy servant, and save the <sup>s</sup>son of thy handmaid.

17 <sup>t</sup>Shew me a token <sup>u</sup>for good; that they which hate me may see *it,* and be ashamed; because thou, LORD, hast holpen me, and comforted me.
