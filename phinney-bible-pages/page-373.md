## PSALM XXXVIII.

David moveth God to take compassion of his pitiful case.

*A Psalm of David,* <sup>a</sup>*to bring to remembrance.*

O LORD, rebuke me not in <sup>b</sup>thy wrath; neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.

2 For thine <sup>c</sup>arrows stick fast in me, and <sup>d</sup>thy hand presseth me sore.

3 *There is* <sup>e</sup>no soundness in my flesh because of thine anger; neither *is there any* rest in my bones because of my sin.

4 For mine iniquities are gone over my head; as a <sup>f</sup>heavy burden they are too heavy for me.

5 My wounds stink, *and* are corrupt, because of my foolishness.

6 I am troubled; I am bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day long.

7 For my loins are filled with a <sup>g</sup>loathsome *disease;* and *there is* no soundness in my flesh.

8 I am <sup>h</sup>feeble and sore broken: I have <sup>i</sup>roared by reason of the disquietness of my heart.

9 LORD, <sup>k</sup>all my desire *is* before thee; and my groaning is <sup>l</sup>not hid from thee.

10 My heart <sup>†</sup>panteth, my strength faileth me: as for the light of mine eyes, it also is gone from me.

11 My <sup>m</sup>lovers and my friends stand aloof from my sore; and my kinsmen stand afar off.

12 They also that seek after my life lay snares *for* me; and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and imagine deceits all the day long.

13 But I, <sup>n</sup>as a deaf *man,* heard not; and *I was* <sup>o</sup>as a dumb man *that* openeth not his mouth.

14 Thus I was as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth *are* no reproofs.

15 For in thee, O LORD, do I hope: thou wilt hear, O LORD my God.

16 For I said, *Hear me;* lest *otherwise* they should rejoice over me: when my foot <sup>||</sup>slippeth, they <sup>p</sup>magnify *themselves* against me.

17 For I *am* ready <sup>q</sup>to halt, and my sorrow *is* continually before me.

18 For I will <sup>r</sup>declare mine iniquity; I will be <sup>s</sup>sorry for my sin.

19 But mine enemies *are* lively, *and* they are strong; and they that hate me wrongfully are <sup>t</sup>multiplied.

20 They also that render <sup>u</sup>evil for good are mine adversaries; <sup>x</sup>because I follow *the thing that* good *is.*

21 Forsake me not, O LORD: O <sup>y</sup>my God, be not far from me.

22 Make haste <sup>†</sup>to help me, O LORD my salvation.

## PSALM XXXIX.

David's care of his thoughts, and prayer.

*To the chief Musician, even to* <sup>a</sup>*Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.*

I SAID, I will <sup>b</sup>take heed to my ways, that I sin not with my tongue; I will keep my <sup>†</sup>mouth with a bridle, while the <sup>c</sup>wicked is before me.

2 I was dumb with silence: I held my peace, *even* <sup>d</sup>from good; and my sorrow was stirred.

3 My heart was hot within me; while I was musing the <sup>e</sup>fire burned: <sup>f</sup>*then* spake I with my tongue,

4 LORD, make me to know mine end, and the measure of my days, what it *is; that* I may know how frail I *am.*

5 Behold, thou hast made my days *as* a handbreadth, and mine age *is* <sup>g</sup>as nothing before thee: verily every man at his best state *is* altogether <sup>h</sup>vanity. Selah.

6 Surely every man walketh in <sup>i</sup>a vain shew: surely they are disquieted in vain; he heapeth up *riches,* and knoweth not who shall <sup>k</sup>gather them.

7 And now, LORD, what wait I for? my hope *is* in thee.

8 Deliver me from all my transgressions: make me not the <sup>l</sup>reproach of the foolish.

9 I was <sup>m</sup>dumb, and opened not my mouth; because thou didst *it.*

10 Remove thy stroke away from me: I am consumed by the blow of thy hand.

11 When thou with rebukes dost correct man for iniquity, thou makest his beauty to consume away like a <sup>n</sup>moth: surely every man *is* vanity. Selah.

12 Hear my prayer, O LORD, and give ear unto my cry; hold not thy peace at my tears: for I *am* <sup>o</sup>a stranger with thee, *and* a sojourner, <sup>p</sup>as all my fathers *were.*

13 O spare me, that I may recover strength, before I go hence, and be <sup>q</sup>no more.
