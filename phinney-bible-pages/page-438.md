## CHAPTER XV.

The lamentable state of Moab.

THE burden of <sup>a</sup>Moab. Because in the <sup>b</sup>night Ar of Moab is laid waste, *and* brought to silence; because in the night Kir of Moab is laid waste, *and* brought to silence:

2 He is gone up to Bajith, and to Dibon, the high places, to weep: Moab shall howl over Nebo, and over Medeba; on all their <sup>c</sup>heads *shall be* baldness, *and* every beard cut off.

3 In their streets they shall gird themselves with sackcloth: on the <sup>d</sup>tops of their houses, and in their streets, every one shall howl, weeping abundantly.

4 And Heshbon shall cry, and Elealeh: their voice shall be heard *even* unto Jahaz: therefore the armed soldiers of Moab shall cry out; <sup>e</sup>his life shall be grievous unto him.

5 My heart shall <sup>f</sup>cry out for Moab; his fugitives *shall flee* unto Zoar, a <sup>g</sup>heifer of three years old: for by the mounting up of Luhith with weeping shall they go it up; for in the way of Horonaim they shall raise up a cry of destruction.

6 For the <sup>h</sup>waters of Nimrim shall be desolate: for the hay is withered away, the grass faileth, there is no green thing.

7 Therefore the abundance they have gotten, and that which they have laid up, shall they carry away to the brook of the <sup>i</sup>willows.

8 For the cry is gone round about the borders of Moab; the howling thereof unto Eglaim, and the howling thereof unto Beer-elim.

9 For the waters of Dimon shall be full of <sup>k</sup>blood: for I will bring more upon Dimon, lions upon him that <sup>l</sup>escapeth of Moab, and upon the remnant of the land.

## CHAPTER XVI.

Moab is threatened for her pride.

SEND ye the <sup>a</sup>lamb to the <sup>b</sup>ruler of the land from Sela to the wilderness, unto the mount of the daughter of Zion.

2 For it shall be, *that* as a <sup>c</sup>wandering bird cast out of the nest, *so* the daughters of Moab shall be at the fords of Arnon.

3 Take counsel, execute judgment, make thy shadow as the night in the midst of the noon-day; hide the <sup>d</sup>outcasts, <sup>e</sup>bewray not him that wandereth.

4 Let mine outcasts <sup>f</sup>dwell with thee, Moab: be thou a covert to them from the face of the spoiler: for the extortioner is at an end, the spoiler ceaseth, the oppressors are consumed out of the land.

5 And in mercy shall the throne be established; and he shall <sup>g</sup>sit upon it in truth in the tabernacle of David, judging and seeking <sup>h</sup>judgment, and hasting righteousness.

6 We have heard of the pride of Moab; *he is* <sup>i</sup>very proud: *even* of his haughtiness, and his pride, and his wrath: *but* his <sup>k</sup>lies *shall not be* so.

7 Therefore shall Moab howl for Moab, every one shall howl: for the <sup>l</sup>foundations of Kir-hareseth shall ye mourn; surely *they are* stricken.

8 For the fields of Heshbon <sup>m</sup>languish, *and* the vine of Sibmah: the lords of the heathen have broken down the principal plants thereof, they are come *even* unto Jazer, they wandered *through* the wilderness; her branches are stretched out, they are gone over the sea:

9 Therefore I will <sup>n</sup>bewail with the weeping of Jazer the vine of Sibmah: I will water thee with my tears, O Heshbon, and Elealeh; for the shouting for thy summer-fruits, and for thy harvest, is fallen.

10 And <sup>o</sup>gladness is taken away, and joy out of the plentiful field; and in the vineyards there shall be no singing, neither shall there be shouting: the treaders shall tread out no wine in *their* presses; I have made *their vintage*-shouting to cease.

11 Wherefore my bowels shall sound like a <sup>p</sup>harp for Moab, and mine inward parts for Kir-haresh.

12 And it shall <sup>q</sup>come to pass, when it is seen that Moab is weary on the high place, that he shall come to his sanctuary <sup>r</sup>to pray; but he shall <sup>s</sup>not prevail.

13 This *is* the word that the LORD hath spoken concerning Moab since that time.

14 But now the LORD hath spoken, saying, <sup>t</sup>Within three years, as the years of a <sup>u</sup>hireling, and the glory of Moab shall be contemned, with all that great multitude; and the remnant *shall be* very small *and* feeble.
