9 <sup>s</sup>Awake, awake, <sup>t</sup>put on strength, O arm of the LORD; awake, as in the ancient days, in the generations of old. *Art* thou not it that hath cut Rahab, *and* wounded the <sup>u</sup>dragon?

10 *Art* thou not it which hath <sup>x</sup>dried the sea, the waters of the great deep; that hath made the depths of the sea a way for the ransomed to pass over?

11 Therefore the <sup>y</sup>redeemed of the LORD shall return, and come with singing unto Zion; and everlasting joy *shall be* upon their <sup>z</sup>head: they shall obtain gladness and joy; *and* sorrow and mourning shall flee away.

12 I, *even* I, *am* he that <sup>a</sup>comforteth you: who *art* thou, that thou shouldest be afraid of a man *that* shall die, and of the son of man *which* shall be made *as* <sup>b</sup>grass;

13 And forgettest the LORD thy Maker, that hath stretched forth the heavens, and laid the foundations of the earth; and hast feared continually every day, because of the fury of the oppressor, as if he were ready to destroy? and <sup>c</sup>where *is* the fury of the oppressor?

14 The captive exile hasteneth that he may be loosed, and that he should not die in the <sup>d</sup>pit, nor that his bread should fail.

15 But I *am* the LORD thy God, that <sup>e</sup>divided the sea, whose waves roared: The LORD of hosts *is* his name.

16 And I have <sup>f</sup>put my words in thy mouth, and have covered thee in the shadow of my hand, that I may <sup>g</sup>plant the heavens, and lay the foundations of the earth, and say unto Zion, Thou *art* my people.

17 <sup>h</sup>Awake, awake, stand up, <sup>i</sup>O Jerusalem, which hast drunk at the hand of the LORD the cup of his fury: thou hast drunken the <sup>k</sup>dregs of the cup of trembling, *and* wrung *them* out.

18 *There is* none to <sup>l</sup>guide her among all the sons *whom* she hath brought forth; neither *is there any* that taketh her by the hand, of all the sons *that* she hath brought up.

19 <sup>m</sup>These two *things* are come unto thee; who shall be sorry for thee? desolation, and destruction, and the famine, and the sword: by whom shall I <sup>n</sup>comfort thee?

20 Thy sons have <sup>o</sup>fainted, they lie at the head of all the streets as a wild bull in a net: they are full of the <sup>p</sup>fury of the LORD, the rebuke of thy God.

21 Therefore, hear now this, thou afflicted, and drunken, but <sup>q</sup>not with wine:

22 Thus saith thy Lord, the LORD, and thy God *that* <sup>r</sup>pleadeth the cause of his people, Behold, I have taken <sup>s</sup>out of thy hand the cup of trembling, *even* the dregs of the cup of my fury; thou shalt no more drink it again:

23 But I will put it <sup>t</sup>into the hand of them that afflict thee; which have said to thy soul, <sup>u</sup>Bow down, that we may go over: and thou hast laid thy body as the ground, and as the street, to them that went over.

## CHAPTER LII.

Christ persuadeth the church to believe his free redemption.

AWAKE, awake; <sup>a</sup>put on thy strength, O Zion; put on thy beautiful garments, O Jerusalem, the holy city: for henceforth there shall no more <sup>b</sup>come into thee the uncircumcised and the unclean.

2 Shake thyself from the dust; arise, *and* <sup>c</sup>sit down, O Jerusalem: <sup>d</sup>loose thyself from the bands of thy neck, O captive daughter of Zion.

3 For thus saith the LORD, Ye have <sup>e</sup>sold yourselves for nought; and ye shall be redeemed <sup>f</sup>without money.

4 For thus saith the Lord GOD, My people went down aforetime <sup>g</sup>into Egypt to sojourn there; and the Assyrian oppressed them <sup>h</sup>without cause.

5 Now, therefore, <sup>i</sup>what have I here, saith the LORD, that my people is taken away for nought? They that rule over them make them to howl, saith the LORD, and my name continually every day *is* <sup>k</sup>blasphemed.

6 Therefore my people shall know <sup>l</sup>my name: therefore *they shall know* in that day that <sup>m</sup>I *am* he that doth speak; behold, *it is* I.

7 How beautiful upon the <sup>n</sup>mountains are the feet of him that bringeth <sup>o</sup>good tidings, that publisheth peace; that bringeth good tidings of good, that publisheth salvation; that saith unto Zion, Thy God <sup>p</sup>reigneth!

8 Thy <sup>q</sup>watchmen shall <sup>r</sup>lift up the voice; with the voice together shall they sing: for they shall see <sup>s</sup>eye to eye, when the LORD shall bring again Zion.

9 Break forth into joy, sing together, ye waste places of Jerusalem: for the LORD hath comforted his people, he hath redeemed Jerusalem.

10 The LORD hath made bare his holy arm in the eyes of all the nations; and <sup>t</sup>all the ends of the earth shall see the salvation of our God.

11 <sup>u</sup>Depart ye, depart ye, go ye out from thence, touch no unclean *thing;* go ye out of the midst of her; be ye <sup>x</sup>clean that bear the vessels of the LORD.

12 For ye shall not go out with <sup>y</sup>haste, nor go by flight: for the LORD will <sup>z</sup>go before you; and the God of Israel *will be* your rere-ward.

13 Behold, my servant shall deal <sup>a</sup>prudently, he shall be <sup>b</sup>exalted and extolled, and be very high.

14 As many were <sup>c</sup>astonished at thee; (his visage was so marred more than <sup>d</sup>any man, and his form more than the sons of men;)

15 So shall he <sup>e</sup>sprinkle many nations; the kings shall shut their mouths at him: for *that* which had <sup>f</sup>not been told them shall they see, and *that* which they had not heard shall they consider.

## CHAPTER LIII.

1 The prophet complaineth of the want of faith....4 The sufferings of Christ, &c.

WHO hath believed our report? and to whom is the <sup>a</sup>arm of the LORD revealed?

2 For he shall grow up before him as a tender plant, and as a root out of a dry ground: he hath no form nor <sup>b</sup>comeliness; and when we shall see him, *there is* no beauty that we should desire him.

3 He is despised and <sup>c</sup>rejected of men; a man of sorrows, and <sup>d</sup>acquainted with grief: and we hid as it were *our* faces from him; he was despised, and we <sup>d</sup>esteemed him not.

4 Surely he hath borne our <sup>f</sup>griefs, and carried our sorrows: yet we did esteem him stricken, <sup>g</sup>smitten of God, and afflicted.

5 But he *was* wounded <sup>h</sup>for our transgressions, *he was* bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace *was* upon him; and with his stripes we are healed.

6 All we, like <sup>i</sup>sheep, have gone astray; we have turned every one to his own way; and the LORD hath <sup>k</sup>laid on him the iniquity of us all.

7 He was oppressed, and he was afflicted; yet he <sup>l</sup>opened not his mouth: he is brought as a <sup>m</sup>lamb to the slaughter, and as a sheep before her shearers is dumb, so he openeth not his mouth.
