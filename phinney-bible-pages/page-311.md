2 Forty and two years old *was* Ahaziah when he began to reign; and he reigned <sup>c</sup>one year in Jerusalem. His mother's name also *was* Athaliah the <sup>d</sup>daughter of Omri.

3 <sup>e</sup>He also walked in the ways of the house of Ahab; for <sup>f</sup>his mother was his <sup>g</sup>counsellor to do wickedly.

4 Wherefore he did evil in the sight of the LORD, like the house of Ahab; for they were his counsellors, after the death of his father, to his <sup>h</sup>destruction.

5 He walked also after their <sup>i</sup>counsel, and went with Jehoram, the son of Ahab, king of Israel, to war against Hazael king of Syria at <sup>k</sup>Ramoth-gilead: and the Syrians smote Joram.

6 And he returned to be healed in <sup>l</sup>Jezreel, because of the wounds which were given him at Ramah, when he fought with Hazael king of Syria. And Azariah, the son of Jehoram king of Judah, went down to see Jehoram, the son of Ahab, at Jezreel, because he was sick.

7 And the <sup>†</sup>destruction of Ahaziah <sup>m</sup>was of God, by coming to Joram; for when he was come, he went out with Jehoram against Jehu, the son of Nimshi, whom the LORD had <sup>n</sup>anointed to cut off the house of Ahab.

8 And it came to pass, that when Jehu was <sup>o</sup>executing judgment upon the house of Ahab, and found the princes of Judah, and the sons of the <sup>p</sup>brethren of Ahaziah, that ministered to Ahaziah, <sup>q</sup>he slew them.

9 And <sup>r</sup>he sought Ahaziah: and they caught him, (for he was hid in Samaria,) and brought him to Jehu: and when they had slain him, they buried him; Because, said they, he *is* the son of Jehoshaphat, who <sup>s</sup>sought the LORD with all his heart. So the house of Ahaziah had no power to keep still the kingdom.

10 But when Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, <sup>t</sup>saw that her son was dead, she arose, and destroyed all the seed-royal of the house of Judah.

11 But <sup>u</sup>Jehoshabeath, the daughter of the king, took Joash the son of Ahaziah, and stole him from among the king's sons that were slain, and put him and his nurse in a <sup>†</sup>bed-chamber. So Jehoshabeath, the daughter of king Jehoram, the wife of <sup>x</sup>Jehoiada the priest, (for she was the sister of Ahaziah,) hid him from Athaliah, so that she slew him not.

12 And he was with them <sup>y</sup>hid in the house of God six years: and Athaliah reigned over the land.

## CHAPTER XXIII.

Jehoiada restoreth the worship of God.

AND in the <sup>a</sup>seventh year Jehoiada strengthened himself, and took the captains of hundreds, Azariah the son of Jeroham, and Ishmael the son of Jehohanan, and Azariah the son of Obed, and Maaseiah the son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat the son of Zichri, <sup>b</sup>into covenant with him.

2 And they went about in Judah, and <sup>c</sup>gathered the Levites out of all the cities of Judah, and the chief of the fathers <sup>d</sup>of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.

3 And all the congregation made <sup>e</sup>a covenant with the king in the house of God. And he <sup>f</sup>said unto them, Behold, the king's son shall reign, as the LORD hath said of the sons of David.

4 This *is* the thing that ye shall do; A third part of you entering on the sabbath, of the priests and of the Levites, *shall be* porters of the <sup>†</sup>doors;

5 And a third part *shall be* at the king's house; and a third part at the <sup>g</sup>gate of the foundation: and all the people *shall be* in the courts of the house of the LORD.

6 But let none come into <sup>h</sup>the house of the LORD, save the priests, and they that minister of the Levites; they shall go in, for they <sup>†</sup>*are* holy: but all the people shall keep the watch of the LORD.

7 And the <sup>i</sup>Levites shall compass the king round about, every man with his weapons in his hand; and whosoever *else* cometh into the house, he shall be put to death; but be you with the king when he cometh in, and when he goeth out.

8 So the Levites, and all Judah, did according to all things that Jehoiada the priest had commanded, and <sup>k</sup>took every man his men that were to come in on the sabbath, with them that were to go *out* on the sabbath: for Jehoiada the priest dismissed not the <sup>||</sup>courses.

9 Moreover, Jehoiada the priest delivered to the captains of hundreds spears, and bucklers, and shields, that *had been* <sup>l</sup>king David's, which *were* in the house of God.

10 And he set all the people, (every man having his weapon in his hand,) from the right side of the temple to the left side of the temple, along by the altar and the temple, by the king round about.

11 Then they <sup>m</sup>brought out the king's son, and put upon him <sup>n</sup>the crown, and *gave him* the <sup>o</sup>testimony, and made him king: and Jehoiada and his sons anointed him, and said, <sup>†</sup>God save the king.

12 Now when Athaliah heard the noise of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people into the house of the LORD.

13 And she looked, and, behold, the king stood at his <sup>p</sup>pillar at the entering in, and the princes and the trumpets by the king: and all the people of the land rejoiced, and <sup>q</sup>sounded with trumpets; also the singers with instruments of music, and such as taught to sing praise. Then Athaliah rent her clothes, and said, <sup>r</sup>Treason, treason!

14 Then Jehoiada the priest brought out the captains of hundreds that were set over the host, and said unto them, Have her forth of the ranges: and whoso <sup>s</sup>followeth her, let him be slain with the sword. For the priest said, Slay her not in the house of the LORD.

15 So they laid hands on her: and when she was come to the entering of the horse-gate, by the king's house, they <sup>t</sup>slew her there.

18 Also Jehoiada appointed the offices of the house of the LORD by the hand of the priests the Levites, whom David had <sup>a</sup>distributed in the house of the LORD, to offer the burnt-offerings of the LORD, as *it is* written in the <sup>b</sup>law of Moses, with rejoicing and with singing *as it was ordained* by <sup>c</sup>David.

19 And he set the <sup>d</sup>porters at the gates of the house of the LORD, that none *which was* unclean in any thing should enter in.
