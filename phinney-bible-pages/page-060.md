1 God instructeth the Israelites in their journey....5 Pharaoh pursueth after them....21 The Israelites pass through the Red Sea, 23 which drowneth the Egyptians.

AND the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2 Speak unto the children of Israel, that they turn and encamp <sup>a</sup>before Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, over against <sup>p</sup>Baal-zephon: before it shall ye encamp by the sea.

3 For Pharaoh will say of the children of Israel, They *are* <sup>c</sup>entangled in the land, the wilderness hath shut them in.

4 And I will <sup>d</sup>harden Pharaoh's heart that he shall follow after them; and I will <sup>e</sup>be honoured upon Pharaoh, and upon all his host; that the Egyptians may know that I *am* the LORD. And they did so.

5 And it was told the king of Egypt that the people <sup>f</sup>fled: and the heart of Pharaoh and of his servants was turned against the people, and they said, <sup>g</sup>Why have we done this, that we have let Israel go from serving us?

6 And he made ready his chariot, and took his people with him:

7 And he took six hundred <sup>h</sup>chosen chariots, and all the chariots of Egypt, and captains over every one of them.

8 And the LORD hardened the heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he pursued after the children of Israel: and the children of Israel went out with a <sup>i</sup>high hand.

9 But the Egyptians <sup>k</sup>pursued after them, (all the horses *and* chariots of Pharaoh, and his horsemen, and his army,) and overtook them encamping by the sea, beside Pi-hahiroth, before Baal-zephon.

10 And when Pharaoh drew nigh, the children of Israel lift up their eyes, and, behold, the Egyptians marched after them; and they were <sup>l</sup>sore afraid: and the children of Israel cried out unto the LORD.

11 And they said unto Moses, Because *there were* <sup>m</sup>no graves in Egypt, hast thou taken us away to die in the wilderness? wherefore hast thou dealt thus with us, to carry us forth out of Egypt?

12 *Is* not this the word that we did tell thee in Egypt, saying, <sup>n</sup>Let us alone, that we may serve the Egyptians? for *it had been* better for us to serve the Egyptians, than that we should die in the wilderness.

13 And Moses said unto the people, <sup>o</sup>Fear ye not, <sup>p</sup>stand still, and see <sup>q</sup>the salvation of the LORD, which he will shew to you to-day: for the Egyptians whom ye have seen to-day, ye shall <sup>r</sup>see them again no more for ever:

14 The LORD shall <sup>s</sup>fight for you, and ye shall <sup>t</sup>hold your peace.

15 ¶ And the LORD said unto Moses, Wherefore <sup>u</sup>criest thou unto me? speak unto the children of Israel, that they go forward:

16 But lift thou up thy rod, and stretch out thine hand over the sea, and divide it; and the children of Israel shall go on dry *ground* through the midst of the sea.

17 And I, behold, I will harden the hearts of the Egyptians, and they shall follow them: and I will get me honour upon Pharaoh, and upon all his host, upon his chariots, and upon his horsemen.

18 And the Egyptians shall <sup>x</sup>know that I *am* the LORD, when I have gotten me honour upon Pharaoh, upon his chariots, and upon his horsemen.

19 ¶ And the <sup>y</sup>angel of God, which went before the camp of Israel, removed, and went behind them; and the pillar of the cloud went from before their face, and stood behind them:

20 And it came between the camp of the Egyptians and the camp of Israel; and it was a <sup>z</sup>cloud and darkness *to them*, but it gave light by night *to these:* so that the one came not near the other all the night.

21 And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea; and the LORD caused the sea <sup>a</sup>to go *back* by a strong <sup>b</sup>east wind all that night, and made the sea <sup>c</sup>dry *land*, and the waters were <sup>d</sup>divided.

22 And the children of Israel went into <sup>e</sup>the midst of the sea upon the dry *ground:* and the waters *were* <sup>f</sup>a wall unto them on their right hand and on their left.

23 And the Egyptians pursued, and went in after them to the midst of the sea, *even* all Pharaoh's horses, his chariots, and his horsemen.

24 And it came to pass, that in the <sup>g</sup>morning-watch the LORD <sup>h</sup>looked unto the host of the Egyptians through the <sup>i</sup>pillar of fire and of the cloud, and <sup>k</sup>troubled the host of the Egyptians,

25 And took off their chariot-wheels, that they drave them <sup>†</sup>heavily: so that the Egyptians said, Let us flee from the face of Israel; for the LORD fighteth for them against the Egyptians.

26 And the LORD said unto Moses, <sup>l</sup>Stretch out thine hand over the sea, that the waters may <sup>m</sup>come again upon the Egyptians, upon their chariots, and upon their horsemen.

27 And Moses stretched forth his hand over the sea, and the sea returned to his strength when <sup>n</sup>the morning appeared; and the Egyptians fled against it; and the LORD <sup>o</sup>overthrew the Egyptians in the midst of the sea.

28 And the waters returned, and covered the chariots, and the horsemen, *and* all the host of Pharaoh that came into the sea after them: there remained <sup>p</sup>not so much as one of them.

29 But the children of Israel <sup>q</sup>walked upon dry *land* in the midst of the sea; and the waters *were* a wall unto them on their right hand, and on their left.

30 Thus the LORD saved Israel that day out of the hand of the Egyptians; and Israel <sup>r</sup>saw the Egyptians dead upon the sea-shore.

31 And Israel saw that great work which the LORD did upon the Egyptians; and the people feared the LORD, and <sup>s</sup>believed the LORD, and his servant Moses.
