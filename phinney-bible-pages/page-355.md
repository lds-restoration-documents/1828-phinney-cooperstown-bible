## CHAPTER XXVII.

The blessings which the wicked have are turned into curses.

MOREOVER, <sup>a</sup>Job continued his parable, and said,

2 *As* God liveth, *who* hath <sup>b</sup>taken away my judgment; and the Almighty, *who* hath vexed my soul;

3 All the while my breath *is* in me, and the <sup>c</sup>spirit of God *is* in my nostrils,

4 My lips shall not speak <sup>d</sup>wickedness, nor my tongue utter deceit.

5 God forbid that I should <sup>e</sup>justify you: till I die I will <sup>f</sup>not remove mine integrity from me.

6 My righteousness <sup>g</sup>I hold fast, and will not let it go: <sup>h</sup>my heart shall not reproach *me* so long as I live.

7 <sup>i</sup>Let mine enemy be as the wicked, and he that riseth up against me as the unrighteous.

8 For what *is* the hope of the hypocrite, though he hath gained, when God <sup>k</sup>taketh away his soul?

9 Will God hear his cry when <sup>l</sup>trouble cometh upon him?

10 Will he delight himself in the Almighty? will he <sup>m</sup>always call upon God?

11 <sup>n</sup>I will teach you by the hand of God: *that* which *is* with the Almighty will I not conceal.

12 Behold, all ye yourselves have seen *it;* why then are ye thus altogether <sup>o</sup>vain?

13 This *is* the portion of a wicked man with God, and the heritage of <sup>p</sup>oppressors, *which* they shall receive of the Almighty.

14 If his children be multiplied, *it is* for the <sup>q</sup>sword; and his offspring shall not be satisfied with bread.

15 Those that remain of him shall be <sup>r</sup>buried in death; and his widows shall <sup>s</sup>not weep.

16 Though he <sup>t</sup>heap up silver as the dust, and prepare raiment as the clay;

17 He may prepare *it,* but <sup>u</sup>the just shall put *it* on, and the innocent shall divide the silver.

18 He buildeth his house <sup>x</sup>as a moth, and as a booth *that* the keeper maketh.

19 The rich man shall lie down, but he shall <sup>y</sup>not be gathered: <sup>z</sup>he openeth his eyes, and he *is* not.

20 Terrors take hold on him as waters, a tempest stealeth him away <sup>a</sup>in the night.

21 The east wind carrieth him away, and he departeth; and, as a storm, <sup>b</sup>hurleth him out of his place.

22 For *God* shall cast upon him, and not spare: he would fain <sup>c</sup>flee out of his hand.

23 *Men* shall <sup>d</sup>clap their hands at him, and shall hiss him out of his place.

## CHAPTER XXVIII.

There is a knowledge of natural things.

SURELY there is a vein for the silver, and a place for gold *where* they <sup>a</sup>fine *it.*

2 Iron is taken out of the earth, and <sup>b</sup>brass *is* molten *out of* the stone.

3 He setteth <sup>c</sup>an end to darkness, and searcheth out all <sup>d</sup>perfection: the stones of darkness, and the shadow of death.

4 The flood breaketh out from the inhabitant; *even the waters* forgotten of the foot: they are dried up, they are gone away from men.

5 *As for* the earth, out of it cometh <sup>e</sup>bread; and under it is turned up as it were fire.

6 The stones of it *are* the place of <sup>f</sup>sapphires; and it hath dust of gold.

7 *There is* <sup>g</sup>a path which no fowl knoweth, and which the vulture's eye hath not seen:

8 The lion's whelps have not trodden it, nor the fierce lion passed by it.

9 He putteth forth his hand upon the rock; he overturneth the mountains by the roots.

10 He <sup>h</sup>cutteth out rivers among the rocks; and his eye <sup>i</sup>seeth every precious thing.

11 He bindeth the floods from overflowing; and *the thing that is* <sup>k</sup>hid bringeth he forth to light.

12 But <sup>l</sup>where shall wisdom be found? and where *is* the <sup>m</sup>place of understanding?

13 Man knoweth not the price thereof; neither is it found in the <sup>n</sup>land of the living.

14 The <sup>o</sup>depth saith, *It is* not in me: and the sea saith, *It is* not with me.

15 It cannot be gotten for <sup>p</sup>gold, neither shall silver be weighed *for* the price thereof.

16 It cannot be valued with the gold of Ophir, with the precious <sup>q</sup>onyx, or the sapphire.

17 The gold and the crystal cannot equal it: and the exchange of it *shall not be for* <sup>r</sup>jewels of fine gold.

18 No mention shall be made of coral, or of <sup>s</sup>pearls: for the price of wisdom *is* above rubies.

19 The topaz of Ethiopia shall not equal it, neither shall it be valued with pure gold.

20 Whence then cometh wisdom? and where *is* the place of understanding?

21 Seeing it is <sup>t</sup>hid from the eyes of all living, and kept close from the fowls of the air.

22 Destruction and death say, We have heard the fame thereof with our ears.

23 <sup>u</sup>God understandeth the way thereof, and he knoweth the place thereof.

24 For he looketh to the ends of the earth, *and* <sup>x</sup>seeth under the whole heaven;

25 To make the <sup>y</sup>weight for the winds; and he weigheth the waters by measure.

26 When he made <sup>z</sup>a decree for the rain, and a way for the lightning of the thunder;

27 Then did he see it, and <sup>a</sup>declare it; he prepared it, yea, and searched it out.

28 And <sup>b</sup>unto man he said, Behold, the <sup>c</sup>fear of the LORD, that *is* wisdom; and to depart from evil *is* understanding.

## CHAPTER XXIX.

Job bemoaneth himself of his former prosperity.

MOREOVER Job <sup>a</sup>continued his parable, and said,

2 Oh that I were as *in* <sup>b</sup>months past, as *in* the days *when* God preserved me;

3 When his candle shined upon my head, *and when* by his light I walked *through* <sup>c</sup>darkness;

4 As I was in the days of my youth when the <sup>d</sup>secret of God *was* upon my tabernacle;

5 When the Almighty *was* yet <sup>e</sup>with me, *when* my children *were* about me;

6 When I <sup>f</sup>washed my steps with butter, and <sup>g</sup>the rock poured me out rivers of oil;

7 When I <sup>h</sup>went out to the gate, through the city, *when* I prepared my seat in the street;

8 The young men saw me, and <sup>i</sup>hid themselves: and the aged arose, *and* stood up.

9 The princes <sup>k</sup>refrained talking, and <sup>l</sup>laid *their* hand on their mouth.

10 The nobles held their peace, and their tongue cleaved to the roof of their mouth.

11 When the ear heard *me,* then it <sup>m</sup>blessed me; and when the eye saw *me,* <sup>n</sup>it gave witness to me:
