## CHAPTER V.

The line of Reuben unto the captivity.

NOW the sons of Reuben the first-born of Israel, (for he *was* the first-born; but forasmuch as he <sup>a</sup>defiled his father's bed, his <sup>b</sup>birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph the son of Israel: and the genealogy is not to be reckoned after the birthright.

2 For Judah <sup>c</sup>prevailed above his brethren, and of him *came* the <sup>||</sup>chief ruler; but the <sup>d</sup>birthright *was* Joseph's;)

3 The sons, *I say*, of <sup>e</sup>Reuben the first-born of Israel, *were*, Hanoch, and Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

4 The sons of Joel; Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,

5 Micah his son, Reaia his son, Baal his son,

6 Beerah his son, whom <sup>f</sup>Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria carried away *captive*: he *was* prince of the Reubenites.

7 And his brethren by their families, (when the genealogy of their generations was reckoned,) *were* the chief, Jeiel, and Zechariah,

8 And Bela the son of Azaz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel, who dwelt in <sup>g</sup>Aroer, even unto <sup>h</sup>Nebo and Baal-meon:

9 And eastward he inhabited unto the entering in of the wilderness from the river Euphrates; because their <sup>i</sup>cattle were multiplied in the land of Gilead.

10 And in the days of Saul they made war with the <sup>k</sup>Hagarites, who fell by their hand: and they dwelt in their tents <sup>†</sup>throughout all the east *land* of Gilead.

11 And the children of Gad <sup>||</sup>dwelt over against them, in the land of <sup>l</sup>Bashan, unto <sup>m</sup>Salcah:

12 Joel the chief, and Shapham the next, and Jaanai, and Shaphat in <sup>n</sup>Bashan.

13 And their brethren of the house of their fathers *were* Michael, and Meshullam, and Sheba, and Jorai, and Jachan, and Zia, and Heber, seven.

14 These *are* the children of Abihail the son of Huri, the son of Jaroah, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Jeshishai, the son of Jahdo, the son of Buz;

15 Ahi the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, chief of the house of their fathers.

16 And they dwelt in Gilead in <sup>o</sup>Bashan, and in her towns, and in all the suburbs of <sup>p</sup>Sharon, upon their borders.

17 All these were reckoned by genealogies in the days of <sup>q</sup>Jotham king of Judah, and in the days of <sup>r</sup>Jeroboam king of Israel.

18 The sons of Reuben, and the Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh, of valiant men, men able to bear buckler and sword, and to shoot with bow, and skilful in war, *were* <sup>s</sup>four and forty thousand seven hundred and threescore, that went out to the war.

19 And they made war with the Hagarites, with <sup>t</sup>Jetur, and Nephish, and Nodab.

20 And they were <sup>u</sup>helped against them, and the Hagarites were delivered into their hand, and all that *were* with them: for they <sup>x</sup>cried to God in the battle, and he was entreated of them; because they <sup>y</sup>put their trust in him.

21 And they took away their cattle; of their camels fifty thousand, and of sheep two hundred and fifty thousand, and of <sup>z</sup>asses two thousand, and of men a hundred thousand.

22 For there fell down many slain, because the <sup>a</sup>war *was* of God. And they dwelt <sup>b</sup>in their steads <sup>c</sup>until the captivity.

23 And the children of the half tribe of Manasseh dwelt in the land: they increased from Bashan unto Baal-hermon, and <sup>d</sup>Senir, and unto mount Hermon.

24 And these *were* the heads of the house of their fathers, even Epher, and Ishi, and Eliel, and Azriel, and Jeremiah, and Hodaviah, and Jahdiel, mighty men of valour, <sup>†</sup>famous men, *and* heads of the house of their fathers.

25 And they transgressed against the gods of their fathers, and went a <sup>e</sup>whoring after the gods of the people of the land, whom God destroyed before them.

26 And the God of Israel <sup>f</sup>stirred up the spirit of <sup>g</sup>Pul king of Assyria, and the spirit of Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria, and he carried them away, (even the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh,) and brought them unto <sup>h</sup>Halah, and Habor, and Hara, and to the river Gozan, unto this day.

## CHAPTER VI.

1 The sons of Levi....4 The line of the priests.

THE sons of Levi; <sup>a</sup>Gershon, Kohath, and <sup>b</sup>Merari.

2 And the sons of Kohath; Amram, Izhar, and Hebron, and Uzziel.

3 And the children of Amram; <sup>c</sup>Aaron, and Moses, and <sup>d</sup>Miriam. The sons also of Aaron; Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

4 ¶ <sup>e</sup>Eleazar begat <sup>f</sup>Phinehas, Phinehas begat Abishua,

5 And Abishua begat Bukki, and Bukki begat Uzzi,

6 And Uzzi begat Zerahiah, and Zerahiah begat Meraioth,

7 Meraioth begat Amariah, and Amariah begat <sup>g</sup>Ahitub,

8 And Ahitub begat Zadok, and Zadok begat Ahimaaz,

9 And Ahimaaz begat Azariah, and Azariah begat Johanan,

10 And <sup>h</sup>Johanan begat Azariah, (he *it is* that executed the priest's office <sup>†</sup>in the temple that Solomon built in Jerusalem,)

11 And Azariah begat Amariah, and Amariah begat Ahitub,

12 And Ahitub begat Zadok, and Zadok begat Shallum,

13 And Shallum begat <sup>i</sup>Hilkiah, and Hilkiah begat Azariah,

14 And Azariah begat <sup>k</sup>Seraiah, and Seraiah begat Jehozadak,

15 And <sup>l</sup>Jehozadak went *into captivity*, when the LORD carried away Judah and Jerusalem <sup>m</sup>by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.

16 ¶ The sons of Levi; Gershom, Kohath, and Merari.

17 And these *be* the names of the sons of Gershom; <sup>n</sup>Libni, and Shimei.
