**Header:** *Zion exhorted to rejoice.* | ZECHARIAH. | *God to be sought unto, &c.*

22 Yea, <sup>i</sup>many people and strong nations shall come to seek the LORD of hosts in Jerusalem, and to pray before the LORD.

23 Thus saith the LORD of hosts, In those days *it shall come to pass,* that ten men shall take hold out of all languages of the nations, even shall take hold of the <sup>k</sup>skirt of him that is a Jew, saying, We will go with you; for <sup>l</sup>we have heard *that* God *is* with you.

## CHAPTER IX.

1 God defendeth his church....9 Zion is exhorted to rejoice for the coming of Christ.

THE <sup>a</sup>burden of the word of the LORD in the land of Hadrach, and Damascus *shall be* the rest thereof: when the eyes of man, as of all the tribes of Israel, *shall be* <sup>b</sup>toward the LORD.

2 And Hamath also shall border thereby; Tyrus, and Zidon, though it be <sup>c</sup>very wise.

3 And Tyrus did build herself a <sup>d</sup>strong hold, and heaped up silver <sup>e</sup>as the dust, and fine gold as the mire of the streets.

4 Behold, the LORD will <sup>f</sup>cast her out, and he will smite her power in the sea; and she shall be devoured with fire.

5 Ashkelon *shall see it,* and fear; Gaza also *shall see it,* and be <sup>g</sup>very sorrowful, and Ekron; for her expectation shall be ashamed; and the king shall perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon shall not be inhabited.

6 And <sup>h</sup>a bastard shall dwell in Ashdod; and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.

7 And I will <sup>i</sup>take away his blood out of his mouth, and his abominations from between his teeth: but he that remaineth, even he, *shall be* <sup>k</sup>for our God; and he shall be as a governor in Judah, and Ekron as a Jebusite.

8 And I will <sup>l</sup>encamp about my house because of the army, because of him that passeth by, and because of him that returneth; and no <sup>m</sup>oppressor shall pass through them any more: for now have I seen with mine eyes.

9 ¶ Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion; shout, O daughter of Jerusalem: behold, <sup>n</sup>thy King cometh <sup>o</sup>unto thee; he *is* just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass.

10 And I will cut off the <sup>p</sup>chariot from Ephraim, and the horse from Jerusalem, and the battle-bow shall be cut off: and he shall speak <sup>q</sup>peace unto the heathen; and his dominion *shall be* from sea *even* to sea, and from the river *even* to the ends of the earth.

11 As for thee also, by the <sup>r</sup>blood of thy covenant I have sent forth thy prisoners out of the pit wherein *is* no water.

12 ¶ Turn ye to the <sup>s</sup>strong hold, ye prisoners of hope: even to-day do I declare, *that* I will render double unto thee;

13 When I have bent Judah for me, filled <sup>t</sup>the bow with Ephraim, and raised up thy sons, O Zion, against thy sons, O Greece, and <sup>u</sup>made thee as the sword of a mighty man.

14 And the LORD shall be <sup>x</sup>seen over them, and his <sup>y</sup>arrow shall go forth as the lightning: and the Lord GOD shall blow the trumpet, and shall go with whirlwinds of the south.

15 The LORD of hosts shall defend them; and they shall <sup>z</sup>devour, and subdue with sling-stones; and they shall drink, *and* make a noise as through wine; and they shall be filled like bowls, *and* as the <sup>a</sup>corners of the altar.

16 And the LORD their God shall save them in that day as the <sup>b</sup>flock of his people: for *they shall be as* the stones of a crown, lifted up as an ensign upon his land.

17 For <sup>c</sup>how great *is* his goodness, and how great *is* his beauty! Corn shall make the young men <sup>d</sup>cheerful, and new wine the maids.

## CHAPTER X.

1 God is to be sought unto, and not idols....5 As he visited his flock for sin, so he will save and restore them.

ASK ye of the LORD rain in the time of the <sup>a</sup>latter rain; so the LORD shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field.

2 For the idols have spoken <sup>b</sup>vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie, and have told false dreams; they comfort in vain: therefore they went their way as a flock, they were troubled, because *there was* no <sup>c</sup>shepherd

3 Mine anger was kindled against the <sup>d</sup>shepherds, and I punished the <sup>e</sup>goats: for the LORD of hosts hath visited his flock the house of Judah, and hath made them as his goodly horse in the battle.

4 Out of him came forth the <sup>f</sup>corner, out of him the <sup>g</sup>nail, out of him the battle-bow, out of him every oppressor together.

5 And they shall be as <sup>h</sup>mighty *men,* which tread down *their enemies* in the mire of the streets in the battle: and they shall fight, because the LORD *is* <sup>i</sup>with them, and the riders on horses shall be confounded.

6 And I will strengthen the house of Judah, and I will save the house of Joseph, and I will <sup>k</sup>bring them again to place them: for I <sup>l</sup>have mercy upon them; and they shall be as though I had not cast them off: for I *am* the LORD their God, and will hear them.

7 And *they of* Ephraim shall be like a <sup>m</sup>mighty *man,* and their heart shall rejoice as <sup>n</sup>through wine: yea, their children shall see *it* and be glad; their heart shall rejoice in the LORD.

8 I will <sup>o</sup>hiss for them, and gather them; for I have redeemed them: and they shall <sup>p</sup>increase as they have increased.

9 And I will <sup>q</sup>sow them among the people: and they shall remember me in far countries: and they shall <sup>r</sup>live with their children, and turn again.

10 I will bring them again also out of the land of <sup>s</sup>Egypt, and gather them out of Assyria; and I will bring them into the land of Gilead and Lebanon, and *place* shall <sup>t</sup>not be found for them.

11 And he shall pass through the sea with affliction, and shall <sup>u</sup>smite the waves in the sea, and all the deeps of the river shall dry up; and the pride of Assyria shall be brought down, and the sceptre of Egypt shall depart away.

12 And I will strengthen them <sup>x</sup>in the LORD; and they shall walk up and down in his <sup>y</sup>name, saith the LORD.

## CHAPTER XI.

1 The destruction of Jerusalem....4 By a type is shewed Christ's care of his flock.

OPEN thy doors, O <sup>a</sup>Lebanon, that <sup>b</sup>the fire may devour thy cedars.

2 Howl, fir-tree; for the cedar is fallen; because the mighty are spoiled: howl, O ye oaks of Bashan; for the forest of the vintage is come down.
