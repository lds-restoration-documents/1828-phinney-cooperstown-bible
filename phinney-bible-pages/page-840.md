3 <sup>b</sup>But in those *sacrifices there is* a remembrance again *made* of sins every year.

4 <sup>c</sup>For *it is* not possible that the blood of bulls and of goats should take away sins.

5 <sup>d</sup>Wherefore, when he cometh into the world, he saith, Sacrifice and offering thou wouldest not, but a body hast thou prepared me:

6 In burnt-offerings and *sacrifices* for sin thou hast had no pleasure:

7 <sup>e</sup>Then said I, Lo, I come (in the volume of the book it is written of me) to do thy will, O God.

8 Above, when he said, Sacrifice, and offering, and burnt-offerings, and *offering* for sin, thou wouldest not, neither hadst pleasure *therein;* which are offered by the law;

9 Then said he, Lo, I come to do thy will, O God. He taketh away the first that he may establish the second.

10 <sup>f</sup>By the which will we are sanctified, through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once *for all.*

11 <sup>g</sup>And every priest standeth daily ministering, and offering oftentimes the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins:

12 <sup>h</sup>But this man, after he had offered one sacrifice for sins, for ever sat down on the right hand of God;

13 From henceforth expecting till his enemies be made his footstool.

14 <sup>i</sup>For by one offering he hath perfected for ever them that are sanctified.

15 *Whereof* the Holy Ghost also is a witness to us: for after that he had said before,

16 <sup>k</sup>This *is* the covenant <sup>†</sup>that I will make with them after those days, saith the Lord; I will put my laws into their hearts, and in their minds will I write them;

17 And their sins and iniquities will I remember no more.

18 Now where remission of these *is, there is* no more offering for sin.

19 <sup>l</sup>Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the Holiest by the blood of Jesus,

20 <sup>m</sup>By a new and living way which he hath consecrated for us, through the vail, that is to say, his flesh;

21 <sup>n</sup>And *having* a high priest over the house of God;

22 <sup>o</sup>Let us draw near with a true heart, in full assurance of faith, having our hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience, and our bodies washed with pure water.

23 <sup>p</sup>Let us hold fast the profession of *our* faith without wavering; (for he *is* faithful that promised;)

24 <sup>q</sup>And let us consider one another, to provoke unto love, and to good works:

25 <sup>r</sup>Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some *is;* but exhorting *one another:* and so much the more as ye see the day approaching.

26 <sup>s</sup>For if we sin wilfully after that we have received the knowledge of the truth, there remaineth no more sacrifice for sins,

27 <sup>t</sup>But a certain fearful looking for of judgment, and fiery indignation, which shall devour the adversaries.

28 <sup>u</sup>He that despised Moses' law died without mercy under two or three witnesses:

29 <sup>x</sup>Of how much sorer punishment, suppose ye, shall he be thought worthy, who hath trodden under foot the Son of God, and hath counted the blood of the covenant, wherewith he was sanctified, an unholy thing, and hath done despite unto the Spirit of grace?

30 <sup>y</sup>For we know him that hath said, Vengeance *belongeth* unto me, I will recompense, saith the Lord. And again, The Lord shall judge his people.

31 <sup>z</sup>*It is* a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.

32 <sup>a</sup>But call to remembrance the former days, in which, after ye were illuminated, ye endured a great fight of afflictions;

33 <sup>b</sup>Partly, whilst ye were made a gazing-stock, both by reproaches and afflictions: and partly, whilst ye became companions of them that were so used.

34 <sup>c</sup>For ye had compassion of me in my bonds, and took joyfully the spoiling of your goods, knowing in yourselves that ye have in heaven a better and an enduring substance.

35 <sup>d</sup>Cast not away therefore your confidence, which hath great recompense of reward.

36 <sup>e</sup>For ye have need of patience; that, after ye have done the will of God, ye might receive the promise.

37 For yet a little while, and he that shall come will come, and will not tarry.

38 Now the just shall live by faith: but if *any man* draw back, my soul shall have no pleasure in him.

39 <sup>f</sup>But we are not of them who draw back unto perdition, but of them that believe to the saving of the soul.

## CHAPTER XI.

1 The nature of faith....6 Without faith we cannot please God: the fruits of faith.

<sup>a</sup>NOW faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.

2 For by it the elders obtained a good report.

3 <sup>b</sup>Through faith we understand that the worlds were framed by the word of God, so that things which are seen were not made of things which do appear.

4 <sup>c</sup>By faith Abel offered unto God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain, by which he obtained witness that he was righteous, God testifying of his gifts: and by it he, being dead, yet speaketh.

5 <sup>d</sup>By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death; and was not found, because God had translated him: for before his translation he had this testimony, that he pleased God.

6 <sup>e</sup>But without faith *it is* impossible to please *him:* for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and *that* he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.

7 <sup>f</sup>By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith.

8 <sup>g</sup>By faith Abraham, when he was called to go out into a place which he should after receive for an inheritance, obeyed; and he went out, not knowing whither he went.

9 <sup>h</sup>By faith he sojourned in the land of promise, as *in* a strange country, dwelling in tabernacles with Isaac and Jacob, the heirs with him of the same promise:

10 <sup>i</sup>For he looked for a city which hath foundations, whose builder and maker *is* God.
