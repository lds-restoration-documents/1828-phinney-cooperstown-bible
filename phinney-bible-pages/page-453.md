**Header:** *The vanity of idols.* | CHAPTER XLV. | *God calleth Cyrus.*

6 Thus saith the LORD the King of Israel, and his Redeemer the LORD of hosts; I *am* the <sup>i</sup>first, and I *am* the last; and besides me *there is* no God.

7 And who, as I, shall <sup>k</sup>call, and shall declare it, and set it in order for me, since I appointed the <sup>l</sup>ancient people? and the things that are coming, and shall come, let them shew unto them.

8 Fear ye not, neither be afraid: have not I told thee from that time, and have declared *it?* ye *are* even my witnesses. Is there a God besides me? yea, *there is* <sup>†</sup>no God; I know not *any.*

9 They that make a graven image *are* all of them vanity; and their delectable <sup>m</sup>things shall <sup>n</sup>not profit: and they *are* their own witnesses; they see not, nor know; that they may be ashamed.

10 Who hath formed a god, or molten a graven image *that* is <sup>o</sup>profitable for nothing?

11 Behold, all his fellows shall be ashamed; and the workmen, they *are* of men: let them all be gathered together, let them stand up; *yet* they shall fear, *and* they shall be <sup>p</sup>ashamed together.

12 The smith with the tongs both worketh in the coals, and fashioneth it with hammers, and worketh it with the strength of his arms: yea, <sup>q</sup>he is hungry, and his strength faileth; he drinketh no water, and is faint.

13 The carpenter stretcheth out *his* rule, he marketh it out with a line, he fitteth it with planes, and he marketh it out with the compass, and maketh it after the figure of a man, according to the beauty of a man; that it may remain in the <sup>r</sup>house.

14 He heweth him down cedars, and taketh the cypress and the oak, which he <sup>s</sup>strengtheneth for himself among the trees of the forest: he planteth an ash, and the rain doth nourish *it.*

15 Then shall it be for a man to burn: for he will take thereof and <sup>t</sup>warm himself; yea, he kindleth *it,* and baketh bread; yea, he <sup>u</sup>maketh a god, and worshippeth *it:* he maketh it a graven image, and falleth down thereto.

16 He burneth part thereof in the fire: with part thereof he eateth flesh; he roasteth roast, and is satisfied; yea, he warmeth *himself,* and saith, Aha, I am warm, I have seen the fire:

17 And the residue thereof he maketh a god, *even* his graven image: he falleth down unto it, and worshippeth *it,* and prayeth unto it, and saith, Deliver me; for thou *art* my god.

18 They have <sup>x</sup>not known nor understood: for he hath <sup>†</sup>shut their eyes, that they cannot see; *and* their hearts, that they cannot understand.

19 And none considereth in his heart, neither *is there* knowledge nor understanding to say, I have burnt part of it in the fire; yea, also I have baked bread upon the coals thereof; I have roasted flesh, and eaten *it;* and shall I make the residue thereof an abomination? shall I fall down to the stock of a tree?

20 He feedeth on ashes: <sup>y</sup>a deceived heart hath turned him aside, that he cannot deliver his soul, nor say, *Is there* not <sup>z</sup>a lie in my right hand?

21 <sup>a</sup>Remember these, O Jacob and Israel; for thou *art* my servant: I have formed thee; thou *art* my servant: O Israel, thou shalt not be forgotten of me.

22 I have blotted out, as <sup>b</sup>a thick cloud, thy transgressions, and, as a cloud, thy sins: return unto me; for I have <sup>c</sup>redeemed thee.

23 <sup>d</sup>Sing, O ye heavens; for the LORD hath done *it:* shout, ye lower parts of the <sup>e</sup>earth: break forth into singing, ye mountains, O forest, and every tree therein: for the LORD hath redeemed Jacob, and glorified himself in Israel.

24 Thus saith the LORD, thy Redeemer, and he that formed thee from the womb, I *am* the LORD that maketh all *things;* that stretcheth forth the heavens alone; that spreadeth abroad the earth by myself;

25 That frustrateth the tokens of the <sup>f</sup>liars, and maketh diviners mad; that turneth wise *men* backward, and maketh their knowledge foolish;

26 That <sup>g</sup>confirmeth the word of his servant, and performeth the counsel of his messengers; that saith to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be inhabited; and to the cities of Judah, Ye shall be built, and I will raise up the decayed places thereof:

27 That saith to the <sup>h</sup>deep, Be dry, and I will dry up thy rivers:

28 That saith of Cyrus, *He is* my shepherd, and shall perform all my pleasure: even saying to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be built; and to the temple, Thy foundation shall be laid.

## CHAPTER XLV.

God calleth Cyrus for his church's sake.

THUS saith the LORD to his <sup>a</sup>anointed, to Cyrus, whose right hand I have holden, to <sup>b</sup>subdue nations before him; and I will loose the loins of kings, to open before him the two-leaved <sup>c</sup>gates; and the gates shall not be shut:

2 I will go before thee, and make the crooked places straight; I will break in pieces the <sup>d</sup>gates of brass, and cut in sunder the bars of iron:

3 And I will give thee the <sup>e</sup>treasures of darkness, and hidden riches of secret places, that thou mayest <sup>f</sup>know that I the LORD which call *thee* by thy name, *am* the God of Israel.

4 For Jacob my servant's sake, and Israel mine <sup>g</sup>elect, I have even called thee by thy name: I have surnamed thee, though thou hast <sup>h</sup>not known me.

5 I *am* the LORD, and *there is* none else, *there is* no God besides me: <sup>i</sup>I girded thee, though thou hast not known me;

6 That they may know from the <sup>k</sup>rising of the sun, and from the west, that *there is* none besides me: I *am* the LORD, and *there is* none else.

7 I form the light, and create darkness; I make <sup>l</sup>peace, and create <sup>m</sup>evil: I the LORD do all these *things.*

8 Drop down, ye <sup>n</sup>heavens, from above, and let the skies pour down <sup>o</sup>righteousness: let the earth open, and let them bring forth salvation, and let righteousness spring up together. I the LORD have created it.

9 Wo unto him that <sup>p</sup>striveth with his Maker! *Let* the potsherd *strive* with the potsherds of the earth. Shall the <sup>q</sup>clay say to him that fashioneth it, What makest thou? or thy work, He hath no hands?

10 Wo unto him that saith unto *his* father, What begettest thou? or to the woman, What hast thou brought forth?

11 Thus saith the LORD, the Holy One of Israel, and his Maker, Ask me of things to come concerning my sons; and concerning the work of my hands <sup>r</sup>command ye me.

12 I have made the earth, <sup>s</sup>and created man upon it: I, *even* my hands, have stretched out the heavens, and all their host have I commanded.

13 <sup>t</sup>I have raised him up in righteousness, and I will direct all his ways: he shall build my city, and he shall let go my captives, not for <sup>u</sup>price nor reward, saith the LORD of hosts.
