## CHAPTER XXXIII.

Two and forty journeys of the Israelites.

THESE *are* the <sup>a</sup>journeys of the children of Israel, which went forth out of the land of Egypt with their <sup>b</sup>armies, under the <sup>c</sup>hand of Moses and Aaron.

2 And Moses wrote their goings out according to their journeys, by the <sup>d</sup>commandment of the LORD: and these *are* their journeys according to their goings out.

3 And they departed from <sup>e</sup>Rameses in the <sup>f</sup>first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month: on the morrow after the passover the children of Israel went out with a <sup>g</sup>high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians.

4 (For the Egyptians buried all *their* first-born which the LORD had <sup>h</sup>smitten among them: upon their <sup>i</sup>gods also the LORD executed judgments.)

5 And the children of Israel removed from Rameses, and pitched in <sup>k</sup>Succoth.

6 And they departed from Succoth, and pitched in <sup>l</sup>Etham, which *is* in the edge of the wilderness.

7 And they removed from Etham, and turned again unto <sup>m</sup>Pi-hahiroth, which *is* before Baal-zephon: and they pitched before Migdol.

8 And they departed from before Pi-hahiroth, and passed through the <sup>n</sup>midst of the sea into the wilderness, and went three days' journey in the wilderness of <sup>o</sup>Etham, and pitched in <sup>p</sup>Marah.

9 And they removed from Marah, and came unto Elim: and in Elim *were* twelve fountains of water, and threescore and ten <sup>q</sup>palm-trees; and they pitched there.

10 And they removed from Elim, and encamped by the Red Sea.

11 And they removed from the Red Sea, and encamped in the wilderness of <sup>r</sup>Sin.

12 And they took their journey out of the wilderness of Sin, and encamped in Dophkah.

13 And they departed from Dophkah, and encamped in Alush.

14 And they removed from Alush, and encamped at <sup>s</sup>Rephidim, where was no water for the people to drink.

15 And they departed from Rephidim, and pitched in the wilderness of <sup>t</sup>Sinai.

16 And they removed from the desert of Sinai, and pitched at <sup>||</sup>Kibroth-hattaavah.

17 And they departed from Kibroth-hattaavah, and encamped at <sup>u</sup>Hazeroth.

18 And they departed from Hazeroth, and pitched in <sup>||</sup>Rithmah.

19 And they departed from Rithmah, and pitched at <sup>||</sup>Rimmon-parez.

20 And they departed from Rimmon-parez, and pitched in <sup>x</sup>Libnah.

21 And they removed from Libnah, and pitched at Rissah.

22 And they journeyed from Rissah, and pitched in Kehelathah.

23 And they went from Kehelathah, and pitched in mount Shapher.

24 And they removed from mount Shapher, and encamped in Haradah.

25 And they removed from Haradah, and pitched in <sup>||</sup>Makheloth.

26 And they removed from Makheloth, and encamped at Tahath.

27 And they departed from Tahath, and pitched at Tarah.

28 And they removed from Tarah, and pitched in Mithcah.

29 And they went from Mithcah, and pitched in Hashmonah.

30 And they departed from Hashmonah, and encamped at <sup>y</sup>Moseroth.

31 And they departed from Moseroth, and pitched in <sup>z</sup>Bene-jaakan.

32 And they removed from Bene-jaakan, and encamped at <sup>a</sup>Hor-hagidgad.

33 And they went from Hor-hagidgad, and pitched in <sup>||</sup>Jotbathah.

34 And they removed from Jotbathah, and encamped at Ebronah.

35 And they departed from Ebronah, and encamped at <sup>b</sup>Ezion-gaber.

36 And they removed from Ezion-gaber, and pitched in the wilderness <sup>c</sup>of Zin, which *is* Kadesh.

37 And they removed from Kadesh, and pitched in mount Hor, in the <sup>d</sup>edge of the land of Edom.

38 And Aaron the priest went up into mount Hor, at the commandment of the LORD, and <sup>e</sup>died there, in the fortieth year after the children of Israel were come out of the land of Egypt, in the first *day* of the fifth month.

39 And Aaron *was* a hundred and twenty and three years old when he died in mount Hor.

40 And king <sup>f</sup>Arad the Canaanite, which dwelt in the south, in the land of Canaan, <sup>g</sup>heard of the coming of the children of Israel.

41 And they departed from <sup>h</sup>mount Hor, and pitched in Zalmonah.

42 And they departed from Zalmonah, and pitched in Punon.

43 And they departed from Punon, and pitched in <sup>i</sup>Oboth.

44 And they departed from Oboth, and pitched in Ije-abarim, in the border of Moab.

45 And they departed from Iim, and pitched in <sup>k</sup>Dibon-gad.

46 And they removed from Dibon-gad, and encamped in <sup>l</sup>Almon-diblathaim.

47 And they removed from Almon-diblathaim, and pitched in the mountains of Abarim, before <sup>m</sup>Nebo.

48 And they departed from the mountains of Abarim, and pitched in the plains of Moab, by Jordan *near* Jericho.

49 And they pitched by Jordan from <sup>n</sup>Beth-jesimoth, *even* unto <sup>||</sup>Abel-shittim, in the plains of Moab.
