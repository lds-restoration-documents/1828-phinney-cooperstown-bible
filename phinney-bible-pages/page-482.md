## CHAPTER XXVII.

The subduing of the neighbouring kings unto Nebuchadnezzar.

IN the beginning of the reign <sup>a</sup>of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, came this word unto Jeremiah from the LORD, saying,

2 Thus saith the LORD to me, Make thee <sup>b</sup>bonds and yokes, and put them upon thy neck,

3 And send them to the king of Edom, and to the king of Moab, and to the king of the Ammonites, and to the king of Tyrus, and to the king of Zidon, by the hand of the messengers which <sup>c</sup>come to Jerusalem unto Zedekiah king of Judah;

4 And command them to say unto their masters, Thus saith the <sup>d</sup>LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, Thus shall ye say unto your masters;

5 I have made the <sup>e</sup>earth, the man and the beast that *are* upon the ground, by my great power, and by my out-stretched arm, and have <sup>f</sup>given it unto whom it <sup>g</sup>seemed meet unto me.

6 And now have I given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, <sup>h</sup>my servant; and the <sup>i</sup>beasts of the field have I given him also to serve him.

7 And all nations shall serve him, and his <sup>k</sup>son, and his <sup>l</sup>son's son, until the very <sup>m</sup>time of his land come; and *then* many nations and great kings shall <sup>n</sup>serve themselves of him.

8 And it shall come to pass, *that* the nation and kingdom which will not serve the same Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, and that will not put their neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, that nation will I punish, saith the LORD, with the sword, and with the famine, and with the pestilence, until I have consumed them by his hand.

9 Therefore hearken not ye to your <sup>o</sup>prophets, nor to your diviners, nor to your dreamers, nor to your enchanters, nor to your sorcerers, which speak unto you, saying, Ye shall not serve the king of Babylon:

10 For they prophesy <sup>p</sup>a lie unto you, to <sup>q</sup>remove you far from your land; and that I should drive you out, and ye should perish.

11 But the nations that bring their neck under <sup>r</sup>the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him, those will I let remain still in their own land, saith the LORD; and they shall till it, and dwell therein.

12 ¶ I spake also to <sup>s</sup>Zedekiah king of Judah according to all these words, saying, Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him and his people, and live.

13 <sup>t</sup>Why will ye die, thou and thy people, by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence, as the LORD hath spoken against the nation that will not serve the king of Babylon?

14 Therefore hearken not unto the words of the prophets that speak unto you, saying, Ye shall not serve the king of Babylon: for they prophesy <sup>u</sup>a lie unto you.

15 For I have <sup>x</sup>not sent them, saith the LORD, yet they prophesy a lie in my name, that I <sup>y</sup>might drive you out, and that ye might perish, ye, and the prophets that prophesy unto you.

16 Also I spake to the priests, and to all this people, saying, Thus saith the LORD, Hearken not to the words of your prophets that prophesy unto you, saying, Behold, the <sup>z</sup>vessels of the LORD's house shall now shortly be brought again from Babylon: for they prophesy a lie unto you.

17 Hearken not unto them; serve the king of Babylon, and live: wherefore should this city be <sup>a</sup>laid waste?

18 But if they *be* prophets, and if the word of the LORD be with them, let them now make <sup>b</sup>intercession to the LORD of hosts, that the vessels which are left in the house of the LORD, and *in* the house of the king of Judah, and at Jerusalem, go not to Babylon.

19 For thus saith the LORD of hosts concerning the <sup>c</sup>pillars, and concerning the sea, and concerning the bases, and concerning the residue of the vessels that remain in this city,

20 Which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took not, when he carried away captive <sup>d</sup>Jeconiah, the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, from Jerusalem to Babylon, and all the <sup>†</sup>nobles of Judah and Jerusalem;

21 Yea, thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, concerning the vessels that <sup>e</sup>remain *in* the house of the LORD, and *in* the house of the king of Judah and of Jerusalem;

22 They shall be <sup>f</sup>carried to Babylon, and there shall they be until the day that I visit them, saith the LORD: then will I bring them up, and <sup>g</sup>restore them to this place.

## CHAPTER XXVIII.

Hananiah prophesieth the return of the vessels and of Jeconiah.

AND it came to pass the same year, in <sup>a</sup>the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fourth year, *and* in the fifth month, *that* Hananiah the son of Azur the <sup>b</sup>prophet, which *was* of Gibeon, spake unto me in the house of the LORD, in the presence of the priests, and of all the people, saying,

2 <sup>c</sup>Thus speaketh the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, saying, I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon.

3 Within two full years will I <sup>d</sup>bring again into this place all the vessels of the LORD's house that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took away from this place, and carried them to Babylon:

4 And I will bring again to this place Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, with all the <sup>e</sup>captives of Judah, that went into Babylon, saith the LORD: for I will <sup>f</sup>break the yoke of the king of Babylon.

5 Then the prophet Jeremiah said unto the prophet Hananiah, in the presence of the priests, and in the presence of all the people that stood in the house of the LORD,

6 Even the prophet Jeremiah said, <sup>g</sup>Amen: the LORD do so; the LORD perform thy words which thou hast prophesied, to bring again the vessels of the LORD's house, and all that is carried away captive, from Babylon into this place.

7 Nevertheless, hear thou now this word that I speak in thine ears, and in the ears of all the people;

8 The <sup>h</sup>prophets that have been before me, and before thee of old, prophesied both against many countries, and against great kingdoms, of war, and of <sup>i</sup>evil, and of pestilence.
