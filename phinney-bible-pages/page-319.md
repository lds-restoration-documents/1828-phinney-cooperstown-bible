## CHAPTER XXXIV.

1 Josiah's good reign....3 He destroyeth idolatry.

JOSIAH *was* <sup>a</sup>eight years old when he began to reign: and he reigned in Jerusalem one and thirty years.

2 And he did *that which was* right <sup>b</sup>in the sight of the LORD, and walked in the ways of David his father, and declined *neither* to the <sup>c</sup>right hand nor to the left.

3 For in the <sup>||</sup>eighth year of his reign, while he was yet <sup>d</sup>young, he began <sup>e</sup>to seek after the God of David his father: and in the twelfth year he began to <sup>f</sup>purge Judah and Jerusalem from the high places, and the groves, and the carved images, and the molten images.

4 And they brake down the altars of Baalim, in his presence: and the images that *were* on high above them he cut down: and the groves, and the carved images, and the molten images, he brake in pieces, and made <sup>g</sup>dust *of them*, and strewed *it* upon the graves of them that had sacrificed unto them.

5 And he burnt the <sup>h</sup>bones of the priests upon their altars, and cleansed Judah and Jerusalem.

6 And *so did he* in the <sup>i</sup>cities of Manasseh, and Ephraim, and Simeon, even unto Naphtali, with their mattocks round about.

7 And when he had broken down the altars and the groves, and had beaten the graven images <sup>k</sup>into powder, and cut down all the idols throughout all the land of Israel, he returned to Jerusalem.

8 Now in the eighteenth year of his reign, when he had purged the land, and the house, he sent Shaphan the son of Azaliah, and Maaseiah the <sup>l</sup>governor of the city, and Joah the son of Joahaz the recorder, to <sup>m</sup>repair the house of the LORD his God.

9 And when they came to Hilkiah the high priest, they <sup>n</sup>delivered the money *that was* brought into the house of God, which the Levites that kept the doors had gathered of the hand of Manasseh and Ephraim, and of all the remnant of Israel, and of all Judah and Benjamin, and they returned to Jerusalem.

10 And they <sup>o</sup>put *it* in the hand of the workmen that had the oversight of the house of the LORD, and they gave it to the workmen that wrought in the house of the LORD, to repair and mend the house;

11 Even to the <sup>p</sup>artificers and builders gave they *it*, to buy hewn stone and timber for couplings, and to floor the houses which the kings of Judah had destroyed.

12 And the men did the work <sup>†</sup>faithfully: and the overseers of them *were* Jahath and Obadiah, the Levites, of the sons of Merari; and Zechariah and Meshullam, of the sons of the Kohathites, to set *it* forward: and *other of* the Levites, all that <sup>q</sup>could skill of instruments of music.
