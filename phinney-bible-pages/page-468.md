## CHAPTER VI.

The enemies sent against Judah encourage themselves.

O YE children of <sup>a</sup>Benjamin, gather yourselves to <sup>b</sup>flee out of the midst of Jerusalem, and blow the trumpet in Tekoa, and set up <sup>c</sup>a sign of fire in Beth-haccerem: for evil appeareth out of the <sup>d</sup>north, and great destruction.

2 I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate *woman.*

3 The <sup>e</sup>shepherds with their flocks shall come unto her; they shall pitch *their* tents against her round about; they shall feed every one in his place.

4 Prepare ye war against her; arise, and let us go up <sup>f</sup>at noon. Wo unto us! for the day goeth away, for the shadows of the evening are stretched out.

5 Arise, and let us go by night, and let us destroy her <sup>g</sup>palaces.

6 For thus hath the LORD of hosts said, <sup>h</sup>Hew ye down trees, and cast a mount against Jerusalem: this *is* the city to be visited; she *is* <sup>i</sup>wholly oppression in the midst of her.

7 As <sup>k</sup>a fountain casteth out her waters, so she casteth out her wickedness: <sup>l</sup>violence and spoil is heard in her; before me continually *is* grief and wounds.

8 Be thou <sup>†</sup>instructed, O Jerusalem, lest my soul depart from thee; lest I make thee desolate, a land not inhabited.

9 Thus saith the LORD of hosts, They shall thoroughly <sup>m</sup>glean the remnant of Israel as a vine: turn back thy hand as a grape-gatherer into the baskets.

10 To whom shall I speak and give warning, that they may hear? Behold, their <sup>n</sup>ear *is* uncircumcised, and they cannot hearken: behold, the word of the LORD *is* unto them a reproach; they have <sup>o</sup>no delight in it.

11 Therefore I am full of the fury of the LORD; I am <sup>p</sup>weary with holding in: I will <sup>q</sup>pour it out upon the <sup>r</sup>children abroad, and upon the assembly of young men together; for even the husband with the wife shall be taken, the aged with *him that is* full of days.

12 And their <sup>s</sup>houses shall be turned unto others, *with their* fields and wives together: for I will stretch out my hand upon the inhabitants of the land, saith the LORD.

13 For from the least of them even unto the greatest of them, every one *is* given <sup>t</sup>to covetousness; and from the prophet even unto the priest, every one dealeth falsely.

14 They have healed also the hurt *of the daughter* of my people <sup>u</sup>slightly, saying, <sup>x</sup>Peace, peace; when *there is* no peace.

15 Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination? nay, they were <sup>y</sup>not at all ashamed, neither could they blush; therefore they shall fall among them that fall: at the time *that* I visit them they shall be cast down, saith the LORD.

16 Thus saith the LORD, <sup>z</sup>Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the <sup>a</sup>old paths, where *is* the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find <sup>b</sup>rest for your souls. But they said, <sup>c</sup>We will not walk *therein.*

17 Also I set <sup>d</sup>watchmen over you, *saying,* Hearken to the <sup>e</sup>sound of the trumpet. But they said, We will not hearken.

18 Therefore hear, ye nations, and know, O congregation, what *is* among them.

19 <sup>f</sup>Hear, O earth; Behold, I will bring evil upon this people, *even* the <sup>g</sup>fruit of their thoughts, because they have not hearkened unto my words, nor to my law, but rejected it.

20 <sup>h</sup>To what purpose cometh there to me incense from Sheba, and the sweet cane from a far country? your <sup>i</sup>burnt-offerings *are* not acceptable, nor your sacrifices sweet unto me.

21 Therefore thus saith the LORD, Behold, I will lay <sup>k</sup>stumbling-blocks before this people, and the <sup>l</sup>fathers and the sons together shall fall upon them; the neighbour and his friend shall perish.

22 Thus saith the LORD, Behold, a people cometh from the north country, and a great nation shall be raised from the sides of the earth.

23 They shall lay hold on bow and <sup>m</sup>spear; they *are* <sup>n</sup>cruel, and have no mercy: their voice roareth like the sea; and they ride upon horses, set in array as men for war against thee, O daughter of Zion.

24 We have heard the fame thereof; our hands wax feeble: anguish hath taken hold of us, *and* pain as of a <sup>o</sup>woman in travail.

25 <sup>p</sup>Go not forth into the field, nor walk by the way; for the sword of the enemy *and* fear *is* on every side.

26 O daughter of my people, gird *thee* with sackcloth, and wallow thyself in ashes; make thee mourning *as for* an <sup>q</sup>only son, most bitter lamentation: for the spoiler <sup>r</sup>shall suddenly come upon us.

27 I have set thee *for* a <sup>s</sup>tower *and* a fortress among my people, that thou mayest <sup>t</sup>know and try their way.

28 They *are* all <sup>u</sup>grievous revolters, walking with <sup>x</sup>slanders: *they are* <sup>y</sup>brass and iron; they *are* all <sup>z</sup>corrupters.

29 The <sup>a</sup>bellows are burnt, the lead is consumed of the fire; the founder melteth in vain: for the wicked are <sup>b</sup>not plucked away.

30 <sup>c</sup>Reprobate silver shall *men* call them, because the LORD hath rejected them.
