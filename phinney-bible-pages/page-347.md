## CHAPTER VII.

3 Doth God <sup>c</sup>pervert judgment? or doth the Almighty pervert justice?

7 Though thy <sup>h</sup>beginning was small, yet thy latter end should greatly increase.

11 Can the rush grow up without mire? can the flag grow without water?

12 Whilst it *is* yet in his greenness, *and* not cut down, it withereth before any *other* herb.

13 So *are* the paths of all that <sup>m</sup>forget God; and the <sup>n</sup>hypocrite's hope shall perish:

14 Whose hope shall be cut off, and whose trust *shall be* <sup>o</sup>a spider's web.

15 He shall <sup>p</sup>lean upon his house, but it shall not stand: he shall hold it fast, but it shall not endure.

16 He *is* <sup>q</sup>green before the sun, and his branch shooteth forth in his garden.

17 <sup>r</sup>His roots are wrapped about the heap, *and* seeth the place of stones.

18 If he <sup>s</sup>destroy him from his place, then *it* shall deny him, *saying,* I have not seen thee.

19 Behold, this *is* the joy of his way, and <sup>t</sup>out of the earth shall others grow.

20 Behold, God will <sup>u</sup>not cast away a perfect *man,* neither will he help the evil-doers;

21 Till he fill thy mouth with laughing, and thy lips with <sup>x</sup>rejoicing.

22 They that hate thee shall be <sup>y</sup>clothed with shame; and the dwelling-place of the wicked shall come to nought.
