12 His troops come <sup>n</sup>together, and raise up their way against me, and encamp round about my tabernacle.

13 He hath put my brethren <sup>o</sup>far from me, and mine acquaintance are verily estranged from me.

29 Be ye afraid of the sword: for wrath *bringeth* the punishments of the sword, that ye may know *there is* <sup>f</sup>a judgment.

## CHAPTER XX.

Zophar sheweth the state and portion of the wicked.

THEN answered <sup>a</sup>Zophar the Naamathite, and said,

2 Therefore do my thoughts cause me to answer, and for *this* <sup>b</sup>I make haste.

3 I have heard the <sup>c</sup>check of my reproach, and the spirit of my understanding causeth me to answer.

4 Knowest thou *not* this <sup>d</sup>of old, since man was placed upon earth,

5 That the triumphing of the wicked *is* <sup>e</sup>short, and the joy of the hypocrite *but* for a moment?

6 Though his excellency mount up to the heavens, and his <sup>f</sup>head reach unto the clouds:

7 *Yet* he shall perish for ever like his <sup>g</sup>own dung: they which have seen him shall say, Where *is* he?

8 He shall fly away as a dream, and shall <sup>h</sup>not be found; yea, he shall be chased away as a vision of the night.

9 The eye also *which* saw him shall *see him* no more; neither shall <sup>i</sup>his place any more behold him.

10 <sup>k</sup>His children shall seek to please the poor, and his hands shall restore their goods.

11 His bones are full *of the sin* of his youth, which shall <sup>l</sup>lie down with him in the dust.

12 Though wickedness be <sup>m</sup>sweet in his mouth, *though* he hide it under his tongue;

13 *Though* he spare it, and <sup>n</sup>forsake it not, but keep it still within his mouth;

14 *Yet* his meat in his bowels is turned, *it is* the <sup>o</sup>gall of asps within him.

15 He hath swallowed down riches, and he shall <sup>p</sup>vomit them up again: God shall cast them out of his belly.

16 He shall suck the poison of asps: the <sup>q</sup>viper's tongue shall slay him.

17 He shall <sup>r</sup>not see the rivers, the floods, the brooks of honey and butter.

18 That which he laboured for shall he <sup>s</sup>restore, and shall not swallow *it* down: according to *his* substance *shall* the restitution *be,* and he shall not rejoice *therein.*

19 Because he hath oppressed *and* hath <sup>t</sup>forsaken the poor; *because* he hath violently taken away a house which he builded not;

20 Surely he shall not feel quietness in his belly, he shall <sup>u</sup>not save of that which he desired.

21 There shall none of his meat be <sup>x</sup>left; therefore shall no man look for his goods.

22 In the <sup>y</sup>fullness of his sufficiency he shall be in straits: every hand of the wicked shall come upon him.

23 *When* he is about to fill his belly, *God* shall <sup>z</sup>cast the fury of his wrath upon him, and shall rain *it* upon him <sup>a</sup>while he is eating.

24 He shall flee from the iron weapon, *and* the <sup>b</sup>bow of steel shall strike him through.

25 It is <sup>c</sup>drawn, and cometh out of the body; yea, the glittering sword cometh out of his gall: terrors *are* upon him.

26 All darkness *shall be* hid in his secret places; <sup>d</sup>a fire not blown shall consume him; it shall go ill with him that is left in his tabernacle.

27 The <sup>e</sup>heaven shall reveal his iniquity; and the <sup>f</sup>earth shall rise up against him.

28 The increase of his house shall <sup>g</sup>depart, *and his goods* shall flow away in the day of his wrath.

29 This *is* the portion of a wicked man from God, and the heritage <sup>h</sup>appointed unto him by God.
