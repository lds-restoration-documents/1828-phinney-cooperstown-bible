9 For, lo, I will raise, and cause to come up against Babylon, an assembly of <sup>s</sup>great nations from the north country: and they shall set themselves in array against her; from thence she shall be taken: their arrows *shall be* as of a mighty expert man; none shall return <sup>t</sup>in vain.

10 And Chaldea shall be a spoil: all that spoil her shall be <sup>u</sup>satisfied, saith the LORD.

11 Because ye were <sup>x</sup>glad, because ye rejoiced, <sup>y</sup>O ye destroyers of my <sup>z</sup>heritage, because ye are <sup>a</sup>grown fat as the heifer at grass, and bellow as bulls;

12 Your <sup>b</sup>mother shall be sore confounded; she that bare you shall be ashamed: behold, the <sup>c</sup>hindermost of the nations *shall be* a wilderness, a dry land, and a desert.

13 Because of the <sup>d</sup>wrath of the LORD it shall not be inhabited, but it shall be wholly desolate: every one that goeth by Babylon shall be astonished, and <sup>e</sup>hiss at all her plagues.

14 Put yourselves in array against Babylon round about: all ye that bend the bow shoot at her, <sup>f</sup>spare no arrows; for she hath <sup>g</sup>sinned against the LORD.

15 Shout against her round about; she hath given her hand: <sup>h</sup>her foundations are fallen, her walls are thrown down; for it *is* the <sup>i</sup>vengeance of the LORD: take vengeance upon her; <sup>k</sup>as she hath done, do unto her.

16 Cut off the <sup>l</sup>sower from Babylon, and him that handleth the <sup>m</sup>sickle in the time of harvest: for fear of the oppressing sword they shall turn every one to his people, and they shall flee every one to his own land.

17 Israel *is* <sup>n</sup>a scattered sheep; the lions have driven *him* away: first the <sup>o</sup>king of Assyria hath devoured him, and last this <sup>p</sup>Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon hath broken his bones.

18 Therefore thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, Behold, I will <sup>q</sup>punish the king of Babylon and his land, <sup>r</sup>as I have punished the king of Assyria.

19 And I will <sup>s</sup>bring Israel again to his habitation, and he shall feed on Carmel and Bashan, and his soul shall be satisfied upon mount Ephraim and Gilead.

20 In those days, and in that time, saith the LORD, the iniquity of Israel shall be sought for, and *there shall be* none; and the sins of Judah, and they shall <sup>t</sup>not be found: for I will <sup>u</sup>pardon them whom I reserve.

21 Go up against the land of <sup>||</sup>Merathaim, *even* against it, and against the inhabitants of Pekod: waste and utterly destroy after them, saith the LORD, and <sup>x</sup>do according to all that I have commanded thee.

22 A sound of battle *is* in the land, and of great <sup>y</sup>destruction.

23 How is the <sup>z</sup>hammer of the whole earth cut asunder and broken! <sup>a</sup>how is Babylon become a desolation among the nations!

24 <sup>b</sup>I have laid a <sup>c</sup>snare for thee, and thou art also taken, O Babylon, and thou wast not aware: thou art found, and also caught, because thou hast striven against the LORD.

25 The LORD hath opened his armoury, and hath <sup>d</sup>brought forth the weapons of his indignation: for this *is* the work of the Lord GOD of hosts in the land of the Chaldeans.

26 Come against her from the <sup>e</sup>utmost border, <sup>f</sup>open her store-houses; cast her up as heaps, and destroy her utterly: let nothing of her be left.

27 Slay all her <sup>g</sup>bullocks; let them go down to the slaughter: wo unto them! for their <sup>h</sup>day is come, the time of their visitation.

28 The voice of them that flee and escape out of the land of <sup>i</sup>Babylon, to declare in Zion the vengeance of the LORD our God, the vengeance of his <sup>k</sup>temple.

29 Call together the archers against Babylon: all ye that bend the bow, camp against it round about: let none thereof escape: <sup>l</sup>recompense her according to her work; according to all that she hath done, do unto her: for she hath been proud <sup>m</sup>against the LORD, against the Holy One of Israel.

30 Therefore shall her young men fall in the streets, and all her men of war shall be cut off in that day, saith the LORD.

31 Behold, I *am* against thee, <sup>†</sup>*O thou* most proud, saith the Lord GOD of hosts: for thy day is come, the time *that* I will visit thee.

32 And the most proud shall stumble and fall, and <sup>n</sup>none shall raise him up: and I will <sup>o</sup>kindle a fire in his cities, and it shall devour all round about him.

33 Thus saith the LORD of hosts, The children of Israel and the children of Judah *were* oppressed <sup>p</sup>together; and all that took them captives held them fast; they <sup>q</sup>refused to let them go.

34 Their Redeemer <sup>r</sup>*is* strong; The LORD of hosts *is* his name; he shall thoroughly plead their cause, that he may give <sup>s</sup>rest to the land, and <sup>t</sup>disquiet the inhabitants of Babylon.

35 A sword *is* upon the Chaldeans, saith the LORD, and upon the inhabitants of Babylon, and upon her princes, and upon her wise *men.*

36 A sword *is* upon the <sup>†</sup>liars; and they shall dote: a sword *is* upon her mighty men; and they shall be dismayed.

37 A sword *is* upon their horses, and upon their chariots, and upon all the mingled people that *are* in the midst of her; and they shall become <sup>u</sup>as women: a sword *is* upon her treasures; and they shall be robbed.

38 <sup>x</sup>A drought *is* upon her waters; and they shall be dried up: for it *is* the land of graven images, and they are <sup>y</sup>mad upon *their* idols.

39 Therefore <sup>z</sup>the wild beasts of the desert, with the wild beasts of the islands, shall dwell *there,* and the owls shall dwell therein: and it shall be no more inhabited for ever; neither shall it be dwelt in from generation to generation.

40 As God overthrew <sup>a</sup>Sodom and Gomorrah, and the neighbour *cities* thereof, saith the LORD; *so* shall no man abide there, neither shall any son of man dwell therein.

41 Behold, a people shall come from the north, and a great nation, and many <sup>b</sup>kings shall be raised up from the coasts of the earth.

42 They shall hold the bow and the lance; they *are* <sup>c</sup>cruel, and will not shew mercy: their voice shall roar like the sea, and they shall ride upon horses, *every one* put in array, like a man to the battle, against thee, O daughter of Babylon.
