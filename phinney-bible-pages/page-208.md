## CHAPTER XIX.

Jonathan discloseth his father's purpose to kill David.

AND Saul spake to Jonathan his son, and to all his servants, that they should <sup>a</sup>kill David.

2 But Jonathan, Saul's son, <sup>b</sup>delighted much in David: and <sup>c</sup>Jonathan told David, saying, Saul my father seeketh to kill thee. Now, therefore, I pray thee, take heed to thyself until the morning, and abide in a secret *place,* and hide thyself:

3 And I will go out and stand beside my father in the field where thou *art,* and I will commune with my father of thee; and what I see, that I will tell thee.

4 And Jonathan spake <sup>d</sup>good of David unto Saul his father, and said unto him, Let not the king <sup>e</sup>sin against his servant, against David; because he hath not sinned against thee, and because <sup>f</sup>his works *have been* <sup>g</sup>to thee-ward very good.

5 For he did <sup>h</sup>put his life in his hand, and slew the Philistine, and the <sup>i</sup>LORD wrought a great salvation for all Israel; thou sawest *it,* and didst <sup>k</sup>rejoice; wherefore then wilt thou <sup>l</sup>sin against innocent blood, to slay David without a cause?

6 And Saul hearkened unto the voice of Jonathan: and Saul <sup>m</sup>sware, *As* the LORD liveth, he shall not be slain.

7 And Jonathan called David, and Jonathan shewed him all those things: and Jonathan brought David to Saul, and he was in his presence as in times past.

8 And there was war again: and David went out and fought with the Philistines, and slew them with a great slaughter; and they fled from him.

9 And the <sup>n</sup>evil spirit from the LORD was upon Saul, as he sat in his house with his <sup>o</sup>javelin in his hand: and David played with *his* hand.

10 And Saul <sup>p</sup>sought to smite David even to the wall with the javelin; but he slipped away out of Saul's presence, and he smote the javelin into the wall: and David fled, and <sup>q</sup>escaped that night.

11 Saul <sup>r</sup>also sent messengers unto David's house, to watch him, and to slay him in the morning: and Michal, David's wife, told him, saying, If thou save not thy life to-night, to-morrow thou shalt be slain.

12 So Michal let David down through <sup>s</sup>a window: and he went, and fled, and escaped.

13 And Michal took an <sup>†</sup>image, and laid *it* in the bed, and put a pillow of goats' *hair* for his bolster, and covered *it* with a cloth.

14 And when Saul sent messengers to take David, she said, He *is* sick.

15 And Saul sent the messengers *again* to see David, saying, Bring him up to me in the bed, that I may slay him.

16 And when the messengers were come in, behold, *there was* an image in the bed, with a pillow of goats' *hair* for his bolster.

17 And Saul said unto Michal, Why hast thou <sup>t</sup>deceived me so, and sent away mine enemy, that he is escaped? And Michal answered Saul, He said unto me, Let me go; <sup>u</sup>why should I kill thee?

18 So David fled, and escaped, and came <sup>x</sup>to Samuel to Ramah, and told him all that Saul had done to him: and he and Samuel went and dwelt in Naioth.

19 And it was <sup>y</sup>told Saul, saying, Behold, David *is* at Naioth in Ramah.

20 And Saul sent <sup>z</sup>messengers to take David; and when they saw the company of the prophets <sup>a</sup>prophesying, and Samuel standing *as* appointed over them, the Spirit of God was upon the messengers of Saul, and they also <sup>b</sup>prophesied.

21 And when it was told Saul, he sent other messengers, and they prophesied likewise. And Saul sent messengers again the <sup>c</sup>third time, and they prophesied also.

22 Then went <sup>d</sup>he also to Ramah, and came to a great well that *is* in <sup>e</sup>Sechu: and he asked and said, Where *are* Samuel and David? And *one* said, Behold, *they be* at Naioth in Ramah.

23 And he went thither to Naioth in Ramah: and the Spirit of God was upon him also, and he went on and prophesied, until he came to Naioth in Ramah.
