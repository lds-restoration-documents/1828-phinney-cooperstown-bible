8 And thou shalt set up the court round about, and hang up the <sup>k</sup>hanging at the court-gate.

9 And thou shalt take the anointing <sup>l</sup>oil, and anoint the tabernacle, and all that *is* therein, and shalt hallow it, and all the vessels thereof: and it shall be holy.

10 And thou shalt anoint the altar of the burnt-offering, and all his vessels, and sanctify the altar: and it shall be an altar <sup>m</sup>most holy.

11 And thou shalt anoint the laver and his foot, and sanctify it.

12 And thou shalt bring Aaron and his sons unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and wash them with water.

13 And thou shalt put upon Aaron the holy garments, and anoint him, and sanctify him; that he may minister unto me in the priest's office.

14 And thou shalt bring his sons, and clothe them with coats:

15 And thou shalt anoint them, as thou didst anoint their father, that they may minister unto me in the priest's office: for their anointing shall surely be an <sup>n</sup>everlasting priesthood, throughout their generations.

16 Thus did Moses: according to all that the LORD commanded him, so did he.

17 ¶ And it came to pass in the first month in the <sup>o</sup>second year, on the first *day* of the month, *that* the tabernacle was reared up.

18 And Moses reared up the <sup>p</sup>tabernacle, and <sup>q</sup>fastened his sockets, and set up the boards thereof, and put in the bars thereof, and reared up his pillars.

19 And he spread abroad the <sup>r</sup>tent over the tabernacle, and put the <sup>s</sup>covering of the tent above upon it; as the LORD commanded Moses.

20 And he took and put the <sup>t</sup>testimony into the ark, and set the staves on the ark, and put the <sup>u</sup>mercy-seat above upon the ark.

21 And he brought the ark unto the tabernacle, and set up the vail of the covering, and <sup>x</sup>covered the ark of the testimony; as the LORD commanded Moses.

22 And he put the table in the tent of the congregation, upon the side of the tabernacle northward, without the vail.

23 And he set the <sup>y</sup>bread in order upon it before the LORD; as the LORD had commanded Moses.

24 And he put the <sup>z</sup>candlestick in the tent of the congregation, over against the table, on the side of the tabernacle southward.

25 And he <sup>a</sup>lighted the lamps before the LORD; as the LORD commanded Moses.

26 And he put the <sup>b</sup>golden altar in the tent of the congregation, before the vail:

27 And he burned sweet incense thereon; as the LORD commanded Moses.

28 And he set up the hanging at the door of the tabernacle.

29 And he put the <sup>c</sup>altar of burnt-offering *by* the door of the tabernacle of the tent of the congregation, and offered upon it the burnt-offering and the meat-offering; <sup>d</sup>as the LORD commanded Moses.

30 And he set the <sup>e</sup>laver between the tent of the congregation and the altar, and put water there, to wash *withal.*

31 And Moses, and Aaron, and his sons, <sup>f</sup>washed their hands and their feet thereat:

32 When they went into the tent of the congregation, and when they came near unto the altar, they washed; <sup>g</sup>as the LORD commanded Moses.

33 And he reared up the <sup>h</sup>court round about the tabernacle and the altar, and set up the hanging of the court-gate. So Moses finished the work.

34 Then a <sup>i</sup>cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the <sup>k</sup>glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

35 And Moses was <sup>l</sup>not able to enter into the tent of the congregation, because the cloud abode thereon, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

36 And when the cloud was taken up from over the tabernacle, the children of Israel <sup>m</sup>went onward in all their journeys:

37 But if the cloud were not taken up, then they journeyed not till the day that it was taken up.

38 For <sup>n</sup>the cloud of the LORD *was* upon the tabernacle by day, and fire was on it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, throughout all their journeys.

# ¶ The THIRD Book of Moses, called LEVITICUS.

## CHAPTER I.

1 The burnt-offerings....3 of the herd....10 of the flocks....14 of the fowls.

AND the LORD <sup>a</sup>called unto Moses, and spake unto him out of the <sup>b</sup>tabernacle of the congregation, saying,

2 Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, If any man of you bring <sup>c</sup>an offering unto the LORD, ye shall bring your offering of the cattle, *even* of the herd, and of the <sup>d</sup>flock.

3 If his offering *be* a <sup>e</sup>burnt-sacrifice of the herd, let him offer a male without <sup>f</sup>blemish: he shall offer it of his own <sup>g</sup>voluntary will, at <sup>h</sup>the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, <sup>i</sup>before the LORD.

4 And he shall put his hand upon the head of the burnt-offering; and it shall be accepted for him, <sup>k</sup>to make atonement for him.

5 And <sup>l</sup>he shall kill the <sup>m</sup>bullock before the LORD: and the priests, Aaron's sons, shall bring the blood, <sup>n</sup>and sprinkle the blood round about upon the altar that *is by* the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.

6 And he shall flay the burnt-offering, and <sup>||</sup>cut it into his pieces.

7 And the sons of Aaron the priest shall <sup>o</sup>put fire upon the altar, and lay the wood in order upon the fire:

8 And the priests, Aaron's sons, shall lay the <sup>p</sup>parts, the head, and the fat, in order upon the wood that *is* on the fire which *is* upon the altar:

9 But the inwards and his legs shall he wash in <sup>q</sup>water: and the priest shall burn all on the altar, *to be* a burnt-sacrifice, an offering made by fire of a <sup>r</sup>sweet savour unto the LORD.

10 And if his offering *be* of the flocks, *namely,* of the <sup>s</sup>sheep, or of the goats, for a burnt-sacrifice: he shall bring it a male without blemish.

11 And he shall kill it on the side of the altar northward before the LORD: and the priests, Aaron's sons, shall sprinkle his blood round about upon the altar:

12 And he shall cut it into his pieces, with his head and his fat: and the priest shall lay them in order on the wood that *is* on the fire which *is* upon the altar.
