25 And *so* it was, at the beginning of their dwelling there, *that* they <sup>t</sup>feared not the LORD; therefore the LORD sent lions among them, which slew *some* of them.

26 Wherefore they spake to the king of Assyria, saying, The nations which thou hast removed, and placed in the cities of Samaria, know not the manner of the God of the land: therefore he hath sent lions among them, and, behold, they slay them, because they know not the manner of the God of the land.

27 Then the king of Assyria commanded, saying, Carry thither one of the <sup>u</sup>priests whom ye brought from thence; and let them go and dwell there, and let him teach them the manner of the God of the land.

28 Then one of the priests, whom they had carried away from Samaria, came and dwelt in <sup>x</sup>Beth-el, and taught them how they should fear the LORD.

29 Howbeit <sup>†</sup>every nation made <sup>y</sup>gods of their own, and put *them* in the houses of the high places which the <sup>z</sup>Samaritans had made, every nation in their cities wherein they dwelt.

31 And the Avites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites burnt <sup>a</sup>their children in fire to <sup>||</sup>Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

32 So they <sup>b</sup>feared the LORD, and made unto themselves of the <sup>c</sup>lowest of them priests of the high places, which sacrificed for them in the houses of the high places.

33 They feared the LORD, and served their own gods, after the manner of the nations whom they carried away from thence.

34 Unto this day they do after the former <sup>d</sup>manners: they fear not the LORD, neither do they after their statutes, or after their ordinances, or after the law and commandment which the LORD commanded the children of Jacob, whom <sup>e</sup>he named Israel:

35 With whom the LORD had made a <sup>f</sup>covenant, and charged them, saying, Ye shall <sup>g</sup>not fear other gods, nor bow <sup>h</sup>yourselves to them, nor serve them, nor sacrifice to them:

## CHAPTER XVIII.

1 Hezekiah's good reign....4 He destroyeth idolatry, and prospereth.

NOW it came to pass, in the <sup>a</sup>third year of Hoshea son of Elah king of Israel, *that* Hezekiah <sup>b</sup>the son of Ahaz king of Judah began to reign.

2 Twenty and five years old was he when he began to reign; and he reigned twenty and nine years in Jerusalem. His mother's name also *was* Abi, the daughter of <sup>c</sup>Zachariah.

3 And he did *that which was* <sup>d</sup>right in the sight of the LORD, according to all that <sup>e</sup>David his father did.

4 He removed <sup>f</sup>the high places, and brake the images, and cut down the groves, and brake in pieces the brazen serpent <sup>g</sup>that Moses had made: for unto those days the children of Israel did burn incense to it: and he called it <sup>||</sup>Nehushtan.

5 He trusted <sup>h</sup>in the LORD God of Israel; so that after him <sup>i</sup>was none like him among all the kings of Judah, nor *any* that were before him.

6 For he <sup>k</sup>clave to the LORD, *and* departed not from following him, but kept his commandments, which the LORD commanded Moses.

7 And the LORD was <sup>l</sup>with him; *and* he <sup>||</sup>prospered whithersoever he went forth: and he <sup>m</sup>rebelled against the king of Assyria, and served him not.

8 He smote <sup>n</sup>the Philistines, *even* unto Gaza, and the borders thereof, from the tower of the watchmen to the fenced city.

9 And it came to pass in the <sup>o</sup>fourth year of king Hezekiah, which *was* the seventh year of Hoshea son of Elah king of Israel, *that* Shalmaneser king of Assyria came up against Samaria, and besieged it.

10 And at the end of three years they <sup>p</sup>took it, *even* in the sixth year of Hezekiah (that *is*, the ninth year of Hoshea king of Israel) Samaria was taken.

11 And the king of Assyria did carry away Israel unto Assyria, and put them in <sup>q</sup>Halah and in Habor *by* the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes:

12 Because they <sup>r</sup>obeyed not the voice of the LORD their God, but transgressed his covenant, *and* all that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded, and would not hear *them*, nor do *them*.

13 ¶ Now, in the <sup>s</sup>fourteenth year of king Hezekiah did Sennacherib king of Assyria come up against all the fenced cities of Judah, and <sup>t</sup>took them.

14 And Hezekiah king of Judah sent to the king of Assyria to <sup>u</sup>Lachish, saying, I have <sup>x</sup>offended; return from me: that which thou puttest on me will I bear. And the king of Assyria appointed unto Hezekiah king of Judah three hundred talents of silver, and thirty talents of gold.

15 And Hezekiah gave *him* all <sup>y</sup>the silver that was found in the house of the LORD, and in the treasures of the king's house.

16 At that time did Hezekiah cut off *the* <sup>z</sup>*gold from* the doors of the temple of the LORD, and *from* the pillars which Hezekiah king of Judah had overlaid, and gave it to the king of Assyria.

17 ¶ And the king of Assyria <sup>a</sup>sent Tartan, and Rabsaris, and Rab-shakeh, from Lachish to king Hezekiah, with a great host against Jerusalem: and they went up, and came to Jerusalem. And when they were come up, they came and stood by the <sup>b</sup>conduit of the upper pool, which *is* in the highway of the fuller's field.

18 And when they had called to the king, there came out to <sup>c</sup>them Eliakim the son of Hilkiah, which *was* over the household, and Shebna the scribe, and Joah the son of Asaph the recorder.

19 And Rab-shakeh said unto them, Speak ye now to Hezekiah, Thus <sup>d</sup>saith the great <sup>e</sup>king, the king of Assyria, What <sup>f</sup>confidence *is* this wherein thou trustest?
