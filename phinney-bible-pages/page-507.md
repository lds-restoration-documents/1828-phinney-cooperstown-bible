## CHAPTER III.

1 Ezekiel eateth the roll....4 God encourageth him.

MOREOVER, he said unto me, Son of man, eat that thou findest; <sup>a</sup>eat this <sup>b</sup>roll, and go speak unto the house of Israel.

2 So I <sup>c</sup>opened my mouth, and he <sup>d</sup>caused me to eat that roll.

3 And he said unto me, Son of man, cause <sup>e</sup>thy belly to eat, and <sup>f</sup>fill thy bowels with this roll that I give thee. Then did I <sup>g</sup>eat *it;* and it was in my mouth <sup>h</sup>as honey for sweetness.

4 And he said unto me, Son of man, go, get thee unto the house of Israel, and speak with my words unto them.

5 For thou *art* not sent to a people of <sup>i</sup>a strange speech, and of a hard language, *but* to the house of Israel;

6 Not to many people of a strange speech, and of a hard language, whose words thou canst not understand: surely, had I sent thee to them, <sup>k</sup>they would have hearkened unto thee.

7 But the house of Israel will not hearken unto thee; for they will not hearken <sup>l</sup>unto me: for all the house of Israel *are* <sup>m</sup>impudent and hard-hearted.

8 Behold, I have made thy <sup>n</sup>face strong against their faces, and thy forehead strong against their foreheads.

9 As an adamant, <sup>o</sup>harder than flint, have I made thy forehead: fear them not, neither be dismayed at their looks, though they *be* a rebellious house.

10 Moreover, he said unto me, Son of man, <sup>p</sup>all my words that I shall speak unto thee receive in <sup>q</sup>thy heart, and hear with thine ears.

11 And go, get thee to them of the captivity, unto the children of <sup>r</sup>thy people, and speak unto them, and tell them, Thus saith the Lord GOD, <sup>s</sup>whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear.

12 Then the <sup>t</sup>spirit took me up, and I heard behind me a voice of a great rushing, *saying,* Blessed *be* the <sup>u</sup>glory of the LORD from <sup>x</sup>his place.

13 *I heard* also the noise of the wings of the living creatures that touched one another, and the noise of the wheels over against them, and a noise of a <sup>y</sup>great rushing.

14 So the spirit lifted me up, and took me away, and I went <sup>z</sup>in bitterness, <sup>a</sup>in the heat of my spirit; but the <sup>b</sup>hand of the LORD was strong upon me.

15 ¶ Then I came to them of the captivity at Tel-abib, that dwelt by <sup>c</sup>the river of Chebar, and I sat where they sat, and remained there <sup>d</sup>astonished among them <sup>e</sup>seven days.

16 And it came to pass, <sup>f</sup>at the end of seven days, that <sup>g</sup>the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

17 Son of man, <sup>h</sup>I have made thee <sup>i</sup>a watchman unto the house of Israel: therefore hear the word <sup>k</sup>at my mouth, and give them <sup>l</sup>warning <sup>m</sup>from me.

18 When I say unto the wicked, Thou shalt surely die; and thou givest him not warning, nor speakest to warn the wicked from his wicked way, <sup>n</sup>to save his life; the same wicked *man* shall <sup>o</sup>die in his iniquity; but his <sup>p</sup>blood will I require at thy hand.

19 Yet if thou warn the wicked, and he turn not from his wickedness, nor from his wicked way, he shall die in his iniquity; but thou hast delivered <sup>q</sup>thy soul.

20 Again, When <sup>r</sup>a righteous *man* doth turn from his <sup>s</sup>righteousness, and commit iniquity, and I lay <sup>t</sup>a stumbling-block before him, he shall die: because thou hast not given him warning, he shall die in his sin, and his righteousness which he hath done shall not be remembered; but his blood will I require at <sup>u</sup>thy hand.

21 Nevertheless, if thou warn the righteous *man,* that the righteous <sup>x</sup>sin not, and he doth not sin, he shall surely <sup>y</sup>live, because he is warned: also thou hast delivered thy soul.

22 ¶ And the <sup>z</sup>hand of the LORD was there upon me; and he said unto me, Arise, go forth into the plain, and I will <sup>a</sup>there talk with thee.

23 Then I arose, and went forth into the plain; and, behold, the <sup>b</sup>glory of the LORD stood there, as the glory which I saw by the river of Chebar: and I <sup>c</sup>fell on my face.

24 Then the <sup>d</sup>spirit entered into me, and set me upon my feet, and spake with me, and said unto me, Go, shut thyself within thy house.

25 But thou, O son of man, behold, <sup>e</sup>they shall put bands upon thee, and shall <sup>f</sup>bind thee with them, and thou shalt not go out among them:

26 And I will make thy tongue cleave to the roof of thy mouth, that thou shalt <sup>g</sup>be dumb, and shalt not be to them <sup>h</sup>a reprover; for they *are* a rebellious house.

27 But when I speak with thee, I will <sup>i</sup>open thy mouth, and thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD, He that <sup>k</sup>heareth, let him hear; and he that <sup>l</sup>forbeareth, let him forbear: for they *are* a rebellious house.

## CHAPTER IV.

By the provision of the siege is shewed the hardness of the famine.

THOU also, son of man, take thee <sup>a</sup>a tile, and lay it before thee, and <sup>b</sup>pourtray upon it the city, *even* <sup>c</sup>Jerusalem:

2 And <sup>d</sup>lay siege against it, and build a fort against it, and cast a mount against it; set the camp also against it, and set *battering*-rams against it round about.

3 Moreover, take thou unto thee an iron pan, and set it *for* a wall of iron between thee and the city; <sup>e</sup>and set thy face against it, and it shall be besieged, and thou shalt lay siege against it. This *shall be* <sup>f</sup>a sign to the house of Israel.

4 Lie thou also upon thy <sup>g</sup>left side, and <sup>h</sup>lay the iniquity of the house of Israel upon it: *according* to the number of the days that thou shalt lie upon it thou shalt bear their iniquity.
