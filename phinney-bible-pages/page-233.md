## CHAPTER XXII.

A psalm of thanksgiving for God's powerful deliverance and manifold blessings.

AND David spake unto the LORD the words of this <sup>a</sup>song, in the day *that* <sup>b</sup>the LORD had delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of <sup>c</sup>Saul:

2 And he said, The LORD *is* my rock, and my fortress, and my <sup>d</sup>deliverer;

3 The God of my rock; in him will I <sup>e</sup>trust: *he is* my <sup>f</sup>shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower, and my refuge, my <sup>g</sup>saviour; thou savest me from violence.

4 I will call on the LORD, *who is* worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies.

5 When the <sup>h</sup>waves of death compassed me, the floods of ungodly men made me afraid;

6 The <sup>i</sup>sorrows of hell compassed me about; the <sup>k</sup>snares of death prevented me.

7 In my distress <sup>l</sup>I called upon the LORD, and cried to my God; and he did hear my voice out of his temple, and my cry <sup>m</sup>*did enter* into his ears.

8 Then the <sup>n</sup>earth shook and trembled; the <sup>o</sup>foundations of heaven moved and shook, because he was wroth.

9 There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and <sup>p</sup>fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it.

10 He <sup>q</sup>bowed the heavens also and came down; and <sup>r</sup>darkness *was* under his feet.

11 And he rode upon <sup>s</sup>a cherub, and did fly: and he was seen upon the wings of the wind.

12 And he made darkness pavilions <sup>t</sup>round about him, dark waters *and* thick clouds of the skies.

13 Through the brightness before him were <sup>u</sup>coals of fire kindled.

14 The LORD thundered from heaven, and the Most High <sup>x</sup>uttered his voice.

15 And he sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them.

16 And the channels of the sea appeared, the foundations of the world were discovered, at the <sup>y</sup>rebuking of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of his <sup>†</sup>nostrils.

17 He sent from <sup>z</sup>above, he took me; he drew me out of many <sup>a</sup>waters;

18 He delivered me from my strong enemy, *and* from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me.

19 They prevented me in the day of my calamity: but the LORD was my stay.

20 He brought me forth also into a large place; he delivered me, because <sup>b</sup>he delighted in me.

21 The LORD rewarded me <sup>c</sup>according to my righteousness: according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.

22 For I have <sup>d</sup>kept the ways of the LORD, and have not wickedly departed from my God.

23 For <sup>e</sup>all his judgments *were* before me: and *as for* his statutes, I did not depart from them.

24 I was also <sup>f</sup>upright before him, and have kept myself <sup>g</sup>from mine iniquity.

25 Therefore the LORD hath recompensed me according to my righteousness; according to my cleanness in his eye-sight.

26 With the <sup>h</sup>merciful thou wilt shew thyself merciful, *and* with the upright man thou wilt shew thyself upright.

27 With the <sup>i</sup>pure thou wilt shew thyself pure; and with the <sup>k</sup>froward thou wilt shew thyself unsavoury.

28 And the <sup>l</sup>afflicted people thou wilt save: but thine <sup>m</sup>eyes *are* upon the haughty, *that* thou mayest bring *them* down.

29 For thou *art* my lamp, O LORD; and the LORD will <sup>n</sup>lighten my darkness.

30 For <sup>o</sup>by thee I have run through a troop: by my God have I leaped over a wall.

31 *As for* God <sup>p</sup>his way *is* perfect; the word of the LORD *is* <sup>q</sup>tried: he *is* a buckler to all them that trust in him.

32 For <sup>r</sup>who *is* God, save the LORD? and who *is* a rock, save our God?

33 God *is* my strength *and* power: and he maketh my way perfect.

34 He maketh my feet like <sup>s</sup>hinds' *feet;* and setteth me upon my <sup>t</sup>high places.

35 He teacheth my hands to war; so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms.

36 Thou hast also given me the <sup>u</sup>shield of thy salvation: and thy gentleness hath <sup>†</sup>made me great.

37 Thou hast enlarged my steps under me; so that my feet did not slip.

38 I have pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them; and turned not again until I had consumed them.

39 And I have consumed them, and wounded them, that they could not arise: yea, they are fallen <sup>x</sup>under my feet.

40 For thou hast girded me with strength to battle: them that rose up against me hast thou subdued under me.
