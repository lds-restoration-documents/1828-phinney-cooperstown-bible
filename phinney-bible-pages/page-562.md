# ¶ OBADIAH.

1 The destruction of Edom for their pride....17 The salvation and victory of Jacob.

THE vision of Obadiah. Thus saith the Lord GOD concerning Edom, <sup>a</sup>We have heard a rumour from the LORD, and an ambassador is sent among the heathen, <sup>b</sup>Arise ye, and let us rise up against her in battle.

2 Behold, I have made thee <sup>c</sup>small among the heathen: thou art greatly despised.

3 The pride of thy heart hath <sup>d</sup>deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation *is* high; that <sup>e</sup>saith in his heart, Who shall bring me down to the ground?

4 Though thou exalt *thyself* as the eagle, and though thou set thy <sup>f</sup>nest among the stars, thence will I bring thee down, saith the LORD.

5 <sup>g</sup>If thieves came to thee, if robbers by night, (how art thou cut off!) would they not have stolen till they had enough? if the grape-gatherers came to thee, would they <sup>h</sup>not leave *some* grapes?

6 How are *the* <sup>||</sup>*things* of Esau searched out! *how* are his hidden things sought up!

7 All the men of thy <sup>i</sup>confederacy have brought thee *even* to the border: the men that were at peace with thee have <sup>k</sup>deceived thee, *and* prevailed against thee; *they that eat* thy bread have laid a wound under thee: *there is* none understanding in him.

8 Shall I not in that day, saith the LORD, even destroy <sup>l</sup>the wise *men* out of Edom, and understanding out of the mount of Esau?

9 And thy <sup>m</sup>mighty *men,* O Teman, shall be dismayed, to the end that every one of the mount of Esau may be cut off by slaughter.

10 For *thy* <sup>n</sup>violence against thy brother Jacob, shame shall cover thee, and thou shalt be cut off <sup>o</sup>for ever.

11 In the day that thou stoodest on the other side, in the day that the <sup>p</sup>strangers carried away captive his forces, and foreigners entered into his gates, and <sup>q</sup>cast lots upon Jerusalem, even thou *wast* as one of them.

12 But thou shouldest not have <sup>r</sup>looked on the day of thy brother, in the day that he became a stranger; neither shouldest thou have <sup>s</sup>rejoiced over the children of Judah in the day of their destruction; neither shouldest thou have spoken proudly in the day of distress.

13 Thou shouldest <sup>t</sup>not have entered into the gate of my people in the day of their calamity; yea, thou shouldest not have looked on their affliction in the day of their calamity, nor have laid *hands* on their substance in the day of their calamity:

14 Neither shouldest thou have stood in the cross-way, to cut off those of his that did escape; neither shouldest thou have delivered up those of his that did remain in the day of distress.

15 For the <sup>u</sup>day of the LORD *is* near upon all the heathen: <sup>x</sup>as thou hast done, it shall be done unto thee; thy reward shall return upon thine own head.

16 For as <sup>y</sup>ye have drunk upon my holy mountain, *so* shall all the heathen drink continually; yea, they shall drink, and they shall <sup>z</sup>swallow down, and they shall be as though they had not been.

17 But upon mount Zion shall be deliverance, and there shall be holiness; and the house of Jacob <sup>a</sup>shall possess their possessions.

18 And the <sup>b</sup>house of Jacob shall be a fire, and the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau for stubble, and they shall kindle in them, and devour them; and there shall not be *any* remaining of the house of Esau: for the LORD hath spoken *it.*

19 And *they of* the south shall possess the <sup>c</sup>mount of Esau; and *they of* the plain the Philistines: and they shall possess the fields of Ephraim, and the fields of Samaria; and Benjamin *shall possess* Gilead.

20 And the captivity of this host of the children of Israel *shall possess* that of the Canaanites, *even* unto Zarephath; and the captivity of Jerusalem, <sup>d</sup>which *is* in Sepharad, shall possess the cities of the south.

21 And <sup>e</sup>saviours shall come up on mount Zion to judge the mount of Esau; and the <sup>f</sup>kingdom shall be the LORD'S.
