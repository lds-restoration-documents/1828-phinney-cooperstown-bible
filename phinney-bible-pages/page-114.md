## CHAPTER XIV.

The people murmur at the news.

9 Only rebel not ye against the LORD, neither fear ye the people of the land; for they *are* <sup>l</sup>bread for us: their <sup>†</sup>defence is departed from them, and the LORD *is* <sup>m</sup>with us: fear them not.

10 But all the congregation bade <sup>n</sup>stone them with stones. And the glory of the LORD <sup>o</sup>appeared in the tabernacle of the congregation, before all the children of Israel.

12 I will smite them with the <sup>s</sup>pestilence, and disinherit them, and will <sup>t</sup>make of thee a greater nation and mightier than they.

13 And Moses said unto the LORD, Then the Egyptians shall hear *it*, (for thou broughtest up this people in thy might from among them;)

14 And they will tell *it* to the inhabitants of this land: *for* they have heard that thou, LORD, *art* among this people; that thou, LORD, art <sup>u</sup>seen face to face; and *that* thy cloud <sup>x</sup>standeth over them; and *that* thou goest before them, by day-time in a pillar of cloud, and in a pillar of fire by night.

15 Now, *if* thou shalt kill *all* this people as one man, then the nations which have heard the fame of thee will speak, saying,

16 Because the LORD <sup>y</sup>was not able to bring this people into the land which he sware unto them, therefore he hath slain them in the wilderness.

17 And now, I beseech thee, let the power of my LORD be great, according as thou hast spoken, saying,

18 The LORD *is* <sup>z</sup>long-suffering, and of great mercy, forgiving iniquity and transgression, and by <sup>a</sup>no means clearing *the guilty*; <sup>b</sup>visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth *generation*.

19 Pardon, I beseech thee, the iniquity of this people, according unto the greatness of thy mercy, and as thou hast forgiven this people from Egypt even <sup>c</sup>until now.

20 And the LORD said, I have <sup>d</sup>pardoned, according to thy word:

21 But *as* <sup>e</sup>truly *as* I live, all the earth shall be <sup>f</sup>filled with the glory of the LORD.

22 Because all those men which have seen <sup>g</sup>my glory, and my miracles which I did in Egypt and in the wilderness, and have tempted me now these ten times, and have not hearkened to my voice;

23 <sup>†</sup>Surely they shall not see <sup>h</sup>the land which I sware unto their fathers, neither shall any of them that provoked me see it:

24 But my servant <sup>i</sup>Caleb, because he had <sup>k</sup>another spirit with him, and hath <sup>†</sup>followed me fully, him will I bring into the land whereinto he went; and his seed shall <sup>l</sup>possess it.

25 (Now the Amalekites and the Canaanites dwelt in the valley.) <sup>m</sup>To-morrow turn you, and get you into the wilderness by the way of the <sup>n</sup>Red Sea.

26 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,

27 <sup>o</sup>How long *shall I bear with* this evil congregation, which murmur against me? I have heard the murmurings of the children of Israel, which they murmur against me.

28 Say unto them, *As truly as* <sup>p</sup>I live, saith the LORD, as ye have spoken in mine ears, so will I do to you:

29 Your carcases shall <sup>q</sup>fall in this wilderness; and all that were <sup>r</sup>numbered of you, according to your whole number, from twenty years old and upward, which have murmured against me,

30 Doubtless ye shall not come into the land *concerning* which <sup>†</sup>I sware to make you dwell therein, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.
