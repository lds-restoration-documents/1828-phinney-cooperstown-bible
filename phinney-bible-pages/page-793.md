27 <sup>x</sup>And shall not uncircumcision which is by nature, if it fulfil the law, judge thee, who by the letter and circumcision dost transgress the law?

28 <sup>y</sup>For he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly; neither *is that* circumcision which is outward in the flesh:

29 <sup>z</sup>But he *is* a Jew, which is one inwardly; and circumcision *is that* of the heart, in the spirit, *and* not in the letter; whose praise *is* not of men, but of God.

## CHAPTER III.

1 The Jews' prerogative not made void by the unbelief of some....20 None justified by the law, but all by God's grace through faith in Christ without difference, &c.

<sup>a</sup>WHAT advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit *is there* of circumcision?

2 <sup>b</sup>Much every way: chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God.

3 <sup>c</sup>For what, if some did not believe? shall their unbelief make the faith of God without effect?

4 <sup>d</sup>God forbid: yea, let God be true, but every man a liar; as it is written, That thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged.

5 <sup>e</sup>But if our unrighteousness commend the righteousness of God, what shall we say? *Is* God unrighteous who taketh vengeance? (I speak as a man,)

6 <sup>f</sup>God forbid: for then how shall God judge the world?

7 For if the truth of God hath more abounded through my lie unto his glory, why yet am I also judged as a sinner?

8 <sup>g</sup>And not *rather* (as we be slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say) Let us do evil, that good may come? whose damnation is just.

9 <sup>h</sup>What then? are we better *than they?* No, in no wise: for we have before proved both Jews and Gentiles, that they are all under sin;

10 <sup>i</sup>As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one:

11 There is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh after God.

12 They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable; there is none that doeth good, no, not one.

13 <sup>k</sup>Their throat *is* an open sepulchre: with their tongues they have used deceit; the poison of asps *is* under their lips:

14 <sup>l</sup>Whose mouth *is* full of cursing and bitterness:

15 <sup>m</sup>Their feet *are* swift to shed blood:

16 Destruction and misery *are* in their ways:

17 And the way of peace have they not known:

18 <sup>n</sup>There is no fear of God before their eyes.

19 <sup>o</sup>Now we know, that what things soever the law saith, it saith to them who are under the law; that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may become guilty before God.

20 <sup>p</sup>Therefore by the deeds of the law there shall no flesh be justified in his sight: for by the law *is* the knowledge of sin.

21 <sup>q</sup>But now the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets;

22 <sup>r</sup>Even the righteousness of God, *which is* by faith of Jesus Christ, unto all and upon all them that believe; for there is no difference:

23 <sup>s</sup>For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;

24 <sup>t</sup>Being justified freely by his grace, through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus:

25 <sup>u</sup>Whom God hath set forth *to be* a propitiation through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God;

26 To declare, *I say,* at this time, his righteousness: that he might be just, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus.

27 <sup>x</sup>Where *is* boasting then? It is excluded. By what law? of works? Nay; but by the law of faith.

28 <sup>y</sup>Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law.

29 <sup>z</sup>*Is he* the God of the Jews only? *is he* not also of the Gentiles? Yes, of the Gentiles also:

30 <sup>a</sup>Seeing *it is* one God, which shall justify the circumcision by faith, and uncircumcision through faith.

31 <sup>b</sup>Do we then make void the law through faith? God forbid: yea, we establish the law.

## CHAPTER IV.

1 Abraham justified by faith: 18 his faith recorded for the sake of those who believe.

<sup>a</sup>WHAT shall we then say, that Abraham, our father, as pertaining to the flesh, hath found?

2 <sup>b</sup>For if Abraham were justified by works, he hath *whereof* to glory: but not before God.

3 <sup>c</sup>For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness.
