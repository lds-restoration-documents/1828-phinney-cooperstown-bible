22 Surely there was not holden such a passover <sup>r</sup>from the days of the judges that judged Israel, nor in all the days of the kings of Israel, nor of the kings of Judah;

23 But in the eighteenth year of king Josiah, *wherein* this passover was holden to the LORD in <sup>s</sup>Jerusalem.

24 Moreover, the *workers with* <sup>t</sup>familiar spirits, and the wizards, and the images, and the idols, and all the abominations that were spied in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, did Josiah put away, that he might perform the words of the <sup>u</sup>law *which were* written in the book that <sup>x</sup>Hilkiah the priest found in the house of the LORD.

25 And <sup>y</sup>like unto him was there no king before him, that turned to the LORD <sup>z</sup>with all his heart, and with all his soul, and with all his might, according to all the law of Moses; neither after him arose there *any* like him.

26 <sup>a</sup>Notwithstanding the LORD turned not from the fierceness of his great wrath, wherewith his anger was kindled against Judah, because of all the <sup>†</sup>provocations that Manasseh had provoked him withal.

27 And the LORD said, I will remove <sup>b</sup>Judah also out of my sight, as I have removed <sup>c</sup>Israel, and will cast off this city Jerusalem which I have chosen, and the house of which I said, <sup>d</sup>My name shall be there.

28 Now the rest of the acts of Josiah, and all that he did, *are* they not written in the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Judah?

29 In <sup>e</sup>his days Pharaoh-nechoh king of Egypt went up against the king of Assyria to the river Euphrates: and king Josiah went against him; and he <sup>f</sup>slew him at Megiddo, when he had <sup>g</sup>seen him.

30 And his servants carried him in a chariot dead from Megiddo, and brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his own sepulchre. And the people of the land took <sup>||</sup>Jehoahaz the son of Josiah, and anointed him, and made him king in his father's stead.

31 ¶ Jehoahaz *was* twenty and three years old when he began to reign; and he reigned <sup>h</sup>three months in Jerusalem. And his mother's name *was* Hamutal, the daughter of Jeremiah of <sup>i</sup>Libnah.

32 And he did *that which was* evil in the sight of the LORD, according to all that <sup>k</sup>his fathers had done.

33 And Pharaoh-nechoh put him in <sup>l</sup>bands at <sup>m</sup>Riblah, in the land of Hamath, that he might not reign in Jerusalem; and put the land to a tribute of a hundred talents of silver, and a talent of gold.

34 And Pharaoh-nechoh made Eliakim the son of Josiah king in the room of Josiah his father, and <sup>n</sup>turned his name to <sup>o</sup>Jehoiakim, and took Jehoahaz away: and he came to Egypt, and <sup>p</sup>died there.

35 And Jehoiakim <sup>q</sup>gave the silver and the gold to Pharaoh; but he taxed the land to give the money according to the commandment of Pharaoh: he exacted the silver and the gold of the people of the land, of every one according to his taxation, to give *it* unto Pharaoh-nechoh.

36 ¶ Jehoiakim *was* <sup>r</sup>twenty and five years old when he began to reign; and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name *was* Zebudah, the daughter of Pedaiah of Rumah.

37 And he did *that which was* <sup>s</sup>evil in the sight of the LORD, according to all that his fathers had done.

## CHAPTER XXIV.

8 Jehoiachin's evil reign....10 Jerusalem is taken.

IN <sup>a</sup>his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon <sup>b</sup>came up, and Jehoiakim became his servant three years: then he turned and rebelled against him.

2 And <sup>c</sup>the LORD sent against him <sup>d</sup>bands of the Chaldees, and bands of the Syrians, and bands of the Moabites, and bands of the children of Ammon, and sent them against Judah to destroy it, according to the word of the LORD, which he spake by his <sup>e</sup>servants the prophets.

3 Surely at the commandment of the LORD came *this* upon Judah, to remove *them* <sup>f</sup>out of his sight for the sins of <sup>g</sup>Manasseh, according to all that he did;

4 And also for the <sup>h</sup>innocent blood that he shed, (for he filled Jerusalem with innocent blood,) which the LORD would <sup>i</sup>not pardon.

5 Now the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and all that he did, *are* they not written in the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Judah?

6 So Jehoiakim <sup>k</sup>slept with his fathers: and Jehoiachin his son reigned in his stead.

7 And the king of Egypt came <sup>l</sup>not again any more out of his land: for the king of Babylon had taken, from the river of Egypt unto the <sup>m</sup>river Euphrates, <sup>n</sup>all that pertained to the king of Egypt.

8 ¶ Jehoiachin *was* eighteen years old when he began to reign: and he reigned in Jerusalem <sup>o</sup>three months. And his mother's name *was* Nehushta, the daughter of Elnathan of Jerusalem.

9 And he did *that which was* evil in the sight of the LORD, according to all that <sup>p</sup>his father had done.

10 ¶ At <sup>q</sup>that time the servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against Jerusalem, and the city was besieged.

11 And Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came against the city, and his servants did besiege it.

12 And Jehoiachin the king of Judah went out to the king of Babylon, he, and his mother, and his servants, and his princes, and his officers; and the king of Babylon <sup>r</sup>took him in the eighth year of <sup>||</sup>his reign.

13 And he carried out thence <sup>s</sup>all the treasures of the house of the LORD, and the treasure of the king's house, and cut in pieces all the vessels of gold which Solomon king of Israel had made in the temple of the LORD, as the LORD had said.

14 And he carried away all Jerusalem, and all the princes, and all the mighty men of valour, *even* ten thousand captives, and all the craftsmen and smiths.
