17 And Abijah and his people slew them with a great slaughter: so there fell down slain of Israel <sup>e</sup>five hundred thousand chosen men.

18 Thus the children of Israel were <sup>†</sup>brought under at that time, and the children of Judah prevailed, because they <sup>f</sup>relied upon the LORD God of their fathers.

20 Neither did Jeroboam recover strength again in the days of Abijah: and the LORD <sup>h</sup>struck him, and he died.

21 But Abijah <sup>i</sup>waxed mighty, and married fourteen wives, and begat twenty and two sons, and sixteen daughters.

## CHAPTER XIV.

Asa destroyeth idolatry, and overcometh Zerah.

SO Abijah <sup>a</sup>slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; and Asa his son reigned in his stead. In his days the land was quiet <sup>b</sup>ten years.

2 And Asa did *that which was* <sup>c</sup>good and right in the eyes of the LORD his God:

3 For he took away the <sup>d</sup>altars of the strange *gods,* and the <sup>e</sup>high places, and brake down the images, and cut down the groves;

4 And commanded Judah <sup>f</sup>to seek the LORD God of their fathers, and to do the law and the commandment.

5 Also he took away, out of all the cities of Judah, the high places and the images: and the kingdom was <sup>g</sup>quiet before him.

6 And he built fenced cities in Judah: for the land had rest, and he had no war in those years; because <sup>h</sup>the LORD had given him rest.

7 Therefore he said unto Judah, Let us build these cities, and make about *them* walls and towers, gates and bars, *while* the land *is* yet before us; because we have sought the LORD our God, we have sought *him,* and he hath given us rest on every side. So they built, and prospered.

8 And Asa had an army *of men* that bare targets and spears, out of Judah three hundred thousand, and out of Benjamin, that bare shields and <sup>†</sup>drew bows, two hundred and fourscore thousand: all these *were* mighty men of valour.

9 And there came out against them Zerah the Ethiopian, with a host of <sup>i</sup>a thousand thousand, and three hundred chariots, and came unto <sup>k</sup>Mareshah.

10 Then Asa went out against him, and they set the battle in array in the valley of <sup>l</sup>Zephathah at Mareshah.

11 And Asa <sup>m</sup>cried unto the LORD his God, and said, LORD, *it is* <sup>n</sup>nothing with thee to help, whether with many, or with them that have no power: help us, O LORD our God; for we rest on thee, and <sup>o</sup>in thy name we go against this multitude. O LORD, thou *art* our God; let not man prevail <sup>p</sup>against thee.

12 So the <sup>q</sup>LORD smote the Ethiopians before Asa, and before Judah; and the Ethiopians fled.

13 And Asa, and the people that *were* with him, pursued them unto <sup>r</sup>Gerar; and the Ethiopians were overthrown, that they could not recover themselves; for they were destroyed before the LORD, and before his host: and they carried away very much spoil.

14 And they smote all the cities round about Gerar: for the <sup>s</sup>fear of the LORD came upon them: and they spoiled all the cities; for there was exceeding much spoil in them.

15 They smote also the <sup>t</sup>tents of cattle, and carried away sheep and camels in abundance, and returned to <sup>u</sup>Jerusalem.

## CHAPTER XV.

Asa and Judah make a covenant with God.

2 And he went out <sup>b</sup>to meet Asa, and said unto him, Hear ye me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin: The LORD <sup>c</sup>*is* with you while ye be with him: and if ye <sup>d</sup>seek him, he will be <sup>e</sup>found of you; but if ye forsake <sup>f</sup>him, he will forsake <sup>g</sup>you.

3 Now for <sup>h</sup>a long season Israel *hath been* without the true God, and without a teaching priest, and without law:

4 But when they in their trouble did turn unto the LORD God of Israel, and sought him, he was found of them.

5 And in <sup>i</sup>those times *there was* no peace to him that went out, nor to him that came in, but great vexations *were* upon <sup>k</sup>all the inhabitants of the countries.

6 And <sup>l</sup>nation was destroyed of nation, and city of city: for God did vex them with all adversity.

7 Be ye <sup>m</sup>strong, therefore, and let not your hands be weak: <sup>n</sup>for your work shall be rewarded.

8 ¶ And when Asa heard these words, and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage, and put away <sup>†</sup>the abominable idols out of all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and out of the <sup>o</sup>cities which he had taken from mount Ephraim, and renewed the <sup>p</sup>altar of the LORD that *was* before the <sup>q</sup>porch of the LORD.

9 And he gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and the <sup>r</sup>strangers with them out of Ephraim and Manasseh, and out of Simeon: (for they fell to him out of Israel in abundance, when they saw that the LORD his God *was* with him:)

10 So they gathered themselves together at Jerusalem in <sup>s</sup>the third month, in the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.

11 And they offered unto the LORD the same time, of the spoil *which* they had brought, seven hundred oxen, and seven thousand sheep.
