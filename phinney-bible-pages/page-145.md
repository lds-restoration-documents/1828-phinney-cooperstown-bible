## CHAPTER XVII.

1 The things sacrificed must be sound....2 Idolaters must be slain.

THOU shalt not sacrifice unto the LORD thy God *any* bullock or sheep wherein is <sup>a</sup>blemish, *or* any evil-favouredness; for that *is* an <sup>b</sup>abomination unto the LORD thy God.

2 ¶ If there be found among you, within any of thy gates which the LORD thy God giveth thee, <sup>c</sup>man or woman, that hath wrought wickedness in the sight of the LORD thy God, in transgressing his covenant,

3 And hath gone and served other gods, and worshipped them, either the <sup>d</sup>sun, or moon, or any of the <sup>e</sup>host of heaven, which I have <sup>f</sup>not commanded;

4 And it be told thee, and thou hast heard *of it,* and <sup>g</sup>inquired diligently, and, behold, *it be* true, *and* the thing certain, *that* such abomination is wrought in Israel:

5 Then shalt thou bring forth that man or that woman, which have committed that wicked thing, unto thy <sup>h</sup>gates, *even* that man or that woman, and shalt <sup>i</sup>stone them with stones, till they die.

6 At the <sup>k</sup>mouth of two witnesses, or three witnesses, shall he that is worthy of death be put to death; *but* at the mouth of one witness he shall not be put to death.

7 The hands of the witnesses shall be <sup>l</sup>first upon him to put him to death, and afterward the hands of all the people. <sup>m</sup>So thou shalt put the evil away from among you.

8 ¶ If there arise a matter too hard for thee in judgment, between <sup>n</sup>blood and blood, between <sup>o</sup>plea and plea, and between stroke and stroke, *being* <sup>p</sup>matters of controversy within thy gates; then shalt thou arise, and get thee up into the place which the LORD thy God shall choose;

9 And thou shalt come unto the <sup>q</sup>priests the Levites, and unto the judge that shall be in those days, and inquire; and they shall shew thee the sentence of judgment:

10 And thou shalt do according to the sentence, which they of that place which the LORD shall choose shall shew thee; and thou shalt observe to do according to all that they <sup>r</sup>inform thee.

11 <sup>s</sup>According to the sentence of the law which they shall teach thee, and according to the judgment which they shall tell thee, thou shalt do: thou shalt not decline from the sentence which they shall shew thee, *to* the right hand nor *to* the left.

12 And the man that will do <sup>t</sup>presumptuously, and will <sup>u</sup>not hearken unto the priest that <sup>x</sup>standeth to minister there before the LORD thy God, or unto the judge, even that man shall <sup>y</sup>die: and thou shalt put away the evil from Israel.

13 And all the people shall hear, and fear, and do no more presumptuously.

14 ¶ When thou art come unto the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee, and shalt possess it, and shalt dwell therein, and shalt say, I will <sup>||</sup>set a king over me, like as all the nations that *are* about me;
