## CHAPTER XX.

By the direction of the prophet the Syrians are slain.

AND <sup>a</sup>Ben-hadad the king of Syria gathered all his host together: and *there were* <sup>b</sup>thirty and two kings with him, and horses and chariots: and he went up and besieged Samaria, and warred against it.

2 And he <sup>c</sup>sent messengers to Ahab king of Israel into the city, and said unto him, Thus saith Ben-hadad,

3 <sup>d</sup>Thy silver and thy gold *is* mine: thy wives also and thy children, *even* the goodliest, *are* mine.

4 And the king of Israel answered and said, My lord, O king, according to thy saying, <sup>e</sup>I *am* thine, and all that I have.

5 And the messengers came again, and said, Thus speaketh Ben-hadad, saying, Although I have sent unto thee, saying, Thou shalt deliver me thy silver, and thy gold, and thy wives, and thy children;

6 Yet I will send my servants unto thee to-morrow about this time, and they shall search thine house, and the houses of thy servants; and it shall be, *that* whatsoever is <sup>†</sup>pleasant in thine eyes, they shall put *it* in their hand, and take *it* away.

7 Then the king of Israel called all the elders <sup>f</sup>of the land, and said, Mark, I pray you, and see how this *man* seeketh <sup>g</sup>mischief: for he sent unto me for my wives, and for my children, and for my silver, and for my gold, and <sup>†</sup>I denied him not.

8 And all the elders and all the people said unto him, Hearken not *unto him,* <sup>h</sup>nor consent.

9 Wherefore he said unto the messengers of Ben-hadad, Tell my lord the king, all that thou didst send for to thy servant at the first I will do: but this thing I may not do. And the messengers departed, and brought him word again.

10 And Ben-hadad sent unto him, and said, <sup>i</sup>The gods do so unto me, and more also, if the <sup>k</sup>dust of Samaria shall suffice for handfuls for all the people that <sup>†</sup>follow me.

11 And the king of Israel answered and said, Tell *him,* <sup>l</sup>Let not him that girdeth on *his harness* boast himself, as he that putteth it off.

12 And it came to pass, when *Ben-hadad* heard this message, as he *was* <sup>m</sup>drinking, he and the kings in the pavilions, that he said unto his servants, Set *yourselves in array:* and they set *themselves in array* against the city.

13 And, behold, there <sup>n</sup>came a prophet unto Ahab king of Israel, saying, Thus saith the LORD, Hast thou seen all this <sup>o</sup>great multitude? behold, I will deliver it into thine hand this day; and thou shalt <sup>p</sup>know that I *am* the LORD.

14 And Ahab said, By whom? And he said, Thus saith the LORD, *Even* by the <sup>q</sup>young men of the princes of the provinces. Then he said, Who shall order the battle? And he answered, <sup>r</sup>Thou.

15 Then he numbered the young men of the princes of the provinces, and they were two hundred and thirty-two: and after them he numbered all the people, *even* all the children of Israel, *being* seven thousand.

16 And they went out <sup>s</sup>at noon: but Ben-hadad *was* drinking himself <sup>t</sup>drunk in the pavilions, he and the kings, the thirty and two kings that helped him.

17 And the young men of the princes of the provinces went out first; and Ben-hadad sent out, and they told him, saying, There are men come out of Samaria.

18 And he said, Whether they be come out for peace, take them alive; or whether they be come out for war, <sup>u</sup>take them alive.

19 So these young men of the princes of the provinces came out of the city, and the army which followed them.

20 And they slew <sup>||</sup>every one his man: and the Syrians <sup>x</sup>fled; and Israel pursued them: and Ben-hadad the king of Syria escaped on a horse with the horsemen.

21 And <sup>y</sup>the king of Israel went out, and smote the horses and chariots, and slew the Syrians with a great slaughter.

22 And the prophet came to the king of Israel, and <sup>z</sup>said unto him, Go, <sup>a</sup>strengthen thyself, and mark and see what thou doest: for at the <sup>b</sup>return of the year the king of Syria will come up against thee.

23 And the servants of the king of Syria said unto him, Their gods *are* gods of the <sup>c</sup>hills, therefore they were stronger than we: but let us fight against them in the plain, and surely we shall be stronger than they.

24 And do this thing: <sup>d</sup>Take the kings away, every man out of his place, and put captains in their rooms:

25 And number thee an army like the army that thou hast lost, horse for horse, and chariot for chariot: and we will fight against them in the plain, *and* surely we shall be stronger than they. And he hearkened unto their voice, and did so.

26 And it came to pass, at the return of the year, that Ben-hadad numbered the Syrians, and went up <sup>e</sup>to Aphek, to fight against Israel.

27 And the children of Israel were numbered, and were all present, and went against them: and the children of Israel pitched before them like two little flocks of kids; but the Syrians <sup>f</sup>filled the country.

28 And there came <sup>g</sup>a man of God, and spake unto the king of Israel, and said, Thus saith the LORD, <sup>h</sup>Because the Syrians have said, The LORD *is* God of the hills, but he *is* not God of the valleys; therefore will I deliver all this great multitude into thine hand, *and* ye shall know that I *am* the LORD.

29 And they pitched one over against the other <sup>i</sup>seven days; and *so* it was, that in the seventh day the battle was joined: and the children of Israel slew of the Syrians <sup>k</sup>a hundred thousand footmen in one day.

30 But the rest fled to Aphek, into the city; and *there* a wall fell upon twenty and seven thousand of the men *that were* left. And Ben-hadad fled, and came into the city, <sup>†</sup>into an inner chamber.

31 And his servants said unto him, Behold now, we have heard that the kings of the house of Israel *are* merciful kings: let us, I pray thee, <sup>l</sup>put sackcloth on our loins, and ropes upon our heads, and go out to the king of Israel; peradventure he will save thy life.

32 So they girded sackcloth on their loins, and *put* ropes on their heads, and came to the king of Israel, and said, <sup>m</sup>Thy servant Ben-hadad saith, I pray thee, let me live. And he said, *Is* he yet alive? he *is* my brother.
