## CHAPTER XXVIII.

1 Isaac blesseth Jacob, and sendeth him to Padan-aram. 12 The vision of Jacob's ladder....18 The stone of Bethel. 20 Jacob's vow.

AND Isaac called Jacob, and <sup>a</sup>blessed him, and charged him, and said unto him, Thou shalt not take a wife of the <sup>b</sup>daughters of Canaan.

2 Arise, go to <sup>c</sup>Padan-aram, to the house of Bethuel thy mother's father, and take thee <sup>d</sup>a wife from thence of the daughters of Laban, thy mother's brother.

3 And <sup>e</sup>God Almighty bless thee, and make thee fruitful, and multiply thee, that thou mayest be <sup>†</sup>a multitude of people;

4 And give thee the <sup>f</sup>blessing of Abraham, to thee, and to thy seed with thee; that thou mayest inherit the land <sup>g</sup>wherein thou art a stranger, which God <sup>h</sup>gave unto Abraham.

5 And Isaac sent away Jacob: and he went to Padan-aram, unto Laban, son of Bethuel the Syrian, the brother of Rebekah, Jacob's and Esau's mother.

6 When Esau saw that Isaac had blessed Jacob, and sent him away to Padan-aram, to take him a wife from thence; and that, as he blessed him, he gave him a charge, saying, Thou shalt not take a wife of the daughters of Canaan;

7 And that Jacob obeyed his father and his mother, and was gone to Padan-aram;

8 And Esau seeing that the daughters of Canaan <sup>i</sup>pleased not Isaac his father;

9 Then went Esau unto <sup>k</sup>Ishmael, and took unto the wives which he had, <sup>l</sup>Mahalath the daughter of Ishmael, Abraham's son, <sup>m</sup>the sister of Nebajoth, to be his wife.

10 ¶ And Jacob went out from Beer-sheba, and went toward Haran.

11 And he lighted upon a certain place, and tarried there all night, because the sun was set: and he took of the <sup>n</sup>stones of that place, and put *them for* his <sup>o</sup>pillows, and lay down in that place to sleep.

12 And he <sup>p</sup>dreamed, and, behold, <sup>q</sup>a ladder set up on the <sup>r</sup>earth, and the <sup>s</sup>top of it reached to heaven: and, behold, the angels of God <sup>t</sup>ascending and descending on it.

13 And, behold, the LORD <sup>u</sup>stood above it, and said, I *am* the LORD God of <sup>x</sup>Abraham thy father, and the God of Isaac: the land whereon thou liest, to thee will I give it, and <sup>y</sup>to thy seed;

14 And thy seed shall be as the dust of the earth; and thou shalt <sup>†</sup>spread abroad to the west, and to the east, and to the north, and to the south: and in thee, and in thy seed, shall <sup>z</sup>all the families of the earth be blessed.

15 And, behold, I *am* with thee, and will keep thee in all *places* whither thou goest, and will bring thee again into this land: <sup>a</sup>for I will not leave thee, until I have done *that* which I have spoken to thee of.

16 And Jacob awaked out of his sleep, and he said, Surely the LORD is in this place, and I knew *it* not.

17 And he was afraid, and said, How dreadful *is* this place! this *is* none other but the house of God, and this *is* the gate of heaven.

18 And Jacob rose up early in the morning, and took the stone that he had put *for* his pillows, and set it up *for* a pillar, and poured oil upon the top of it.

19 And he called the name of that place <sup>b</sup>Beth-el: but the name of that city *was called* Luz at the first.

20 And Jacob vowed a vow, saying, If God will be with me, and will keep me in this way that I go, and will give me <sup>c</sup>bread to eat, and raiment to put on,

21 So that I come again to my father's house in peace, then shall the LORD be <sup>d</sup>my God:

22 And this stone, which I have set *for* a pillar, shall be <sup>e</sup>God's house: and of all that thou shalt give me, <sup>†</sup>I will surely give the tenth unto thee.

## CHAPTER XXIX.

1 Jacob cometh to the well of Haran: 18 He covenanteth for Rachel: 23 He is deceived with Leah: 28 He marrieth also Rachel, and serveth for her seven years more.

THEN Jacob <sup>†</sup>went on his journey, and came into the land of the people of the east.

2 And he looked, and behold <sup>a</sup>a well in the field, and, lo, there *were* three flocks of sheep <sup>b</sup>lying by it; for out of that well they watered the flocks: and a great stone *was* upon the well's mouth.

3 And thither were all the flocks gathered: and they <sup>c</sup>rolled the stone from the well's mouth, and watered the sheep, and <sup>d</sup>put the stone again upon the well's mouth in his place.

4 And Jacob said unto them, My brethren, whence *be* ye? And they said, Of Haran *are* we.

5 And he said unto them, Know ye Laban <sup>e</sup>the son of Nahor? And they said, We know *him*.

6 And he said unto them, <sup>†</sup>*Is* he well? And they said, *He is* well; and, behold, Rachel his daughter cometh with the sheep.

7 And he said, Lo, *it is* <sup>f</sup>yet high day, neither *is it* time that the cattle should be gathered together: water ye the sheep, and go *and* feed *them*.

8 And they said, <sup>g</sup>We cannot until all the flocks be gathered together, and *till* they roll the stone from the well's mouth; then we water the sheep.

9 And <sup>h</sup>while he yet spake with them, Rachel came with her father's sheep; for she <sup>i</sup>kept them.

10 And it came to pass, when Jacob saw Rachel the daughter of Laban his mother's brother, and the sheep of Laban his mother's brother, that Jacob went near, and rolled the stone from the well's mouth, and watered the flock of Laban his mother's brother.

11 And Jacob <sup>k</sup>kissed Rachel, and <sup>l</sup>lifted up his voice, and wept.

12 And Jacob told Rachel that he *was* her <sup>m</sup>father's brother, and that he *was* Rebekah's son: and she ran and told her father.

13 And it came to pass, when Laban heard the tidings of Jacob his sister's son, that he ran to meet him, and embraced him, and kissed him, and brought him to his house. And he told Laban all <sup>n</sup>these things.

14 And Laban said to him, Surely thou *art* <sup>o</sup>my bone and my flesh. And he abode with him the space of a month.

15 And Laban said unto Jacob, Because thou *art* <sup>p</sup>my brother, shouldest thou therefore serve me for nought? tell me, what *shall* thy wages *be*?

16 And Laban had two daughters: the name of the elder *was* Leah, and the name of the younger *was* Rachel.

17 Leah *was* tender-eyed; but Rachel was beautiful and well-favoured.

18 And Jacob loved Rachel: and said, I will <sup>q</sup>serve thee seven years for Rachel thy younger daughter.

19 And Laban said, *It is* <sup>r</sup>better that I give her to thee, than that I should give her to another man: abide with me.
