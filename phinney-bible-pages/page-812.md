## CHAPTER V.

Paul, in assured hope of immortal glory, laboureth to approve himself to Christ, &c.

<sup>a</sup>FOR we know that if our earthly house of *this* tabernacle were dissolved, we have a building of God, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.

2 <sup>b</sup>For in this we groan, earnestly desiring to be clothed upon with our house which is from heaven:

3 <sup>c</sup>If so be that being clothed we shall not be found naked.

4 <sup>d</sup>For we that are in *this* tabernacle do groan, being burdened: not for that we would be unclothed, but clothed upon, that mortality might be swallowed up of life.

5 <sup>e</sup>Now he that hath wrought us for the selfsame thing *is* God, who also hath given unto us the earnest of the Spirit.

6 <sup>f</sup>Therefore *we are* always confident, knowing that, whilst we are at home in the body, we are absent from the Lord;

7 <sup>g</sup>(For we walk by faith, not by sight:)

8 <sup>h</sup>We are confident, *I say,* and willing rather to be absent from the body, and to be present with the Lord.

9 ¶ <sup>i</sup>Wherefore we labour, that, whether present or absent, we may be accepted of him.

10 <sup>k</sup>For we must all appear before the judgment-seat of Christ; that every one may receive the things *done* in *his* body, according to that he hath done, whether *it be* good or bad.

11 <sup>l</sup>Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men; but we are made manifest unto God, and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences.

12 <sup>m</sup>For we commend not ourselves again unto you, but give you occasion to glory on our behalf, that ye may have somewhat to *answer* them which glory in appearance, and not in heart.

13 <sup>n</sup>For whether we be beside ourselves, *it is* to God: or whether we be sober, *it is* for your cause.

14 For the love of Christ constraineth us; because we thus judge, that if one died for all, then were all dead:

15 <sup>o</sup>And *that* he died for all, that they which live should not henceforth live unto themselves, but unto him which died for them, and rose again.

16 <sup>p</sup>Wherefore henceforth know we no man after the flesh: yea, though we have known Christ after the flesh, yet now henceforth know we *him* no more.

17 <sup>q</sup>Therefore if any man *be* in Christ, *he is* a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.

18 <sup>r</sup>And all things *are* of God, who hath reconciled us to himself by Jesus Christ, and hath given to us the ministry of reconciliation;

19 <sup>s</sup>To wit, that God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself, not imputing their trespasses unto them; and hath committed unto us the word of reconciliation.

20 <sup>t</sup>Now then we are ambassadors for Christ; as though God did beseech *you* by us, we pray *you* in Christ's stead, be ye reconciled to God.

21 <sup>u</sup>For he hath made him *to be* sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.

## CHAPTER VI.

1 Paul's sufferings and patience in the ministry....14 Exhortation to avoid idolaters, &c.

<sup>a</sup>WE then, *as* workers together *with him,* beseech *you* also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain.

2 <sup>b</sup>(For he saith, I have heard thee in a time accepted, and in the day of salvation have I succoured thee: behold, now *is* the accepted time; behold, now *is* the day of salvation.)

3 <sup>c</sup>Giving no offence in any thing, that the ministry be not blamed:

4 <sup>d</sup>But in all *things* approving ourselves, as the ministers of God, in much patience, in afflictions, in necessities, in distresses,

5 <sup>e</sup>In stripes, in imprisonments, in tumults, in labours, in watchings, in fastings;

6 <sup>f</sup>By pureness, by knowledge, by long-suffering, by kindness, by the Holy Ghost, by love unfeigned,

7 <sup>g</sup>By the word of truth, by the power of God, by the armour of righteousness on the right hand and on the left,

8 <sup>h</sup>By honour and dishonour, by evil report and good report: as deceivers, and *yet* true;

9 <sup>i</sup>As unknown, and *yet* well known; as dying, and, behold, we live; as chastened, and not killed;

10 <sup>k</sup>As sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; as poor, yet making many rich; as having nothing, and *yet* possessing all things.

11 <sup>l</sup>O *ye* Corinthians, our mouth is open unto you, our heart is enlarged.

12 <sup>m</sup>Ye are not straitened in us, but ye are straitened in your own bowels.

13 <sup>n</sup>Now for a recompense in the same, (I speak as unto *my* children,) be ye also enlarged.

14 <sup>o</sup>Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers: for what fellowship hath righteousness with unrighteousness? and what communion hath light with darkness?

15 <sup>p</sup>And what concord hath Christ with Belial? or what part hath he that believeth with an infidel?

16 <sup>q</sup>And what agreement hath the temple of God with idols? for ye are the temple of the living God; as God hath said, I will dwell in them, and walk in *them;* and I will be their God, and they shall be my people.
