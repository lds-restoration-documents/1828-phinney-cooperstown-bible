20 Thou makest <sup>z</sup>darkness, and it is night, wherein all the beasts of the forest do creep *forth.*

21 The young lions roar after their <sup>a</sup>prey, and seek their meat <sup>b</sup>from God.

22 The <sup>c</sup>sun ariseth, they gather themselves together, and lay them down in their dens.

23 Man goeth forth unto his <sup>d</sup>work, and to his labour, until the evening.

24 O LORD, <sup>e</sup>how manifold are thy works! <sup>f</sup>in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is <sup>g</sup>full of thy riches;

25 *So is* this great and wide sea, wherein *are* things creeping innumerable, both small and great beasts.

26 There go the ships; *there is* that <sup>h</sup>leviathan, *whom* thou hast made to play therein.

27 These <sup>i</sup>wait all upon thee, that thou mayest give *them* their meat in due season.

28 *That* thou givest them, they gather: thou <sup>k</sup>openest thy hand, they are filled with good.

29 Thou hidest thy face, they are troubled; thou takest away their breath, they die, and return to their <sup>l</sup>dust.

30 <sup>m</sup>Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created: and thou <sup>n</sup>renewest the face of the earth.

31 The glory of the LORD shall endure for ever: the LORD shall <sup>o</sup>rejoice in his works.

32 He looketh on the earth, and it <sup>p</sup>trembleth; he toucheth the hills, and they <sup>q</sup>smoke.

33 I will sing unto the LORD <sup>r</sup>as long as I live; I will sing praise to my God while I have my being.

34 My meditation of him shall be sweet: I will be glad in the LORD.

35 Let the sinners be <sup>s</sup>consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless thou the LORD, O my soul. <sup>†</sup>Praise ye the LORD.

## PSALM CV.

An exhortation to praise God, and to seek out his works.

O GIVE <sup>a</sup>thanks unto the LORD; call upon his name; <sup>b</sup>make known his deeds among the people.

2 Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him: <sup>c</sup>talk ye of all his wondrous works.

3 <sup>d</sup>Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart <sup>e</sup>of them rejoice that seek the LORD.

4 Seek the LORD, and <sup>f</sup>his strength; seek his face evermore.

5 Remember his <sup>g</sup>marvellous works that he hath done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;

6 O ye seed of Abraham his <sup>h</sup>servant, ye children of Jacob his <sup>i</sup>chosen.

7 He *is* the LORD our God; his <sup>k</sup>judgments *are* in all the earth.

8 He hath remembered his covenant for ever, the word *which* he <sup>l</sup>commanded to a thousand generations:

9 Which *covenant* he made <sup>m</sup>with Abraham, and his oath unto Isaac;

10 And confirmed the same unto Jacob for a law, *and* to Israel *for* an everlasting covenant;

11 Saying, Unto thee will I give the <sup>n</sup>land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance:

12 When they were *but* a few men in number; yea, very few, and <sup>o</sup>strangers in it.

13 When they went from one nation to another, from *one* kingdom to another people,

14 He suffered no man to do them wrong; yea, he <sup>p</sup>reproved kings for their sakes,

15 *Saying,* Touch not mine <sup>q</sup>anointed, and do my <sup>r</sup>prophets no harm.

16 Moreover, he <sup>s</sup>called for a famine upon the land: he brake the whole <sup>t</sup>staff of bread.

17 <sup>u</sup>He sent a man before them, *even* Joseph, *who* was <sup>x</sup>sold for a servant;

18 Whose feet they hurt <sup>y</sup>with fetters: he was laid in iron;

19 Until the time that <sup>z</sup>his word came; the word of the LORD <sup>a</sup>tried him.

20 The king <sup>b</sup>sent and loosed him; *even* the ruler of the people, and let him go free.

21 He made him <sup>c</sup>lord of his house, and ruler of all his substance;

22 To <sup>d</sup>bind his princes at his pleasure, and teach his senators wisdom.

23 <sup>e</sup>Israel also came into Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of <sup>f</sup>Ham.

24 And he <sup>g</sup>increased his people greatly, and made them stronger than their enemies.

25 He turned their heart to hate his people, to deal <sup>h</sup>subtilly with his servants.

26 <sup>i</sup>He sent Moses his servant, *and* Aaron whom he had chosen.

27 They shewed his <sup>k</sup>signs among them, and wonders in the land of Ham.

28 He sent <sup>l</sup>darkness, and made it dark; and they <sup>m</sup>rebelled not against his word.

29 He turned their <sup>n</sup>waters into blood, and slew their fish.

30 The land brought forth frogs <sup>o</sup>in abundance, in the <sup>p</sup>chambers of their kings.

31 He spake, and there came <sup>q</sup>divers sorts of flies, *and* <sup>r</sup>lice in all their coasts.

32 He gave them <sup>s</sup>hail for rain, *and* <sup>t</sup>flaming fire in their land.

33 He smote their vines also, and their fig-trees; and brake the trees of their coasts.

34 He spake, and the <sup>u</sup>locusts came, and caterpillars, and that without number,

35 And did eat up all the herbs in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground.

36 He smote also all the <sup>x</sup>first-born in their land, the <sup>y</sup>chief of all their strength.

37 He brought them forth also with silver and gold; and *there was* <sup>z</sup>not one feeble *person* among their tribes.

38 Egypt was glad when they departed; for <sup>a</sup>the fear of them fell upon them.

39 He spread <sup>b</sup>a cloud for a covering, and <sup>c</sup>fire to give light in the night.

40 *The people* asked, and he brought <sup>d</sup>quails, and satisfied them with the <sup>e</sup>bread of heaven.

41 He opened the <sup>f</sup>rock, and the waters gushed out; they ran in the dry places *like* <sup>g</sup>a river.

42 For he <sup>h</sup>remembered his holy promise, *and* Abraham his servant.

43 And he brought forth his people with joy, *and* his chosen <sup>i</sup>with gladness;

44 And gave them the <sup>k</sup>lands of the heathen: and they inherited the labour of the people;

45 That they might <sup>l</sup>observe his statutes, and keep his laws. Praise ye the LORD.

## PSALM CVI.

The Psalmist exhorteth to praise God.

PRAISE ye the LORD. <sup>a</sup>O give thanks unto the LORD; for *he is* good: for his mercy *endureth* for ever.

2 *Who* can utter the mighty acts of the LORD? *who* can shew forth <sup>b</sup>all his praise?
