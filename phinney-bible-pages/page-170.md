## CHAPTER XVIII.

The tabernacle is set up at Shiloh.

AND the whole congregation of the children of Israel assembled together at <sup>a</sup>Shiloh, and set up the <sup>b</sup>tabernacle of the congregation there. And the land was subdued before them.

2 And there remained among the children of Israel seven tribes which had not yet received their inheritance.

3 And Joshua said unto the children of Israel, <sup>c</sup>How long *are* ye slack to go to possess the land which the LORD God of your fathers hath given you?

4 Give out from among you three men for *each* tribe: and I will send them, and they shall rise and <sup>†</sup>go through the land, and <sup>d</sup>describe it, according to the inheritance of them; and they shall come *again* to me.

5 And they shall divide it into seven parts: <sup>e</sup>Judah shall abide in their coast on the <sup>f</sup>south, and the house of Joseph shall abide in their coasts on the north.

6 Ye shall therefore describe the land *into* seven parts, and bring *the description* hither to me, that I may cast lots for you here <sup>g</sup>before the LORD our God.

7 But the Levites have <sup>h</sup>no part among you; for the <sup>i</sup>priesthood of the LORD *is* their inheritance: and Gad, and Reuben, and half the tribe of Manasseh, have received their inheritance beyond Jordan on the east, which Moses the servant of the LORD gave them.

8 And the men arose, and went away: and Joshua charged them that went to describe the land, saying, Go and walk through the land, and describe it, and come again to me, that I may here cast lots <sup>k</sup>for you before the LORD in Shiloh.

9 And the men went, and passed through the land, and described it by cities, into seven parts, in a book, and came *again* to Joshua to the host <sup>||</sup>at Shiloh.

10 And Joshua <sup>l</sup>cast lots for them in Shiloh before the LORD: and there Joshua divided the land unto the children of Israel, according to their divisions.

11 And the lot of the tribe of the children of Benjamin <sup>m</sup>came up according to their families: and the coast of their lot came up forth <sup>n</sup>between the children of Judah and the children of Joseph.

12 And their border, on the north side, was from Jordan: and the border went up to the side of Jericho on the north side, and went up through the mountains westward; and the goings out thereof were at the wilderness of <sup>o</sup>Beth-aven.

13 And the border went over from thence toward Luz, to the side of <sup>p</sup>Luz, (which *is* Beth-el,) southward; and the border descended to <sup>q</sup>Ataroth-adar, near the hill that *lieth* on the south side of the nether Beth-horon.

14 And the border was drawn *thence,* and compassed the corner of the sea southward, from the hill that *lieth* before Beth-horon southward; and the goings out thereof were at Kirjath-baal (which *is* Kirjath-jearim,) a city of the children of Judah: this *was* the west quarter.

15 And the south quarter *was* from the end of Kirjath-jearim; and the border went out on the west, and went out to the well of waters of <sup>r</sup>Nephtoah:

16 And the border came down to the end of the mountain that *lieth* before the valley of the son of Hinnom, *and* which *is* in the valley of the giants on the north, and descended to the valley of <sup>s</sup>Hinnom, to the side of Jebusi on the south, and descended to <sup>t</sup>En-rogel,

17 And was drawn from the north, and went forth to <sup>u</sup>En-shemesh, and went forth toward <sup>x</sup>Geliloth, which *is* over against the going up of <sup>y</sup>Adummim, and descended to the stone of <sup>z</sup>Bohan the son of Reuben,

18 And passed along toward the side over against <sup>||</sup>Arabah northward, and went down unto Arabah:

19 And the border passed along to the side of <sup>a</sup>Beth-hoglah northward: and the outgoings of the border were at the north bay <sup>†</sup>of the salt sea, at the south end of Jordan: this *was* the south coast.

20 And Jordan was the border of it on the east side. This *was* the inheritance of the children of Benjamin, by the coasts thereof round about, according to their families.

21 Now the cities of the tribe of the children of Benjamin, according to their families, were Jericho, and Beth-hoglah, and <sup>||</sup>the valley of Keziz,

22 And <sup>b</sup>Beth-arabah, and <sup>c</sup>Zemaraim, and Beth-el,

23 And Avim, and Parah, and <sup>d</sup>Ophrah,

24 And Chephar-haammonai, and Ophni, and <sup>e</sup>Gaba; twelve cities with their villages:

25 <sup>f</sup>Gibeon, and <sup>g</sup>Ramah, and <sup>h</sup>Beeroth,

26 And <sup>i</sup>Mizpeh, and <sup>k</sup>Chephirah, and Mozah,

27 And Rekem, and Irpeel, and Taralah,

28 And <sup>l</sup>Zelah, Eleph, and <sup>m</sup>Jebusi (which *is* Jerusalem,) Gibeath, *and* Kirjath; fourteen cities with their villages. This *is* the inheritance of the children of Benjamin, <sup>n</sup>according to their families.

The children of Israel give an inheritance to Joshua.

2 And they had in their inheritance, <sup>c</sup>Beer-sheba or <sup>||</sup>Sheba, and <sup>d</sup>Moladah,

3 And <sup>e</sup>Hazar-shual, and <sup>f</sup>Balah, and Azem,

4 And <sup>g</sup>Eltolad, and Bethul, and <sup>h</sup>Hormah,

5 And <sup>i</sup>Ziklag, and <sup>k</sup>Beth-marcaboth, and Hazar-susah,

6 And <sup>l</sup>Beth-lebaoth, and Sharuhen; thirteen cities and their villages:

7 <sup>m</sup>Ain, <sup>n</sup>Remmon, and <sup>o</sup>Ether, and <sup>p</sup>Ashan; four cities and their villages:
