10 And they burnt all their cities wherein they dwelt, and all their goodly castles, with fire.

11 And they took all the spoil, and all the prey, *both* of men and of beasts.

12 And they brought the captives, and the <sup>m</sup>prey, and the spoil, unto Moses and Eleazar the priest, and unto the congregation of the children of Israel, unto the camp at the plains of Moab, which *are* by Jordan *near* Jericho.

14 And Moses was wroth with the officers of the <sup>†</sup>host, *with* the captains over thousands, and captains over hundreds, which came from the battle.

17 Now therefore kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman that hath <sup>p</sup>known man by lying with <sup>†</sup>him.

23 Every thing that may <sup>z</sup>abide the fire, ye shall make *it* go through the fire, and it shall be clean; nevertheless it shall be purified with the <sup>a</sup>water of separation: and all that abideth not the fire ye shall make go through the <sup>b</sup>water.

24 And ye shall wash your clothes on the seventh day, and ye shall be clean, and afterwards ye shall come into the camp.

25 ¶ And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

26 Take the sum of the prey that was taken, *both* of man and of beast, thou, and Eleazar the priest, and the chief fathers of the congregation;

27 And divide the prey into <sup>c</sup>two parts; between them that took the war upon them, who went out to battle, and between all the congregation:

28 And levy a tribute <sup>d</sup>unto the LORD of the men of war which went out to battle: one soul of five hundred, *both* of the persons, and of the beeves, and of the asses, and of the sheep.

29 Take *it* of their half, and give *it* unto <sup>e</sup>Eleazar the priest, *for* a heave-offering of the LORD.

30 And of the children of Israel's half, thou shalt take one portion of fifty, of the persons, of the beeves, of the asses, and of the flocks, of <sup>f</sup>all manner of beasts, and give them unto the Levites, which <sup>g</sup>keep the charge of the tabernacle of the LORD.

31 And Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the LORD commanded Moses.

33 And threescore and twelve thousand beeves,

34 And threescore and one thousand asses,

35 And thirty and two thousand persons in all, of women that had not known man by lying with him.

36 And the half, *which was* the portion of them that went out to war, was in number three hundred thousand, and seven and thirty thousand and five hundred sheep:

37 And the LORD's tribute of the sheep was six hundred and threescore and fifteen.

38 And the beeves *were* thirty and six thousand; of which the LORD's tribute *was* threescore and twelve.

39 And the asses *were* thirty thousand and five hundred; of which the LORD's <sup>h</sup>tribute *was* threescore and one.

40 And the persons *were* sixteen thousand; of which the LORD's tribute *was* thirty and two persons.

41 And Moses gave the tribute, *which was* the LORD's heave-offering, unto <sup>i</sup>Eleazar the priest; as the LORD commanded Moses.

42 And of the children of Israel's half which Moses divided from the men that warred,

45 And thirty thousand asses and five hundred,

46 And sixteen thousand persons,)

47 Even of the children of Israel's half, Moses took one portion of fifty, *both* of man and of beast, and gave them unto the Levites, which <sup>k</sup>kept the charge of the tabernacle of the LORD; as the LORD commanded Moses.
