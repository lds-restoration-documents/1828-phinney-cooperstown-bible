## CHAPTER XVI.

Job maintaineth his innocence.

THEN Job answered and said,

2 I have heard many <sup>a</sup>such things: <sup>b</sup>miserable comforters *are* ye all.

3 Shall vain words have an end? or what emboldeneth thee that thou answerest?

4 I also <sup>c</sup>could speak as ye *do:* if your soul were in my soul's stead, I could heap up words against you, and shake my head at you.

5 *But* I would strengthen you with my mouth, and the moving of my lips should <sup>d</sup>assuage *your grief.*

6 Though I speak, my grief is not assuaged; and *though* I forbear, what am I <sup>e</sup>eased?

7 But now he hath made <sup>f</sup>me weary: thou hast made desolate all my company.

8 And thou hast filled me <sup>g</sup>with wrinkles, *which is* a witness *against me:* and my leanness rising up in me beareth witness to my face.

9 <sup>h</sup>He teareth *me* in his wrath who hateth me: he gnasheth upon me with his teeth; mine enemy sharpeneth his eyes upon me.

10 They have <sup>i</sup>gaped upon me with their mouth; they have smitten me upon the cheek reproachfully; they have gathered themselves together against me.

11 <sup>k</sup>God hath delivered me to the ungodly, and turned me over into the hands of the wicked.

12 I was <sup>l</sup>at ease, but he hath broken me asunder: he hath also taken *me* by my neck, and shaken me to pieces, and set me up for his mark.

13 His archers compass me round about; he cleaveth my reins asunder, and doth <sup>m</sup>not spare; he poureth out my gall upon the ground.

14 He breaketh me with <sup>n</sup>breach upon breach; he runneth upon me like a giant.

15 I have sewed sackcloth upon my skin, and <sup>o</sup>defiled my horn in the dust.

16 My face is <sup>p</sup>foul with weeping, and on mine eyelids *is* the shadow of death;

17 <sup>q</sup>Not for *any* injustice in my hands: also my prayer *is* pure.

18 O earth, cover not thou my blood, and let <sup>r</sup>my cry have no place.

19 Also now, behold, <sup>s</sup>my witness *is* in heaven, and my record *is* on high.

20 My friends scorn me; *but* mine eye <sup>t</sup>poureth out *tears* unto God.

21 Oh that one might plead for a man with God, as a man <sup>u</sup>*pleadeth* for his neighbour!

22 When a few years are come, then I shall <sup>x</sup>go the way *whence* I shall not return.

## CHAPTER XVII.

Job appealeth from men to God.

MY breath is corrupt, my days are extinct, the <sup>a</sup>graves *are ready* for me.

2 *Are there* not mockers with me? and doth not mine eye continue in their <sup>b</sup>provocation?

3 Lay down now, put me in <sup>c</sup>a surety with thee; who *is* he *that* will strike hands with me?

4 For thou hast <sup>d</sup>hid their heart from understanding: therefore shalt thou not exalt *them.*

5 He that speaketh flattery to *his* friends, even the eyes of his children <sup>e</sup>shall fail.

6 <sup>f</sup>He hath made me also a by-word of the people, and aforetime I was as a tabret.

7 Mine eye also is <sup>g</sup>dim by reason of sorrow, and all my members *are* as a shadow.

8 Upright *men* shall be <sup>h</sup>astonished at this, and the innocent <sup>i</sup>shall stir up himself against the hypocrite.

9 The righteous also shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be <sup>k</sup>stronger and stronger.

10 But as for you all, do you return, and come now: for I cannot find <sup>l</sup>*one* wise *man* among you.

11 My days are past, my purposes are broken off, *even* the thoughts of my heart.

12 They <sup>m</sup>change the night into day: the light *is* short because of darkness.

13 If I <sup>n</sup>wait, the grave *is* my house: I have made my bed in the darkness.

14 I have said to corruption, Thou *art* my father: to the worm, *Thou art* my mother and my sister.

15 And where *is* now <sup>o</sup>my hope? as for my hope, who shall see it?

16 They shall go down to the bars of the pit, when *our* <sup>p</sup>rest together *is* in the dust.

## CHAPTER XVIII.

Bildad reproveth Job of presumption and impatience.

THEN answered <sup>a</sup>Bildad the Shuhite, and said,

2 How long *will it be ere* you <sup>b</sup>make an end of words? mark, and afterwards we will speak.

3 Wherefore are we counted <sup>c</sup>as beasts, *and* reputed vile in your sight?

4 <sup>d</sup>He teareth himself in his anger: shall the earth be forsaken for thee? and shall the rock be removed out of his place?

5 Yea, the light of the wicked shall be <sup>e</sup>put out, and the spark of his fire shall not shine.

6 The light shall be <sup>f</sup>dark in his tabernacle, and his candle shall be put out with him.

7 The steps of his strength shall be straitened, and his <sup>g</sup>own counsel shall cast him down.

8 For he is cast into <sup>h</sup>a net by his own feet, and he walketh upon a snare.

9 The gin shall take *him* by the heel, and the <sup>i</sup>robber shall prevail against him.

10 The snare *is* laid for him in the ground, and <sup>k</sup>a trap for him in the way.

11 <sup>l</sup>Terrors shall make him afraid on every side, and shall drive him to his feet.

12 His strength shall be hunger-bitten, and destruction *shall be* <sup>m</sup>ready at his side.

13 It shall devour the strength of his skin: *even* the first-born of death shall devour his strength.

14 <sup>n</sup>His confidence shall be rooted out of his tabernacle; and it shall bring him to the <sup>o</sup>king of terrors.

15 It shall <sup>p</sup>dwell in his tabernacle, because *it is* none of his: brimstone shall be scattered upon his habitation.

16 His <sup>q</sup>roots shall be dried up beneath, and above shall his branch be cut off.

17 <sup>r</sup>His remembrance shall perish from the earth, and he shall have no name in the street.

18 He shall be <sup>s</sup>driven from light into darkness, and chased out of the world.

19 He shall neither have <sup>t</sup>son nor nephew among his people, nor any remaining in his dwellings.

20 They that come after *him* shall be astonished <sup>u</sup>at his day, as they that went before were affrighted.

21 Surely such *are* the dwellings of the wicked, and this *is* the place *of him that* <sup>x</sup>knoweth not God.
