## CHAPTER XIV.

Job entreateth God for favour, by the shortness of life.

MAN *that is* born of a woman *is* of <sup>a</sup>few days, and full of trouble.

2 He cometh forth like a flower, <sup>b</sup>and is cut down: he fleeth also as a shadow, and continueth not.

3 And dost thou <sup>c</sup>open thine eyes upon such a one, and bringest me into judgment with thee?

4 Who can bring a clean *thing* out of <sup>d</sup>an unclean? not one.

5 Seeing his days *are* determined, the number of his months *are* with thee; thou hast appointed his <sup>e</sup>bounds that he cannot pass:

6 Turn from him that he <sup>f</sup>may rest, till he shall accomplish, as a hireling, his day.

7 For there is <sup>g</sup>hope of a tree, if it be cut down, that it will sprout again, and that the tender branch thereof will not cease.

8 Though the root thereof wax old in the earth, and the stock thereof <sup>h</sup>die in the ground;

9 *Yet* through the scent of water it will bud, and bring forth boughs like a plant.

10 But man dieth, and wasteth away; yea, man giveth up the ghost, and <sup>i</sup>where *is* he?

11 *As* the waters fail from the sea, and the flood decayeth and drieth up;

12 So man <sup>k</sup>lieth down, and riseth not: till the heavens *be* <sup>l</sup>no more, they shall not awake, nor be raised out of their sleep.

13 Oh that thou wouldest hide me in the grave, that thou wouldest keep me <sup>m</sup>secret until thy wrath be past, that thou wouldest appoint me a set time, and remember me!

14 If a man die, shall he <sup>n</sup>live *again?* All the days of my appointed time, will I wait, till my change come.

15 Thou shalt <sup>o</sup>call; and I will answer thee: thou wilt have a desire to the work of thy hands.

16 For now thou <sup>p</sup>numberest my steps; dost thou not watch over my sin?

17 My transgression *is* <sup>q</sup>sealed up in a bag, and thou sewest up mine iniquity.

18 And surely the mountain falling <sup>r</sup>cometh to nought, and the rock is removed out of his place.

19 The waters wear the stones: thou washest away the things which grow *out* of the dust of the earth; and thou destroyest the <sup>s</sup>hope of man.

20 Thou <sup>t</sup>prevailest for ever against him; and he passeth: thou changest his countenance, and sendest him away.

21 His sons come to honour, and he knoweth *it* not; and they are brought low, but he <sup>u</sup>perceiveth *it* not of them.

22 But his flesh upon him shall have pain, and his soul within him shall <sup>x</sup>mourn.

## CHAPTER XV.

Eliphaz reproveth Job's impiety in justifying himself.

THEN answered Eliphaz the Temanite, and said,

3 Should he reason with <sup>b</sup>unprofitable talk? or with speeches wherewith he can do no good?

4 Yea, thou castest off fear, and <sup>c</sup>restrainest prayer before God.

5 For thy <sup>d</sup>mouth uttereth thine iniquity, and thou choosest the tongue of the crafty.

6 Thine own mouth <sup>e</sup>condemneth thee, and not I, yea, thine own lips testify against thee.

7 *Art* thou the <sup>f</sup>first man *that* was born? or wast thou made before the hills?

8 Hast thou heard the <sup>g</sup>secret of God? and dost thou <sup>h</sup>restrain wisdom to thyself?

9 What knowest thou, that <sup>i</sup>we know not? *what* understandest thou, which *is* not in us?

10 <sup>k</sup>With us *are* both the gray-headed and very aged men, much elder than thy father.

11 *Are* the <sup>l</sup>consolations of God small with thee? is there any secret thing <sup>m</sup>with thee?

12 Why doth thy heart carry thee away? and what do thine eyes wink at,

13 That thou turnest thy spirit against God, and lettest <sup>n</sup>*such* words go out of thy mouth?

14 What *is* man, that he should be <sup>o</sup>clean? and *he which is* born of a woman, that he should be righteous?

15 Behold, he putteth no trust in his saints; yea, <sup>p</sup>the heavens are not clean in his sight:

16 How much more abominable and filthy *is* man, which <sup>q</sup>drinketh iniquity like water?

17 I will shew thee, hear me; and that *which* I have <sup>r</sup>seen I will declare;

18 Which wise men have told <sup>s</sup>from their fathers, and have not hid *it:*

19 Unto whom alone the earth was given, and <sup>t</sup>no stranger passed among them.

20 The wicked man <sup>u</sup>travaileth with pain all *his* days, and the number of years is hidden to the oppressor.

21 <sup>x</sup>A dreadful sound *is* in his ears: in prosperity the destroyer shall come upon him.

22 He <sup>y</sup>believeth not that he shall return out of darkness, and he is waited for of the sword.

23 He <sup>z</sup>wandereth abroad for bread, *saying,* Where *is it?* he knoweth that the day of darkness is ready at his hand.

24 Trouble and anguish shall make him afraid; they shall <sup>a</sup>prevail against him, as a king ready to the battle.

25 For he <sup>b</sup>stretcheth out his hand against God, and strengtheneth himself against the Almighty.

26 He <sup>c</sup>runneth upon him, *even* on *his* neck, upon the thick bosses of his bucklers;

27 Because he covereth his face with his <sup>d</sup>fatness, and maketh collops of fat on *his* flanks.

28 And he dwelleth <sup>e</sup>in desolate cities, *and* in houses which no man inhabiteth, which are ready to become heaps.

29 He shall not be <sup>f</sup>rich, neither shall his substance continue, neither shall he prolong the perfection thereof upon the earth.

30 He shall not depart out of darkness: the flame shall dry up his branches, and by the <sup>g</sup>breath of his mouth shall he go away.

31 Let not him that is deceived <sup>h</sup>trust in vanity; for vanity shall be his recompense.

32 It shall be <sup>i</sup>accomplished before his time, and his branch shall not be green.

33 He shall shake off his unripe grape as the vine, and shall cast <sup>k</sup>off his flower as the olive.

34 For the congregation of hypocrites *shall be* desolate, and fire shall consume the <sup>l</sup>tabernacles of bribery.

35 They <sup>m</sup>conceive mischief, and bring forth vanity, and their belly prepareth deceit.
