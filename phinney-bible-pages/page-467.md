**Header:** *A lamentation for Judah.* | CHAPTER V. | *Judgments on the Jews.*

18 Thy way and thy doings have <sup>m</sup>procured these *things* unto thee: this *is* thy wickedness, because it is <sup>n</sup>bitter, because it reacheth unto thy heart.

19 <sup>o</sup>My bowels, my bowels! I am pained at my very heart; my heart maketh a noise in me: I cannot hold my peace, because thou hast heard, O my soul, the sound of the trumpet, the alarm of war.

20 Destruction <sup>p</sup>upon destruction is cried: for the whole land is spoiled: suddenly are my tents spoiled, *and* my curtains in a moment.

21 How long shall I see the standard, *and* hear the sound of the trumpet?

22 For my people *is* <sup>q</sup>foolish, they have not known me; they *are* sottish children, and they have none understanding: they *are* <sup>r</sup>wise to do evil, but to do good they have <sup>s</sup>no knowledge.

23 I beheld the earth, and, lo, *it was* <sup>t</sup>without form and void; and the heavens, and they *had* <sup>u</sup>no light.

24 I beheld the mountains, and, lo, they <sup>x</sup>trembled, and all the hills moved lightly.

25 I beheld, and, lo, *there was* no man, and all the <sup>y</sup>birds of the heavens were fled.

26 I beheld, and, lo, the fruitful place *was* a wilderness, and all the cities thereof were broken down at the <sup>z</sup>presence of the LORD, *and* by his fierce anger.

27 For thus hath the LORD said, The whole land shall be desolate; yet will I not make <sup>a</sup>a full end.

28 For this shall the earth <sup>b</sup>mourn, and the heavens above be black: because I have spoken *it,* I have purposed *it,* and will <sup>c</sup>not repent, neither will I turn back from it.

29 The whole city shall flee for the noise of the horsemen and bowmen; they shall go into <sup>d</sup>thickets, and climb up upon the rocks: every city *shall be* forsaken, and not a man dwell therein.

30 And *when* thou *art* spoiled, what wilt thou do? Though thou clothest thyself with crimson, though thou <sup>e</sup>deckest thee with ornaments of gold, though thou rendest thy face with painting, in vain shalt thou make thyself fair; *thy* <sup>f</sup>lovers will despise thee, they will seek thy life.

31 For I have heard a voice as <sup>g</sup>of a woman in travail, *and* the anguish as of her that bringeth forth her first child; the voice of the daughter of Zion, *that* bewaileth herself, *that* <sup>h</sup>spreadeth her hands, *saying,* Wo *is* me now! for my soul is wearied because of <sup>i</sup>murderers.

## CHAPTER V.

The judgments of God upon the Jews for their perverseness.

RUN ye to and fro through the streets of Jerusalem, and see now, and know, and seek in the broad places thereof, if ye can <sup>a</sup>find a man, if there be *any* that executeth judgment, that seeketh the truth; and I will <sup>b</sup>pardon it.

2 And though they <sup>c</sup>say, The LORD liveth, surely they swear <sup>d</sup>falsely.

3 O LORD, *are* not <sup>e</sup>thine eyes upon the truth? thou hast <sup>f</sup>stricken them, but they have not grieved; thou hast consumed them, *but* they have <sup>g</sup>refused to receive correction: they have made their faces harder than a <sup>h</sup>rock; they have refused to return.

4 Therefore I said, Surely these *are* poor; they are foolish: for they know not the <sup>i</sup>way of the LORD, *nor* the judgment of their God.

5 I will get me unto the <sup>k</sup>great men, and will speak unto them: for they have known the way of the LORD, *and* the judgment of their God: but <sup>l</sup>these have altogether broken the yoke, *and* burst the bonds.

6 Wherefore a lion out of the forest shall slay them, *and* a wolf of <sup>m</sup>the evenings shall spoil them, a <sup>n</sup>leopard shall watch over their cities: every one that goeth out thence shall be torn in pieces; because their transgressions are many, *and* their backslidings are increased.

7 How shall I pardon thee for this? thy children have forsaken me, and sworn by *them that are* <sup>o</sup>no gods: when I had <sup>p</sup>fed them to the full, they then committed <sup>q</sup>adultery, and assembled themselves by troops in the harlots' houses.

8 They were *as* <sup>r</sup>fed horses in the morning: every one neighed after his <sup>s</sup>neighbour's wife.

9 Shall I not visit for these *things?* saith the LORD: and shall not my soul be <sup>t</sup>avenged on such a nation as this?

10 Go ye up upon her <sup>u</sup>walls, and destroy; but make not a full end: take away her battlements; for they *are* <sup>x</sup>not the LORD'S.

11 For the house of Israel and the house of Judah have <sup>y</sup>dealt very treacherously against me, saith the LORD.

12 They have <sup>z</sup>belied the LORD, and said, <sup>a</sup>*It is* not he, neither shall evil come upon us, neither shall we see sword nor famine;

13 And the prophets shall become <sup>b</sup>wind, and the word *is* <sup>c</sup>not in them: thus shall it be done unto them.

14 Wherefore thus saith the Lord GOD of hosts, Because ye speak this word, behold, I will make my words in thy mouth <sup>d</sup>fire, and this people wood, and it shall devour them.

15 Lo, I will bring a nation upon you <sup>e</sup>from far, O house of Israel, saith the LORD: it *is* a mighty nation, it *is* an <sup>f</sup>ancient nation, a nation whose language thou knowest not, neither understandest what they say.

16 Their quiver *is* as an open sepulchre, they *are* all mighty men.

17 And they shall <sup>g</sup>eat up thy harvest, and thy bread, *which* thy sons and thy daughters should eat: they shall eat up thy flocks and thy herds; they shall eat up thy vines and thy fig-trees: they shall impoverish thy fenced cities, wherein thou trustedst, with the sword.

18 Nevertheless, in those days, saith the LORD, I will not make a full end with you.

19 And it shall come to pass, when ye shall say, <sup>h</sup>Wherefore doeth the LORD our God all these *things* unto us? then shalt thou answer them, Like as ye have forsaken me, and served strange gods in your land; so shall ye serve <sup>i</sup>strangers in a land *that is* not yours.

20 Declare this in the house of Jacob, and publish it in Judah, saying,

21 Hear now this, O <sup>k</sup>foolish people, and without <sup>†</sup>understanding; which have <sup>l</sup>eyes, and see not; which have ears and hear not:

22 <sup>m</sup>Fear ye not me? saith the LORD: will ye not tremble at my presence, which have placed the sand *for* the <sup>n</sup>bound of the sea, by a perpetual decree, that it cannot pass it; and though the waves thereof toss themselves, yet can they not prevail; though they roar, yet can they not pass over it?

23 But this people hath a revolting and a rebellious heart; they are revolted and gone.

24 Neither say they in their heart, Let us now fear the LORD our God that giveth <sup>o</sup>rain, both the <sup>p</sup>former and the latter, in his <sup>q</sup>season: he reserveth unto us the <sup>r</sup>appointed weeks of the harvest.
