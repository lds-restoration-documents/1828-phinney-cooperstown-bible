## CHAPTER V.

God being praised, giveth a visible sign of his favour.

THUS all the work that Solomon made for the house of the LORD was finished: and Solomon brought in *all* the <sup>†</sup>things that David his father had dedicated; and the silver and the gold, and all the instruments, put he among the treasures of the house of God.

2 Then Solomon <sup>a</sup>assembled the elders of Israel, and all the heads of the tribes, the chief of the fathers of the children of Israel, unto Jerusalem, to bring up the <sup>b</sup>ark of the covenant of the LORD out of the city of David, which *is* Zion.

3 Wherefore all the men of Israel assembled themselves unto the king, in the feast which *was* in the <sup>c</sup>seventh month.

4 And all the elders of Israel came; and the <sup>d</sup>Levites took up the ark.

5 And they brought up the ark, and the tabernacle of the congregation, and all the <sup>†</sup>holy vessels that *were* in the tabernacle, these did the <sup>e</sup>priests *and* the Levites bring up.

6 Also king Solomon, and all the congregation of Israel that were assembled unto him before the ark, sacrificed <sup>f</sup>sheep and oxen, which could not be told nor numbered for multitude.

7 And the priests brought in the ark of the covenant of the LORD unto his place, to the <sup>g</sup>oracle of the house, into the <sup>h</sup>most holy *place, even* under the wings of the cherubims:

8 For the cherubims spread forth *their* wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubims covered the ark and the <sup>i</sup>staves thereof above.

9 And they drew out the staves *of the ark,* that the <sup>k</sup>ends of the staves were seen from the ark before the oracle; but they were not seen without. And there it is unto this day.

10 *There was* <sup>l</sup>nothing in the ark save the two tables which Moses put *therein* at <sup>m</sup>Horeb, when the LORD made *a covenant* with the children of Israel, when they came out of Egypt.

11 And it came to pass, when the priests were come out of the <sup>n</sup>holy *place;* (for all the priests *that were* present were <sup>o</sup>sanctified, *and* did not *then* <sup>p</sup>wait by course;

12 Also the Levites, *which were* the <sup>q</sup>singers, all of them of Asaph, of Heman, of Jeduthun, with their sons and their brethren, *being* <sup>r</sup>arrayed in white linen, having cymbals and psalteries and harps, stood at the east end of the altar, and with them a hundred and twenty priests sounding with trumpets;)

13 It came even to pass, as the trumpeters and singers *were* as one, to make one sound to be heard in praising and thanking the LORD, and when they lifted up *their* voice with the trumpets and cymbals and instruments of music, and praised the LORD, *saying,* For *he is* <sup>s</sup>good; for his mercy *endureth* for ever: that *then* the house was filled with <sup>t</sup>a cloud, *even* the house of the LORD;

14 So that the priests could not stand to minister by reason of the cloud; for <sup>u</sup>the glory of the LORD had filled the house of God.
