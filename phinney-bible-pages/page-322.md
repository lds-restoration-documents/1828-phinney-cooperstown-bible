# ¶ EZRA.

## CHAPTER I.

The proclamation of Cyrus for the building of the temple.

NOW in the first year of <sup>a</sup>Cyrus king of Persia, (that the word <sup>b</sup>of the LORD by the mouth of Jeremiah might be <sup>c</sup>fulfilled,) the LORD <sup>d</sup>stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, that he made <sup>e</sup>a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and *put it* also in writing, saying,

2 Thus saith Cyrus king of Persia, The LORD God of heaven hath given me all the kingdoms of the earth; and he hath <sup>f</sup>charged me to build him a house at Jerusalem, which *is* in Judah:

3 <sup>g</sup>Who *is there* among you of all his people? his God be <sup>h</sup>with him, and let him go up to Jerusalem, which *is* in Judah, and build the house of the LORD God of Israel (<sup>i</sup>he *is* the God) which *is* <sup>k</sup>in Jerusalem.

4 And whosoever remaineth in any place where he sojourneth, let the men of his place <sup>†</sup>help him with silver, and with gold, and with goods, and with beasts, besides the <sup>l</sup>free-will-offering for the house of God that *is* in Jerusalem.

5 Then rose up the <sup>m</sup>chief of the fathers of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests, and the Levites, with all *them* whose <sup>n</sup>spirit God had raised to go up to build the house of the LORD which *is* in Jerusalem.

6 And all <sup>o</sup>they that *were* about them strengthened their hands with vessels of silver, with gold, with goods, and with beasts, and with precious things, besides all *that* was <sup>p</sup>willingly offered.

7 Also Cyrus the king brought forth the vessels of the house of the LORD, which Nebuchadnezzar had brought <sup>q</sup>forth out of Jerusalem, and had put them in the house of his <sup>r</sup>gods;

8 Even those did Cyrus king of Persia bring forth by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, and numbered them unto <sup>s</sup>Sheshbazzar the prince of Judah.

9 And this *is* the <sup>t</sup>number of them: thirty chargers of gold, a thousand chargers of silver, nine and twenty knives,

10 Thirty basons of gold, silver basons of a second *sort* four hundred and ten, *and* <sup>u</sup>other vessels a thousand.

11 All the vessels of gold and of silver *were* five thousand and four hundred. All *these* did Sheshbazzar bring up with *them of* the captivity, that were brought up from Babylon unto Jerusalem.

## CHAPTER II.

The number that return of the people and priests.

NOW these *are* the <sup>a</sup>children of the <sup>b</sup>province that went up out of the captivity, of those which had been <sup>c</sup>carried away, whom Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon had carried away unto Babylon, and came again unto Jerusalem and Judah, every one unto his city;

2 Which came with Zerubbabel: <sup>d</sup>Jeshua, Nehemiah, Seraiah, Reelaiah, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mizpar, Bigvai, Rehum, Baanah. The number of the men of the people of Israel.
