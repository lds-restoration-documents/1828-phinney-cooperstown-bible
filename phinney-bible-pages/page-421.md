## CHAPTER XXVIII.

General observations of impiety, and religious integrity.

THE <sup>a</sup>wicked flee when no man pursueth: but the righteous are <sup>b</sup>bold as a lion.

2 For the transgression of a land many *are* the princes thereof: but by a man of understanding *and* knowledge the state *thereof* shall be <sup>c</sup>prolonged.

3 A poor man that <sup>d</sup>oppresseth the poor *is like* a sweeping rain, which leaveth no food.

4 They that forsake the law <sup>e</sup>praise the wicked; but such as keep the law <sup>f</sup>contend with them.

5 Evil men <sup>g</sup>understand not judgment: but they that seek the LORD <sup>h</sup>understand all *things.*

6 Better *is* the poor that walketh in his uprightness, than *he that is* perverse <sup>†</sup>*in his ways,* though he *be* rich.

7 Whoso keepeth the law *is* a wise son: but he that is a companion of riotous *men,* shameth his father.

8 He that by usury and unjust gain increaseth his substance, he shall gather it for him that will pity the poor.

9 He that <sup>i</sup>turneth away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer *shall be* abomination.

10 Whoso causeth the righteous to go astray in an evil way, he shall fall himself into his own pit: but the upright shall have <sup>k</sup>good *things* in possession.

11 The rich man *is* wise <sup>l</sup>in his own conceit; but the poor that hath understanding searcheth him out.

12 When righteous *men* do <sup>m</sup>rejoice, *there is* great glory: but when the wicked rise, a man is <sup>n</sup>hidden.

13 He that <sup>o</sup>covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso <sup>p</sup>confesseth and forsaketh *them* shall have mercy.

14 Happy *is* the man that <sup>q</sup>feareth always: but he that <sup>r</sup>hardeneth his heart shall fall into mischief.

15 *As* a <sup>s</sup>roaring lion, and a ranging bear; *so is* <sup>t</sup>a wicked ruler over the poor people.

16 The prince that wanteth understanding *is* also a great oppressor: *but* he that hateth covetousness shall prolong *his* days.

17 A man that doth violence to the blood of *any* person shall flee to the pit; let no man stay him.

18 Whoso walketh uprightly shall be saved: but *he that is* <sup>u</sup>perverse *in his* ways shall fall at once.

19 He that tilleth his land shall have <sup>x</sup>plenty of bread: but he that followeth after vain *persons* shall have <sup>y</sup>poverty enough.

20 A faithful man shall abound with blessings: but he that maketh haste to be rich shall not be innocent.

21 To have respect of persons *is* not good; for, for a piece of bread *that* man will transgress.

22 He that hasteth to be rich *hath* an evil eye, and <sup>z</sup>considereth not that poverty shall come upon him.

23 He that <sup>a</sup>rebuketh a man, afterwards shall find more favour than he that flattereth with the tongue.

24 Whoso robbeth his father or his mother, and saith, *It is* no transgression; the same *is* the companion of a destroyer.

25 He that is of a proud heart stirreth up strife: but he that <sup>b</sup>putteth his trust in the LORD shall be made fat.

26 He that <sup>c</sup>trusteth in his own heart is a fool: but whoso walketh wisely, he shall be delivered.

27 He that giveth unto the poor shall <sup>d</sup>not lack: but he that hideth his eyes shall have many a curse.

28 When the wicked rise, men hide themselves: but when they perish, the righteous <sup>e</sup>increase.

## CHAPTER XXIX.

Observations of public and private government.

HE that, being often reproved, hardeneth *his* neck, shall suddenly be <sup>a</sup>destroyed, and that without remedy.

2 When the righteous are <sup>b</sup>in authority, the people rejoice: but when the wicked <sup>c</sup>beareth rule, the people mourn.

3 Whoso loveth wisdom rejoiceth his father: but he that keepeth company with harlots <sup>d</sup>spendeth *his* substance.

4 The king <sup>e</sup>by judgment establisheth the land: but he that receiveth <sup>f</sup>gifts overthroweth it.

5 A man that <sup>g</sup>flattereth his neighbour <sup>h</sup>spreadeth a net for his feet.

6 In the transgression of an evil man *there is* a snare: but the righteous doth <sup>i</sup>sing and rejoice.

7 The righteous <sup>k</sup>considereth the cause of the poor: *but* the wicked regardeth not to know *it.*

8 Scornful men bring a city into a snare: but wise <sup>l</sup>*men* turn away wrath.

9 *If* a wise man contendeth with a foolish man, <sup>m</sup>whether he rage or laugh, *there is* no rest.

10 The blood-thirsty <sup>n</sup>hate the upright: but the just seek his soul.

11 A fool uttereth <sup>o</sup>all his mind: but a wise *man* keepeth it in till afterwards.

12 If a ruler hearken <sup>p</sup>to lies, all his servants *are* wicked.

13 The poor and the deceitful man meet together: the LORD <sup>q</sup>lighteneth both their eyes.

14 The king that <sup>r</sup>faithfully judgeth the poor, his throne shall be established for ever.

15 The rod and reproof give wisdom: but a child <sup>s</sup>left *to himself* bringeth his mother to shame.

16 When the wicked are multiplied, transgression increaseth: but the righteous shall see their <sup>t</sup>fall.

17 Correct thy son, and he shall give thee rest: yea, he shall give delight unto thy soul.

18 Where *there is* no vision, the people <sup>u</sup>perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy *is* he.

19 A servant will <sup>x</sup>not be corrected by words: for though he <sup>y</sup>understand, he will not answer.

20 Seest thou a man *that is* hasty in his words? *there is* more hope of a fool than of him.

21 He that <sup>z</sup>delicately bringeth up his servant from a child, shall have him become *his* son at the length.

22 An angry man stirreth up strife, and a furious man aboundeth in transgression.

23 A man's pride shall bring him <sup>a</sup>low: but honour shall uphold the humble in spirit.

24 Whoso is <sup>b</sup>partner with a thief hateth his own soul: he heareth cursing, and bewrayeth *it* not.

25 The <sup>c</sup>fear of man bringeth a snare: but whoso putteth his trust in the LORD shall be safe.

26 Many seek the ruler's favour; but <sup>d</sup>*every* man's judgment *cometh* from the LORD.

27 An unjust man *is* an abomination to the <sup>e</sup>just; and *he that is* upright in the way *is* abomination to the wicked.
