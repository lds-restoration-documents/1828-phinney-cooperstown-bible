14 The heart of him that hath understanding <sup>y</sup>seeketh knowledge: but the mouth of fools <sup>z</sup>feedeth on foolishness.

15 All the days of the afflicted *are* <sup>a</sup>evil: but he that is of a merry heart *hath* a continual feast.

16 <sup>b</sup>Better *is* little with the fear of the LORD, than great treasure and trouble therewith.

17 Better *is* a dinner of herbs where love is, than <sup>c</sup>a stalled ox and hatred therewith.

18 <sup>d</sup>A wrathful man stirreth up strife: but *he that is* <sup>e</sup>slow to anger appeaseth strife.

19 The way of the slothful *man is* <sup>f</sup>as a hedge of thorns: but the way of the righteous *is* made <sup>g</sup>plain.

20 A wise son maketh a glad father: but a foolish man despiseth his mother.

21 <sup>h</sup>Folly *is* joy to *him that is* destitute of wisdom: but a man of understanding <sup>i</sup>walketh uprightly.

22 Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the <sup>k</sup>multitude of counsellors they are established.

23 A man hath joy by the answer of his mouth; and a word *spoken* <sup>l</sup>in due season, how good *is it!*

24 The way of life *is* <sup>m</sup>above to the wise, that he may depart from hell beneath.

25 The LORD will <sup>n</sup>destroy the house of the proud: but he will establish the border of the widow.

26 <sup>o</sup>The thoughts of the wicked *are* an abomination to the LORD: but *the words* of the pure *are* <sup>p</sup>pleasant words.

27 He that is greedy of gain <sup>q</sup>troubleth his own house: but he that hateth gifts shall live.

28 The heart of the righteous <sup>r</sup>studieth to answer: but the mouth of the wicked poureth out evil things.

29 The LORD *is* far <sup>s</sup>from the wicked: but he <sup>t</sup>heareth the prayer of the righteous.

30 The light of the eyes rejoiceth the heart; *and* <sup>u</sup>a good report maketh the bones fat.

31 The ear that heareth the <sup>x</sup>reproof of life abideth among the wise.

32 He that refuseth instruction despiseth his own soul: but he that heareth reproof getteth understanding.

33 The fear of the LORD *is* the instruction of wisdom; and <sup>y</sup>before honour *is* humility.

## CHAPTER XVI.

THE <sup>a</sup>preparations of the heart in man, and the <sup>b</sup>answer of the tongue, *is* from the LORD.

2 All the ways of a man *are* <sup>c</sup>clean in his own eyes: but the LORD <sup>d</sup>weigheth the spirits.

3 <sup>e</sup>Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy <sup>f</sup>thoughts shall be established.

4 The LORD hath made all *things* <sup>g</sup>for himself; yea, even the wicked <sup>h</sup>for the day of evil.

5 Every one *that is* <sup>i</sup>proud in heart *is* an abomination to the LORD: *though* <sup>k</sup>hand *join* in hand, he shall not be unpunished.

6 By mercy and truth iniquity is <sup>l</sup>purged; and by the <sup>m</sup>fear of the LORD *men* depart from evil.

7 When a man's ways <sup>n</sup>please the LORD, he maketh even his <sup>o</sup>enemies to be at peace with him.

8 <sup>p</sup>Better *is* a little with righteousness, than great revenues <sup>q</sup>without right.

9 A man's heart <sup>r</sup>deviseth his way: but the LORD <sup>s</sup>directeth his steps.

10 <sup>t</sup>A divine sentence *is* in the lips of the king: his mouth <sup>u</sup>transgresseth not in judgment.

11 <sup>x</sup>A just weight and balance *are* the LORD's; all the weights of the bag *are* his work.

12 *It is* an abomination to kings to commit wickedness; for the <sup>y</sup>throne is established by righteousness.

13 Righteous lips *are* the <sup>z</sup>delight of kings; and they love him that speaketh right.

14 The <sup>a</sup>wrath of a king *is as* messengers of death; but a wise man will <sup>b</sup>pacify it.

15 In the <sup>c</sup>light of the king's countenance *is* life; and his favour *is* as a cloud of the <sup>d</sup>latter rain.

16 How much better *is it* to get wisdom <sup>e</sup>than gold? and to get understanding rather to be chosen than silver?

17 <sup>f</sup>The highway of the upright *is* to depart from evil: he that keepeth his way preserveth his soul.

18 Pride *goeth* before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall.

19 Better *it is to* be of a humble spirit with the lowly, than to divide the spoil with the proud.

20 He that handleth a matter wisely shall find <sup>g</sup>good; and whoso <sup>h</sup>trusteth in the LORD, happy *is* he.

21 The <sup>i</sup>wise in heart shall be called prudent: and the sweetness of the lips increaseth learning.

22 Understanding *is* <sup>k</sup>a well-spring of life unto him that hath it: but the <sup>l</sup>instruction of fools *is* folly.

23 <sup>m</sup>The heart of the wise teacheth his mouth, and addeth learning to his lips.

24 <sup>n</sup>Pleasant words *are as* a honey-comb, <sup>o</sup>sweet to the soul, and <sup>p</sup>health to the bones.

25 There is a way that seemeth right unto a man; but the end thereof *are* the ways of death.

26 He that <sup>q</sup>laboureth, laboureth for himself; for his mouth craveth it of him.

27 An ungodly man <sup>r</sup>diggeth up evil; and in his <sup>s</sup>lips *there is* as a burning fire.

28 A froward man soweth strife; and <sup>t</sup>a whisperer separateth chief friends.

29 <sup>u</sup>A violent man <sup>x</sup>enticeth his neighbour, and <sup>y</sup>leadeth him into the way *that is* not good.

30 He shutteth his eyes to devise froward things; moving his lips, he bringeth evil to pass.

31 The hoary head *is* a crown of glory, *if* it be found in the way of righteousness.

32 *He that is* <sup>z</sup>slow to anger *is* better than the mighty; and he that <sup>a</sup>ruleth his spirit than he that taketh a city.

33 The <sup>b</sup>lot is cast into the lap; but the whole <sup>c</sup>disposing thereof *is* of the LORD.

## CHAPTER XVII.

BETTER *is* <sup>a</sup>a dry morsel, and quietness therewith, than a house full of <sup>b</sup>sacrifices *with* strife.

2 <sup>c</sup>A wise servant shall have rule over a son that causeth shame, and shall have part of the inheritance among the brethren.

3 The fining-pot *is* for silver, and the <sup>d</sup>furnace for gold: but the LORD <sup>e</sup>trieth the hearts.

4 A wicked doer giveth heed to false lips; *and* a liar giveth ear <sup>f</sup>to a naughty tongue.

5 Whoso <sup>g</sup>mocketh the poor reproacheth his Maker; *and* he that <sup>h</sup>is glad at calamities shall not be unpunished.

6 <sup>i</sup>Children's children *are* the crown of old men; and the glory of children *are* <sup>k</sup>their fathers.

7 Excellent speech becometh <sup>l</sup>not a fool: much less do lying lips a prince.

8 A gift *is as* a precious stone in the eyes of him that hath it; whithersoever it turneth, <sup>m</sup>it prospereth.

9 He that <sup>n</sup>covereth a transgression seeketh love: but he that <sup>o</sup>repeateth a matter separateth *very* friends.

10 A reproof entereth more into a wise man than <sup>p</sup>a hundred stripes into a fool. <!-- uncertain: letter looks like r in crop, but margin sequence gives p -->
