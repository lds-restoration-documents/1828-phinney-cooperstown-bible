14 And Jacob set up <sup>a</sup>a pillar in the place where he talked with him, *even* a pillar of stone; and he poured a drink-offering thereon, and he poured oil thereon.

15 And Jacob called the name of the place, where God spake with him, Beth-el.

16 ¶ And they journeyed from Beth-el; and there was but a little way to come to <sup>b</sup>Ephrath: and Rachel travailed, and she had <sup>c</sup>hard labour.

17 And it came to pass, when she was in hard labour, that the midwife said unto her, Fear not; thou shalt have this son also.

18 And it came to pass, as her soul was in departing, (for she <sup>d</sup>died,) that she called his name <sup>||</sup>Ben-oni: but his father called him <sup>||</sup>Benjamin.

19 And Rachel died, and was buried in the way to Ephrath, <sup>e</sup>which *is* Beth-lehem.

20 And Jacob set a pillar upon her grave: that *is* the pillar of Rachel's grave unto this day.

21 ¶ And Israel journeyed, and spread his tent beyond the tower of Edar.

22 And it came to pass, when Israel dwelt in that land, that Reuben went and <sup>f</sup>lay with Bilhah his father's concubine: and Israel heard *it.* Now the sons of Jacob were <sup>g</sup>twelve:

23 The sons of Leah; Reuben, Jacob's first-born, and Simeon, and Levi, and Judah, and Issachar, and Zebulun:

24 The sons of Rachel; Joseph, and Benjamin:

25 And the sons of Bilhah, Rachel's handmaid; Dan, and Naphtali:

26 And the sons of Zilpah, Leah's handmaid; Gad, and Asher. These *are* the sons of Jacob, which were born to him in <sup>h</sup>Padan-aram.

27 ¶ And Jacob came unto Isaac his father unto <sup>i</sup>Mamre, unto the city of <sup>k</sup>Arbah, which *is* Hebron, where Abraham and Isaac sojourned.

28 And the days of Isaac were <sup>l</sup>a hundred and fourscore years.

29 And Isaac gave up the ghost, and died, and was gathered unto <sup>m</sup>his people, *being* old and full of days: and his sons Esau <sup>n</sup>and Jacob buried him.

NOW these *are* the <sup>a</sup>generations of Esau, who *is* <sup>b</sup>Edom.

2 Esau <sup>c</sup>took his wives of the daughters of <sup>d</sup>Canaan; <sup>e</sup>Adah the daughter of Elon the Hittite, and Aholibamah the daughter of Anah, the daughter of Zibeon the Hivite;

3 And <sup>f</sup>Bashemath, Ishmael's daughter, sister of <sup>g</sup>Nebajoth.

4 And Adah bare to Esau, <sup>h</sup>Eliphaz; and Bashemath bare Reuel.

5 And Aholibamah bare Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah. These *are* the sons of Esau, which were born unto him in the <sup>i</sup>land of Canaan.

6 And Esau <sup>k</sup>took his wives, and his sons, and his daughters, and all the persons of his house, and his cattle, and all his beasts, and all his substance, which he had got in the land of Canaan, and went into the country from the face of his brother Jacob:

7 For their riches were more than that they might <sup>l</sup>dwell together; and the land wherein they were <sup>m</sup>strangers could not bear them, because of their cattle.

8 Thus dwelt Esau in mount <sup>n</sup>Seir. Esau *is* Edom.

9 ¶ And these *are* the generations of Esau, the father of the <sup>†</sup>Edomites, in mount Seir.

10 These *are* the names of Esau's sons; Eliphaz the son of Adah the wife of Esau; Reuel the son of Bashemath the wife of Esau.

11 And the sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, <sup>o</sup>Zepho, and Gatam, and Kenaz.

12 And <sup>p</sup>Timna was concubine to Eliphaz, Esau's son; and she bare to Eliphaz, <sup>q</sup>Amalek: these *were* the sons of Adah, Esau's wife.

13 And these *are* the sons of <sup>r</sup>Reuel; Nahath, and Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah: these were the sons of Bashemath, Esau's wife.

14 ¶ And these were the sons of Aholibamah, the daughter of Anah, daughter of Zibeon, Esau's wife; and she bare to Esau, Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah.

15 ¶ These *were* <sup>s</sup>dukes of the sons of Esau: the sons of Eliphaz, the first-born *son* of Esau; duke Teman, duke Omar, duke Zepho, duke Kenaz,

16 Duke <sup>t</sup>Korah, duke Gatam, *and* duke Amalek. These *are* the dukes *that came* of Eliphaz in the land of Edom: these *were* the sons of Adah.

17 ¶ And these *are* the sons of Reuel, Esau's son; duke Nahath, duke Zerah, duke Shammah, duke Mizzah. These *are* the dukes *that came* of Reuel in the land of Edom: these *are* the sons of Bashemath, Esau's wife.

18 ¶ And these *are* the sons of Aholibamah, Esau's wife; duke Jeush, duke Jaalam, duke Korah: these *were* the dukes *that came* of Aholibamah, the daughter of Anah, Esau's wife.

19 These *are* the sons of Esau, who is Edom, and these *are* their dukes.

20 ¶ These *are* the sons of Seir <sup>u</sup>the Horite, <sup>x</sup>who inhabited the land; Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and Anah,

21 And Dishon, and Ezer, and Dishan. These *are* the dukes of the Horites, the children of Seir in the land of Edom.

22 And the children of Lotan were Hori and <sup>y</sup>Heman: and Lotan's sister *was* <sup>z</sup>Timna.

23 And the children of Shobal *were* these; <sup>a</sup>Alvan, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shepho, and Onam.

24 And these *are* the children of Zibeon; both Ajah and Anah: this *was that* Anah that found the <sup>†</sup>mules in the wilderness, as he fed the asses of Zibeon his father.

25 And the children of Anah *were* these; Dishon, and <sup>b</sup>Aholibamah the daughter of Anah.

26 And these *are* the children of <sup>c</sup>Dishon; Hemdan, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran.

27 The children of Ezer *are* these; Bilhan, and Zaavan, and Akan.

28 The children of Dishan *are* these; Uz, and Aran.

29 These *are* the dukes *that came* of the <sup>d</sup>Horites; duke Lotan, duke Shobal, duke Zibeon, duke Anah,

30 Duke Dishon, duke Ezer, duke Dishan. These *are* the dukes *that came* of Hori, among their <sup>||</sup>dukes in the land of Seir.

33 And Bela died; and Jobab, the son of Zerah of <sup>f</sup>Bozrah, reigned in his stead.
