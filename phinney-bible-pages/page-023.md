## CHAPTER XV.

1 God encourageth Abram, 4 promiseth him a son, and a multiplying of his seed....6 Abram is justified by faith....7 Canaan is promised, and confirmed by a vision.

AFTER these things the word of the LORD came unto Abram <sup>a</sup>in a vision, saying, <sup>b</sup>Fear not, Abram: I *am* thy <sup>c</sup>shield, *and* thy exceeding <sup>d</sup>great reward.

3 And Abram said, Behold, to me thou hast given no seed: and, lo, <sup>f</sup>one born in my house is mine heir.

4 And, behold, the word of the LORD *came* unto him, saying, This shall not be thine heir; but he that shall come forth out of thine own <sup>g</sup>bowels shall be thine heir.

5 And he brought him forth abroad, and said, Look now towards heaven, and tell the <sup>h</sup>stars, if thou be able to number them. And he said unto him, So shall thy seed be.

6 And he <sup>i</sup>believed <sup>k</sup>in the LORD; and <sup>l</sup>he counted it to him for righteousness.

7 And he said unto him, I *am* the LORD that brought thee out of <sup>m</sup>Ur of the Chaldees, to give thee this land to inherit it.

8 And he said, LORD God, <sup>n</sup>whereby shall I know that I shall inherit it?

9 And he said unto him, <sup>o</sup>Take me a heifer of three years old, and a she-goat of three years old, and a ram of three years old, and a turtle-dove, and a young pigeon.

10 And he took unto him all these, and <sup>p</sup>divided them in the midst, and laid each piece one against another; but the birds divided he not.

11 And when the <sup>q</sup>fowls came down upon the carcases, Abram drove them away.

12 And when the sun was going down, <sup>r</sup>a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and, lo, a horror of great <sup>s</sup>darkness fell upon him.

13 And he said unto Abram, Know of a surety, that thy seed shall be a stranger in a land *that is* <sup>t</sup>not theirs, and shall serve them; and they shall afflict them <sup>u</sup>four hundred years;

14 And also that nation, whom they shall serve, will <sup>x</sup>I judge; and afterwards shall they come out with <sup>y</sup>great substance.

15 And thou shalt go to <sup>z</sup>thy fathers in peace; thou shalt be buried in a good old age.

16 But in the fourth generation they shall come hither again; for the iniquity of the Amorites *is* not <sup>a</sup>yet full.

17 And it came to pass, that, when the sun went down, and it was dark, behold, a smoking furnace and a <sup>†</sup>burning lamp that passed between those pieces.

18 In that same day the LORD made a covenant with Abram, saying, <sup>b</sup>Unto thy seed have I given this land, from the river of Egypt unto the great river, the river Euphrates:

19 The Kenites, and the Kenizzites, and the Kadmonites,

20 And the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Rephaims,

21 And the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.

## CHAPTER XVI.

1 Sarai being barren giveth Hagar to Abram....15 Ishmael is born.

NOW Sarai, Abram's wife, <sup>a</sup>bare him no children: and she had a <sup>b</sup>handmaid, an Egyptian, whose name *was* Hagar.

2 And Sarai said unto Abram, Behold now, the LORD hath <sup>c</sup>restrained me from bearing: I pray thee, go in unto my maid; it may be that I may <sup>†</sup>obtain children by her. And Abram <sup>d</sup>hearkened to the voice of Sarai.

3 And Sarai, Abram's wife, took Hagar her maid, the Egyptian, after Abram had dwelt <sup>e</sup>ten years in the land of Canaan, and gave her to her husband Abram to be his <sup>f</sup>wife.

4 And he went in unto Hagar, and she conceived: and, when she saw that she had conceived, her mistress was <sup>g</sup>despised in her eyes.

5 And Sarai said unto Abram, <sup>h</sup>My wrong *be* upon thee: I have given my maid into thy bosom; and when she saw that she had conceived, I was despised in her eyes: the LORD <sup>i</sup>judge between me and thee.

6 But Abram said unto Sarai, Behold, thy maid *is* <sup>k</sup>in thy hand: do to her as it pleaseth thee. And when Sarai <sup>l</sup>dealt hardly with her, she <sup>m</sup>fled from her face.

7 And the angel of the LORD <sup>n</sup>found her by a fountain of water in the wilderness, by the fountain in the way to Shur.

8 And he said, Hagar, Sarai's maid, <sup>o</sup>whence camest thou? and whither wilt thou go? And she said, <sup>p</sup>I flee from the face of my mistress Sarai.

9 And the angel of the LORD said unto her, Return to thy mistress, and <sup>q</sup>submit thyself under her hands.

10 And the angel of the LORD said unto her, I will <sup>r</sup>multiply thy seed exceedingly, that it shall not be numbered for multitude.

11 And the angel of the LORD said unto her, Behold, thou *art* with child, and shalt bear a son, and shalt call his name Ishmael; because the LORD <sup>s</sup>hath heard thy affliction.

12 And he will be a wild man; his hand *will be* <sup>t</sup>against every man, and every man's hand against him: and he shall dwell in the presence of all his brethren.

13 And she called the name of the LORD that spake unto her, <sup>u</sup>Thou God seest me: for she said, Have I also here <sup>x</sup>looked after him that seeth me?

14 Wherefore the well was called <sup>||</sup>Beer-laha-roi: behold, *it is* between Kadesh and Bered.

15 And Hagar bare Abram a son; and Abram called his son's name, which Hagar bare, Ishmael.

16 And Abram *was* fourscore and six years old when Hagar bare Ishmael to Abram.
