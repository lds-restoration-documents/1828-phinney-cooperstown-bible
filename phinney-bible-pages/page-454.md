## CHAPTER XLVI.

Idols not comparable to God for power.

BEL boweth down, Nebo stoopeth; their idols *were* upon the <sup>a</sup>beasts, and upon the cattle: your carriages *were* heavy loaden: *they are* a burden to the weary *beast.*

2 They stoop; they bow down together; they could not <sup>b</sup>deliver the burden, but themselves are gone into captivity.

3 Hearken unto me, O house of Jacob, and all the remnant of the house of Israel, which are <sup>c</sup>borne *by me* from the belly, which are carried from the <sup>d</sup>womb:

4 And *even to your* <sup>e</sup>old age <sup>f</sup>I *am* he; and *even* to hoar hairs will I carry *you:* I have made, and I will bear; even I will carry, and will deliver *you.*

5 To whom will ye <sup>g</sup>liken me, and make *me* equal, and compare me, that we may be like?

6 They <sup>h</sup>lavish gold out of the bag, and weigh silver in the balance, *and* hire a goldsmith, and he maketh it a god: they fall down; yea, they worship.

7 They <sup>i</sup>bear him upon the shoulder, they carry him, and set him in his place, and he standeth; from his place shall he not remove: yea, *one* shall cry unto him, yet can he not answer, nor save him out of his trouble.

8 Remember this, and shew yourselves <sup>k</sup>men; bring *it* again to mind, O ye transgressors.

9 Remember the former things of old: for I *am* God, and *there is* <sup>l</sup>none else; *I am* God, and *there is* none like me;

10 Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times *the things* that are not *yet* done, saying, <sup>m</sup>My counsel shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure:

11 Calling a <sup>n</sup>ravenous bird from the east, the man that executeth <sup>o</sup>my counsel from a far country: yea, I have spoken *it,* I will also bring it to pass; I have purposed *it,* I will also do it.

12 Hearken unto me, ye <sup>p</sup>stout-hearted, that *are* far from righteousness:

13 I bring near <sup>q</sup>my righteousness; it shall not be far off, and my salvation shall not tarry: and I will place salvation in Zion for Israel <sup>r</sup>my glory.

## CHAPTER XLVII.

God's judgments upon Babylon and Chaldea.

COME down, and sit in the <sup>a</sup>dust, O virgin daughter of Babylon; sit on the ground: *there is* no throne, O daughter of the Chaldeans: for thou <sup>b</sup>shalt no more be called tender and delicate.

2 Take the millstones, and grind meal: uncover thy locks, <sup>c</sup>make bare the leg, uncover the thigh, pass over the rivers.

3 Thy nakedness shall be uncovered, yea, thy shame shall be seen: I will <sup>d</sup>take vengeance, and I will <sup>e</sup>not meet *thee as* a man.

4 *As for* <sup>f</sup>our Redeemer, the LORD of hosts *is* his name, the Holy One of Israel.

5 Sit thou silent, and get thee into <sup>g</sup>darkness, O daughter of the Chaldeans: for thou shalt no more be called, The lady of kingdoms.

6 I was <sup>h</sup>wroth with my people; I have polluted mine inheritance, and given them into thy hand: thou didst shew them <sup>i</sup>no mercy; upon the ancient hast thou very heavily laid thy yoke.

7 And thou saidst, I shall be a <sup>k</sup>lady for ever: *so* that thou didst not lay these *things* to thy heart, neither didst remember the latter end of it.

8 Therefore hear now this, thou *that art* given <sup>l</sup>to pleasures, that dwellest carelessly; that sayest in thy heart, <sup>m</sup>I *am,* and none else besides me; I shall not sit *as* a widow, neither shall I know the loss of children:

9 But <sup>n</sup>these two *things* shall come to thee in a moment in one day, the loss of children, and widowhood: they shall come upon thee in their perfection for the multitude of thy <sup>o</sup>sorceries, *and* for the great abundance of thine enchantments.

10 For thou hast <sup>p</sup>trusted in thy wickedness: thou hast said, None seeth me. Thy <sup>q</sup>wisdom and thy knowledge it hath perverted thee; and thou hast said in thy heart, I *am,* and none else besides me.

11 Therefore shall evil come upon thee; thou shalt not know <sup>r</sup>from whence it riseth: and mischief shall fall upon thee; thou shalt not be able <sup>s</sup>to put it off: and desolation shall come upon thee suddenly, *which* thou shalt not know.

12 Stand now with thy <sup>t</sup>enchantments, and with the multitude of thy sorceries, wherein thou hast laboured from thy youth; if so be thou shalt be able to profit, if so be thou mayest prevail.
