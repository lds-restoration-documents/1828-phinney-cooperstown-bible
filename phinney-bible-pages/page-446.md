## CHAPTER XXXII.

1 The blessings of Christ's kingdom....9 Desolation is foreshewn.

BEHOLD, a King <sup>a</sup>shall reign in righteousness, and <sup>b</sup>princes shall rule in judgment.

2 And <sup>||</sup>a man shall be as a hiding-place from the wind, and a covert from the tempest; as <sup>c</sup>rivers of water in a dry place; as the shadow of a great rock in a weary land.

3 And the eyes of them that see shall not be <sup>d</sup>dim; and the ears of them that hear shall hearken.

4 The heart also of the rash shall understand knowledge, and the tongue of the stammerers shall be ready to speak plainly.

5 The <sup>e</sup>vile person shall be no <sup>f</sup>more called liberal, nor the churl said *to be* bountiful.

6 For the vile person will speak villany, and <sup>g</sup>his heart will work iniquity, to practice hypocrisy, and to utter error against the LORD, to make empty the soul of the hungry; and he will cause the <sup>h</sup>drink of the thirsty to fail.

7 The instruments also of the churl *are* evil: he <sup>i</sup>deviseth wicked devices to destroy the poor with lying words, even when the needy speaketh right.

8 But the liberal <sup>k</sup>deviseth <sup>l</sup>liberal things; and by liberal things shall he stand.

9 Rise up, ye women that are at <sup>m</sup>ease; hear my voice, ye careless daughters; give ear unto my speech.

10 Many days and years shall ye be troubled, ye careless women: for the <sup>n</sup>vintage shall fail, the gathering shall not come.

11 Tremble, ye women that are at ease; be troubled, ye careless ones: <sup>o</sup>strip ye, and make ye bare, and gird *sackcloth* upon *your* loins.

12 They shall lament <sup>p</sup>for the teats, for the pleasant fields, for the fruitful vine.

13 Upon the land of my people shall come up <sup>q</sup>thorns *and* briers, yea, upon all the houses of joy in the joyous city:

14 Because the palaces <sup>r</sup>shall be forsaken; the multitude of the city shall be left; the forts and towers shall be for dens for ever, a joy of wild asses, a pasture of flocks;

15 Until the <sup>s</sup>Spirit be poured upon us <sup>t</sup>from on high, and the wilderness be a fruitful field, and the fruitful field be counted for a forest.

16 Then <sup>u</sup>judgment shall dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness remain in the fruitful field.

17 And the work of righteousness shall be <sup>x</sup>peace; and the <sup>y</sup>effect of righteousness, quietness and assurance for ever.

18 And my people shall dwell in a peaceable habitation, and in <sup>z</sup>sure dwellings, and in quiet resting-places,

19 When it shall <sup>a</sup>hail, coming down on the forest; and the city shall be low in a low place.

20 Blessed *are* ye that <sup>b</sup>sow beside all waters, that send forth *thither* the feet of the <sup>c</sup>ox and the ass.

## CHAPTER XXXIII.

God's judgments against the enemies of the church.

WO to thee that <sup>a</sup>spoilest, and thou *wast* <sup>b</sup>not spoiled; and dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee! when thou shalt <sup>c</sup>cease to spoil, thou shalt be spoiled; *and* when thou shalt make an end to deal treacherously, <sup>d</sup>they shall deal treacherously with thee.

2 O LORD, be gracious unto us; we have <sup>e</sup>waited for thee: be thou <sup>f</sup>their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble.

3 At the noise of the tumult the people <sup>g</sup>fled; at the lifting up of thyself the nations were <sup>h</sup>scattered.

4 And your spoil shall be gathered *like* the gathering of the caterpillar: as the running to and fro of locusts shall he run upon them.

5 The LORD is <sup>i</sup>exalted; for he dwelleth <sup>k</sup>on high: he hath filled Zion with judgment and righteousness.

6 And <sup>l</sup>wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, *and* strength of salvation: the fear of the LORD *is* his <sup>m</sup>treasure.

7 Behold, their valiant ones shall cry without; the <sup>n</sup>ambassadors of peace shall weep bitterly.

8 The highways lie waste, the wayfaring man ceaseth: <sup>o</sup>he hath broken the covenant, he hath <sup>p</sup>despised the cities, he regardeth no man.

9 The <sup>q</sup>earth mourneth *and* languisheth; Lebanon is ashamed *and* hewn down; Sharon is like a wilderness; and Bashan and Carmel shake off *their fruits.*

10 <sup>r</sup>Now will I rise, saith the LORD; now will I be exalted; now will I lift up myself.

11 Ye shall <sup>s</sup>conceive chaff; ye shall bring forth stubble: your breath *as* fire shall devour you.

12 And the people shall be *as* the <sup>t</sup>burnings of lime; *as* thorns cut up shall they be burned in the fire.

13 Hear, ye *that are* <sup>u</sup>far off, what I have done; and ye *that are* near, acknowledge my might.

14 The <sup>x</sup>sinners in <sup>y</sup>Zion are afraid; fearfulness hath surprised the hypocrites: Who among <sup>z</sup>us shall dwell with the devouring fire? who among us shall dwell with <sup>a</sup>everlasting burnings?

15 He that walketh <sup>b</sup>righteously, and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of oppressions, that shaketh his hands from holding of bribes, that stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and <sup>c</sup>shutteth his eyes from seeing evil;

16 He shall dwell on high: his place of defence *shall be* the <sup>d</sup>munitions of rocks; bread shall be given him, his water *shall be* sure.

17 Thine eyes shall see the <sup>e</sup>King in his beauty: they shall behold the land that is <sup>g</sup>very far off.

18 Thine heart shall <sup>h</sup>meditate terror. <sup>i</sup>Where *is* the scribe? where *is* the receiver? where *is* he that counted the towers?

19 Thou shalt <sup>k</sup>not see a fierce people; a people of a deeper speech than thou canst perceive; of a stammering tongue, *that thou canst* not understand.

20 Look <sup>l</sup>upon Zion, the city of our solemnities: thine eyes shall see Jerusalem a quiet habitation, a tabernacle *that* shall not be taken down; not one of the stakes thereof shall ever be <sup>m</sup>removed, neither shall any of the cords thereof be broken.

21 But there the glorious LORD *will be* unto us a place of <sup>n</sup>broad rivers *and* streams; wherein shall go <sup>o</sup>no galley with oars, neither shall gallant ship pass thereby.

22 For the LORD *is* our judge, the LORD *is* our <sup>p</sup>lawgiver, the LORD *is* our king; he will <sup>q</sup>save us.

23 Thy tacklings are loosed; they could not well strengthen their mast; they could not spread the sail: then is the <sup>r</sup>prey of a great spoil divided; the lame take the prey.

24 And the inhabitants shall not say, I am <sup>s</sup>sick: the people that dwell therein *shall be* <sup>t</sup>forgiven *their* iniquity.
