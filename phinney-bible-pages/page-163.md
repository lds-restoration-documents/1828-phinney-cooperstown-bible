22 And the other issued out of the city against them; so they were in the midst of Israel, some on this side, and some on that side: and they smote them, so that they let <sup>t</sup>none of them remain or escape.

23 And the <sup>u</sup>king of Ai they took alive, and brought him to Joshua.

25 And *so* it was, *that* all that fell that day, both of men and women, *were* twelve thousand, *even* all the men of Ai.

26 For Joshua <sup>x</sup>drew not his hand back, wherewith he stretched out the spear, until he had utterly destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai.

27 Only the cattle and the spoil of that city, Israel <sup>y</sup>took for a prey unto themselves, according unto the word of the LORD, which he commanded Joshua.

28 And Joshua burnt Ai, and made it a heap <sup>z</sup>for ever, *even* a desolation, unto this day.
